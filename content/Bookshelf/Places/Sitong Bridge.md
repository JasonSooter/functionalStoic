## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e30e75c5v1rm4hq6n1phx4)

> Censors have struck the highway bridge from China’s mapping services. If you put in “Sitong Bridge,” the service says that there is no result.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** A man disguised as a construction worker hangs two banners on the highway bridge, burns a tire, and is arrested. Censors then remove the bridge from mapping services.
- **Why it matters:** The erasure of the bridge from maps makes the place itself a symbol of censorship and individual courage.
