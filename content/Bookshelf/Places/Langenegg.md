## The Towns That Invent Their Own Money — Kaja Šeruga (2025)

Source: [Original](https://reasonstobecheerful.world/towns-invent-community-currencies/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebptqc05xd1hfjsm7tq1es)

> This is the case in Langenegg, an Austrian village some 200 kilometers west of Wörgl, where the village grocer retired in 2008 without a successor.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** After the village grocer retired in 2008 the municipality built a new village store and launched the Langenegger Talente currency in 2009. About 20 percent of households subscribe and the municipality pays subsidies in the currency, keeping over 600,000 euros a year in the village.
- **Why it matters:** It is the modern, in-depth case study, with named residents and figures, showing how a small rural place uses a local currency to keep its shop and community alive.
