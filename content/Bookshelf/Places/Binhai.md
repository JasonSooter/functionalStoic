## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)

> The skyscrapers in Binhai, the zone that Tianjin has designated a financial district, are mostly empty.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In 2020 the author took the train from Beijing to see Binhai's hollow financial district; the central mall had few people, and the Binhai Library's shelves held printed images of book spines. Tianjin later revised its GDP down by nearly 20 percent.
