## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqz38ckh64jw2hybazwryt)

> Zeng Zhaoqi, newly appointed party secretary of Guan County, was humiliated that it ranked last in the province for family planning.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Zeng summoned twenty-two senior officials in April 1991 and demanded zero births between May 1 and August 10; residents said every woman was forced to abort regardless of stage, with toughs imported from other counties. Known as the 'childless hundred days' or 'slaughter of the lambs'; Zeng was later promoted.
- **Why it matters:** The book's notorious example of strict enforcement of the one-child policy, in Song Jian's home province. Chapter 4, so site-level detail is thin.
