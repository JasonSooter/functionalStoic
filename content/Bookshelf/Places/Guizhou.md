## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dcnam93bpsjb6vyr3h7g7d)

> Over five days, we cycled nearly four hundred miles through Guizhou province and arrived in the city of Chongqing.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Chapter 2's extended case. In 2021 the author and two friends cycled nearly four hundred miles across the province to Chongqing; Guizhou, long poor and mountainous, has been transformed by bridges, expressways, high-speed rail and data centres, but carries heavy debt and underused airports.
- **Why it matters:** The book's extreme example of the engineering state's building strategy, with both its benefits and its costs.
