## Peace Corps: The Great Adventure — Peace Corps (2002)
Source: [[Peace Corps - The Great Adventure]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crcd8fhw2h3v3kxj1nc0d6)

> Kathy Crabb (Morocco 1995-1997) was a maternal-child health Volunteer in M'semrir, which is located in Morocco's eastern High Atlas Mountains.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 'The Rhythm of Women', Crabb spends a day surveying home-birthing practices with her friend Fadma and ends up dancing at a neighbours' wedding. The essay calls it only 'my little village in the High Atlas'; the author note names M'semrir.
- **Why it matters:** The village is the setting of a whole essay about patience and the communal rhythm of women's lives.
