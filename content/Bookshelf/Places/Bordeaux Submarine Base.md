## One City’s Race to ‘Solarize Everything We Possibly Can’ — Peter Yeung (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dckqwgbakqk70g0dfcpw3k)

> Bordeaux’s submarine base was once a place of battle. Built by the Germans during the Second World War, the hulking concrete edifice in the north of the wine-loving French city was used to launch attacks against the Allied Forces.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Built by the Germans to launch attacks on the Allies, the base's roof now carries 6,600 solar panels flown in by helicopter, a 3.4 MW plant billed as France's largest urban rooftop solar site. Part of the roof was left bare so the panels cannot be seen from the ground, after a dispute over heritage against climate urgency.
- **Why it matters:** The flagship of the city's solar drive and the clearest case of the heritage-versus-energy tension the article explores.
