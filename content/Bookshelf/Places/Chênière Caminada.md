## The Awakening — Kate Chopin (1899)
Source: [[The Awakening]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crcqpdzmnt7brjbffzf0qf)

> The long line of little gray, weather-beaten houses nestled peacefully among the orange trees. It must always have been God’s day on that low, drowsy island, Edna thought.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Edna and Robert sail over for mass at the church of Our Lady of Lourdes; she leaves the service feeling faint, rests and sleeps in Madame Antoine's cottage, and the two spend the day together before sailing back at night.
- **Why it matters:** Their first day alone together, when Edna feels newly free and the attraction to Robert deepens; the drowsy island lies outside her ordinary life.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real settlement, named in the text; Madame Antoine is fictional.
