## France Built the World’s Most Expensive Road: In the Ocean — The B1M (2026)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crbtrze1bn8rjfxqw5cjgb)

> On the 24 March, 2006, an entire section of the cliff gave way, sending tonnes of rock onto the highway below, and killing two people.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The whole video is about this 12.5 km offshore road between Saint-Denis and La Possession, built to replace the cliff road after the 2006 rockfall. It covers the 5.4 km Grande Chaloupe viaduct, the sea dyke and its rock-quarry fight, the cost overrun to over \$2bn, and the decision to finish the last stretch as a viaduct.
- **Why it matters:** The road is the video's single subject; its story of isolation, a quarry ban and miscounted rock is the argument.
