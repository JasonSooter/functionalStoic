## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling
Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e324cpk7b1p97ecyj2n4r0)

> The mission had been set up by the Sisters of the Cross and Passion and the five nuns there, all of different nationalities, were known as “the passionate sisters.”

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The team's base for weeks: nuns run a hostel with a laid table, orange liqueur and guest rooms without running water. A young Congolese nun, Sister Kalunga, joins the team. The team washes in the river after village days.
- **Why it matters:** Supplies the nuns and nurse as part of the chain of trust and gives comic relief (liqueur, fried rat). Mainly a base rather than a turning point.
