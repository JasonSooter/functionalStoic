Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QiYvXKQksgI) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqw7fwj75e474hmgzxpyxk)

> I’m currently standing in Rødbyhavn on the Danish side of the Fehmarn Belt

- **Role:** setting
- **In the video:** The presenter stands at the Danish portal site, where a work harbour, a village for 1,300 workers and a huge factory casting 89 tunnel elements have been built. Elements will float out to sea from here.
- **Why it matters:** It is the main construction base of the tunnel and where most of the video is filmed.
