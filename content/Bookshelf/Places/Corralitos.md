Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edhxzydcp6n326sw2r8kqq)

> Most recently, I stayed in a very small cabin in Corralitos, a small town just south of Santa Cruz, with the intent of going bird-watching at the Elkhorn Slough National Estuarine Research Reserve.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Odell retreats to a cabin for several days without speaking to anyone and drives from there to Elkhorn Slough on the third day, listening to a Santa Cruz radio DJ.
- **Why it matters:** It is the solitary base from which the epiphany trip begins; minor in itself.
