## How long could you drive towards the sunset? — xkcd's What If? (2026)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5beythqvrhwrb1rw37tp4x9)

> The closest road that qualifies is probably in Longyearbyen, on the island of Svalbard, Norway.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** To keep up with the Sun you want to be as near a pole as possible, but there are no paved roads near either; Longyearbyen is the closest, and its longest sunset lasts just under an hour. The answer then turns to northern Scandinavia, where on the right day the day-night line reverses direction and a carefully chosen drive can stretch a sunset to about 95 minutes.
- **Why it matters:** Longyearbyen is the first test case and the benchmark the better route beats; the real answer isn't tied to one named place.
