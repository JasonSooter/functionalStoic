## Ireland’s $27BN Mission to Save Dublin — The B1M (2026)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_Jz9xzOoO4) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crb8m6pqbp2jsbcg6r3zxh)

> Albert College Park is the destination of choice.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The metro's section between Collins Avenue and Griffith Park is nearly 1,500 m long, over the EU's 1,000 m limit, so an intervention shaft for escape and ventilation must be built between the stations; the park, with its trees and playground, is chosen and will become the centre of the shaft works.
- **Why it matters:** The park is the concrete local cost of the metro: a neighbourhood green space turned into a construction site.
