## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)
Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebvb3fczkekhzs9jr6h0bd)

> I took a cab out to Murray Canyon, a trail maintained by the Agua Caliente Band of Cahuilla Indians on their reservation.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Walking the canyon she uses iNaturalist to name brittlebush, fan palms, a phainopepla and a chuckwalla. She uses the experience to defend naming as a first step toward kinship rather than alienation.
- **Why it matters:** It is a place where she practices bioregional attention while traveling; it illustrates that 'to behold is to become beholden to'.
