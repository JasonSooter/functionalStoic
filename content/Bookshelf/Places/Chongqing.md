## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dcnf1rdkg3p46naavcpkx1)

> Chongqing is my favorite Chinese city to visit because it has the country’s, and perhaps the world’s, most dramatic urban setting.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The cycling trip ends in Chongqing, built on steep hillsides between the Yangtze and Jialing rivers: stacked buildings, a metro line running through an apartment block, wartime air-raid tunnels turned into hotpot restaurants. Returning in 2022, the author finds the Jialing nearly dry in a drought.
- **Why it matters:** The end of the bike trip and a vivid case of urban engineering and of climate vulnerability.
