Source: [[My Miserable Week in the ‘Happiest Country on Earth’ - The New York Times]] · [Original](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/05/02/magazine/finland-happiest-country.html/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2xnkh67bdgz9yx36bh22j)

> On my second day, I visited a place called Löyly, pronounced LOW-luh.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** She visits Löyly, a pricey swimsuit-required sauna in a rock-heap-like building. From its terrace she walks to the sea and jumps through a hole cut in the ice.
- **Why it matters:** The ice-dip is her most vivid moment of the trip and her clearest taste of Finnish sauna and winter bathing.
