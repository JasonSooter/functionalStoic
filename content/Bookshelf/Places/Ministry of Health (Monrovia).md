## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling (2019)
Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebtebkdcjbjvhntqvb39qb)

> A quarter of an hour later, the car pulled up in front of the health ministry’s three-story building of yellowing concrete.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Rosling is taken here on his second morning; the ministry's guards enforce chlorinated-water hygiene at the door. Within twenty minutes Luke Bawo gives him a desk in his own room as deputy head of ebola surveillance. He works here for months, fixing the daily case report and its flawed database.
- **Why it matters:** The core of the ebola chapter: the physical seat of Liberia's surveillance and where Rosling's data fixes happen. Elsewhere it would be a different story.
