## Stadt Freiburg lädt zum Empfang für SC Freiburg nach Europa-League-Finale — Matthias Joers (2026)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bre9h8fxzh04m8jakftkfq)

> Der SC Freiburg wird am Donnerstag, 21. Mai, mit einem großen Empfang an der Neuen Messe in Freiburg für seinen Einzug ins Europa-League-Finale gewürdigt.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The city invites fans to a free, ticketed reception at the Neue Messe the day after the final: the stage programme starts at 17:00, the team is expected around 18:00, and visitors are told to come by Stadtbahn line 4 or the Breisgau S-Bahn.
- **Why it matters:** It is where the city celebrates the club's biggest success; the venue was chosen for capacity, so the place itself carries little meaning beyond that.

## Lauf in den Frühling: Freiburg-Marathon lockt 18.500 Läufer — Matthias Joers (2026)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bs6fwfvaswrw2bk95p54fb)

> Der Start- und Zielbereich ist wie üblich auf dem Messegelände.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The 21st Freiburg Marathon starts and finishes on the trade-fair grounds, which also host a sports expo and a family programme; the route then runs through many districts of the city.
- **Why it matters:** The grounds are the event's hub; as with the SC reception, the city uses the Messe when it needs room for a crowd.
