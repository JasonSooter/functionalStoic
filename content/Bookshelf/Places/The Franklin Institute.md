## I Didn't Bring My Son to a Museum to Look at Screens — Seth Purcell (2025)
Source: [[I Didn't Bring My Son to a Museum to Look at Screens]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5d20g0063kzqjxm5x4whdxq)

> She led our son gently but firmly away to the glorious four-story Foucault pendulum which hangs in a stairwell.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The museum was one of the writer's two favourite places as a child in the 1980s. Visiting with his six-year-old, he finds the main halls and the Wondrous Space exhibit dominated by touchscreens, with the real hands-on exhibits tucked away in Sir Isaac's Loft and the Air Show room. He asks for the screens to go, while allowing that the Franklin Memorial rotunda is gorgeous.
- **Why it matters:** The museum is the case for his argument that children learn from physical things, not digital simulations of them.
