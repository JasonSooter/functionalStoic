# Mont Cenis Base Tunnel

57.5-km rail base tunnel under construction between Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne, France, and Susa, Italy, for the Lyon–Turin line.

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFrr-L_BcC4) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce47n3v2f49gn6ya45yzq6)

> Stretching for 57.5km, it'll become

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The presenter tours the works: drill-and-blast sections, a lining gantry, the TBM Viviana, a huge cavern nicknamed the cathedral, and ventilation shafts. The tunnel runs from Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne, whose station will be replaced by a bigger international one, to Susa, where over 3,000 demonstrators protested in 2023. It should cut Lyon–Turin from 3h47 to 1h47 for about 25 billion euros.
- **Why it matters:** The tunnel is the subject; the valley protests are the political cost.
