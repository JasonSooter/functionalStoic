## Yet another bike ride to find Burlington County’s trolls — and some of its best pizza — alliumstozinnias (2024)

> Turn right by the law office sign and keep going all the way back — and there is Big Rusty!

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Big Rusty is Thomas Dambo's original troll, living down an unpaved road in Hainesport since June 2023. The group does an extra out-and-back to see it, reached via Route 38 and Bancroft Lane. Officials were holding a site meeting that day to start a project improving the park around it, with access from Bancroft Lane.
- **Why it matters:** He is the destination everyone was most excited about; the trek is a local riff on this work.
