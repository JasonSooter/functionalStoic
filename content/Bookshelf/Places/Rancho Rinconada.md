## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)
Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebv0frhwckypcxbd531b6k)

> I came across an aerial map of Rancho Rinconada, the Cupertino neighborhood I grew up in, as it was being built in the 1950s.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Looking at a 1950s aerial photo against Google Maps, she finds her childhood house and realizes a wiggly line is Saratoga Creek, which ran past the neighborhood pool. This launches her rediscovery of the local watershed.
- **Why it matters:** It is the starting point of the creek story and her ignorance of home geography; minor as a place in itself.
