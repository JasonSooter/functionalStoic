## While Riding on a Train Going West — Linnesby-Maria (2024)
Source: [Original](https://notesfromlinnesby.substack.com/p/while-riding-on-a-train-going-west) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ede8vv38racsmapeyn1nbg)

> If the train breaks down, we will be lost in the heat under these huge, alien red rocks.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** She is terrified by the huge red cliffs at 100 F, while her Utah seat-mate rejoices because the canyons are her home. She is too intimidated to photograph them and emerges from Ruby Canyon into desert.
- **Why it matters:** The canyons expose how the same landscape is menacing to the visitor and home to the local.
