## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)
Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e31p2xd4tdeae8qwjf6wtt)

> Merton arrived and was accepted at the Abbey of Gethsemani in rural Kentucky in 1941. So much did he desire solitude that he spent years petitioning to become a hermit on the monastery grounds.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Merton enters the Trappist abbey in rural Kentucky, petitions for years to be a hermit on its grounds, and writes the journal that becomes The Seven Storey Mountain there, a book of contemptus mundi.
- **Why it matters:** It stands for the total retreat that the author contrasts with the Louisville epiphany; the abbey is the place of withdrawal he later partly repudiates.
