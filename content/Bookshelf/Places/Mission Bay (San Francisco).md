## Why Cities Feel so Lonely Now. — Type Ashton (2025)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crd4rygfjmeezgcjzjf28q)

> Consider the case of San Francisco's Mission Bay neighborhood. Once an industrial area, it was redeveloped over the last few decades, largely through private investment.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** A case of developer-led redevelopment of a former industrial area: dense housing, offices and labs, with green space broken into small parks and waterfront strips.
- **Why it matters:** It stands for the modern failure to set aside large public space.
