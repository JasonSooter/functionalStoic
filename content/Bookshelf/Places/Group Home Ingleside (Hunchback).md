> Group Home Ingleside, the facility in a converted block of studio flats in which I was living, had been left to me by my parents as somewhere that I could live out the rest of my days.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Shaka Izawa lives in a single sixteen-square-metre room at Ingleside, cared for by staff including Yamashita and Tanaka, and never leaves the building. From her west-facing window she can faintly see Mount Fuji. Here she works online, writes erotic fiction for income and arranges her plan with Tanaka. She later renames the home in memory of Anne of Ingleside, and Tanaka leaves it after the hospital episode.
- **Why it matters:** It is the enclosed world of the novel, where her disability, wealth and plan unfold. The name comes from Montgomery.
