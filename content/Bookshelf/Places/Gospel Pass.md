## It's All About the Bike — Robert Penn (2010)

Source: [[It's All About the Bike - In Pursuit of Happiness on Two Wheels]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce6j4h6a3se107ey44ab0b)

> I dropped down into the Llanthony Valley, commanding the bike through the corners with the tiniest shifts in balance.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On the first ride of the finished bike, from his back door in the Black Mountains, Penn drops into the Llanthony Valley, climbs past Capel-y-ffin and reaches the Gospel Pass, with views into mid-Wales. There he declares "It's all about the bike".
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's ending: the whole quest pays off on this road.
