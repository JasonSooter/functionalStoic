## Join us at the Transportation Projects Fair! — Transfort (n.d.)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebrn1e83pehygdr43aqb15)

> The Lincoln Center is in the heart of Fort Collins' walkable streets.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The city's Transportation Projects Fair is held here on Thursday March 6, 5-7 p.m. The newsletter explains how to arrive by MAX, Routes 6 and 10, bike, foot or car, and notes parking and EV charging at neighbouring Mulberry Pool.
- **Why it matters:** The venue anchors the whole lead item; the access directions are written around it. Held elsewhere, the route and parking advice would change entirely.

## Get Involved and Stay Informed! — Transfort (n.d.)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebrpkv611qne5af6026sxa)

> The Lincoln Center, 417 W. Magnolia

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The newsletter announces a free open-house Transportation Projects Fair on Thursday, March 6, 5-7 p.m., at the Lincoln Center. It then explains how to get there by MAX and Routes 6 and 10, whose stops are on Mulberry and Meldrum right by the building.
- **Why it matters:** It is the physical venue the event notice and the transit directions revolve around; the transit tips only make sense relative to this spot. Minor overall since the piece is a city newsletter.
