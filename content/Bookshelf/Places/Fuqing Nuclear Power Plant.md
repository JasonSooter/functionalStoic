## Why China is Winning The Nuclear Power Race — The B1M (2026)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bfwwpve92pkgm1sgyh0x26)

> Construction began in 2015, with the pouring of the first concrete for the reactor containment building.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The plant is the worked example of China's own Hualong One design: first concrete in 2015, a 153-piece steel dome two years later, fuel loading and grid connection in 2020 and commercial operation the following January, about five and a half years from first concrete. A second unit followed a year later; four older reactors on the site are a predecessor model.
- **Why it matters:** It is the video's evidence for its argument that China builds reactors faster than anyone else. See also [[Zhangzhou Nuclear Power Plant]].
