## The Absolute Best Transportation for Cities (trams) — Not Just Bikes (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce51mg0264vkgdy8pxx804)

> Coolsingel, which was recently redesigned to make more space for walking, cycling,

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** Recently redesigned, half of the Coolsingel was taken from cars for a wide sidewalk and bike path. Its tram runs directly over a metro line and shares stations with it; Rotterdam kept the tram because trams and metros do different jobs.
- **Why it matters:** It is the video's rebuttal to the North American idea that trams are just metros-in-waiting.
