Source: [[My Miserable Week in the ‘Happiest Country on Earth’ - The New York Times]] · [Original](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/05/02/magazine/finland-happiest-country.html/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2xpw4ybh50aa54c08whhk)

> The day after Löyly I visited a more typical sauna called Kotiharjun, which had a neon red sign and two plastic chairs out front

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** She visits Kotiharjun, a modest public sauna with a beer freezer and a creaky stair to the women's locker room. She describes its air of utility and faded charm, balanced between authentic and 'authentic'.
- **Why it matters:** It is her contrast to Löyly and where she observes everyday sauna culture, central to her argument about Finnish trust and community.
