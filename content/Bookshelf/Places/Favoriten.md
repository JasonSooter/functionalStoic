## How ‘Superblocks’ Can Create People-Centered Cities — Kaja Šeruga (2025)
Source: [[How ‘Superblocks’ Can Create People-Centered Cities]] · [Original](https://reasonstobecheerful.world/superblocks-are-for-people/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edfebsjdw8dhbfbrbgs51m)

> The pilot is located in the district of Favoriten, a lower-income area where the environmental and health benefits of a superblock are up to three times higher than in more affluent parts of the city

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Vienna’s first superblock (Supergrätzl) pilot sits in Favoriten, where a study found benefits up to three times higher than in richer districts; the city added 62 trees, water features and play areas.
- **Why it matters:** It supports the equity argument that superblocks matter most in lower-income areas; a different district would weaken that point.
