## I thought the Schmid Peoplemover was impossible — Tom Scott
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A2g4u9F9i90) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crba2eandq43btwv0r90xp)

> This is the larger one, at a train station in Altbach, Germany.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** Tom Scott explains how the Schmid Peoplemover, a cabin that moves horizontally and then vertically without stopping, works; the larger of the two surviving units is at the station in Altbach. He covers its rack-rail cogs, its docking with a carrier and counterweight, and compares it with an underpass, or two lifts and a footbridge.
- **Why it matters:** The station holds the working installation that is the video's whole subject.
