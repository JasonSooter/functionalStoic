## Touring the Indian Himalayas: Bikepacking the Markha Valley – Jan Zdansky — The Radavist (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce3bjztrn7cngax0svvgn7)

> Nimaling is an improvised camp with prepared tents and, most importantly, the possibility of refreshment. I settled my upset stomach with black tea and got to it: the final, most brutal section up to the 5,250-meter-high Konmaru La Pass.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** After recovering with black tea at the Nimaling camp, Zdansky tackles "the final, most brutal section" to the pass in snow, pushing the bike twenty steps at a time. He reaches the top barely understanding how; the descent down the riverbed toward Shang is the worst walking he has done with a loaded bike.
- **Why it matters:** The pass is the ride's climax and the reason the trip is a test rather than a tour.
