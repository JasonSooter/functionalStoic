## Why Are Homes in Western States So Expensive? — Brian Potter (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.construction-physics.com/p/why-are-homes-in-western-states-so) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqv3yetpqkqqgjx911wdjv)

> is a town on the northern edge of Wyoming, very close to what I’d call the middle of nowhere.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Potter cites Sheridan as a surprising example: a town of about 18,000 more than 100 miles from any major city or international airport, where basic suburban houses cost close to $300 per square foot, more than his metro Atlanta home. Population grew 18% between 2000 and 2020.
- **Why it matters:** It illustrates the thesis that western rural areas face unusually high housing demand.
