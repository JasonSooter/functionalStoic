# Mundenhof

## Admission ticket = travel ticket: How ZMF, the city and VAG became pioneers 40 years ago — VAG Freiburg (2026)
Source: [Original](https://blog.vag-freiburg.de/eintrittskarte-fahrschein-wie-zmf-stadt-und-vag-vor-40-jahren-pioniere-wurden/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5b44zpwq0a6aqxvnwjnvtb8)

> the festival was relocated from inner-city squares out to the grounds of the Mundenhof, where initially there was neither a public transport service nor any notable number of parking spaces.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** The ZMF began in 1983 on the old Meßplatz and today's Platz der Alten Synagoge, moved to the Eschholzpark in 1984, and then to the Mundenhof to spare residents the noise and traffic. To reach the remote site, VAG set up a shuttle from the tram and made the concert ticket valid for travel. Since 1994 the transfer has been at the line 3 terminus at Munzinger Straße, and since 1997 also at Maria-von-Rudloff-Platz on line 5. From 1993 the same model was used for SC Freiburg games at the Dreisamstadion.
- **Why it matters:** The site's remoteness caused the innovation: "making a virtue out of a parking shortage". In the city centre the festival would never have needed the combined ticket.
