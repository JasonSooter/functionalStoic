## learning to save a whale — rebecca hooper (2026)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bre1pc8t5q908sg336h0nq)

> On Sanday, a neighbouring isle, mass strandings are particularly frequent.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author lives on Westray. She describes a six-hour rescue of stranded dolphins in January and a lost pilot whale calf in the harbour the year before, and realises the island has no active marine mammal medic; she trains as one and fundraises for local rescue kit. Neighbouring Sanday, where mass strandings are frequent, sharpens the need.
- **Why it matters:** The island's isolation is the argument: help from the mainland can't arrive in time, so the islanders have to be the rescuers.
