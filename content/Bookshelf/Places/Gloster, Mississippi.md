## The Curative Power of Quieter Cities — Elizabeth Hewitt (2025)
Source: [Original](https://reasonstobecheerful.world/noise-pollution-quieter-cities/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edfmgbdddpjqh7nwn5d5am)

> Loud 18-wheeler trucks drive along the country roads every couple minutes. Machinery and loudspeakers echo out from the plant across the two-mile-wide town.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Residents of this town of almost 900 live beside a wood pellet plant. Local students monitor air and noise there, and Walker's study found noise up to 10 decibels higher than in a comparable town without a biomass plant.
- **Why it matters:** Gloster is the lead case study that shows noise as a health and justice issue; the story opens and closes there.
