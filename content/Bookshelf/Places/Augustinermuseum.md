## So sieht es im sanierten Augustinermuseum aus – Eröffnung am 28. Februar — Saskia Schuh (2026)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5c3p630mr2zdxj7j3fpnf95)

> Nach mehr als zwei Jahrzehnten Bauzeit öffnet das komplett sanierte Augustinermuseum am Samstag, 28. Februar, seine Türen.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The Freiburger Wochenbericht reports that the fully renovated museum reopens on 28 February after more than twenty years of work in three phases, at a cost of about €95 million. New areas include a treasury, a Baroque gallery and a city-history section. The mayor also promises that the Augustinerplatz outside will be fully open again by May, with more space, seating and a bigger playground.
- **Why it matters:** The museum is the whole subject of the piece, and the reopening ends one of the city's longest building projects.
