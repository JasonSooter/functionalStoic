## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)
Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edhvbxzr32dknraet1rz55)

> Vincent Medina, a Muwekma Ohlone tribal councilman, spoke of a current proposal to build condos at an Ohlone shellmound site in West Berkeley.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** At an event of the Ohlone group mak-'amham, Odell hears of a proposal to build condos on a shellmound burial site now used as a parking lot. She describes the Ohlone proposal for a forty-foot mound covered in poppies with an arbor and a daylighted Strawberry Creek, and notes nearby Shellmound Street.
- **Why it matters:** The site is 'one of the best examples' of doing something by not building; the place's burials and history are what make the refusal of development meaningful.
