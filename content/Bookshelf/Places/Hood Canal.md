## The cost of growing up online — Gates Notes (2024)
Source: [Original](https://www.gatesnotes.com/The-Anxious-Generation) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edgkz317xm2ze44xjxbjh0)

> when I would isolate myself in a cabin on Washington’s Hood Canal with nothing but a big bag of books and technical papers.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Gates describes his annual Think Week, seven days alone in a cabin reading, thinking and writing, seeing only the person who delivered meals and not checking email. He uses it as the model of uninterrupted deep concentration.
- **Why it matters:** It is the concrete emblem of the focus the book warns is being lost; the isolated location is what makes the example work.
