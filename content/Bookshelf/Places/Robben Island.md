## The Daily Stoic — Ryan Holiday (2016)

Source: [[The Daily Stoic]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edhjn9adj4afj4cs51ar2t)

> Nelson Mandela was imprisoned for resistance to the brutal apartheid regime in South Africa for twenty-seven years.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Mandela spent twenty-seven years imprisoned, eighteen with a bucket for a toilet, a hard cot and one thirty-minute visitor a year. He asserted his will by walking slowly, shadowboxing and encouraging other prisoners.
- **Why it matters:** The cell shows how to keep dignity under deprivation. The text does not name the island or prison, so it is a loosely specified setting.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The entry names no prison; Mandela spent 18 of his 27 years on Robben Island (Wikipedia, Nelson Mandela), which matches the 'eighteen' years the entry describes. ⚠️ Named from that reference, not from the text.
