## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)

Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqztmfvha5mat0r9bd1816)

> I was on a solitary trip to the Sierra Nevada to work on a project about the Mokelumne River, and the cabin I had booked had no cell reception and no Wi-Fi.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Odell accidentally spent several days offline in a cabin, panicked for twenty minutes, then found her phone inert. She uses it as her own small digital detox before arguing that retreat is not the answer.
- **Why it matters:** The cabin itself is not located; the river gives the region.
