## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)
Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqzsqfvft6vdafhwh98r0y)

> a project I did while in residence at Recology SF, I spent three months photographing, cataloging, and researching the origins of two hundred discarded objects.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Odell was artist in residence at the city dump and made The Bureau of Suspended Objects there, a catalogued archive of 200 discarded objects. It illustrates her 'observational eros' and her medium of context.
- **Why it matters:** A concrete site of her art practice of giving attention to existing things.
