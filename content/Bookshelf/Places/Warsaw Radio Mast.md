# Warsaw Radio Mast

Former longwave radio mast at Konstantynów near Gąbin, central [[Poland]]; the tallest structure on Earth from 1974 until its collapse in 1991.

## The Bizarre Bases of Antenna Towers — Practical Engineering (2025)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5d206yfx6js2rwy01p21318)

> Soaring to 646 meters or 2,120 feet, the Warsaw Radio Mast was built to broadcast radio programs to Polish-speaking audiences across Europe.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The piece opens with the mast: built in 1974, the tallest structure on Earth, it broadcast Polish-language radio across Europe. By 1991 its guy wires needed replacing; on 8 August 1991 a main cable was released before the temporary ones were in place, the wind twisted the tower and it fell, injuring no one.
- **Why it matters:** The collapse is the hook for the explainer on how guyed towers and their pinned bases work; the rest of the piece is generic engineering.
