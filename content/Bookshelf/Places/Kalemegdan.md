## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bekgcnnhe6sy9hdkventvt)

> I strolled through the main shopping streets to a central city park called Kalemegdan, built around an old fortress and neatly arrayed with trees and benches and statues of Yugoslavian, and particularly Serbian, heroes.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 20. Most benches are taken by men hunched over chessboards, each pair with onlookers advising both players. From a high terrace at the park's edge he sees "the spot where the Sava and the Danube flow together to make one truly monumental river."
- **Why it matters:** It is the calm heart of a Belgrade he is "rather taken with", before his long, frustrating attempt to get a ticket out to Sofia.
