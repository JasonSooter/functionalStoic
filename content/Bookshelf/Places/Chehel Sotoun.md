## Rick Steves' Iran (2009) — Rick Steves' Europe (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CYoa9hI3CXg) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqx928grvzs81r40fx46k6)

> The Chehel Sotun Palace is a vivid reminder that Isfahan was the capital of Persia 400 years ago.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the video:** Steves tours the palace with its reflecting pool, gardens, wooden-columned portico and mirrored interior. Wall paintings in the grand hall show the Safavid shah putting down an Uzbek revolt and fighting the Ottomans.
- **Why it matters:** It evokes Persia's 16th and 17th-century golden age and the shah's court life.
