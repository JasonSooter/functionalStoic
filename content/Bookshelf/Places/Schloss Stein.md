# Schloss Stein

Castle complex at Stein an der Traun, Upper [[Bavaria]], Germany: an upper castle, a cave castle (Höhlenburg) in the cliff, and a lower castle that houses a boarding school.

## Team Stein macht sich bereit! 08.08.2026 — goethe.de (2026)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bey7frj8ajafgmgrdx3a03)

> Ihr werdet mit uns im Schloss Stein wohnen, es ist wunderschön hier.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** A Goethe-Institut course blog, written to arriving teenagers. The team decorates and organises the site before Sunday's arrivals; the photos show the teaching house, the dining hall, playing fields, a football pitch and basketball court, and the school entrance where participants arrive.
- **Why it matters:** The castle school is where the whole three-week course lives and learns; every later post starts or ends here.

## Schlechtes Wetter – gute Laune 19.08.2026 — goethe.de (2026)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bey92sf8ybv2kdn41bbas9)

> Die Höhlenburg hier in Stein an der Traun stammt aus dem 12. Jahrhundert und ist die besterhaltene Höhlenburg in ganz Europa!

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** After a Monday outing to nearby Traunreut and activities on the grounds (a bouldering hall, yoga, table tennis), Wednesday's highlight is a guided tour of the Höhlenburg on the school's own grounds: legends from the age of knights, lemonade from the neighbouring brewery, and a view over the region from the hill.
- **Why it matters:** The cave castle is the week's centrepiece, and the claim that it is Europe's best-preserved cave castle is the post's one piece of history.
