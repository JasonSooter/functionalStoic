## The Surprising Success of Gondola Transit Systems — Wendover Productions (2025)
Source: [Original](https://youtube.com/watch?v=a5126u88E7E&si=1gWTN5lb3hW199bC) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e30zaxqktmj9h8w63q8shp)

> split in two by the deep coastal Penfeld.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the video:** Brest's 2016 cable car crosses the Penfeld between the old naval-base workshops and the city centre, chosen over a $30-60 million bridge at about $20 million. Ridership has mostly been 700,000-800,000 a year against 600,000 expected, despite shutdowns.
- **Why it matters:** A non-mountain example where a river barrier justifies a gondola.
