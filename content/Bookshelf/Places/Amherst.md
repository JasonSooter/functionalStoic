## Emily Dickinson Wouldn't Have Survived Social Media — Elif Shafak (2026)
Source: [[Emily Dickinson Wouldn't Have Survived Social Media]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bs6y157f5113e1hd39bqtv)

> Amherst was the first place I arrived in when I came to America, leaving Istanbul behind for the first time.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Shafak recalls arriving in Amherst from Istanbul for a fellowship at nearby [[Mount Holyoke College]], the winter snow, and her search for traces of Dickinson; she dreams of going back to the poet's estate for a fruit seed to plant in London.
- **Why it matters:** The town is where the essay's two threads meet: Dickinson's withdrawn life and Shafak's own migration.
