## Peace Corps: The Great Adventure — Peace Corps (1997)
Source: [[Peace Corps - The Great Adventure]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e33hrwyfsd757pgs9440g2)

> Taran'ganya was the place where they had lived and served as Volunteers so many years ago.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On a trip to Malawi the narrator's parents, former Peace Corps teachers in Kenya, take the family to the tiny village in Nyanza Province where they served in 1969. A former student, now a professor in Nairobi, arranges the 350-mile trip to Kuria District to visit Taran'ganya Secondary School and old classmates, and they find the school twice its old size.
- **Why it matters:** The village is the emotional destination of a second-generation return story, proof that the parents' Peace Corps tales were true.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Name, district and province in the text match one GeoNames village. GeoNames 179548 Taranganya, Migori County, Kenya (populated place, -1.2343, 34.6006); OSM shows Taranganya Boys/Girls schools at about -1.2345,34.602. Kuria is in Migori County, formerly Nyanza Province.
