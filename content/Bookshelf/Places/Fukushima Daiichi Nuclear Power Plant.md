## Not the End of the World — Hannah Ritchie (2024)
Source: [[Not the End of the World]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e31hed9rgpzbzxyg9thhcz)

> In 2011, the Fukushima nuclear plant in Japan was hit by a tsunami after the country’s largest recorded earthquake. Remarkably, no one died directly from the incident.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** After the tsunami, the text says no one died directly, one possible cancer death was reported, and around 2,700 premature deaths are attributed to evacuation stress.
- **Why it matters:** Second of the two accidents used to put nuclear risk in perspective.
