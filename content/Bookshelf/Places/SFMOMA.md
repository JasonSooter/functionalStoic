## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)
Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebtybdn7njz583g23rphxb)

> Killing time before a meeting with someone at SFMOMA, I was wandering through the different floors and ended up in the exhibition

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Odell wanders the museum before a meeting and is stopped by Ellsworth Kelly's four-panel Blue Green Black Red. She finds the flat blue vibrating and spends equal time on each panel. The encounter teaches her about attention and duration.
- **Why it matters:** It is the concrete scene for the claim that paintings train attention; a different venue would change little, but the physical presence of the work is the point.
