## The Absolute Best Transportation for Cities (trams) — Not Just Bikes (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce4c0gce6ey5182hv2c750)

> The tram network here in Amsterdam is pretty great.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the video:** The narrator lives in Amsterdam and uses its trams as his model: level boarding, screens, transit signal priority so a trip can pass no red lights, dedicated lanes, renewable power and grassy tracks. He also notes driver shortages, funding cuts and the possible removal of tram line 3 in March 2026.
- **Why it matters:** The city is the baseline the whole video measures other places against.
