## Ranking EVERY night train in Europe: Italy 🇮🇹 — Simply Railway (2026)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bs79ezbftarykj34akf5b2)

> Train are literally loaded into a boat to cross the straight of Mesina to reach the island of Sicily.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the video:** Intercity night trains to Palermo or Syracuse are loaded onto a ferry for the crossing, which the video calls the last train ferry of its kind in Europe; the narrator suggests an espresso on board.
- **Why it matters:** The signature feature that sets the Italian night trains apart in the ranking.
