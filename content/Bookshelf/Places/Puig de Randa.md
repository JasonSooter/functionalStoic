## There Are Places in the World Where Rules Are Less Important Than Kindness — Carlo Rovelli (2020)

Source: [[There Are Places in the World Where Rules Are Less Important Than Kindness]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bevgpy07brpa4e7vn3h3t7)

> In 1274, at the end of a long hermitage on Puig de Randa, a mountain on the island of Majorca, Ramon Llull conceives

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The opening sentence of the essay "Ramon Llull: _Ars magna_". At the end of a long hermitage on the mountain, Llull conceives, "by divine revelation, he claims", the great work that becomes the core of his life: a combinatorial system of tables and rotating paper circles meant to bring order to the world and convert Jews and Muslims to Christianity.
- **Why it matters:** Minor as a place: it is a single origin scene, and the essay moves on to combinatorics and Llull's influence. But it is the essay's starting point.
