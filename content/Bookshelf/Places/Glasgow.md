## Not the End of the World — Hannah Ritchie (2024)
Source: [[Not the End of the World]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebvkwkn049wr7psb23bn6a)

> At the 2021 international climate conference in Glasgow – COP26 – the Bolsonaro government pledged to end illegal deforestation by 2028, two years earlier than previously agreed.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Brazil's pledge at Glasgow raised hopes that deforestation could be ended globally, since most forest loss occurs in Brazil. Within months INPE data showed 2021 deforestation at a 15-year high. Ritchie uses this to caution against taking pledges at face value.
- **Why it matters:** Glasgow serves as the stage for the pledge-versus-reality contrast; it is minor as a place.
