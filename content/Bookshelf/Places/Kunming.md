## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)
Source: [[Breakneck]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebt6z7m55f19eczz9hvwzh)

> Both of my parents were born in Kunming, the capital of southwestern Yunnan province.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Kunming is the parents' hometown, a laid-back third-tier backwater with stagnant salaries and property values. Both worked there (father teaching programming, mother at a coal plant then as a provincial broadcaster), and the chapter repeatedly imagines the life they would have had if they had stayed.
- **Why it matters:** It anchors the family counterfactual that drives the chapter's emotional argument; the 'what if they stayed' question hinges on this place.
