## The Hidden Engineering Behind the Falkirk Wheel — Wesley Crump (2025)

Source: [[The Hidden Engineering Behind the Falkirk Wheel]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crdce2mbjv1asefp6kbwey)

> When a segment of the canal was relocated as part of the Millennium Link, it needed to cross the Antonine Wall, a Roman-era defensive perimeter and UNESCO World Heritage Site. Rather than disturb it, the new canal was built into a tunnel below.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** The relocated canal had to cross the Roman wall, so it runs through a tunnel beneath it, and two new locks lift boats the rest of the way after the tunnel.
- **Why it matters:** It forced a design choice that explains why the [[Falkirk Wheel]] does not lift boats the whole height.
