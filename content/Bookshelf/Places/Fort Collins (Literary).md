## Foco Bike Mob News, 12/18/25 — Foco Bike Mob (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce3efcvx5t8ywbcqvskt1p)

> Our goal is to do our part in making Fort Collins the most prominent cycling city in the US by holding space for people have fun riding bikes with one another.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The Foco Bike Mob newsletter announces its 3rd Friday night ride from The Sound Bar, which it has used as a base since 2019, and looks back on its Bike the Lights holiday ride, about sixty riders with a hot-cocoa stop at Lightning Mini Golf. It frames the group as part of making Fort Collins a fun, inclusive cycling city.
- **Why it matters:** The city is the setting of every ride; the named bar and mini-golf course are folded in here rather than pinned.
