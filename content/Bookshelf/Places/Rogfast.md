# Rogfast

Undersea road tunnel under the Boknafjord in Rogaland, Norway, with an underground interchange beneath the island of Kvitsøy. See [[Norway (Literary)]].

## The MASSIVE Ventilation Shafts Under Norway — The B1M (2026)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/01P7B1Q5suU) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bfxrvp4a7e1v880paqcbj9)

> There's a freaking underground roundabout here.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The presenter stands at the bottom of a finished ventilation shaft under the island (spelled "Kavitzoy" in the auto-transcript, population 500). Some 250 m below, the interchange has two roundabouts, a link tunnel and a 4.1 km slip road climbing to the island, with access tunnels and shafts threaded between them, which the video calls a world first.
- **Why it matters:** The interchange is the video's subject. The text's spellings ("Rockfast", "Kavitzoy") are transcription errors for Rogfast and Kvitsøy.

## Inside the World’s Deepest Road Tunnel — The B1M (2026)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ho2luOd8JH4) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5brfjn4mg6rf6f0xwk9w815)

> a twin bore tunnel running 27-kilometres across the mighty Boknafjord.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** Starting from Stavanger, the presenter walks about 6.5 km into the tunnel to the drill-and-blast face: jumbo drills, grouting, and wet fault zones in the gneiss. The twin bores reach about 390 m below sea level, and the video points to the Kvitsøy interchange and to Norway's wider plan of fjord crossings such as Sulafjorden.
- **Why it matters:** The tunnel is the subject; this earlier video shows construction from the inside, where the later one shows the finished interchange shafts.
