## The 10 Biggest Construction Fails of 2025 — The B1M (2025)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5c4qh80hkypvhaw5npba5gv)

> The attack blasted a 6 m wide hole in the hermetically sealed shelter covering the infamous reactor 4.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** A drone hit the shelter at 2 a.m. on 14 February, blasting a 6 m hole and setting the cladding alight; the fire took weeks to put out. The B1M reports no immediate danger but doubts the shield can be fully repaired.
- **Why it matters:** The segment is about damage to one of the world's most important containment structures.
