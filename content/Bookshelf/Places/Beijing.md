## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dcn8d6hjkkfgjjap36cs3s)

> Beijing enthralls not because it is nice but because it isn’t. By most measures, life in Beijing is dreary.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The introduction starts in Beijing as China's most riveting city: dusty, arid, laid out with avenues fit for army parades and with its liveliest places bricked up, yet a city of gravity that draws the country's smartest people. For the rest of the book it stands for the Party and the central government.
- **Why it matters:** It sets up the book's picture of the engineering state and its urge to control the future.
