## It's All About the Bike — Robert Penn (2010)
Source: [[It's All About the Bike - In Pursuit of Happiness on Two Wheels]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce6eqxwycvqh2md0mwha9w)

> The first competitive six-day race was held in the Islington Agricultural Hall, London, in 1878.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In the saddle chapter Penn describes the first six-day race, held here in 1878: riders on high-wheelers circled for six days in front of crowds. Bill Cann of Sheffield won with 1,060 miles on a wooden saddle.
- **Why it matters:** It roots the saddle chapter's history of endurance (and discomfort) in a real room.
