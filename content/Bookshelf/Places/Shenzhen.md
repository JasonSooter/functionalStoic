## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqytr5kjprqpcj96r2wk6q)

> In 1980, Shenzhen was best known for its oysters.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Chapter 3 opens with Shenzhen as fishing and oyster-farming country in 1980, before Deng Xiaoping made it a special economic zone abutting British-ruled Hong Kong. Population rose from 300,000 (1980) to 18 million (2020); it made toys and clothes, then the iPhone from 2007, and became the 'Silicon Valley of hardware'. Wang visited often while living in Hong Kong, by ferry or subway, exploring urban villages with textile workshops by day and seafood griddles by night.
- **Why it matters:** Central case for the chapter's argument that technology is process knowledge held in a community of engineering practice. Also hub of BYD, DJI, Huawei and Luxshare.
