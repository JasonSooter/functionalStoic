## The Skyscraper That Ruined Paris — The B1M (2026)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=68JUxRH8AZo) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5c3psxvy2mgx4g4agqje5rg)

> Ok, this is the Tour Montparnasse.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The B1M calls it the first skyscraper built in Paris: 210 m tall with 120,000 m² of offices, disliked by Parisians and now set for a renovation to help it blend in. It tells how Senator Edgard Pisani picked Montparnasse, once an artists' haunt, for a modern office district around the overloaded Gare Montparnasse, and how the tower was built over metro line 6 from 1970 and finished in 1973. The skyscrapers of La Défense, beyond the ring road, serve as the contrast.
- **Why it matters:** The tower is the whole subject, and its reception explains why central Paris stayed low-rise.
