## MyCycle Keeps Growing, Tour de Farms Perfect Weather, Hughes for Everyone and More! — Bike Fort Collins (2025)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5d20v31shtrgphb7kdp56eb)

> This November, Fort Collins voters have the chance to shape the future of the Hughes property in west Fort Collins—a decision that will benefit our community for generations.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The newsletter backs Ballot 2H on the land's future: natural areas, recreation, a wildlife rehabilitation and education centre, and up to 35 acres for a community bike park. Bike Fort Collins is campaigning for it with partner groups.
- **Why it matters:** The vote decides what the land becomes; for the club, the bike park is the prize.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The text says only "west Fort Collins"; the pin is the GeoNames point for the former Hughes Stadium.
