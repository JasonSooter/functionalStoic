> Warren (Buffett) was playing golf at Pebble Beach with Charlie Munger (Berkshire Hathaway Vice-Chairman)

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** In a quoted anecdote Buffett is playing golf at Pebble Beach with Munger and Jack Byrne when they offer him $10,000 for a hole-in-one against a $10 stake. He declines, saying that indiscipline in small things leads to indiscipline in big things.
- **Why it matters:** The course is just where the bet happens; the lesson holds anywhere. It is a light setting.
