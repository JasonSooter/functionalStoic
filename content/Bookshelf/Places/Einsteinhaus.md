## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)
Source: [[Neither Here Nor There]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce7j6aahhzbzm0q7a74rhq)

> I tried also to go to the Albert Einstein museum, housed in his old flat on Kramgasse, one of the main arcaded streets. I walked up and down the street half a dozen times before I found the modest entrance to the building, tucked away between a restaurant and a ladies’ boutique.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bryson walks Kramgasse half a dozen times before finding the modest entrance to the Einstein museum in the physicist's old flat. The door is locked and dusty and no one answers, despite the brochure; he is struck that Bern does nothing to mark Einstein.
- **Why it matters:** A small comic scene that carries his verdict on Bern's sleepiness.
