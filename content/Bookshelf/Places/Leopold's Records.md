## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)
Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebvdsnz3f02p0n71nmna9j)

> In 1972, the world’s first public bulletin board system (BBS) appeared in the form of a coin-operated kiosk at the top of the stairs to Leopold’s Records in Berkeley.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The Community Memory kiosk, with a teletype linked by modem to a San Francisco computer, was placed under the store's physical bulletin board. The text quotes its flyer and describes a network rooted in neighborhood and space.
- **Why it matters:** It is the local-history counterexample to context-collapsing social media: a slow network that you had to travel to, grounded in place.
