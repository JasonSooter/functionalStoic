## Why swimming in the Rhine is allowed in Switzerland but banned in Germany — Franziska Müller, Simon Ormiston (2026)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce7rg69tcy77wardsq1870)

> His association says funding of up to €1 million is available for a pilot bathing area in the Spree Canal, where there is no longer shipping, but the project cannot proceed while swimming remains prohibited.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Berlin's Flussbad association wants a pilot bathing area in the Spree Canal, where shipping has ended, and says up to €1 million is available; the blanket swimming ban stops it.
- **Why it matters:** It is the article's German counterpart to Basel's Rhine swimming.
