## Charlotte's Web — E. B. White (1952)
Source: [[Charlotte's Web]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dr0ps63srz2ytrjxmm62m2)

> It was the kind of barn that children like to play in. And the whole thing was owned by Fern’s uncle, Mr. Homer L. Zuckerman.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Wilbur is sold to Fern's Uncle Homer and goes to live in the manure pile in the cellar of Zuckerman's barn, where he meets Charlotte, Templeton and the other animals. Over the summer and autumn Charlotte's webs make him famous, and people come from miles around to see him. Wilbur returns here from the Fair at the end and spends the winter and later seasons in the barn.
- **Why it matters:** Nearly all of the story happens in and around this barn. It is the home Wilbur fights to keep, and the cycle of seasons there frames the book.
- **Real vs. fictional:** fictional farm; real model
