## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e30grnx485fp6t31djttj5)

> Wuhan officials refused to call off a community feast that attracted a hundred thousand people only six miles away from the Huanan Seafood Market, where the coronavirus was already circulating.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Officials suppressed news of the virus to protect a political meeting and Lunar New Year; Li Wenliang died there and a hospital was built in eleven days. The author watched from Beijing.
- **Why it matters:** It is the origin point of the pandemic story and the 'battleground' later invoked for Shanghai, but the author is not there.
