## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)
Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebv1ap64qj0bqb3vjd0v1q)

> at an old Oakland theater in Grand Lake.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Odell sees Blindspotting at an old theater in Grand Lake the day before reading Vivrekar's thesis. The film's treatment of gentrification and the Collin/Miles stare-down shapes her argument about attention, bias and will.
- **Why it matters:** It is where the key film scene was encountered; the theater matters mostly as her neighborhood and the occasion for the argument.
