# Capri

The island and hilltop town in the Bay of Naples, Italy. The town's central square is the Piazza Umberto I, known as the Piazzetta.

## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5b44b6r68jrrpmypcgcybwn)

> I passed beneath an arch and stepped out into one of the loveliest squares I have ever seen. It was packed with German and Japanese tourists. The tears streamed down my cheeks.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 14. Bryson takes the ferry over from [[Sorrento]], finds the funicolare not running, and climbs from the quay past villas and up steep cobbled alleys until he comes out into the square. He takes a room at the Hotel Capri and describes "an infinitely charming little place of villas and tiny lemon groves". Its heart is the Piazza Umberto I, with cream-coloured buildings, café tables, a white church up wide steps at one end and a railed terrace at the other.
- **Why it matters:** It's the climax of his southern-Italy chapter and a joke at his own expense: after the climb, the perfect square is full of tour groups. ⚠️ This run read the Capri scene only up to Bryson's walk past the shops beyond the church; the next run will read on.
