## This Bridge’s Bizarre Design Nearly Caused It To Collapse — Practical Engineering (2025)

Source: [Original](https://practical.engineering/blog/2025/3/18/this-bridges-bizarre-design-nearly-caused-it-to-collapse) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebqhbsh1186h0ahb8s17pn)

> This is the Washington Bridge that carries I-195 over the Seekonk River in Providence, Rhode Island… or at least, it was the Washington Bridge.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The westbound span was closed in December 2023 after broken tie-down rods were found under the navigation span. It was then demolished. The video traces how a 1960s design compromise led to hidden, uninspectable structural elements.
- **Why it matters:** The whole piece is a case study of this one structure. Its design came from a fight over how it looked beside the 1930 arch bridge next to it, so the site is central.
