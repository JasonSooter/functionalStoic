- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** John Watson, drawing a bull-elk tag in a New Mexico lottery, drives up to his hunting unit and rides his cargo bike along the Continental Divide Trail to spike-camp with friends Kyle and Kim. After three days of walking into the wind they move deeper by bike, he shoots a bull, and he hauls about 250 lb of meat back to camp and the cars by bike over several trips along the CDT.
- **Why it matters:** The unit and the CDT trail through it are the arena for the whole story: the backcountry the bike gives access to and the route the loaded cargo bike has to climb out along.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The text does not name the real place; the pin is a best guess.
