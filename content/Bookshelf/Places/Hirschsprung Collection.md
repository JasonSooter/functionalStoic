## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5b441y6yxj7jj0yhjdrzh8f)

> The best museum of all I saved for last—the Hirschsprung Collection in Østre Anlaeg Park. Everything about it is wonderful.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Chapter 10, the end of a museum day that began at the National Museum and the Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek. Bryson describes 884 paintings, assembled over forty years by one man, almost all from the nineteenth-century Skagen school, packed into twenty or so small rooms. Their themes are simple: summer landscapes, friends at dinner, a sea view through an open window.
- **Why it matters:** It's his favourite discovery in Copenhagen and fits the chapter's theme: a modest, humane city that is best when it isn't showing off. He recommends it because it is "so terrific and so little visited".
