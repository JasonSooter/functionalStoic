## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)
Source: [[Neither Here Nor There]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce7t19z6npqqqce5htw2vd)

> I presented myself at the guardroom of the Questura and was directed upstairs to a waiting room, a bare cell with gray, flaking walls and a high ceiling.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After a child pickpocket takes his travellers' cheques as he leaves Florence, an unhelpful station policeman sends Bryson to the Questura. He takes a cab and waits upstairs in a bare cell of a room with flaking grey walls.
- **Why it matters:** It sets up the cheque-refund saga that follows him to Geneva.
