## Brazil is Taking America’s Skyscraper Crown — The B1M (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xpwGBR19D2U) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqttt7t62z2xj6y46ybzhn)

> Senna Tower is a prime example - just look at how small the plot of land is for what will be a 550 metre tall tower. Developers are trying to get as much floor space as possible onto this tiny site.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** Planned 550 m reinforced-concrete residential tower honouring Ayrton Senna, on a tiny waterfront plot. It is to have 700 auger-cast piles of at least 40 m, two 1,000-tonne tuned mass dampers, and wind-tested by RWDI; completion is expected in about seven years.
- **Why it matters:** It would be the world's tallest residential skyscraper and is the video's central subject, though it is a proposed building not yet built.
