## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e30mkrh99ba96150a23p9h)

> In Chiang Mai, these creative types gathered around a bookstore founded by a journalist. Nowhere Books had its first store in Taiwan before opening a second branch in Chiang Mai, offering books that can’t be bought on the mainland.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The bookstore stocks books not publishable in mainland China, a Chinese translation of the Whole Earth Catalog, a sticker of the Urumqi Road sign and 'Republic of Nowhere' passports. Similar shops have opened in New York and Washington.
- **Why it matters:** It is a gathering place and symbol of exile culture, tying the Shanghai protests to the diaspora.
