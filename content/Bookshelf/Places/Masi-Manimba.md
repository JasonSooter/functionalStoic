## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling
Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e325tyh0mehdfmm1yvj1qv)

> Just before sunset, we reached Masi-Manimba, a small town in the highlands, around six hundred meters above sea level.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After the road from Kinshasa past Kenge, the team reaches the town at sunset; the text explains its Kikongo name, 'the place where people sleep', tied to sleeping sickness and colonial blood sampling. Local health officials advise bringing nuns along to blood-drawing.
- **Why it matters:** Gives historical context for why villagers distrust blood sampling; otherwise a waypoint.
