## Hurricane vs. Tiny House — Practical Engineering (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce49hjb7f2ajsvp9tvw4xy)

> The OH Hinsdale Wave Research Laboratory is one of the largest facilities of its kind in the world. Since the 1970s, this lab has supported cutting-edge research into coastal engineering challenges like sediment movement, tsunami behavior, and wave-structure interactions.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The narrator watches two one-third-scale houses, at different elevations, hit by simulated storm surge in the lab's Directional Wave Basin. The lower one collapses; the raised one is barely damaged. The test answers the question raised by Hurricane Ian's flood claims in Fort Myers: how high is high enough.
- **Why it matters:** The experiment, and so the article, happens here.
