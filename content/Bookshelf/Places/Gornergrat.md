## The Best 30-Minute Train Ride in Switzerland — Simply Railway (2026)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5beyddq1rn30b90hyqyc4f7)

> Welcome to Gornergrat — 3,089 meters, the end of the line.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the video:** The ride up from Zermatt on the Gornergrat Bahn, past [[Riffelberg]] and Rotenboden (for the [[Riffelsee]]), ends here. From the top you can see 29 peaks over 4,000 metres. The Kulmhotel Gornergrat, first built in 1896, had two observatory domes added to its towers in 1996; there is also what the narrator calls Europe's second-highest post office, a museum with a virtual paragliding ride, and the "Golden Spot", a restored, gold-plated first-generation steam locomotive posed in front of the Matterhorn.
- **Why it matters:** The summit is the ride's destination and the video's payoff.
