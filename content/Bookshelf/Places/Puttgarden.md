> That route was then extended to a new ferry port at Puttgarden, bringing trains right up to the water's edge.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the video:** A railway reached the ferry port at Puttgarden in the 1960s, and trains were loaded onto ferries for the 45-minute crossing to Denmark. Germany will upgrade the Puttgarden to Lübeck route for the tunnel.
- **Why it matters:** It is the German end of the old ferry link the tunnel will replace.
