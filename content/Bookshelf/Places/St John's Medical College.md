## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling (2019)
Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqz9v8957vw9apyq6fb5xx)

> Part of the plan for our Asian trip was for me to be a visiting student at the St. John’s Medical College in Bangalore.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In 1972 Rosling did an elective here and found the fourth-year Indian students knew far more than he did, notably over an angiogram of a kidney tumor and the big Harrison textbook.
- **Why it matters:** The place where his 'West is best' worldview was first overturned.
