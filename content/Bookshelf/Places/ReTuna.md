## How Sweden’s ‘secondhand only’ shopping mall is changing retail — Mary-Ann Ball (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5d20jnw39se4h08tdgt4w86)

> ReTuna was established in 2015 as part of Eskilstuna’s climate and waste reduction strategy.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Residents drop unwanted items at The Return, a point built into the mall next to the city's recycling centre; they are sorted and passed to the mall's shops. The author studied the mall for 18 months and credits public funding and local-government backing for making the model work.
- **Why it matters:** The mall is the article's whole case for circular retail.

## What We’re Reading: The Success Of Sweden’s ‘Secondhand-Only’ Shopping Mall — RTBC Staff (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dcjh1skr0f1q8sxt85mx02)

> Not at ReTuna in Eskilstuna, Sweden, the world’s first mall to sell secondhand and repurposed items only.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The lead item in a Reasons to be Cheerful reading roundup presents ReTuna as the world's first mall selling only secondhand and repurposed goods, a stylish but socially meaningful space whose success reflects rising demand for pre-loved goods.
- **Why it matters:** The item's whole subject: a mall that works against the throwaway culture malls usually feed.
