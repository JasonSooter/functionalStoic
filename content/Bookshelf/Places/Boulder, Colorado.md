## For Modern Foragers, This Map Reveals Urban Abundance — Natasha Khullar Relph (2025)
Source: [[For Modern Foragers, This Map Reveals Urban Abundance]] · [Original](https://reasonstobecheerful.world/falling-fruit-urban-foraging/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebpfhcwjmfjmrvxwnecexc)

> “The City of Boulder sent me a bunch of data, including their tree inventory,” he says.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Ethan Welty moved to Boulder for graduate school and began seeing the city as a foraging ground, starting with apple trees in his backyard. A university project there on urban food mapping, using the city's tree inventory, led him to see trees as opportunities. This led to Falling Fruit.
- **Why it matters:** Boulder is where the idea originated. The piece traces the project to this city's trees and data.
