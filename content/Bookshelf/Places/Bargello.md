## The Lost Towers of the Guelph-Ghibelline Wars — exurbe (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.exurbe.com/the-lost-towers-of-the-guelph-ghibelline-wars/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edefsnnqy3qy5cpf0x1cmr)

> The tower on the right here is part of Bargello, the prison & police fortress, but it didn’t start that way.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The Bargello tower was built by a private family, sold to the city when the tower-height ban passed, and incorporated into the city prison fort. Its brick battlements are a later addition marking the shift from private to public fortress.
- **Why it matters:** It is the worked example of what happened to a private tower under Florence's ban, and it is the one tower allowed to stay tall.
