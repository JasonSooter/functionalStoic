## 88 football-loving kids celebrate their own summer fairy tale in Vörstetten — BZ editorial team (2026)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bey60kts3yfqa6t8qfq913)

> Recently, the sports field in Vörstetten was all about football.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** A Badische Zeitung report. The VfR Vörstetten youth department runs its second summer football camp for 88 children aged 5 to 14, with 20 coaches and 30 helpers. SC Freiburg goalkeeper Florian Müller visits and faces a child's penalty. The week ends with parents against children, a barbecue, and a night camp on the pitch in a large tent lent by the municipality and 18 small tents.
- **Why it matters:** The village sports field is the whole setting; it's a local-news piece whose point is the community around it.
