## Dept. of Speculation — Jenny Offill (2014)
Source: [[Dept. Of Speculation.md]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bfwhhsdveh0366hfbxrr5h)

> The daughter cries for Brooklyn.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The marriage, the baby and the affair all play out here: an apartment with a church across the street, a Rite Aid a block away where the colicky baby is calm under the bright lights, a later apartment on the eleventh floor. In the last sections the family leaves the city for the wife's sister's ramshackle house in the country, and "The daughter cries for Brooklyn."
- **Why it matters:** Brooklyn is the book's home ground, almost never described directly but always there; the move away from it is the novel's last turn. No street or neighbourhood is ever named, so the pin is the borough.
