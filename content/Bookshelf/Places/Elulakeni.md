## Peace Corps: The Great Adventure — Peace Corps (2002)
Source: [[Peace Corps - The Great Adventure]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crcemr8n71rpfqwqb33y95)

> There was no electricity at Elulakeni, so we unloaded the lumber in darkness, throwing it through the open back door of my woodworking shop, into a huge jumble.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Sam Birchall teaches woodworking at the school in Elulakeni; the story follows his trip for lumber and the overloaded truck's long ride back, ending at the school in the dark.
- **Why it matters:** The school is the destination and the reason for the whole lumber run.
