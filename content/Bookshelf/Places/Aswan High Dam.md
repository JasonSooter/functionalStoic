## Egypt is Building a New Nile — The B1M (2026)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bfxp51hpdedatxab8xs4nr)

> The first major change came with the construction of this: the Aswan High Dam.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the video:** Opened in 1970, the dam brought hydropower and steady irrigation but trapped the floodwater and silt that once fertilised the land downstream, so Egypt now spreads about a million tons of artificial fertiliser a year. The video sets it beside the upstream [[Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam]] and Egypt's plan to build farmland in the desert (the New Delta project, which the video doesn't locate precisely enough to pin).
- **Why it matters:** The dam is the first act of the video's story of Egypt remaking its river.
