## How to poop in the backcountry — B. Derrick Taff (2025)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dcm01yjyevrtqy384xctt5)

> In our own research, pending publication, we surveyed hikers on Colorado’s Mount Elbert. Up to 70% of those needing to poop ended up doing so in the backcountry despite the presence of a trailhead toilet.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The authors' field site: up to 70% of surveyed hikers who needed to defecate did so in the backcountry despite a trailhead toilet; a kiosk at the start of the summit trail offers free waste bags, and about 30% of those who defecated used one.
- **Why it matters:** Supplies the article's main data on behaviour and on how well free bags work.
