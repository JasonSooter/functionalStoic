## There Are Places in the World Where Rules Are Less Important Than Kindness — Carlo Rovelli (2018)

Source: [[There Are Places in the World Where Rules Are Less Important Than Kindness]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dr0kcxvhtr3pg5c94461r2)

> And then we arrive in Sao. A Sao that turns out to be completely unlike the one I was expecting.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Rovelli, staying at the Institute of Mathematics in Mbour in January, picks Sao off the map for its name and travels there by taxi, bus and Barri's battered Peugeot. He finds a scattered village of huts among baobabs where children crowd round, women pound millet, and the school head talks about his teaching and receives a donation in euros. Barri then drives him north along a dirt track to Route Nationale 2.
- **Why it matters:** It is the first stop of the 'real Africa' day trip whose later mosque scene in Mbour yields the title line; the village overturns his expectation of a dirty teeming settlement.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real village, identified by inference.
