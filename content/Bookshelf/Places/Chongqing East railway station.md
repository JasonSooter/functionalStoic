## China Built a City Sized Train Station — The B1M (2026)

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The station sits on a mountainside about 20 km from downtown, with 15 platforms over eight floors and a 120,000 m² roof on tree-shaped columns; it was built in 38 months with the help of construction robots.
- **Why it matters:** The station is the whole video, an example of the scale of China's high-speed rail build-out.
