> This is the famous San Antonio River Walk, a city park below street level that winds around the downtown district.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The narrator stands on the River Walk as a 650-ton crane is set up for a gate replacement. He explains how the channel is held at a constant level, and describes the project's pedestrian traffic, barricades and spotters during lifts.
- **Why it matters:** The project's constraint is that the River Walk can't be drained or disrupted; its role as a heavily visited downtown park shapes the logistics and the goal.
