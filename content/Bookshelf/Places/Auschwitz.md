## The Daily Stoic — Ryan Holiday (2016)
Source: [[The Daily Stoic]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edhh3kyd80d8xmt6ab0wcj)

> The psychologist Viktor Frankl spent three years imprisoned in various concentration camps, including Auschwitz.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Frankl was held for three years in camps including Auschwitz, losing his family and his work. He kept the ability to decide what his suffering meant, and found a chance to test his theories and serve others.
- **Why it matters:** The camp is the extreme setting that proves the point about inner freedom. Any severe prison could stand in, but Auschwitz makes the case starkly.
