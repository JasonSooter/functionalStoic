## Is The Line Really Dead? — The B1M (2025)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce3f4426c5x06ytfsbydcz)

> In case you’ve been living under a rock, this is The Line: Saudi Arabia’s plan for a massive linear city.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The video reviews The Line, planned at 170 km long, 200 m wide and 500 m tall: foundations with over 16,000 piles and dewatering wells, a first phase of only 2.4 km, and unanswered problems of supply, cooling and transport. It calls 2026 make-or-break.
- **Why it matters:** The project is the subject; the video's argument is that its ambition outruns its engineering.
