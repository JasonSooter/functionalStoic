## Not the End of the World — Hannah Ritchie (2024)
Source: [[Not the End of the World]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e31dwx7tq8x78m8snp5h40)

> When researchers looked at the preserved teeth of hunter-gatherers from Qesem Cave in Israel from 400,000 years ago they found pollutants from charcoal in them.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Teeth of hunter-gatherers from Qesem Cave, 400,000 years old, contained charcoal pollutants, which researchers suggest came from indoor fires for roasting meat.
- **Why it matters:** It is one piece of evidence that air pollution is ancient; the cave itself is minor to the argument.
