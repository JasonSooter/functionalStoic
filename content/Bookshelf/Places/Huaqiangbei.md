## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqyw9xc88bfz150fdd4st1)

> The center of Shenzhen is the Huaqiangbei mall complex. It is a giant bazaar spread across several buildings, with stalls filled not with spices or silks but wholesale electronics.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Wang describes the wholesale electronics bazaar with bins of wires, semiconductors and capacitors, and buying a single whale-print phone case when vendors normally sell by the hundreds. Engineers rummaged through its bins to prototype new products. On his last visit in 2021 vendors were selling more cosmetics than cables, a state media headline calling it 'Huaqiangbei Trades Computer Chips for Lipsticks', before it reverted to electronics.
- **Why it matters:** The concrete place where Shenzhen's component ecosystem is visible, and the book's barometer for whether hardware is commoditizing or the engineering state holds against consumer demand.
