Written #🗓️/2026/08/02. The problem is not the wishlist, it is that 33 books are open at once.

|---|---|

I cannot get to 40 by reading harder. I get there by not starting anything new and converting what is already open. A half-read book is the cheapest finish available.

### Four States, Not Two

- **`#📚/status/currently-reading`** — genuinely in rotation. Target **6–8 books, not 33**
- **`#📚/status/paused`** — deliberately set down, will return. The missing state, and where most of the shelf belongs. Honest, and it clears the decision without declaring failure. Mirror as a `paused` tag in StoryGraph
- **`#📚/status/did-not-finish`** — dead. Orientalism, Meditations for Mortals, Good Anger, Relativity, Slow Birding, Saved in Time
- **`#📚/status/reference`** — never "finished", only consulted. [[Great Short Books]], [[Engineering in Plain Sight]], Fluent Forever, Modern German Pronunciation, German - How to Speak and Write It. These should not sit on the active shelf at all

Protected exception: [[Meditations]] and 365 Tao are **daily readers that complete on 31 December**. I did exactly this with [[The Daily Stoic]] in 2024 — started 13 Jan, finished 25 Dec. Leave them running. Two books that land in December.

50. \#🗓️/2026/08/03 Seeing Like a State by James C. Scott #📚/status/to-read #media-type/ebook #priority/1
    - If what actually attracted me to _Timefulness_ was how landscape and infrastructure decisions play out over centuries, this is the book — and it is closer to my urbanism lane than to my geology one.
