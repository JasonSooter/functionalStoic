# Infrastructure

![[Attachments/Infrastructure Hero.jpg]]

The systems underneath everyday life, and how they actually work.

**Boundary with Urban Planning:** Urban Planning is how cities are shaped _for people_ (design, policy, public space). Infrastructure is how the systems _physically work_ (engineering, utilities, networks). Trams, transit, rail, and cycling infrastructure live here; policy and design arguments about them (traffic law, walkability, placemaking) stay in Urban Planning. The full rule is in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Branches

- [[Trains]]: long-distance passenger rail, freight, rail bridges and tunnels
- [[Cycling Infrastructure]]: bike networks, lanes, rail trails and towpaths
- [[Public Transit]]: trams, buses, subways, the Schauinslandbahn
- [[Water]]: supply, radio water meters, wastewater, stormwater, [[Canals and Towpaths|canals]]
- [[Energy]]: generation, storage, grids, the energy transition
- [[Roads and Bridges]]: streets, bridges, tunnels
- [[Telecom]]: fiber, cellular, low-power radio networks
