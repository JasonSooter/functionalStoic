## Walks to do

### Boswell's walk to Rousseau: the Areuse, Val-de-Travers (canton of Neuchâtel)

> [!quote] Rebecca Solnit, _Wanderlust_, quoting James Boswell (1764)
> When an adoring James Boswell came to visit Rousseau near Neuchâtel, Switzerland, in 1764, Boswell wrote, "To prepare myself for the great Interview, I walked out alone. I strolled pensive by the side of the River Ruse in a beautifull Wild Valley surrounded by immense Mountains, some covered with frowning rocks, others with glittering snow."

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://readwise.io/open/868537791) · [[Bookshelf/Highlights & Notes/Books/Wanderlust - A History of Walking|Wanderlust]]

**Decoding it:**
- **Where Rousseau was:** **Môtiers**, in the **Val-de-Travers**, where he lived 1762–65 after _Emile_ and _The Social Contract_ were banned; he left after his house was stoned in September 1765 ([Wikipedia: Môtiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B4tiers)). Boswell visited in **December 1764** ([jamesboswell.info](https://www.jamesboswell.info/biography/jean-jacques-rousseau)).
- **The "River Ruse"** is the **Areuse**, historically _La Reuse_, which runs down the Val-de-Travers past Môtiers to Lake Neuchâtel ([Wikipedia: Areuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Areuse)).
- ⚠️ In December, the "glittering snow" is plausible, but Boswell's _exact_ spot wasn't identified; he was somewhere in the valley near Môtiers.

**The walk to do today: Gorges de l'Areuse** (the river's lower stretch, below the Val-de-Travers)
- **Noiraigue station → Boudry station**, **11.5 km**, about **3 h**, rated easy; season **April–November** ([MySwitzerland](https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-ch/experiences/route/through-the-areuse-gorge/) · [Neuchâtel tourism](https://www.j3l.ch/en/V1571/things-to-do/sport-leisure/hiking/areuse-gorges)). Train at both ends, so no car needed. Kid-friendly length.
- **To walk Boswell's actual valley** instead, walk the Areuse upstream around **Môtiers** itself. ⚠️ No Rousseau museum or marked site in Môtiers was confirmed; the town's museums listed online are the absinthe museum and the regional museum (Maison des Mascarons).
- **Getting there from Freiburg:** by train via Basel. ⚠️ Journey time not checked.
