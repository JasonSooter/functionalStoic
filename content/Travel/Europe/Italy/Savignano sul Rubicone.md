# Savignano sul Rubicone

## Summary

Small town in **Emilia-Romagna**, halfway between **Cesena and Rimini** on the Via Emilia, where the Fiumicino river was officially declared the historical **[[Concepts/Rubicon|Rubicon]]** in 1933 (the town was renamed for it). The thing to see is the old **Roman consular bridge** on the Via Emilia, with a modern statue of Caesar beside it.

Manage expectations: the bridge is not from Caesar's time, the identification is contested (the Uso near San Vito is the main rival), and Savignano is an industrial town — the river has a reputation as one of the region's most polluted. This is a half-hour "stand on the spot" stop, not a destination.

## Why go

- Stand at the literal origin of an idiom used in every European language.
- Easy add-on to a **Romagna coast** trip: Rimini (Ponte di Tiberio, Augustan arch), Ravenna (Byzantine mosaics), San Marino, Cesena (Biblioteca Malatestiana).
- **Cycling angle:** the Via Emilia is flat and straight. Riding Rimini → Savignano → Cesena (~30 km) lets you cross the Rubicon the way Caesar's legion did — northbound-to-southbound, toward Rome. Riding to the Uso bridge at San Vito too covers both candidates.

## Getting there

- On the **Bologna–Rimini railway** — Savignano has its own station; regionals stop there.
- From Freiburg: Basel → Milan → Bologna → Savignano, roughly a long day by train _(estimate — check routing)_.
