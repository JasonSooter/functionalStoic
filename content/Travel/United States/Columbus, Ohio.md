# Columbus, Ohio

Ohio's capital and largest city. In David Byrne's _Bicycle Diaries_ he rides through a suburban office park and mall complex here at dusk and finds its landscaping eerily unreal.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7641d7sm8qsjtee7myrkc5j)

> I bike across a suburban industrial park, which brings me to the backside of a complex that includes a shopping mall and a simulated street filled with restaurants and some condos.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** At dusk under sodium-vapour lamps, Byrne glides through manicured parking lots and smooth lawns that feel "otherworldly". He argues that such landscaping is a visual description of a place rather than the place itself, and links its hidden cameras and guard-dog signs to the same puritan impulse he saw in Sweetwater.
- **Why it matters:** The unnamed complex is his example of suburban America built to imitate a town, set right after [[Sweetwater, Texas]] in the American Cities chapter.
