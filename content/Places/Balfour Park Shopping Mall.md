# Balfour Park Shopping Mall

Shopping centre in [[Highlands North]], Johannesburg. In Born a Crime it is where Trevor Noah and his friend Teddy are caught shoplifting, and where Teddy is arrested.

## At a glance
| Source | Author | Role | In one line |

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6z18rq03mrsskaewc6j5vzp)

> The Balfour Park Shopping Mall was a few blocks from my house. It’s not a big mall, but it has everything—an arcade, a cinema, restaurants, South Africa’s version of Target, South Africa’s version of the Gap.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 13 ("Colorblind"). With no money, Trevor and Teddy hang out at the mall and steal alcohol chocolates through the gate of a stationery shop. A mall cop sees Trevor with his arm through the gate. Both run, across the car park, past the petrol station and the fire station, to a hole in a fence. Trevor gets away but Teddy is caught. Later the principal shows Trevor the security video, and none of the white adults recognise him on it. The mall arcade is also where Trevor met his friend Tom.
- **Why it matters:** the chase leads to the chapter's point: on grainy camera footage the light-skinned Trevor reads as "white", while Teddy is arrested. It is the clearest scene in the book of how race decides who gets punished.
