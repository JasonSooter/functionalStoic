## The Theseus Paradox of Software — corgibytes.com (2018)

Source: [Original](https://corgibytes.com/blog/2018/01/15/theseus-paradox-software/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7f780qvr3yh83q2n8n8zf)

> Athenians had made promises to deliver gold to Delos.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** Athenians vowed an annual tribute to Delos honoring Apollo and sailed Theseus's ship there each year for centuries, about 200 km. The author uses the voyage and the ship's constant repair as an analogy for maintaining legacy software.
- **Why it matters:** Destination of the annual pilgrimage that the ship must remain seaworthy for.
