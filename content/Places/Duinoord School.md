# Duinoord School

Protestant primary school on Bentinckstraat in The Hague's [[Statenkwartier]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where the young Catholic Alexander Münninghoff is an outsider after his parents' break-up.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zme4jfbt5mqc4rhbvhzvt)

> Since Catholicism was all I’d known, I felt uncomfortable at the Duinoord School, where, much to my surprise, the other children didn’t make the sign of the cross at the end of the morning prayer.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After leaving Briva Latvija, Wera gets her son into the Duinoord School, "a bastion of Protestant Christianity on Bentinckstraat", whose kindly director Mr. Van Westering admits a Catholic boy. Teased for crossing himself, he learns to disguise the gesture, and the loneliness drives him out into the streets after school. After his father's side has him kidnapped from Germany in 1951, he is allowed back at the school, "which was at least familiar".
- **Why it matters:** A small place, but it marks the first time the boy's mixed inheritance (Catholic, German-speaking, Latvian-Dutch) sets him apart, a theme the whole memoir returns to.
