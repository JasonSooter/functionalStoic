- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The family house fills with tanks, vivaria, a wire-fronted animal room and a garden enclosure holding lungfish, parakeets, a cockatoo, bush babies, a gibbon and a woolly monkey. Jane cares for most of them.
- **Why it matters:** Domestic counterpart of the expeditions and where the animals are cared for; a lively but anecdotal setting.
