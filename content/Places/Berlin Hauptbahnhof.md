# Berlin Hauptbahnhof

Berlin's main railway station, north of the government quarter in [[Berlin]], built on the site of the old Lehrter Bahnhof. It appears in the reading as the arrival point of a European Sleeper night-train review and, as Lehrter Station, of the US Olympic team in _The Boys in the Boat_.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m87k3j9a5a9efdja9gxsghve)

> When they arrived in the city’s palatial old Lehrter Station, just north of the Tiergarten, that afternoon, they were stunned by the reception that greeted them.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On the afternoon of July 24, 1936, the team's train from Hamburg pulls into Lehrter Station. A brass band plays Sousa, Avery Brundage and the Duke of Mecklenburg exchange kisses, and the athletes march past a black locomotive emblazoned with swastikas into a hall where thousands of Germans have packed in to see them. Shorty Hunt felt "like a freak in a sideshow" as people pointed and said "zwei meter". They then board open-top military buses for the procession down [[Unter den Linden]].
- **Why it matters:** The boys' first, overwhelming taste of Berlin's staged welcome, the start of the book's Olympic chapters.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The old Lehrter Bahnhof was demolished after the war; today's Hauptbahnhof stands on its site.
