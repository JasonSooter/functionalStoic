# Cajon Pass

Mountain pass between the San Bernardino and San Gabriel ranges, southern [[California]]; the B1M video names it the steep crux of the Brightline West high-speed line.

## The $140BN Race to Build America's First High-Speed Railway — The B1M (2024)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GEFjL5KEE3c) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5d21er9fs3sw6df5h0d8grp)

> there’s one part of the route that’s likely to be especially challenging — the Cajon Pass.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the video:** Brightline West's 218-mile line would run from the Las Vegas Strip to Rancho Cucamonga, 96% of it in or beside the I-15 corridor. The Cajon Pass, between the San Bernardino and San Gabriel mountains, has grades steep even for freight trains, so the high-speed trains will have to slow there. The video still expects Brightline West to finish before California High-Speed Rail.
- **Why it matters:** The pass is the one named obstacle on the route the video calls America's best chance at true high-speed rail.
