## India's innovations are still changing the world – Bill Gates — Bill Gates

Source: [Original](https://www.gatesnotes.com/India-is-changing-the-world-with-innovation) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmr64zp1yq65xtgh74pmvw)

> During my trip I’ll get to see India’s DPI in action. I’ll visit an agricultural monitoring center in the state of Odisha where government officials use DPI to give farmers real-time guidance.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Gates previews his visit to Odisha, where he will see an agricultural monitoring center serving a registry of 7.5 million farmers and a pest-management service whose use cut crop losses by 90 percent. He will also visit a low-income community in Odisha where a women's construction program operates.
- **Why it matters:** Odisha is the state showcased as the working example of India's digital public infrastructure and urban poverty programs. Elsewhere in India the specific examples would differ.
