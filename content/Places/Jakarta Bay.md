## The $40BN Plan to Save The World’s Fastest Sinking City — The B1M (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B7PHRVQgTKM) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqxmqnjt28013m7kfhkdgf)

> They’re going to build a 32-kilometre sea wall here along Jakarta Bay.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The proposed $40BN Garuda-shaped sea wall and 17 artificial islands would be built along Jakarta Bay, first by strengthening existing dykes (by 2030) and then closing the wall into a reservoir by 2050. Critics warn it could turn the bay into a stagnant toxic lagoon and displace fishing villages.
- **Why it matters:** The site of the engineering megaproject that gives the video its title.
