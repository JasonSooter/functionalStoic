# Russian Compound

Historic compound in central Jerusalem that held a British-era prison. A setting in Colum McCann's _Apeirogon_: British-era prison in Jerusalem where two Jewish fighters died by grenade in 1947.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjr99n51q06mk12srhe56h)

> When the British took control of Mandatory Palestine they used the Russian compound in Jerusalem as a prison for members of the underground resistance.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The text describes the cold cells and execution room and tells how Moshe Barazani and Meir Feinstein, hours before execution in 1947, assembled a grenade hidden in hollowed oranges and died embracing.
- **Why it matters:** It is a historical aside on Jewish underground prisoners that echoes the book's themes of violence, death and chosen embrace.
