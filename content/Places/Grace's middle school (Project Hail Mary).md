## Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir (2021)
Source: [[Project Hail Mary]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/611060/project-hail-mary-by-andy-weir/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvsbybaj0nkwv6ancrdbba)

> So it was that with the apocalypse looming—possibly caused by an alien life-form—I stood in front of a bunch of kids and taught them basic science.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Grace teaches science in his cluttered mad-scientist classroom with a solar-system mobile, runs the beanbag lightning round, and is approached by Eva Stratt in the empty room after sixth period. Later he returns to teach his class about Astrophage and then storms out to confront Stratt.
- **Why it matters:** Where Grace's love of teaching and children is established; his motive for saving the world ("my children") comes from this room.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The novel never names the school or its city. Grace lives in San Francisco and the publisher's description calls him a junior high science teacher; pinned at the San Francisco city point as a best guess. Alternative: anywhere in the Bay Area.
