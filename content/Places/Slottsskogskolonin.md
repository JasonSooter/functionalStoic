# Slottsskogskolonin

Allotment-cottage colony beside the Slottsskogen park in Gothenburg, Sweden. In a LifeEdited post it is a model for small, cheap vacation homes.

## A Case for Building More Small, Inexpensive Vacation Homes — David Friedlander (2015)

Source: [Original](http://lifeedited.com/a-case-for-building-more-small-inexpensive-vacation-homes/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g16kbpqdn3m5ghhn5484jf)

> Each house is individually owned, but the land is rented by owners. Houses are typically vacated during the winter.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The post holds up the colony as an example of the Swedish kolonistuga: small cottages owned individually on rented land in the middle of the city, used in summer and left empty in winter.
- **Why it matters:** It is the post's lead example of affordable second homes.
