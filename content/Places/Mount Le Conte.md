## Hiking Tennessee’s Mount LeConte — Bob Doucette (2015)
Source: [Original](https://proactiveoutside.wordpress.com/2015/12/08/hiking-tennessees-mount-leconte/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1ngg0y73ywdx4fx2qk4z)

> LeConte’s summit was still a half-mile away, so we kept trucking.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** They top out at a giant cairn tucked in the trees with no summit view, then find overlooks down the trail. The summit is the goal of the hike, and Jen later reflects on the sadness of descending.
- **Why it matters:** The destination of the whole piece, 6,593 ft; the text also covers its geology and the surrounding tourist towns.
