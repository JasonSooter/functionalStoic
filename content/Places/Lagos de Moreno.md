# Lagos de Moreno

Colonial town in Los Altos de Jalisco, Azuela's birthplace. In The Underdogs the band carouses there before the convention.

## The Underdogs: A Novel of the Mexican Revolution — Mariano Azuela (1915)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1209983W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8za2axx0qsk0qkb91rs23)

> At last they reached Lagos. Blondie bet that he would make Demetrio laugh that evening.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Demetrio enters the bar El Cosmopolita with his staff; Blondie shoots at tables, wounds a waiter's ear with a bottle-shooting game, smashes the bar and tells the bartender to send the bill to Villa. He then shoots his way along the street toward the red-light district while Demetrio sings the song of the knife wound.
- **Why it matters:** A scene of unchecked brutality by a drunken soldiery, showing why civilians hate the revolutionaries.
