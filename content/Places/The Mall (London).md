# The Mall (London)

Ceremonial road in central [[London]] running up to Buckingham Palace. The Brompton book names it as the home the Brompton World Championship settled on after Barcelona, Blenheim Palace and Goodwood.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8cqw0j6g09h07trb9z5ah2b)

> Since then, the Championships have also taken place at the motor-racing track at Goodwood, and have now settled on the Mall in the centre of London outside Buckingham Palace.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 14. In 2006 Koos Kroon, the Spanish distributor, called from Barcelona asking to race the bikes. The first two Championships were held in Barcelona. By 2008 the event had outgrown the city, so Brompton moved it to Blenheim Palace in Oxfordshire and ran it from the factory. It then went to the Goodwood motor-racing track and finally to the Mall. Regional heats around the world (the USA, Mexico, Germany, Korea) send their male and female winners to London for the final.
- **Why it matters:** The race is there to prove that a small-wheeled folding bike can go fast, and holding it in front of Buckingham Palace makes the brand's community event part of the London scene. Barcelona, Blenheim and Goodwood are earlier stops on the same route and are folded in here.
