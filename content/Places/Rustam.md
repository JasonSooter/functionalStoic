## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6mv6c0zyh6z62p2ptg6f4)

> I arrived there at 7 p.m. and after a supper of fried eggs and dry bread (shades of Bamian!) I was given a charpoy outside a tea-house under an awning of cane-leaves and wrote till it got dark at 8 p.m., when I went to sleep with the entire male population of Rustam standing around in an awestruck circle.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After a 78-mile day over three passes in 102 degree heat, with swims in a river and sand-covered descents, she reaches Rustam, a tiny village far from anywhere, and sleeps outside a tea-house watched by the male villagers.
- **Why it matters:** Marks the end of her hot, enjoyable ride out of Swat and shows the simple village hospitality she meets.
