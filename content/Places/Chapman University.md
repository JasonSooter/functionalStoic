## Ray Bradbury on Storytelling, Friendship, and Why He Never Learned to Drive: A Lost Vintage Interview, Found and Animated — Maria Popova (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.brainpickings.org/2015/05/07/ray-bradbury-blank-on-blank-interview/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fzcfdg3epve8rpzvjt4z7w)

> Potts and her classmate Chadd Coates were driving Bradbury — a resolute, lifelong nondriver — from his home in West Los Angeles to their university, Orange County’s Chapman College, where he was about to deliver a lecture.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** In 1972 two journalism students drive Bradbury, who never learned to drive, from West Los Angeles to Chapman College for a lecture; the interview recorded on the way is the subject of the piece.
- **Why it matters:** The drive to Chapman is the occasion of the lost interview.
