# Jordan River

River running from the Sea of Galilee to the Dead Sea along the Israel–Jordan border. A subject in Colum McCann's _Apeirogon_: Sacred but depleted river, ten percent of natural flow; route of Costigin's 1835 boat journey.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjq7jqzy8mqa2rkp69yt7f)

> It is often a surprise to travelers that the River Jordan is, in so many places, not much more than a trickle.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The text opens its water sequence with the Jordan as a trickle, then reports that irrigation and dams leave it at about ten percent of natural strength, partly sewage. Costigin and the sailor follow it through rapids and portages from the Sea of Galilee toward the Dead Sea. Smadar wrote a school report on it, and Bassam hears a spiritual about it in US churches.
- **Why it matters:** The river is the connecting thread of the water chapters: sacred in Costigin's mind, depleted and polluted in fact.
