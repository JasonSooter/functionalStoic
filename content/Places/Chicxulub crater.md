# Chicxulub crater

A buried impact crater under Mexico's Yucatán Peninsula and the Gulf coast. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ it is confirmed as the dinosaur-killing impact site.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W)

> Yucatán Peninsula at Chicxulub, near the city of Progreso, about 600 miles due south of New Orleans.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Alan Hildebrand hears from a reporter about a huge ring formation under the Yucatán at Chicxulub, found by the oil company Pemex in 1952 and judged volcanic. He travels to the site and decides fairly swiftly that this is the crater; by early 1991 it is accepted by nearly everyone as the impact site, displacing Manson.
- **Why it matters:** It closes the search for the dinosaur-killing impact.
