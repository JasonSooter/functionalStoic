## Dubai Has a Sewage Problem — The B1M (2026)

Source: [[Dubai Has a Sewage Problem]] · [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0CWb9CVPShM) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce8z7wyx754f0rkzc9b0v1)

> In 2013, 30 percent of all waste arriving at this treatment plant in Jebel Ali was delivered by road. The poop snake was back.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the video:** The plant is the end of the line for tanker-delivered sewage. In 2013 a third of its waste arrived by road, queues stretched up to 24 hours, and some drivers dumped illegally; the planned deep sewerage tunnels will feed a new pumping station here.
- **Why it matters:** It is where Dubai's sewage problem becomes visible.
