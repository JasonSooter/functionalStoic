> Schulman began his hunt in Bryce Canyon, a dry, desert wilderness in Southern Utah, known for its spectacular geology.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** In 1939 Schulman began his search for the oldest trees in Bryce Canyon, where he found an 860-year-old ponderosa pine at 2,000 metres. He then moved on to Central Utah and found a 975-year-old pinyon.
- **Why it matters:** Minor: the first stop on Schulman's long search that eventually led to the White Mountains.
