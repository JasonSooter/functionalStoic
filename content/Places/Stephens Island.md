# Stephens Island

Small rocky island in Cook Strait, [[New Zealand]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ a lighthouse keeper's cat wiped out the Stephens Island wren there in 1894.

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m83dbh3dppdk7hmyaz32cxsj)

> In 1894, when a lighthouse was built on a lonely rock called Stephens Island, in the tempestuous strait between the North and South Islands of New Zealand, the lighthouse keeper

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The keeper's cat kept bringing him strange little birds, and specimens went to the museum in Wellington. A curator realised they were a flightless wren, a new species, and set off for the island at once, but by the time he got there the cat had killed them all. Twelve stuffed specimens remain.
- **Why it matters:** Bryson's example of an extinction that was not cruel so much as majestically foolish.
