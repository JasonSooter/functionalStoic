# Pera Palace Hotel

Historic Orient Express-era hotel in Beyoğlu, [[Istanbul]]. It is David Byrne's base in _Bicycle Diaries_ during the Istanbul trip whose music festival is cancelled and then revived.

## At a glance
| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bicycle Diaries | David Byrne | setting | Byrne's faded-grand base, where Atatürk's room is a museum and the festival's cancellation is announced. |

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7641zp9p8kyh7abb2rkrz3e)

> I stay at the Pera Palas Hotel, a slightly run-down joint that was once, in the days of the Orient Express, the height of elegance.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Byrne lists the hotel's famous guests, from Hemingway and Garbo to the spies Mata Hari and Kim Philby, and notes that Atatürk's room, number 101, is kept as a museum. His hosts meet him there, and a press conference in the hotel garden announces that the festival is cancelled.
- **Why it matters:** The hotel is the hub of the Istanbul chapter, the place he rides out from and back to.
