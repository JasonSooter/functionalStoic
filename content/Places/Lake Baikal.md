# Lake Baikal

Deep rift lake in southern Siberia, between Irkutsk Oblast and Buryatia. In _Last of the Breed_ it is the landmark the escaped pilot uses to work out where in Siberia he is being held.

## Last of the Breed — Louis L'Amour (1986)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL51048W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6xdskw9m4mzbwvb4wsksf2s)

> Lake Baikal he remembered well, as it is one of the most interesting bodies of water on the planet.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** From the first chapter Joe Mack believes he is "somewhere in the vicinity of Lake Baikal, in Siberia." On the run he recalls a lecture and its map: the lake some four hundred miles long and over five thousand feet deep, the Lena to the north, the Amur to the south, and the Yablonovyi, Stanovoy and Verkhoyansk ranges between him and the Bering Strait.
- **Why it matters:** Baikal is his mental anchor rather than a scene; reasoning from it, he concludes that he cannot cross Siberia before winter, which shapes the rest of the book.
