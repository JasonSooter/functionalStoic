# Pangbourne

Village on the Thames in [[Berkshire]], with the Swan inn by the river. In Three Men in a Boat it is where the friends give up in the rain, leave the boat with the boatman and slink off to the station.

## Three Men in a Boat — Jerome K. Jerome (1889)
Source: [[Exploration of Three Men in a Boat]] · [[Three Men in a Boat]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1793164W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gkzj5xcbwb48mg4p6466kg)

> We had deceived the boatman at Pangbourne.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On the rainy return the friends plan to put up at Pangbourne, imagine the miserable evening ahead and instead leave the boat and everything in it with the boatman at the Swan, creeping off to the railway station with the dog to catch a train to Paddington.
- **Why it matters:** This is where the river trip ends, not with a triumphant return but a sheepish retreat, which is the book's final joke on its heroes.
