# Avenida Insurgentes 432

Apartment address on Avenida Insurgentes in central [[Mexico City]]. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it is where Frida moves, with her spider monkey, after discovering Diego's affair with her sister Cristina in 1935.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)

Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m737j1wgxq53wn05hsz5qsk9)

> And early that year Frida abruptly moved out of her San Angel house and, taking her favorite spider monkey with her, went to live in a small modern apartment in the center of Mexico City at Avenida Insurgentes 432.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In Chapter 12, early in 1935, Frida leaves the [[Casa Estudio Diego Rivera y Frida Kahlo|San Ángel house]] for a small modern flat here. Diego furnishes it with blue leatherette and chrome to match a red set he gave Cristina, and Alejandro Gómez Arias recalls Frida's rage at seeing Cristina at a gas station across the street.
- **Why it matters:** It marks the first of the couple's many separations and Frida's first attempt at a life of her own.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real address; ⚠️ not pinned, because the avenue has since been renumbered and split (Norte, Centro, Sur) and the 1935 building is not identified.
