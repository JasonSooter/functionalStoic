# Golden Gate Park

Large urban park in [[San Francisco]]. In Stranger in a Strange Land (Robert A. Heinlein) it is where Mike first laughs and says he groks people.

## Stranger in a Strange Land — Robert A. Heinlein (1961)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL59688W)

> It was cold and windy out at Golden Gate Park but Mike did not notice it and Jill had learned that she didn't have to be cold or uncomfortable if she did not wish it.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On a cold, windy day Mike goes to the zoo at Golden Gate Park hoping to ask a camel why it is sour. In the monkey house he watches a capuchin beaten by a bigger one, who then beats a smaller one, and he bursts into uncontrollable laughter; Jill takes him home in an air cab, and he says he now groks people.
- **Why it matters:** A turning point of the novel: Mike finally understands why humans laugh, which starts his mission.
