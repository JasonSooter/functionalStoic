# Hudson, Wisconsin

River town on the St. Croix in western Wisconsin. The first Little Free Library was built there on Todd Bol's deck, as his Star Tribune obituary recalls.

## Little Free Library founder dies, leaving a legacy of helping cities 'one book at a time' — Jenna Ross (2018)

Source: [Original](http://www.startribune.com/little-free-library-founder-dies-leaving-a-legacy-of-helping-cities-one-book-at-a-time/497938831/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7ckg5ktx354c9wsf4dxbm)

> Using wood from his old garage door, Bol fashioned the first library-on-a-stick in 2009 on his deck in Hudson, Wis.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** The first Little Free Library was built on Bol's deck in Hudson from an old garage door, as a tribute to his mother. Neighbors reacted warmly at a 2010 garage sale, which led him to build more. Hudson is also where he lived and gave his last interview.
- **Why it matters:** Hudson is where the Little Free Library movement started, which is why the obituary dwells on it.
