## Peace Corps: The Great Adventure — Peace Corps (2002)

Source: [[Peace Corps - The Great Adventure]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL16050643W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5c3q5zxmq8m5cnnqm5rn8bd)

> My two-year assignment was to teach English and social studies at a boys' school in Erigavo, a village in northern Somalia.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In "Finding My Village" (Charles R. Baquet III, Somalia 1965–67), the author teaches at a boys' school built under British rule and shares a cement duplex with two other Volunteers. Goats destroy their garden, and library books and a volleyball net disappear. Decades later he returns as ambassador to help with a peace and reconciliation conference in Erigavo, where former students admit they took the items to test him.
- **Why it matters:** The town holds both the essay's frustrations and its late reinterpretation. The return to the same place turns the early failures into a story about trust.
