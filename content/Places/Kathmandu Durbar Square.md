# Kathmandu Durbar Square

Royal palace square in the old city of Kathmandu, Nepal. In _On the Hippie Trail_ it is the heart of the Hippie Trail's destination.

## On the Hippie Trail — Rick Steves (2025)
Source: [[On the Hippie Trail]] · [Original](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/rick-steves/on-the-hippie-trail/9781641717458/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kjf6rsmxxkw4vcaga0mvam)

> I lost myself in Durbar Square. This was a tangled, medieval-ish world of tall, terraced temples; fruit and vegetable stands; thin, wild, and hungry people praying, begging, and going through rituals;

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In Kathmandu they take a hotel on the square, climb its temples, see the erotic carvings and the living goddess Kumari on her balcony, and return at night to wander the square and climb a pagoda. Nearby are the hippie cafés of Pig Alley.
- **Why it matters:** The square is the heart of the trail's destination city.
