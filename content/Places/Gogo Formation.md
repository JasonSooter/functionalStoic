## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy8e0c52k6cy984na30t50)

> Later on that same trip, we wanted to film at a site in the central Australian desert called Gogo .

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The crew flies in by helicopter to a fossil site already picked over by London palaeontologists. Attenborough picks up an arthrodire scute within a yard of the landing bar, to the scientist's mock indignation.
- **Why it matters:** A memorable small anecdote about fossil hunting.
