# Dowa

District town in the hills of Malawi's Central Region, northeast of [[Lilongwe]]. In _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ it is where Kamkwamba's father traded, fought, and found God before moving the family north.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8dps3cvys0a955rmgtdxp4r)

> This was when he lived in Dowa, a small town southeast of Masitala perched high in the brown hills.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Kamkwamba's father keeps a stall in the Dowa market under Banda, drinks hard and fights. On Independence Day he brawls with police at a Robert Fumulani concert at Dowa District Hall and spends the night in jail. Later, after nearly killing a man, he faces trial. The prosecutor, church deacon Mister Kabisa, offers to drop the case if he turns to God. He converts, quits drinking, courts William's mother at the market, and moves the family north to Masitala. The Dowa Highlands, "which separated us from the lake", later rise beyond William's bird-trapping grounds.
- **Why it matters:** Dowa is the father's turning point: the brawler becomes the sober, religious farmer whose decisions shape William's childhood. If it were set elsewhere, little would change. What matters is the life he leaves behind.
