## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6n9r4gkdt2sp20mtk520z)

> After ten miles I reached this village, to which a jeep penetrates twice weekly, and was sadly back in civilisation – if one may so describe twenty wooden huts plus a PWD rest-house with no mod cons whatever.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** She is blocked by a landslide and stays at the PWD rest-house in Naran, a fishing centre, with a Karachi family. She gets invited by Mrs Haddow of Delhi to stay in Delhi.
- **Why it matters:** Return to a village with road traffic.
