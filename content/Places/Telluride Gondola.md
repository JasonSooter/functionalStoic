# Telluride Gondola

Telluride Gondola is the free gondola linking Telluride and Mountain Village, Colorado. It turns up in Walkable City (Jeff Speck): It is Speck's example of effective small-scale transit connecting two walkable districts across a geographic obstacle.

## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)

Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbm214h9yxxtxj9ajxv63d)

> The former, such as the gondola connecting the town of Telluride, Colorado, with Telluride Mountain Village, can be powerfully effective at both reducing car trips and improving walkability.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The gondola carries more than 2 million riders a year at one-fifth the cost per passenger of local bus service, many of them low-wage workers. Speck presents it as a small nodal transit system that works.
- **Why it matters:** It is Speck's example of effective small-scale transit connecting two walkable districts across a geographic obstacle.
