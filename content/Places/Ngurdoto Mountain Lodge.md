# Ngurdoto Mountain Lodge

Conference hotel about thirty kilometres outside Arusha, in northern Tanzania. In _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ it hosts the 2007 TED conference where William Kamkwamba gets a standing ovation.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8ebddasrda013wpeep62rzx)

> The conference was held at the Ngurdoto Mountain Lodge about thirty kilometers outside Arusha.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On his first flight William lands in Arusha, where the roads look like Malawi's "but unlike Malawi, Arusha had trees", and the shuttle driver points out [[Mount Kilimanjaro]]. At the conference Tom Rielly shows him a laptop, Google and, on Google Earth, a photo of [[Wimbe]] "taken from a camera in outer space". On stage he answers in broken English, "And I try, and I made it," and the audience stands: "I was now among colleagues." Investors he meets there pay for his schooling and the next windmills.
- **Why it matters:** This is the hinge of the memoir, where a village experiment becomes a world story. Everything after it, from Lilongwe schools to America, follows from this room.
