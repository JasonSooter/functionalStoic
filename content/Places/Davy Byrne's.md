# Davy Byrne's

Historic pub on Duke Street, off Grafton Street in Dublin, known from Joyce's _Ulysses_. In _The Country Girls Trilogy_ Kate and Baba share one Pernod there, hoping to be bought drinks.

## At a glance

|---|---|---|---|

## The Country Girls Trilogy — Edna O'Brien (1986)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL69252W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75vamdctabwyqfaqkr2k8)

> Then we went to Davy Byrnes’s cocktail bar and ordered a Pernod between us.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After the fortune-teller in Donnybrook, Baba and Kate go to the cocktail bar with money for only one Pernod. Baba is sure 'some moron' will buy them a drink; instead a barboy from Tipperary brings beer, and they talk to a lame country boy from Oranmore who is bound for England.
- **Why it matters:** A minor scene that shows how poor and hopeful the girls are in Dublin. Its literary fame adds to Kate's habit of measuring life against books.
