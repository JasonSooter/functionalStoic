# Jenin

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjq1bat1kgrmz7y76vms9d)

> On the day of the Israeli army’s departure from Jenin, in 1996, in the wake of the Oslo Accords, Bassam stood in the central square amid the waving flags and the songs and the loudspeakers and the dancing and the celebratory wailing.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In 1996 Bassam stands in Jenin's central square as the Israeli army leaves after Oslo. Young men hand olive branches to soldiers, strangers embrace, and that evening he sweeps up the festival refuse with a borrowed mosque broom.
- **Why it matters:** The scene is the book's moment of hope, set against the later failure of Oslo; it places Bassam two years out of prison and a year before Abir's birth.
