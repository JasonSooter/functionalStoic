# Allen Street

Busy north–south avenue on Manhattan's [[Lower East Side]], with a tree-lined median. In _Happy City_ its median surprises Montgomery's researchers by lifting walkers' moods despite the traffic.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m83qjn22ktsdck6ns7t4bnp0)

> We were even more surprised by what happened to the people we led out onto the median of Allen Street, a loud and congested arterial road nearby.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On the "emotional tour" from the [[BMW Guggenheim Lab]], volunteers felt happiest at a restaurant wall with painted brick and planters, and on Allen Street's median lined with mature maples, despite the noise and congestion around them.
- **Why it matters:** It suggests even a thin strip of greenery in a hostile street raises happiness, which supports the book's call for small nature interventions everywhere.
