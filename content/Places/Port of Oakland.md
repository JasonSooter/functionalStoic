## Why Aren’t America’s Shipping Ports Automated? — Priceonomics (2015)
Source: [Original](http://priceonomics.com/why-arent-americas-shipping-ports-automated/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gm1an6jfdmm88c9fba1d3r)

> The massive cranes that sit at the Port of Oakland are veritable money-printing machines.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article opens at Oakland, where ships from Asia dock and gantry cranes unload about 40 containers an hour. It argues the port is technologically antiquated, with every gantry crane, transport vehicle and stacking crane operated by people, and explains how union agreements and costs hold back automation.
- **Why it matters:** Oakland is the American case the whole piece is built around.
