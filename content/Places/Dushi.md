- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After eleven hours and ninety miles by truck, she reaches the Governor's primitive house in horse-breeding country, hearing about Buzkashi. She shares a room with her host and eats a banquet that aggravates her rib.
- **Why it matters:** The Governor here sends her next day to hospital and looks after Roz while she recovers.
