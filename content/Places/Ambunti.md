## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx3cj6pmtctpxwg9m6hg24)

> So in April 1971, the three of us, together with Keith Adam, a film director from the ABC who had made all the arrangements on our behalf, flew in to Ambunti.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Ambunti, on the middle Sepik, is the base from which the patrol is organised. The team meets Laurie Bragge, waits a week while carriers are recruited, and travels by canoe to cult-house villages.
- **Why it matters:** Starting point of the exploratory patrol and the chapter's main journey.
