## The Joyful, Illiterate Kindergartners of Finland — Timothy Walker (2015)

Source: [Original](http://taughtbyfinland.com/the-joyful-illiterate-kindergartners-of-finland/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy940r22675w5fpvjxs41k)

> Approaching the school’s playground that morning, I watched as an army of 5- and 6-year-old boys patrolled a zigzagging stream behind Niirala Preschool in the city of Kuopio, unfazed by the warm August drizzle.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author spends a morning at a Finnish public kindergarten: children dam a stream in the mud, hold Morning Circle, play at stations such as a pretend ice-cream shop, and flip through books without decoding them. The visit is the evidence for the claim that Finland lets young children learn through play.
- **Why it matters:** The preschool is the concrete scene against which the article contrasts American academic kindergartens.
