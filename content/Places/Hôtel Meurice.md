# Hôtel Meurice

Palace hotel on the Rue de Rivoli, Paris, once the German occupation headquarters. In Tom Hanks's 'A Junket in the City of Light' it is where Rory's press tour collapses.

## Uncommon Type — Tom Hanks (2017)
Source: [[Uncommon Type]] · [Original](https://penguin.co.uk/books/1114786/uncommon-type/9781786091338.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5v2bcgxspbp0wek47nk37)

> “Say what?” Rory was still a bit woozy, having taken advantage of the Hotel Meurice’s bar until just four hours ago.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 'A Junket in the City of Light' Rory Thorpe wakes in his suite after a late night in the Hotel Meurice bar to learn that the press tour is cancelled. He notes the hotel's renovation and its past as the headquarters of the German occupation staff.
- **Why it matters:** The hotel is where the tour collapses and Rory is left alone, and its Nazi past is part of his running joke about Europe's grand hotels.
