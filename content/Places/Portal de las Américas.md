# Portal de las Américas

TransMilenio bus terminal in the Kennedy district of southwest [[Bogotá]], Colombia. In _Happy City_ it is where Charles Montgomery ends a bus ride across the city and starts to see how the system serves the poor.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m84c7vz65333pj7c71c16h4r)

> This was Portal de Las Américas, a transit hub appointed with the flair of a bullet-train station.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Chapter 10, "Who Is the City For?". Montgomery slips out of his host's apartment and rides TransMilenio along Avenida Caracas about ten miles to the end of the line, in twenty minutes, then takes a pedicab into the poor barrio of El Paraíso. The system took the best road space from cars and was given a high-status look; a rider he meets at the Portal, Fabien Gonzáles, says his commute fell from two hours to 45 minutes.
- **Why it matters:** The ride is the way into the chapter's case that Peñalosa's Bogotá spent its scarce public resources where most people would benefit, starting with fast buses for those who cannot afford cars.
