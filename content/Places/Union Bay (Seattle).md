# Union Bay (Seattle)

Bay at the western end of Lake Washington beside the [[University of Washington]] in [[Seattle]], reached from campus across the Montlake Cut. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is where Joe Rantz and Joyce spend an idyllic canoe afternoon in April 1935.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m86yej1nyg4nfdpzq1anc0dp)

> There he stripped off his shirt, helped her into a boat, and paddled briskly across the Cut.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On the first warm day of spring 1935, Joe and Joyce are among the first to rent a canoe from the university canoe house. He paddles across the Cut to the lily pads and beaver lodges on the south side of the bay, lets the canoe drift and falls asleep, then plays guitar and sings; they talk about marriage and a home until the sun sinks behind Capitol Hill. Both would remember the day "well into old age."
- **Why it matters:** A rare moment of ease in a book of hardship: the same water Joe fights in the shell becomes a place of rest and love, and the good mood carries him to his father the next day.
