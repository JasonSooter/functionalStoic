## How Ordinary People Can Save Their Country — Ariel Dorfman (2020)

Source: [Original](https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/archive/2020/10/voting-lessons-from-a-not-so-distant-land/616597/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8xbrrvs0n7jj3y1dw2fce)

> I am not describing the current U.S. election, but a plebiscite in Chile 32 years ago, which would determine whether General Augusto Pinochet, the country’s dictator since the coup of September 1973, would remain in power for another eight years.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The essay uses Chile's 1988 plebiscite as a parallel to the 2020 US election. Dorfman, exiled by Pinochet, returned with his wife to vote on October 5, 1988, with 90 percent turnout, and 56 percent voted to oust Pinochet. The dictator, cowering in the presidential palace, was isolated when the air force, national police and conservatives accepted the result.
- **Why it matters:** Whole-country reference is the subject of the argument; no specific site is dwelt on.
