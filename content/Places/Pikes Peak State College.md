## Free college in Colorado? It’s covered if you’re interested in teaching, firefighting, law enforcement, construction or forestry — Jenny Brundin (2023)

> One of the community colleges that hopes to benefit is Pikes Peak State College in Colorado Springs.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article uses the college as its main example of a school hoping to benefit from Career Advance Colorado. President Lance Bolton calls it a remarkable opportunity and describes its construction, law enforcement, fire science and early childhood programs. He says local police and fire chiefs report significant shortages in Colorado Springs.
