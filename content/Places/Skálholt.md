# Skálholt

Historic bishop's seat in south Iceland. In Nordic Tales it is the haunted bishop's house where a fearless boy wrestles a goblin.

## Nordic Tales — Chronicle Books (2017)

Source: [Original](https://chroniclebooks.com/products/nordic-tales) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fyd9a2h9kdcj36me65a2fs)

> Once he came to a cottage, where he slept the night, and there the people told him that the Bishop of Skálholt was just dead.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 'The Boy Who Did Not Know What Fear Was', the boy reaches Skálholt after the bishop's death and stays alone at night in the house the residents leave after twilight. He wrestles a goblin that pours out a chest of gold, defeats it at dawn and marks the spot with crosses, so people can live there again.
- **Why it matters:** The real bishop's seat gives the Icelandic tale a concrete, church-haunted setting; the story's ghost-laying only works in a grand, emptied house.
