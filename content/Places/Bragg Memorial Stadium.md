# Bragg Memorial Stadium

Football stadium of Florida A\&M University in Tallahassee. In How to Be an Antiracist (Ibram X. Kendi) it is where the chapter on colour opens, at a Marching 100 halftime show.

## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fye0rx58ehyqyjdddpykd8)

> My arms flailed sluggishly as I stood on the highest of the seven hills in Tallahassee, Florida.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The chapter on Color opens at a September 2000 FAMU game against Morgan State, where freshman Kendi watches the Marching 100 with his roommate Clarence. The scene frames his reflections on colourism, his own coloured contact lenses and his dating habits, and the chapter returns to the halftime show at the end.
- **Why it matters:** The band's show is his picture of Black unity across skin tones, the counterpoint to the colourism he confesses.
