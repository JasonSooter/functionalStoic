## Have archaeologists proven the ancient tale of mass suicide in the Judaean desert or twisted science for political end? — Eric H Cline

Source: [Original](https://aeon.co/essays/decoding-the-ancient-tale-of-mass-suicide-in-the-judaean-desert) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5rjdr387v8p86k0hahs8s)

> In 73 or 74 CE, 960 Jewish zealots – men, women and children – committed suicide on top of the mountain of Masada by the Dead Sea in Israel rather than be captured by the Romans.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Masada is the flat-topped desert mountain fortress where, per Josephus, Sicarii rebels held out against Rome before a supposed mass suicide. Yigael Yadin excavated it in 1963-65, finding two Herodian palaces, storerooms, cisterns, bodies and scroll fragments. Cline examines whether the finds prove Josephus and whether Yadin's reading served a nationalist agenda. Today it is a major tourist site reached by the Snake Path or cable car.
- **Why it matters:** The entire essay is about this site, its archaeology, and the dispute over what happened there.
