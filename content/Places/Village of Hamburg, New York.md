## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)
Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbjxhfck05qrnxynayweqa)

> This is what happened in the Village of Hamburg, New York (population roughly ten thousand), where Mayor John Thomas was being told by NYDOT to accept three major road widenings that would have removed parallel parking, increased travel speeds, and generally trashed his town center.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Mayor John Thomas, working with pedestrian advocate Dan Burden, refused the state DOT's road widenings until a new design with public participation was produced. The result is a two-lane Main Street with bikeways and parking that the DOT now showcases.
- **Why it matters:** It is Speck's model of a small town beating the state DOT through leadership. A different town would serve the same point, but the example shows that even small places can win.
