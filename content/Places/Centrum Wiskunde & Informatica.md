## Python: The Documentary | An origin story — CultRepo (formerly Honeypot)
Source: [Original](https://youtube.com/watch/?v=GfH4QL4VqJ0) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dckssrw7hk72n0m7t55kqs)

> So this is the office where Guido and I were holed up when he created Python.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the video:** The documentary's origin story starts at CWI in the 1980s, home of the ABC language project. After ABC was dropped, a Christmas-holiday project there became Python; colleagues at CWI were its first users, and it was released from there via Usenet.
- **Why it matters:** The birthplace of the language; the whole first act of the film is anchored here.
