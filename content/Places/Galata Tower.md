# Galata Tower

Medieval stone tower above the Golden Horn in Istanbul's Beyoğlu district. In _On the Hippie Trail_ Rick and Gene climb it at sunset and look east to Asia.

## On the Hippie Trail — Rick Steves (2025)

Source: [[On the Hippie Trail]] · [Original](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/rick-steves/on-the-hippie-trail/9781641717458/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kjec866ngxk024ax2jw7vf)

> we ascended the Galata Tower for a fantastic view of the Golden Horn, Bosphorus, and a sprawling metropolis with millions of people where Asia meets Europe. Gene and I gazed eastward across the water and said to each other, “Asia.”

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After a Turkish bath and a walk across the Galata Bridge, they climb the tower at sunset for the view over the Golden Horn and the Bosphorus, and look east across the water towards Asia.
- **Why it matters:** The symbolic moment of looking from Europe to the road ahead.
