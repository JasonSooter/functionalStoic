# Khonuu

Village on the Indigirka in Sakha (Yakutia), Russia, the centre of Momsky District. In _Last of the Breed_ its airfield is where Joe Mack, Botev and Borowsky free the dissident Yakov from the KGB.

## Last of the Breed — Louis L'Amour (1986)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL51048W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6ycpbyh0kacdcw9frf1rgyj)

> Khonuu? It was a town on the Indigirka, and Yakov was a prisoner there.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** A downed helicopter pilot lets slip that the KGB is holding Yakov "at the airfield", near Khonuu. Though he has avoided populated districts all along, Joe Mack runs north at dawn, sees "a city against the sky and a river between", crosses a bridge and, heading for the airfield, meets Botev and Borowsky, who have come for the same purpose. Together they free Yakov and take the helicopter and its pilot.
- **Why it matters:** The one time Joe Mack walks into a town by choice: repaying Yakov's help turns the fugitive into a rescuer, and the stolen helicopter carries him on into the Chersky country.
