# Wimbe

Village and trading centre east of [[Kasungu]] in central Malawi. It is William Kamkwamba's home in _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_, together with the family's village of Masitala just down the road.

## At a glance

| The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind | William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer | setting | The home world of the memoir: the family farm at Masitala, the trading centre, Chief Wimbe's yard and Wimbe Primary School. |

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8dprzxc715tk3rvpmdjrvhh)

> We traveled four hours north to the Wimbe trading center, where my relatives were waiting to greet us. They helped us move down the road to Masitala village and into a one-room house near Uncle John.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The family comes from [[Dowa]] to Masitala, where the father turns farmer on a plot from Uncle John: "This is where my father became a farmer and my childhood began." The trading centre is the boys' world. It has Chief Wimbe's house with petitioners under the blue gums, the Ofesi Boozing Centre, whose cartons become toy trucks, the Iponga Barber Shop, the thatched "video show", and the bins where William scavenges batteries for radio repairs. He sits the Standard Eight exam at Wimbe Primary School, where Muluzi had made empty promises in 1999. In the famine the chief's blue gum grove fills with starving travellers: "The blue gum grove had become a patchwork of Gilbert’s family’s bedding."
- **Why it matters:** This is the village whose lack of power, money and food drives the whole memoir. It is the place the windmill will be built for, so the story only works here.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real. ⚠️ Masitala itself isn't pinned: GeoNames lists two Masitalas in Kasungu District, and neither clearly matches "down the road" from Wimbe, so the pin is on Wimbe village.
