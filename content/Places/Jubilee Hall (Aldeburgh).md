# Jubilee Hall (Aldeburgh)

Small concert hall in Aldeburgh, Suffolk, home of Britten's festival; in _Life on Air_ Attenborough lectures there on Balinese music.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx3b6r1q7dp8b2wpaxx40s)

> I walked out on to the stage of the Jubilee Hall at Aldeburgh confidently enough.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Attenborough lectures on Balinese music at Britten's Aldeburgh Festival and is disconcerted to find Britten and Peter Pears in the front row. Britten is delighted afterwards that the Prince of the Pagodas gamelan borrowing was recognised.
- **Why it matters:** A short but memorable anecdote scene; the place itself is incidental to the story.
