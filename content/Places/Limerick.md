# Limerick

City on the River Shannon in the west of Ireland. In _The Country Girls Trilogy_ it is the nearest big city to Caithleen's village, where Mr. Gentleman takes her to buy her convent uniform.

## The Country Girls Trilogy — Edna O'Brien (1986)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL69252W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75n9xe4dq4emmy9fr2z29)

> I said that I was going to Limerick and he said to sit in.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Before the convent, Caithleen rides to Limerick with Mr. Gentleman, buys her gym frock, rides a lift for the first time and buys presents for everyone. He gives her lunch in a city hotel, ordering little chickens and wine, and asks her about kissing. Later, on 'the Limerick road' in falling snow, he kisses her for the first time; the bus to Limerick passing the gate is what she watches when she is trapped at home.
- **Why it matters:** Limerick is the girls' first taste of the city and the start of the Mr. Gentleman affair. It is a modest setting, but it marks how near and yet how far escape is.
