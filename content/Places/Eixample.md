> The test area will be in the Eixample district, which has a famous gridded design and officials believe is the right place to start.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Barcelona plans a prototype car-free superblock in the Eixample, routing traffic around a neighborhood to cut traffic by 21% and address 3,500 premature deaths a year linked to air pollution.
- **Why it matters:** The piece frames it as a city using the idiosyncrasies of its own grid design for change; it is the lead item of a short newsletter roundup.
