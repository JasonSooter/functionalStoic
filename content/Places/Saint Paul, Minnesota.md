## Minimalism For All — David Friedlander (2015)
Source: [Original](https://lifeedited.com/minimalism-for-all/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ga13cpf16zew23g6xv3ffe)

> I also was gobsmacked that we seemed to be the only family living downtown St. Paul

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** After leaving a big suburban house near Rochester and a year in Nicaragua, the family moves into a small downtown apartment. The author is surprised to be the only family living downtown and lists the museums, parks, river, library and skyways within walking distance.
- **Why it matters:** The walkable downtown is the reason for the family's small-space, slowed-down life and for the blog itself.
