# Würzburg

Episcopal city on the Main in Lower Franconia, Bavaria. In _Germany: Memories of a Nation_ it is where the sculptor Tilman Riemenschneider worked and was ruined after the Peasants' War; in _The Son and Heir_ it is where the author finds his mother living in poverty in 1969.

## Germany: Memories of a Nation — Neil MacGregor (2014)
Source: [[Germany - Memories of a Nation]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20201162W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75w5xbmae049afena234c)

> In 1525, Würzburg was the site of one of its most important episodes.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 12 follows Riemenschneider to Würzburg, where he settled in 1483 and rose to mayor and councillor. In the Peasants' War of 1525 the council opened the gates to the rebels while the prince-bishop withdrew to the fortress above the city. When the revolt was crushed the bishop returned in triumph, and Riemenschneider was arrested and lost his patrons.
- **Why it matters:** Where the sculptor's career ended, and the root of the later myth of Riemenschneider the rebel that Thomas Mann and both post-war German states took up.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zmznspcgdswrzzhdz23k3)

> Poverty distorts a person, physically and mentally. I understood this as soon as I walked into that cold, dark cave where my mother had been living with her monstrously fat husband for years.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In early summer 1969 the author and his fiancée Ellen hitchhike to Bavaria for a wedding at [[Mergentheim Castle]] and take a taxi out of the old town to "the poor neighborhoods on the banks of the Main", where Wera, now Frau Bauer, lives with her husband Siegfried and their four-year-old Monika on an unlit dirt road. The walls are mouldy from the river's floods and there are no windows. He confronts her over his unanswered letters, learns that Tatiana has been arrested in Frankfurt for heroin, and leaves feeling nothing but "a kind of cold armor".
- **Why it matters:** The first meeting with his mother since childhood shows the author what the family's war over him cost her, and it is the low point from which their slow reconciliation in [[Höchberg]] begins.
