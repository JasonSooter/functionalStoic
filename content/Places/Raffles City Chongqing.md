## Chongqing, the world’s largest city – in pictures — Alessandro Gandolfi (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.theguardian.com/world/gallery/2025/apr/27/chongqing-the-worlds-largest-city-in-pictures) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2zrya40nd37bz3gh3w6rf)

> The point where the Yangtze and Jialing rivers meet. The square is at the base of the Raffles City skyscrapers in Yuzhong district

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Photos show the confluence of the Yangtze and Jialing rivers from a square at the base of the Raffles City skyscrapers, and from a 250-metre observation deck with a glass floor.
- **Why it matters:** It is the vantage point repeatedly used to show the city's river geography and skyline.
