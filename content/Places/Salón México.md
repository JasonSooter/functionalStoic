# Salón México

Former working-class dance hall in central [[Mexico City]], which gave Aaron Copland the title of _El Salón México_. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it is where Frida goes dancing with friends and watches the couples swirl.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)
Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m737j63e17g4fsr31h1tve42)

> and I would go dancing at the Salón México, a popular working-class dancing place. I would dance with Cristina. Frida watched.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In Chapter 13, among Frida's evenings at circuses, street theatre, movies and boxing, Jean van Heijenoort recalls going dancing here with Frida and Cristina. He danced with Cristina while Frida sat watching with her half-smile, absorbed in the music that inspired Copland.
- **Why it matters:** A small scene, but it shows Frida's taste for popular _la raza_ culture; it would read much the same at any city dance hall.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real venue, since closed; ⚠️ not pinned, as no standard source gives its location.
