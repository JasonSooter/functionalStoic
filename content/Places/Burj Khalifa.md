## Why the Jeddah Tower Won’t be as Tall as You Think — The B1M (2026)
Source: [[Why the Jeddah Tower Won’t Be as Tall as You Think]] · [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vVfEs9wrW98) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce8r0qh7h21fatmf5cgnm7)

> the Burj Khalifa holds the record for the biggest vanity height of any building on earth.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** In a video about vanity height, the Burj Khalifa is the tallest building by every measure and also holds the record for the biggest vanity height: 242 metres between its top mechanical floor and the spire tip. Dubai used it to remake itself as a skyscraper city.
- **Why it matters:** It is the benchmark that the Jeddah Tower is measured against.
