# St Helena

Remote island in the South Atlantic. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ Edward Fenton plans to seize it and make himself king, and Lancaster's men later find a marooned English tailor there.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m82efe0zs404yq31g4ekby7z)

> On 25 September 1582, he summoned his lieutenants to a meeting in his cabin and told them of his plan to seize the island of St Helena ‘and theire to be proclaimed kyng’.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 1582 Edward Fenton tells his lieutenants he means to seize St Helena "and theire to be proclaimed kyng" and is talked out of it. On the way home from Lancaster's first voyage the crew land on the mostly uninhabited island, hear a chant from a makeshift chapel, and find John Segar, a tailor put ashore 14 months earlier, sound in body but "crazed in mind".
- **Why it matters:** It frames the follies and hardships of the early English voyages: a would-be king on one, a castaway on another.
