# Yerba Buena Center for the Arts

Arts center in the SoMa district of [[San Francisco]]. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne sees an outsider-art show there that sets off his essay on judging art apart from the artist.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m772x2mnac1d0q2pyky82cxa)

> I stop to see an art show at the Yerba Buena Center featuring work that comes out of a place in Oakland called Creative Growth.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Byrne bikes down Mission Street into SoMa, which is flat because it was built on landfill over rotting ship hulls. At the Yerba Buena Center he sees work from Creative Growth, an Oakland art center for people with mental or psychological challenges, including Judith Scott's yarn-wrapped cocoons.
- **Why it matters:** The show opens his "Inside and Outside" section, which argues that such work should be judged as art, not by the artist's biography.
