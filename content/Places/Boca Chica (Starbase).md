## Why I Lugged My 27-Pound Toddler to a Rocket Launch — Tim Urban (2024)
Source: [Original](https://waitbutwhy.com/2024/10/spacex-toddler.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f0q4frb35vkaxkvtmpvyxf)

> I knew one thing: I sure as shit wasn’t going to miss this. I made arrangements to travel to Boca Chica, Texas, to watch.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Urban travels to Boca Chica with his 19-month-old daughter to watch Starship's fifth test launch. After a 30-minute walk along the beach from the hotel to the viewing spot, they watch the launch and the booster's catch by the tower's arms; the crowd roars and his wife cries.
- **Why it matters:** It is the setting of the whole piece, and being there in person is its point.
