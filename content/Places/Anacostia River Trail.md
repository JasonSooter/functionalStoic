## Year in Review: 2024 — MG (Chasing Mailboxes) (2024)
Source: [[17 Responses to “Year in Review - 2024”]] · [Original](https://chasingmailboxes.com/2024/12/30/year-in-review-2024/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5d20ndreqp3g2m77sfjx85c)

> AND we also went meandering along the Anacostia River Trail, one of my favorite places in the world.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** In September, with Felkerino riding outdoors again after his injuries, the two meander along the trail. She praises it as wide, scenic and uncrowded; the only drawback is the likely headwind on the way back.
- **Why it matters:** The ride marks a return to normal in a hard year, and the trail is the place she chooses for it.
