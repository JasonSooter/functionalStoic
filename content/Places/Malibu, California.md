# Malibu, California

Beach city on the coast west of Los Angeles. In Greenlights it is where Matthew McConaughey lands in Hollywood, sleeping on producer Don Phillips's couch.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zp453qyc7784tqf2mbfd0e)

> At 10:36 p.m., I arrived at Don Phillips’s house on the beach in Malibu. I rang the bell.

- **Role:** journey waypoint / setting
- **In the book:** After driving from Austin with a loaded U-Haul, he arrives late at night at the beach house of Don Phillips, the producer he met at a bar in Austin, who opens the door naked with "Welcome to Hollywood." He sleeps on the couch for months. After a month riding motorcycles through Europe he heads "back to Malibu" before getting an agent.
- **Why it matters:** Malibu is his first foothold in Los Angeles; the stay sets up the agent meeting and the first studio jobs.
