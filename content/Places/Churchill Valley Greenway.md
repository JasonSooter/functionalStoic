## Former Golf Courses Are Going Wild — Jennifer Cole (2025)

Source: [Original](https://reasonstobecheerful.world/rewilding-golf-courses/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebqky7dw3bg9ttf6cph4f0)

> “When the Churchill Valley Golf Club closed in 2013, it was bought by a private developer,”

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Residents who had walked the abandoned course's cart paths resisted housing or industrial plans. Allegheny Land Trust bought it in 2021 and began rewilding. Nearby abandoned coal mines mean a costly drainage filtration system.
- **Why it matters:** It shows the community fight over development and the extra cost of local coal-mine pollution.
