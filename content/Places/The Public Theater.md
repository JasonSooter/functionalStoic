# The Public Theater

New York theater company in the former Astor Library on Lafayette Street, Lower Manhattan. In David Brooks's _How to Know a Person_, an emotional panel there is the start of his turn away from a reserved, detached life.

## How to Know a Person — David Brooks (2023)
Source: [[How to Know a Person]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/652822/how-to-know-a-person-by-david-brooks/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8g94xhkqxefwdc6h4nzayx6)

> But on this particular day, I was invited to appear on a panel at the Public Theater in New York

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In chapter 1, Brooks, used to buttoned-up Washington think-tank panels, is invited to a Public Theater panel on the arts in public life, with Anne Hathaway and the clown Bill Irwin. There is a group hug backstage, Hathaway sings, there are tissues on the stage, and the panelists talk about being undone by art. "Even I started emoting things!"
- **Why it matters:** Brooks treats the afternoon as the symbolic start of the journey the book describes, from a guarded pundit to someone trying to see and be seen. The theater's warmth matters because it contrasts with his D.C. world; a think-tank stage would not have done it.
