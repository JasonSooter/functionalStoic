# Greenford

Suburb in the London Borough of Ealing, west London. Brompton moved into its current 100,000 sq ft factory here in 2015.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8c38x880nhs8zxf8jggb48x)

> Moving to the current factory in Greenford was a big physical and organisational change, but it also involved some big financial and managerial changes.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The book uses the Greenford factory as its yardstick for precision: things there are "generally calibrated to plus or minus 0.2 mm tolerance". When Knight Cycles stopped supplying wheels in 2015, wheel-building came in-house at Greenford after a two-year handover. Part 2 opens with the 2015 move from Brentford and the financial and managerial changes that came with it.
- **Why it matters:** Greenford is where the company's growth ended up: room to build wheels and more parts in-house, and the scale that the second half of the book is about.
