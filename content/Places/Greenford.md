# Greenford

Suburb in the London Borough of Ealing, west London. Brompton moved into its current 100,000 sq ft factory here in 2015.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8c38x880nhs8zxf8jggb48x)

> Moving to the current factory in Greenford was a big physical and organisational change, but it also involved some big financial and managerial changes.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The book uses the Greenford factory as its yardstick for precision: things there are "generally calibrated to plus or minus 0.2 mm tolerance". When Knight Cycles stopped supplying wheels in 2015, wheel-building came in-house at Greenford after a two-year handover. Part 2 opens with the 2015 move from [[Brentford]] and the financial and managerial changes that came with it. Chapter 8 describes the four-year search for a site, the choice of Unit 1 of Greenford Park (100,000 sq ft against a 65,000 sq ft alternative) and the room it gave for two lines side by side, an in-house paint plant (painting had been contracted out to a factory in Wales) and a computerised assembly line built from cheap touchscreens and Raspberry Pis. Chapter 9 tells of the split board vote: Andrew Ritchie in favour, Tim Guinness fearing rent as debt, until Will found new investors so sceptics could sell; the first post-move board meetings were grim before the plan beat its projections.
- **Why it matters:** Greenford is where the company's growth ended up, and the decision to take it is the book's main example of betting on space ahead of need.
