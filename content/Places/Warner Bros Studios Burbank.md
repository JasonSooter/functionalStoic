# Warner Bros Studios Burbank

Film studio lot in Burbank, in the Los Angeles area. In Greenlights it is where Matthew McConaughey walks into Bungalow 22 and is hired on the spot for Angels in the Outfield.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zp47jrqj8p8rgx3cdwx4b8)

> I wore my American flag baseball cap and a white T-shirt for this job interview. Warner Bros. lot, Bungalow 22, parking-lot level.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** A week after his first audition with his new agency he is called in for the Disney film Angels in the Outfield, for the role of an "all-American baseball player". Backlit in the doorway in his flag cap, he is greeted with "All-American kid!", asked whether he played baseball, and told he has the job: $48,500 for ten weeks of shooting in Oakland, when he has $1,200 to his name.
- **Why it matters:** His first studio job; the windfall leads straight into the Las Vegas Super Bowl weekend with his brother Pat.
