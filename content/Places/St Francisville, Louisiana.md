# St. Francisville, Louisiana

Small town in West Feliciana Parish, Louisiana. In a New Yorker profile it is Rod Dreher's home town, where he returned after his sister's death.

## Rod Dreher’s Monastic Vision – The New Yorker — Joshua Rothman (2017)

Source: [Original](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2017/05/01/rod-drehers-monastic-vision) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7fwyb5pyf8q7xnkbc1vdh)

> His sister, Ruthie, lived in their Louisiana home town, outside St. Francisville (pop. 1,712). Dreher’s family had been there for generations, but he had never fit in.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Dreher’s home town, where his sister Ruthie lived and died of cancer. Her funeral showed him a place of deep roots and love, so he moved his family back in the hope of reconciling with his family and God. They would not accept him, he fell into depression, and the town becomes the model of the integrated community his Benedict Option book asks why there are not more of.
- **Why it matters:** St. Francisville is the starting point of Dreher's case for withdrawing into rooted communities.
