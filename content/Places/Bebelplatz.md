# Bebelplatz

Square on Unter den Linden in Berlin, laid out under Frederick the Great as the Forum Fridericianum. In _Germany: Memories of a Nation_ it is the site of the 1933 book burning and its memorial.

## Germany: Memories of a Nation — Neil MacGregor (2014)

Source: [[Germany - Memories of a Nation]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20201162W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m76qmh7kj0hjt02rnqs0c7)

> The bonfire was held on what is now Bebelplatz, a square in the centre of Berlin, and in the middle of Bebelplatz there is now a memorial: a large glass plate set in the cobbles.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 24, 'Purging the degenerate', recalls the night of 10 May 1933 when Nazi students burned some 20,000 'un-German' books here. A glass plate in the cobbles now looks down onto empty white shelves, beside Heine's 1821 line about burning books and then people. Chapter 30 adds that the square was Frederick the Great's Forum Fridericianum of opera, church and library.
- **Why it matters:** It joins a monument to Prussian Enlightenment with the opening act of the Nazi purge of culture, a site of shame deliberately kept in view.
