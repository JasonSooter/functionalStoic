## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fms8cbmnawv1g2z8bqedp9)

> A Black woman greeted us at the front door of Grace Lutheran School.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On an April 1990 afternoon, seven-year-old Kendi visits the private school on Long Island with his parents and asks the teacher why she is the only Black teacher. Ma remarks he has 'entered racial puberty'.
- **Why it matters:** The scene marks the dawning of his racial consciousness; the commute out of Queens illustrates Black families separating children from poor neighborhood schools.
