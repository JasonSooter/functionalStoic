# Pátzcuaro

Colonial lakeside town in Michoacán, [[Mexico]]. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ the Bretons, Riveras and Trotskys travel here in July 1938 for a series of evening "conversations".

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)

Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m73hnb3fhxdh0ch6jg260qyr)

> In July, the Bretons, the Riveras, and the Trotskys traveled to Pátzcuaro in Michoacán, a graceful town of cobblestone streets, large plazas, and low white houses with carved wooden pillars and tiled roofs.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In Chapter 14 the party plans daytime trips to the villages around Lake Pátzcuaro and evening "Conversations in Pátzcuaro". Trotsky dominates the first evening, expounding on art under Communism, while Frida and Jacqueline Breton sit in a corner playing Surrealist and Mexican hand games and slip outside to smoke despite his disapproval.
- **Why it matters:** It shows Frida's friendship with Jacqueline Breton and her distance from the men's theorizing, in the circle that will label her a Surrealist.
