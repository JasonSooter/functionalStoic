## We Need to Talk About Kids and Smartphones — Markham Heid (2017)
Source: [Original](https://time.com/4974863/kids-smartphones-depression/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7f6p2bxzr9r3tw5gf7a2z)

> One of her duties at Connecticut’s Granby Memorial High School is to monitor students during their lunch periods.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Guidance counselor Colleen Nisbet watches students at lunch. She says they once socialized and let off energy, but now sit with phones out and barely talk. The scene illustrates how phones displace face-to-face talk.
- **Why it matters:** Cafeteria scene illustrating the effect of phones on teen socializing.
