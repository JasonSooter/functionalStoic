# Stühlinger Brücke

Tram bridge over the railway tracks by [[Freiburg Hauptbahnhof]], Freiburg. It turns up as the steepest stretch of the tram network and as the transfer point whose lifts and escalators VAG rebuilt.

## Lifts and escalators at the main station: More comfort for passengers — VAG Freiburg (2026)
Source: [Original](https://blog.vag-freiburg.de/aufzug_rolltreppe/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6rxkepgjayp67rtvg237fg6)

> However, VAG Freiburg provides escalators and lifts for its tram passengers in order to ensure accessibility in the area of the tram bridge. This is above all to enable tram passengers with reduced mobility to access the individual platforms or, conversely, the tram bridge.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** A VAG question-and-answer piece on the renewal of the lifts and escalators between the tram bridge and the platforms of [[Freiburg Hauptbahnhof]]. The 1983 installations were replaced: all five lifts (1,000 kg capacity instead of 630 kg, about half the energy) by the end of 2024, the platform 1 escalator in March 2025 and the platforms 2/3 escalator on 9 July 2025, for €6.4 million. Lift shafts had to be lifted in at night during track closures booked 40 weeks ahead, and the lifts were swapped one after another so that at least three always ran.
- **Why it matters:** The bridge is the subject: it is VAG's own step-free link between trams and trains, while the station itself is Deutsche Bahn's job. VAG explains it stopped waiting for DB's delayed station rebuild and renewed its 40-year-old installations itself.
