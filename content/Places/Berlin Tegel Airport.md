## Inside Berlin’s Abandoned Tegel Airport — The B1M (2026)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d-LArPe1Rk0) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bre7rd0p2wtybrvfnzw4bf)

> The solution was a former artillery firing range in the French controlled northwestern sector of the city. It was flat, open, and perfect

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** During the 1948 Berlin Airlift, when Tempelhof and Gatow couldn't cope, about 19,000 largely volunteer Berliners built Tegel on a former artillery range in 90 days. A hexagonal terminal by von Gerkan and Marg opened in 1974; the airport closed in November 2020 and its ground is being cleared of old munitions for the Berlin TXL redevelopment.
- **Why it matters:** The airport is the whole story: Cold War lifeline, modernist icon and abandoned site in one place.
