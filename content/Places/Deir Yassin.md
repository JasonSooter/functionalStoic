# Deir Yassin

Former Palestinian village on the western edge of Jerusalem, site of the 1948 massacre. A subject in Colum McCann's _Apeirogon_: Village on Jerusalem's outskirts where the 1948 massacre occurred; the Kfar Shaul hospital now stands on the site.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjqfr5tnr4m6ga6we7y4bd)

> The Kfar Shaul Mental Health Center was built on the site of Deir Yassin.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Following the account of Jerusalem syndrome, the text says the mental health center that receives the afflicted tourists stands where the Palestinian village of Deir Yassin lay. It recounts the April 1948 massacre by Jewish militia and its role in setting off the Nakba.
- **Why it matters:** The placement of a psychiatric facility for delusional visitors on a massacre site layers madness and erasure on the same ground, and ties to the book's theme of buried history.
