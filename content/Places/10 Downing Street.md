## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2wjgr2p5jezrz9mnfp30)

> An Outside Broadcast unit was on its way to Downing Street and I had to join it there as soon as possible.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On the night of the Suez crisis broadcast, Attenborough finds Eden in bed in the top-floor flat with his Cabinet around him, endlessly redrafting his speech. The main living room is turned into a studio, and Lady Eden paints on his moustache with mascara just before transmission.
- **Why it matters:** The setting for the book's most dramatic political episode, with strong detail of the flat and living room.
