# Diepkloof

Well-off district on the eastern side of [[Soweto]]. In Born a Crime the crew from Alexandra travel there for a dance battle, and police stop and beat them on the way home.

## At a glance
| Source | Author | Role | In one line |

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zbjmxmnm2d6chqtt01tbhq)

> Hector was from Diepkloof, which was the nice, well-off part of Soweto. Diepkloof was where the first million-rand houses were built after democracy.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 16 ("The Cheese Boys"). Trevor, Bongani, Mzi, Bheki, G and Hitler take a minibus to Diepkloof for a dance competition against Hector, who wins. G is caught kissing one of the local girls and it ends in a fight. Pulling out of Diepkloof towards the freeway at one in the morning, police stop the minibus, claim to have found a gun, slap each of them and abuse them as "dogs from Alex".
- **Why it matters:** Diepkloof stands for the snobbish, newly prosperous Soweto against "gritty" Alexandra, and the stop shows how the police treat boys from the hood. When Trevor gives his Highlands North address, it also exposes him as an outsider among his own crew.
