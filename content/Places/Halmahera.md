## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy8xqbe0sfnws6abx9m9bg)

> An English ornithologist, David Bishop, found a population, not on Bacan but on the neighbouring larger island of Halmahera.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Using map references from the 1983 sighting, the team reach a patch of forest and camp there. Over a hundred males in one or two trees are shrieking and dancing, their white wing-standards raised like masts, and doing vertical jumps.
- **Why it matters:** Wallace discovered the species on Bacan; the rediscovered colony on Halmahera lets Attenborough finally see it alive.
