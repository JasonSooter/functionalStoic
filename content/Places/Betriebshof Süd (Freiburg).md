# Betriebshof Süd (Freiburg)

Art nouveau tram depot on Urachstraße in the Wiehre district of [[Freiburg]], Germany. The Wikipedia history of Freiburg's trams describes it as the network's original depot, now home to the museum fleet.

> The southern depot in Wiehre has existed since 1901 and was until 1994 one of the two regular depots for the network. It is located on Urachstraße close to the stop for Lorettostraße.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The depot was enlarged in 1907–08 and could hold 77 vehicles. It lost regular service once the western depot was connected in 1994; today the Friends of Freiburg's Tramways keep historic trams in its western part, and the fire brigade uses the eastern hall.
- **Why it matters:** It is where the network began operating and where its history is now kept.
