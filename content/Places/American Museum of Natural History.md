## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W)

> The American Museum of Natural History in New York has an absorbing diorama that records the moment of their passing.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The museum has a diorama of the two Laetoli australopithecines walking side by side. Bryson talks to Ian Tattersall, its curator of anthropology, who admits that nearly everything above the footprints is supposition and that he would now make the figures more apelike; Tattersall also tells him how little hominid fossil material exists worldwide.
- **Why it matters:** Bryson's window on how much imagination goes into reconstructing human ancestors.
