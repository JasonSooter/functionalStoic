# Kilometer Post 836 turnout

Roadside turnout south of Vyborg on the Leningrad–Vyborg highway, [[Russia]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is the rendezvous where the British diplomats pick up Gordievsky.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7xy64rss3b3m2y67k8rtkk9)

> The entrance to the turnout was three hundred yards ahead, marked by the distinctive rock. It turned off in a wide D-shaped loop a hundred yards in length, with a screen of trees on the roadside and thick undergrowth of bracken and scrub.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Gordievsky reaches the turnout, "marked by the distinctive rock," by bus and on foot and hides in the undergrowth among bracken and mosquitoes. Ascot's and Gee's cars, tailed by the KGB, skid in at 2:47 while the Soviet cars race on past. Gordievsky erupts from the bushes like "a tramp" and grunts, in English: "Which car?"
- **Why it matters:** It is the pickup point on which the whole PIMLICO plan depends: a spot just big enough to pull off the road unseen. The book reproduces its photograph among the plates.
