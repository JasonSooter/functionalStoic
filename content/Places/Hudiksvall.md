# Hudiksvall

Coastal town in Gävleborg, [[Sweden]], where Hans Rosling and his wife worked at the hospital before and after Mozambique, as told in How I Learned to Understand the World.

## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling (2019)
Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20831115W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqzcarprh1y3rdhhvreeza)

> In November 1975 our son, Ola, was born, while we were living in the northern city of Hudiksvall.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Rosling and Agneta worked in the small hospital here (he as junior doctor, she as midwife) in preparation for Mozambique, and returned here after his 1978 cancer treatment.
- **Why it matters:** Base for the years before and after his cancer diagnosis.
