# Route de l'Esterel

Road through the Esterel hills between Saint-Raphaël and Cannes on the [[French Riviera]], where Anne's fatal car crash happens in Bonjour Tristesse.

## Bonjour Tristesse — Françoise Sagan (1954)
Source: [[Bonjour Tristesse]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL584333W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5d21hkmnwc079zsahj2y9s5)

> ‘She has had an accident,’ he said. ‘It happened on the Route de l’Esterel.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After Anne drives off from the villa, a call at ten o'clock tells Raymond she has crashed on the Route de l'Esterel; the car fell fifty metres at the most dangerous spot, where there had been many accidents that summer. Cécile senses it may have been suicide; she and her father drive through the night to the clinic where Anne has died.
- **Why it matters:** The crash is the novel's climax and the price of Cécile's scheme; the ending's sadness grows out of it.
