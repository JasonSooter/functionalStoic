## Project for Public Spaces Awards Three Community Placemaking Grants to Transform Public Spaces for Street Safety and Mobility — Project for Public Spaces (2025)

Source: [Original](https://www.pps.org/article/project-for-public-spaces-awards-three-community-placemaking-grants-for-street-safety-and-mobility) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2yfn4drmse77trjdph50j)

> While the Joy-Alpine Trailhead is a popular point for greenway access, the space does not yet host active community programming, a strong desire voiced by legacy residents.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The trailhead sits on the Joe Louis Greenway, described as a 29-mile trail system connecting 23 Detroit neighborhoods. The grant will fund a mobility hub with new amenities and programming, with the Detroit Economic Growth Corporation and partners.
- **Why it matters:** One of three funded sites; the text says residents want it to be more than a starting point.
