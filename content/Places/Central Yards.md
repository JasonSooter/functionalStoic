## Hong Kong’s $63BN Groundscraper — The B1M (2026)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYhGqtQELd0) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7xy65dwdbg8f742n6befaxw)

> Central Yards is going to be constructed here on Hong Kong Island right next to Victoria Harbour.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The video introduces Central Yards as 1.6 million square feet of office, retail and green space, to be built "here on Hong Kong Island right next to Victoria Harbour." Building wide instead of high preserves the harbour vistas for ferry passengers and people on the island, and the project is meant to act as a bridge between the harbourfront and the rest of Hong Kong Island, with a sky garden and an underground theatre.
- **Why it matters:** The site is the whole argument: only a scarce piece of flat waterfront in a mountainous, skyscraper city makes a groundscraper both possible and worth building.
