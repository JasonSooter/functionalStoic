# Banda Aceh

City at the northern tip of Sumatra, Indonesia, historically the port of Achin. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ it is the first stop of the East India Company's first fleet.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m82efvgzp17kqr8ajqe5r8s8)

> On 5 June 1602, more than sixteen months after leaving Woolwich, Lancaster’s fleet finally arrived at the Sumatran port of Achin.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Lancaster reaches the rich, cosmopolitan port in June 1602 and is carried to court on elephants with Queen Elizabeth's letter; the Sultan agrees to the Queen's requests and feasts him, but pepper costs far more than expected, and the fleet leaves in November without full holds.
- **Why it matters:** It is the new Company's first diplomatic and trading test, and its high prices push Lancaster on to Bantam.
