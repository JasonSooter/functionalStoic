> Visser was our first troll, hanging out along the Delaware River near the southern end of the city.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** Visser, the first of Burlington City's three trolls, is a Dutch fisherman with a bamboo pole ending in a bird house, standing by the Delaware near the southern end of the city. The author talks to a kindergartner there and counts Visser among the author's favourites.
- **Why it matters:** A waypoint, made memorable by the river setting and the encounter. The pin marks Burlington City's centre (approximate).
