## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)

Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20078135W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebv9rbynnan65p66txzefj)

> where I was privately tortured by the allure of the San Jacinto Mountains.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The mountains rise straight up, purple at sunset, and she cannot stop wanting to look at them. Later, reading Gloria Bird on a mountain as a person, she realizes her reaction was a suspicion that 'someone was there'.
- **Why it matters:** They trigger her reflection on animate landscapes and the limits of Western language for perceiving the nonhuman.
