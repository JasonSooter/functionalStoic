## The Road Not Taken is Guaranteed Minimum Income — Jeff Atwood (2025)
Source: [[The Road Not Taken Is Guaranteed Minimum Income]] · [Original](https://blog.codinghorror.com/the-road-not-taken-is-guaranteed-minimum-income/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebpwcvsgt942ymxzpqt6dw)

> The following is drawn from a speech I delivered today at Cooper Union's Great Hall, where I joined Lieutenant Colonel Alexander Vindman to discuss the future of the American Dream.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The essay is a speech delivered in the Great Hall. Near the end the speaker says the hall hosted a standing-room-only memorial service for Aaron Swartz eight days after his death.
- **Why it matters:** The venue frames the piece as a public address and gives the Swartz tribute its weight. The argument would stand elsewhere, but that link would be lost.
