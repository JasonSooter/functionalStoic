## This Formerly Homeless Harvard Grad Is Using Her Story To Change How We Help The Homeless — Gila Lyons (2018)

Source: [Original](https://www.good.is/articles/homeless-to-harvard) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jkz19grs5dfae5z15x5z99)

> Given her academic prowess, one might be surprised to find that Chung spent six years of her youth homeless and alone on the streets of Los Angeles.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Chung was homeless on the streets of Los Angeles for six years from age 12, while staying in school full time. She remembers running from men in Hollywood who tried to kidnap her.
- **Why it matters:** The city is the setting of the homelessness the article contrasts with her later degrees. It is not described beyond that.

## Abundance — Ezra Klein & Derek Thompson (2025)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL44034521W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m77zzt80hrq9ab0raz3pmr)

> In 2016, the people of Los Angeles overwhelmingly passed Proposition HHH, a ballot measure that raised \$1.2 billion through a higher property tax to create 10,000 new apartments for the homeless.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Chapter 1 names Skid Row as a sight of California's homelessness crisis. In chapter 3, Los Angeles voters pass Proposition HHH in 2016, but by March 2024 the city has built only 4,344 units, at about \$600,000 each. In Venice, homeowners fight and sue the Venice Dell Community project, a planned 140-unit building on a city parking lot.
- **Why it matters:** It is the main example of public money for housing being eaten up by process and local opposition, even for housing liberals say they want.
