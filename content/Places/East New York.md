# East New York

Neighborhood in eastern [[Brooklyn]], New York City. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne rides there to see an art piece powder-coated and is struck by the city's plain neglect.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m772x74m70bvjg2rrtct6y2a)

> A few days later I bike to East New York (a neighborhood in Brooklyn) to see one of my art chair pieces being powder coated.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Byrne bikes through a string of Brooklyn neighborhoods to a powder-coating shop. At its worst, he writes, East New York looks like "some of the very bleak places I've seen in the former Soviet bloc—derelict housing surrounded by crumbling industrial superstructures," and he concludes that the official neglect is obvious.
- **Why it matters:** It is the blunt counterweight to the cheerful bike tour, showing how unevenly the city looks after its neighborhoods.
