# MI5 headquarters (Gower Street)

MI5 office building on Gower Street in central [[London]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ a top-floor flat there is where Michael Bettaney is confronted and finally confesses.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7vbqe2vrn7cdf9k7j70pr6j)

> On September 15, Bettaney was summoned to a meeting at MI5’s Gower Street headquarters to discuss an urgent counterintelligence case that had arisen.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Instead of a meeting, Bettaney is taken to a flat on the top floor, where John Deverell and Eliza Manningham-Buller lay out the evidence while senior MI5 and MI6 officers listen from a monitoring room below. He talks only hypothetically, stays the night with a bottle of whisky and begins calling the Russians "us". The next morning he declares: "I think I ought to make a clean breast of it."
- **Why it matters:** The confession closes the Koba affair, which had put Gordievsky in danger. The setting matters: MI5 could not legally hold him, so everything hung on a bugged flat and a long night of talk.
