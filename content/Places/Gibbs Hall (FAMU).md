# Gibbs Hall (FAMU)

Residence hall at Florida A&M University in Tallahassee. In How to Be an Antiracist (Ibram X. Kendi) it is where he watches the contested 2000 election unfold.

## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fye1tn2jb1mbegxbgtbw7k)

> when I first moved into Gibbs Hall at FAMU.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Kendi recalls his freshman dorm, where students watch the 2000 election results in the television room. The next morning Bush leads Florida narrowly, and Kendi hears of Black FAMU students and families blocked from voting.
- **Why it matters:** The dorm is the vantage point for his account of voter suppression in Florida, which feeds the chapter on White racism.
