## Peace Corps: The Great Adventure — Peace Corps (2002)

Source: [[Peace Corps - The Great Adventure]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bs7sd86vrzwvzk5jt5ervp)

> Mondi Timber was a huge lumber operation with a large sawmill. I found the yard manager and followed him to his office.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** The narrator drives about sixty kilometres from Mbabane through forested mountains to the sawmill at Piggs Peak; the yard manager lets him take planks, the driver overloads the truck, and they unload in the dark at his school in Elulakeni, which has no electricity.
- **Why it matters:** The sawmill is the source of the wood the story is about.
