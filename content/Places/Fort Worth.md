## Uncommon Type — Tom Hanks (2017)

Source: [[Uncommon Type]] · [Original](https://penguin.co.uk/books/1114786/uncommon-type/9781786091338.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5tbza8q6taenvr4x7m72m)

> The DFW Sun Garden Hotel. Not so much a hotel as a collection of efficiency suites and vending machines meant for business travelers with limited expense accounts.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** In 'Three Exhausting Weeks' Anna drags the sick narrator to Fort Worth to hand-hold a client. He sees the crowded DFW airport and the bland DFW Sun Garden Hotel, a block of efficiency suites and vending machines, where he doses himself with cold remedies while Anna takes charge.
- **Why it matters:** The trip is Anna's controlling energy at full pitch; the narrator's illness and her cure regime lead directly to his decision on Day 21 that he is not the man for her.
