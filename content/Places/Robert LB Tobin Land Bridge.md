## The Hidden Engineering of Wildlife Crossings — Practical Engineering (2024)

Source: [Original](https://practical.engineering/blog/2024/12/17/the-hidden-engineering-of-wildlife-crossings) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edf5m55ycat63e1xmhvk3v)

> Opened in 2020, the Robert LB Tobin Land Bridge crosses the six-lane Wurzbach Parkway in San Antonio, Texas. These are full-on bridges designed specifically for the use of animals.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** The narrator cites this local overpass as an example of a full wildlife bridge, alongside Dutch ecoducts and Banff overpasses.
- **Why it matters:** It is a secondary illustration of the overpass type and could be swapped for any similar bridge.
