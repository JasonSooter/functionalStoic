## Why Place Matters — White Space — Alex Nichols (2016)
Source: [Original](https://blog.whiteboard.is/why-place-matters-cf9a104b1e27?gi=73dc6c502106) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jkyxsx8n7z48xy9drwfr7x)

> we had crossed the Boston Common, climbed up and over Beacon Hill, visited a coffee shop, walked through the Back Bay, attended a concert, drank another coffee, and eaten dinner.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** On the Friday afternoon of the weekend visit, the author and a roommate crossed Boston Common as the first stretch of a walking evening that took in Beacon Hill and Back Bay.
- **Why it matters:** It is one stop in the author's evidence that a connected city lets you do a lot on foot. Any walkable city would serve the point.
