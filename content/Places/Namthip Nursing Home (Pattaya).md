> Namthip Nursing Home in Pattaya – This one seems like a typical nursing home that can accommodate foreigners.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Under the option of relocating to a cheaper location, the author lists a nursing home in Pattaya he found online as a candidate for his father. He notes single occupancy costs about 50,000 baht a month, including meals, maid service, nursing and utilities.
- **Why it matters:** It is a concrete example of the article's argument that care abroad can cost far less than US long-term care. The text only gives a name and price; Pattaya itself is not described.
