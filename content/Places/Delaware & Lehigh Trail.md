# Delaware & Lehigh Trail

Long-distance trail of about 140 miles in eastern Pennsylvania, from Bristol to the Black Diamond Trailhead. A cycling blog rides part of it near Allentown.

## Quakertown to Allentown and beyond plus a dinosaur footprint, 4 rail trails and a bike — alliumstozinnias (2024)

Source: [Original](https://exploringbybike.com/2024/09/07/quakertown-to-allentown-and-beyond-plus-a-dinosaur-footprint-4-rail-trails-and-a-bike/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f1whvwq7xz0awtzjppkqnn)

> This is the trail that runs 140 or so miles from Bristol, just north of Philadelphia, to the Black Diamond Trailhead with only 1 real gap (which we hit) and some sections temporarily closed for repairs.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The riders follow the D\&L toward East Allentown and hit its big remaining gap where the river curves north, detour by road as far as the Race Street Bridge, and reach the previous year's turnaround point.
- **Why it matters:** It is the main trail of the ride. Corridor point (approximate).
