## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1vpcveet7mtynpbd2qeh)

> Leafing through an atlas with this question in mind, my eye lit on Madagascar.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Attenborough picks Madagascar as the next Zoo Quest because it split from Africa early in mammal evolution and has unique lemurs, chameleons and moths, plus an Asiatic-descended people. The chapter follows the three-month trip across the island.
- **Why it matters:** The chosen destination and organising idea of the chapter, with its isolation and endemic wildlife.
