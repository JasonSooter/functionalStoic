## Nimble Electric Trucks Are Supercharging African Trade — Geetanjali Krishna & Sally Howard (2024)

Source: [Original](https://reasonstobecheerful.world/nimble-electric-trucks-are-supercharging-african-trade/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edg5bkq82fjqx1wm76gn81)

> In Kyazo, a fertile agricultural area in Rwanda’s Western Province, Jean Paul grows chili peppers.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Jean Paul grows chili peppers in Kyazo. Before Ox trucks, he pushed 100-kilo sacks on bicycles to town, then took a seven-hour bus ride to Kigali, a two-day ordeal. After renting space on an Ox truck in 2021 he went from 400 to 4,000 kg of peppers a week.
- **Why it matters:** Kyazo is the before/after case study that makes the abstract logistics claim concrete; the story depends on its distance from Kigali.
