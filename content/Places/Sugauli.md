# Sugauli

Town in West Champaran, Bihar, once a British cantonment. 'Rikki-Tikki-Tavi' in _The Jungle Book_ is set in a bungalow garden in the Segowlee cantonment.

## The Jungle Book — Rudyard Kipling (1894)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19870W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne2x3v71rwa1qx2mqm06yv)

> This is the story of the great war that Rikki-tikki-tavi fought single-handed, through the bath-rooms of the big bungalow in Segowlee cantonment.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Rikki-tikki is washed out of his burrow by a flood and adopted by an English family at their bungalow. In its bath-room, veranda, thorn-bush, melon bed and rubbish heap he kills Karait, Nag and Nagaina and protects Teddy.
- **Why it matters:** The English bungalow garden with its bathroom sluice and cobras' rat-hole supplies the entire stage for the story.
