# Poznań

City in western [[Poland]], German Posen during the occupation. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where the resettled Baltic Germans gather and Frans, in SS uniform, wins Wera back.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85b09vm54x9tfrjzpeqjf3p)

> Members of the younger Baltic generation gathered daily at Café Schwan, and in no time, three-quarters of Riga’s handsome young men were marching around in SS uniforms.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Posen, a major railway junction in the annexed east, becomes home to most of the resettled Baltic Germans, among them Wera and her mother; Wera works for the chief dramaturg of the Grand Theatre. Young Baltic Germans meet daily at Café Schwan, and many join the SS. Frans arrives in SS uniform with an interpreter's armband, signs the café's guestbook behind the bar and wins Wera back; the author, their son, is born in Posen in 1944.
- **Why it matters:** The city links the book's love story to its politics: the reunion that leads to the author's birth happens inside the community that marched into the SS.
