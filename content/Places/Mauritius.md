# Mauritius

Island nation in the Indian Ocean, east of Madagascar. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ it is where the dodo was hunted out of existence in the 1680s.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m83dbc0szw3pp2frhyex7bn0)

> a rather less desirable milestone was being passed on the island of Mauritius, far out in the Indian Ocean some eight hundred miles off the east coast of Madagascar.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The farewell chapter opens with two events of the 1680s: the publication of Newton's _Principia_, and, on Mauritius, the end of the flightless, ground-nesting dodo, whose eggs and chicks fell to the pigs, dogs and monkeys outsiders brought. It was probably gone by 1683 and certainly by 1693.
- **Why it matters:** The pairing sets up the chapter's argument that humans are at once brilliant and destructive, and begins its long roll call of extinctions.
