## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7w89ne962m0fh835a1qa8)

> The tunnel runs under the town of Beit Jala, dovetailing in parts with the Way of the Patriarchs, the ancient Biblical route.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Rami enters the kilometer-long tunnel on his motorbike before dawn and emerges near the red sign forbidding Israelis in Area A. From there he takes the back road up through Beit Jala.
- **Why it matters:** It marks the crossing from Jerusalem into Palestinian territory in Rami's morning journey.
