## Uncommon Type — Tom Hanks (2017)
Source: [[Uncommon Type]] · [Original](https://penguin.co.uk/books/1114786/uncommon-type/9781786091338.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5v82cwbkaz6f1qgx47ms6)

> The first time Rory met Willa Sax—who was crazy beautiful, beyond-real-life beautiful—was over cups of green tea in her offices in the Capitol Records Building on Vine Street in Hollywood.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In flashback, Rory is called back from a Utah ski season to meet Willa over green tea. She talks about Fiji, art and horses, and within the hour his phone fills with texts saying she loved him. Two weeks later he is cast as Caleb Jackson.
- **Why it matters:** This meeting is the lottery-win moment that took Rory from bit parts to the film and, eventually, the doomed tour.
