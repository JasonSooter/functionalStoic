# Rosebank Union Church

Church in Sandton in Johannesburg's northern suburbs. In Trevor Noah's Born a Crime it is "white church", the last stop of the family's Sunday church circuit and the start of the night his mother throws him out of a moving minibus.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6yq3t0pffrfgmmev4vxhmy5)

> White church was Rosebank Union in Sandton, a very white and wealthy part of Johannesburg.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 1 ("Run"). Every Sunday Trevor's mother takes him to three churches: mixed, black and white. Rosebank Union is "white church", where Trevor skips the main service for Sunday school. On the "carless Sunday" they walk out of Rosebank Union after nine at night with no minibus in sight. They hitchhike from the corner of Jellicoe Avenue and Oxford Road, but a Zulu minibus driver with an iwisa (war club) forces them into his minibus. When he turns threatening, his mother throws Trevor out of the moving vehicle and jumps after him with his baby brother Andrew.
- **Why it matters:** this is the book's opening set piece. It shows his mother's faith and fearlessness and the violence of early-1990s Johannesburg. The scene depends on the gap between wealthy white suburbia, where no minibuses run at night, and the townships the family needs to reach.
- **Real vs. fictional:** real church. ⚠️ The pin is the church's present building in Hurlingham Gardens, Sandton. The corner of Jellicoe Avenue and Oxford Road, where the hitchhiking starts, lies in nearby Rosebank, and the text says only that they "walked and walked" to get there.
