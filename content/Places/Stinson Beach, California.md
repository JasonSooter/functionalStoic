# Stinson Beach, California

Beach town in Marin County, north of San Francisco. In _Just for Fun_ it is where David Diamond hosts Linus and his daughters for a day.

## Just for Fun: The Story of an Accidental Revolutionary — Linus Torvalds and David Diamond (2001)

Source: [[Just for Fun - The Story of an Accidental Revolutionary]] · [Original](https://search.worldcat.org/oclc/45610395) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kks2zahk6dme86y89v3p02)

> I suggested Linus bring the kids over to a house I was renting in Stinson Beach.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** While Tove shops at IKEA, Linus brings the girls to Diamond's rented house. He kayaks in the lagoon, reads and retitles a chapter of the book at Diamond's Macintosh, and the girls look for starfish on the beach.
- **Why it matters:** A small, relaxed scene of the book being made.
