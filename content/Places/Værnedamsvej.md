# Værnedamsvej

Short shopping street on the border of Vesterbro and Frederiksberg, Copenhagen. In _The Little Book of Hygge_ it is a hygge-safari stop for a slow afternoon.

## The Little Book of Hygge: The Danish Way to Live Well — Meik Wiking (2016)

Source: [[The Little Book of Hygge - The Danish Way to Live Well]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19352003W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jrdfk62s97bb2tyrtstn106t)

> At Værnedamsvej cars zigzag between cyclists and pedestrians. This short street will make you slow down your pace, smell the flowers and the coffee.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Wiking recommends the street for a lazy afternoon among florists, cafés, wine bars and interior-design shops, where cars zigzag between cyclists and pedestrians. Later he recalls talking about nostalgia and hygge with the book's designer at a café on the street.
- **Why it matters:** A minor stop, but one that recurs; it illustrates the slow, small-scale side of hygge.
