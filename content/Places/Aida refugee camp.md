## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7x9kmac1e35gfgq3pmnak)

> A giant steel key sits on top of a keyhole-shaped archway in the Aida refugee camp in Bethlehem, a reminder of the houses left behind by Palestinians in 1948.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The Key of Return, nine meters high and almost a ton, is marked with bullet scars and tear-gas dents and a Not For Sale sign. It echoes Hassan's kept keys.
- **Why it matters:** A monument of memory and dispossession.
