## A World Without Work — Derek Thompson (2015)
Source: [Original](https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/archive/2015/07/world-without-work/395294/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7f2strvbzz2t1h4f403sn)

> The Columbus Idea Foundry is the country’s largest such space, a cavernous converted shoe factory stocked with industrial-age machinery.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Thompson detours to this makerspace in a converted shoe factory on a bitterly cold February afternoon. He describes men fixing a 60-year-old lathe, welding, a laser cutter and a communal pizza oven, then talks to members over pizza, one of whom calls working there with essentials covered 'utopia'. It stands for the communal-creativity future.
- **Why it matters:** Concrete example of an artisanal, post-wage community.
