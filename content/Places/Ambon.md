# Ambon

Clove island in the Moluccas, Indonesia. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ Henry Middleton's hopes of trade there are dashed when a Dutch fleet seizes the Portuguese fort.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m82efx64qca9gnts6crzkr2q)

> It was with considerable relief that the ships at last arrived at Amboyna, a clove-fringed island that lay at the very heart of the ‘spiceries’.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Middleton is told trade needs the Portuguese garrison's permission and wins it with a letter about peace between England and Spain; before the deal is done, nine Dutch ships arrive, demand the castle and take it after a short bombardment, and Middleton leaves empty-handed.
- **Why it matters:** It shows the Dutch displacing the Portuguese and shutting the English out of the clove trade, which drives them towards the Bandas.
