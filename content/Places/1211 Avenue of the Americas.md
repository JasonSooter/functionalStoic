# 1211 Avenue of the Americas

Office tower on Sixth Avenue in Midtown Manhattan, home of Fox News. Richard Branson's punctuality story ends with him sprinting down Sixth Avenue to its lobby.

## Want to Be More Productive? Be More Punctual — Richard Branson (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/productivity-hacks-want-more-productive-punctual-richard-branson?trk=eml-mktg-inf-m-prodhacks-1008-p1) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gm0p93723av1fn8kqekmwm)

> As I peered out of the car window into the immobile queues, I jumped out of the front seat and sprinted down Sixth Avenue. I spotted the Fox sign, hopped across the lobby and began banging on the window.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Stuck in immobile Manhattan traffic on his way to a live Fox News interview, Branson jumps out of the car, sprints down Sixth Avenue, spots the Fox sign, crosses the lobby and bangs on the window, arriving with a minute to spare.
- **Why it matters:** The one concrete anecdote behind the article's case for punctuality.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The text names only Sixth Avenue and the Fox lobby; Fox News is headquartered at 1211 Avenue of the Americas.
