## Former Golf Courses Are Going Wild — Jennifer Cole (2025)

> These days, when it rains heavily in the southeast Houston neighborhood of Clear Lake, residents can rest easy.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** A closed golf course was bought by the Clear Lake Water Authority and turned into detention ponds and a 200-acre nature reserve. During Hurricane Harvey, not one of 200 formerly flood-prone houses flooded.
- **Why it matters:** It is the lead example, showing a former course solving a flooding problem as well as providing green space.
