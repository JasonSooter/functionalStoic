# Chicken Coop (Blyn)

A farmer's chicken coop turned dance hall in Blyn, near [[Sequim]], Washington. In _The Boys in the Boat_ Joe Rantz's band plays there on Saturday nights, which lets him take Joyce Simdars out.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m869y4ns6e7k55e5scasr7p6)

> On Saturday nights they played at a dance hall in nearby Blyn, where a farmer had turned, with the addition of some strings of electric lights, his chicken coop into Sequim’s most popular dance venue.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Girls get into the Chicken Coop free and boys pay twenty-five cents, but Joe and his bandmates play for nothing. That matters to Joe because Joyce has come back from Montana and free admission means he can bring her along, though only when her mother can ride along to chaperone.
- **Why it matters:** A small, warm scene of the life Joe makes for himself after being abandoned; music and Joyce are what keep him going in Sequim.
