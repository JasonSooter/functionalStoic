> At Mashpee Commons, a strip mall on Cape Cod that was retrofitted into a new downtown, this feat is accomplished by tiny one-story shops, each about the size of a two-car garage.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Cited as an example of hiding surface parking behind a thin crust of tiny one-story shops. It was a strip mall retrofitted into a new downtown.
