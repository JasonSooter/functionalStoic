## Uncommon Type — Tom Hanks (2017)
Source: [[Uncommon Type]] · [Original](https://penguin.co.uk/books/1114786/uncommon-type/9781786091338.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5v6z9twhtaswheg17qsa9)

> Four days of junket were held in New York City, starting with a press conference staged in the ballroom of the Waldorf Astoria, attended by 152 media outlets.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The New York leg opens with a press conference where Willa talks for thirty minutes before Rory gets a question. Rory then spends twenty minutes at each table answering the same three questions, followed by fifty-seven TV interviews in the Media Center on the eighth floor. The premiere is moved to cinemas after a storm threat.
- **Why it matters:** It lays out the repetitive grind of the junket and the 'kiss for the ages' line that shows Irene's coaching.
