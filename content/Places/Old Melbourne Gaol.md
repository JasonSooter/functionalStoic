# Old Melbourne Gaol

Former jail in central [[Melbourne]], now a museum, where the bushranger Ned Kelly was hanged. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne bikes there for its Kelly exhibition.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76rjahpqyn9keke5x4th1c5)

> I decide to pay homage to the local outlaw legend Ned Kelly, so I bike back through town to the exhibition at the jail where he was executed.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On Australia Day, after the opening of a new riverside park, Byrne bikes back through town to the exhibition at the jail where Ned Kelly was executed. He then retells Kelly's story: an Irishman who felt wronged by the English, turned outlaw, made his own armour and was shot in the legs.
- **Why it matters:** It anchors the Australian outlaw legend in Byrne's account and his theme of who is treated as an outsider.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The book calls it only "the jail where he was executed"; Ned Kelly was hanged at the Old Melbourne Gaol.
