## The Underdogs: A Novel of the Mexican Revolution — Mariano Azuela (1915)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1209983W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8z96m8qazw77zafeet1xw)

> In Cuquio Demetrio received a message.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Near Cuquio Blondie's cruelty is recounted and War Paint pulls Camilla from her horse. At Cuquio a dispatch orders the troop back to Tepatitlan and on to Aguascalientes, and Demetrio expels War Paint, who stabs Camilla before being driven off.
- **Why it matters:** Here War Paint is cast out of the story and the army is pulled away from the sierra toward the Convention.
