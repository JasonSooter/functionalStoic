# Walled Off Hotel

Hotel beside the Wall in Bethlehem, designed by the artist Banksy. A setting in Colum McCann's _Apeirogon_: Banksy's art hotel beside the Wall, where Bassam is filmed and uneasy at being made to fit foreign visitors' ideas.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjqs94gmax3artt9w3nh3z)

> The door of the Walled Off was swung open by a man in a red bow tie. Bassam stood stunned a moment by the sight of a plastic chimp carrying a suitcase.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bassam goes to the Walled Off Hotel to be interviewed by an Italian film crew. He is unsettled by the plastic chimp, cherubs with gas masks and cameras, sits at a table a few yards from the Wall, and is interviewed in a room beneath a pillow-fight mural before walking along the Wall past the portrait he once mistook for Abir. The hotel also appears in earlier fragments about its museum and its location beside Checkpoint 300.
- **Why it matters:** The hotel lets the book show Bassam as a creature of the camera and shows the Wall as a stage for outside visitors; Bassam finds it both ludicrous and necessary. Set elsewhere, the scene would lose the Wall's shadow as a backdrop.
