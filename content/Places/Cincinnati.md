- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article profiles Sanphillippo, whose latest real-estate investment is in Cincinnati. It is his lead example of a second- or third-tier city worth living in: walkable streets of pre-car architecture, safety, affordability and human scale, against squeezed cities like New York and San Francisco.
- **Why it matters:** Cincinnati is the argument made concrete; the piece's thesis is that cities like it are the affordable urban alternative.
