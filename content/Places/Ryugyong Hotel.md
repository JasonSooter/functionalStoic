## Inside North Korea’s Abandoned Hotel of Doom — The B1M (2025)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kJPK1rb5GRs) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5d208h80qh0qav1g634mbmd)

> This is Ryugyong. It’s loomed over the city of Pyongyang for decades

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The concrete pyramid was begun in 1987, stalled in the 1990s and resumed in 2008, when the exterior was finished; it has never hosted a guest. Simon Cockerell of Koryo Tours, one of two Westerners to go inside, describes walking the lower floors and riding up to the 99th-floor observation deck, where the city below looked "like a matte painting". The video also covers the rumoured casino plan and Kempinski's interest in 2012.
- **Why it matters:** The building is the whole subject: a symbol of ambition outrunning the economy that was meant to fill it.
