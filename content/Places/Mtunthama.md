# Mtunthama

Small town near [[Kasungu]] in central Malawi, seven kilometres from William Kamkwamba's village. In _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ its Anglican hospital is where his mother nearly dies of malaria.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8ebd7tcdv6rw8b82j37hea4)

> The nurses took one look at her and demanded that she be rushed immediately to the Anglican hospital in Mtunthama.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Early in the book Mtunthama is just where the petrol station sells kerosene, "seven kilometers away". In late 2003 William's mother comes back from [[Lake Malawi|the lake]] with malaria. His father pushes her on his bicycle to the clinic at [[Wimbe]], and the nurses send her on by pickup to the Anglican hospital here. There were "no free beds", so she is sent home, but two days later she slips toward a coma and is readmitted. "It appears it’s gone to her brain," the doctor says, in a room with "pink walls and ESCOM-powered lights" and a woman from [[Chamama]] in the next bed. After several days "her fever miraculously broke." In the afterword William's youngest sister Tiyamike attends "the prestigious Kamuzu Academy in Mtunthama", studying Latin, French and Mandarin.
- **Why it matters:** It is the family's closest brush with death outside the famine, and it shows how far real medical care is from the village. The hospital's electric lights stand for what Wimbe lacks. The afterword's return, with a sister at the country's elite school, measures how far the family has come.
