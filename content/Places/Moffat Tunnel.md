## While Riding on a Train Going West — Linnesby-Maria (2024)
Source: [Original](https://notesfromlinnesby.substack.com/p/while-riding-on-a-train-going-west) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ede7j83nbkqw3br0rb6dqx)

> This is the pass that people in covered wagons died getting over. It took us two hours and ten minutes to reach and traverse it, in air conditioning.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** After a climb through green mountains, the train reaches the Moffat Tunnel under the Continental Divide, a ten-minute passage. She contrasts it with pioneers, and a seat-mate says Mormon ancestors would have been glad of the effortless crossing.
- **Why it matters:** It prompts reflection on past hardship versus easy modern travel.
