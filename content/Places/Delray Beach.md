## Addicts For Sale — BuzzFeed News (2016)

Source: [Original](https://www.buzzfeed.com/catferguson/addiction-marketplace) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7v5zmc2m25zjw6pc69qty)

> In South Florida’s Delray Beach, home to hundreds of rehab facilities and halfway houses, scams abound to profit off of addicts and their insurance policies.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The piece opens at a Starbucks on Atlantic Avenue where addicts with suitcases wait for a place to stay and are approached by paid recruiters. It describes a town of rehab centers and halfway houses where insurance fraud, patient brokering and overdoses are routine.
- **Why it matters:** Delray Beach is the epicentre of the recovery-industry scam the article investigates.
