# Santo Niño Shrine (Tacloban)

Shrine and museum built by Imelda Marcos in Tacloban, Leyte, her home region. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne reads it as a shrine to Imelda herself.

## At a glance

| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bicycle Diaries | David Byrne | subject | A disco-lit Christ-child chapel that is really a shrine to Imelda, with her furniture and dioramas of her life as she imagined it. |

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76rj16v9gak3cz6qecp5anv)

> In later years she built a “shrine” here in Tacloban, ostensibly to Santo Niño, the Christ child.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In Tacloban, Imelda's home ground, Byrne describes the shrine she built, ostensibly to Santo Niño: a chapel with the Christ child floating amid disco lights, jeepneys marked simply "Imelda", her furniture collection and a series of dioramas of her life story "as she imagined it". ⚠️ The text does not make entirely clear whether Byrne visits in person.
- **Why it matters:** It shows Imelda writing her own legend, the theme of the song cycle Byrne is researching.
