# Vega Archipelago

Cluster of hundreds of small islands off the Helgeland coast of [[Norway]], just below the Arctic Circle. It is the world of James Rebanks's memoir of a spring spent with an eider-duck keeper.

## The Place of Tides — James Rebanks (2024)

Source: [[The Place of Tides]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/315745/the-place-of-tides-by-rebanks-james/9780241426937) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7h2cqjdfqf94g2nm77rxnd0)

> I discovered the archipelago was halfway up the Norwegian coast, in the region called Helgeland, on the 66th parallel, just below the Arctic Circle.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Rebanks is first sent here for work on protecting fragile places from tourism and learns that hundreds of small islands lie beyond Vega, where women still go out each spring to tend eider ducks. The opening chapters describe the strandflat, the shallow shelf from which thousands of islands and skerries rise, and the "Stovelhav" or "boot sea", so shallow that people said you could walk between islands dry-shod at low tide.
- **Why it matters:** The archipelago's islands, skerries and sea make the farmer-fisher life and the eiderdown trade the book is about possible.
