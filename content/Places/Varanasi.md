## On the Hippie Trail — Rick Steves (2025)
Source: [[On the Hippie Trail]] · [Original](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/rick-steves/on-the-hippie-trail/9781641717458/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kjf3t2862evjr0bw21vev8)

> Today we were up at 4:30 a.m. joining the other pilgrims to visit the Ganges River.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Up at 4:30 a.m., they take a boat upstream past ghats flooded by the monsoon, watch bathers and pilgrims, and land at the burning ghat to see cremations; Rick is chased off after taking a photograph. The bustling old town and its bazaar are the other side of the city.
- **Why it matters:** The most intense religious scene of the trip, prompting Rick's reflections on death and joy.
