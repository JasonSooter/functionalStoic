## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)

Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbmc92fvzcv3trzdwbsw4t)

> In the late nineties, I was helping to design Mount Laurel, a new town outside of Birmingham, Alabama, that was modeled on that city’s most successful prewar neighborhoods.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Speck's team planned streets matching the dimensions of the best Birmingham neighborhoods but were told they violated standards, and the engineering firm would not stamp the drawings. They then toured the county engineer around to try to persuade him.
- **Why it matters:** His anecdote for how street standards creep toward higher design speeds.
