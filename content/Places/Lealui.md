# Lealui

Dry-season royal residence of the Lozi king on the Barotse floodplain, western Zambia; in _Life on Air_ the end of the royal barge procession.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2sh9apjaah1z4wh0y86z)

> The voyage took over four hours and it was too dark to film by the time we arrived at Lealui.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Destination of the Litunga's ceremonial barge procession (the Nalikwanda) down the canal from Limalunga to the dry-season residence. Further ceremonies there the next day install the king, and he makes a farewell remark about royal salutes being too noisy indoors.
- **Why it matters:** The goal of the filmed fuluhela procession; the arrival is described briefly, the canal journey more fully.
