# Poudre River Trail at Arapaho Bend

Stretch of the Poudre River Trail in southeast Fort Collins, Colorado, from the Arapaho Bend Natural Area to the I-25 underpass. Bike Fort Collins reported the new half-mile paved link when it opened.
