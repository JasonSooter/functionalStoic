> “We really kind of refer to this as our third aqueduct,” says John Bednarski, an interim assistant general manager at the Metropolitan Water District of Southern California — a powerful agency, as the largest wholesale provider of water in the nation and owner of the Colorado River Aqueduct.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The aqueduct carries shrinking Colorado River water to millions of Southern California residents. The piece opens with sun glimmering off it. A planned wastewater-recycling project is framed as a local 'third aqueduct' to reduce dependence on it.
- **Why it matters:** It is the infrastructure whose vulnerability motivates the whole story; the argument is about reducing reliance on it.
