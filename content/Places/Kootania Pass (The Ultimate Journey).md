# Kootania Pass (The Ultimate Journey)

The snowbound pass in northern Glacier National Park, Montana, where Ryback's book opens, pinned at the Fifty Mountain camp the brothers were looking for. It is the first day of their walk down the Continental Divide from Canada to Mexico.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8berem3hn50e008f23dw8zy)

> We were on Kootania Pass in northernmost Montana, overlooking an endless expanse of towering rock and glistening, melting snow.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The book opens here on June 16, one day and about 15 miles in from Waterton Lakes National Park, with Eric and his 18-year-old brother Tim on the pass in deep, melting snow. Eric looks on the map for the Fifty Mountain shelter that is supposed to be at the pass; they search the pass, the woods and the valley but it is nowhere in sight, perhaps buried under the record winter's snow. With a storm coming they pitch a tarp on a rock outcrop, and Tim sums up his first day: "hiking is a little bit of heaven and a whole lot of hell."
- **Why it matters:** It sets the terms of the whole trek: far more snow than Eric expected, a younger brother out of his depth, and plans that the mountains overrule on the first day.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ No pass of this name appears in the gazetteers; the book's own clues are that it lies about 15 miles in from the Waterton Lakes border on day one and that the Fifty Mountain shelter "was supposed to be at Kootania Pass". The pin is therefore the Fifty Mountain camp on the Highline/Continental Divide Trail in Glacier National Park (inferred). Kootenai Peak, just west of Fifty Mountain, is the likely source of the name; the alternative is a saddle somewhat north of the camp on the same ridge.
