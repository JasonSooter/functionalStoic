# Alcor Life Extension Foundation

Cryonics organization in Scottsdale, Arizona, that stores its patients there. A backdrop in Tim Urban's “Why Cryonics Makes Sense”: Home of Alcor's storage facility, chosen as the US place least at risk of natural disasters.

## Why Cryonics Makes Sense — Tim Urban (2016)
Source: [Original](https://waitbutwhy.com/2016/03/cryonics.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjqhnpexgz3m8q0bktbm0b)

> Neither major US cryonics company is in a location highly prone to natural disasters—Alcor actually located itself in Scottsdale, AZ because it is the place in the US

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** In the section on what could go wrong with cryonic storage, the author says Alcor, one of the two big US cryonics companies, deliberately located itself in Scottsdale, AZ because the place is least at risk of natural disasters in the US. Scottsdale is also named as the destination to fly to for a deathbed patient going to Alcor, and the text imagines Scottsdale might not even be part of the US in the far future.
- **Why it matters:** Minor: the city is the site of the storage facility and its siting is part of the argument that patients are safe; the argument would be the same with any low-risk location.
