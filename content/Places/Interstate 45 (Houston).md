## Urban Planner Jeff Speck Revisits ‘Walkable City,’ 10 Years Later - Bloomberg — Bloomberg.com (2022)
Source: [Original](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2022-12-05/urban-planner-jeff-speck-revisits-walkable-city-10-years-later) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8vt584gsmc76mxagfmrja)

> They had come to the theater for the same reason I had, to stop the ill-conceived $7 billion expansion of US Interstate 45 through their city.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** In February 2019 Speck arrived to lecture in Houston and was met by protesters opposing the TxDOT plan to widen I-45. He lays out its costs: 1,235 housing units and 331 businesses destroyed, about $135 million a year in lost taxes, and 40% of Bruce Elementary students losing homes. He notes asthma risk near the highway and that the project is tied up in an environmental lawsuit.
- **Why it matters:** It is his extended example of what he calls infrastructural racism.
