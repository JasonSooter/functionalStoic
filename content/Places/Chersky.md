# Chersky

Settlement on the lower Kolyma in Sakha (Yakutia), Russia, with an airfield. In _Last of the Breed_ it is Colonel Zamatev's field headquarters for the last stage of the hunt.

## Last of the Breed — Louis L'Amour (1986)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL51048W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6ycpdhbybcx92bn6zzwfg4f)

> Colonel Arkady Zamatev landed in Chersky. Lieutenant Suvarov awaited him as the plane landed. He led the way to a Volga parked near the airfield.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Zamatev arrives under pressure to show results and is driven down "a bumpy, icy street" of five-storey concrete blocks whose "bleak ugliness" he hates. On his desk lie reports of failure; on the wall hangs a map of "the vast stretch of country between Chersky and the Bering Strait, everything from Magadan east". From here he sends out helicopters and patrols the roads from Chersky to Talovka and Ust'chaun, and hears that Kyra Lebedev has tracked Natalya to Iman.
- **Why it matters:** It moves the hunters' command into the far northeast: the map on its wall frames the end of the chase as a race to the sea.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Not to be confused with the [[Chersky Range]], far to the south-west; both are named after the explorer Jan Czerski.
