# Dudley

Black Country town in the West Midlands. Knight Cycles, the Hesson family's workshop here, hand-built every Brompton wheel until 2015.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8c39ckrakm98jmhj4g9fpn7)

> Our happier experience, and the way that ideally I’d like all our supplier relationships to go, was with one of our best suppliers, the Hesson family, who ran Knight Cycles of Dudley.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Mike Hesson, an old-school custom builder, and his daughter Angie laced and trued Brompton's wheels by hand at their workshop, Mike riding twelve miles in from Wolverhampton each day. As demand grew, the author took Mike to see automatic wheel-lacing machines at Holland Mechanics in Amsterdam; Brompton bought one and leased it to Knight Cycles, repaid through cheaper wheels, and Mike helped adapt it to 16-inch rims. Wheel-building moved in-house at [[Greenford]] after a two-year handover in 2015.
- **Why it matters:** The book holds it up as the model supplier relationship, the opposite of the near-disaster at [[Nottingham]]: trust, shared investment and a planned handover.
