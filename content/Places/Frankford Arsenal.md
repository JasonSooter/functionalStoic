# Frankford Arsenal

Former US Army ammunition plant in north-east Philadelphia. In _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_ it is where Feynman takes his first war job, designing gears for an artillery computer.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfq3n60x3ra6pbengtasde)

> I went to the Frankfort Arsenal, in Philadelphia, and worked on a dinosaur: a mechanical computer for directing artillery.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** During the war Feynman goes to "the Frankfort Arsenal, in Philadelphia" to work on a mechanical computer for directing artillery. He learns to design machinery with non-circular gears and keeps quiet about his role when a lieutenant visits; a later project, which depends on powder-train fuses, fails because the fuses fizzle at altitude, and he goes back to Princeton.
- **Why it matters:** It is his first defence work, a step before Los Alamos; the episode is mostly about learning machine design rather than about the arsenal itself.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real; the book spells it "Frankfort".
