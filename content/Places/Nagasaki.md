## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7xe4d0k64406xhtf3sak9)

> There were, Sweeney was told, no clouds over Nagasaki.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Major Sweeney circles Kokura eleven times, unable to see the target through cloud, and is redirected to Nagasaki. Rami reflects that because of cloud vapor seventy-five thousand lives were lost in one place and preserved in another, like Smadar's chance.
- **Why it matters:** A model of fate decided by an accident of weather, paralleling the two girls.

## The International City of Streetcars — Nagasaki – Nebula Original — Day Pass (2026)
Source: [Original](https://nebula.tv/videos/daypass-s2e2/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6ycpq61m9wrgd2atskvgnyv)

> Nagasaki has a long history of being an international city, as one of the few Japanese cities where foreigners were allowed to live during the Tokugawa shogunate.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** A Day Pass episode on Nebula about Nagasaki as "the international city of streetcars". The saved text is only the episode's one-line description, which frames the city by its history as one of the few places in Japan where foreigners were allowed to live under the Tokugawa shogunate.
- **Why it matters:** The city is the episode's whole subject. ⚠️ Only the description was available, so what the video shows of the tram network is not covered here.
