## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7xe4d0k64406xhtf3sak9)

> There were, Sweeney was told, no clouds over Nagasaki.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Major Sweeney circles Kokura eleven times, unable to see the target through cloud, and is redirected to Nagasaki. Rami reflects that because of cloud vapor seventy-five thousand lives were lost in one place and preserved in another, like Smadar's chance.
- **Why it matters:** A model of fate decided by an accident of weather, paralleling the two girls.
