## Year in Review: 2024 — MG (Chasing Mailboxes) (2024)
Source: [[17 Responses to “Year in Review - 2024”]] · [Original](https://chasingmailboxes.com/2024/12/30/year-in-review-2024/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5d20m2c6mqvn58yvxrhm9ws)

> The best part of my walks to pickleball was the stroll by the Tidal Basin.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** Her walks to the pickleball courts pass the Tidal Basin, and she photographs the same view from March to August, coming to love a space she had never paid attention to. She also notes that Stumpy, a famous cherry tree, was removed with others around the basin for a seawall restoration.
- **Why it matters:** A small recurring pleasure in a year-in-review built from everyday walks around the city.
