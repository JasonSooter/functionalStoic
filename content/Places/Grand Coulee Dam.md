# Grand Coulee Dam

Huge concrete dam on the Columbia River in central [[Washington (state)|Washington]], built from 1933. In _The Boys in the Boat_ Joe Rantz spends the summer of 1935 hanging on a rope over its canyon with a jackhammer, earning his next year at university.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m86yekpnszewtc18efz9zgkd)

> For eight hours a day, he dangled on a rope in the furnacelike heat of the canyon, pounding at the wall of rock in front of him.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After the 1935 season Joe drives east, crosses the Columbia on a steel bridge and walks out of the National Reemployment Service office with a laborer's job at the dam site, a project the book describes as the largest masonry structure since the Great Pyramid. He hangs from ropes on the canyon cliffs with a jackhammer, eats at the company mess hall in Mason City, and works alongside Johnny White and Chuck Day. The brutal work comes to suit him.
- **Why it matters:** The dam pays for another year of school, and so for Joe's chance at Berlin; it also binds him to two future crewmates, and the book sets the boys' summer inside the New Deal's biggest act of rebuilding.
