# Lethem

Main town of the Rupununi savannah on Guyana's Brazilian border; in _Life on Air_ the Zoo Quest team's base for catching anteaters and anacondas.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1zb1eb5w8604n5yf2k3n)

> Teddy Melville, who ran the hotel at Lethem, took us under his wing.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Lethem, the main Rupununi settlement, is where the weekly plane lands. Teddy Melville's hotel is the base for a week, from which cowhands on horseback find a giant anteater and the team catches an anaconda and a caiman at a nearby lake.
- **Why it matters:** The base for the savannah collecting; a stop of about a week, described through the people and animals rather than the town.
