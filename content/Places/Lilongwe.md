# Lilongwe

Capital of Malawi, in the Central Region. In _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ it is the distant city of tobacco auctions and the electricity office, where the village's fortunes are decided.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8dps5thk2r5407ax10ba6bx)

> His tobacco fetched a high price each year at the Auction Holdings Limited in Lilongwe, where the farmers sold their hundred-kilogram bales on the auction floor.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Uncle John's burley tobacco sells well at the Lilongwe auction floors. Under Muluzi, mass-produced tobacco floods them and prices fall, and after John's death Jeremiah spends the profits "in the bars of Lilongwe and Kasungu". To get electricity, William explains, a family must take a pickup to [[Kasungu]] and a minibus a hundred kilometres to the capital to apply at the ESCOM office in Magetsi House.
- **Why it matters:** Lilongwe is never visited in these chapters. It stands for the far-off markets and bureaucracy that decide what a farm family earns and whether it gets power, which is the gap William's windmill will close.
