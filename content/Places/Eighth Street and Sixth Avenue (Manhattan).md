# Eighth Street and Sixth Avenue (Manhattan)

Street corner in Greenwich Village, Manhattan. A turning point in “Humans of New York”: Corner where a closeted student finds Greenwich Village and meets Charlie, his first lover.

## Humans of New York — Humans of New York (2016)

Source: [Original](http://www.humansofnewyork.com/post/142356414341) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjrbrzqbm2z72nsqj8p2fd)

> Finally I get to the corner of 8th Street and 6th Avenue, and I’m waiting for the light to change. And this guy starts hitting on me.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** A closeted young man runs away from a college Christmas break by Greyhound to New York City and walks down 5th Avenue asking for the Village. At the corner of 8th Street and 6th Avenue a man named Charlie approaches him, and the night becomes his first gay bar and first sexual encounter.
- **Why it matters:** The corner is where the narrator's long fear and shame give way to his first experience of being accepted; the story hangs on arriving at this specific crossing.
