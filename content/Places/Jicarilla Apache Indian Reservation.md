## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs2q4n1m2vzvkcq3qpyng6)

> I was three days beyond my food pickup at South Fork Ranger Station and had crossed the first few miles of the New Mexico flatlands when I came to the narrow dirt road that followed the Divide across the Jicarilla Apache Indian Reservation, the first of several tribal reservations I would pass near in the roughly 30 more days of hiking to the Mexican border.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Eric follows a dirt road across the reservation, saddened by the litter along it. Apache men in trucks keep stopping to offer water or rides, and an old man tells him it "was better when we walked." Rain turns the clay road to mud.
- **Why it matters:** Opens the book's chapter on Indian country, and its reflections on what had been taken from the people there.
