# Atlantic City, Wyoming

Former gold-mining town at the south end of the Wind River Range, Wyoming. The Rybacks resupply there before crossing the Great Divide Basin.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs267w80jay5y1fhd4pmsh)

> We opened the door to the general store and stepped back 100 years into the American past.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** In the near-deserted mining town's general store, a dozen grizzled men sing over their beer. A young bartender in shirt and tie sells the brothers canned food and two water flasks and gives them directions across the basin.
- **Why it matters:** The last outpost before the desert, and the source of the route that soon goes wrong.
