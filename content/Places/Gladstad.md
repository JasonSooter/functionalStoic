# Gladstad

Small administrative centre of the island of [[Vega (Norway)|Vega]], Norway, with its supermarket and big white wooden church. In James Rebanks's memoir it is the tiny town Anna drives through at the end of the season, where her family lie buried and where the town gossips about her.

## The Place of Tides — James Rebanks (2024)

Source: [[The Place of Tides]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/315745/the-place-of-tides-by-rebanks-james/9780241426937) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8242bcb27r08rr5asj7a9b3)

> Through the tiny town of Gladstad. Past the supermarket. Past the giant, white wooden church where her family were buried.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Back on Vega after the island season, Anna drives Rebanks across the island to return Henning's rifle, along "the winding road across the island", through Gladstad, past the supermarket and the church where her family are buried, to the flatter part of the island where she once lived with Henning. On the way back they stop at the Spar in town, where women in an aisle watch and gossip about Anna's "younger man".
- **Why it matters:** A minor waypoint, but it is where the book shows the small-island community Anna returns to after the isolation of the duck island: watchful, gossiping, and holding her family's graves.
