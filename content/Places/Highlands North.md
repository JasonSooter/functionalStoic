# Highlands North

Northern suburb of [[Johannesburg]]. In Born a Crime it is the white suburb where Trevor Noah's family moves in his teens, and his home base through high school.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6z18pjqmctzaxq4j5jaxzxf)

> My mom found that house and bought it, which is how she snuck a black family into a place as white as Highlands North.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The family moves here after the Eden Park house is sold and Abel's garage fails. The house is a thirty-minute walk from H. A. Jack, and the long walk to Sandringham High School makes him late every day. In chapter 12 he writes that in Highlands North he was "the only black kid in the white suburb", a largely Jewish neighbourhood. The houses hide behind high walls with electric wire, and his only friends are the children of domestic workers. Later, a McDonald's opens two blocks from the house.
- **Why it matters:** the house sets up the loneliness of his teens and his friendship with Teddy, and it is the base for the mall and school scenes that follow.
