# Campagnolo headquarters (Vicenza)

Head office and factory of the component maker Campagnolo in [[Vicenza]], Italy; in It's All About the Bike Penn is refused a factory tour and interviews the marketing director in the boardroom instead.

## It's All About the Bike: In Pursuit of Happiness on Two Wheels — Robert Penn (2010)

Source: [[It's All About the Bike]] · [[It's All About the Bike - In Pursuit of Happiness on Two Wheels]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crbp04gcxzc001srb89ndc)

> We were sitting in the boardroom above the factory in Vicenza. For several weeks, I’d tried to convince the press office to let me have a tour of the factory. For several weeks, they’d refused.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Penn interviews Campagnolo's marketing director Lorenzo Taxis in the boardroom above the factory after weeks of being refused a tour. The company guards its know-how, so the boardroom is as far as he gets, and founder Tullio Campagnolo's story is told from there.
- **Why it matters:** Frames the Campagnolo section; the refusal to show the factory is the point of the scene.
