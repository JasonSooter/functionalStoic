# Yvesport (Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking)

Fictional fishing town on the Down East coast of Maine, most likely modelled on [[Eastport]]. In Aoibheann Sweeney's novel it is the mainland town across the channel from Crab Island, where the narrator goes to school and is always "from away".

## Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking — Aoibheann Sweeney (2007)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/among-other-things-ive-taken-up-smoking-9781101202227) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m765d043x3sj271a92c7ze47)

> Across the water I could see the harbor, Yvesport clustered on the other side of the channel, the boats and houses and empty sardine factories leading around the point to Estes Head Pier and Deep Cove.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Yvesport is the mainland town a mile across the water from Crab Island. Miranda attends Yvesport Elementary and has a schoolyard friendship with Julie Peabody, whose house is on Favor Street; Mr. Blackwell, the part-Passamaquoddy fisherman who once pulls her out of the channel, lives in a trailer on the south side. The sardine factories stand empty and the herring trade is dying.
- **Why it matters:** The town is the social world Miranda and her father stand apart from: "If you aren't born in Yvesport, then you are 'from away,' no matter how young you are when you arrive." Its closeness, and its distance, define the island childhood of Part 1.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Yvesport is invented; the novel names no real town.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Inferred. The text places it Down East in Maine and names Estes Head Pier, Deep Cove, Hanson's Point, empty sardine factories, a Canadian company buying the herring, and a Passamaquoddy family. Estes Head and Deep Cove are real places in Eastport, the old sardine capital beside the Passamaquoddy reservation at Pleasant Point. Reviews and interviews (Wikipedia, Irish America) only say "an island in Maine" and name no model. Chosen: Eastport. Alternative: Lubec, the neighbouring sardine town across Cobscook Bay.
