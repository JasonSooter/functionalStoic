# Top Notch (Austin)

Hamburger drive-in on Burnet Road in North Austin, Texas. In Greenlights it is the Dazed and Confused set where Matthew McConaughey improvises his first words on film.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zp42wd77sg3k05a8dnxe51)

> They were shooting at the Top Notch hamburger drive-in that night. I remember stepping out of the makeup trailer, in full wardrobe, onto the sidewalk off Burnet Road in North Austin about thirty yards from set.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** He has only come for a wardrobe check as Wooderson, who is in none of the night's scenes. Rick Linklater likes the look and asks whether Wooderson would try to pick up Cynthia, "the redheaded intellectual", who is sitting in a car at the drive-in. With no written lines and thirty minutes to think, he pulls up next to her car in Wooderson's car on his first ever film set.
- **Why it matters:** It is the start of his acting career; the book treats the improvised scene as the moment the role, and the path, opened up.
