# Mountain Dwellings

Terraced housing block by Bjarke Ingels in Ørestad, [[Copenhagen]], built over a parking garage. In _Happy City_ it is an attempt to give apartment dwellers suburban backyards in a dense, transit-served district.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m83qjq7014037w1ams4eke17)

> Ingels’s Mountain Dwellings stack eighty apartments with generous patios over eleven stories of sloping roof atop a neighborhood parking lot.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Montgomery presents Ingels's building as a fusion of suburban and urban living: each apartment gets a private backyard-like patio and a south-facing view, while the district stays dense enough to support transit.
- **Why it matters:** It is one of the book's designed hybrids between vertical and horizontal living, a short example rather than a scene.
