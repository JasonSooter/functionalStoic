# King Street Station

Seattle's historic railroad station south of downtown. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is where the Washington crews board the _Empire Builder_ for Poughkeepsie in 1934, seen off by a band and a thousand fans.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m86m5s4tasfby5bf9dtfyr9b)

> the ornate marble lobby of the King Street railroad station in Seattle, cheering and singing fight songs as the freshman and varsity crews boarded a Great Northern train

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On the evening of June 1, 1934, the university's marching band and more than a thousand fans crowd the station's marble lobby to send the freshman and varsity crews east on the Great Northern's _Empire Builder_. Few of the freshmen have ever left western Washington or ridden a train. The journey that follows crosses Stevens Pass, Montana and North Dakota on the way to Poughkeepsie.
- **Why it matters:** The send-off marks the crew's first step onto a national stage and shows how much the city has invested in them. It is a brief departure scene.
