## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W)

> Trowbridge Hall in Iowa City is a turn-of-the-century pile of red brick that houses the University of Iowa’s Earth Sciences department

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bryson visits the state geologists Ray Anderson and Brian Witzke in their cramped, low-ceilinged garret offices at the top of Trowbridge Hall. There they tell him about drilling Manson with the US Geological Survey and how refined dating cost Manson its claim to be the dinosaur killer.
- **Why it matters:** It is the scene of the book's first-hand account of the Manson story.
