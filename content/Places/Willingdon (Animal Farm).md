## Animal Farm — George Orwell (1945)

Source: [[Animal Farm]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/56486/animal-farm)

> Mr. Jones went into Willingdon and got so drunk at the Red Lion that he did not come back till midday on Sunday.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Willingdon is the human world's nearest town: Jones drinks at the Red Lion and later complains there; Mollie is seen outside a public-house beyond it; the lawyer Whymper lives there; eggs and goods are sold there; Boxer's hospital and Alfred Simmonds's knacker's van (Horse Slaughterer and Glue Boiler) are there. Old Major's show name was 'Willingdon Beauty'.
- **Why it matters:** The link between the closed farm and commerce; Boxer's one-way trip there is the book's cruellest scene.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The text gives only that it is the nearest town, with the Red Lion, a solicitor, a vet and an egg market. A real Willingdon lies beside Eastbourne, where Orwell went to prep school; pinned there as a best guess, though the novel's geography (near Wallington, Hertfordshire) does not fit Sussex. Alternative: an invented town near Wallington.
