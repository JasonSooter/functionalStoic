## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjq6edkfdk28492cpx47rb)

> One afternoon on a visit to her publishers in Tel Aviv, Nurit had a fender bender at a traffic light on King George Street, near Meir Garden.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Nurit dents the Mercedes of a Mekorot vice president and sends him a check and a copy of her book; he cashes the check.
- **Why it matters:** A small wry episode tied to the water-inequality thread.
