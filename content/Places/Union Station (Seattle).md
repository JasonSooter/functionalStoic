# Union Station (Seattle)

Former railway station in the Pioneer Square area of [[Seattle]]. In _The Boys in the Boat_ the Washington crews leave from here in June 1936 for Poughkeepsie and the Olympic trials.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m878tj0t23r2536ycpvhqz1g)

> a police escort led a convoy of cars carrying the Washington crews and coaches down Greek Row, past cheering students, then through downtown Seattle, en route to Union Station.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On the evening of June 10, 1936, a police escort leads the crews' convoy down Greek Row and through downtown to the station, where a band, cheerleaders, newsreel cameras and a large crowd see them off. Joe finds Joyce in the crowd holding a four-leaf-clover sign before the train pulls out for the East.
- **Why it matters:** It is the send-off for the crew's run at the national title and Berlin, and the moment the city adopts the boys as its own.
