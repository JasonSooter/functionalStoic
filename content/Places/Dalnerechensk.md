# Dalnerechensk

Town in Primorsky Krai on the Ussuri River, Russia's border with China, called Iman until 1972. In _Last of the Breed_ it is the border crossing that the fugitives Zhikarev and Natalya Baronas are both heading for.

|---|---|---|---|

## Last of the Breed — Louis L'Amour (1986)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL51048W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6y2dgdr6phg38pdtyqk6kxz)

> Iman! It was right on the river! If he could not wangle some way to cross the border to make their deal, he would be surprised.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Baronas weighs Voroshilov and Iman as ways over the border, and Yakov's map sends Natalya down the Vagou River, which "goes to near Iman, on the border." Separately, the fugitive fur trader Evgeny Zhikarev hears that Potanin has a post near Iman, offers a contact "in Hulin, just across the river", and boards the midnight truck praying, "Let me cross the river into China!" Kyra later reasons that the Baronases must make for the Ussuri and plans to fly to Iman herself.
- **Why it matters:** Iman is where the book's escape lines converge: with the sea blocked, the Ussuri crossing is the nearest way out of the Soviet Union, and the hunters know it too.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real; the book uses the town's old name, Iman. The text only anticipates arriving there in this part of the book.
