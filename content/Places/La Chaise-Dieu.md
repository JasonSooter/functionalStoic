# La Chaise-Dieu

Small mountain town in the Haute-Loire, in the Massif Central of [[France]]. In Clifford L. Graves's essay on Velocio it is the destination of the ride that turned Paul de Vivie into a cyclist.

## Velocio, Grand Seigneur — Clifford L. Graves
Source: [Original](https://cycling.ahands.org/bicycling/velocio.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7vbqtkskk8b108frj50f0s7)

> One day, on a bet, he rode sixty-six miles in six hours. The trip took him to the mountain resort of Chaise-Dieu. Suddenly he discovered a new world.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** On his first bicycle in 1881, Paul de Vivie rides to the mountain resort on a bet. The exercise, the fresh air and the countryside take possession of him, and the essay says his life was beginning to take shape.
- **Why it matters:** It is the moment the silk merchant becomes a cyclist; any long ride would have served, but the mountain destination foreshadows his later interest in climbing and gears.
