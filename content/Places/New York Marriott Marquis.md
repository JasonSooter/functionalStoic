## The Rise and Fall of the Hotel Mini-Bar — Priceonomics (2015)

Source: [Original](http://priceonomics.com/the-rise-and-fall-of-the-hotel-mini-bar/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fzbtyk433vx2fzn6rwjg0n)

> In 2004, New York’s Marriott Marquis — a 4-star behemoth in Times Square — excavated all 1,946 of its mini-bars, citing unfavorable operation costs and dwindling popularity.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** In 2004 the hotel removed all 1,946 of its mini-bars, citing operating costs and falling use, an early sign of the decline the article traces.
- **Why it matters:** It is the article's signal example of the mini-bar's fall, the counterpart to the Hong Kong Hilton's rise.
