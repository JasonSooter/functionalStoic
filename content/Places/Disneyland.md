## Dispatch from Disneyland — Study Hacks (2025)
Source: [Original](https://calnewport.com/dispatch-from-disneyland/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqtwg1yqxfa8e1q0mbb8eq)

> A few days ago, I went to Disneyland. I had been invited to Anaheim to give a speech about my books, and my wife and I decided to use the opportunity to take our boys on an early summer visit to the supposed happiest place on earth.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Cal Newport visits Disneyland in Anaheim with his wife and sons after a speaking invitation. He describes riding Pirates of the Caribbean and noticing its seams (jerky animatronics, acoustic panels in the 'sky'), and uses the park as an analogy for the diluted, purified stimulation of phones.
- **Why it matters:** The park is the article's concrete analogy for digital life; he liked it but was done after a couple of days.

## The Goldilocks Rule: How to Stay Motivated in Life and Business — James Clear (2016)

> In 1955, Disneyland had just opened in Anaheim, California, when a ten-year-old boy walked in and asked for a job.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Ten-year-old Steve Martin gets a job selling guidebooks at newly opened Disneyland, then moves to the magic shop where he starts performing.
- **Why it matters:** Origin point of Martin's career in the habit-building anecdote.
