# Markha Valley

Valley of the Markha River in Hemis National Park, [[Ladakh]], India, home to one of Ladakh's best-known treks. It is the route a lone cyclist rides loaded with bags in The Radavist's bikepacking piece.

## Touring the Indian Himalayas: Bikepacking the Markha Valley – Jan Zdansky — The Radavist (2025)
Source: [Original](https://theradavist.com/touring-the-indian-himalayas-bikepacking-the-markha-valley) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce399c378z03z9640c8ktj)

> My goal was the Markha Valley, which is home to probably the most famous and popular trek in Ladakh.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Jan Zdansky rides the Markha Valley trek from Leh on a loaded Surly, the only cyclist on the route and the only traveller without a guide. Past Chiling there is no bridge, only a hand-pulled cable car over the Zanskar River; then come repeated fords, a night of snow short of the Nimaling camp, and the climb to the 5,250-metre Konmaru La pushing the bike twenty steps at a time. He says he rode it in 2018 and the route may have changed.
- **Why it matters:** The valley is the whole subject: the piece is about what the terrain does to a rider on a bike built for it.
