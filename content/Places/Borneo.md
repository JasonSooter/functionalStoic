## Not the End of the World — Hannah Ritchie (2024)

Source: [[Not the End of the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL37518722W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebvr7j043pzc9qcbvf0bzs)

> Indonesia and Malaysia produce around 85% of the world’s palm oil. It’s undeniable that both countries have cut down forests to make space for their palm plantations.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Ritchie recounts hating palm oil after the 2018 Iceland advert, then finding in research that boycotting it would worsen deforestation. Indonesia and Malaysia produce about 85% of palm oil; a satellite study of Borneo found most plantations replaced already-logged land. Palm yields far more oil per hectare than alternatives.
- **Why it matters:** These countries are the geography of the palm oil argument: the case that a simple boycott is counterproductive depends on where and how the plantations were established.
