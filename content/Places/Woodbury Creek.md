# Woodbury Creek

Creek in Gloucester County, [[New Jersey]], flowing to the Delaware. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ it is where the first dinosaur bone was found in 1787, and promptly lost.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m80ghz0aw8nheek3dd8yqebp)

> found an enormous thighbone sticking out of a stream bank at a place called Woodbury Creek.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Chapter 6 opens here: someone, now forgotten, finds an enormous thighbone in the bank, probably a hadrosaur's. It is sent to Caspar Wistar in Philadelphia, who describes it without seeing its significance; it goes into a storeroom and disappears.
- **Why it matters:** It sets up the chapter's theme of discoveries missed or squandered; America had the chance to discover dinosaurs half a century early.
