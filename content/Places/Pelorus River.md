# Pelorus River

The Pelorus River is a river in the Marlborough region of New Zealand's South Island, reaching the sea at Havelock. It is pinned for _The Hobbit_ because Peter Jackson's Hobbit films shot the Forest River barrel escape there; it is not a place in the book.

## The Hobbit (Middle-Earth Universe) — J. R. R. Tolkien (1937)

Source: [[The Hobbit]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL27482W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m778rvv79r3hs7mfz5vvc1)

> Out they went under the overhanging branches of the trees on either bank.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** In the book, Bilbo frees the dwarves from the Elvenking's dungeons by packing them into empty barrels that are tipped through trapdoors into a stream and out of the water-gate. Clinging to a barrel, he rides the Forest River under the overhanging trees until the barrels are roped into a raft and floated down to Lake-town.
- **Why it matters:** The barrel ride is the hinge between the prison of Mirkwood and the welcome of Lake-town, and Bilbo's first escape he plans alone.
- **Real vs. fictional:** This is where Peter Jackson's Hobbit films were shot; it is not a place in the book.
- **Where is it?:** Wikipedia (The Hobbit film series, filming locations) lists the Pelorus River in Marlborough as a location for the Forest River (citing the Marlborough Express via Stuff, 2011), alongside the Aratiatia Spillway near Taupō.
