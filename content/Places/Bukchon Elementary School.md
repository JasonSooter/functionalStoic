# Bukchon Elementary School

Primary school in Bukchon on Jeju's north coast, site of the Bukchon massacre of January 1949. In _The Island of Sea Women_ it is where Young-sook loses her husband and son.

## At a glance

| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Island of Sea Women | Lisa See | turning point | The 1949 massacre: Jun-bu and Sung-soo are shot and Mi-ja walks away. |

## The Island of Sea Women — Lisa See (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19865296W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvcjhzrrvz8bhk5t0zpcda)

> We were steered through the school’s main gates. Husbands, brothers, and fathers were separated and pushed to the left.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Soldiers herd the village into the schoolyard, men to the left, where ten elders already lie shot. When the commander seizes Yu-ri, Jun-bu steps forward and is shot, their son Sung-soo is killed against the school wall, and Mi-ja refuses to take Young-sook's children before walking away with her husband. In the acknowledgements Lisa See notes that the real massacre site is now a garlic field with a small memorial tablet.
- **Why it matters:** It is the novel's central atrocity and the source of Young-sook's lifelong grievance against Mi-ja.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The Bukchon massacre is real; the characters are fictional.
