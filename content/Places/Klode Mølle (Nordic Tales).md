## Nordic Tales — Chronicle Books (2019)

Source: [Original](https://chroniclebooks.com/products/nordic-tales) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmszj9nywkte1evsf4zpgw)

> a mansion down near Klode Mill, in the district of Lysgaard.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Toller and his wife serve at a mansion near Klode Mill in Lysgaard, then are given a cottage on a wild heath studded with grave-mounds said to house the Mount-folk. They live there as neighbours of the trolls for years, and the heath becomes farmland. The Mount-folk finally leave Jutland because of the church bells.
- **Why it matters:** The tale is explicitly rooted in a named Jutland district and its heath and burial mounds; set elsewhere the mound-dwelling folk and the 'heath turned to fields' motif would lose their local anchor. The text names the mill and district but not the cottage's exact spot.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Klode Mill and Lysgaard are real; the cottage and mounds are not specifically identified.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Clues: 'near Klode Mill, in the district of Lysgaard', 'middle of a wild heath', 'leave Jutland'. Klode Mølle is a hamlet on the Jutland heath, in the historic Lysgård herred; chosen as the named landmark. Alternative: Lysgård hamlet (56.3507, 9.3390).
