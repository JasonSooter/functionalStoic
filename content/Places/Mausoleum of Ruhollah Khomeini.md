## Rick Steves' Iran (2009) — Rick Steves' Europe (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CYoa9hI3CXg) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqx55eg7j7753vsb5m5x3v)

> Just outside of town, we dropped by the great mosque containing the tomb of Ayatollah Khomeini.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** Just outside Tehran Steves visits the mosque holding Khomeini's tomb, which can hold up to a million worshipers and is being expanded with small donations from the poor. He finds it easygoing and describes how Khomeini is seen as a sage and champion of traditional values.
- **Why it matters:** It lets Steves revise his image of Khomeini and show why the revolution's leader still inspires ordinary Iranians.
