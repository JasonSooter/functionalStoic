# Hamburg Rathaus

Hamburg's city hall, in the old town of [[Hamburg]]. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is the American Olympic team's first stop in Germany, where a Nazi mayor welcomes them in July 1936.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m87k3gq4wg2911v3ejm6zb2v)

> They waved and smiled and climbed aboard buses and were driven through narrow streets to Hamburg’s Rathaus

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After a floodlit voyage up the Elbe the night before, the SS Manhattan docks in Hamburg early on July 24, 1936, and the boys unload the Husky Clipper in the rain. The team is greeted by an oompah band, then bused to the Rathaus, where the city's Bürgermeister, the staunch Nazi Carl Vincent Krogmann, gives a long oration in German; not understanding a word, the boys "just sat and took it", then perk up as officials hand out free cigars, wine, beer and orange juice. By noon they are on a train to Berlin.
- **Why it matters:** The first taste of the regime's lavish welcome, a brief waypoint that opens the book's Berlin chapters.
