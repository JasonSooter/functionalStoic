# Numantia

Numantia was a Celtiberian hill town near Soria in northern Spain, destroyed by Rome in 133 BC. _Sapiens_ opens its chapter on empires with the siege.

## Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind — Yuval Noah Harari (2014)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17075811W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75s3m9rfaxcxy381b7ke0)

> A small, insignificant mountain town called Numantia, inhabited by the peninsula’s native Celts, had dared to throw off the Roman yoke.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The empires chapter opens in 134 BC with Scipio Aemilianus besieging Numantia with over 30,000 soldiers until the starving Numantians burn their town. Centuries later it became a Spanish symbol of freedom, celebrated by Cervantes and made a national monument, yet the patriots honour it in a language, religion and law inherited from Rome.
- **Why it matters:** It is the chapter's emblem of how empires erase conquered peoples and then absorb even their memory.
