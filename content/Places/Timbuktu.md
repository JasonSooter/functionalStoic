# Timbuktu

Historic trade and learning city on the edge of the Sahara in Mali. In Greenlights it is a stop on Matthew McConaughey's way to the Bandiagara.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m700jgcrp6p2pmvfex34keca)

> On the way to the Bandiagara, we stopped in the legendary town of Timbuktu. A center for art and learning, it is a quiet little trade settlement situated just north of the Niger River on the south side of the Sahara Desert.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On the way to the Bandiagara the group stops in Timbuktu. After an afternoon racing camels in the Sahara they are finishing dinner on the hotel veranda when a young woman working as a prostitute approaches. Issa's friends Ali and Amadou argue over whether to judge her; when he sides with Ali, Ali snaps that it is about understanding, and Amadou tells him, "You'd better be different, not sorry."
- **Why it matters:** A brief waypoint; its legend adds colour, but the scene's point is the argument, which could happen in any town on the route.
