# Tarrytown (Austin)

Quiet residential neighbourhood in west Austin, Texas. In Greenlights it is where Matthew McConaughey rents a house, and where the 1999 "bongo naked" arrest happens.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m700j9wmgg9ebpn91x3ymfse)

> I decided to rent a two-bedroom house in the sleepy little neighborhood of Tarrytown, in the heart of Austin, Texas.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After more than three years living on the road he rents a two-bedroom house in Tarrytown, where dogs run off the leash. In October 1999, after a long party following a Texas football win, police break in on him playing the bongos naked at 2:30 a.m. and arrest him. The local paper prints a picture of the house with its address, it becomes a tourist attraction, and he leaves the peace he had found on Meadowbrook Drive.
- **Why it matters:** "Fame can change people, but in this case, it changed a place": the neighbourhood is his attempt at anonymity, and losing it pushes him back to Los Angeles.
