# Circle, Alaska

Small community on the Yukon River in Alaska, a mining town in the 1890s. In _The Call of the Wild_ Buck attacks 'Black' Burton in its bar to defend Thornton.

## The Call of the Wild — Jack London (1903)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14942956W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne5qk0ee71g354frrbwp0j)

> It was at Circle City, ere the year was out, that Pete’s apprehensions were realized. “Black” Burton, a man evil-tempered and malicious, had been picking a quarrel with a tenderfoot at the bar, when Thornton stepped good-naturedly between.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Black Burton picks a quarrel with a tenderfoot at a bar in Circle City and knocks Thornton down when he intervenes. Buck leaps for Burton's throat and tears it open; a miners' meeting rules the attack justified and Buck is discharged.
- **Why it matters:** Buck's reputation spreads through every camp in Alaska, and it is his first killing of a man in defence of Thornton.
