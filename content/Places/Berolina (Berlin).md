# Berolina (Berlin)

Large housing cooperative of modernist blocks in the former East [[Berlin]]. In _Happy City_ its residents turn an unused lawn into a community garden.

## At a glance
| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m83qjhbq2650r5p8bejz0r95)

> In the summer of 2012, I met up with a group of elderly women on a lawn in the heart of Berolina, a massive Soviet housing cooperative in the former East Berlin.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Montgomery meets elderly women on a lawn among Berolina's high-modernist blocks, one of them 437 yards long, around open space nobody used. With Humboldt University agronomists and BMW Guggenheim Lab psychologist Corrine Rose, long-time residents have built a community garden, planting herbs and planning to garden with schoolchildren.
- **Why it matters:** Gardening turns sterile common ground into a meeting place, which supports the book's claim that touching nature, not just seeing it, matters.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The cooperative owns many blocks in Mitte and the book does not say which lawn; no pin yet.
