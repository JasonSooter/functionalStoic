# Warnervale

Small locality on the Central Coast of New South Wales, Australia, north of Sydney. In Greenlights it is where the teenage Matthew McConaughey spends a fraught exchange year with the Dooley family.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zp3w2qdczjx59r9pytp681)

> It read Warnervale, pop. 305. With no civilization in view, we drove another mile past that sign, and took the first left turn we could, then the first right, then pulled into a gravel driveway up to the garage door of the only house in sight

- **Role:** setting / turning point
- **In the book:** After the "near the beach, on the outskirts of Sydney" promise, his host Norvel Dooley drives him from Sydney airport past Gosford, Toukley and the one-street town of Gorokan, down a dirt road to the only house in sight. There he lives with Norvel and Marjorie Dooley under strange house rules, turns vegetarian and runs every day. During a tornado warning he runs to the house of the local Rotary Club president, Harris Stewart, to ask for a new family; when Norvel orders him to "unpack your bags" for the rest of the year, he puts his fist through the bedroom door and leaves.
- **Why it matters:** The Australian year is the book's lesson in endurance and the turning point of his youth; the isolation of Warnervale is what makes it so hard.
