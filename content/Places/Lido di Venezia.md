# Lido di Venezia

Barrier island and beach resort across the lagoon from [[Venice]], Italy, where Aschenbach watches Tadzio and dies in Thomas Mann's Death in Venice.

## Death in Venice — Thomas Mann (1912)
Source: [[Death in Venice]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL16246083W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5craw729rbxc5fn3awt63h6)

> The view of the beach, the spectacle of civilization indulging in carefree sensuality on the brink of the watery element, entertained and pleased him as rarely before.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On the Lido's hotel beach Aschenbach settles in a rented cabana and spends his mornings watching Tadzio. In the last chapter, on a nearly deserted autumn beach, he watches Tadzio walk out along a sandbar, turns slack in his chair and dies.
- **Why it matters:** The beach is the novella's main stage and the foil to the infected city across the water; the sandbar death scene needs this shore.
