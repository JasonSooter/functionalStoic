# Pul-e Khumri

Town in Baghlan Province, northern Afghanistan. In _Full Tilt_ Dervla Murphy's broken ribs are treated here, ending her trip north.

## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)
Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6mamqt0kjs7gnccjhpbga)

> A young Afghan doctor said that three ribs are broken; he plastered them and ordered me to bed and banned cycling for a month.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** At the German-built hospital she is plastered, then re-strapped by a Czech doctor, and told that Mazar-i-Sharif must be abandoned. A scorpion bites her toe, and she moves to the Germans' former club hotel and tours the textile works.
- **Why it matters:** This is the turning point forcing her back to Kabul and ending the northern excursion.
