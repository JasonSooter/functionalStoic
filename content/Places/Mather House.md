# Mather House

Concrete undergraduate house of [[Harvard University]] by the Charles River in Cambridge, Massachusetts. In _Happy City_ it is the house students dread in the housing lottery, which turns out to make them no less happy.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m83qj8r4y4xcxdyah8xcrrza)

> The concrete tower of Mather House was described in _The Harvard Crimson_, the student newspaper, as a “riot-proof monstrosity designed by a prison architect,” although its bacchanalian soap-foam parties have achieved mythical status.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Mather's concrete tower is the least wanted outcome of the Harvard housing lottery. In Elizabeth Dunn's study, students sent to houses like it ended up happier than they had predicted, while those in the most prestigious houses were less happy than they expected.
- **Why it matters:** Paired with [[Lowell House]], it shows that the social life inside a building matters more to happiness than its looks or prestige.
