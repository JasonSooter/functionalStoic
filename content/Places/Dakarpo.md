# Dakarpo

Ancient Buddhist monastery on an outcrop above the Shaba valley in Bhutan. In The Comfort Crisis (Michael Easter) a monk below it tells him Americans don't know how good they have it.

## The Comfort Crisis — Michael Easter (2021)
Source: [[The Comfort Crisis]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/634446/the-comfort-crisis-by-michael-easter/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g0f7j3c3gr7fffqvy00ccq)

> It was wooden, tin roofed, and in the shadow of Dakarpo, an ancient Buddhist monastery built on an outcropping overlooking the Shaba valley.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Easter hikes to the cliffside shack of the monk Khenpo Phuntsho Tashi below the monastery, is purified with incense and water, and hears that Americans chase a checklist of acquisitions; pilgrims circle Dakarpo 108 times outside.
- **Why it matters:** The monk's verdict is the book's sharpest outside view of the comfort it criticises.
