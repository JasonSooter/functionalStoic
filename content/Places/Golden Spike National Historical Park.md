## Golden Spike National Historic Site — Jess (2015)
Source: [Original](http://currentlywandering.com/2015/07/13/golden-spike-national-historic-site/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ga1cx9j073d75p0e3pmw1e)

> On May 10, 1869 the Union and Central Pacific Railroads joined at Promontory Summit, Utah Territory completed the first transcontinental railroad.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The family visits the place where the Union and Central Pacific met in 1869, sees the replica locomotives Jupiter and No. 119 in a steam demonstration, hears a ranger talk and earns Junior Ranger badges. The author stresses how remote it is, west of Brigham City.
- **Why it matters:** The whole post is about this site and the event it marks.
