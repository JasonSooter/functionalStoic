# Orlando East

Oldest part of Soweto, in Johannesburg. In Born a Crime it is where Trevor Noah's grandmother lives, and where much of his early childhood takes place.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6yq3yks5nzt6nka6t81bq50)

> My grandmother lived in Orlando East. She had a two-room house.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapters 2–4. The two-room house holds his grandmother, his aunt, cousins and an uncle. Under apartheid a light-skinned child could not be seen in the street, so Trevor is kept inside, and at three he digs under the driveway gate to escape. Chapter 3 is set around its outdoor toilet and the Tuesday prayer meetings, and his hidden mess on the kitchen floor triggers an emergency meeting against "the demon in the house". In Chapter 4 he is so conspicuous that neighbours give directions by him: "The house on Makhalima Street. At the corner you'll see a light-skinned boy."
- **Why it matters:** the house is where the book shows what "born a crime" meant day to day: a child who had to be hidden. It is also where the women-run household and faith that shaped him are set.
