# Newlyn

Fishing port beside Penzance in Cornwall, England. In _Cod_ it is the British example of a fishing town in decline.

## Cod: A Biography of the Fish That Changed the World — Mark Kurlansky (1997)
Source: [[Cod]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/1032753/cod) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kksswqvakbeftj11ec8z1b)

> But Newlyn is a fishing town—or, increasingly, an out-of-work fishing town.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Unlike its resort neighbours Penzance and Mousehole, Newlyn is a working port where trawlers are being decommissioned. Skipper William Hooper and the Cornish Fish Producers Organization's Mike Townsend talk about falling stocks; locals, recalling Spanish raiders of centuries past, blame the Spanish fleet and fly Canadian flags when Canada arrests the Estai.
- **Why it matters:** A British case of a fishing town losing its cod, and of nationalist blame.
