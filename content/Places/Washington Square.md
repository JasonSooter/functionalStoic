> Then afterward we went to his mom’s loft apartment on Washington Square.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** After the gay bar, Charlie takes the narrator to his mother's loft on Washington Square while she is in Rome. The narrator describes the first night together and the next morning when Charlie tells him to take off the towel, his first lesson in shedding shame.
- **Why it matters:** The loft is the sheltered, door-closed space where the narrator first feels safe; the text gives no detail of the square itself, so its role is as an address.
