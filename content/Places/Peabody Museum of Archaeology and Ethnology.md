# Peabody Museum of Archaeology and Ethnology

Harvard's anthropology museum in Cambridge, Massachusetts. In The Comfort Crisis (Michael Easter) it is where Dan Lieberman explains that humans are built to carry.

## The Comfort Crisis — Michael Easter (2021)

Source: [[The Comfort Crisis]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/634446/the-comfort-crisis-by-michael-easter/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g16w6mftc4rqh28mvb4ts8)

> It was at the top floor of Harvard’s Peabody Museum of Archaeology and Ethnology (think: a place where Indiana Jones might store his finds).

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Easter meets the anthropologist Dan Lieberman in his office on the top floor; Lieberman explains that humans evolved for endurance and carrying rather than strength.
- **Why it matters:** The conversation sets up the book's part on carrying loads.
