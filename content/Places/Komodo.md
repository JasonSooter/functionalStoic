# Komodo

Island in Indonesia's Lesser Sunda chain, home of the Komodo dragon; the goal and climax of the 1956 Zoo Quest in _Life on Air_.

## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2qsebqdx37car2wvx8p3)

> The headman received us in his thatched house. Yes, it was Komodo; and yes, there were buaja darat, land crocodiles, roaming around in the hills.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The destination of the expedition. From a village beside a bay, they hang a goat carcass in a dry stream bed and wait behind a hide; a dragon appears ten yards behind them. They later trap, measure (just over nine feet) and release one, and learn the headman's story of the gun-running captain.
- **Why it matters:** The climax of the chapter and the reason for the whole trip: the first view of the Komodo dragon.
