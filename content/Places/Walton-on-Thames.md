# Walton-on-Thames

Riverside town in Surrey ([[England]]). In Three Men in a Boat (Jerome K. Jerome) the party sculls past it.

## Three Men in a Boat — Jerome K. Jerome (1889)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1793164W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gbyes2f4n39av9gvfzhx16)

> We sculled up to Walton, a rather large place for a riverside town.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** The three scull up to Walton, a large town of which only a corner reaches the water. The narrator recalls Caesar's camp, Elizabeth, Cromwell and Bradshaw, the iron scold's bridle in the church, and the Duchess of York's dog cemetery at Oatlands Park.
- **Why it matters:** A typical stop for the book's mix of history and jokes.
