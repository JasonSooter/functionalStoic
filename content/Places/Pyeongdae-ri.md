# Pyeongdae-ri

Coastal village in Gujwa-eup on Jeju's north-east coast. In _The Island of Sea Women_ the haenyeo protest against the Japanese ends at the district office here.

## The Island of Sea Women — Lisa See (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19865296W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvcsnhjv7sdenk337z542h)

> By the time we reached Pyeongdae, thousands of women had come together.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In early January the Hado haenyeo gather at the Sehwa five-day market and march on, shouting independence slogans. By Pyeongdae thousands of women have come together at the Japanese district office, where organisers present demands, soldiers guard the door and Young-sook's mother shouts for independence; a man takes the list inside and nothing is negotiated.
- **Why it matters:** The protest is the climax of Part One's early chapters and Young-sook's proudest memory of her mother; arrests and a few concessions follow.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The 1932 Jeju haenyeo protest against Japanese rule is a real event.
