## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m700j5ac0mc8c1s0sbp9zbct)

> The Monastery of Christ in the Desert sits in miles of undisturbed desert, on the banks of the Chama River, in Abiquiu, New Mexico.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After the success of A Time to Kill he feels lost in excess and guilt and goes looking for a monk to talk to. He walks in on the washed-out 13.5-mile dirt road, arrives after sundown and is welcomed by Brother Andre. On a long walk he pours out his confessions to Brother Christian, who listens in silence and at the end whispers, "Me, too."
- **Why it matters:** The episode turns his post-fame crisis into one of the book's lessons: sometimes we need to hear we are not the only one, not advice. The remoteness of the desert monastery, reachable only on foot, is part of the penance.
