## The bohemian investor — The Investor (2015)
Source: [Original](https://monevator.com/the-bohemian-investor/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gbgn5rbmja5gezyeacfjzn)

> A few Mondays ago I was enjoying drinks at a friend’s book launch in Bloomsbury.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** At a friend's book launch, old friends tease the author as a 'bohemian who became a banker'. He later notes that the original Bloomsbury bohemians mostly lived on inherited wealth.
- **Why it matters:** The district's history gives the essay its twist: bohemian freedom often rested on capital.
