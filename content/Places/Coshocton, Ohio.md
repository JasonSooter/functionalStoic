## Distributing Awesome Developer Swag Across The Globe — Nick Harley (2016)

Source: [Original](https://raygun.com/blog/2016/05/developer-swag/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jkz7z8bvk7vyr4jfynntrf)

> Seeing a child drop her schoolbooks in the dirt on Main Street spurred Meek to approach his friend, Mr. Cantwell, owner of Cantwell Shoes, with an idea about building store traffic, name recognition, and ultimately increasing sales.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The article names Coshocton, Ohio as the home of printers Jasper Meek and Henry Beach, considered originators of the branded merchandise industry. Meek saw a child drop her schoolbooks on Main Street and pitched Cantwell Shoes on imprinted book bags.
- **Why it matters:** The town is the origin story the article uses for promotional products; set elsewhere the anecdote loses its claim to be the industry's beginning.
