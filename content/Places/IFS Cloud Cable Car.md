## The Surprising Success of Gondola Transit Systems — Wendover Productions (2025)

Source: [Original](https://youtube.com/watch?v=a5126u88E7E\&si=1gWTN5lb3hW199bC) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e31105sgyszcwkzkee7d4d)

> Just look to London.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the video:** London's cable car over the Thames, championed by Boris Johnson, drew an average of just 37 people per day in the 7-9 a.m. commuting hours in 2013 and is mocked as a white elephant. It survives as a tourist attraction with 1.5 million riders in 2023.
- **Why it matters:** The counter-example showing gondolas fail as transit where trains already serve both sides.
