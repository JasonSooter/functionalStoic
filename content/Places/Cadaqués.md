## Mini-Retirement: How to Retire Before You’re Ready — Chad Carson

> Finally, during the last 2 weeks we decided to settle down in a remote fishing town called Cadaques in northeastern Spain along the rocky Mediterranean coast near France.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** The couple spent the last two weeks of the Spain leg in this remote fishing town. After these lazy, thoughtful days the author says he finally relaxed inside, as if a knot had loosened.
- **Why it matters:** The release he felt here convinced him mini-retirements were essential, which is the premise of the article.
