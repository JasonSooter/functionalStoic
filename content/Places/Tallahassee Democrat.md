## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fzdf46b2dh4ke8qpmmgq2m)

> editor, in the autumn of 2003, I saw the tall, slim, light-skinned actor Christopher Duncan.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** In autumn 2003 Kendi, an intern, is called into editor Mizell Stewart's office over a column about White people. Stewart describes being treated as a threat when pulled over, and Kendi sees his own racism mirrored; he must end the column or lose the internship.
- **Why it matters:** The meeting starts his turn from anti-White racism toward antiracism.
