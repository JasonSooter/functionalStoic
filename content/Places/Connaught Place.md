# Connaught Place

Circular commercial centre of New Delhi, India. In _On the Hippie Trail_ Rick and Gene base themselves at the YMCA near it.

## On the Hippie Trail — Rick Steves (2025)

Source: [[On the Hippie Trail]] · [Original](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/rick-steves/on-the-hippie-trail/9781641717458/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kjewd96ajhphs3nb8szsys)

> we got to the “Times Square” of modern India, Connaught Place, and checked into our room at the YMCA.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Flying from Kashmir into monsoon-soaked Delhi, they check into the YMCA near Connaught Place, India's "Times Square". They feud with the dining-room waiters and invent a game of fan baseball in their room.
- **Why it matters:** Their home base in Delhi and the source of running jokes.
