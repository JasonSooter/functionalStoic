## The Anxious Generation — Jonathan Haidt (2024)
Source: [[The Anxious Generation]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL37575384W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kk2jp241wx72prxpq1qnsv)

> New York City is blessed with one such playground on Governors Island, the best playground my children have ever experienced.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Haidt describes the adventure playground run by play:groundNYC, where signs ask parents not to interfere and children build towers and forts from loose parts. He watches a boy hit his thumb with a hammer, shake it off and keep working, while staff step in only for serious risks.
- **Why it matters:** The playground is his model of the risky free play the book argues children need.
