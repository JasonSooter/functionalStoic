# University of Pennsylvania

The University of Pennsylvania is a research university in Philadelphia. In _Abundance_ it is where Katalin Karikó was demoted for her mRNA work and then met Drew Weissman at a photocopier.

## Abundance — Ezra Klein & Derek Thompson (2025)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL44034521W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m74z7cve90ce3p8071v3qc)

> The nearest large Xerox machine was in a different hall, inside Robert Wood Johnson Pavilion, which housed the biomedical library.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** At Penn, Karikó pursued mRNA while grants went to DNA research; funders rejected her so often she lost count, and the university demoted her to "senior research investigator". In the fall of 1997 she went to photocopy articles at the Robert Wood Johnson Pavilion, which housed the biomedical library, and while waiting struck up a conversation with immunologist Drew Weissman. Their partnership led to the mRNA work behind the COVID vaccines.
- **Why it matters:** It is the chapter's main setting for how the research funding system nearly lost a world-changing idea, rescued by a chance meeting.
