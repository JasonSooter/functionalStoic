## Gare de Orange — Mostly Bikes x Me (2025)

Source: [Original](https://funkadelic.net/pages/15442925722.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dck593c62adz4p614ce743)

> Caromb turns out the lights at night, but they flick on before we exit towards Aubignan.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The ride begins before dawn in the still-warm village, where the riders lock the shutters at a quarter to six; Caromb's street lights come back on just as they leave towards Aubignan, heading for the train at Orange.
- **Why it matters:** The start of the day's journey north; the village is the riders' base in these posts.

## Lac du Paty — Mostly Bikes x Me (2025)

Source: [Original](https://funkadelic.net/pages/15422939049.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dck830bwwdt0zrhapgmkhd)

> The Lac du Paty is Caromb’s water supply and, by necessity, is located both near the village and well above it.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** A post about riding up to the Lac du Paty, the village's water supply: it sits near Caromb but well above it, and the fastest way up is a set of steep switchbacks, painful on a hot day; gravel shortcuts and a longer northern approach exist, but there is no avoiding the climb.
- **Why it matters:** The lake is the whole subject; it has no standard-source coordinate, so it is recorded here under the village.

## Big blue box, little blue box — Mostly Bikes x Me (2025)

Source: [Original](https://funkadelic.net/pages/15234081625.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dr1f5xg32f0fkev82zmqzy)

> Upon my return I swung by Saint Marc, Caromb’s large and friendly cooperative wine maker for and the second blue box of the day, a 3L cube of dry, easy drinking, rosé.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** A cyclist based in Caromb makes a round trip to IKEA against the mistral wind, then stops at the village cooperative winery for a 3L box of rosé. Barricades are going up in the village for a Friday-evening moules frites fundraiser for the sporting club.
- **Why it matters:** Caromb is the home base and village setting of the short post.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real; Saint Marc cooperative is a minor sub-site (not reported separately).

## Hello Again Provence. — Mostly Bikes x Me (2025)

Source: [Original](https://funkadelic.net/pages/15114818679.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dr1gvzedaan813p1rg7qz8)

> Perched upon the rampart of Caromb, a lively village between Mont Ventoux and Les Dentelles de Montmirail, it has three compact levels and a cellar.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author announces that he and his partner have bought a three-level 1880 house on the rampart of Caromb, between Mont Ventoux and the Dentelles de Montmirail. They plan to return repeatedly before Paris-Brest-Paris 2027.
- **Why it matters:** Caromb becomes their base for Provence cycling.

## Tour de France — Mostly Bikes x Me (2025)

Source: [Original](https://funkadelic.net/pages/15200806826.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dr1jh27g2xnm16fj28bg3j)

> the breaks and the peloton careened down Caromb's narrow ring road and hooked a right towards Crillon Le Brave, Bedoin and their finish for the day at the top of Mont Ventoux

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** On the day of the Tour de France Ventoux stage the writer watches from under a plane tree on the wall of the village's 14th century church yard, pouring beers into jars with the crowd. The caravan and then the breakaway and peloton race down Caromb's narrow ring road.
- **Why it matters:** The vantage point for watching the 2025 Tour stage that finished on Mont Ventoux.
