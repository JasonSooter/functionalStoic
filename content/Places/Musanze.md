## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy7njpftna9teyqcq38egn)

> At the Security Police Headquarters we were lined up against a wall and given a detailed body search.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After being stopped at gunpoint by a Belgian official, the crew are taken to Ruhengeri, searched and locked in an open-air barbed-wire enclosure. They are interrogated, then expelled, keeping their relabelled film.
- **Why it matters:** The tense end to the gorilla trip, minor as a place but plot-relevant.
