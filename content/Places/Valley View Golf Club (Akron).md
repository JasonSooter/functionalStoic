> In Akron, Ohio, it cost \$3 million to purchase Valley View Golf Club and more to ecologically restore its 200 acres.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Summit Metro Parks bought the course in 2016, stripped the land and planted native meadow and over 120,000 nut trees. It links 1,800 acres of green space in Summit County.
- **Why it matters:** A full-scale restoration example, ending with the anecdote of the interviewee's father liking it.
