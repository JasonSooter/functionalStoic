## Quiet — Susan Cain (2012)

Source: [[Quiet]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/55983/quiet-by-cain-susan/9780141970172) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g0ffbqatmvs69c9knkgwr5)

> They behave the same way inside the social hothouse of the Spangler Center, the sumptuously decorated student center.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Cain visits in autumn, watches students parade through the Spangler Center and interviews a first-year introvert about the school's participation-driven culture.
- **Why it matters:** Her second case study of the Extrovert Ideal, in an elite training ground for leaders.

## Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World — David Epstein (2019)

Source: [[Range]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550188/range-by-david-epstein/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvsw0zyxqb8d01pefvxv6s)

> It was early afternoon in fall, and Jake and six of his second-year Harvard Business School classmates found a shady spot where they could eat their lunches and talk.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** A student group debates whether the fictional Carter Racing team should race, voting 4-3 to race, and the next day the professor reveals the data are the Challenger launch data. The classroom exposes how bright students rely only on the data put in front of them.
- **Why it matters:** It frames the chapter's lesson about familiar tools; the setting itself is the stage for a teaching trick.
