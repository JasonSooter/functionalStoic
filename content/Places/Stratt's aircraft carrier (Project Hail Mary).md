## Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir (2021)
Source: [[Project Hail Mary]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/611060/project-hail-mary-by-andy-weir/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvsjnx2tcht6ekmr0ccpyr)

> Despite objections from the sailors aboard, the name stuck. We wandered around the South China Sea, never getting too close to land.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Grace lands on the carrier by helicopter, is led through its passageways to an officer's conference room, and presents his breeding discovery to an international council. He sleeps in an officers' bunkroom, showers in the locker room, eats in the conference room, then spends weeks building the Astrophage breeder in the hangar deck. Stratt explains the carrier was chosen so an accidental Astrophage explosion would not harm cities, and later drinks gin there while deciding on the comas. Dimitri shows Stratt and Grace his spin-drive test in a vacuum chamber on the hangar deck. Grace reluctantly hands over two grams of Astrophage; the test produces 60,000 N for 100 microseconds and melts a metric ton of silicon block, cooled with seawater. Dimitri notes the carrier is 300 km from land in case of a catastrophe. Grace meets Dr. Lokken at a steel table in a small room of the carrier. She shows him a CERN draft paper explaining that Astrophage stores energy as neutrinos, and then asks how space radiation works so she can design Astrophage-slurry radiation shielding for the Hail Mary hull.
- **Why it matters:** It is Project Hail Mary's base for the early planning: where the mission, the Tau Ceti target, the fuel plan and the coma-crew decision are all made. The carrier name is invented but the sea is real.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The carrier (_Gansu_, nicknamed Stratt's Vat) is invented. The text says "We wandered around the South China Sea, never getting too close to land" and later that it kept about 300 km from land. Pinned at the sea's representative point.
