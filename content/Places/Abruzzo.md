## The Book That Taught Me What I Want to Teach My Daughter – The New Yorker — Belle Boggs (2016)
Source: [Original](https://www.newyorker.com/books/page-turner/the-book-that-taught-me-what-i-want-to-teach-my-daughter) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy7p9bcqnq5pvn90n340p0)

> or they appear only through their toys, which “covered the floor” beneath the table where her husband wrote at their home in the Abruzzi, in Fascist-imposed exile.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Boggs describes Ginzburg's essays about her wartime exile with her family in the Abruzzi, where her husband wrote at a table while the children's toys covered the floor. The essay 'Winter in the Abruzzi' about her last winter with her first husband moves a student to tears.
- **Why it matters:** It is the emotional ground of the book Boggs discusses, though the region is evoked briefly rather than described.
