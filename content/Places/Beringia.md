# Beringia

Beringia is the region around the Bering Strait where an Ice Age land bridge joined Siberia and Alaska. _Sapiens_ describes it as the route by which the first humans walked into the Americas.

## Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind — Yuval Noah Harari (2014)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17075811W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m92dtjtney85evrhck4xcr)

> For mammoth and man alike, Alaska was a mere extension of Siberia.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Sapiens bands learned to survive Arctic Siberia with snowshoes, furs and needles, following the mammoths, and around 14,000 BC walked across the land bridge into Alaska. When the glaciers melted about 12,000 BC they spread south through the Americas, reaching Tierra del Fuego by 10,000 BC.
- **Why it matters:** It is the route by which humans reached the Americas, and the start of the second great wave of extinctions.
