## An Army of Robots Is China’s Weapon in Trump’s Tariff War - The New York Times — Keith Bradsher (2025)
Source: [[An Army of Robots Is China’s Weapon in Trump’s Tariff War - The New York Times]] · [Original](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/04/23/business/china-tariffs-robots-automation.html?partner=slack&smid=sl-share) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2zb4hb187pvx4zka63zvq)

> A robot sorts lug nuts before attaching wheels to electric cars at a Zeekr plant in Ningbo, China.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The reporter describes a huge Zeekr electric-car factory in Ningbo with 820 robots, up from 500 at opening four years ago. Robot carts haul aluminum, hundreds of robotic arms weld car bodies in a lights-off dark factory, and cameras inspect finished cars. A forklift driver there says he worries about losing his job to a robot.
- **Why it matters:** It is the main example of the article's claim that automation is China's edge in the tariff war; most of the piece's detail comes from this plant.
