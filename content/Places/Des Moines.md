# Des Moines

Capital of Iowa, on the Des Moines and Raccoon rivers. It is Bill Bryson's home town in _The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid_, his memoir of a 1950s childhood South of Grand.

## The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid — Bill Bryson (2006)
Source: [[The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4620956W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5khtee1z9e9agj4wd85cngs)

> It was miles and miles of the American dream. This was my district. It was known as South of Grand.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bryson grows up in a white clapboard house on the west side, in the wooded district South of Grand Avenue that runs down to Waterworks Park and the Raccoon River. Grand Avenue leads him downtown, where his parents work at the Register and Tribune and where he spends Saturdays at Younkers, Bishop's cafeteria and the old movie palaces. The book ends by mourning how much of that downtown has since been torn down.
- **Why it matters:** The city is the memoir's whole world; its safety, dullness and comfort stand for 1950s America.
