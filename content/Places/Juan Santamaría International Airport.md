## Our 2 Week Costa Rica Vacation — retirebyforty (2015)

Source: [Original](https://retireby40.org/our-2-week-costa-rica-vacation/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gm1rfwr3yqp44kxg6h4mqa)

> The Juan Santamaria airport is pretty small and there were only a few United Airline flights scheduled.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Arriving at 6 pm for a 1 am flight, the family waits until 9 pm for the United counter to open, hunts for a pen to fill in the departure forms, and is pulled out of the security line to see immigration because they are travelling with a child.
- **Why it matters:** The comic coda of the trip report, which the author calls a bizarre travel day.
