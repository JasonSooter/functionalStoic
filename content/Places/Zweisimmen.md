# Zweisimmen

Village in the Bernese Oberland, Switzerland, at the end of the MOB line from Montreux (see [[Switzerland]]). It appears in Simply Railway's winter train video as the terminus of the ride.

## I Took a Train Through a Winter Wonderland — Simply Railway (2026)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IDcYsdqw3lQ) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5c3p4n3fkdxaf4pg4624vhr)

> We arrived on time in Zweisimmen — nothing to complain about on this ride.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the video:** Zweisimmen is the end of the Montreux–Zweisimmen line, which the narrator gives as 62.4 km long and the first fully electrified railway in Switzerland when it opened in 1905. She arrives on time at sunset.
- **Why it matters:** The terminus closes the journey that starts at [[Montreux railway station]]; the video spends little time in the village itself.
