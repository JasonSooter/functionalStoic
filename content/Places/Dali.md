# Dali

City in [[Yunnan]], China, where Dan Wang spent months fleeing Covid lockdowns in _Breakneck_ and where his parents were sent after graduating.

## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL42416011W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e30kdr3n19p0r4p1s8f322)

> Silvia and I spent several months in the city of Dali, which has a lake on one side and a mountain range on the other.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** They live among the Bai people with lakeside white-walled houses. Dali, nicknamed Dalifornia, draws organic farmers, parents seeking nature education, foreigners running bakeries and techno clubs, and a crypto community. Silvia does fieldwork while he works remotely.
- **Why it matters:** It is the author's refuge and the place where he sees the 'rùn' type of young people who later appear in Chiang Mai.

## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)

Source: [[Breakneck]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL42416011W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebt7t3ey84qvbcw77eh4jr)

> The state dispatched the two of them to the Yunnan city of Dali—which also happens to be where Silvia and I fled in 2022 to escape the Covid lockdowns.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After graduating, Wang's parents were sent to Dali as middle school teachers in a corps of three dozen youths, became a couple and married there, handing guests milk candy. Decades later Wang fled to the same city to escape Covid lockdowns.
- **Why it matters:** It links the parents' youth to the author's own flight, a quiet echo within the family story, though the text spends little time describing Dali itself.
