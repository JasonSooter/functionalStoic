# Eastport

Small island city on Passamaquoddy Bay in Down East Maine, the easternmost city in the United States. Its harbour, Estes Head and Deep Cove appear to be the model for the fictional town in Aoibheann Sweeney's _Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking_.
