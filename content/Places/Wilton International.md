# Wilton International

Chemical-industry complex on the outskirts of Redcar, near Middlesbrough, built by ICI. Will Butler-Adams ran the maintenance of a plastics plant here before he joined Brompton.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8c390etq19z6y55t0sc18ct)

> My experience at that time was based on having been thrown into running the maintenance of a massive chemical plant at ICI Wilton, on the outskirts of Redcar near Middlesbrough.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Before Brompton, Will was put in charge of maintenance at Melinar 2/4, a plant at ICI Wilton making PET plastic chips for Coca-Cola bottles, after two planned managers turned the job down. He calls it the "polar opposite" of the Brompton factory: on that scale mistakes can make the evening news, so the work is all control, procedure, teamwork and checking.
- **Why it matters:** It is the contrast the author uses to explain his management style at Brompton: he arrived from a world of procedures into one dominated by a single inventor, and had to learn to listen.
