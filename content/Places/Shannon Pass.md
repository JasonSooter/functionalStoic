# Shannon Pass

Pass on the Continental Divide in the northern Wind River Range, Wyoming. In The Ultimate Journey it is where Tim Ryback nearly breaks down.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs22bxnh4hk3wrmb173y3x)

> Shannon Pass, just beyond Royer Peak, was a mysterious silent world of desolate blue ice and black rock.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Tim has been fading since Pete's Lake, where he arrived in tears and could say only that the mountains were pretty. On the ridge above Elbow Lake, past the silent ice and black rock of Shannon Pass, Tim is close to collapse; Eric returns from scouting to find him talking to three small boulders, loses his temper, then apologises and talks him through what the mountains do to a person; Tim shakes his hand and goes on.
- **Why it matters:** The emotional crisis of the brothers' trek; the bleak pass mirrors Tim's state, and his choice to continue carries them through the Wind Rivers.
