## Hiking Tennessee’s Mount LeConte — Bob Doucette (2015)

Source: [Original](https://proactiveoutside.wordpress.com/2015/12/08/hiking-tennessees-mount-leconte/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1mwhbfpd6a4e898eeksb)

> And in front of us, a collection of rustic cabins, collectively known as the LeConte Lodge.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** Near the top the trails converge at the hike-in lodge cabins, closed for the season. After the summit they return to a picnic table here, eat, share water with a couple, and get a Butterfinger bar in return.
- **Why it matters:** Rest stop and social scene near the summit; a vivid but secondary place.
