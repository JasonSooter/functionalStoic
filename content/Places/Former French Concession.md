## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL42416011W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e307adr4pte00cxsry1qtt)

> My home in Shanghai was in the former French Concession, which is still full of plane trees and cafés. I loved this area.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The author lives here and walks twenty minutes in each direction to a French bakery, a Starbucks Roastery and the museum at the site of the Communist Party's first congress. He and Silvia cycle its cafes, and a friend there helps explain police tactics after neighbors topple a lockdown barricade.
- **Why it matters:** It is the lived-in home base for the whole Shanghai chapter and embodies the city's colonial-turned-consumer charm that the lockdown disrupts.
