## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx34zsbkmn0f918r1yj2xg)

> We would try to solve one of the enduring problems of British archaeology - the function and origin of Sil bury Hill, the immense cone-shaped mound standing alongside the A4 road in Wiltshire.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** BBC2 funds and broadcasts a multi-year excavation by Richard Atkinson, who tunnels to the centre. Outside Broadcast units sit by the A4. Atkinson finds a layered structure of coloured earths, dates it to 2500 BC, and finds no golden horseman.
- **Why it matters:** The subject of BBC2's pioneering televised dig; the hill's mystery drives the passage.
