## Deep Work vs. Messy: How to Balance Productivity and Creativity — Matthew Guay (2017)
Source: [Original](https://zapier.com/blog/deep-work-vs-messy/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5rccw7rgerftd6c5zcdg1)

> During a London Underground workers' strike, commuters were forced to find alternate routes to work.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** During a strike on the Tube, commuters had to find other routes. Transit-card data showed a substantial minority found a better journey than the one they had used for years. The book's author uses it to argue that disruption can improve habits.
- **Why it matters:** The Tube strike is the case study illustrating the benefits of mess.
