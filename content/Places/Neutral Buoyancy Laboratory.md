## Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir (2021)

Source: [[Project Hail Mary]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/611060/project-hail-mary-by-andy-weir/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jx9njsfsjrx9v776r0kkj1)

> NASA’s Neutral Buoyancy Lab at Johnson Space Center was a marvel of engineering in itself. The gigantic swimming pool was large enough to fit a full-size replica of the International Space Station inside.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Grace, in a Russian Orlan EVA suit, tests the IVME in vacuo microbiology kit at the bottom of the pool, dropping sample vials with the clamper tool while Forrester monitors. Divers stand by and he is hoisted out by crane.
- **Why it matters:** A vivid real-world training site where the Hail Mary's EVA science tools are prototyped; Grace enjoys walking through 'his country's space history'.
