# Haymarket Stores

A shop in the Haymarket, London, in the 1880s. In Three Men in a Boat its lobby is the scene of a dog riot started by a fox-terrier.

## Three Men in a Boat — Jerome K. Jerome (1889)

Source: [[Exploration of Three Men in a Boat]] · [[Three Men in a Boat]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1793164W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gkzfz6de3v2znzskh17a33)

> I remember being in the lobby of the Haymarket Stores one day, and all round about me were dogs, waiting for the return of their owners, who were shopping inside.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The narrator remembers waiting in the lobby of the Haymarket Stores among dogs left by shoppers; a fox-terrier bites a poodle, and within moments every dog is fighting, a crowd gathers in the Haymarket outside and men come with poles and ropes.
- **Why it matters:** A digression illustrating Montmorency's fox-terrier nature; the lobby is just a crowded room for the joke.
- **Real vs. fictional:** ⚠️ The shop itself could not be identified in a standard source; the pin is the Haymarket street.
