## What Changes When a School Embraces Mindfulness? — Katrina Schwartz (2016)
Source: [Original](https://ww2.kqed.org/mindshift/2016/03/30/what-changes-when-a-school-embraces-mindfulness/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjqnb6da8w1tavsztzvd4x)

> Teachers quickly herded their students out of the building to the sports field behind the school as the old colonial-style building burned.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** A fire broke out at lunch time at the K-8 Marysville School in Southeast Portland in 2009. Students and staff were herded out to the sports field and then displaced for three years while the building was rebuilt. After it reopened the school adopted the MindUP curriculum to address trauma from the fire and from poverty.
- **Why it matters:** The fire and displacement are the trauma that led the school to mindfulness, so the school is the case study the article is built on.
