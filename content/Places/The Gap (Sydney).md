# The Gap (Sydney)

Ocean cliff at Watsons Bay on Sydney's South Head. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne rides out to it through Rose Bay and Vaucluse.

## At a glance

| Source | Author | Role | In one line |

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76rj5k2d5bv2xhs46qt7vex)

> The next day I decide to bike to the Gap, one of the rocky points to the east of the town center that encloses Sydney harbor like a pair of sandstone pincers, one from the north side of the bay and one from the south.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Avoiding the big roads, Byrne rides along Rose Bay and up through Vaucluse, past modest houses that feel like an English town dropped into a semitropical landscape. At the point the Pacific cliffs give "a spectacular view—for the dead": a cemetery holds the most scenic spot.
- **Why it matters:** The ride shows Sydney as a transplanted England on a hostile coast, leading straight into his reflections on Australia's indifferent nature.
