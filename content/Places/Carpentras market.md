## Where randonneuring medals go — Mostly Bikes x Me (2025)

Source: [Original](https://funkadelic.net/pages/15252330354.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqwy6z1qf90vzp3bf3j2gt)

> Piled in the corner of a folding table at the Carpentras Sunday market: a lifetime of randonneuring accomplishments.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author finds a vendor's heap of a former cyclist's (M. Richard, 1965-1985) audax medals, flèche vélocio cards and Paris-Brest-Paris and BPF brevet cards.
- **Why it matters:** The market is where the cycling memorabilia surfaces; the post is a small elegy. Earlier progress notes judged Carpentras thin.
