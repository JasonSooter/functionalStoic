## The Steepest Streets in San Francisco — Priceonomics (2015)
Source: [Original](http://priceonomics.com/the-steepest-streets-in-/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gby490ysaz1zhy4nhh445e)

> It was long-claimed that Filbert Street (particularly the section between Leavenworth and Hyde, where Letterman let his watermelon loose) was the steepest in the city, at a 31% grade.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The block between Leavenworth and Hyde, where David Letterman let watermelons loose in 1996, was long claimed to be the city's steepest at a 31% grade; the survey puts it barely in the top ten.
- **Why it matters:** The myth the article sets out to test.
