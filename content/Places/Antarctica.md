> Five days later the ship—carrying crew, scientists, engineers, a few family members, and Philbert—set off. For the next 28 days, they navigated their way from base to base, picking up and dropping off researchers, delivering supplies and carrying out repairs.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Philbert's dream destination. After months of setbacks she is accepted aboard a Chilean Navy logistics ship and spends 28 days travelling between bases, seeing penguins, whales and icebergs.
- **Why it matters:** The goal of the second traveler's story; the setting where her dream is fulfilled.
