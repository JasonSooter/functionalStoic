# Cats'Heuvel

1920s luxury apartment building in The Hague's [[Statenkwartier]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is the author's grandmother Omi's home after the patriarch's death, and his own home as a teenager.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zmhqsd2dwt65tp3kkbg2v)

> After Grandpa died, she moved into the luxurious Cats’Heuvel apartment complex in the Statenkwartier. This unique building from the 1920s had an entrance hall entirely covered in mausoleum marble, and in the doorway a beautiful stained-glass window made of thousands of pieces of colored glass.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After the Old Boss dies in 1954, Omi moves into Cats'Heuvel, with its porter's lodge, guard and stately staircase. Her sons gather there for rare Sunday meals that turn on their failed attempts to recover the family fortune. When Frans and Mimousse leave Marlot for [[Netersel]], the author moves in with Omi, first in her alcove guest room and, in his last year of high school, in a guesthouse in the other wing of the building.
- **Why it matters:** The building is the faded court of the old family: the grandmother keeps its rituals going while the money and the sons drift away, and it gives the author the nearest thing to a stable home in his teens.
