# Fresnillo

Mining city in central Zacatecas. In The Underdogs Demetrio joins General Natera's advance on Zacatecas there.

## The Underdogs: A Novel of the Mexican Revolution — Mariano Azuela (1915)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1209983W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8z4y5mh2gyr946qr969kr)

> On the day General Natera began his advance against the town of Zacatecas, Demetrio with a hundred men went to meet him at Fresnillo.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Demetrio meets General Natera, is addressed as Colonel, and Luis Cervantes meets Solis, who voices his disillusion over drinks. The men carouse in saloons and brothels, and after the failed first attack on Zacatecas the rebels return in disorder, selling and smashing loot and camping in a corral. They hear tales of Villa in a saloon.
- **Why it matters:** Fresnillo is where Demetrio is absorbed into the larger revolutionary army and where the novel's critique of the Revolution is first stated.
