## Eating Our Way Across Mexico — JustinRoG (2015)
Source: [Original](http://rootofgood.com/amazing-food-mexico/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gap3j06gbctdyvpm19sd5k)

> When we plan on being away from home for many hours at a time (like the trip to the Monte Alban pyramids in Oaxaca), possibly in areas without a lot of food service options, we’ll often pack a picnic lunch.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** Much of the food in the post, from tlayudas and memelas to grasshoppers sold in the market, is eaten in Oaxaca, where the family stays; for the long day at the Monte Albán pyramids they pack a picnic.
- **Why it matters:** One of three cities on the trip; the food post leans on it.

## July 2015 Financial Update — JustinRoG (2015)

- **Why it matters:** A cost example in the monthly report.
