# Wimbledon

Suburb in south-west [[London]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where Frans's secret daughter Andrea settles, and where the money he promised her becomes the last quarrel between them.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zn67y2nryehvzxazj4em3)

> She did, in fact, move to Wimbledon, Richard’s hometown.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Andrea moves to Wimbledon with her partner Richard, and they buy a £38,000 flat on a loan, counting on money Frans had seemingly promised. Frans takes fifteen paintings to Sotheby's in London, stays in a hotel in Haslemere, and raises less than four thousand pounds. Andrea's letters invite him to Wimbledon with a two-week ultimatum; he refuses to go or send more money, and her last letter, after the flat sells for £82,000, ends contact.
- **Why it matters:** Wimbledon is where Frans's guardian-angel role runs out: the money that bound him to Andrea becomes the reason they part, and the letters from here are how his son learns the story.
