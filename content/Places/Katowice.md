# Katowice

Industrial city in Upper Silesia, southern Poland. Brompton's hinges have been cast at the Swan Shephard foundry here since the move away from hand-moulding in [[Walsall]].

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8c3961zb4r52gz8zpcnqk78)

> Visiting the Swan Shephard plant in Katowice was a revelation. I had thought that it might be cold, but I was ready for something like a British winter, rather than –20°C.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The author visited in a –20°C winter, ran out of petrol between the airport and the foundry, and borrowed a coat from the metallurgist Przemek. Everything was "built on the former communist scale": a furnace the size of a swimming pool fed whole cars by magnetic crane, and a DISAmatic machine that moulds 72 hinges per cycle. Working with the foundry, Brompton refined the hinge casting, and the author still visits Przemek.
- **Why it matters:** Katowice is the answer to Walsall's fragility: industrial scale and constant measurement made the hinge, the bike's most critical part, consistent.
