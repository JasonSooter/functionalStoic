## The Light That Never Goes Out — Helen Russell (2025)

Source: [Original](https://mshelenrussell.substack.com/p/the-light-that-never-goes-out-on) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5cre70axpr79rhzhmpzwwp8)

> A Polish journalist told me on my last visit to Warsaw: “We already know how to be sad.”

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** The author is travelling to Warsaw for the Polish edition of her book and a live TV appearance around All Saints' Day; she recalls a journalist's remark from her last visit and plans to light a candle in the city.
- **Why it matters:** The trip is the frame for the piece's argument that Poland knows how to live with sadness; no particular site in the city is described.
