# Columbus Avenue (Meriden)

Street in South Meriden, Connecticut, where Tomie dePaola's family rented an apartment before moving to [[26 Fairmount Avenue]]. In his memoir it is where the family rides out the Hurricane of 1938.

## 26 Fairmount Avenue — Tomie dePaola (1999)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/351865/26-fairmount-avenue-by-tomie-depaola/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8d25pxjrw4sf35nke36fs4e)

> We were living in an apartment on Columbus Avenue. We all lived on one floor. Another family lived upstairs, and we lived downstairs.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The opening chapters are set here. The family rides out the Hurricane of 1938 in the apartment with neighbours Mrs. Crane and the Morins, and holds its last Christmas and New Year's Eve party here before the move. Harbor Brook, a polluted stream that "wound all the way through Meriden", runs near the backyard, and Tomie is told not to go near it. At the end of the block is Hemlock Grove, "a small forest of tall hemlock trees". The hurricane flattens it, Tomie later uses it as a shortcut, and the scary woods in _Snow White_ remind the children of it.
- **Why it matters:** This is the "before" of the book: cramped, shared, and battered by the hurricane, in contrast to the new house the family is building.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Columbus Avenue and Harbor Brook are real; ⚠️ Hemlock Grove could not be found in any gazetteer and is folded in here.
