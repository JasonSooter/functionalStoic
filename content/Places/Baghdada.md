## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)
Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6mpwse30dh48yx5wnn7d0)

> This sounds as though I’ve somehow got to Iraq overnight and forgotten my spelling en route, but in fact it’s a delightful frontier village forty-five miles north of Peshawar where I’m staying with the chief in a vast but primitive house.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** She stays with a Pathan chief, Suinabhayat Khan, brother of Colonel Zeb, eating with her fingers off plates on the floor. During supper gunfire from the bazaar announces that a policeman has been shot in a tea-house, and she learns of Pathan codes of honour and inheritance.
- **Why it matters:** A vivid portrait of Pathan frontier life, violent and hospitable at once, and a link in the chain of introductions from Teheran.
