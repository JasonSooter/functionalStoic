## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7vnzg1qq5asgdk8gzzcww)

> Five hundred million birds arc the sky over the hills of Beit Jala every year.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Beit Jala is the hill town above the migratory flyway where Tarek finds the tangled ortolans, where Combatants for Peace first meets, and where Rami rides twice a week. Its terraces, olive groves and vineyard sit beside the Wall, settlements and new roads.
- **Why it matters:** The opening fragments tie Beit Jala to birds, stones and borders, and several strands of the book (Rami, Tarek, the first peace meetings) converge here.
