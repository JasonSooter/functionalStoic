## Introducing LifeEdited: Maui — David Friedlander (2016)

Source: [Original](https://lifeedited.com/introducing-lifeedited-maui/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7tyyf7q7ertkpj4gxprsj)

> A few years ago he bought a 2.2 acre piece of land in Maui with the intention of one day designing and building a home that incorporated many of the same ideas that informed LE1.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Graham Hill bought a 2.2 acre plot in Maui where LifeEdited is now building a mini compound. The build is capped at 1000 square feet, so the plan is a four bedroom, 2.5 bath house whose rooms transform by day, off-grid and net zero, with solar, water catchment and agriscaping.
- **Why it matters:** The whole piece introduces a low-impact home designed around this Maui land and its building limits.
