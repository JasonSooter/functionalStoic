# 8 West Thirteenth Street

Address in Greenwich Village, [[Manhattan]], near Fifth Avenue. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it is the two-room flat the Riveras move to after the Rockefeller Center firing, where their marriage frays.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)
Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m737hs5fy6ddfr8dfb3gjhqb)

> On June 3, one month after the beginning of the Rockefeller Center battle, Frida and Diego moved downtown to a two-room flat at 8 West Thirteenth Street so that Rivera could be nearer to his work.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In Chapter 11 the Riveras move here to be near Rivera's new panels at the [[New Workers' School (51 West Fourteenth Street)|New Workers' School]] and keep open house every evening, with neighbours Louise Nevelson and Marjorie Eaton. Diego begins spending time with Nevelson; Frida stays home with a numb foot, cooking puddings while he returns at dawn, and the two argue over going back to Mexico.
- **Why it matters:** It is where the strain in the marriage and Frida's homesickness come to a head before the return to Mexico.
