## The Race to Expand Monaco’s Borders — The B1M (2025)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IlnW8x4TJ2I) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqy7fggd5z6q0rgetbwzfm)

> Mareterra, or Portier Cove, is the latest addition to the country’s footprint.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** Monaco spent about \$2 billion reclaiming six hectares from the Mediterranean, enlarging the country by 3 percent. Eighteen 10,000-tonne caissons built in Marseille were towed in to form a belt filled with 1.5 million tonnes of rock and sand, after seagrass was transplanted. The finished district has a promenade, park, marina and homes that sold out before its December inauguration.
- **Why it matters:** It is the article's subject and, at 50 m depth, probably Monaco's last reclamation, which is the point of the closing question about what Monaco does next.
