# Brijuni

Island group off Istria in [[Croatia]], the real-world model for the unnamed Adriatic island in _Death in Venice_. It is Aschenbach's failed first stop, which sours him and sends him on to Venice.

## Death in Venice — Thomas Mann (1912)

Source: [[Death in Venice]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL16246083W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dr0sz844v3thp3wcvg68k0)

> What he sought was something exotic and distinctive yet of easy access, and so he stopped at an island in the Adriatic, not far from the coast of Istria

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After a night train to Trieste and a ship to Pola, Aschenbach settles on an island off Istria. Rain, a heavy atmosphere, closed Austrian hotel society and the lack of a sandy beach sour him. After a week and a half he leaves by motorboat for the naval base and boards a steamer for Venice.
- **Why it matters:** The failed first stop pushes him toward Venice, where the rest of the novella unfolds.
- **Real vs. fictional:** unnamed in text; real model
