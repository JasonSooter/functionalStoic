## The World-Wide Work. — ethanmarcotte.com (2019)

Source: [Original](https://ethanmarcotte.com/wrote/the-world-wide-work/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7chgas9kgq9kp1v2eqh49)

> Brenda commutes two hours by bus to her job, where she works for a company that produces “training data.”

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Brenda Monyangi, a single mother living in Kibera, the largest slum in Nairobi, commutes two hours by bus to a company that produces training data for machine learning. She labels images for roughly \$9 a day, work feeding the AI research of major tech firms. The author uses her story to illustrate exploitative, precarious labor behind automation.
- **Why it matters:** Her home community and the company's low-wage Nairobi-area location anchor the argument about exploited labor.
