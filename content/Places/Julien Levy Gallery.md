# Julien Levy Gallery

Surrealist-leaning art gallery at 15 East 57th Street, [[Manhattan]]. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it gives Frida Kahlo her first solo exhibition, in November 1938.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)

Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m73hndmyvkvdsts0ty942sfv)

> An exhibition of paintings by Frida Kahlo (FRIDA RIVERA) opens Tuesday, November 1st, at the JULIEN LEVY GALLERY, 15 East 57th St.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In Chapter 14 Levy writes unexpectedly asking for a show, and a doubtful Frida accepts; Breton writes the brochure essay. The exhibition opens on November 1, 1938, with Frida in Mexican costume at a crowded opening. The press is mostly delighted, about half the paintings sell, and Levy photographs her and introduces her to the Surrealist circle.
- **Why it matters:** It is her debut as a painter in her own right rather than Rivera's wife, and it launches the Surrealist label and the sales and commissions that follow.
