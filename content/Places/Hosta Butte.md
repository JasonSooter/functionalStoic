## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs2rqzsgbpmzerj4wmp84z)

> Toward the end of the fifth day, October 4, I was skirting the southern perimeter of Hosta Butte and was nearly around the crumbling monolith when dust trails approached me from the south.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Rounding the butte, Eric is stopped by three men in a lowered late-fifties car who ask him to ride with them off the reservation; he has become "the talk of the reservation". They drop him at its border with promises that the deeds of "Chief Crazyfoot" will be remembered.
- **Why it matters:** A tense, then comic, encounter that ends his walk through Indian country.
