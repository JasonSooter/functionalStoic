# Copacabana

Beach neighbourhood in the south zone of Rio de Janeiro. In _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_ Feynman lives there and marches down its streets with a samba school before Carnaval.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfqdvvnnegnncx97jw1s6k)

> We walked down the street, around the corners, and all over the damn Copacabana, at random!

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Feynman stays at the Miramar Hotel on Copacabana beach and joins a local samba school as a frigideira player. Before Carnaval the school parades unlicensed down Avenida Atlântica, with bystanders holding ropes and a policeman diverting traffic, and wanders "all over the damn Copacabana, at random". In the competition the beach schools march in costume down the same avenue.
- **Why it matters:** The neighbourhood is where Feynman is accepted as a musician rather than a famous scientist, one of the book's happiest threads.
