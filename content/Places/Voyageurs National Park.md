## 5 Family Friendly Activities at Voyageurs National Park — Jess (2015)

Source: [Original](https://currentlywandering.com/2015/08/08/5-interesting-fun-things-to-do-with-kids-at-voyageurs-national-park/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gap8fzp6jtrdvg0qwbyf07)

> This park is named after the French trappers who roamed the backwater lakes and rivers in large canoes transporting goods for various trading companies in the late 17th and early 18th centuries.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The family spends four days in the park: a costumed voyageur ranger program at the Kabetogama visitor centre, the Blind Ash Bay trail with berries, camping at Woodenfrog and paddling rented canoes out to the islands.
- **Why it matters:** The park is the whole subject of the post.
