## Barack Obama and Doris Kearns Goodwin: The Ultimate Exit Interview — Doris Kearns Goodwin (2016)
Source: [Original](https://www.vanityfair.com/news/2016/09/barack-obama-doris-kearns-goodwin-interview) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy8c1z9add7bz219wzgshs)

> And they had emptied the pyramids for us, and we could just wander around for a couple hours [at] the pyramids and the Sphinx.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Obama recalls being flown by helicopter to the pyramids after his Cairo speech, with the site emptied for him. Looking at the tombs and hieroglyphics he reflects that people then anguished over their standing just as people do now, yet only the pyramids remain.
- **Why it matters:** The place anchors his central idea of perspective: ask what you are building that lasts, not how you poll today.
