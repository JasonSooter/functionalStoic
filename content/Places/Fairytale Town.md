> With the whole day now to fill, Kenny and his mom spent the late morning at Fairytale Town, a place for kids run by the Parks Department.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** The two visit the storybook village and the nearby zoo, lingering at the Reptile House. Kenny has outgrown the sets. The family used to come here without his father.
- **Why it matters:** Shows how much has changed since the divorce.
