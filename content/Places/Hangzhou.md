# Hangzhou

Capital of Zhejiang province, [[China]], about two hours from Shanghai. In _The Brompton_ it is where a would-be e-motor supplier met Brompton in smart offices while its real factory sat beside a pig farm.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8cdhp56hz636jqw8sk6s6p4)

> They met me at offices in Hangzhou, about two hours’ drive from Shanghai.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Looking for an electric-bike motor, Will meets the Chinese maker Tongxin at its Hangzhou offices, where nothing is made, and rides with them in a park, but they won't show him the factory. Designer Will Carleysmith is sent back to insist, and is driven for several hours into the countryside to an industrial estate whose main industry is a large pig farm. There workers hand-wind coils, slop on glue and smoke clay pipes.
- **Why it matters:** The visit ends the Tongxin route to the electric Brompton and illustrates the book's rule of always seeing where your parts are made.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The factory's town isn't named; the pin is the Hangzhou office.
