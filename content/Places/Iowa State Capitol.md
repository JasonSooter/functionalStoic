# Iowa State Capitol

Seat of Iowa's government in Des Moines, with the state's Civil War monument on its grounds. In _The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid_ the boys cycle there to gaze at the statue called Iowa.

## The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid — Bill Bryson (2006)

Source: [[The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4620956W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5khtvzmbyxn91bk17x5npyy)

> It was part of the state’s large Civil War monument on the capitol grounds.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** In the chapter on sex, Bryson writes that Des Moines had "the most erotic statue in the nation", a seated woman called Iowa holding her bare breasts, on the Civil War monument in the capitol grounds. The boys cycle there on Saturdays to stare up at it and joke about the plaque that says "Erected in 1890".
- **Why it matters:** A comic local landmark; the statue matters more than the capitol itself.
