## Soy You Bought a Tofu Factory — Wailin Wong (2016)

Source: [Original](https://m.signalvnoise.com/soy-you-bought-a-tofu-factory-f4ef494b113b) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy7gj7kmfa6tp57ztwwsc0)

> Jenny had discovered a business called Phoenix Bean Tofu, which had been operating from the Edgewater neighborhood on Chicago’s north side since 1981.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Jenny Yang spots the storefront on the way home from a playground, buys tofu weekly, then buys the business and the building in 2007. The cramped thousand-square-foot kitchen with 17 employees is where tofu is made.
- **Why it matters:** The main setting of the story; the building holds the business and the real-estate bet.
