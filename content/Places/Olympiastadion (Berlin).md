# Olympiastadion (Berlin)

Olympic stadium on the western edge of [[Berlin]], by the Grunewald forest. In _The Boys in the Boat_ Hitler visits the old Deutsches Stadion on this site in 1933 and orders a vastly larger stadium for the 1936 Games.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m869xpa5rg2vg9qg6hbjqp4b)

> They had come to inspect the old Deutsches Stadion, built in 1916 for the ill-fated Olympic Games of that year.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In October 1933, the same week the Washington freshmen sign up for crew, Hitler steps out of his car on a rise west of Berlin, with the Grunewald forest on one side and the city's spires on the other, accompanied by Werner March, Theodor Lewald and Wilhelm Frick. March explains that the adjoining racetrack prevents a major expansion of the stadium his father built for the cancelled 1916 Games; Hitler declares that the racetrack must "disappear" and that a stadium for at least a hundred thousand people and a massive sports complex be built.
- **Why it matters:** The scene sets the stakes on the other side of the book: the 1936 Games are to be Nazi propaganda on a grand scale, the stage the Seattle boys will eventually row toward.
