## On Stones — Ellyn Gaydos (2024)

Source: [Original](https://harpers.org/archive/2024/08/on-stones-ellyn-gaydos-vermont-granite/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqv2ahnc7t0pjtv902j6r9)

> Heather took me in her red Jeep to Green Mount Cemetery in Montpelier.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Over a Veterans Day long weekend Heather takes the author to her friend Tiki's grave under a hydrangea. She shows the pink granite footstone with Tiki's name, a mushroom and a heart, which she carved with a portable Dremel saw shortly before Tiki died in August 2022.
- **Why it matters:** It is the emotional centre of the profile: a carver's personal act of memorial, outside Barre itself.
