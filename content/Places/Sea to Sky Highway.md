# Sea to Sky Highway

Coastal highway from [[Vancouver]] past Howe Sound and Squamish up to Whistler, [[British Columbia]], Canada. An OsmAnd blog post uses the RBC GranFondo Whistler ride along it to show how a season of GPS tracks can be organised.

## Organizing Your Tracks with Smart Folders — OsmAnd Blog (2026)
Source: [Original](https://osmand.net/blog/smart-folders) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7h2c5k3c7343nmd72ansvje)

> More than a thousand riders roll out of Stanley Park for the RBC GranFondo Whistler: 122 kilometres of the Sea to Sky Highway, closed to traffic, with Howe Sound on one side and the mountains on the other.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The post opens with the race: riders leave Stanley Park in Vancouver and ride the closed highway past Squamish, climbing to Whistler. The rider's season of training (seawall mornings, climbs up Cypress, rides to Squamish and back) is then sorted with Smart Folders, which group tracks by Vancouver, West Vancouver and Whistler, the August course recon and the race itself.
- **Why it matters:** The ride is the story the feature demo is built on, though the post's real subject is the software.
