# Spacetown (The Caves of Steel)

The Spacers' open-air enclave of domes outside the enclosed City in Isaac Asimov's The Caves of Steel, where Dr. Sarton is murdered. Pinned at a best guess in [[New Jersey]].

## The Caves of Steel — Isaac Asimov (1954)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL46401W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hj3fmtvsdzkzdhx00tv4mw)

> It can just be made out in the gap between the two Brunswick Sectors. Low domes spread out. It’s the difference between us and the Spacers.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Spacetown is the walled-off settlement of Spacers, low domes with land between them, reached through a screening entrance where City visitors are showered and tested. Dr. Sarton is killed in his dome there; Baley's first accusation against R. Daneel fails there, and the final deduction turns on Sarton's dome.
- **Why it matters:** It is the other pole of the novel: open space against the crowded City, and the source of the conflict between Earthmen and Spacers.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Fictional enclave outside a future New York.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Clues: seen from City Hall by looking 'westward', 'in the gap between the two Brunswick Sectors'; Sarton's dome lies outside the City across open country and truck farms. The Brunswicks are New Jersey place names (New Brunswick and its neighbours); no reference work found places Spacetown. Best guess: New Brunswick, New Jersey. Alternatives: elsewhere in central New Jersey west of Manhattan.
