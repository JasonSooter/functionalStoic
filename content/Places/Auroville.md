## This experimental utopian "city of the future" in rural India may hold a dark secret. — Atlas Obscura (2016)
Source: [Original](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/auroville) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy8ejmf712b11xhyhbb6wr)

> Welcome to Auroville, the self-proclaimed “city of the future” and the “city of dawn.” Despite being in a rural region of India, this experimental community was started by a Westerner.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article describes the community founded in 1968 by Mirra Alfassa as a universal township with communal property and no politics or religion. It has about 2,000 residents against a planned 50,000, mixes UNESCO-backed respectability with new-age oddities, and faces allegations of abuse of local children.
- **Why it matters:** The whole piece is about this place and the gap between its utopian aims and reality.
