## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)

Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edhtes0z722f8hpyapb6np)

> Just a few hours south of here on the Carmel River, the concrete San Clemente Dam had been built in 1921 by a real estate company in the Monterey Peninsula in order to provide water to a growing number of Monterey residents.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Odell recounts the 2015 removal of the dam, the largest in California history: the river was rerouted, the dam picked apart by excavators and hammers, and steelhead trout habitat restored. She notes the strange forward-and-backward feeling of a time-lapse of construction played as destruction.
- **Why it matters:** It is her main worked example of 'manifest dismantling', progress as removal and remediation. It is a specific, real site whose story carries the argument.
