## When Kitty Litter Caused a Nuclear Catastrophe — Wesley Crump (2025)
Source: [Original](https://practical.engineering/blog/2025/4/15/when-kitty-litter-caused-a-nuclear-catastrophe) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2y38d9qkkn7mqhcw1my0p)

> The Waste Isolation Pilot Plant, or WIPP, was built in the 1980s in the desert outside Carlsbad, New Mexico, a site selected for a very specific reason: salt.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** WIPP is an underground nuclear waste repository about 2000 feet down in a salt formation. A drum burst there in 2014, the facility shut for nearly three years and cleanup cost about half a billion dollars.
- **Why it matters:** The piece is about what happens in this place; the salt geology explains both its choice and its shielding.
