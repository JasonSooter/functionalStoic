# Museum of World Cultures (Riyadh)

Museum tower under construction inside [[King Salman Park]] in Riyadh. A B1M video describes it as the park's tallest building and its cultural landmark.

## This Will Be The World’s Biggest Park — The B1M (2026)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VpuWi9a3MrE) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m73hnvyk571hz8x60r276dvv)

> the Museum of World Cultures, which

- **Role:** setting
- **In the video:** At 110 m the museum towers over everything else in the park. Its planned height grew from 60 m to 80, 100 and finally 110 m, with Bofill recalculating the geometry to keep its golden-ratio proportions; the video calls the building itself "an artifact". It sits in a cultural hub with a national theatre, libraries and exhibition halls.
- **Why it matters:** It backs the video's point that the park is more than green space: it is also meant to be a cultural centre and a wayfinding landmark for Riyadh.
