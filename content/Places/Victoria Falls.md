# Victoria Falls

Great waterfall of the Zambezi on the Zambia–Zimbabwe border; in _Life on Air_ Attenborough sits on Livingstone's island at its edge.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2wwzqaekra4y6110jq66)

> We sat with our feet dangling over the rocky edge of the fall, just as Livingstone had recorded doing in his j ournal, looking over the eastern cataract with its permanent rainbow in the spray, and peering down to the brown water boiling among the boulders three hundred and fifty feet below.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Attenborough seeks a fresh vantage point and lands on the small island in the middle of the Falls where Livingstone had landed, which is tricky because the river is still high. He sits at the edge as Livingstone did.
- **Why it matters:** A moment of direct physical connection with Livingstone's discovery; a high point of the Zambezi journey.
