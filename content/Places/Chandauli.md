## The inside story of Facebook’s biggest setback — Rahul Bhatia (2016)
Source: [Original](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2016/may/12/facebook-free-basics-india-zuckerberg) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvmka8xrg3aw2w328z1y5m)

> He saw wheat fields and power lines, and a classroom with children sitting on a dirt floor. The heat warped the horizon.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Zuckerberg lands by helicopter in October 2014 to visit a digital-literacy community centre set up by the Digital Empowerment Foundation. The visit is staged, the power cuts out, and a boy's Facebook page loads slowly on 2G. Zuckerberg promises the connectivity will be fixed.
- **Why it matters:** Opens the article as a parable of Facebook's top-down plan for India.
- **Real vs. fictional:** ⚠️ The article places Chandauli in Rajasthan; no standard gazetteer entry for the village was found, so it has no pin yet.
