# Capitol Theatre (Meriden)

Cinema in Meriden, Connecticut. In Tomie dePaola's childhood memoir it is where young Tomie sees Disney's _Snow White_ in 1938 and objects loudly to the changes from the "true story".

## 26 Fairmount Avenue — Tomie dePaola (1999)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/351865/26-fairmount-avenue-by-tomie-depaola/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8d25r985fksvhrf7ems28kq)

> We got in line early at the Capitol Theatre so that we could get good seats.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In spring 1938 Tomie and his family queue early for _Snow White_. His brother Buddy panics at the witch and is taken out. Tomie, who knows the fairy tale, shouts at the screen that the film has the "true story" wrong and is taken out by his mother.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's best-known set piece and shows the young author's fierce sense of how stories should be told. Any cinema would do for the plot, but the scene is tied to this town's theatre.
