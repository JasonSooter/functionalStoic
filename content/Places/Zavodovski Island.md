# Zavodovski Island

Volcanic island in the South Sandwich Islands; in _Life on Air_ helicopters land Attenborough for an hour among millions of chinstrap penguins.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy7vqjq4f7fk6hvqpq45qe)

> One of them, Zavodovsky, was blanketed by chinstrap penguins - fourteen million of them, according to one estimate.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Helicopters from Endurance land the crew for about an hour on this wave-battered island with no beach, to film the vast chinstrap penguin colony, then whisk them off before a gale can maroon them.
- **Why it matters:** A dramatic filming location for the polar programme, but described briefly.
