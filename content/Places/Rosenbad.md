## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling (2019)

Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20831115W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebtgwvhqt91gj7atvabvv7)

> It was October 10. We were in the government building in Stockholm.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After a meeting on Sweden's ebola contribution, MSF's Eugene Bushayija tells Rosling that nobody knows what is happening in Monrovia. Rosling decides he must go.
- **Why it matters:** The room where his decision to go to Liberia crystallizes; brief.
