# Carmel, Indiana

Suburban city north of Indianapolis in [[Indiana]], known for having more roundabouts than any other U.S. city. Priceonomics uses it as its main case study for roundabout safety.

## The Case for More Traffic Roundabouts — Priceonomics (2015)

Source: [Original](http://priceonomics.com/the-case-for-more-traffic-roundabouts/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gm0dzyszzfxcv1ec11d0f0)

> With 60 roundabouts, it has more than any other city in the United States — and, as a result, has seen an 80% reduction in injuries, and a 40% reduction in overall crashes.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article presents Carmel as a case study: with 60 roundabouts it has more than any other city in the country, and it reports an 80% reduction in injuries and a 40% reduction in overall crashes.
- **Why it matters:** Concrete evidence for the article's central argument that roundabouts are safer than signals.
