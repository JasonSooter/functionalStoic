# Waco, Texas

City on the Brazos River in central Texas. In _How to Know a Person_, David Brooks goes there to find the community "Weavers" and has the breakfast with LaRue Dorsey that opens his chapter on illumination.

## How to Know a Person — David Brooks (2023)
Source: [[How to Know a Person]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/652822/how-to-know-a-person-by-david-brooks/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8g94yz147peb5qtv2egmagz)

> In Waco, a number of people told me about a ninety-three-year-old Black woman named LaRue Dorsey. I reached out, and we arranged to get together over breakfast at a diner.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 3 opens with Brooks in Waco, asking residents who holds the place together. Over breakfast at an unnamed diner, LaRue Dorsey, a former teacher, comes across as a stern disciplinarian until Jimmy Dorrell, a local pastor to the homeless, walks in, grabs her shoulders and shouts his love. Her face becomes that of a "joyous, delighted nine-year-old girl".
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's demonstration that the attention we give calls forth a different version of a person. The scene depends on Waco's tight community of "Weavers"; the specific town matters less than its web of people, which Brooks went there to find.
