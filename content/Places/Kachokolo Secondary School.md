# Kachokolo Secondary School

Village secondary school in Kasungu District, central Malawi, about five kilometres from [[Wimbe]], with a tobacco estate's scrapyard across the road. In _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ it is the school Kamkwamba is forced to leave, and the scrapyard opposite supplies the parts for his windmill.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8e117jqp6cat3zz6gqvwnnr)

> As I mentioned, the scrapyard sat directly across from Kachokolo Secondary School, where I’d left a bit of my heart those many weeks ago.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** His exam results send William to Kachokolo, "possibly the worst one in the district", a forty-minute walk over hills and past the _dambo_. The school sits in a valley among tobacco estates. He starts in January 2002, then drops out when his father can't pay the fees. Next term he sneaks back for a while, hiding in the latrines during fee checks, until the headmaster bars him. Later he spends weeks in the abandoned garage at Estate 24 across the road, "littered with machine parts and stripped bodies of cars and tractors". There he finds the tractor fan, shock absorber and bearings for the windmill while students jeer at the "madman". In the afterword, "instead of holding a grudge", he returns to solar-power the whole compound and install eGranary computers, an "Internet in a box" open to anyone in range "whether they’ve paid their school fees or not."
- **Why it matters:** Losing school here is what sends William to the library and the scrapyard. The windmill is built from what this place threw away, and he comes back to give it what it lacked.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Not pinned. The text gives only "about five kilometers" from home, a valley among tobacco estates and a nearby dam. GeoNames' only Kachokolo is a hill about 14 km east of Wimbe (id 929461), which doesn't fit, and Nominatim has no record.
