## How Costco Hacked the American Shopping Psyche - The New York Times — Ben Ryder Howe (2024)
Source: [Original](https://www.nytimes.com/2024/08/20/dining/costco.html/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f1w8qqwh0049agambt4zqv)

> When it opened in 1984, the Costco on West Dimond Boulevard in Anchorage did not seem like the future of food.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The article opens at the shed-like 1984 warehouse, its parking lot full of 4x4s and mobile homes, with shoppers buying whole pigs. It presents the store as an early outpost of the stockpiling trend.
- **Why it matters:** It is the article's opening scene and argument.
