# Westminster Abbey

Gothic abbey church in Westminster, London, where English monarchs are crowned. In _Brave New World_ it has become the Westminster Abbey Cabaret.

## Brave New World — Aldous Huxley (1932)

Source: [[Brave New World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL64365W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvag1tm0f7kqw5q2bj9zak)

> At twenty past nine they walked across the street to the newly opened Westminster Abbey Cabaret.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After dinner at Henry's forty-storey apartment house in Westminster, Lenina and Henry take soma and walk across the street to the newly opened Westminster Abbey Cabaret. Calvin Stopes and his Sixteen Sexophonists play for four hundred couples under a scent and colour organ, and the pair return to Henry's room afterwards.
- **Why it matters:** A national church turned into a dance hall shows how the old culture has been converted into entertainment, the soma-holiday theme in one image.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The abbey is real; its cabaret is Huxley's invention.
