# Orlando

## ★ The Crazy Side of Paying Off \$55,000 of Debt in 14 Months — Andrew (The Bean Counter) (2015)

Source: [Original](http://rockstarfinance.com/crazy-side-debt-repayment/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hj1kw62qc6ndbc94nyqh8n)

> During our move from Miami to Orlando, we set up a separate budget of what we needed to purchase for our new place and what we were willing to sacrifice on.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** The couple move from Miami to Orlando to be near the author's business partner, set a separate budget for the new apartment and go without a couch. The Orlando lease later ties them down during the Miami contract.
- **Why it matters:** One of the sacrifices the article catalogues; the place matters less than the budgeting decisions made there.

## My Net Worth is… — Rob Greenfield (2020)

Source: [Original](http://robgreenfield.tv/mynetworth/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjrv9hxnp7d8zfb6bvf09q)

> I built a tiny house in Orlando, Florida where I based myself for nearly 2 years. The tiny house was built for under \$1,500 using secondhand materials.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Greenfield built a tiny house in Orlando and lived there for nearly two years, doing a year-long project of growing and foraging all his food. He left Florida in November 2019 and gave away nearly everything.
- **Why it matters:** His base during the peak-possessions period before he gave things away; the place matters mainly as a stage in the net worth timeline.
