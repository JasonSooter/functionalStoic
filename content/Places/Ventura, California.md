## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)
Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbkw7893a763g2bx87hwcp)

> In Ventura, Shoupista Mayor Bill Fulton introduced on-street parking rates of one dollar per hour, aiming toward 85 percent occupancy.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Mayor Bill Fulton introduced $1 per hour on-street rates in September 2010 and blogged that it worked within thirty minutes, with employee vehicles moving to nearby lots.
- **Why it matters:** A recent example of Shoup's 85 percent occupancy target, minor but illustrative of simply raising meter rates from zero.

## Uncommon Type — Tom Hanks (2017)
Source: [[Uncommon Type]] · [Original](https://penguin.co.uk/books/1114786/uncommon-type/9781786091338.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5w8n6qtx5sbm51e99s718)

> When we got to the Ventura Bowling Complex, he put the ball in a machine that turned out to be a bowling ball polisher.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The friends bowl here weekly with Steve Wong, who rolls a first 300 with his custom yellow-and-brown ball Chinese Lightning, then repeats it days later. A crowd of 140 gathers, with pizza delivered, phones recording and a manager ringing a ship's bell, and Steve goes viral.
- **Why it matters:** This is Steve's home alley, where the streak happens and the legend starts. Later the ESPN host calls it the Ventura Party, Billiards, and Bowling Complex.
