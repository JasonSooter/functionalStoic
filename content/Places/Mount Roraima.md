## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx29z30sgw6062brvx7cjc)

> We were within fifty miles of one of the most romantic mountains in the world - Roraima.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** With a spare week, Attenborough and Charles Lagus canoe up the Kako hoping to see the tepui that inspired The Lost World. They glimpse its rectangular outline across a savannah, twenty miles off, before the river turns north and they must turn back to catch the float-plane.
- **Why it matters:** An unreached goal: the mountain is seen only from afar, but the detour is a memorable episode.
