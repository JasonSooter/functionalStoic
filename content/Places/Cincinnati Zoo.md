# Cincinnati Zoo

Zoo and botanical garden in [[Cincinnati]], Ohio. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ the last Carolina parakeet died there in 1918, four years after the last passenger pigeon.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m83dbnnn8ymnh8c8r43p9t14)

> The last one, named Inca, died in the Cincinnati Zoo in 1918

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Hunted as pests, Carolina parakeets dwindled until only a few remained in captivity. The last, named Inca, died at the Cincinnati Zoo in 1918, not quite four years after the last passenger pigeon died in the same zoo. Its body was stuffed, and the zoo then lost it.
- **Why it matters:** The zoo is where two American species ended within a few years, and a sharp example of the carelessness Bryson finds even toward extinct species.
