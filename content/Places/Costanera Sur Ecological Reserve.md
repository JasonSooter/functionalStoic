# Costanera Sur Ecological Reserve

Wetland nature reserve on the River Plate waterfront beside Puerto Madero, [[Buenos Aires]]. David Byrne, who calls it the Parque Ecológico, rides its marsh paths in _Bicycle Diaries_.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76e99dw25k16kyec5qakt9f)

> I bike through the Parque Ecológico, a park that has paths through the wetlands that border one whole side of the city.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Byrne bikes the paths through the reeds and marshes, comparing it to the New Jersey Meadowlands attached to Manhattan. Signs warn that it is not a place for _encuentros_ (sexual liaisons), the reeds hide the city right next door, and you cannot leave the paths without wading into the wetland.
- **Why it matters:** A rare stretch of wild ground inside the city, it shows Byrne's bike tours taking in the edges as well as the monuments.
- **Real vs. fictional:** ⚠️ Byrne calls it the "Parque Ecológico" and gives no other name; a wetland park along one whole side of the city fits the Costanera Sur reserve, the city's waterfront wetland park.
