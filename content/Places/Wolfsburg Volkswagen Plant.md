# Wolfsburg Volkswagen Plant

Volkswagen's main car factory on the Mittelland Canal in Wolfsburg, Lower Saxony, founded in 1938. In _Germany: Memories of a Nation_ it stands for the Beetle and the post-war German engineering story.

## Germany: Memories of a Nation — Neil MacGregor (2014)

Source: [[Germany - Memories of a Nation]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20201162W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m76968ayxrka0mq6xp956d)

> The Nazis put together a vast factory in Wolfsburg, but production never happened because the outbreak of the war prevented it.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 19, 'Masters of metal', hears that the Nazis built a vast factory at Wolfsburg for the Volkswagen, but war stopped production, and Hitler's promised price under 1,000 Reichsmarks would have been ruinous. After the war the British first produced Beetles there for their occupation forces; British carmakers turned it down, and once the factory was handed back to the Germans it became a great export success.
- **Why it matters:** It carries the Beetle's journey from Nazi propaganda project to the face of post-war German engineering.
