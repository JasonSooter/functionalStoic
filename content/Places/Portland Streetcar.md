## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)

Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbm44a6k97vgdb1tacqyk7)

> Since then, over \$3.5 billion of new investment has sprung up around the trolley line—an astounding sixty-four times the initial investment.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Speck uses the Portland trolley as the successful model: it brought billions in new investment, rising property values and a jump in pedestrian counts outside Powell's Books. He argues it worked because it was first a tool for neighborhood development.
- **Why it matters:** The anchor case for his claim that streetcars create new urban districts rather than simply enliven downtown; without it his Seattle and Tampa comparisons lose their benchmark.
