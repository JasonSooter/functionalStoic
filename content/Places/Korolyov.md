# Korolyov

Former Soviet "science city" on the outskirts of Moscow, once the centre of the Russian space programme. The titanium firm Rapid made the frames for Brompton's first Superlight here.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8c397v0k35rhnphcqv6t73v)

> Rapid was based in Korolev, a former Soviet ‘science city’ on the outskirts of Moscow which used to be the centre of the Russian space programme and before that a major centre for missile and tank production.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Around 2001 the adventurer Rupert Wilbraham took a Brompton rear frame to Rapid, a firm of former aerospace engineers. Their copy, made without jigs, was rejected by Andrew. After finding the prototype in the [[Brentford]] stores, the author went to Korolev himself, sat through heavy vodka-and-caviar hospitality, and carried jigs out in suitcases. In 2005 he and Andrew wired money to Rapid to buy titanium, "the biggest and craziest risk of the lot", which led to the Superlight in 2006.
- **Why it matters:** The Superlight depended on a supplier the company could not control, a recurring theme. Delays from Korolev later pushed Brompton toward other titanium sources and, eventually, [[Sheffield]].
