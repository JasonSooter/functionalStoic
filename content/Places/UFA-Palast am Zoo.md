# UFA-Palast am Zoo

Grand cinema by Berlin's Zoologischer Garten in Charlottenburg, [[Berlin]]. In _The Boys in the Boat_ Leni Riefenstahl's _Olympia_ premieres there in April 1938 before Hitler and the Nazi elite.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m87k3q3g2a9gj2mb4ewf40af)

> Hitler and the entire Nazi elite were there, along with ambassadors and envoys from more than forty nations, including the United States and Great Britain.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In the epilogue, _Olympia_ premieres at the UFA-Palast am Zoo on April 20, 1938, with military leaders, film stars, Max Schmeling and the Berlin Philharmonic in attendance. Riefenstahl enters to wild applause, and the film launches her on a European and American tour. The day after, Goebbels gives her a hundred-thousand-reichsmark bonus, and Hitler meets General Keitel to plan the seizure of the Sudetenland.
- **Why it matters:** The premiere is the final payoff of the Olympic illusion, and the book pairs it directly with the turn toward war.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The 1938 cinema was destroyed in the war; the pin marks its site, where the Zoo Palast stands today.
