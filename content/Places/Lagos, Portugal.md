## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmssw5p9cszaykm5xn8084)

> One of Zurara’s stories chronicled Prince Henry’s first major slave auction in Lagos, Portugal, in 1444.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Zurara described the 1444 auction in Lagos where captives of differing complexions were blended into a single group worthy of enslavement. This is presented as the origin of race making.
- **Why it matters:** The auction is the concrete moment where racial categories are invented to serve slave trading profit.
