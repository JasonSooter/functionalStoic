# Alcedo Volcano

Shield volcano on Isabela Island in the Galápagos; in _Life on Air_ the crew descend into its crater to film giant tortoises.

## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy7fbbm1vfwm1f92eb305d)

> The biggest population lives in the huge crater of an extinct volcano called Alcedo.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The crew carry water over black lava fields to the 3700-foot rim and descend into the crater to film about two hundred breeding tortoises. A rainy night follows in leaking tents, with two flattened by amorous males.
- **Why it matters:** The opening location of the Life on Earth shoot, tied to Darwin and the tortoises.
