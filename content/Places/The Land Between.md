# The Land Between

The Land Between is a region of the Canadian Shield in central Ontario. It turns up in Four Thousand Weeks (Oliver Burkeman): The farm is the destination of the one worked example of a deliberate retreat from convenience culture; the place's remoteness is the point.

## Four Thousand Weeks — Oliver Burkeman (2021)
Source: [[Four Thousand Weeks]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL22355777W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fcq0840xvzh7yfpvzg4mbw)

> She moved with her husband and children to a farm in the vast swath of the Canadian interior known as the Land Between

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Sylvia Keesmaat leaves a full-time university post in Toronto and moves her family to a farm in the Land Between. Her quoted account describes lighting the woodstove fire, tending cows, chickens, horses and a garden, so that a hot cup of tea takes an hour. Burkeman uses her as an example of choosing inconvenience to build a more meaningful life.
- **Why it matters:** The farm is the destination of the one worked example of a deliberate retreat from convenience culture; the place's remoteness is the point. No exact farm location is given.
