# Lower Rock Island Lake

Alpine lake below Homer Youngs Peak in the Beaverhead Mountains, Montana, near the Idaho border. The Ryback brothers camp in what Eric calls an "enchanted paradise" before the climb that injures Tim.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bes3bh78nf68bhxn0pd589)

> The larger of the two, Lower Rock Island Lake, was blessed by such beauty and tranquility that we made camp there, although the afternoon was still young.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After a string of bare alpine lakes (Slag-a-melt, Lena, Little Lake), the brothers drop into a wooded valley with two larger lakes and camp early at the larger, Lower Rock Island Lake, which Eric describes as an enchanted paradise where "dreams could come true". Above it looms Homer Young's Peak, which they climb next. After Tim's fall they come back to the lake and head south along Trail 54 toward Selway Mountain.
- **Why it matters:** The calm before the book's worst accident, set deliberately against the "bleak eminence" of the peak above it.
