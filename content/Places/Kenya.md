## Four Thousand Weeks — Oliver Burkeman (2021)

Source: [[Four Thousand Weeks]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL22355777W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fcq233k9pkdwqbmnpaeqkb)

> The Swiss psychologist Carl Jung, visiting Kenya in 1925, was setting out on a hike in the first glow of dawn when he, too, was suddenly plunged into timelessness:

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Carl Jung, on a hike at dawn in 1925, looks from a low hill over a broad savanna with herds of gazelle, antelope, gnu and zebra and feels plunged into timelessness. Burkeman cites it as an example of 'deep time'.
- **Why it matters:** The savanna landscape supplies the vivid example of timeless experience; the text gives only Kenya, not a specific site, so the pin is the country.
