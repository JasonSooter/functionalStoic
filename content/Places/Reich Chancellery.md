# Reich Chancellery

The German chancellor's seat on Wilhelmstraße in [[Berlin]], Hitler's headquarters from 1933 and demolished after the war. In _The Son and Heir_ the sight of its guards wins Frans Münninghoff over to Hitler.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85b0ct8zgmvrdkpc374fa9d)

> He was enchanted by the dynamic city, and when they went to see Hitler’s guards at the Reich Chancellery palace on Wilhelmstrasse, he was sold.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On his first trip home from Huize Katwijk, Frans has a few hours in Berlin between trains; the Old Boss's business partner takes him to breakfast at Café Kranzler and then to see Hitler's guards at the Chancellery. The confidence and power of the helmeted soldiers ignite a fascination that, the author writes, bordered on obsession.
- **Why it matters:** A morning's sightseeing turns a homesick schoolboy toward the regime he will later serve in SS uniform.
