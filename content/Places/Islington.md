# Islington

District of north London. In _The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy_ an Islington party is where Arthur first met Trillian and lost her to Zaphod.

## The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy — Douglas Adams (1979)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL2163649W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne2caw6xrwwp5g5rfjgvj1)

> telephone number of an Islington ﬂat where Arthur once went to a very good party and met a very nice girl whom he totally failed to get off with – she went off with a gatecrasher.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In chapter 13 aboard the Heart of Gold bridge, Arthur recognises Zaphod as 'Phil', who gatecrashed a fancy-dress party in Islington six months earlier on Earth and left with the girl Arthur had been chatting up. The girl turns out to be Tricia McMillan, now Trillian. The party also ties back to the ch. 8 note that the improbability figure is the phone number of an Islington flat where Arthur went to a good party.
- **Why it matters:** Islington anchors the coincidence that explains the Improbability Drive rescue and links Arthur, Zaphod and Trillian to Earth.
