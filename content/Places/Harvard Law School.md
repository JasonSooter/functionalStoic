## Quiet — Susan Cain (2012)
Source: [[Quiet]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/55983/quiet-by-cain-susan/9780141970172) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g8wysedsdxsht1kepdwbg6)

> Recently I spoke on a panel at Harvard Law School.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Cain speaks on a panel marking fifty-five years of women at the school and afterwards meets Alison and Jillian, whose stories she uses to contrast healthy self-monitoring with self-negation.
- **Why it matters:** A brief setting for the chapter's lesson on acting out of character without losing yourself.
