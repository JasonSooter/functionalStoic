# Brompton Junction Kobe

Brompton's first concept store, opened in 2011 in Kobe, [[Japan]], with its distributor Loro Cycles. In _The Brompton_ it opened weeks after the Fukushima disaster and started the company's flagship-store strategy.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8cdhmrac0s62mt751ck6vkg)

> Three weeks before the scheduled opening of Brompton Junction in Kobe, the Fukushima nuclear power station was struck by the 2011 earthquake and tsunami.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Hiroshi Oeshi of Loro Cycles proposes a concept shop in Kobe; Asia manager Quinton Pullinger names it "Junction" after Clapham Junction. When the earthquake and tsunami hit, expats urge Will to cancel. He checks a map (Fukushima is about 500 km north, the winds blowing away from Kobe), tells Oeshi he is still coming, and wins standing with Japanese customers. The store succeeds.
- **Why it matters:** Kobe is the template for the Junction stores that follow in London and elsewhere.
