# GORUCK headquarters

Headquarters of the rucking company GORUCK in Atlantic Beach, Florida. In The Comfort Crisis (Michael Easter) he rucks there with founder Jason McCarthy.

## The Comfort Crisis — Michael Easter (2021)
Source: [[The Comfort Crisis]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/634446/the-comfort-crisis-by-michael-easter/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g16xr8w6ah80k1n117e1gy)

> The company resides in The George R. Lucier Jr. Building, a 1968 brick structure that sits two blocks from the ocean.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Easter and former Green Beret Jason McCarthy ruck five miles from McCarthy's house to the company's building near the ocean, where they talk about rucking and its weekly group walks.
- **Why it matters:** The visit grounds the book's argument for carrying weight in everyday life.
