High pass and old railway grade over the Continental Divide west of Boulder, Colorado. Eric wakes there buried in snow.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs2e9t3pspyw6d1ndkpfnv)

> Towering grey clouds and a cold wind were pushing over the mountains as I reached Rollins Pass.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After a 28-mile day Eric camps under his tarp on the bare ridge and wakes under deep snow; he digs out "like a shiny-backed tropical tortoise" and thaws his stove with burning fuel.
- **Why it matters:** An early taste of the autumn weather that dogs the solo Colorado section.
