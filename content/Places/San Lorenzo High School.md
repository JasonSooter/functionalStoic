## We Need to Talk About Kids and Smartphones — Markham Heid (2017)

Source: [Original](https://time.com/4974863/kids-smartphones-depression/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7f61q8rh26vq56hr62rkv)

> Gina Spiers, an English teacher at San Lorenzo High School near Oakland, Calif., says she used to confiscate phones, but students would panic and cause a disruption in class.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The school joined a program with Yondr, using lockable pouches to keep phones closed during school hours. The principal reports kids more focused, less anxious, and fewer fights. The article treats it as informal evidence for restricting phones.
- **Why it matters:** The school is the article's concrete test case for limiting smartphones.
