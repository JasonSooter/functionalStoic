# Bladel

Small town in the Kempen of North Brabant, in the south of the [[Netherlands]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where Mimousse spends her last year and where Frans Münninghoff dies alone in 1990.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zn4se71g4btzxyj83n2ys)

> When my father finally died, on the morning of January 9, 1990, at home in Bladel, hardly anyone cared. He didn’t want anyone to care.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After the farm in [[Netersel]], Mimousse is nursed through her final illness in Bladel by her sister Jacquot, who hides from her the letters arriving from a woman. Frans stays on there, running gas stations and drinking. He dies in his recliner, "surrounded by twenty empty liquor bottles, the TV on the German channel", the last person to see him a takeaway delivery man; sorting the house, the author finds Andrea's letters.
- **Why it matters:** Bladel is the end of the line for the heir of a Baltic fortune: a man who chose his German past over his family dies isolated in a Brabant village, and the letters there let his son piece the story together.
