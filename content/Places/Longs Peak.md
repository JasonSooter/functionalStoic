## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs2bd0psdpkzrajg3e4b3m)

> A brisk wind greeted me as I reached the summit of the mountain and stood panting and exhilarated, looking out over the domain of Longs Peak — Storm Peak, Pagoda Mountain, Mount Lady Washington, all of the slopes I knew so well from my weekend hikes during the previous school year in Denver.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** A ranger warns him of the daily afternoon lightning storms. Eric climbs early, finds the trail easy, and stands on the windy summit over peaks he knew from weekend hikes while at school in Denver; at treeline he lunches on chili and lectures a chipmunk.
- **Why it matters:** A buoyant start to the solo half of the trek, on home ground.
