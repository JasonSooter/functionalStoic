# Sheffield

Steel city in South Yorkshire. Brompton's titanium frames are now made here, by a joint venture with the nuclear and aerospace engineers CW Fletcher.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8c39b4v08dzrxsg1v1d88nr)

> When I asked Steve if he could help us to find a way to make titanium bicycle frames in Sheffield, he looked at me as if I had gone mad.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In 2015 the author went to the Sheffield University innovation hub to learn about metal injection moulding and met Steve Kirk, managing director of CW Fletcher, which makes huge assemblies for aero engines. He visited the factory and asked Steve to help make titanium frames in Sheffield. Conventional wisdom said British titanium bikes were uneconomic, but the two concluded that much of the welding cost came from how the metal was normally used, which led to the Brompton Fletcher joint venture.
- **Why it matters:** It ends the long reliance on distant titanium suppliers ([[Korolyov]], Taiwan, [[Baoji]]) and fits the book's case for bringing manufacturing back to Britain.
