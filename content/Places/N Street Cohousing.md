## What’s Right With This Picture? — David Friedlander (2015)

Source: [Original](http://lifeedited.com/whats-right-with-this-picture/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gbgm3a7qgtbx8pab7ck94k)

> But if you look closely, right between N St and Lessley Pl, there’s a group of homes with irregular yards.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The post looks at an aerial view of a suburban block where the backyards of adjoining houses have been merged. The community began in 1986 when Kevin Wolf and Linda Cloud joined two houses, and more neighbours have joined since.
- **Why it matters:** The place is the article's example of turning existing suburbs into shared, smaller-footprint living.
