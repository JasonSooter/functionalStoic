# Lefortovo Prison

KGB prison in [[Moscow]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is where Gordievsky's wife Leila is questioned again and again after his escape.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7xy5s1ex0r6g6ajve3pckb0)

> Almost every day she was hauled into Lefortovo Prison for more questions.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After Gordievsky's escape, Leila is brought back from her holiday on the Caspian and taken to Lefortovo; from then on she is "hauled into Lefortovo Prison for more questions" almost every day. She turns on her interrogators: "You didn't do your job. It wasn't my job, it was yours. You ruined my life." Over time she gets to know them.
- **Why it matters:** It is where the cost of the escape falls on the family he left behind, and the book's main view of the aftermath in Moscow.
