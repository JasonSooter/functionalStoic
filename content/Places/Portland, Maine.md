## Uncovering Mattress Manufacturing — Mansi Kothari (2015)

Source: [Original](http://makersrow.com/blog/2015/11/uncovering-mattress-manufacturing/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hk5g1r06jrg3v04zg4mynf)

> Portland Mattress Makers are a local mattress retailer in the hipster town of Portland, Maine, whose niche is that they manufacture everything they sell.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article profiles Portland Mattress Makers, which makes everything it sells. The owner left banking to buy a small, low-tech Maine company; when two key employees of the previous owners died their knowledge was lost, so he now cross-trains his ten staff. The firm also runs shops and a boat-mattress line.
- **Why it matters:** The factory is the article's subject; Portland is its home rather than a theme.
