# Leutzsch

Western outer district of [[Leipzig]], an independent village until 1922. The BBSR's 15-minute-city report uses it as the outer-edge counterpart to the Waldstraßenviertel.

## Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde — Björn Schwarze et al. (BBSR) (2025)
Source: [[Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde]] · [Original](https://www.bbsr.bund.de/BBSR/DE/veroeffentlichungen/bbsr-online/2025/bbsr-online-027-2025-dl.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7kmhzzxfmk65jynnyzaasj3)

> Der Stadtteil Leutzsch war ein bis 1922 selbständiger Ort im westlichen Außenbereich der Stadt Leipzig.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** A case-study quarter on the city edge: a Gründerzeit core around the old village centre, then 1920s and later extensions, plus detached-house and villa areas. The model rates it a 5.4-minute city with 98.6 % fulfilment and an index of 88.8, slightly below the Waldstraßenviertel because there is less choice among options.
- **Why it matters:** It shows that an outer district can still meet the 15-minute standard, and isolates choice rather than distance as the gap to the inner city.
