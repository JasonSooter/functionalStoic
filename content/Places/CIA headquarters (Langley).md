# CIA headquarters (Langley)

Headquarters of the CIA in Langley, [[Virginia]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is where Gordievsky briefs American officers after his escape.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7xy5y1kenzr96ydbv40bf6q)

> At CIA headquarters in Langley, Gordievsky was wheeled out to give a series of briefings to senior officers.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On his visit to the United States, Gordievsky is "wheeled out to give a series of briefings to senior officers" at Langley. At one of them he is introduced to "a tall, bespectacled man with a thin mustache" who listens to his every word with particular interest: Aldrich Ames, the CIA officer who betrayed him to the KGB.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's sharpest irony: the defector meets, without knowing it, the man who exposed him, inside the agency meant to protect him.
