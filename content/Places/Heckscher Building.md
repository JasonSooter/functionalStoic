# Heckscher Building

Office tower at Fifth Avenue and 57th Street in [[Manhattan]], today the Crown Building, which housed the young Museum of Modern Art. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it is where Diego Rivera's 1931 retrospective opens, with Frida in Tehuana dress at his side.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)

Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m737hawm80pj25z2rk3w7anq)

> After installing their belongings in an apartment at the Hotel Barbizon-Plaza, on Sixth Avenue and Central Park South, Frida and Diego went at once to the Heckscher Building, on Fifty-seventh Street and Fifth Avenue, which at that time housed the Museum of Modern Art.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In Chapter 9, on arriving in New York in November 1931, the couple inspect the galleries and the high-floor studio fitted out for Rivera, who has little more than a month to prepare. The December 22 opening draws the Rockefellers and New York society, with Frida in Tehuana dress beside her husband, and the show sets an attendance record.
- **Why it matters:** The MoMA retrospective is the reason for the first New York stay, and its opening is Frida's first appearance as a conspicuous Tehuana figure in American society.
