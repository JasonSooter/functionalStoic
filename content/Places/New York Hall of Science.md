> This weekend I drove down to NY from Boston for the World Maker Faire mainly just to see the Glowforge in action.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author drives from Boston to Queens for World Maker Faire mainly to see the Glowforge, spends from 11:30 am to about 6 pm around its tent and returns at 4 pm to watch the machines run; his review rests on what he saw there: no etching, the software kept out of sight and an empty air-filter casing.
- **Why it matters:** The Faire is the only setting of the review.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The text names World Maker Faire and Queens; the Faire was held at the New York Hall of Science.
