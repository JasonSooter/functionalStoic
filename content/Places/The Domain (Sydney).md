# The Domain (Sydney)

Large park on the eastern edge of central [[Sydney]], once the governor's grounds. In _Bicycle Diaries_ it opens David Byrne's Sydney chapter with its colony of bats.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76rj43yyn49pvtatqqr58e5)

> I bike through the downtown park—the Domain—so-called because in the late 1700s it was the private grounds of the governor.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Byrne bikes through the Domain, named for the governor's private grounds of the late 1700s, and sees hundreds of large bats clinging to the trees. He remembers an outdoor opera recital there when they swarmed overhead at sunset during _La Traviata_.
- **Why it matters:** The pairing of an imperial name with ominous native creatures sets up the chapter's theme of colonists and an indifferent nature.
