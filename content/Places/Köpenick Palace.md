# Köpenick Palace

Baroque palace on an island at the southern end of old [[Köpenick]], in south-east Berlin. In _The Boys in the Boat_ Joe Rantz sits in its gardens on his first evening in Germany, looking down the Langer See toward the Olympic course.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m87k3egc1kc389whnfwttnd9)

> At the southern end of town, he crossed a bridge over a moat and came to a castle built by a Prussian prince in 1690. Behind the castle he found formal gardens.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On the evening of July 24, 1936, Joe walks alone through Köpenick, crosses the moat to the castle and sits on a bench in its formal gardens, looking down the Langer See toward Grünau, where his Olympic hopes will be decided. The lake glows "like a polished stone" in the last light, one of the most peaceful things he has ever seen. In later evenings the boys row rented boats around the castle among the swans.
- **Why it matters:** The scene is the calm before the race and the setup for the narrator's next line: Joe has no way of knowing the "bloody secret" that Köpenick and its placid waters conceal.
