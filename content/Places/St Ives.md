# St Ives

Seaside town on the north coast of Cornwall, England, known for its harbour and art galleries. It appears in the reading as the holiday setting of one of Dave Walker's Diagram Club newsletters.

> We were fortunate to be in St Ives, a favourite place, last week. We stay in accommodation that overlooks the harbour, and it is glorious.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Walker and his family stay in accommodation overlooking the harbour, with boats, food, galleries and an art workshop. He walks the coast path, finds a pub and writes in his notebook.
- **Why it matters:** It sets the issue's relaxed tone; the town's harbour and art scene give the newsletter its mood. Set elsewhere, the issue would lose that holiday colour.
