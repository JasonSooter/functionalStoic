# Hagerstown, Maryland

City in western Maryland. It turns up in Stranger in a Strange Land (Robert A. Heinlein): out-of-town restaurant where Ben and Jill dine safely and see the fake Man from Mars broadcast.

## Stranger in a Strange Land — Robert A. Heinlein (1961)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL59688W)

> THE RESTAURANT IN HAGERSTOWN had "atmosphere" as well as good food,

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Ben Caxton flies Jill by random taxi to Hagerstown, Maryland, to a restaurant with lawn tables by a little lake and tables in old trees under a force-field roof. He picks an unbugged table by the water so they can talk safely, and they watch the Secretary General's broadcast with the stand-in 'Man from Mars' there, where Jill insists it is not the patient.
- **Why it matters:** A deliberate out-of-town refuge from surveillance and the scene of the false-Smith revelation; any other far-from-Washington town would serve, so the real town is a modest anchor.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The restaurant is unnamed and invented; Hagerstown is real. No highlight: Reader could not anchor one in this book.
