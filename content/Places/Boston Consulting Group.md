# Boston Consulting Group

Boston Consulting Group is a global management consulting firm headquartered in Boston's Seaport district. In _Co-Intelligence_ its consultants are the subjects of the experiment that maps AI's "Jagged Frontier".

## Co-Intelligence — Ethan Mollick (2024)
Source: [[Co-Intelligence]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL37565105W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m76yj1jfqvf6bmkxqg7j8d)

> In our study of Boston Consulting Group, where previously the gap between the average performances of top and bottom performers was 22 percent, the gap shrank to a mere 4 percent once the consultants used GPT-4.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Mollick and Harvard, Warwick and MIT colleagues run a study with BCG in which nearly eight hundred consultants do eighteen realistic tasks, half of them with GPT-4. The AI group is faster and its work rated more creative and better written, but on one task built to lie outside the AI's abilities they get the answer right only 60 to 70 percent of the time, against 84 percent without AI. The gap between top and bottom performers shrinks from 22 to 4 percent.
- **Why it matters:** The study is the book's central evidence for the Jagged Frontier, for Centaur and Cyborg ways of working, and for AI as a leveller of skill.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The study was run by the firm and its consultants, not at one site; the pin marks BCG's Boston headquarters.
