> Another facility that's worthy of mention is this one, the Zhangzhou Nuclear Power Plant.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** Called the largest site with only Hualong One reactors: two operating, two under construction and two planned, with work begun in 2019. Completed, it could meet 75% of the energy needs of Zhangzhou and neighbouring Xiamen.
- **Why it matters:** The second example of scale in the video's argument, after the [[Fuqing Nuclear Power Plant]].
