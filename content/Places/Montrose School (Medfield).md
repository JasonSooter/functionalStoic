## How Teens Benefit From Reading About the Struggles of Scientists — Deborah Farmer Kris (2016)

> Montrose began a schoolwide emphasis on “growth mindset” three years ago, and Blabac says that the science classroom is fertile ground for teaching teens about the power of embracing setbacks.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Teacher Kristen Blabac describes how the school teaches growth mindset, hosting local scientists to judge the sixth-grade science fair, including a failed sugar-crystal experiment students want to redo.
- **Why it matters:** The classroom evidence for the study; the place is a modest backdrop.
