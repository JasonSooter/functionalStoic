# Louisiana Museum of Modern Art

Art museum on the Øresund coast at Humlebæk, north of Copenhagen. In The Year of Living Danishly, Russell spends part of an anniversary weekend there.

## The Year of Living Danishly — Helen Russell (2015)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20000553W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f1vcwb16txa4kyaf603c59)

> Now, we’re kicking back at the Louisiana Museum of Modern Art, just north of Copenhagen.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In the September chapter, on an anniversary weekend, Russell and Lego Man visit the Louisiana. They look at Picassos, Giacometti and Warhol, sit in the gardens by a Henry Moore sculpture looking across the sea to Sweden, and add wishes to a wish tree.
- **Why it matters:** It is the chapter's emblem of Danish culture and its link to the landscape; Russell reflects on great art with a sea view on the doorstep.
