# The Serpentine

Recreational lake in Hyde Park, London. In _The Country Girls Trilogy_ it is where Baba's drummer lover invites her to swim, and where she later feeds the ducks his smoked salmon.

## The Country Girls Trilogy — Edna O'Brien (1986)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL69252W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m76ejde23rqb4248x6n5jb)

> “Would you like to come down to the Serpentine and have a swim?” he said.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** During Frank's dinner party the drummer phones and asks Baba to 'come down to the Serpentine and have a swim' while the guests listen. She does not go; when she finds he has skipped his flat, she goes to the Serpentine to look for him. 'The ducks got the smoked salmon, bag and all.'
- **Why it matters:** A small comic place that marks the end of Baba's affair, which leads to her pregnancy. Mostly a symbol of the freedom she does not take.
