# Ein Gedi water park

Abandoned water park at Ein Gedi on the shore of the Dead Sea. A setting in Colum McCann's _Apeirogon_: Abandoned water park by the Dead Sea where Elik camps after his military service.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjq4t1t97hav1f33jfaqy1)

> After his service, Elik threw away his beret and drove on the main road from Jerusalem to the Dead Sea where he camped overnight in an abandoned water park near Ein Gedi.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Elik drinks vodka, wanders among lifeguard chairs, walks the dry waterslides and wakes on the floor of an empty pool.
- **Why it matters:** The drained park is an image of aftermath that also echoes the shrinking Dead Sea.
