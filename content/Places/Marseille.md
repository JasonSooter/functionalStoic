# Marseille

Port city on France's Mediterranean coast. In _The Wind in the Willows_ it is the Sea Rat's next port, the harbour he tempts Rat to sail for.

## At a glance

| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Wind in the Willows | Kenneth Grahame | journey waypoint | The Sea Rat's next port, with old shipmates and the shellfish he dreams of. |

## The Wind in the Willows — Kenneth Grahame (1908)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL28570037W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne2nc76sb1xt66bcx3an7d)

> And so at length, by easy stages, and partly on foot, partly by sea, to Marseilles, and the meeting of old shipmates, and the visiting of great ocean-bound vessels, and feasting once more. Talk of shell-fish! Why, sometimes I dream of the shell-fish of Marseilles, and wake up crying!

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** The Sea Rat travels from the Riviera to Marseilles, meeting old shipmates and eating shellfish (ch. IX), part of the voyage that enchants Rat.
- **Why it matters:** Named end-point of the Mediterranean leg, with Alassio, Palermo and Sardinia/Corsica in the same itinerary.
