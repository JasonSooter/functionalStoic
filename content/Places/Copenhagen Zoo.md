## The Year of Living Danishly — Helen Russell (2015)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20000553W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f0r53jmkawqh42yahss9em)

> Marius was an eighteen-month-old giraffe living in Copenhagen Zoo.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Russell recounts how Marius, an eighteen-month-old giraffe, was killed at Copenhagen Zoo in 2014, publicly dissected and fed to the lions. The case anchors her April conversation with Peter Sandøe about Danes' unsentimental view of animals.
- **Why it matters:** The zoo's decision is the hook for the chapter's argument.
