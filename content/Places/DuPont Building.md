# DuPont Building

DuPont's former headquarters building in Wilmington, [[Delaware]]. In The Intercept's C8 investigation it is where managers decided in 1984 to keep using the chemical.

## DuPont and the Chemistry of Deception — The Intercept (2015)

Source: [Original](https://theintercept.com/2015/08/11/dupont-chemistry-deception/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ga17fydvmxd6wqjjag38bp)

> In May 1984, DuPont convened a meeting of 10 of its corporate business managers at the company’s headquarters in Wilmington, Delaware, to tackle some of these questions.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** In May 1984 DuPont gathered ten corporate business managers at its Wilmington headquarters to weigh how to cut C8 emissions; they reviewed the options and decided to keep using the chemical.
- **Why it matters:** The meeting is the article's pivotal decision. The text names the company's headquarters in Wilmington; the pin is the DuPont Building, its headquarters at the time.
