# The lake north of Dallas (The Subtle Art)

## The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F\*ck — Mark Manson (2016)

Source: [[The Subtle Art of Not Giving a Fck]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17590212W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvb77d1t5zs9qkzrs6f4nx)

> My friend Josh had taken me to a party on a lake just north of Dallas, Texas. There were condos on a hill and below the hill was a pool, and below the pool was a cliff overlooking the lake.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** At nineteen Manson goes with his friend Josh to a party on a lake just north of Dallas, with condos on a hill, a pool below and a cliff over the water. Josh jumps the roughly thirty feet into the dark lake and drowns; divers take three hours to find him, and Manson drives home to Austin the next morning and breaks down on the phone to his father.
- **Why it matters:** Josh's death is the root of the chapter's argument about confronting mortality, and the event Manson says changed his life.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The lake is real but unnamed.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The text says only "a lake just north of Dallas", with condos on a hill and a cliff about thirty feet above the water. No reference work names it. Pinned at Lewisville Lake, the large reservoir directly north of Dallas. Alternatives: Grapevine Lake, Lake Ray Roberts.
