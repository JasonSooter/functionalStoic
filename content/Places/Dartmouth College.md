# Dartmouth College

Ivy League college in Hanover, New Hampshire. In the afterword of _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ it is where William Kamkwamba goes to study engineering.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8ebdhkpb5ktwkp63ej2bsz1)

> I also loved Dartmouth because the school of engineering was part of a larger liberal arts college, which would allow me to explore other areas of interest.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On a campus visit William is shown the engineering school's "tool library": "Imagine, checking out power tools like books!" Kenyan students tell him, "Come to Dartmouth, brother. Here you have a family," and the college president, Jim Yong Kim, says "the reason I came to Dartmouth was to recruit people like you." He is accepted and finds his first year very hard. He meets his fiancée, Olivia Scott, there.
- **Why it matters:** It closes the arc that began with a borrowed library book in [[Wimbe]]: the boy who dropped out for lack of fees ends up training as an engineer. This material is from the later-edition afterword, not the 2009 narrative.
