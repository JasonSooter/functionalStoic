# Old Windsor

Village on the Thames below Windsor in [[Berkshire]], site of Edward the Confessor's palace. Three Men in a Boat rows past it and recalls its history.

## Three Men in a Boat — Jerome K. Jerome (1889)
Source: [[Exploration of Three Men in a Boat]] · [[Three Men in a Boat]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1793164W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gky4wpdkg3vda02afyfjjn)

> Old Windsor is a famous spot in its way.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** On the way up from Picnic Point the narrator praises the reach to Old Windsor lock and the Bells of Ouseley inn, then recalls that Edward the Confessor had a palace here and tells the story of Earl Godwin choking to death on a piece of bread.
- **Why it matters:** A short historical aside on the journey; the place colours the passage but nothing happens here.
