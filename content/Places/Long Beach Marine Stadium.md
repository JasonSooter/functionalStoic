# Long Beach Marine Stadium

Straight saltwater rowing course in Long Beach, [[California]], built for the 1932 Olympics. In _The Boys in the Boat_ Washington loses a 2,000-meter race there to California by half a second in 1935.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m86yet5j7fyzbx4pgey0qr4c)

> Somewhat more than six thousand fans packed into the Long Beach Marine Stadium on the day of the race, sitting in bleachers or standing on the sand along both sides of the arrow-straight saltwater course, a forest of oil derricks rising behind them.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After the third place at Poughkeepsie in 1935, Ulbrickson takes his varsity south for a 2,000-meter race, hoping to redeem the season. With a smell of petroleum in the air, Washington and California race locked together; Cal edges ahead, Bobby Moch drives his crew back in the last fifty meters, but Ebright's Bears win in 6:15.6, half a second ahead. Ulbrickson heads home with another defeat, "Quite possibly his last."
- **Why it matters:** The narrow loss over the Olympic distance deepens the pressure on Ulbrickson and makes the next season, the Olympic one, a do-or-die year.
