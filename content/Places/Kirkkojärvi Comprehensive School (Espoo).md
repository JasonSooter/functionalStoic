## Why Are Finland’s Schools Successful? — LynNell Hancock (2011)

Source: [Original](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/why-are-finlands-schools-successful-49859555/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8y8hk5bp050jcaqmchbbc)

> It was the end of term at Kirkkojarvi Comprehensive School in Espoo, a sprawling suburb west of Helsinki, when Kari Louhivuori, a veteran teacher and the school’s principal, decided to try something extreme—by Finnish standards.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The reporter visits this immigrant-heavy school where principal Kari Louhivuori kept a struggling Kosovo-Albanian boy, Besart Kabashi, back a year and tutored him personally, and later sits in Maija Rintola's classroom. The school shows Finland's 'whatever it takes' approach, with special educators and extra city 'positive discrimination' funds.
- **Why it matters:** The school is the article's main window into how Finnish teachers work with struggling pupils.
