## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7wjc31sqmvgx2nvcpnfza)

> he had always wanted Abir to see the sea, it was the thing he had promised her for so many years, his pledge to his daughter, that he would bring her the short drive to the coast at Akka

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Bassam's unfulfilled promise to take Abir to the sea at Akka explains his refusal of organ donation. In school Abir drew the Mediterranean from rooftops.
- **Why it matters:** It represents the freedom Abir was denied.
