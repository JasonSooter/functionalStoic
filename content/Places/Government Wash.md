> My initial thought after seeing Government Wash, our boondock site at Lake Mead, was that it looked like the place had blown up.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The writers' first boondocking site near Las Vegas, a rocky wash they first thought looked blown up. It had a lake view, quail, and a nightly coyote pack that howled to another across the lake. They stayed 15 days, the limit.
- **Why it matters:** Home base for the first half of a month-long stay, and the place whose odd character they came to enjoy.
