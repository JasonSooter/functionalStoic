# Snape Maltings

Concert hall in converted maltings near Aldeburgh, Suffolk; in _Life on Air_ BBC2 records Britten's Owen Wingrave there.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx33k4v3vzkdynad12pd9h)

> Eventually we agreed that we would build a huge temporary studio using Outside Broadcast camera units in and around the Concert Hall at the Snape Maltings near Aldeburgh.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** BBC2 builds a temporary TV studio at the Maltings so Britten can conduct Owen Wingrave with his singers in view. Attenborough visits during recording, finds the tension intense and is relieved to leave.
- **Why it matters:** Location of the production of a new work; moderately significant.
