> This was an interesting read about a Toronto suburb that has found success growing its bus ridership namely by running frequent buses.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The newsletter quotes Bloomberg that Brampton, population about 700,000, has 226,500 bus riders on an average weekday and 288 percent ridership growth from 2004 to 2018. It is presented as a sprawling suburb that succeeded without high density.
- **Why it matters:** It is the case study the item is about, though only a short summary is given; the point is that the suburban structure is typical of North America.
