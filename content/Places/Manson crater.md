# Manson crater

A buried impact crater under the town of Manson in north-west [[Iowa]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ it is briefly the most geologically exciting place on Earth.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m80ty8t7xg3ejmdzd3vc8gec)

> PEOPLE KNEW FOR a long time that there was something odd about the earth beneath Manson, Iowa.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 1912 a well driller in Manson brings up deformed rock and unusually soft water; University of Iowa geologists later blame volcanic action. The real cause is an impact whose crater now lies buried under glacial till, so the town's librarian tells visitors there is nothing to see. For a brief period in the 1980s, Manson is "the most geologically exciting place on Earth".
- **Why it matters:** It opens the chapter on impacts and stands in as the dinosaur-killer candidate until Chicxulub.
