# Lake Fryken

Chain of long lakes (Fryken) in Värmland, western Sweden. It turns up in Nordic Tales (Chronicle Books): Long Värmland lake whose two ends (Frykstad, Fryksend) frame the twin-sister race against the Devil.

## Nordic Tales — Chronicle Books (2019)
Source: [Original](https://chroniclebooks.com/products/nordic-tales) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmt19syzffm5cksv7wa5bq)

> So they agreed to race to the end of Lake Fryken and back, one on each side of the lake, which, as you know, is a very long one indeed.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Old Nick and the dancing girl agree to race to the end of Lake Fryken and back, each on one side. The girl's twin waits at Frykstad at the south end while she stays at Fryksend in the north, and Old Nick runs through Sanne and Emtervik parishes until his shoes are shredded. (Chunk ends mid-story.)
- **Why it matters:** The lake's great length is what makes the trick of twins at opposite ends work; a short lake would break the joke. The story is anchored by named ends and parishes.
