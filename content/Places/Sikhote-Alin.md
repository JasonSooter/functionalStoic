# Sikhote-Alin

Mountain range along Russia's Sea of Japan coast, in Primorsky and Khabarovsk Krai. In _Last of the Breed_ Natalya and her father cross it on foot from [[Plastun Bay]] towards the Chinese border, and it is where Stephan Baronas dies.

## Last of the Breed — Louis L'Amour (1986)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL51048W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6y2df9sxkjy3rfj0wfn883d)

> The Sikhote Alin Mountains were at no place in the southern part of the range higher than five thousand feet, but on the ridges in the middle of winter the cold could be intense.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Planning their flight, Baronas sees that escape means "crossing the Sikhote Alin Mountains, low but formidable," with dense forest, man-eating tigers and brigands. Warned of arrest, they leave the cabin above Plastun Bay by night with Yakov, who leads them up a trail with the Iodzihe River to their right, shows them on his map how to cross the divides and follow the Vagou River "to near Iman, on the border", and then turns back. On the third day Stephan asks to stop early; next morning Natalya finds him dead: "Her father was dead, and she was alone."
- **Why it matters:** The crossing turns Natalya from a dependent exile into a fugitive in her own right. The range is the only land route to the border once the radar-watched coast is closed, so her story now runs parallel to Joe Mack's.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The range is real; the Vagou and Iodzihe rivers are named in the text but not located here. ⚠️
