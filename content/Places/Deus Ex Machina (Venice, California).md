## What are people working on in coffee shops? — Tristan de Montebello (2016)
Source: [Original](https://medium.com/@montebello/what-are-people-working-on-in-coffee-shops-cdf351e28b6) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvmez25xw2jebngtkbrs9f)

> Almost every day I sit down in a coffee shop in Venice, California, to work on

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author and a friend walk around the coffee shop at lunchtime interviewing every working customer about what they are doing. Answers come from film, music, advertising, writing and tech people, and one owns a building in Venice. Afterwards both feel elated at connecting with strangers.
- **Why it matters:** The whole experiment is staged in this one cafe; the Venice creative-class crowd shapes the answers.
