> The compound is called the Kashgar Professional Skills Education and Training Center, according to a sign posted outside its gates.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** A walled, barbed-wire compound on a tree-lined street in northern Kashgar, formerly a school, now a political education center where Uighurs are held. The reporter is stopped from photographing its sign, and people with friends and relatives inside say detainees disappear there.
- **Why it matters:** It is the concrete, physical emblem of the article's argument about detention and surveillance in Xinjiang.
