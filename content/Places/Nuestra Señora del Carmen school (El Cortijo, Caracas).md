## Venezuela Is Falling Apart — Moisés Naím, Francisco Toro (2016)

Source: [Original](http://www.theatlantic.com/international/archive/2016/05/venezuela-is-falling-apart/481755/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvmqr8a1b8dwsr440wf552)

> At Nuestra Señora del Carmen school in El Cortijo, a struggling neighborhood of Caracas, supplies for the school-lunch program have been stolen twice this year already: Thugs have broken into the school’s pantry late at night after fresh food is delivered.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Thieves break into the school pantry twice, once leaving the children unfed for at least a week, illustrating the breakdown of law and order and hunger keeping kids from school.
- **Why it matters:** One of the article's concrete vignettes of state collapse at street level.
