## Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World — David Epstein (2019)

Source: [[Range]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550188/range-by-david-epstein/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjskyvkd67g1dx69yw9xz9)

> Django grew up outside Paris in an area called la Zone, where the city’s cesspool cleaners unloaded waste each night.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Django Reinhardt, born in a Romani caravan in Belgium, grew up in la Zone outside Paris. There he skipped school, crashed movie theaters, shot billiards, and was surrounded by Romani music that he learned by imitation.
- **Why it matters:** Its informal, unschooled musical surroundings are used to illustrate improvisers learning by osmosis rather than formal instruction.
