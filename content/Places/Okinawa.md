## What Do the Healthiest People in the World Eat? — Janice Stanger (2015)
Source: [Original](https://www.forksoverknives.com/longevity-diet/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx228d94wytx7x756f1edz)

> A look at the Okinawan islands of Japan, one of the Blue Zones, offers some important insight. Careful research has validated the birthdates on record for Okinawan centenarians.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Okinawa is the article's central case study of a Blue Zone with validated centenarians. Its traditional 1949 diet (69% sweet potatoes) is tabulated, and older Okinawans are said to have the longest survival in Japan, with almost two-thirds still independent at 97. The younger generation's shift to animal foods, white rice and oils is said to have eroded the advantage.
- **Why it matters:** The whole argument for a plant-based diet rests on Okinawa as the evidence; it is the one place the article dwells on.
