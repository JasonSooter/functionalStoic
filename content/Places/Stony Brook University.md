## For Every Winner a Loser — John Lanchester (2024)
Source: [[For Every Winner a Loser]] · [Original](https://www.lrb.co.uk/the-paper/v46/n17/john-lanchester/for-every-winner-a-loser/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f1vn54xqpth7rj8yx3hvaf)

> Simons stepped aside from running Renaissance in 2010 to concentrate on philanthropy. He gave between $4 billion and $6 billion to causes focusing on science and maths, and also made the largest ever single unrestricted donation to a university: $500 million to Stony Brook.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Lanchester recounts Jim Simons becoming head of the maths department at SUNY Stony Brook in 1968, making it one of the leading US departments within ten years, and later giving it $500 million.
- **Why it matters:** It is the academic base of the Simons story in the essay.
