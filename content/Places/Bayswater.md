# Bayswater

District of west London north of Kensington Gardens. It is Kate and Baba's first London address in _The Country Girls Trilogy_, and the site of MI6's safe flat for Gordievsky in _The Spy and the Traitor_.

## The Country Girls Trilogy — Edna O'Brien (1986)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL69252W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m76d23nakkvak3k2m5f5kw)

> I work in a delicatessen shop in Bayswater and go to London University at night to study English.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In the short coda to The Lonely Girl Kate reports that she works in a delicatessen shop in Bayswater and studies English at night, sharing a small bed-sitting room with Baba, who is training as a hotel receptionist in Soho. Later Baba scorns 'a crook who'd do a job on a kitchen table in Bayswater'.
- **Why it matters:** A minor setting that records the girls' humble start in London, before the marriages of the third novel.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7rsbpxs2yfx93e09pmr6ps2)

> And every week or so, on a different, prearranged day, when MI5’s Watchers were known to be elsewhere, he would tell colleagues he was meeting a contact for lunch, climb into his car, and drive to the safe house in Bayswater.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Veronica Price scouts West London for weeks before choosing the flat: an underground garage, a rear gate as an escape route, far enough from the Soviet embassy to avoid chance sightings but close enough for a two-hour round trip. From 1982 Gordievsky comes here at lunchtime, first monthly, then weekly: James Spooner debriefs him while Price makes lunch and Sarah Page photographs smuggled documents in the bedroom. Price also rehearses the escape plan with him here. Once, parking in nearby Connaught Street, he sees Guk's ivory Mercedes glide past.
- **Why it matters:** The flat is where the case pays off; the book calls it a haven from the infighting of the rezidentura. ⚠️ The book gives no address, so the pin marks Bayswater as a whole.
