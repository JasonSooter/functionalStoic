# Bayswater

District of west London north of Kensington Gardens. In _The Country Girls Trilogy_ it is where Kate works in a delicatessen and shares a bedsit with Baba when they first arrive.

## The Country Girls Trilogy — Edna O'Brien (1986)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL69252W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m76d23nakkvak3k2m5f5kw)

> I work in a delicatessen shop in Bayswater and go to London University at night to study English.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In the short coda to The Lonely Girl Kate reports that she works in a delicatessen shop in Bayswater and studies English at night, sharing a small bed-sitting room with Baba, who is training as a hotel receptionist in Soho. Later Baba scorns 'a crook who'd do a job on a kitchen table in Bayswater'.
- **Why it matters:** A minor setting that records the girls' humble start in London, before the marriages of the third novel.
