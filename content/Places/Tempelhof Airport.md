# Tempelhof Airport

Former airport in [[Berlin]]: a Nazi-era terminal building and an airfield that is now the public park Tempelhofer Feld.

## Inside The World's Darkest Airport — The B1M (2026)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sRF_aTASC5c) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5b44tbspeype3rr929sfskz)

> This is Tempelhof Airport, the last remnants of Germania.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The presenter walks the vast terminal and the "Hall of Honors", then goes down into the bunkers beneath it, including a film bunker built under the airport in 1937, where a fire in 1945 burned for several days. The video then covers the airport's time as a US-controlled Cold War outpost and hub of the Berlin Airlift, when "Planes landed here every 90 seconds during peak periods", with a memorial to the 76 pilots who died. It ends on the runway, now a park where kids learn to ride bikes, protected since "a 2014 referendum achieved 64% support, legally preventing any construction on the field."
- **Why it matters:** The site is the whole story: the same building carries Nazi intimidation architecture, the Airlift, and the city's decision to keep the field open. The presenter's unresolved feelings about it are the point of the video.
