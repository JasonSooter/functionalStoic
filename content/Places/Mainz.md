# Mainz

Cathedral city on the Rhine opposite the mouth of the Main, capital of Rhineland-Palatinate. In _Germany: Memories of a Nation_ it is where Gutenberg printed his bible.

## Germany: Memories of a Nation — Neil MacGregor (2014)
Source: [[Germany - Memories of a Nation]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20201162W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m762t18swh7dan92pq8dv5)

> This man was a great combiner, a great entrepreneur, and there are few places where he could have operated so successfully in the 1450s as in Mainz.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 16, 'In the beginning was the printer', places Gutenberg's bible in Mainz in the early 1450s. The text argues that the city's wine presses, nearby metalworking in the Mosel and Saar, Italian paper bought at the Frankfurt fairs and the Rhine for distribution all favoured him. Mainz was an archbishop's state; civil strife in 1460 damaged his business, and his statue now stands in the square before the cathedral.
- **Why it matters:** The chapter's argument is that printing could only have been invented in a place like this.
