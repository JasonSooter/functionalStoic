# Jamaica Avenue and 164th Street (Queens)

Shopping crossroads in Jamaica, Queens, known locally as 'the Ave'. In How to Be an Antiracist (Ibram X. Kendi) it is the centre of his teenage cultural life.

## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fydx79rfrrc3ts3nydb8e2)

> We called the central artery of Southside Queens the Ave, the place where Jamaica Avenue crosses 164th Street.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In the chapter on Culture, Kendi describes weekends on the Ave, reached by dollar cab: blocks of shops, sneakers, hip-hop and Ebonics. He uses it to argue against ranking Black culture below White culture, before the family leaves Queens in 1997.
- **Why it matters:** The Ave is his evidence that Black urban culture is a culture, not a deficit; its specific sounds and styles carry the argument.
