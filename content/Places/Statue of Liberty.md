## The Road Ahead — Kirk Douglas (2016)

Source: [Original](http://m.huffpost.com/us/entry/us_57e03be4e4b08cb1409749f2/amp) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy8d86v39gsedn8fq2j6sj)

> What they did not realize until after they arrived was that those beautiful words carved into the Statute of Liberty in New York Harbor:

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Douglas's immigrant parents arrived believing in a land paved with gold, then discovered that the inscription's welcome did not apply equally to Jews, Catholics, Poles and others. He uses the inscription to frame his warning about current anti-immigrant rhetoric.
- **Why it matters:** A symbolic reference carrying the essay's argument, though the text does not dwell on the physical place.
