# Kasungu

District town in Malawi's Central Region, beneath the hill of Nguru ya Nawambe. In _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ it is the nearby city of Kamkwamba's childhood, where Chief Wimbe is beaten at a presidential rally during the famine.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8dps1afb594axq16gbw9jhp)

> So when President Muluzi announced he was stopping in Kasungu to appoint a local chief, all the subchiefs from the district pleaded with Gilbert’s father to stand up and speak on their behalf.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Kamkwamba's father tells the legend of Mwase, who kills a rhino and is given the land seen from the hill Mwala wa Nyenje, "The Rock of the Edible Flies". The hill is renamed Nguru ya Nawambe after Chewa warriors defeat the Ngoni there, and it "casts a long shadow over the city of Kasungu, just near my village". Banda grows up in that shadow and returns to die there. Kasungu is also the hospital Uncle John never reaches, and the tobacco estate whose closing sends Uncle Socrates's family to live with them. During the famine, Chief Wimbe speaks at Muluzi's rally in Kasungu, asking the president to buy grain. Afterwards thugs knock him down and beat him with clubs and batons.
- **Why it matters:** Kasungu frames the book's two scales: the founding legends of the Chewa land around the village and the national politics of the famine. The beating of the chief makes the government's failure personal, and it happens here in the town nearest home.
