# Aguascalientes

City in central Mexico, seat of the 1914 revolutionary convention. In The Underdogs Demetrio's men find its streets filthy and full of soldiers.

## The Underdogs: A Novel of the Mexican Revolution — Mariano Azuela (1915)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1209983W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8zatpmqpmmpnhcvehm04n)

> The streets in Aguascalientes were so many refuse piles.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Demetrio's men eat badly in a small inn, pass a street vendor selling prayers and charms, and reach General Natera, who explains that the Convention will not recognise Carranza and that Villa now fights Carranza. Demetrio says he will fight on whichever side he is told.
- **Why it matters:** The Convention city is where Demetrio's lack of any political cause is made explicit.
