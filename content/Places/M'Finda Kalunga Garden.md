# M'Finda Kalunga Garden

Gated community garden inside Sara D. Roosevelt Park on Manhattan's [[Lower East Side]]. In _Happy City_ it is where volunteers on the BMW Guggenheim Lab's mood walks felt happiest.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m83qj1hdq7sbdm6tz7nptn7r)

> People reported the biggest spike in happiness, and an easing of arousal, just moments after entering the gated M’Finda Kalunga seniors’ garden in Sara Roosevelt Park.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On the [[BMW Guggenheim Lab]]'s measured walks, volunteers' happiness jumped and their arousal eased as soon as they entered this junglelike garden of varied plants and mature trees, even before they met its resident chicken.
- **Why it matters:** It is the strongest result of Montgomery's own experiment, backing the case that nature restores people even in the densest city.
