# Sun Valley, Idaho

Resort town in central [[Idaho]], host of Allen & Co.'s annual media conference. In Quiet (Susan Cain) it is where Warren Buffett warns of the tech bubble in 1999.

## At a glance

| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|

## Quiet — Susan Cain (2012)

Source: [[Quiet]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/55983/quiet-by-cain-susan/9780141970172) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g8wvx30hs2mcwc15gjdjzr)

> Every summer since 1983, the boutique investment bank Allen & Co. has hosted a weeklong conference in Sun Valley, Idaho.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** At the lavish Allen & Co. conference in July 1999, Buffett gives the closing keynote and explains in detail why the tech bull market cannot last. The audience applauds but dismisses him.
- **Why it matters:** The glitzy, extroverted crowd set against Buffett's careful forecast illustrates the chapter's point about reward-seeking and caution.
