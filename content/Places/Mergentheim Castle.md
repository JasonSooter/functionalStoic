# Mergentheim Castle

Former residence of the Teutonic Order in Bad Mergentheim, [[Baden-Württemberg]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it hosts the wedding reception where the author, a day after seeing his mother's poverty, feels a sudden bond with the exiled Russian nobility.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zmyg7vc9hxz25g3g31dsc)

> Nearly every member of the exiled Russian nobility in Europe was invited, and the reception was set to be held at the castle of Bad Mergentheim, not far from Würzburg.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In early summer 1969 the author and his fiancée Ellen hitchhike from Voorburg to the wedding of Prince Leonid von Manssyreff, his former Military Intelligence teacher, and use the trip to visit his mother in [[Würzburg]]. The next evening, at a long table with eighty Russian exiles in the castle, a wedding singer's melancholy ballad stirs him: he thinks of his two grandmothers and feels connected to everyone there, "even though I’d never been to Russia".
- **Why it matters:** After the numbness of the Würzburg visit, the castle gives the author his first glimpse of an emotional home in his Eastern, exiled roots.
