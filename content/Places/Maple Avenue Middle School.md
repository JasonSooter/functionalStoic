# Maple Avenue Middle School

Middle school on Maple Avenue, north of Saratoga Springs in [[New York State]], USA. In _Happy City_ it is where a twelve-year-old's bike ride to school turned into a fight over who roads are for.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m84ph2nf838ca49j5emknhz4)

> Maple Avenue Middle School is a gigantic amalgamated institution plunked down north of town on its namesake avenue, which looks more like a rural highway than an urban boulevard.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 13, "Save Your City, Save Yourself". On National Bike to Work Day 2009, twelve-year-old Adam Kaddo Marino rides to school with his mother along Broadway and a forest trail. The principal confiscates his bike, citing a 1994 district ban on biking and walking; Maple Avenue has no sidewalks. After media coverage the district legalises biking and a rack goes in, and the family keeps campaigning for sidewalks and safe routes.
- **Why it matters:** The school is Montgomery's example of citizens forcing a city to "reconsider what roads are for", by insisting on their right to move as they please.
