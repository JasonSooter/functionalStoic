## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy79mq9hbzenc93n79b8f4)

> An Oba still rules the city of Benin. His palace is a huge compound, surrounded by tall mud brick walls.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The crew film brass casters near the palace walls, courtiers arriving each morning, the Oba's judgements and a harem that turns out to be dusty mud-brick rooms. Prince Humphrey takes them to the royal shrines with tusks and bronze heads, where stone axe blades are called thunderbolts. The text also recounts the 1897 ambush and the Punitive Expedition that took the bronzes.
- **Why it matters:** One of the longest-dwelt-on places in the range, tying the 1897 conquest history to the crew's filming of the living court.
