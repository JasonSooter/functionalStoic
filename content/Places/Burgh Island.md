# Burgh Island

Tidal island off the south coast of [[Devon]], England, with an Art Deco hotel. It is the real model for the island off "Leathercombe Bay" in Agatha Christie's _Evil Under the Sun_, as retold in _Great Short Books_.

## Great Short Books — Kenneth C. Davis (2022)
Source: [[Great Short Books]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8f027tk07s3yr4wxe50c2x6)

> When Captain Roger Angmering built himself a house in the year 1782 on the island off Leathercombe Bay, it was thought the height of eccentricity on his part.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Davis quotes Christie's first lines: Angmering's house stands on "the little windswept gull-haunted promontory—cut off from land at each high tide." He calls it another of his "Dangerous Beach Holidays": holidaymakers at "an awfully jolly hotel" on a picturesque island off the English coast, Hercule Poirot among them, until one of them is found dead on an isolated beach and Poirot must piece the puzzle together.
- **Why it matters:** The cut-off tidal island makes the closed circle of suspects that the mystery needs.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The island and Leathercombe Bay are fictional, and Davis names no real place. Wikipedia (Burgh Island; Burgh Island Hotel) says the island and its hotel inspired the setting of _Evil Under the Sun_ (and of Soldier Island in _And Then There Were None_, which Davis mentions as "also set on a small island off the coast").
