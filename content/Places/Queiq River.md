# Queiq River

River that runs through Aleppo, Syria. In _The Beekeeper of Aleppo_ it runs below Nuri's house, and the bodies of executed men and boys are pulled from it in Bustan al-Qasr.

## The Beekeeper of Aleppo: A Moving Testament to the Human Spirit — Christy Lefteri (2019)

Source: [[The Beekeeper of Aleppo - A Moving Testament to the Human Spirit]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20112913W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kgr44hk6wtmq82bedyyprk)

> At the bottom of the hill was the Queiq. The last time I saw the river it was full of rubbish.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The Queiq flows at the bottom of the hill below Nuri's house. One winter, in Bustan al-Qasr, bodies of men and boys with their hands tied are fished out of it and laid in the courtyard of an old school; Nuri recognises Mustafa's son Firas. The river also runs through the novel's memories: Nuri first met Mustafa beside it, under a narenj tree, and in his last dream he follows a child to the river and finds Sami there.
- **Why it matters:** The river is where the war becomes personal; Firas's death, and Sami's a week later, end the family's hope of staying.
