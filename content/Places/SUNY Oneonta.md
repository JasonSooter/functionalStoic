## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g0exsctvm9bahmat8tdwgv)

> Caridad was probably the one who ushered me to the lecture at SUNY Oneonta, our state college in the town of Oneonta, in upstate New York.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In February 2010, as a fellow there, Kendi attends a lecture by Boyce Watkins and asks whether racism is better seen as a disease or a part of the body; his colleague Caridad is a friend from this time.
- **Why it matters:** The lecture feeds the metaphor of racism as cancer that runs through the book's end.
