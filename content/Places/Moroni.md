## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy7h1r18t201vb46h6rdwg)

> Peter and I arrived in Moroni, the capital.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Nightly launch trips lower a camera for coelacanths until the cable snaps on a reef. At the airport the Minister of the Interior hands Attenborough a package and then appears as customs chief to question him. After the others leave, Peter Scoones films a live coelacanth in the harbour.
- **Why it matters:** Source of the first footage of a living coelacanth, plus a comic airport episode.
