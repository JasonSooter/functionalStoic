# SAS hotel (The Spy and the Traitor)

Scandinavian Airlines hotel in [[Copenhagen]] where, in _The Spy and the Traitor_, Gordievsky tells MI6 that nobody knows he is meeting them. Pinned at a best guess, the SAS Scandinavia Hotel on Amager Boulevard.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7p6zmn3csgcv3akwcqr4m6p)

> They agreed to meet at the SAS hotel, run by Scandinavian Airlines, a brand-new building never frequented by Soviet officers.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In October 1974, after eight months of silence, Bromhead proposes one more meeting before his posting to Northern Ireland. They meet in the bar at one o'clock, with two PET officers behind a potted palm. Bromhead says "You're KGB" and asks who the PR Line deputy is; Gordievsky grins and says "I am." He then says "No one is aware that I am meeting you", telling MI6 he has not reported the contact.
- **Why it matters:** This is the moment of recruitment: by keeping the meeting secret from the KGB he puts his life in MI6's hands. The hotel was chosen because Soviet officers never went there.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The book says only "the SAS hotel, run by Scandinavian Airlines, a brand-new building" in 1974. Copenhagen had two SAS hotels then: the SAS Royal Hotel (Arne Jacobsen, 1960) and the SAS Scandinavia Hotel on Amager Boulevard, reportedly opened in 1973 ⚠️. "Brand-new" fits the Scandinavia better, so the pin is there (now the Radisson Blu Scandinavia Hotel). The main alternative is the SAS Royal, now the Radisson Collection Royal Hotel, by the Central Station. No reference work naming the hotel was found.
