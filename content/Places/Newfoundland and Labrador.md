## Not the End of the World — Hannah Ritchie (2024)

Source: [[Not the End of the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL37518722W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f0qzh536qb0605cvbjw4h9)

> Looking at the catch of North Atlantic cod from Newfoundland and Labrador in Eastern Canada, we can see that it started to ramp up from the 17th century.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Ritchie traces the catch of North Atlantic cod from Newfoundland and Labrador: rising from the 17th century, about 100,000 tonnes a year in the 18th and around 250,000 in the 20th, peaking in 1968 and then collapsing, with fisheries closed in the early 1990s.
- **Why it matters:** It is her example of a rich country's fish stock crashing. Province point, not the Grand Banks (approximate).
