## Westerners think that Buddhism is about peace and non-violence. So how come Buddhist monks are in arms against Islam? — Michael Jerryson

Source: [Original](https://aeon.co/essays/buddhism-can-be-as-violent-as-any-other-religion) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7ddvt3cekk5d66rdvrkes)

> In Pattani’s capital district, the My Gardens Hotel is popular with tourists. I had gone there to collect people’s opinions on the killing of Buddhist monks.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author interviews two devout Buddhist police officers stationed in the near-empty hotel lobby because insurgents had been bombing local businesses. They give a cool analysis of economics and poverty, but turn to passionate rage when asked about killings of monks, which shocks the author and prompts his questioning of the peaceful-Buddhism image.
- **Why it matters:** Scene of the author's first-hand encounter with Buddhist justification of violence.
