# Montesano

Logging town on the Chehalis River in Grays Harbor County, southwestern [[Washington (state)|Washington]]. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is the hometown of coxswain Bobby Moch.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m878tbwkgb01z87jgppzep5v)

> He had grown up in Montesano, a foggy little logging town on the Chehalis River in southwestern Washington.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Moch grows up in a wet, dusky world of big trees, logging trucks and lumberjacks, the son of Swiss watchmaker Gaston Moch. Small and asthmatic, he plays scrub football on a vacant lot on Broad Street and graduates as Montesano High's valedictorian in 1932 before going to the University of Washington.
- **Why it matters:** The book traces the coxswain's grit and brains, the qualities that later let him hold the boat back and steer it to victory, to this small-town upbringing.
