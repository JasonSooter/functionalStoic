## The Year of Living Danishly — Helen Russell (2015)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20000553W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edhne28jekm663sat7t0fg)

> There’s a laid-back Silicon-Valley-meets-Google-HQ vibe from the moment I step inside the glass-fronted head office in Billund’s sleepy residential centre.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The author visits her husband's workplace for lunch: circular sofas shaped like the Lego brick, meeting rooms named after toys, a canteen serving rye bread and pork, and a factory tour where minifigures are made. It is where she learns about Jante's Law, office singing and drumming, and 'arbejdsglæde'.
- **Why it matters:** It is the main source of the February chapter's argument about Danish work-life balance and the reason the family is in Denmark at all.
