# Buford Highway (Atlanta)

Buford Highway is a multi-lane road through Atlanta's northeastern suburbs. It turns up in Walkable City (Jeff Speck): The street is the concrete image of the book's health argument: a seven-lane, sidewalk-free road with low-income residents.

## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)
Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbjg6z36f22w29rcgnmgs6)

> For Dr. Jackson, the epiphany came in 1999, when he was driving on Atlanta’s Buford Highway

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Epidemiologist Richard Jackson, driving Buford Highway in a ninety-five-degree afternoon, sees a woman in her seventies struggling with two shopping bags. He realizes the real causes of death from heat or traffic would be recorded as medical events rather than lack of sidewalks, trees and transit, the 'aha' moment behind Urban Sprawl and Public Health.
- **Why it matters:** The street is the concrete image of the book's health argument: a seven-lane, sidewalk-free road with low-income residents. The anecdote anchors the chapter on a real, notorious auto-oriented corridor.
