## America's iconic war machine - BBC News — James Morgan (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-33766644) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1vwfywpdk2s5bqekvxh8)

> We are sweltering in the Louisiana summer. The baking hot tarmac of Barksdale runway feels like burning coals.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The reporter visits Barksdale to meet B-52 crews, mechanics and officers of the 96th bomb squadron. Aircraft like Cajun Fear and the Grim Reaper are parked on the hot tarmac, and the crews talk about the bomber. It is the base where the officers' careers and stories are anchored.
- **Why it matters:** The article's central place: the home base of the B-52 fleet and the departure and return point of the record-long Desert Storm and round-the-world flights.
