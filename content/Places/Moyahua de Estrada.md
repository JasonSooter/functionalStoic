# Moyahua de Estrada

Town in the Juchipila canyon, Zacatecas. In The Underdogs it is the market town of Demetrio's enemy, the cacique Don Mónico, which his men later enter.

## The Underdogs: A Novel of the Mexican Revolution — Mariano Azuela (1915)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1209983W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8z7j2v616m0v9gzdr1r5b)

> As the sun's rays began to slip from the housetops, they made their entrance into Moyahua, four abreast, to the sound of the bugle.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Demetrio's army rides into Moyahua, which he calls almost his native town, to confront the cacique Don Monico. Demetrio spares Monico but has his house burned; the square is black with smoke. That night Cervantes brings him a sack of gold and jewels from the looting, and they lodge in another of the cacique's houses.
- **Why it matters:** It is the place where the revolution turns into plunder, and the first sign of Demetrio's moral drift, since Monico is the kind of cacique whose persecution started his rebellion.
