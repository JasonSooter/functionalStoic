# Meteor Crater

A meteorite crater in the desert east of Flagstaff, [[Arizona]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ Eugene Shoemaker proves there that it was made by an impact.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m80tyaa96cqwg9fwss8vnh0e)

> The story begins in the early 1950s when a bright young geologist named Eugene Shoemaker paid a visit to Meteor Crater in Arizona.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In the early 1950s Eugene Shoemaker visits what was then still often called Barringer Crater, after the mining engineer who staked a claim in 1903 and spent 26 years tunnelling for a meteor's iron. Shoemaker concludes it was made by an impact. After his death in the Australian outback, part of his ashes go to the Moon and the rest are scattered around Meteor Crater.
- **Why it matters:** It is where impact geology begins in the book, the start of the road to the dinosaur-extinction theory.
