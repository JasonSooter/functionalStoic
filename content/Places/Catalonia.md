## How YNAB Helped Me Launch A Profitable Business — You Need A Budget (2016)

Source: [Original](http://www.youneedabudget.com/blog/post/how-i-started-a-business-on-a-very-limited-budget) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvmgtb1tdz2vqg0darqgtd)

> During my travels, I fell in love with a Catalan farmer, who lived just north of Barcelona.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** After a sabbatical, the author (Lisa Hoashi) moves from Portland, Oregon to live with her Catalan farmer fiance in Spain, using a spare bedroom as her office while launching a coaching business on limited savings. She later notes she is settled, married, has a Spanish licence and basic Catalan.
- **Why it matters:** It is the setting of the business launch and the budget story, though described only loosely.
