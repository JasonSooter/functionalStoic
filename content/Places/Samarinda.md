Port city on the lower Mahakam in East Kalimantan, Borneo; in _Life on Air_ the starting point of the Mahakam river trip.

## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2n3zc5mnb6p8y56gmbqq)

> 'Well,' I said, 'we are sailing for Samarinda in the morning and I think that is a lovely name.'

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Samarinda is the port where the team lands, buys stores and hires the Kruwing for the river trip. The night before they sail, Attenborough suggests the town's name for Charles Lagus's newborn daughter, which becomes one of her names. Sabran, their helper, returns there at the end.
- **Why it matters:** A departure and return point; it is named mostly in the baby-naming anecdote.
