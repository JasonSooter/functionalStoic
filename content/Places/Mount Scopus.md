## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7waj3yk7hg4dssqbxpdg9)

> then parked his bike at the overlook at Mount Scopus, sat on the low stone wall, shaded his eyes and stared upwards, watching the blimp drift in the blue.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Rami follows the surveillance blimp and sits at the Mount Scopus overlook, imagining speaking to boys on horses below. He voices his inner conflicts and his grief for Smadar.
- **Why it matters:** It is the setting of one of Rami's longest introspective passages.
