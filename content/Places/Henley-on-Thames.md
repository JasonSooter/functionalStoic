# Henley-on-Thames

Thames town in [[Oxfordshire]], home of the Henley Royal Regatta. It is a regatta-time backdrop in Three Men in a Boat, and in The Brompton a chance bus ride from its station brought the author to the company.

## Three Men in a Boat — Jerome K. Jerome (1889)
Source: [[Exploration of Three Men in a Boat]] · [[Three Men in a Boat]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1793164W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gkymqd1bew1v48qq46r6pb)

> Henley was getting ready for the regatta, and was full of bustle.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** From the Shiplake islands George and the narrator walk into Henley to meet friends while the town prepares for the regatta, and then face the long tramp back along the towpath in the rain.
- **Why it matters:** Mostly a backdrop for the lost-boat episode, though the regatta bustle fixes the season and the crowds.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8c38ypjkt84ktx48844crky)

> Sitting next to Tim on the bus from Henley train station to the reception was a young engineer looking for something interesting to do in life.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Brompton's new chairman, Tim Guinness, went by chance to the swan-upping ceremony at Henley, where the City livery companies claim their swans. On the bus from the station he sat next to Will Butler-Adams, then about to start an MBA. Tim invited him to meet Andrew Ritchie and tour the factory, and Will joined Brompton in 2002.
- **Why it matters:** The company's second life, under its second chief executive, starts with this seat on a bus. The book plays up the chance of it.
