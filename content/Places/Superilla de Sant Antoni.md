## How ‘Superblocks’ Can Create People-Centered Cities — Kaja Šeruga (2025)
Source: [[How ‘Superblocks’ Can Create People-Centered Cities]] · [Original](https://reasonstobecheerful.world/superblocks-are-for-people/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edfa63z8mp9fytx1yxwm7x)

> one of five “superblocks” in Barcelona: areas where traffic has been rerouted to prioritize people, community and active mobility.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The piece opens on a Thursday evening in late October at a small intersection here, full of parents, babies, teenagers with skates, children in Halloween costumes and a girl learning to ride a bike. Later Ana Pérez Mendoza recalls living in Sant Antoni when it became a superblock in 2018, with residents split between enthusiasm and fear about traffic.
- **Why it matters:** It is the concrete, lived example that frames the whole argument about people-centered streets; without it the article would be abstract policy.
