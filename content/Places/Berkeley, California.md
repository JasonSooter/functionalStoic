- **Role:** setting

## How iron-air batteries could fill gaps in renewable energy — Alissa Greenberg
Source: [Original](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/nova/article/iron-air-battery-renewable-grid/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmq1stv3tv4d9dhe44nw2t)

> Inside a low-slung warehouse near the marshy coast of Berkeley, California, sleek trays filled with iron dust wait to be assembled into a new form of energy storage.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Form Energy's lab in a Berkeley warehouse holds vats, racks of modules and battery cyclers where batteries are tested at extremes. The plan is to keep Berkeley for testing and engineering and move manufacturing to Weirton.
- **Why it matters:** Berkeley is the R&D contrast to Weirton's manufacturing; the article treats it as the cradle of new technology.
