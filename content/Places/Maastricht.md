# Maastricht

City on the Meuse in Limburg, at the southern tip of the [[Netherlands]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is Frans Münninghoff's last-chance school town, where he falls apart over Wera.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85b0832htf5twj83wz11ytd)

> Alone in his rented room in Maastricht, Frans fell apart.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Expelled from [[Huize Katwijk]], Frans is placed through the Old Boss's Jesuit friends at the Stedelijk Gymnasium; a priest meets him at the station, and a card game at a baroness's castle outside the city secures his place. Later, alone in a rented room after Wera's farewell letter, he skips school, wanders the streets at night and writes home daily that he must come back, while military exercises barricade the bridges over the Meuse.
- **Why it matters:** Maastricht is the end of the Old Boss's attempts to make Frans Dutch; his collapse here pushes the story back toward Riga and Wera.
