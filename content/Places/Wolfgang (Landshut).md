# Wolfgang (Landshut)

District north of the railway in [[Landshut]], Bavaria, founded as a railway workers' settlement in the 1920s. In the BBSR's 15-minute-city report it is one of the worst-rated case-study quarters.

## Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde — Björn Schwarze et al. (BBSR) (2025)
Source: [[Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde]] · [Original](https://www.bbsr.bund.de/BBSR/DE/veroeffentlichungen/bbsr-online/2025/bbsr-online-027-2025-dl.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7kmj369qsrsg9s9rabantxw)

> Der Stadtteil Wolfgang wurde in den 1920er-Jahren als Eisenbahnersiedlung nördlich der Bahnstrecke begründet.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Partly destroyed in the Second World War, rebuilt and then extended with partly suburban building and detached houses. The model still finds good proximity, but with less choice than in Nikola. In the survey, residents judge its gastronomy, cultural offer and the quality of its public space "sehr negativ", and it ranks with Wittenberge and Schwerin-Lankow among the most negatively rated quarters.
- **Why it matters:** It is the report's warning that measured proximity is not the same as a lived 15-minute city when the offer is thin.
