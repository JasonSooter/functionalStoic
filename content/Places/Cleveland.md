# Cleveland

City on Lake Erie in north-east Ohio. A subject in Andrew Sullivan's “America Has Never Been So Ripe for Tyranny”: Site of the 2016 GOP convention, where Trump threatened riots; also the setting of Windrip's convention in Lewis's novel.

## America Has Never Been So Ripe for Tyranny — Andrew Sullivan (2016)
Source: [Original](http://nymag.com/daily/intelligencer/2016/04/america-tyranny-donald-trump.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jkyvyqyraptm0jvwp5y865)

> There will be riots in Cleveland if he doesn’t get his way. The RNC will have “a rough time” if it doesn’t cooperate.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Sullivan reports Trump saying there will be riots in Cleveland, site of the Republican convention, if he does not get his way, and that delegates heading there have received death threats. He also notes that the nominating convention in Sinclair Lewis's novel It Can't Happen Here was held in Cleveland.
- **Why it matters:** Cleveland stands for the convention floor where the nomination fight, and the threat of violence around it, would be decided. The echo with Lewis's fictional convention is the essay's own point.
