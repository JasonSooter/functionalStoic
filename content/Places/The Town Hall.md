# The Town Hall

Historic auditorium on West 43rd Street in [[Manhattan]]. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne stages a bike-advocacy forum there, sponsored by the _New Yorker_.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m772xe6pd0bqn7mkbmbam4pm)

> It is the perfect venue for something like this, having been historically a place where the issues of the day were aired and debated.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Byrne spends about a year trying to organize a public event on biking before the _New Yorker_ offers to sponsor it at Town Hall, where race, birth control and the founding of Israel were once debated. He imagines a forum of ordinary people, advocates and city agencies interspersed with bike-related music, comedy and slide lectures.
- **Why it matters:** It is where Byrne turns his personal riding into civic action; afterwards the Department of Transportation approaches him to judge a bike-rack design contest.
