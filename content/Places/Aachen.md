## Poverty What Does It Mean To Be Poor? — DER SPIEGEL (2016)

Source: [Original](https://www.spiegel.de/international/tomorrow/a-1093371.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvr57ghs506jsfyjbmsjvm)

> Living as she does in the city of Aachen, though, she however can see how much more other people can afford and how people judge others based on their material circumstances.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Monika Kramer, a single mother on Hartz IV, lives in Aachen, enjoys its parks, cheap cafes and free exhibitions, and refuses to call herself poor.
- **Why it matters:** Setting for the third case study; the city is a modest backdrop.
