## Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir (2021)
Source: [[Project Hail Mary]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/611060/project-hail-mary-by-andy-weir/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvstn6py5qtft3z8gcxvpb)

> Africa needs infrastructure. To do that, they need power. And they have nine million square kilometers of useless land that gets some of the most intense continuous sunlight on Earth.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Redell argues the Sahara's sunlight makes it the place to build power and Astrophage-breeding blackpanels, proposing to cover roughly a quarter of it. Grace objects it would wreck ecology; later Leclerc blames the paved Sahara for heat disruption around the Mediterranean and tornadoes in Italy, Spain and Greece.
- **Why it matters:** The planned site of humanity's biggest-ever construction project; discussed rather than visited, so a subject-type place.
