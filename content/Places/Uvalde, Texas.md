# Uvalde, Texas

Small town in South Texas, west of San Antonio. In Greenlights it is Matthew McConaughey's first hometown, where the family's backyard barn sets the scene for the book's rough early stories of his father.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zp3rmcz6jetshwhhsmyr4y)

> It was the summer of 1979 when Dad moved Mom, me, and Pat from Uvalde, Texas (pop. 12,000), to the fastest-growing oil boom East Texas city in the nation, Longview (pop. 76,000).

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The family house in Uvalde has an old wooden barn by the dirt alleyway where Dad keeps an empty eighteen-wheeler from his pipe-hauling days. One Saturday night there, after beer and knife-throwing, Dad tries to make Mike "roll pipe" from Mike's own client, Don Knowles; Mike refuses and the two fight in the sandy gravel with fists and a 2 x 4. Earlier, in 1969, Dad drives the 112 miles from Fred Smither's hunting camp back to the Uvalde house in the middle of the night to fetch Pat for a bet.
- **Why it matters:** McConaughey sums it up himself: "Where Uvalde taught me to deal, Longview taught me to dream." Uvalde is the hard-knocks childhood the rest of the memoir grows out of.
