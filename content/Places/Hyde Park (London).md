# Hyde Park (London)

Royal park in central [[London]]. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne rides through it and reads class in its dogs and clothes.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76rjfbqg93mtby0h6b9439h)

> On my way back to the hotel, I ride through Hyde Park. The sun is shining brightly, rare for this town.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Riding back to his hotel on a rare sunny day, Byrne notices that only a few "rarified breeds" of dog, and people, seem to use the park. He watches a matron in a green hunting jacket and Wellington boots with children dressed as miniatures of her.
- **Why it matters:** The park is the stage for his "Country Life" riff on clothes as class signals in the city.
