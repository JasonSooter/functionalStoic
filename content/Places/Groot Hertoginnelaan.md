# Groot Hertoginnelaan

Elegant avenue in the Duinoord and Zorgvliet area of [[The Hague]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where Wera and her young son start over in a guesthouse attic after being pushed out of Briva Latvija.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85na156xwvyzatetxtdpfbj)

> Life on The Hague’s elegant Groot Hertoginnelaan was extremely difficult for my mother and me.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** To force Wera to give up her son, the Old Boss announces in 1950 that Briva Latvija is too big and she must leave by 1 February. By the end of January she has found work as a housekeeper in a guesthouse on Groot Hertoginnelaan run by a widow, Mrs. Roovers, for sixty guilders a month plus room and board. Mother and son share an attic room at no. 128, split by a clothesline and sheets, with two windows on the grey January sky; Part Two of the book opens here.
- **Why it matters:** The attic is the price of Wera's refusal to hand her child to the Old Boss, and the start of the author's life outside the family fortress.
