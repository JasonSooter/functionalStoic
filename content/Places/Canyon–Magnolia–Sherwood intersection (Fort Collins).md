# Canyon–Magnolia–Sherwood intersection (Fort Collins)

Five-way street intersection west of downtown Fort Collins, Colorado, where Canyon Avenue meets West Magnolia and South Sherwood Streets. Bike Fort Collins chose it as a MyCycle focus spot after an asphalt-art installation.

## MyCycle - Still Time to Help, Nancy Nichols Receives an Award, New Trail Section Opens, and More! — Bike Fort Collins (2024)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f0p2zcr6dfwtc1b7x2ast7)

> The survey is a unique opportunity for community members to share insights on the recent asphalt art installation at the Canyon, Magnolia, and Sherwood intersection.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the newsletter:** Bike Fort Collins makes this junction its monthly MyCycle focus, asking readers to report conflicts or feeling unsafe when cycling through it. A second survey asks about the recent asphalt art installation at the intersection and whether it has changed how people choose to walk or bike.
- **Why it matters:** It is a small but concrete case of the advocacy group's method: pick one awkward junction, gather riders' experiences, and test whether street art changes behaviour. Elsewhere the item would read the same; the point is the local, block-level focus.
