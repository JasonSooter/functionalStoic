# Netersel

Village in the Kempen of North Brabant, part of Bladel, in the south of the [[Netherlands]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is the farm where Frans and Mimousse hide from German justice and where their marriage slowly falls apart.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zmth3phc8zetsrh5xhfck)

> He and Mimousse left for Netersel, a parish in southern Brabant where he’d been in 1945 on his way to Belgium.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After the [[Lingen]] verdict, Frans considers The Hague too dangerous and buys a run-down farm in Netersel, planning a mansion with stables and a library cottage that never gets built; the couple live in the front half, making plans at the kitchen table. The author visits at Christmas and Easter and watches the inheritance quarrels grow, until Mimousse tells Frans to go to Germany "if you love it so much". In March 1979 the author sits beside his drunk, changed father at Mimousse's funeral in the St. Anthony and Brigid church.
- **Why it matters:** Netersel is where Frans's grand plans shrink to a farmhouse kitchen and his marriage to Mimousse ends; the funeral there is the author's last close look at the father he once knew.
