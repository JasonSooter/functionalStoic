# Cornell University

Private university in Ithaca, New York. In _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_ it is where a burned-out young professor watches a plate wobble in the cafeteria and finds his way back to physics.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfqafhgxjfasn6f7g56er4)

> As the plate went up in the air I saw it wobble, and I noticed the red medallion of Cornell on the plate going around.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After the war Feynman becomes a professor at Cornell and feels burned out. In the cafeteria someone throws a plate in the air; he notices the red Cornell medallion going round as the plate wobbles and works out the motion just for fun. Playing with physics again leads him back to the problems in quantum electrodynamics. Elsewhere he complains about Ithaca's small-town life and weather before he leaves for Caltech.
- **Why it matters:** The cafeteria scene is a turning point: he says the work that won him the Nobel Prize came out of this piddling with the wobbling plate.
