# Yankee Stadium (1923)

The original Yankee Stadium in the Bronx, New York City (1923–2008). In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is the scene of Max Schmeling's 1936 upset of Joe Louis, which the Nazi press turns into propaganda.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m878tmzfzy36x2vz6emva3pb)

> When the fight began, in a sold-out Yankee Stadium on the evening of June 19, Louis was, at eight to one, the overwhelming favorite in New York.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** On June 19, 1936, three days before the Poughkeepsie regatta, a sold-out stadium watches Louis, an eight-to-one favourite, dominate the first rounds before Schmeling floors him in the fourth and goes on to beat him. Even Ulbrickson and Ebright give predictions. The book follows the reaction in Harlem and among German Americans, and Goebbels's gloating in Berlin.
- **Why it matters:** A parallel story rather than a crew scene, it frames the racial politics and Nazi propaganda that will surround the Berlin Games.
