# World Health Organization headquarters

WHO headquarters on Avenue Appia in [[Geneva]], which appears in Hans Rosling's memoir as the place where he left samples from the Mozambique epidemic for analysis in 1981.

## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling (2019)

Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20831115W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqzj6fy558pya1j6ebb7nj)

> We left the massive WHO building feeling content.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On the way home in November 1981 the family rents a car in Geneva so Rosling can leave blood and urine samples from the epidemic in a basement fridge of the WHO toxicology department, because Mozambique lacks the capacity to analyze them. The family also buys clothes for their tattered children.
- **Why it matters:** A minor but telling waypoint: the handover of samples starts the cyanide analysis that leads to his thesis, and Geneva's cleanliness marks the culture shock of returning.
