# San Jose Convention Center

Convention centre in downtown San Jose, California. In _Just for Fun_ it hosts LinuxWorld on the day Red Hat goes public.

## Just for Fun: The Story of an Accidental Revolutionary — Linus Torvalds and David Diamond (2001)

Source: [[Just for Fun - The Story of an Accidental Revolutionary]] · [Original](https://search.worldcat.org/oclc/45610395) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kkryy9dtdxkp2z9wdr5bev)

> It was the second day of the Linux World convention and trade show which had been taking place at the San Jose Convention Center.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Linus, Dirk Hohndel and Tove drive to LinuxWorld on the day of Red Hat's IPO. On the conference floor Linus shakes hands and gets word of the share price, which makes his stock options worth a fortune; he later holds a press Q\&A at the same expo.
- **Why it matters:** Where Linux first makes him rich, a turning point in his relationship with money and fame.
