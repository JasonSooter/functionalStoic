# Guatemalan town museum (Surely You're Joking)

An unnamed museum in a small Guatemalan town where Feynman finds a copy of the Dresden Codex on his honeymoon. The book never names the town, so it is pinned at a representative point for Guatemala.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfr50672ncy5bmh8nydfv7)

> In some little Guatemalan town in the middle of nowhere we went into a museum that had a case displaying a manuscript full of strange symbols, pictures, and bars and dots.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On his honeymoon with Mary Lou, Feynman is worn out by climbing pyramids. "In some little Guatemalan town in the middle of nowhere" he sees a copy, made by Villacorta, of the Dresden Codex in a museum case, buys one, and deciphers its bars and dots in his hotel room while his wife climbs more pyramids.
- **Why it matters:** It starts his amateur expertise in Mayan astronomy and mathematics, which leads to his later lectures on the codex.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real museum, town unnamed.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The text gives only "some little Guatemalan town in the middle of nowhere" on a honeymoon tour of Mexican and Guatemalan pyramids, with a museum that sold copies of Villacorta's edition of the codex. One web search found no reference work naming the town. The pin is a representative point for Guatemala; the likeliest candidates are towns near the Maya sites the couple visited, such as Flores near Tikal, or Guatemala City.
