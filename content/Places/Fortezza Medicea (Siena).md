## The Technology That Created a New Generation of Runners — Jennifer Stahl (2015)
Source: [Original](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2015/11/the-technology-that-created-a-new-generation-of-runners/417126/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hk5dsqmyv25drxfh9tkz9y)

> My path took me to the top of a 16th-century fort in Siena, Italy, as the rising sun lit the surrounding rooftops a brilliant yellow.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author runs to the top of a 16th-century fort in Siena at sunrise, then feels the run does not count because without wi-fi she cannot log it on Runkeeper. The scene frames her piece on run-tracking technology and the backlash toward running without it.
- **Why it matters:** It is the essay's hook; any beautiful untracked run would do, but the Tuscan setting sharpens the irony.
