# Tiergarten

Berlin's great central park, west of the Brandenburg Gate. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is the route of the military parade for Hitler's forty-seventh birthday in April 1936.

| Source | Author | Role | In one line |

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m878tg17y7x0dvhane0qyxks)

> In Berlin thousands of celebrants gathered to watch and to cheer as Hitler reviewed a procession of more than fifteen hundred tanks, armored vehicles, and artillery pieces rumbling through the city’s massive park, the Tiergarten.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** On April 20, 1936, two days after the Washington crew's win over California, crowds (some with rented periscopes) watch Hitler review more than fifteen hundred tanks, armoured vehicles and artillery pieces rolling through the park. The book lists his gifts, among them a hand-copied _Mein Kampf_, and sets the day after the March reoccupation of the Rhineland.
- **Why it matters:** The book cuts between Seattle and Berlin; this scene shows the regime's growing power just as it prepares to stage the Olympics as propaganda.
