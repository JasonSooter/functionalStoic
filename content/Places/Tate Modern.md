# Tate Modern

Modern art museum in a former power station on Bankside, [[London]]. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne finds a room of Soviet propaganda layouts there.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76rjkxpf2yd0012hkny4z5t)

> I cycle back along the South Bank promenade until I reach the Tate Modern.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Byrne cycles along the South Bank to the Tate Modern, where a single room shows spreads from the 1930s Soviet magazine _USSR in Construction_, often designed by Rodchenko and El Lissitzky. He finds the layouts gorgeous and is chilled that they sold a murderous regime as a hip, enlightened place.
- **Why it matters:** The room raises the chapter's question of whether formal innovation can be separated from the ideology it serves.
