## Brazil is Taking America’s Skyscraper Crown — The B1M (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xpwGBR19D2U) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqtsc9g6afhw2pb9cn6jj2)

> It’ll stand here in Balneário Camboriú, a small coastal city that’s just a third the size of Denver International Airport and home to a mere 145,000 people.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The video explains why this small coastal city of about 145,000 people, once a fishing village, became Brazil's skyscraper capital: post-dictatorship zoning reform handed power to municipalities, waterfront land is scarce and costly, and air rights carry no height limits once bought. It hosts four of South America's five tallest towers and is where the 550 m Senna Tower is to rise.
- **Why it matters:** The whole argument is about this city's unusual regulatory and land-value conditions, nicknamed the Dubai of Brazil and a 'billionaires row'.
