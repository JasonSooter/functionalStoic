# Hillbrow

Dense inner-city neighbourhood of Johannesburg. In Born a Crime it is where Trevor Noah's parents meet and where he is born.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6yq401tmsayb4sjy44w7s1f)

> My mom’s secret flat was in a neighborhood called Hillbrow. She lived in number 203.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 2. Trevor's mother lives illegally in flat 203. Down the corridor in 206 lives Robert, a Swiss/German expat. Trevor calls the Hillbrow of the time "the Greenwich Village of South Africa", a cosmopolitan scene of clubs and underground parties where black and white people mixed. His mother is a regular at the Hillbrow Tower's nightclub with its rotating dance floor, and she and Robert go dancing there. She asks Robert to father her child, and on February 20, 1984, she checks into Hillbrow Hospital for the C-section. There she claims the light-skinned baby's father is from Swaziland.
- **Why it matters:** the neighbourhood's brief tolerance made the book's central "crime" possible: a black woman and a white man having a child under apartheid. Set anywhere less mixed, the meeting could not have happened.
