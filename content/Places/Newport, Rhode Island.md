## How to Not Get Overwhelmed by All of the Advice Online — Groove Blog (2015)
Source: [Original](https://www.groovehq.com/blog/friday-qa-july-17-2015) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ga10t1dmv15kw1b56mfqhp)

> I started Groove from my house in Newport, Rhode Island.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Answering a reader who asks whether a startup must be in Silicon Valley, the founder points out that he started Groove from his house in Newport and lists the tech hubs he did without. He argues the drawbacks of being off the beaten path were mostly superficial.
- **Why it matters:** The answer turns on this location as the counter-example to the startup hubs.
