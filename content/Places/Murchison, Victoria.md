# Murchison, Victoria

Small town in the Goulburn Valley of Victoria, [[Australia]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ it is where the Murchison meteorite fell in 1969, carrying amino acids from space.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8159hzqmc03a0sarks1ejgq)

> The fireball exploded above Murchison, a town of six hundred people in the Goulburn Valley north of Melbourne, and came raining down in chunks, some weighing up to twelve pounds.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In September 1969, after sonic booms and a strange smell, a fireball explodes over Murchison and rains down in chunks of up to twelve pounds. The townspeople collect about two hundred pounds of the rare carbonaceous chondrite, which turns out to be 4.5 billion years old and to contain 74 amino acids, eight of them used in earthly proteins; in 2001 a team also finds polyols in it.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's key evidence that life's building blocks can arrive from space, the basis of its discussion of panspermia.
