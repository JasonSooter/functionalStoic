## Not the End of the World — Hannah Ritchie (2024)

Source: [[Not the End of the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL37518722W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e31cgvby7cadx9v96jajvc)

> Delhi’s average concentration of tiny particulates – those smaller than 2.5 micrometres – is twenty times higher than the WHO guidelines. In winter, things get even worse.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Delhi's winter pollution is the case for why the 2016 and 2019 'odd-even' car rules only cut pollution by 5% and 13%. The text says cars were only about 4% of the winter PM2.5, with crop-burning, industry, dust and generators bigger sources.
- **Why it matters:** Delhi shows that fixes must target the real sources of pollution; it also anchors the later India crop-burning section.
