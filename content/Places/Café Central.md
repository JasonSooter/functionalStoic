# Café Central

Historic coffee house in the Palais Ferstel, [[Vienna|Vienna]], Austria; in Neither Here Nor There it is one of the famous cafés that disappoint Bryson.

## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74125W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bek6cc641d92jyrqd00gqc)

> Even the Café Central, where Trotsky used to hang out, sitting for long hours every day doing nothing, was a disappointment.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 19. It has some atmosphere ("vaulted ceilings, marble tables, a pianist"), but coffee costs thirty-four schillings and the service is indifferent. He retells the story of two Viennese at the Central doubting that the idle Trotsky could ever lead a revolution.
- **Why it matters:** Part of his argument that Vienna's famous cafés were "the biggest disappointment"; the exception is the [[Café Hawelka]].
