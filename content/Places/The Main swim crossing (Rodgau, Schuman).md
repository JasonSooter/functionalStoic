# The Main swim crossing (Rodgau, Schuman)

The Main swim crossing is the stretch of the Main river between Hesse and Bavaria where a Rodgau commuter swims to work, pinned as a best guess at the ferry crossing near [[Seligenstadt]]. It appears in Rebecca Schuman's article as the second example of commuting by swimming.

> “I can see my office from here!” she laments on the Hesse side of the river, as she gazes across at the Bavarian side. Alas, the nearest bridge is six kilometers downriver.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Jutta Maaßen of Rodgau rides to the Main bank, stuffs work clothes in a dry bag and swims across because the ferry stops early. She changes on the Bavarian side and cycles the rest of the way on a second bike she keeps there.
- **Why it matters:** Second example, with the Isar swimmer, of commuting by swimming.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The text does not name the real place; the pin is a best guess.
