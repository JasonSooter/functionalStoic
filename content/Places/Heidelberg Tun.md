# Heidelberg Tun

The Heidelberg Tun is a giant wine vat in the cellars of Heidelberg Castle in Germany. In _Moby-Dick_ it names the oil-filled case in the sperm whale's head, which gives Chapter 77 its title.

## Moby-Dick — Herman Melville (1851)

Source: [[Exploration of Moby Dick]] · [[Moby Dick]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL102749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m77a27ev62pg6maepev52f)

> upper part, known as the Case, may be regarded as the great Heidelburgh Tun of the Sperm Whale.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In Chapter 77 Ishmael compares the case of the sperm whale's head to the great tun of Heidelburgh: as that famous tierce is carved in front and filled with the best Rhenish wines, the whale's plaited forehead holds the most precious spermaceti. In Chapter 78 Tashtego, baling spermaceti from the head, falls into the 'Heidelburgh Tun' as the head sinks, and Queequeg dives in and delivers him.
- **Why it matters:** It is a figure of speech, not a place visited, but it frames a chapter and the Tashtego rescue. It shows Ishmael's habit of measuring the whale against the wonders of the land.
