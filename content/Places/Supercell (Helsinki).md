# Supercell (Helsinki)

Finnish mobile-game company based in Helsinki. In _The Nordic Theory of Everything_ its colourful office is the book's exhibit against the claim that Nordic welfare stifles entrepreneurship.

## The Nordic Theory of Everything — Anu Partanen (2016)

Source: [[The Nordic Theory of Everything]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20028831W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kjftv99wj0q4w01915d3yn)

> On this day, however, I opened a door to a different world entirely—one of bright orange walls, canary yellow and forest green chairs, pink curtains, sneakers thrown in piles by the coat rack as if I’d walked into a house full of teenagers, and huge white blocks of letters in the middle of the space spelling “SUPERCELL.”

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On a rainy October morning Partanen enters Supercell's brightly coloured office and meets the CEO, Ilkka Paananen, who explains the company's small autonomous teams, five weeks' holiday and small administration, and how the founders paid high Finnish taxes after selling a stake to SoftBank.
- **Why it matters:** The office is the book's main evidence that the Nordic model and entrepreneurship can coexist.
