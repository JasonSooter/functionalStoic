## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)

Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbnqmachrnhn5svbfexps6)

> That building now graces East Bay Street, where it does three important things right.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Mayor Joe Riley persuaded a local architect to make a new parking garage look like a traditional Charleston building. The garage on East Bay Street puts commercial space at the sidewalk, hides its ramps, and details its upper floors with shuttered window openings, so cars are hidden behind it.
- **Why it matters:** It is Speck's first model of 'invisible parking': a garage that serves cars but reads as a street building. The specific garage is not named, so the street coordinate is only a representative point.
