# 26 Fairmount Avenue

House on Fairmount Avenue in South Meriden, Connecticut, that Tomie dePaola's family built and moved into in 1940. It is the title place of his childhood memoir, which follows the house from the 1938 hurricane to moving day.

## 26 Fairmount Avenue — Tomie dePaola (1999)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/351865/26-fairmount-avenue-by-tomie-depaola/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8d25n3pfbn0v8qhsveafbf4)

> I didn’t always live in the house at 26 Fairmount Avenue. We moved there when I was five years old.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The house is the book's goal from the first chapter. Building starts after the Hurricane of 1938. When the city regrades the dirt road, the house is left high above the street, and a nor'easter turns the plowed backyard to mud. On moving day, just after New Year's 1940, the family drives up from Wallingford through Yalesville, Tracy and South Meriden, along Hanover Street and up Highland Avenue to Fairmount Avenue. The book ends with Tomie in his new bedroom, with the house as his "wonderful home". From the house he can see West Peak and Castle Craig.
- **Why it matters:** It is the title and the frame of the whole memoir: every episode (the hurricane, Snow White, kindergarten) is a step on the way to living here. Set anywhere else, the book would lose its organising thread.
