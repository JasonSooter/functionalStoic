## Scott Pruitt, the EPA and the looming legacy of Tom Joad — proactiveoutside1 (2016)
Source: [Original](https://proactiveoutside.wordpress.com/2016/12/13/scott-pruitt-the-epa-and-the-looming-legacy-of-tom-joad/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5t6wyz2cxmpre76zmmt7v)

> The Sequoyah Fuels Corporation had a nuclear fuel processing plant near the small town of Gore, Okla., and in 1986, an explosion there released 29,500 pounds of gaseous uranium hexafluoride, launching a 30-foot column of poisonous fumes into the air.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** A 1986 explosion at the plant released uranium hexafluoride, killing one person and hospitalizing 37. Later spills and groundwater contamination followed. Nearby buildings including the old Carlisle School remain abandoned, and waste is buried under berms.
- **Why it matters:** Second of four Oklahoma environmental disasters the author uses as a reminder to Pruitt.
