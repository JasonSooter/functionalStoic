# Ol Pejeta Conservancy

Wildlife conservancy in Laikipia County, central Kenya. In Not the End of the World, it is home to the last two northern white rhinos.

## Not the End of the World — Hannah Ritchie (2024)

Source: [[Not the End of the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL37518722W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f0qp8esbvbpz2j8ntj7jdv)

> Najin and Fatu live in Ol Pejeta Conservancy, a protected wildlife area in Kenya.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The biodiversity chapter opens with Najin and Fatu, the last two northern white rhinos, living here under armed guard with their horns sawn off while labs try reproductive treatments. Ritchie uses them to ask why we care about a species that is no longer functionally essential.
- **Why it matters:** It sets up the chapter's argument about the value of species. The scene is specific to this reserve.
