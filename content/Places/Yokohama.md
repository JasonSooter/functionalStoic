# Yokohama

Port city on Tokyo Bay, Japan. In _Around the World in Eighty Days_ a penniless Passepartout wanders its Japanese quarter, joins Batulcar's acrobats and is found by Fogg.

## Around the World in Eighty Days — Jules Verne (1872)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1100007W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne3pryrm2bhn02t6mzbt9m)

> The Japanese quarter of Yokohama is called Benten, after the goddess of the sea, who is worshipped on the islands round about.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The Carnatic reaches Yokohama on 13 November, and the penniless Passepartout wanders the European quarter and then Benten, the Japanese quarter, hungry, and swaps his clothes for Japanese ones. He joins William Batulcar's troupe of acrobats to earn a passage and is at the base of a human pyramid when he spots Fogg in the audience and brings the pyramid down.
- **Why it matters:** It is where master and servant are reunited, a comic turning point on the way to San Francisco.
