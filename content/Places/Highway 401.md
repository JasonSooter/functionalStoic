## I GOT A NEW TRUCK!! (AND A MILLION SUBSCRIBERS!) — Not Just Bikes (2023)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8nZh7A7qTPo) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8cqw69v5d6byshqbnws276t)

> This is the busiest highway in North America, and it’s up to 18 lanes wide.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the video:** In this satire the narrator plays a proud suburban truck owner who drives the 401 and calls it a beautiful example of the engineering that makes suburbia possible. Passing roadworks, he cheers that the section is finally being widened, while a coworker points out that one more lane won't fix the traffic.
- **Why it matters:** The highway stands in for the whole car-dependent system the channel argues against. The video names only "Highway 401", so the pin is on its widest Toronto stretch.
