# Vega (Norway)

Largest island of the [[Vega Archipelago]] off Helgeland, Norway, with a small town, church and harbour. In James Rebanks's memoir it is the last outpost of "civilization" and the harbour from which boats leave for the outer islands.

> Vega was still civilization. Further out were places with no electric or shops or other amenities.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Vega has a tiny town, a large wooden church with a plaque to those lost at sea, a fishing village and a harbour. The night before the voyage Rebanks sits in Anna's living room there while she tells him stories. Next morning the party loads her boxes through her family's sea barn, signed "MÅSØY BRYGGE", onto Henrik's boat; Rebanks finds the harbour quiet, some sea barns turned into holiday lets, like "a retirement village, or a museum".
- **Why it matters:** It is the threshold between the modern world and the outer islands, and the place against which the remoteness of the duck island is measured.
