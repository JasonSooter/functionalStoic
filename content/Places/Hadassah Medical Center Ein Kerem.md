# Hadassah Medical Center Ein Kerem

Hospital in Ein Kerem, west [[Jerusalem]]. In _Apeirogon_ it is where Abir dies and where Smadar was born.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7w2krp5b1w1mb8ev17870)

> The Israeli hospital. At Ein Kerem. An ancient Palestinian village, once.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Hadassah is where Abir is meant to be transferred for proper equipment and where Smadar was born. The hours spent trying to reach it, and the doctors' request for organ donation, are central to Abir's death.
- **Why it matters:** It links Rami's and Bassam's families and is the goal Bassam is kept from reaching.
