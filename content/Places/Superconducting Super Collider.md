# Superconducting Super Collider

The abandoned site of a giant particle accelerator near Waxahachie, [[Texas]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ it is the most expensive hole in the universe.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m80ty5p95k5nv4h2w2hta19h)

> which began being constructed near Waxahachie, Texas, in the 1980s, before experiencing a supercollision of its own with the United States Congress.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The Superconducting Supercollider begins construction near Waxahachie in the 1980s. Congress spends $2 billion on it, then cancels it in 1993 after fourteen miles of tunnel have been dug, leaving Texas with "the most expensive hole in the universe" and the site a vast cleared field.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's example of how big and costly particle physics has become, set against CERN.
