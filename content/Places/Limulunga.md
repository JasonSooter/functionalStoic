# Limulunga

Wet-season royal residence of the Lozi king near Mongu, western Zambia; in _Life on Air_ Attenborough has an audience with the Litunga there.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2r610nfetar567ekxgr1)

> Limalunga was photogenically traditional - clusters of circular thatched houses, each surrounded by a neat palisade of reeds.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The Litunga's wet season residence: a palace in the middle with a tall palisade, drums and sirimbi played continuously, and people dancing. Attenborough requests an audience by clapping a kandalela, waits through an awkward silent conversation with the Litunga, and films court musicians. It was also David Livingstone's first mission station in Barotseland.
- **Why it matters:** A principal scene of the Zambezi chapter, vividly described with the protocol comedy of meeting the king.
