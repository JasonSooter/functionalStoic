## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2y927veqess4d6j514zh)

> There, in the little settlement of Shupanga we sought out the grave of Livingstone's wife Mary who had accompanied him on his expedition to the lower reaches of the Zambezi and there had died from malaria.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** At the lower Zambezi, the team finds Mary Livingstone's grave. The baobab beneath which she was buried has fallen and the cross was replaced by a khaki-painted iron one. He lays flowers on it.
- **Why it matters:** A small, moving end-point of the Livingstone thread.
