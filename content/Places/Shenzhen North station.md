# Shenzhen North station

High-speed rail hub in the northern part of [[Shenzhen]], Guangdong, opened in 2011. It is where Simply Railway's overnight high-speed sleeper to Beijing begins.

## 2,000 km Overnight on China’s Bullet Train with Beds — Simply Railway
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XD79kv_CanM) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6cg5e8byjt9nry09bbdwp76)

> Today I'm in Shenzhen, in southern China,
> just outside Shenzhen North station.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the video:** Thibault opens the trip report outside the station, which he calls immense, "one of the biggest stations I've ever seen", with an airport vibe and a crowded waiting hall. He explains that tickets are linked to an ID card or passport and scanned at the gates before security, then waits for the gate to open and goes down to platform 16 for the D902 to Beijing West, departing 18:25.
- **Why it matters:** It is the origin of the 2,424 km night run and the place where the video shows how Chinese rail travel works before boarding. Any big Chinese terminus could have served, but the scale of this one sets up the video's theme of China's rail ambition.
