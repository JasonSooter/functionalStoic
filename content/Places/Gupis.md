## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)
Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6n1yh9hrwv4axv929ra0e)

> I forgot to tell you about the pseudo-soccer match I saw in Gupis.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Cyclist arrives with a donkey caravan at a village post with a meteorological station, then watches a local soccer match played beside a 200-foot ravine. Earlier she delivered the herd, rested, and was fed rancid-ghee paratis here.
- **Why it matters:** A comic, vivid scene of remote Gilgit-valley life and the end of her donkey-caravan stage.
