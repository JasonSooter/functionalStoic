# Bukchon (Jeju)

Fishing village in Jocheon-eup on Jeju's north coast. In _The Island of Sea Women_ Young-sook moves there with her teacher husband, and it becomes the Village of Widows.

## The Island of Sea Women — Lisa See (2019)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19865296W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvchccwxkd4btpe6f3j6ap)

> Bukchon was built around a small, well-protected cove. The beach had more sand than Hado, but it still had plenty of lava rocks to step on as I made my way to the bulteok.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In September 1945 Young-sook moves to Bukchon, built around a small cove, to join her husband Jun-bu, the new teacher, and joins Chief Yang Gi-won's collective. After martial law in 1948 mountain refugees are herded to a camp outside the village wall; after the January 1949 massacre so many men are dead that it is called the Village of Widows, and Young-sook flees at night to Hado.
- **Why it matters:** It is where Young-sook's married life is built and destroyed, and where the Jeju 4.3 violence lands on the novel's characters.
