# Madura

Island off the north-east coast of Java, Indonesia. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ the first Dutch fleet massacres a local prince and his flotilla there.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m82efnjthr8zfqktcvnyqcey)

> As the Dutch ships passed Madura, a low-lying island off the Javanese coast, the local prince (not yet privy to the events in Bantam) decided to put on a display of friendship

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** As Houtman's ships pass Madura, the local prince comes out with a flotilla of boats to show friendship; the suspicious Dutch open fire, setting off a general massacre in which the prince and all but twenty of his men are killed.
- **Why it matters:** It is the starkest example of the brutality and paranoia of the first Dutch voyage.
