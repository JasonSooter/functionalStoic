## Four Thousand Weeks — Oliver Burkeman (2021)
Source: [[Four Thousand Weeks]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL22355777W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fcq9mps2e7j5hhp48xpwcn)

> Robert Pirsig describes arriving with his young son beside the blazing blue expanse of Crater Lake in Oregon, a collapsed prehistoric volcano that is America’s deepest body of water.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Quoting Pirsig's Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance, the author describes Pirsig arriving with his son at the lake and failing to feel the experience, because it is smothered by being so pointed to.
- **Why it matters:** A secondary example of the paradox of trying to be present; the lake is described but the scene is a quoted illustration. Another scenic spot would serve equally.
