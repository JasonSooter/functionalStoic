# Parkway Central Library

Main branch of the Free Library of Philadelphia on the Benjamin Franklin Parkway in [[Philadelphia]]. It is where NPR's piece on libraries and the cost of living finds patrons sewing and playing music for free.

## As budgets tighten, people are breaking out their library cards — Joe Hernandez (2026)

Source: [Original](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/05/nx-s1-5992908/libraries-and-cost-of-living) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6vfvhp53c395hs8684813p0)

> At a biweekly crafting meetup called "Sew What?!" at the Free Library of Philadelphia's Parkway Central Branch, the library provides sewing machines and materials at no cost.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The closing section visits the branch: Chloe deBeus works on her Halloween costume at the free sewing meetup, and down the hall Nathaniel Muns, a recent graduate whose own keyboard broke, practises "All the Things You Are" in the music department and says the library helps him decompress.
- **Why it matters:** It illustrates the article's last point, that libraries lend far more than books; the scene could be any big-city branch, but it is the one place the piece shows a "library of things" in use.
