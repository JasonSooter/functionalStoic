## Uncommon Type — Tom Hanks (2017)

Source: [[Uncommon Type]] · [Original](https://penguin.co.uk/books/1114786/uncommon-type/9781786091338.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5w0xg6y9m1gjbftmvcrb0)

> The small house they all lived in was far out of town on Webster Road, closer to Molinas than to Iron Bend, which was the county seat and where Kenny’s father was head cook at the Blue Gum Restaurant.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 'A Special Weekend' Kenny's blended family lives in a small house far out on Webster Road, near Iron Bend, the county seat, where his father cooks at the Blue Gum Restaurant. His mother collects him from there for a weekend in Sacramento, and he is flown back over the Valley at the end.
- **Why it matters:** The cramped rural home is the contrast to his mother's city life and the place he is returned to.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Iron Bend, Molinas and Webster Road are invented; the surrounding towns (Chico, Willows, Orland, Maxwell, Sacramento) and Mount Shasta are real.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Best guess. The story makes Iron Bend a county seat in the northern Sacramento Valley among almond orchards, with only Channel 12 from Chico on TV, Mount Shasta visible on clear days, and Willows and Orland on the highway south toward Sacramento. No reference work names a real model. Red Bluff, the Tehama County seat north of Orland and closest to Shasta, fits these clues best; Corning (not a county seat) and Willows (on the route, not beyond it) are the main alternatives.
