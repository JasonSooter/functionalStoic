# Begnemato

Dogon village on the Bandiagara Escarpment in Mali. In Greenlights it is where Matthew McConaughey wrestles the village champion and spends a night under the stars.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m700jm9spw377pmqz60q9j91)

> That night, alone on a straw mattress, I lay on the roof of a mud hut in the Dogon village of Begnemato in the middle of Mali, Africa, staring into the heavens where I counted twenty-nine shooting stars cascade across the sky.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In the Dogon country, in Begnemato, he accepts a wrestling challenge from the village champion, Michel, in a dirt pit; they fight to exhaustion, the chief raises both their hands, and the villagers chant his Bambara name, "Daouda." That night he lies on the roof of a mud hut counting shooting stars. Next morning Michel silently holds his hand for the fifteen-mile walk to the next village.
- **Why it matters:** The climax of the Africa section: far from any phone line and from fame, he is accepted on his own terms. The remoteness of the Dogon village is the point.
