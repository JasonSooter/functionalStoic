## Peace Corps: The Great Adventure — Peace Corps (2002)
Source: [[Peace Corps - The Great Adventure]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bs7pye5v96926s226pfj0b)

> I was fortunate to be sent to lovely old Bukhara to spend my term of service.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** John Smart serves as a crafts-marketing adviser and high-school economics teacher. He is shown the [[Ark of Bukhara]] on his first day, goes to mosque and to weddings, and tutors the daughter of the regional mufti; the story turns on the gift of a doppa (skullcap).
- **Why it matters:** The city's age and its Muslim community shape everything that happens to him; the story is about being taken in by this particular place.
