# City Palace, Udaipur

Palace complex of the Maharanas of Mewar in Udaipur, Rajasthan. In _The Jungle Book_ Bagheera tells Mowgli he was born in the cages of the King's palace at Oodeypore.

## The Jungle Book — Rudyard Kipling (1894)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19870W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne2srx25n0e9qs9psw840d)

> “There is no one in the jungle that knows that I, Bagheera, carry that mark—the mark of the collar; and yet, Little Brother, I was born among men, and it was among men that my mother died—in the cages of the king’s palace at Oodeypore.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Bagheera reveals to Mowgli that he was born in captivity and that his mother died in the cages of the king's palace at Oodeypore, where he wore a collar before breaking the lock and escaping. This explains why he paid the bull to buy Mowgli into the Pack.
- **Why it matters:** The only named real city in the first stories, it anchors Bagheera's backstory in a real Rajasthani court and explains his tie to men.
