# Dawson City

Klondike gold-rush town at the mouth of the Klondike River, Yukon. In _The Call of the Wild_ Buck wins Thornton's bet by breaking out a thousand-pound sled in front of its Eldorado Saloon.

## The Call of the Wild — Jack London (1903)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14942956W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne5nkrw3g6k2epmpsx6hx3)

> The Eldorado emptied its occupants into the street to see the test. The tables were deserted, and the dealers and gamekeepers came forth to see the outcome of the wager and to lay odds.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Buck's team reaches Dawson mid-book, hauling freight to the mines while the huskies howl at night. In winter, men boasting in the Eldorado Saloon lead Matthewson to bet Thornton $1,000 that Buck cannot break out and pull a thousand-pound sled a hundred yards; the Eldorado empties into the street at sixty below, and Buck does it.
- **Why it matters:** The wager is Buck's greatest feat for love of Thornton and pays for the journey east that ends the novel.
