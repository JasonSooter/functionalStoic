# 42 Holland Park

Residential building on the street called Holland Park in Notting Hill, [[London]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is the KGB rezident Arkadi Guk's flat, where the "Koba" letter arrives.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7rsbsm0spjh9h7s4hzff8wc)

> On April 3, 1983, Easter Sunday, Arkadi Guk returned to his flat in 42 Holland Park and found an envelope had been pushed through the letterbox.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The envelope holds the MI5 legal brief for the expulsion of Igor Titov and two GRU men, with details of how they were identified, and a note signed "Koba" offering more secrets and elaborate contact instructions. "Someone inside British intelligence was offering to spy for the Soviet Union." Guk, suspicious by nature, takes it for a provocation.
- **Why it matters:** It opens the Koba thread, a new danger for Gordievsky because a mole inside British intelligence could expose him. It happens in the rezident's own home, the most direct line into the KGB in London.
