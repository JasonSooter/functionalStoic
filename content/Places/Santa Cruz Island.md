## All Queens Must Die - The Verge — Ryan Bradley (2016)

Source: [Original](https://www.theverge.com/2016/8/25/12608928/santa-cruz-island-argentine-ants-extermination-nature-conservancy) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy7f7f4m9kngfehn3t4ym0)

> The sheep were trickier. There were a lot more of them, something like 40,000, grazing over 96 square miles of mountainous island covered in dense chaparral, little oak woodlands, deep canyons, towering cliffs, and some of the largest sea caves in the world.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The island is where cows, sheep, bees, pigs and turkeys were eradicated in turn, and where Christina Boser now plans to poison Argentine ants with gel beads dropped from a helicopter. The author spends days there, reflecting on what counts as native. Ants arrived in the late 1950s/60s, crept up the wash into the orchard, and were noticed in 1996; the poison drop later begins near the citrus grove where they were found.
- **Why it matters:** The whole article is about this island as a laboratory for rewilding and the ethics of invasive-species eradication.
