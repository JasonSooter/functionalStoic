## ‘Poverty is often looked at in isolation, but it is an American problem’ — the Guardian (2016)
Source: [Original](https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/2016/may/29/matt-black-photography-poverty-geography-california-us-sean-ohagan) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvq4b1q3kz7c8aa4c0xkwy)

> USA. Allensworth, California. 2014. Fence post. Allensworth has a population of 471 and 54% live below the poverty level.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article opens with Matt Black's photograph of a hand on a fence post taken in Allensworth, a town of 471 people where 54% live below the poverty level. It stands as the emblem image of his Geography of Poverty project.
- **Why it matters:** The lead example the piece uses for its argument that poverty is a national, not local, problem.
