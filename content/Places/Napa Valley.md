## Inside OpenAI, Elon Musk's Wild Plan to Set Artificial Intelligence Free — Cade Metz (2016)
Source: [Original](https://www.wired.com/2016/04/openai-elon-musk-sam-altman-plan-to-set-artificial-intelligence-free/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jkzbjb2cf5105artrf0yqe)

> Brockman picked the ten researchers he wanted the most and invited them to spend a Saturday getting wined, dined, and cajoled at a winery in Napa Valley.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Brockman invited ten top researchers to a Saturday at a winery. The drive and the day gave the group chemistry, and by day's end he asked all ten to join; nine did within three weeks.
- **Why it matters:** The recruiting day that turned the idea into a lab; the article quotes Brockman and Sutskever on the talk mattering more than the wine.
