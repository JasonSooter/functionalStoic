## Germany: Memories of a Nation — Neil MacGregor (2014)

Source: [[Germany - Memories of a Nation]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20201162W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75cjb2jvcz87k1ccy3mvb)

> The Elector of Saxony spirited Luther away from Worms to safety in the Wartburg Castle in Eisenach.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In Chapter 6, 'A language for all Germans', the Elector of Saxony spirits Luther away from the Diet of Worms in 1521 to the Wartburg above Eisenach. In his solitary room there Luther translated the New Testament in eleven weeks, and it was on sale by September 1522.
- **Why it matters:** Germany's political patchwork made the refuge possible, and the book written there shaped a common written German. The same Eisenach later schooled Bach, who absorbed Luther's words.
