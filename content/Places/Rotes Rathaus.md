# Rotes Rathaus

Berlin's red-brick city hall in Mitte, near Alexanderplatz. In _The Boys in the Boat_ Avery Brundage accepts the keys to the city here on the American team's arrival in July 1936.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m87k3kq1xrnmg669qzj4ngys)

> Avery Brundage accepted the keys to the city and made a brief speech.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** At the end of the bus procession down Unter den Linden on July 24, 1936, the team stops at the redbrick city hall. Brundage, thrilled after the long fight over the boycott, exults that "no nation since ancient Greece has captured the true Olympic spirit as has Germany", while Joe and his crewmates, up since five in the morning, warily eye the uniformed men around them. Most athletes then go to the Olympic village; the oarsmen go on to [[Köpenick]].
- **Why it matters:** A short scene, but it puts the American Olympic chief's embrace of the regime on record at the very start of the Games.
