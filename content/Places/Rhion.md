# Rhion

Port on the narrow strait at the mouth of the Gulf of Corinth, facing Antirhion on the northern shore. In _Gates of Fire_ Alexandros and Xeones follow the army here and are cast adrift mid-gulf.

## Gates of Fire — Steven Pressfield (1998)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/133742/gates-of-fire-by-steven-pressfield/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m896j2np5a9nf3e433wzfzp8)

> The twin ports of Rhion and Antirhion commanded the western approach to the Gulf of Corinth. This avenue threatened the Peloponnese and all of central Greece.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Leonidas marches to bring Antirhion into the alliance. Alexandros and Xeones trail the army through Selassia and Tegea and reach the bluff at Rhion after midnight on the third day, with Antirhion's lights visible across the strait. From an obscure launching beach called the Ovens they hire a "pirate" smack, which abandons them in the widest part of the gulf. They spend hours in the water talking about the people they love.
- **Why it matters:** It is the boys' first step out of Lakedaemon toward real fighting. The strait's strategic position is why the army is there at all.
