## The Martian — Andy Weir (2011)
Source: [[The Martian]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17091839W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7v7gb8qx9zwzjb3aqzt75)

> Bruce Ng sat in the JPL cafeteria along with hundreds of engineers who had given everything they had to Iris. They watched the live feed on a projection screen. Some fidgeted, unable to find comfortable positions. Others held hands. It was 6:13 a.m. in Pasadena, yet every single employee was present.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After Watney revives Pathfinder, JPL engineers in Pasadena build a makeshift control center in a former conference room and hold press conferences in the JPL press room. Bruce Ng's teams then build the Iris supply probe, sealing it after sixty-three days and watching its launch from the cafeteria, and later build the stripped-down Taiyang Shen payload in twenty-eight days.
- **Why it matters:** Real NASA lab in Pasadena where the rescue probes are built and where staff gather at dawn to watch the launch.
