## Quiet — Susan Cain (2012)
Source: [[Quiet]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/55983/quiet-by-cain-susan/9780141970172) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g8wc7z2zxrfee71cn2vbbm)

> After much hemming and hawing, I signed up for a workshop at the Public Speaking–Social Anxiety Center of New York.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Cain signs up for a workshop led by Charles di Cagno with a cross-section of New Yorkers. Students speak at levels of anxiety they can manage and get feedback; she is told she looks confident.
- **Why it matters:** The class is where she stretches her temperament, and the practice behind her later speaking career starts here.
