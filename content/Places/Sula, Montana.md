# Sula, Montana

Tiny community in the Bitterroot valley of Ravalli County, Montana. It is the Ryback brothers' second food pickup.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bes0gecta98zyqmexv15h2)

> Sula, Montana — a single, somewhat rundown building which proved to be the gas station, general store, post office and gatehouse for the cluster of trailers that comprised the village of 43 people.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On July 14 the brothers reach Sula: a single rundown building that is gas station, general store, post office and gatehouse for a trailer court, in a village of 43 people. Mr. Anderson, the owner, hands over their 30-pound supply box and later offers the trailer-court showers; Eric notes it was not the "royal treatment" of Lincoln. They repack and head back up into the Bitterroots.
- **Why it matters:** A resupply stop that measures how far they are from anywhere; the book's days are counted from here ("the eighth day out from Sula").
