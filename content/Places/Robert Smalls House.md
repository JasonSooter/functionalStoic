# Robert Smalls House

House in Beaufort, South Carolina, where Robert Smalls was born enslaved. He later bought it from his former owner, and it bookends his story.

> The house in Beaufort, South Carolina, where Smalls was born.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Smalls was born a slave in Beaufort. After the war he bought the house from his former owner and died there in his 70s. It became a National Historic Landmark in 1974.
- **Why it matters:** Bookends his life story as the place of birth, enslavement, and eventual ownership.
