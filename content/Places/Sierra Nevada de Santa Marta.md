## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy787hgcjh9s9fzq9v197m)

> We drove to one of the small towns in the southern foothills of the Sierra Nevada, but thereafter we had to travel on horseback.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After two days on horseback the party reaches a deserted Spanish monastery with an empty cemetery, the monks having failed to convert the Ika. In a nearby village they see coca-chewing men, a sacred enclosure and a dark men's house.
- **Why it matters:** Presented as the refuge of the Tairona's descendants, giving a living glimpse of the culture whose gold was looted; a substantial scene in the chapter.
