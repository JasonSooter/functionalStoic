# Soho

District of the West End of [[London]]. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne watches two football louts barge into a smart Soho restaurant, a scene he reads as British class antagonism.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m772wrkv74z8x66gjw5mp288)

> We go for drinks at a nice place in Soho, a white tablecloth place, but nothing hoity-toity.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Over drinks, a couple of tattooed football louts stroll in and shout that "come the revolution you lot will all be sorry." The maître d' backs away, the staff reach for their phones, and the pair toss insults at the diners before wandering out; Byrne notes the restaurant is next door to the Ivy.
- **Why it matters:** It is the concrete scene behind the chapter's claim that British class antagonism "keeps the yobs in their place and makes the toffs nervously squirm in theirs."
