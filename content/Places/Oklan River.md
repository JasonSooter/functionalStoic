# Oklan River

River in Kamchatka Krai, Russia, the second-largest tributary of the Penzhina. In _Last of the Breed_ Joe Mack heals for weeks in a cave above one of its minor tributaries after his escape.

## Last of the Breed — Louis L'Amour (1986)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL51048W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6ycpgr8am73hc59ppqw6kyk)

> Three weeks later Joe Mack huddled in a cave above one of the minor tributaries of the Oklan River.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Without the weapons he cached before his capture, and with his feet "in terrible shape", he has lived on marmots, voles and the odd ptarmigan, then on fish from a trap woven of plant fibres. He finds the cave by accident while drinking from a stream: an opening masked by dwarf stone pine, with a sandy floor where someone long ago built fires. There he makes a new bow, arrows and a sling and rebuilds his strength before setting out for the sea.
- **Why it matters:** The book's last refuge: the recovery here is what makes the final walk to the coast possible.
