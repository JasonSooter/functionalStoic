# Vaalimaa border crossing

Border crossing on the Leningrad–Helsinki road at Vaalimaa, between Russia and [[Finland]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is where Gordievsky is smuggled out of the Soviet Union in the boot of a British diplomat's car.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7xy61bpvppzcj8yxw684pbr)

> The next checkpoint brought them to the border itself. The men parked the cars side by side in the fenced parking lot of the immigration holding area, and then joined the queue at the customs and immigration kiosk.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The Finnish reception team gathers at the Vaalimaa Motel, about ten miles from the border, and waits in a forest clearing off a logging track. At the frontier the two British cars queue at the customs and immigration kiosk with Gordievsky hidden in Arthur Gee's trunk; when an Alsatian sniffs at it, Caroline Ascot changes her baby's nappy on the lid and drops the diaper beside the dog, which "duly slunk off, offended." Past the last barrier, the cars turn onto the forestry road, Gordievsky staggers out, and tells Jean: "Jean, I was betrayed."
- **Why it matters:** This is the climax of the escape plot. Everything in the book's second half builds toward these few barriers; set at any other crossing it would lose the one road that leads straight from Leningrad to Helsinki.
