## Is Private School Worth It? — Retire by 40 (2015)

Source: [Original](https://retireby40.org/is-private-school-worth-it/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hj1v7s8x0dsqya1casw6vw)

> There was a better-rated public school in SW Portland, Ainsworth elementary.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Ainsworth is described as better rated, in one of Portland's wealthiest areas and closer to home than Chapman. When Portland Public Schools redistricts, the son ends up there, and the author credits its parent fundraising and support.
- **Why it matters:** It resolves the article's question: a good public school makes private school unnecessary for this family.
