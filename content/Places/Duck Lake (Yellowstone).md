# Duck Lake (Yellowstone)

Small lake near West Thumb in [[Yellowstone National Park]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ the park geologist shows Bryson that it is the crater of a hydrothermal explosion.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W)

> We drove to a place called Duck Lake, a body of water a couple of hundred yards across.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On their tour Paul Doss stops at Duck Lake, a pond a couple of hundred yards across, and tells Bryson it "didn't used to be here": it was formed by a hydrothermal explosion within the last 15,000 years. He adds the 1989 blast at Pork Chop Geyser, which left a crater about five metres wide, and says such explosions come without warning.
- **Why it matters:** A small, concrete example of the park's everyday violence, scaled down from the supervolcano to something a visitor could stand beside.
