# Rosenheim

Town in Upper Bavaria, Germany, on the Inn near the Alps. In Greenlights it is home to Johan's motorcycle shop, the start and end of Matthew McConaughey's ride across Europe.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zp4972905ga8cqfsvn4edb)

> After landing, we rented a car and headed south, where we found a first-class motorcycle shop in Rosenheim, Germany.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Sent to Europe by Don Phillips, he and his friends Cole and Rory fly to Amsterdam, drive south and walk into Johan's shop. The month's rental is over $12,000, but Johan, who toured Europe by motorbike as a young man, lets them take three new bikes for $400 apiece, holding their airline tickets, over his wife's objections. Three weeks and thousands of miles later they ride back to the shop and he greets them with bear hugs.
- **Why it matters:** The trust of a stranger ("What a mensch") is the heart of the episode; Rosenheim frames the whole trip.
