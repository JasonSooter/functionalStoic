# Baoji

City in western Shaanxi, China, known for its titanium industry. Titanium for early Superlight forks was made here, in a plant the author describes as built inside a mountain.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8c399kzw0j8903hbcdjtvj6)

> If the Rapid factory in Korolev had been a slight culture shock, the titanium plant in Baoji was something else. It is literally built inside a titanium mountain, where the ore was mined.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** The Taiwanese supplier Ain Tec, which made Superlight forks, subcontracted the titanium to its subsidiary in Baoji, so the author went to see it. He describes the workforce marching in and out of the mountain "like a river of humanity", "like stepping into the Land of Mordor", and billets leaving on trucks to become grade 9 alloy. He also recalls the remoteness, a banquet of toasts and an unexpected karaoke evening.
- **Why it matters:** It shows how far the supply chain for one premium model reached, and why the company later wanted titanium made closer to home.
- **Real vs. fictional:** ⚠️ The book says the plant is "hundreds of kilometres from any other human habitation"; the pin is Baoji city, not the plant itself.
