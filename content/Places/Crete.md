## Podcast #195: How to Live Like an Ancient Greek Hero — Brett McKay (2016)

Source: [Original](https://www.artofmanliness.com/2016/04/24/podcast-195-how-to-live-like-an-ancient-greek-hero/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jkzg9vatwd5p0je8femswr)

> During World War II the Greek island of Crete was occupied by the Nazis and was a strategic stronghold.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** A British-backed band of poets, historians and Cretan shepherds resisted the Nazi occupation and kidnapped a German general. The guest went to Crete to follow in their footsteps and found they had tapped the ancient Greek art of the hero.
- **Why it matters:** The island is the stage for the story and the source of the Cretan running, eating and mountain-crossing practices in the episode.
