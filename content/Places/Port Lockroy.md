# Port Lockroy

Old British research station on Goudier Island off the Antarctic Peninsula. In Tom Hanks's story 'Three Exhausting Weeks' it is the first stop of the friends' Antarctic trip.

## Uncommon Type — Tom Hanks (2017)
Source: [[Uncommon Type]] · [Original](https://penguin.co.uk/books/1114786/uncommon-type/9781786091338.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5td35xk0tcqbcs8mnjpra)

> We’ll fly to Lima, Peru, then change planes for Punta Arenas, Chile, where we’ll board a boat to make the crossing from South America to the old science station at Port Lockroy, our first stop.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** In 'Three Exhausting Weeks' Anna plans an Antarctic trip to see iceberg B15K. After the breakup the narrator, Anna and MDash still go: via Lima and Punta Arenas, across the Drake Passage to the old science station at Port Lockroy, their first stop.
- **Why it matters:** The destination bookends the story: it is the next big adventure the group shares and fits MDash's ship-and-compass proverb, ending the story on a note of friendship rather than rancor.
