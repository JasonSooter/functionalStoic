## The Little Book of Hygge: The Danish Way to Live Well — Meik Wiking (2016)

Source: [[The Little Book of Hygge - The Danish Way to Live Well]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19352003W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jrdfggpbrd5whtbc7v8bsenk)

> Christianshavn is part of the city centre in Copenhagen but it is separated from the rest of the centre by the Inner Harbour.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In the "hygge safari", Wiking describes Christianshavn as cut off from the rest of the centre by the Inner Harbour and dominated by canals, a little like Amsterdam, and suggests renting a rowing boat with blankets, wine and a picnic basket.
- **Why it matters:** A short travel tip that ties hygge to slow time on the water.
