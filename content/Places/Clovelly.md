## #089 A comprehensive guide to Devon — Diagram Club (2026)
Source: [[089 a Comprehensive Guide to Devon]] · [Original](https://diagramclub.substack.com/p/089-a-comprehensive-guide-to-devon) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bey049gf0f91bech8swj6m)

> Clovelly is a seaside village for which you need a wristband.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The entry fee covers upkeep and, quoted from the sign, "a 'contribution towards care of the donkeys'. Yes, there are donkeys." It is "downhill all the way. And uphill all of the way back", though a Land Rover lift back up is available for a few pounds; there are cobbles and a picturesque harbour.
- **Why it matters:** One of the trip's named highlights, described for its oddity. Minor beside [[Appledore]], the base.
