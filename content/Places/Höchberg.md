# Höchberg

Market town on the western edge of [[Würzburg]] in Bavaria. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where the author's mother Wera finally gets a decent home in the 1980s, bought by her granddaughter's father.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zn7s1v294g8h4s65j5vtz)

> He bought a respectable apartment for his daughter Dorothea in Höchberg, a quiet middle-class suburb of Würzburg where many civil servants lived with their families.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Hans Rakemann, father of Tatiana's daughter Dorothea, finds them in the shed on the Main and buys a four-room flat in Höchberg for the four women: Dorothea, Tatiana, Wera and Monika, whom Siegfried Bauer has abandoned. He also gives them a monthly allowance. Wera now has a telephone, and Ellen plans to restore contact and visit after a ski trip.
- **Why it matters:** The flat ends Wera's long poverty and makes possible a late reconciliation with the son she lost in 1951.
