# Mount Zion Protestant cemetery

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjqps8jvf52a5dq5qnz2j9)

> On the morning of Monday, September 7, 1835, Christopher Costigin was buried in a cemetery in the shade of Mount Zion overlooking the Kidron Valley, sloping down towards the Dead Sea where he had traveled.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After rescuers bring him back to Jerusalem, Costigin dies after being nursed in the Casa Nuova hospice, and Reverend Nicolayson buries him; a single cloud gives brief relief from the heat as the coffin is lowered.
- **Why it matters:** Ends Costigin's story and links the grave to the Kidron Valley sloping toward the sea he explored.
