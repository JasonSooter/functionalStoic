## Minimalism By Default — Jim (And Then We Saved) (2016)
Source: [Original](http://andthenwesaved.com/minimalism-by-default/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy7118nm2angh99bpm376s)

> Parked on Colfax I awoke on a February morning in my ice encrusted sleeping bag to a serene calm.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** After separating and selling his house, Jim slept in his truck parked on Colfax in winter. One February morning he woke in calm, then walked Colfax thinking about the future and decided against re-accumulating stuff, starting his minimalism.
- **Why it matters:** The site of the author's epiphany; named street, Denver implied by the bio (Jim lives and works in Denver).
