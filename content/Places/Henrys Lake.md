# Henrys Lake

Lake in Fremont County, Idaho, just west of Yellowstone, ringed by the Continental Divide. The Ryback brothers reach it at the end of a three-day storm.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bes9fj0nc0n78hpjprh1v9)

> by mid-morning reached Henry's Lake, around which the Continental Divide looped before dropping down into Yellowstone Park.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** A freezing, soaking storm lasts three days; Eric remembers nothing of the country they pass through to Red Rock Pass. Chewing dry oatmeal as they walk, they reach Henry's Lake by mid-morning, where the Continental Divide loops around before dropping into Yellowstone. It is "a milestone of sorts", and soon after the sun comes out near Targhee Pass.
- **Why it matters:** It ends the storm and marks the threshold of Yellowstone, the next chapter's subject.
