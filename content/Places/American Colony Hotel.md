## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjqafr31j6w1kgpa4j3x2e)

> Rami saw Vanunu sitting in the back courtyard of the American Colony Hotel in East Jerusalem.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Rami, ten minutes early for a meeting, sees Mordechai Vanunu holding court at a courtyard table under mulberry trees. Rami listens through the sound of a fountain, thinks of introducing himself, and the two exchange a glance of recognition as he leaves.
- **Why it matters:** A quiet East Jerusalem courtyard where two men branded traitors by Israelis cross paths without speaking; the fountain muffling their words suits the scene's theme of what goes unsaid.
