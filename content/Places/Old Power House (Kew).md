# Old Power House (Kew)

Brompton's first production site, a small workshop beside Kew Gardens station in west London. The first 500 Brompton bicycles were made here around 1981.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8c38rda6nec150p1qhkjypc)

> Andrew was able to raise money from the first group of investors in Brompton Bicycle and set up production in the Old Power House, near Kew Gardens. After making 500 bikes, however, he lost a supplier – the company producing the hinges went bankrupt.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Andrew Ritchie first pre-sold bikes on a "when built" basis to pay for parts, a kind of early crowdfunding. By 1981 he had raised money from the first investors and set up production at the Old Power House; the book's note on the factories calls it "a small workshop beside Kew Gardens Station". After 500 bikes, the company making the hinges went bankrupt, and for four years every bank Andrew asked for a loan turned him down.
- **Why it matters:** The book traces Brompton's habit of controlling everything in-house to this setback. Losing one outside supplier stopped the whole company, and the authors say the lesson shaped Brompton for decades.
