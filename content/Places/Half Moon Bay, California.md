## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy90hqp8zzsfb771394028)

> We joined him south of San Francisco on the coast of Half Moon Bay.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bruce Mates tracks radio-tagged blue whales; pilot Steve circles above while Barb drives a Zodiac. Attenborough, tied into the bows, sees a blue whale surface close by and gets his long-wanted whale two-shot.
- **Why it matters:** Place of the blue whale two-shot, a set piece of the Mammals chapter.
