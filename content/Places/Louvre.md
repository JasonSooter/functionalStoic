## How to Stop Lying to Ourselves: A Call for Self-Awareness — James Clear (2015)
Source: [Original](https://jamesclear.com/stethoscope-self-awareness) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ga124x1yjp7m2a1t2ht9gx)

> It was September of 1816 and two Parisian boys were playing in the courtyard of the Louvre, the famous museum in Paris.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** In September 1816 René Laennec, crossing the Louvre's courtyard on the way to a patient, sees two boys tapping a long piece of wood with a pin and listening at the other end. The scene gives him the idea for the stethoscope.
- **Why it matters:** It is the opening scene and the spark of the essay's anecdote, though the place itself matters little to the argument about self-awareness.
