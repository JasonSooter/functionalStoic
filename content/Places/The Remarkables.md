# The Remarkables

The Remarkables are a mountain range beside Lake Wakatipu and Queenstown in Otago, New Zealand. They are pinned for _The Hobbit_ because Peter Jackson's Hobbit films used them for the Misty Mountains; they are not a place in the book.

## The Hobbit (Middle-Earth Universe) — J. R. R. Tolkien (1937)
Source: [[The Hobbit]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL27482W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m779t52zrctzw82mc8fp4c)

> There were many paths that led up into those mountains, and many passes over them.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** In the book, the company leaves Rivendell on Elrond's advice and climbs for long days toward the right pass over the Misty Mountains. A thunder-battle breaks over them, they shelter in a cave, and goblins seize them through a crack in its back, which leads to Bilbo's fall into the dark and his meeting with Gollum.
- **Why it matters:** The mountains are where the adventure turns: Bilbo is separated from the others, finds the ring, and starts to prove himself.
- **Real vs. fictional:** This is where Peter Jackson's Hobbit films were shot; it is not a place in the book.
- **Where is it?:** Wikipedia (The Hobbit film series, filming locations) lists The Remarkables, Otago, as a location for the Misty Mountains. The book's crossing itself is modelled on Tolkien's 1911 Swiss hike (see Lauterbrunnen).
