# Trinity Site

Site in the Jornada del Muerto desert, New Mexico, of the first nuclear explosion in July 1945. _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_ gives Feynman's eyewitness account from twenty miles away.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfq98v07safxd64z0tetrv)

> I flew back, and I arrived just when the buses were leaving, so I went straight out to the site and we waited out there, twenty miles away.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Back from a short break after his wife's death, Feynman goes straight out to the site and waits "twenty miles away". The radio fails until minutes before the shot; he watches through a truck windshield, sees the flash and the rising fireball, and hears the bang about a minute and a half later.
- **Why it matters:** It is the climax of the Los Alamos chapters and the moment the work becomes real.
