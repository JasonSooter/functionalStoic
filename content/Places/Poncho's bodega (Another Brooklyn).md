> Then to the STOP sign on the corner. Around the corner to Poncho’s bodega, but only together.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Poncho's store is around the corner from the narrator's home, and the children are allowed there only together. A jar of pickled pig's feet sits on the counter and Poncho chooses for customers. The girls also run there for razor blades.
- **Why it matters:** Bushwick with Knickerbocker Avenue nearby is the reliable anchor; the store is fictional.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ inferred.
