## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy7jtn6kspjzwvgb1cq99d)

> We arrived on Cape May in the late afternoori.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On one night of the year, about a million horseshoe crabs emerge to spawn while gulls and waders gather. The crew watch the females bury eggs and the gulls feast, then wonder how the crabs time the tide.
- **Why it matters:** A single spectacular wildlife set piece.
