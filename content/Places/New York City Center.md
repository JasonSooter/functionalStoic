# New York City Center

Theater on West 55th Street, Manhattan, home of Alvin Ailey's New York seasons. Casey Gerald's essay 'The Black Art of Escape' opens with a performance of Revelations there.

## The Black Art of Escape — Casey Gerald (2019)

Source: [Original](https://nymag.com/intelligencer/2019/08/the-black-art-of-escape.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7gc6ppe808seq6nmwm1ch)

> as we shuffled out of the theater behind Ms. Lauryn Hill, who eased past the taxis that lined 55th Street and rounded a corner, into the night.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Where he first saw Revelations on December 4, 2015, beside a friend from Texas, and noticed Lauryn Hill in the audience. The performance frames the essay’s theme of flight and escape; the venue is not named.
- **Why it matters:** The performance that opens and closes the essay takes place here.
