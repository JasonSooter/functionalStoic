## Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir (2021)

Source: [[Project Hail Mary]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/611060/project-hail-mary-by-andy-weir/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvsrv0rwwkhnvp3k49mjjw)

> Auckland Prison, which the locals called “Pare” for some reason, was New Zealand’s only maximum-security prison unit.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Grace and Stratt are searched at the gate by head guard Easton, who makes Stratt surrender her Taser. They are led through the maze-like corridors to the Maximum Security Unit to interview imprisoned engineer Dr. Robert Redell. Redell pitches his 'blackpanel' idea for mass-producing Astrophage, and Stratt recruits him.
- **Why it matters:** Setting of the scene that produces the blackpanel plan to breed Astrophage at scale; part of Grace's recovered memories.
