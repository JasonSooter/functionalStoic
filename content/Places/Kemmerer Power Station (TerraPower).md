## The road ahead reaches a turning point in 2024 – Bill Gates — Bill Gates (2024)
Source: [Original](https://www.gatesnotes.com/The-Year-Ahead-2024) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8x9zf9p5zhjyt1x88apje)

> Earlier this year, I visited the future home of the first TerraPower plant in Kemmerer, Wyoming.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Gates describes the planned advanced nuclear plant in Kemmerer, which is replacing the Naughton coal plant. It will bring 1,600 construction jobs and 200 to 250 operating jobs, including former coal workers, and a sodium test facility starts construction in 2024.
- **Why it matters:** Concrete example of next-generation nuclear and a coal-town transition.
