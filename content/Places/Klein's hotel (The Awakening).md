# Klein's hotel (The Awakening)

## The Awakening — Kate Chopin (1899)

Source: [[The Awakening]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL65430W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebwq2e993hxeemse8xx3s0)

> There was a band playing down at Klein’s hotel, and the strains reached them faintly, tempered by the distance.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Léonce Pontellier walks over to Klein's hotel to play billiards and returns near eleven at night in high spirits with his winnings. From the Grand Isle cottages Edna and the others can faintly hear the hotel band. It serves as the nearby men's gaming and social place.
- **Why it matters:** Grand Isle is explicitly the setting, so the island is the real anchor even though Klein's itself is fictional.
