## Project for Public Spaces Awards Three Community Placemaking Grants to Transform Public Spaces for Street Safety and Mobility — Project for Public Spaces (2025)

Source: [Original](https://www.pps.org/article/project-for-public-spaces-awards-three-community-placemaking-grants-for-street-safety-and-mobility) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2ye76bykzjee2b75y675n)

> Without traffic calming measures or adequate pedestrian space, Clarkson Street is hectic, congested with cars during peak hours, and dangerous, with drivers using it as a cut-through to the nearby Holland Tunnel.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** A building on Clarkson Street houses two specialized schools without a gym. Open Plans receives a grant to make permanent the street's Open Streets for Schools improvements so students and neighbors get a safer, calmer space.
- **Why it matters:** One of the three funded sites; the text gives the most concrete account of its problem (cut-through traffic toward the Holland Tunnel).
