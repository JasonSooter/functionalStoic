## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)

Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e31wpchc9v1sac01088c72)

> I was asked to give a lecture on David Hockney to docents at the de Young Museum in San Francisco.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The author lectures docents while the museum shows Hockney's Seven Yorkshire Landscapes; docents report that visitors then saw the nearby San Francisco Botanical Garden anew, in slow, broken-up textures.
- **Why it matters:** The museum-to-garden anecdote shows art acting as an 'attentional prosthesis' that carries into the surrounding environment.
