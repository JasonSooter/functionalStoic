# Linksfield Hospital

Private hospital in the north-east of Johannesburg, near Linksfield. In Born a Crime it is where Trevor Noah's mother is treated after Abel shoots her in the head.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zbjx0yjhe6zf5fhy623xsa)

> There was an outdoor sitting area by the entrance to the emergency room. Andrew was standing there waiting for me, alone, his clothes smeared with blood.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 18 ("My Mother's Life"). Andrew phones Trevor to say that Abel has shot their mother and that they are at Linksfield Hospital. Trevor finds his brother waiting outside the emergency room and sees his mother in triage. Because she has dropped her health insurance, a nurse says she must go to a state hospital, until Trevor hands over his credit card, afraid of being pulled back into the family's cycle. The doctor tells him the bullet's path makes her survival a miracle.
- **Why it matters:** the book's climax happens here. Trevor's choice to pay, despite his fear, is the decision to stay tied to his family, and his mother's survival sets up the closing pages.
