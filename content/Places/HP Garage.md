## You're writing lots of content. It's not getting shared. Is it engaging? (Updated October 2020) — Copyhackers (2020)
Source: [Original](https://copyhackers.com/2016/02/how-to-write-engaging-content/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6k3abbbmqde6aarftzhqt)

> They started their company, Hewlett Packard, and built their first product in a garage in Palo Alto, CA, with a budget of only $538. Today that garage is not only the birthplace of one of the largest computer corporations in the world – it’s also known as the birthplace of Silicon Valley.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The author uses Hewlett and Packard's garage as her example of storytelling in content. Two founders with a $538 budget built their first product there, and HP still references the garage story across its websites and marketing.
- **Why it matters:** The garage is the concrete place anchoring the section's argument that narrative humanizes otherwise dry products.
