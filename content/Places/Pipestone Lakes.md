# Pipestone Lakes

Small lakes in the Wind River Range of western Wyoming. The Ryback brothers camp there on a granite tower Tim makes his castle.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs23jmagdvmadqw70hxvpg)

> After a long rest at the lake, which Tim needed by then, we set out again to Pipestone Lake, a mile beyond our planned destination, where we ended the day.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** A mile past their planned stop, the brothers camp on a 30-foot granite tower above the shore. Tim's spirits lift: he charges a flock of sheep shouting "Charge!" and plays a knight, the "Thane of Pipestone", while they eat freeze-dried stew and talk of lamb dinners.
- **Why it matters:** A light interlude that shows Tim recovering after his breakdown at Shannon Pass.
