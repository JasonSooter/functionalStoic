# Holyland (Belfast)

Neighbourhood of south Belfast, Northern Ireland, whose streets carry Holy Land names. A setting in Colum McCann's _Apeirogon_: Belfast streets named Palestine, Jerusalem, Damascus; Bassam walks them at night during a peace conference.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjprwqa03nnkmhxt19sr9y)

> Late one night he walked around what the locals called the Holyland. Bassam was taken by the names of the streets: Palestine, Cairo, Jerusalem, Damascus.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bassam, in Belfast from Bradford for a three-day peace conference, walks the Holyland streets at night, struck by streets named Palestine, Cairo, Jerusalem and Damascus. He sees Palestinian and Israeli flags over rooftops and murals on the Peace Walls, feels a kinship with the city, and then gives his talk and ponders whether it is easier to be Palestinian abroad.
- **Why it matters:** The Holyland mirrors the book's Jerusalem in miniature: street names, flags and walls echo the Separation Wall and the two peoples. It gives Bassam a place where he feels recognised and tempted to stay away.
