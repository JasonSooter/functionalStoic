# Occupy Madison tiny house village

Tiny-house project for homeless people in Madison, Wisconsin, run by Occupy Madison Inc. In a LifeEdited follow-up it is still going strong.

## Follow Up Friday: Tiny House Projects Go Extra Mile — David Friedlander (2015)

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The group bought an abandoned garage as its workshop; three inhabited tiny houses stand on the lot, with a second phase planned to add a kitchen, community room and more houses.
- **Why it matters:** The project is the post's main update on tiny houses for the homeless.
