## We’re a Full-time Traveling Family. This is How We Pay For It. All the Financial Details! — Jessica Gee (2018)

Source: [Original](https://medium.com/@settie4444/we-re-a-full-time-traveling-family-this-is-how-we-pay-for-it-7c1d31c5563e) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvq82hjwg3p6hahjy1yaeg)

> I wasn’t super excited to be moving back to Provo, Utah because I felt like we were going backwards in life.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** The Gees return to Provo, Utah after a few months in Los Angeles so Garrett can finish his senior year of BYU soccer. Jessica dislikes going back to the college town, and Garrett promises it is only for the season, after which she picks the family's next move. The talk of that next move turns into the world trip.
- **Why it matters:** Provo is the place they leave behind; it is the launch point for the travelling life, but gets only a short mention.
