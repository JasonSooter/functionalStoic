- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** With no bike tours that summer, the couple take a trip to Pittsburgh instead: a "haunted Airbnb", long walks, the Vanka murals (spelled "Maxo Vanca" in the text) and the city's alleyways.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7641jjsyzyqmjktmnz8nb2b)

> Some old-timers still remember when Pittsburgh was booming and smoky. Between the smoke from the foundries, the coal dust, and the exhaust from the coal heat in the houses, the sky was often dark at noon.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In "The Return of Pittsburgh" Byrne meets John Chernoff at the Mattress Factory on the North Side, who explains the closed steel mills, the brownfield sites being redeveloped, and the stadium debts left to the poorer residents still in the city. They visit the Vanka murals in [[St Nicholas Croatian Church (Millvale)|Millvale]]; on a later visit Byrne finds the Strip District and the downtown cultural district, renovated with Heinz money, thriving.
- **Why it matters:** Pittsburgh is the book's hopeful case among the declining industrial cities: proof that "it doesn't have to be that way".
