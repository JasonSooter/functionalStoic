## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx3932w60mvfdezg8nqa3q)

> We should sail up the Paraguay River for a hundred miles and then turn east up the Jejui, one of its major tributaries.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On the company launch Cassel they go up the Paraguay River, capsize the speedboat among floating camelote weed, then turn up the winding Jejui. The Captain refuses to go farther, and a night-time logger, Cajo, offers to carry them up the Curuguati tributary instead.
- **Why it matters:** The river route structures the rain-forest leg of the expedition.
