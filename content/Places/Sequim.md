# Sequim

Small town on the northern Olympic Peninsula, Washington. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is where Joe Rantz grows up and is left to fend for himself at fifteen.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m869xyprj7mkesdhd9jgtwqy)

> Sequim sat on a wide expanse of prairie between the snowcapped Olympic Mountains to the south and the broad, blue Strait of Juan de Fuca to the north.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The family arrives in 1925 and lives over Harry's auto repair and tyre shop on Washington Street, the town's main road; Joe enrolls at the Sequim school. The book describes a dry, sunny, neighbourly town of square dances and ice cream socials. After the 1929 crash downtown empties out, and Joe, abandoned on the family farm, sits on the hood of the old Franklin on Washington Street playing his banjo for spare change. In 1931 he boards up the house and leaves for Seattle.
- **Why it matters:** Sequim is the closest Joe comes to a home as a boy, and where he learns to survive on his own; his wish for something more than Sequim can offer pushes him toward the university.
