## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL42416011W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqz0g20v79anc4nbg07rze)

> Beijing did something unprecedented for Tesla in 2018: It allowed the company to fully own its plant in Shanghai.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Tesla's wholly owned Shanghai plant began rolling out cars in 2019; BYD's sales fell 11 percent and profits 42 percent. Chinese business circles called it the 'catfish effect', and Tesla's tooling investments helped rivals; BYD recorded record profits in 2023.
- **Why it matters:** Wang's example of China using a foreign firm to raise domestic competitors and process knowledge. Minor spatially (text only says 'Shanghai Gigafactory'); an added mention at most, possibly in the existing Shanghai note.
