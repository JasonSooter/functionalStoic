# Talitha Kumi

Lutheran school in [[Beit Jala]], West Bank, with a bird-ringing station. Where the ortolans are ringed in _Apeirogon_.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7vtt2xcxh5s4q1e056155)

> The bird-ringing station at the Talitha Kumi school is one of two of its kind in the West Bank: it is part of an environmental center with a natural history museum, a recycling program, a water treatment project, an educational unit, and a botanical garden filled with jasmine, hollyhocks, thistles, Roman nettles and rows of yellow-flowered African rue.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** A ten-acre environmental center at the Talitha Kumi school, looking down on the Wall. Tarek brings the ortolans here to be ringed, and Rami later passes it on his climb through Beit Jala.
- **Why it matters:** A whole fragment is devoted to it, and it anchors the ringing and tagging motif and the image of the tightening lung.
