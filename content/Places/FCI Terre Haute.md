# FCI Terre Haute

Federal prison complex in Terre Haute, [[Indiana]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is where Aldrich Ames, the CIA officer who betrayed Gordievsky, serves his life sentence.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8066nytmz25qe6mdm3ss6se)

> Rick Ames, Prisoner 40087-083, is currently imprisoned at the Federal Correctional Institution in Terre Haute, Indiana.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** The epilogue follows Ames from his 1994 arrest to life imprisonment here. In 1997 Ted Koppel interviews him in prison and plays him Gordievsky's videotaped message calling him "a greedy bastard"; Ames answers with self-justification. Gordievsky, learning that the man he took for a model patriot had tried to have him killed, writes that Ames "did not kill me."
- **Why it matters:** It closes the book's second storyline: the traitor who exposed Gordievsky ends behind bars in Indiana while Gordievsky lives free in England. Minor as a setting, but it is where the Ames thread ends.
