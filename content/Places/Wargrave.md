# Wargrave

Village on the Thames in [[Berkshire]], reached by a backwater short cut above Marsh Lock. In Three Men in a Boat the friends lunch in a meadow here and Harris vanishes with the beefsteak pie.

## Three Men in a Boat — Jerome K. Jerome (1889)
Source: [[Exploration of Three Men in a Boat]] · [[Three Men in a Boat]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1793164W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gkyhe6nc3j7nmt54g6rjqj)

> We went up the backwater to Wargrave.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** They take the backwater short cut to Wargrave, hung with notice-boards, and lunch in a meadow, where Harris sits back and disappears with the beefsteak pie into a hidden gully. The next chapter praises Wargrave itself, 'nestling where the river bends' and its George and Dragon inn with a sign painted by two Academicians.
- **Why it matters:** Another comic set piece tied to a specific spot on the river, followed by one of the warmest village portraits in the book.
