## How to Raise a Genius: Lessons from a 45-Year Study of Supersmart Children — Tom Clynes (2016)

Source: [Original](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/how-to-raise-a-genius-lessons-from-a-45-year-study-of-supersmart-children/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy7qmccpnbc6hefrs9t2t1)

> On a muggy August day, Benbow and her husband, psychologist David Lubinski, describe the origins of SMPY as they walk across the quadrangle at Vanderbilt University.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The reporter walks the quadrangle with Benbow and Lubinski as they recount SMPY's origins, passing the 1915 psychology laboratory, the first US building for the field. At noon the clock strikes and packs of gifted adolescents from the Programs for Talented Youth race to the dining hall, closing the piece.
- **Why it matters:** Vanderbilt frames the article as a present-day scene and is where SMPY returned in 1998, bringing Stanley's research 'full circle'.
