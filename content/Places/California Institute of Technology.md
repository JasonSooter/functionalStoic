# California Institute of Technology

Research university in Pasadena, California. In _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_ it is where Feynman decides, on a walk across campus, to stay for the rest of his career.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfqp8dcqdq6bgtvqr6srms)

> I didn’t even make it across the campus to get to my office, when another guy came up—Matt Meselson, a biologist who had minored in physics.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Feynman moves from Cornell to Caltech, though his first year is spent in Brazil. After a smog attack he phones Cornell about going back, but the next day he is stopped on the way to his office first by news of Baade's discovery that the universe is older than thought, then by Matt Meselson with his density-gradient result on DNA. He decides he has to stay. Caltech is also where he spends a summer in the biology lab and where his drawings are later shown.
- **Why it matters:** The campus is where his career settles; Pasadena becomes the base for most of the book's later stories.
