# La Esquina (Barrio Logan)

Small live/work building in the Barrio Logan neighbourhood of [[San Diego]], held up by LifeEdited as dorm-style living for adults.

## Dorm Living for Grown Ups — David Friedlander (2015)
Source: [Original](http://www.lifeedited.com/dorm-living-for-grown-ups/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hk4v79ntt5hv08rhmr7xbm)

> of Woodbury University pooled together several faculty members to purchase lots of land in the Barrio Logan neighborhood in San Diego.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Woodbury University faculty bought land in Barrio Logan for a campus extension; when that plan was derailed they built La Esquina instead: eight two-level live/work units of 450–595 sq ft with patios and shared terraces, at about $130 a square foot, let to Woodbury students, graduates and others.
- **Why it matters:** The building is the article's whole example of keeping a dorm's sociability in small adult homes.
