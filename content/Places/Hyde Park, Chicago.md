## How One Agency Drove 200% More Sales with Their Facebook Ads — Stephanie Saretsky (2015)
Source: [Original](http://unbounce.com/ppc/agency-drove-more-sales-with-facebook-ads/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gm1732pkda3m4ms4ge8faq)

> Hyde Park is their newest store, so we launched a campaign specifically for Hyde Park residents — specifically with a Hyde Park landing page.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The agency Matchnode builds a campaign just for residents around New Balance Chicago's newest store in Hyde Park, with its own Hyde Park landing page, and the cost per email conversion falls by 25 percent.
- **Why it matters:** Minor but concrete: one neighbourhood store as the target of hyper-local ads.
