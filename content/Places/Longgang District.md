## China’s AI frenzy: DeepSeek is already everywhere – cars, phones, even hospitals — Khadija Alam (2025)
Source: [Original](https://restofworld.org/2025/china-embeds-deepseek-ai-in-everything/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2yqgn8f207gg6s38qa4cm)

> Shenzhen’s Longgang county reported “great improvement in efficiency” after adopting DeepSeek in a system used by 20,000 government workers.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Shenzhen was one of the first to use DeepSeek in internal government systems. Longgang reported a 95% accuracy rate for documents DeepSeek wrote and a 90% cut in administrative approval time.
- **Why it matters:** It is the article's main example of government adoption.
