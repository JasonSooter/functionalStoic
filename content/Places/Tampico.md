# Tampico

Gulf port city in Tamaulipas, [[Mexico]]. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it is where Frida meets Leon and Natalia Trotsky off the tanker _Ruth_ in January 1937.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)

Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m737jb0hq384kj0de4k2raer)

> arrived in the harbor of Tampico on the morning of January 9, 1937.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** In Chapter 13 the tanker _Ruth_ brings the Trotskys into the harbour; wary of assassins, they refuse to land until they see friends' faces. A government cutter brings a welcoming party that includes Max Shachtman, George Novack, officials, journalists and Frida, representing the hospitalized Diego, and they walk together down the wooden pier.
- **Why it matters:** It is Trotsky's arrival in Mexico and Frida's first meeting with him, the start of a chapter that leads to their affair.
