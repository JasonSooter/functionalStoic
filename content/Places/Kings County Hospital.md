- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After a window shatters, the children are taken by ambulance to Kings County; August remembers curtained wards, a nurse giving her cookies, and wondering where her brother is.
- **Why it matters:** A brief, vivid scene of injury and fear; minor beyond that episode.
