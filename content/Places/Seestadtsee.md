# Seestadtsee

Seestadtsee is the artificial lake at the centre of Aspern Seestadt in Vienna. It turns up in Can Urban Design Have a Gender? In This Vienna District, the Answer Is Yes. - The New York Times (Valeriya Safronova): It is the district's public heart and shows the 'something for everyone' open-space idea, though the text treats it briefly; moderate rather than major.

## Can Urban Design Have a Gender? In This Vienna District, the Answer Is Yes. - The New York Times — Valeriya Safronova (2024)

Source: [[Bookshelf/Highlights & Notes/Articles/Can Urban Design Have a Gender In This Vienna District, the Answer Is Yes. - The New York Times.md|Can Urban Design Have a Gender?]] · [Original](https://www.nytimes.com/2024/07/17/realestate/vienna-aspern-seestadt-gender.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbj2ytwkvhzb3t4fz1dxf7)

> The centerpiece of Aspern Seestadt is its 12-acre artificial lake with its surrounding park. The space is open to the public.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** A photo caption calls the 12-acre artificial lake and surrounding park the centerpiece of the district; another says waterfront apartments line the central park and lake. The body text says it is even closer than the services hub to the subway station.
- **Why it matters:** It is the district's public heart and shows the 'something for everyone' open-space idea, though the text treats it briefly; moderate rather than major.
