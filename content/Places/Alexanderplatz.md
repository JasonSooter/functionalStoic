> Alexanderplatz, a pedestrianised square in Berlin.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the video:** Shown as proof that trams fit into pedestrianised squares better than any other surface transport, with a Jason Bourne joke over the footage. The video ties it to the argument that East Berlin kept and expanded its trams while West Berlin ripped them out.
- **Why it matters:** A visual example rather than a setting.
