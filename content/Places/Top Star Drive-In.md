# Top Star Drive-In

Former drive-in cinema on a mine dump just south of central Johannesburg. In Born a Crime it is one of the places Trevor Noah's mother takes him that black families never went.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6yq48y014gaj09bhazma6gs)

> Johannesburg used to have this epic drive-in movie theater, Top Star Drive-In, on top of a massive mine dump outside the city.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Chapter 5. As apartheid loosens, his mother takes Trevor to places black people didn't go, like ice rinks, parks and the drive-in. At Top Star they hang the speaker on the car window and look out over "the city, the suburbs, Soweto". He feels "on top of the world".
- **Why it matters:** it is the book's image of his mother raising him "as if there were no limitations on where I could go or what I could do". The view takes in, all at once, the divided city they are refusing to be confined by.
- **Real vs. fictional:** real drive-in, now gone. The pin is the suburb of Park Central, built on the mine dumps where the drive-in stood.
