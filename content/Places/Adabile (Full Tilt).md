# Adabile (Full Tilt)

Town Dervla Murphy calls 'Adabile' in Iranian Azerbaijan, between Tabriz and the Caspian; most likely Ardabil. The scene of an assault she escapes in _Full Tilt_.

## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)
Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6kmjf2ya2r7ggfw2x8xh7)

> Passing through Adabile at lunchtime on a cold day (we had now risen to a considerable altitude) I paused to treat myself to a hot meal in an eating-house. As usual Roz and I attracted a curious crowd and soon a young police officer, gorgeously uniformed and braided, approached me to say that as this was a Restricted Area, because of Russia’s proximity, I must accompany him to the police-barracks to fill in some forms.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In a Restricted Area a young police officer lures her to an empty house and locks the door. She escapes after fending him off and flees to the town centre.
- **Why it matters:** The reason Azerbaijan is the one region she would not revisit alone.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real place, named in the book by a name that is not on modern maps.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The text never gives a real name that matches a map. Its clues: the stretch is 'between Tabriz and the Caspian coast', the town lies in a 'Restricted Area, because of Russia's proximity', and the road has 'risen to a considerable altitude'. One web search found no reference work that identifies 'Adabile'. Ardabil, the main town on the high road from Tabriz to the Caspian near the old Soviet border, fits all three clues and the name is close, so the pin is there (inferred). Alternative: a smaller town on the same road, such as Sarab.
