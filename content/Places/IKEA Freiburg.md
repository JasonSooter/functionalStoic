## Drei LastenVelos bei IKEA — Robert
Source: [Original](https://www.lastenvelofreiburg.de/drei-lastenvelos-bei-ikea/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m68tjybressjn6hj80m6mc2x)

> Zwei der Räder sind immer zu den Öffnungszeiten von IKEA für IKEA reserviert.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** LastenVelo Freiburg adds three cargo bikes at IKEA. Bikes 10 and 11 can be borrowed free through IKEA for up to four hours while the store is open and by anyone when it is closed; bike 12 is for LastenVelo users only. The post admits the site is "etwas weit draußen", but the bike has a motor and IKEA swaps its battery.
- **Why it matters:** The store is the station and the partner; the post is about how sharing works at this one location. Minor.
