# Kamarang

Settlement on the Mazaruni River in western Guyana; the rain-forest base of the Guiana Zoo Quest in _Life on Air_.

## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx28ghmkyb29a38spc85rf)

> Our main work, however, was in the rain forest. For that we flew up to Kamarang in the north-west of the country.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** A District Officer's station beside a long straight stretch of the Mazaruni River is the base for three weeks. The team films leaf-cutter ants and three-toed sloths (one gives birth), and travels in outboard canoes to Akawaio villages, filming woodskin canoe making, cassava processing and a cliff of ochre paintings.
- **Why it matters:** The main rain-forest location of the chapter, where most of the filming happens.
