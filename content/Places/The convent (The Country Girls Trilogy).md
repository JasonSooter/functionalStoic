# The convent (The Country Girls Trilogy)

The convent boarding school in an unnamed lake-side town where Caithleen and Baba are sent as boarders; pinned at St Raphael's College, the Mercy convent school in Loughrea, County Galway, which Edna O'Brien attended. In _The Country Girls Trilogy_ it is where the girls spend a hungry, lonely year before getting themselves expelled.

## The Country Girls Trilogy — Edna O'Brien (1986)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL69252W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75m6cg618v7nx59wex09n)

> The convent was a gray stone building with hundreds of small square curtainless windows, like so many eyes spying out on the wet sinful town.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In The Country Girls the two girls arrive on a wet evening through the town past 'a dark sheet of water' and are shown the refectory, chapel and open dormitory by Sister Margaret. They endure bad cabbage, modesty lectures and loneliness, befriend Cynthia, and Caithleen is punished after a retreat for pinning a notice on the wrong door. Desperate to leave, Baba decides 'We can always get expelled', and the note they plant does it. The head nun later sends Kate prayers and medals in Wicklow.
- **Why it matters:** The convent gives the trilogy its first picture of institutional Catholic control, and the expulsion is what frees the girls for Dublin. It is a hinge in the plot rather than just a backdrop.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The text names neither the town nor the order. Clues: 'We got into the convent town at dusk; just outside it there was a lake, a dark sheet of water'; the convent has 'green railings around it and high green gates that led to a dark cypress avenue'; it is a long car drive from the village. Reference works (Wikipedia on O'Brien, Clare Libraries) say O'Brien boarded from 1941 to 1946 at St Raphael's College, a Sisters of Mercy school in Loughrea, Co. Galway, and Loughrea stands on its own lake. No source I found formally names it as the model. Chosen: St Raphael's College, Loughrea. Alternative: some other convent town in the region.
