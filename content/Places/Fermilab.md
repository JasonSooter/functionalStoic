# Fermilab

US national particle-physics laboratory at Batavia, [[Illinois]]. It is the source end of the neutrino beam in a B1M video on the project that fires it 1,286 km to a detector in South Dakota.

## Why America is Hollowing Out a Mountain — The B1M (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8d1ugv8vQ4w) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqy2qnsq5vsnerzv7nqwjp)

> the experiment starts here at the Fermilab campus just outside of Illinois

- **Role:** setting
- **In the video:** The experiment starts at the Fermilab campus where the PIP II accelerator will fire protons into a target to make a neutrino beam angled down through a grassy mound. A near detector sits about 600 m away, and the beam travels 1,286 km to South Dakota.
- **Why it matters:** It is the source end of the neutrino beam and the home of the project's brain trust.
