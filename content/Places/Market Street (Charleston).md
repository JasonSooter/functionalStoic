> Five blocks west of this garage can be found another essay in urban parking. This newer, bigger structure sits twenty-five feet back from Market Street.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** A second, bigger garage sits 25 feet back from Market Street, five blocks west of the East Bay Street one.
