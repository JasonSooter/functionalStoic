# Greenwood Elementary School (Des Moines)

Public elementary school off Grand Avenue in Des Moines, built in 1901. In _The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid_ it is Bryson's school, where he is exiled to the cloakroom during a civil-defence drill.

## The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid — Bill Bryson (2006)

Source: [[The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4620956W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5khtnd56k6qathekhgx678t)

> GREENWOOD, MY ELEMENTARY SCHOOL, was a wonderful old building, enormous to a small child, like a castle made of brick. Built in 1901, it stood off Grand Avenue at the far end of a street of outstandingly vast and elegant homes.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bryson describes the overheated old building with its auditorium, upstairs gym and chaotic cloakroom, his poor attendance and Dick and Jane readers. When a military man runs a civil-defence drill, he refuses to take part and is sent to the cloakroom, where he spends much of the rest of his elementary years. His paper route later runs through the mansions around the school.
- **Why it matters:** The main setting of the school chapter, where his dreaminess and defiance play out.
