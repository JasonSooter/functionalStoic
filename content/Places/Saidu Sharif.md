## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)
Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6ms63h693r8zf3rxxkfdh)

> The little ‘capital’ town of Saidu Shariff (altitude 3,150 feet), with the Waliahad on a slight rise above it, looks north towards the snow peaks and gives me the same feeling of carefree isolation as Andorra out of the tourist season.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** She arrives soaked after the Malakand Pass and, asking for Aurang Zeb's house, discovers it is the Wali of Swat's palace. She is taken in by Prince Aurang Zeb and his wife Naseem, President Ayub's daughter, lent trousers, and given a tour of Swat by his brother, learning its history and visiting Buddhist excavations and a leper colony.
- **Why it matters:** The social high point of the Pakistan section, an episode she calls one of the classic ones of the expedition, and the source of her fondest impressions of Pathan graciousness.
