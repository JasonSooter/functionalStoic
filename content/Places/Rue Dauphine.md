## I’ll never see that smile again — Shaun Usher (2025)

Source: [Original](https://news.lettersofnote.com/p/ill-never-see-that-smile-again) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edf8pk68xjk02ys6gkxynj)

> It was three years after that triumph that tragedy struck: as he crossed a Paris street one rainy morning, Pierre slipped beneath a horse-drawn carriage and was killed instantly.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** In 1906 Pierre Curie slips under a horse-drawn carriage while crossing a Paris street and dies on the spot. The text does not name which street.
- **Why it matters:** It is the event the letter mourns, but only briefly in the framing; the piece is about the grief after it.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The letter's framing names only "a Paris street"; Pierre Curie was killed crossing Rue Dauphine near the Quai des Grands-Augustins on 19 April 1906 (Wikipedia, Pierre Curie). ⚠️ The street is named from that reference, not from the text.
