# Novaya Zemlya

Arctic archipelago between the Barents and Kara Seas, Russia. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ it is where Willem Barents and Jacob van Heemskerck winter in the ice in 1596–97, searching for a north-east route to the Spice Islands.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6hpq2edg3rr45dphj5h7wyv)

> Trapped in ice somewhere to the north of Novaya Zemlya, the two men were convinced that their experience of Arctic climes would enable them to survive the winter.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The third Dutch attempt on the North-East Passage sails in 1596. Trapped in the ice, the crew builds a shelter of logs and driftwood, "so well constructed that it was still standing three centuries later", and hibernates for eight months. In January they crown the ship's constable "King of Novaya Zemlya"; in February they shoot a polar bear. When the ice thaws in June the ship is crushed, and the survivors build two small craft to escape.
- **Why it matters:** It shows how far the Dutch and English would go to reach the spices by a northern route, and it introduces Heemskerck, who later sails to the Banda Islands.
