## In Zion National Park, ‘the Smog Is Gone’ — Morgan Sjogren (2025)
Source: [Original](https://reasonstobecheerful.world/zion-electric-shuttle-buses/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqwvcxppt3c1naq8shkkf2)

> Beneath red temples of Navajo sandstone, the Virgin River winds through Zion National Park.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article describes Zion's shuttle system, launched in 2000 for 2.4 million annual visitors (now five million), which went all-electric with a fleet of 30 buses in 2024. Before shuttles, cars parked along the road, denuding vegetation and driving animals away; transportation manager Lisa White recalls fistfights over parking spaces. Shuttles immediately relieved congestion.
- **Why it matters:** Zion is the subject: a case study in managing overcrowding in a national park with electric transit.
