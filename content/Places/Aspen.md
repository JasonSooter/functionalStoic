## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)
Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbktrqwkp865mev1ggvzxv)

> Shoup reports on how, by 1990, Aspen’s downtown merchants were suffering from overcrowded curbside parking. The city responded by building an expensive parking garage, but that structure sat half empty as the parking crush continued.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Aspen proposed $1 an hour on-street parking in 1990 amid a 'Honk if You Hate Paid Parking' campaign. Paid parking took effect in 1995, the garage filled, and the city now earns over half a million dollars a year, mostly from tourists.
- **Why it matters:** A short success case for raising meter rates. It also appears among the few successful pedestrian malls.
