## High Fidelity — Basecamp (2015)
Source: [Original](https://thedistance.com/grado-labs/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g8vrvqec1f12qgzg19a0xd)

> In 1955, he moved the operations of his fledging company into his Italian immigrant father’s fruit store in Sunset Park. The fruit store is long gone, but Grado Labs is still there.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Joseph Grado moved his cartridge business into his father's fruit store in 1955; the building is still the factory and was the family home. Cartridges are built in the cramped basement, headphones on the upper floors, and John Grado tunes headphones in the top-floor listening room.
- **Why it matters:** The building is the centre of the story; John calls it a lucky rabbit's foot, and it ties the company to the neighbourhood.
