## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)
Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6mz384g4929f2xjnxbg5g)

> I got to the airport at 1.30 p.m. and after the six-mile cycle run out from the city I needed to drink every drop in my six-pint water-bottle.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** She flies from Pindi in a Dakota through a narrow gorge among the Himalayan peaks, and is met by the Gilgit Scouts and Colonel Shah, who tries to dissuade her from the Babusar Pass. She climbs the mountain above the town, finds a corpse, and finds the townspeople surly. She finally leaves at dawn with Roz for the valley trek.
- **Why it matters:** The gateway to the Karakoram trek, which becomes the book's high-adventure section, and where she must decide to leave Roz behind.
