# Great Divide Basin

Endorheic basin in south-central Wyoming where the Continental Divide splits in two. The brothers' near-fatal desert crossing in The Ultimate Journey.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs27wc44nsw4vev4xhac8d)

> Tim stood atop the ridge, his arms thrust skyward before the wasteland of the Great Divide Basin, which stretched to the horizon.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In 98-degree heat the road dead-ends and the jeep trail leads them west instead of south. They run out of water and Tim collapses. At a locked ranch house they find water beneath kerosene in a barrel, and they finally stagger out at Highway 80, drinking from a roadside café's faucet.
- **Why it matters:** The book's most dangerous episode; it breaks Tim physically and sets up his decision to stop in Colorado.
