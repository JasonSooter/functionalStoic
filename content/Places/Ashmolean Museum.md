# Ashmolean Museum

University museum of art and archaeology in [[Oxford]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ it is where the last stuffed dodo was thrown on a bonfire in 1755.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m83dbvsrq8ybgatk3m5hak0x)

> the director of the Ashmolean Museum in Oxford decided that the institution

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Some seventy years after the dodo's extinction, the museum's director decided its stuffed specimen was becoming unpleasantly musty and ordered it burned. It was by then the only dodo in existence, stuffed or otherwise; a passing employee managed to save only its head and part of one limb.
- **Why it matters:** It explains why no one knows what a living dodo was really like, and it is Bryson's first example of how carelessly we treat even the remains of lost species.
