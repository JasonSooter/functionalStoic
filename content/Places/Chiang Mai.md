## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL42416011W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e30qh07n9sjajkrpk47a0p)

> At the end of 2023, I spent a month in Thailand’s Chiang Mai with people who have

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The author spends a month with young Chinese who left after lockdowns, many met earlier in Yunnan. They meditate in golden-roofed temples, use psychedelics and cannabis, hide the truth from parents, and meet software developer Yiju who fled after the white paper protests.
- **Why it matters:** It is the destination of the 'rùn' emigration theme, a place of escape from Chinese controls; the section's reporting setting.

## Is Private School Worth It? — Retire by 40 (2015)
Source: [Original](https://retireby40.org/is-private-school-worth-it/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hj1szbc6y5pakx28spft8n)

> I grew up in Chiang Mai, Thailand and went to school there until I finished 7th grade.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author grew up in Chiang Mai and attended Montfort, one of the few private primary schools there, which were then better than the local public schools. His parents made sacrifices to pay for it before the family moved to the US.
- **Why it matters:** His own Chiang Mai schooling is the background against which he weighs private school for his son in Portland.
