## Why Are Finland’s Schools Successful? — LynNell Hancock (2011)
Source: [Original](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/why-are-finlands-schools-successful-49859555/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8y94g426d9h76cbpp7b9e)

> To get a second sampling, I headed east from Espoo to Helsinki and a rough neighborhood called Siilitie, Finnish for “Hedgehog Road” and known for having the oldest low-income housing project in Finland.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author visits this school in a low-income Helsinki neighborhood, where half the students have learning disabilities, to test whether Kirkkojarvi was a fluke. She watches outdoor math lessons among the pines and birches and teachers combining classes, and learns the community rose up to keep the school from closing.
- **Why it matters:** Siilitie tests whether the Espoo example holds in a poorer neighbourhood, and the article finds it does.
