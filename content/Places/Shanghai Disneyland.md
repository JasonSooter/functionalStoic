## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL42416011W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e30f3s34zjrw5cx970bws4)

> visitors to Shanghai Disneyland were told that they could no longer depart from the happiest place on earth because a close contact of a confirmed case had passed through it.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Thirty thousand visitors were trapped for much of a day in 2022 until all tested negative, with rides still running. Later the author imagines a drama about a couple stuck there.
- **Why it matters:** A short but memorable example of surprise lockdowns of public space; minor in scale.
