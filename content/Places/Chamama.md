# Chamama

Village and trading centre in Kasungu District, central Malawi, about fifteen kilometres from [[Wimbe]]. In _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ its government maize depot is where the young Kamkwamba queues for food at the height of the famine.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8e115xbn76krrq9105kmvqd)

> The ADMARC was located in the center of Chamama’s trading center, along a gravel road amid a row of white, one-story storefronts.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** When word comes that a lorry of maize is headed for the Chamama ADMARC, William's mother sends him the fifteen kilometres there before dawn. He waits in a huge, hungry queue and gets through a crush at the door. Inside he may buy only twenty kilograms, and the man at the scale shorts him. Behind him the crowd learns the bags are running out, and "The riot I’d just escaped now exploded into one giant fight."
- **Why it matters:** This is the famine at its most physical: the state depot that should feed the district becomes a scene of trampling and cheating. It shows why the family's survival hangs on a few kilos of maize, and it is the low point the windmill story climbs out of.
