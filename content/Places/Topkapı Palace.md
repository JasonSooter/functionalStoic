# Topkapı Palace

The Ottoman sultans' palace on Seraglio Point, [[Istanbul]], now a museum. David Byrne rides there in _Bicycle Diaries_ to see the harem and is struck by its room of holy relics.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76424p2pbh3c9p0pdgn90cf)

> The next day I ride over to the beautiful Topkapi Palace, a tourist attraction, to see its harem museum.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Byrne admires the scrollwork but is more taken by the relics grouped in one museum room: a hair of the Prophet, Muhammad's sandal print, the arm bone of St. John the Baptist and other bones.
- **Why it matters:** The display becomes his evidence of how thoroughly Atatürk secularised the country.
