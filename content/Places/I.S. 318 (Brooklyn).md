## Why Character Can’t Be Taught Like The Pythagorean Theorem — Paul Tough (2016)

Source: [Original](http://ww2.kqed.org/mindshift/2016/06/09/why-character-cant-be-taught-like-the-pythagorean-theorem/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvra45xw4fzvt7reknvxsr)

> She teaches chess at Intermediate School 318, a traditional, non-magnet public school in Brooklyn that enrolls mostly low-income students of color.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Elizabeth Spiegel teaches chess here and built a national-championship team without ever naming character traits; students acquired persistence and resilience through close analysis of their games.
- **Why it matters:** Main example for the argument that environment, not lessons, builds character.
