## Get Rich With: Your Own Urban Tribe — Mr. Money Mustache (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.mrmoneymustache.com/2015/08/19/urban-tribe/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gbghf571ppqnz1r1c5qynk)

> This is an island city of 80,000 people which happens to feature the highest rate of bicycle commuting in Canada.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The author spends three days in Victoria on a borrowed bike, riding to a barbecue and out to nearby lakes and mountains, joining a reader meetup in a public park and walking home at midnight. He contrasts friends there, living close together, with friends scattered across Ottawa's suburbs.
- **Why it matters:** The city is the post's main example of how a compact, bikeable place makes a social life cheap and easy.
