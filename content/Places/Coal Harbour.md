# Coal Harbour

Harbour beside Stanley Park in downtown [[Vancouver]], British Columbia. In _The Boys in the Boat_ George and Dick Pocock build their first racing shells there in a leaky floating shed, where Hiram Conibear finds them.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m869y6hexf8ytr3hdwcjz4np)

> The Pococks set up shop in an old, derelict shed floating on timbers fifty yards offshore in Coal Harbour and then finally resumed what would be their life’s work—crafting fine racing shells.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The brothers work in the shop downstairs and sleep in an unheated room above it. Daylight shows through the roof, they bathe by diving out of the window into the harbour, row to Stanley Park for drinking water, and at times the shed slips its anchor and drifts among ocean liners. Hiram Conibear, Washington's coach, tracks them down here and asks them to move to Seattle and build a fleet of eight-oared shells on campus.
- **Why it matters:** It is where Pocock's shell-building career in North America begins, and Conibear's visit brings him to Washington, where his boats and advice will shape the crew.
