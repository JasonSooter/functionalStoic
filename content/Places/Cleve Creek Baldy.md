# Cleve Creek Baldy

Roughly 11,000-foot peak in White Pine County, eastern Nevada. In The Comfort Crisis (Michael Easter) it is where Donnie Vincent explains how to prepare for a hunt.

## The Comfort Crisis — Michael Easter (2021)

Source: [[The Comfort Crisis]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/634446/the-comfort-crisis-by-michael-easter/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fyczpx8bchfc1e9az9s8c6)

> “Let’s say you wanted to start hunting tomorrow,” he said as we were hiking to the peak of Cleve Creek Baldy, a roughly 11,000-foot vantage point where we planned to glass for elk.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Recalled in chapter 7: on the Nevada trip, while climbing Cleve Creek Baldy to glass for elk, Donnie tells Easter that hunting means preparing for a specific animal in a specific place at a specific time.
- **Why it matters:** A framing scene for Easter's six months of preparation for Alaska; the peak itself matters less than the advice given on the way up.
