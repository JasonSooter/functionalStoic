## Which path would the three wise men have walked? — xkcd's What If? (2026)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/d5TD3r1nLqM) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bs75fdz0q5pj5k1znfag8s)

> their path actually takes them through Bethlehem.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Starting from Jerusalem and walking towards a star, the wise men wander off in most versions; in the night-walking version the computed path actually passes through Bethlehem before wandering on and eventually circling Botswana.
- **Why it matters:** The joke's payoff: the arithmetic briefly agrees with the Christmas story.
