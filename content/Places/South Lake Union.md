## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)

Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbm5n5jkzpw2fyjwp9sghf)

> This was precisely the case in Seattle, where landowners in the city’s South Lake Union district, led by Microsoft cofounder Paul Allen, contributed fully half the cost of a new \$52 million trolley line connecting their property to downtown.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Landowners led by Paul Allen paid half of the new trolley connecting the district to downtown, with federal, state and city property sales covering the rest. Amazon and the Gates Foundation relocated there and property values along the line rose at twice the city average.
- **Why it matters:** Example of private beneficiaries funding a streetcar; real-estate demand rather than transit alone is what makes it work.
