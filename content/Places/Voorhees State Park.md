# Voorhees State Park

State park in Hunterdon County, [[New Jersey]], with a public observatory built by amateur astronomers; it is the stargazing destination of a cycling blog post on the Columbia Trail.

## Biking and stargazing (if skies are clear) along the Columbia Trail — alliumstozinnias (2024)
Source: [Original](https://exploringbybike.com/2024/09/02/biking-and-stargazing-if-skies-are-clear-along-the-columbia-trail/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f1vjm9t6bs3evw2gtt413n)

> The observatory is open to the public on Saturday nights and Sunday afternoons from June through October (plus the fourth Saturday night in March, April and May).

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** After the ride the author drives up a 12% grade to the park on a cloudy night. The post tells how amateur astronomers built the observatory from 1965 on land leased for $1 a year, around a 26-inch telescope, and opened the dome anyway.
- **Why it matters:** It is the destination and the story the post tells.
