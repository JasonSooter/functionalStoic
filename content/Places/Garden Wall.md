# Garden Wall

Knife-edge arête along the Continental Divide in Glacier National Park, Montana. In Ryback's Divide memoir it is where Eric breaks through the ice and the first route south fails.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8berfgfcqmp3dy9nze476ae)

> "See that rock face?" I asked, pointing into the misty distance, "that's the Garden Wall.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On the second day Eric points out the Garden Wall through the mist as the way south. Crossing the snow toward it, he breaks through the ice and hangs over a sub-glacier stream, his pack wedged in the opening, until Tim pulls him out by the pack. The attempt is abandoned and the brothers retreat south down the Waterton Lake valley.
- **Why it matters:** It is the first real danger of the trek and the first of several failed attempts to get through Glacier's high country, which forces the route-finding that shapes the opening chapter.
