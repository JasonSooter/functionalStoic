## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6ndrsrk65tyajzs94npc7)

> The landscape is flatter than Belgium, even more overpopulated and enlivened only by thousands of cattle, the majority at a most gruesome stage of emaciation.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In a dak bungalow she is mistaken for a corpse, and from there she reflects at length on the dull Punjab, cow worship, Hinduism and caste, Indian attitudes to Britain, ruined mosques left from Partition, and the Grand Trunk Road. She resolves to cycle by night.
- **Why it matters:** Source of her first sustained judgements on India and the decision to ride by night.
