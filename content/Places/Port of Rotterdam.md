# Port of Rotterdam

Europe's largest seaport, stretching from [[Rotterdam]] to the Maasvlakte on the North Sea. Priceonomics holds it up as the automated port that Oakland is not.

## Why Aren’t America’s Shipping Ports Automated? — Priceonomics (2015)

Source: [Original](http://priceonomics.com/why-arent-americas-shipping-ports-automated/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gm1bvx4qa8353c8yfx9p5e)

> Rotterdam began implementing port automation back in the 1990s.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Rotterdam is the foil to Oakland: it began automating in the 1990s, software runs its stacking cranes and transport vehicles, and at the newest terminal office workers control the gantry cranes remotely. The article cites a build cost of over \$535 million and compares cranes and container throughput with Oakland.
- **Why it matters:** It is the benchmark for what an automated port looks like.
