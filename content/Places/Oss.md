# Oss

Town in North Brabant, in the south of the [[Netherlands]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where Frans goes to school as a boy and where, after the war, he hides for ten weeks with a classmate's mother.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85n9r12j96v5ps2xjhew520)

> Frans stayed with Martha in Oss for no less than ten weeks.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** At the end of June 1945, after another argument with Wera, Frans walks and hitchhikes from Voorburg to Oss to find Robert, a classmate from the [[Saint Nicholas Institute (Oss)|Saint Nicholas Institute]]. Robert's widowed mother Martha takes him in and admits that her son, like many Catholic boys from the area, joined the SS and went east. Frans stays ten weeks, sleeping by day and telling his story each night over her late husband's jenever, until the searches for collaborators make it unsafe and she has him smuggled into Belgium.
- **Why it matters:** Oss gives Frans the listener his own family refuses him; the town of his first exile becomes his refuge, and the start of his flight to Belgium.
