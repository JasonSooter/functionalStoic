## Rick Steves' Iran (2009) — Rick Steves' Europe (2009)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CYoa9hI3CXg) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqxbjhhrcna3ezmgsrzrtg)

> The Imam Mosque, one of the holiest in Iran, is both huge and beautiful, with the elaborate decoration typical of Persian mosques.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the video:** Steves admires the tilework and the dome's acoustics, which the cantor demonstrates. He then attends Friday prayers among thousands and notes the separate women's section, the soldiers standing guard, and a banner reading Death to Israel.
- **Why it matters:** It is where the film confronts the mix of politics and religion, which leads into the Sunni and Shiite discussion with his guide.
