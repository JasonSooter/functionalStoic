# Ashland Avenue and Addison Street (Chicago)

Street corner on Chicago's North Side where the florist Ashland Addison began. In a Basecamp story it is where Roy Sheffield sold his first bouquets in 1932.

## Special Arrangements — Basecamp (2015)

Source: [Original](https://thedistance.com/ashland-addison-florist-co/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g16nnytp5asgcsndpqgk6w)

> When Roy Sheffield opened Ashland Addison in 1932, he was face to face with his customers every day, selling bouquets at the corner of Ashland Avenue and Addison Street in Chicago.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Sheffield sells flowers on the corner during the Depression, flowers growers paid him with, and names the business after it before moving to a storefront on Ashland.
- **Why it matters:** The corner is the florist's origin and gives it its name.
