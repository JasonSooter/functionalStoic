## ‘Call me a racist, but don’t say I’m a Buddhist’: meet America's alt right — Sanjiv Bhattacharya (2016)
Source: [Original](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2016/oct/09/call-me-a-racist-but-dont-say-im-a-buddhist-meet-the-alt-right) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy9903ka9e4wssjpn1xv95)

> But it was his two-year mission to Tohoku, Japan, that turned him.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Johnson's Mormon mission going door to door in Tohoku, where locals praised white America and treated missionaries like celebrities, shaped his racial views. Later working in Japan as an attorney he wrote a book advocating repatriation of non-whites.
- **Why it matters:** Explains the origin of Johnson's white nationalism and the odd Japan connection the article remarks on.
