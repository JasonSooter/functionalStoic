# Two Medicine Pass

Windswept pass above the Two Medicine valley in southern Glacier National Park, Montana. For the Ryback brothers it is a moment of renewal after days of failure.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8berjygcfq4eyx2dhrgm46e)

> The path abruptly leveled off and we found ourselves at the pass, a long ridge stripped of all but a thin carpeting of lichen, moss and tiny inch-high plants by the strong and unrelenting wind.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Exhausted and dispirited, the brothers camp at Two Medicine Lakes, planning to hike to Two Medicine Pass in the morning. Beyond Rockwell Falls only a goats' path climbs to the pass, a long wind-stripped ridge carpeted in lichen and moss, where Eric feels the mountains' "strange power". That night a mountain goat drifts past their camp in the mist, which he takes as an omen of "strength and triumph for our southward trek."
- **Why it matters:** It is the emotional turn of the Glacier chapter: after three failed attempts, the trek finds its footing here.
