## Why Are so Many of the World's Oldest Businesses in Japan? — Priceonomics (2015)
Source: [Original](http://priceonomics.com/why-are-so-many-of-the-worlds-oldest-businesses-in/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g8vb7pe8921ced92q6d2cn)

> Perhaps the greatest example of this is the backstory of Hoshi, a Japanese inn founded in Komatsu in 718.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article closes with the inn's backstory: a monk told in a dream by the god of Mount Haku to open a hot spring, and a family said to have run it for 46 generations, most of the heirs adopted as adults.
- **Why it matters:** It is the article's culminating example of how adult adoption keeps Japan's oldest businesses in the family.
