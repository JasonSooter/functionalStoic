# Kidron Valley

Valley running from Jerusalem through the Judaean Desert to the Dead Sea. A turning point in Colum McCann's _Apeirogon_: Wadi and hills south-east of Bethlehem where Dalia records sounds and disappears.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjr70mj4e3tpepfyzw3vpk)

> Another shoe was found almost two miles further down the Kidron Valley. It was impossible to determine what had happened to her body in the rushing water.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Dalia el-Fahum cycles from Bethlehem into the Kidron Valley hills in 2008 to record natural sounds, is stopped by patrols, and later vanishes there; her bicycle parts and shoes are found along a wadi and her body is never found.
- **Why it matters:** The valley is the landscape of her recording project and her disappearance, and the walls of the valley are also described as hiding places for snipers and archers.
