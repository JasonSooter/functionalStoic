## When adventure happens: Things don’t go as planned on Crestone Peak — proactiveoutside1 (2016)
Source: [Original](https://proactiveoutside.wordpress.com/2016/08/16/when-adventure-happens-things-dont-go-as-planned-on-crestone-peak/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy7m3q90g0731m6ts6y2xc)

> Broken Hand Pass is just shy of 1,000 feet above South Colony Lakes and is gained by hiking and scrambling up a loose, rubble-filled mess of a gully before ending with a short, grassy slope near the top.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** They climb it in the morning, and recross it with thunder rumbling to the east and rockfall a concern, descending in segments.
- **Why it matters:** The hardest, most dreaded part of the trip, crossed twice.
