# McMann's (Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking)

Fictional truckers' and sailors' bar on the shore road out of [[Eastport]], Maine, toward Pleasant Point. In Aoibheann Sweeney's novel it is where Miranda skips her college test, drinks her first whiskey and is led onto a supertanker.

## Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking — Aoibheann Sweeney (2007)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/among-other-things-ive-taken-up-smoking-9781101202227)

> McMann’s was too far out on the road between Yvesport and Pleasant Point for anyone from town to go there much.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On the October day of her college admissions test, Miranda walks along the shore at low tide, comes around the point and finds McMann's "suspended out over the pier". It is busy with truckers from Canada and crews from the container ships. The bartender, Susie, pours her first whiskey; they play pool with two sailors from a supertanker and drive with them to the docks.
- **Why it matters:** The bar is the doorway out of Miranda's sheltered island life: skipping the test, drinking and going to the ship all start here, and Susie is the first person she recognises as lonelier than herself. A town bar would not work: the point is that nobody from Yvesport goes there.
- **Real vs. fictional:** McMann's is invented, like Yvesport; Pleasant Point is the real Passamaquoddy community next to Eastport.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Best guess. The text puts it "on the road between Yvesport and Pleasant Point", over a pier, reached on foot along the shore "around the point" at low tide. Yvesport is inferred as [[Eastport]]. No source names a model. Chosen: Carlow Island, on the Route 190 shore road between Eastport and Pleasant Point. Alternative: the shore nearer Pleasant Point itself.
