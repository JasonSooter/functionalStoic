## The chilling stories behind Japan’s ‘evaporating people’ — Maureen Callahan (2016)

Source: [Original](https://nypost.com/2016/12/10/the-chilling-stories-behind-japans-evaporating-people/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5sfscwkmq3rvq0k8e2rqh)

> He and his wife, Tomoko, lived among the cherry blossoms in Saitama, a prosperous city just outside of Tokyo.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The opening anecdote places martial arts master Ichiro and his wife in prosperous Saitama, where they owned a house and took a loan for a dumpling restaurant. After the market crashed they sold the house and disappeared.
- **Why it matters:** Sets up the before-the-disappearance life in the opening story.
