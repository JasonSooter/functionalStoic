# White River

Silt-laden tributary joining the Yukon River south of Dawson. At John Thornton's camp at its mouth in _The Call of the Wild_ Thornton saves Buck from Hal.

## At a glance

| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|

## The Call of the Wild — Jack London (1903)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14942956W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne5px0ne3s5ptbmg0x5f51)

> they staggered into John Thornton’s camp at the mouth of White River.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In spring, Hal's party reaches Thornton's camp. Thornton warns of rotten ice, Hal ignores him and beats Buck; Thornton cuts Buck's traces, and the sled goes through the ice with Hal, Charles, Mercedes and the dogs.
- **Why it matters:** Buck is saved by Thornton here, ending the Hal-Charles-Mercedes episode and starting the love story of chapter VI.
