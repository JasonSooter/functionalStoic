## The Lie That Helped Build Nintendo — Joe Skrebels (2019)
Source: [Original](https://www.ign.com/articles/2019/12/09/the-lie-that-helped-build-nintendo) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8wg3a9wsk6xqkh64ajj7w)

> He arrived on a Thursday night, went to bed exhausted, and woke on Friday with a plan to call Nintendo to let the team know what had happened, and when he’d arrive at HQ.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** After a grounded DC-10 costs him a day, Bergsten heads straight to Kyoto, where he learns it is a national holiday and everything is closed. He extends his trip by lying to Japan Airlines, then turns up unannounced at Nintendo's Kyoto HQ. After talks, lunch and a long dinner with the export manager, plus a crystal ashtray gift, he is told 'you can have Sweden'.
- **Why it matters:** Where the Swedish distribution deal is won.
