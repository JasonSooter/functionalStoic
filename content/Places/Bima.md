## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2s633fjw4ca3b7zcs594)

> so we continued our journey westwards to the little port of Bima on Sumbawa, the next island in the chain.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After paying off the captain, the team camps for three days in the concrete airport office, waiting for any flight to Java, before a plane for Surabaya arrives. Villagers watch them eat, and the pilot who had left them at Maumere brings them packed lunches.
- **Why it matters:** A comic limbo stop on the way home; minor but described with some detail.
