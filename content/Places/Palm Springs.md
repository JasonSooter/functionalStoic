## My Right to Die — Kevin Drum (2016)
Source: [Original](https://medium.com/mother-jones/my-right-to-die-634643b0b104) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy9cpy6zxrz07aws2krjya)

> A friend persuaded him to call both of them, and on January 30 we all drove out to Palm Springs to say our last goodbyes.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** The family drives to Palm Springs on January 30 to say goodbye to Harry, the author's father-in-law. After writing a note that no one assisted him, he goes into his bedroom, puts a bag over his head and opens a helium tank.
- **Why it matters:** It is the place of the personal story that frames the essay, and where the consequences of California's then-ban on assisted suicide play out.
