# Ben Yehuda Street

Pedestrian street in central [[Jerusalem]]. In _Apeirogon_ it is where a suicide bombing kills Smadar Elhanan.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7w47awzcfy4gp3gwppap2)

> The force of the blast on Ben Yehuda Street knocked her high in the air.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The pedestrian street where bombers killed Smadar and others on a September afternoon. The text shows the shoppers, the cobblestones, the Atara café awning and the ZAKA cleanup.
- **Why it matters:** Smadar's death here is the origin of Rami's story and one of the book's two central events.
