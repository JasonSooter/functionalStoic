# Ridderborn Castle

Castle estate at Vliermaalroot near Hasselt, in the hills of Belgian Limburg ([[Belgium]]). In _The Son and Heir_ it is Mimousse's grandparents' home, where she grew up and where she and Frans hide until the police come.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85n9v7akwwsbndyfr6xba2v)

> The baroness and her four children moved in with her parents on the Ridderborn Estate, tucked away deep in the hills of Belgian Limburg.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Around 1930, abandoned by her husband, Mimousse's mother moves her four children to her parents' estate; "Ridderborn was more of a cloister than a castle", run on strict Catholic lines. In December 1946, instead of leaving for South America, Frans and Mimousse go there, and the lonely old baron and baroness give the couple a wing. On the night of 12 February 1947 the two are taken from their beds at the castle while police raid her Brussels apartment.
- **Why it matters:** By choosing Ridderborn over the escape route, Frans defies both Sweerts and his father, and the arrest that follows sends him back to prison for ten months.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The text places the estate only "deep in the hills of Belgian Limburg"; the pin is Kasteel Ridderborn at Vliermaalroot (Hasselt), the castle of that name in OpenStreetMap.
