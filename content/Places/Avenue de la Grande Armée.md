# Avenue de la Grande Armée

Avenue running west from the Arc de Triomphe in [[Paris (Literary)|Paris]], the start of the 1869 Paris–Rouen race described in Robert Penn's It's All About the Bike.

## It's All About the Bike — Robert Penn (2010)
Source: [[It's All About the Bike]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21030895W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bs7j0cbsvytbyrkwhbn5b7)

> All kinds of machines set off down Avénue de la Grande Armée on 7 November 1869: monocycles, tricycles and quadricycles were among them.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** In the history running through the early chapters, Penn describes the first organised bicycle road race, Paris to Rouen, starting beneath the Arc de Triomphe; James Moore wins on a velocipede with ball bearings in the pedal axle.
- **Why it matters:** It ties the story of a component (the bearing) to the birth of road racing.
