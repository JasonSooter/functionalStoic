# Antongil Bay

Large bay on the north-east coast of Madagascar. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ the first East India Company fleet finds carvings there showing the Dutch were just ahead of them.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m82efyexjk8310ea21a6tj72)

> Arriving on Christmas Day in the bay of Atongill a reconnaissance party discovered a series of carvings on a rock close to the water.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Blown to Madagascar instead of their planned stop, Lancaster's men land on Christmas Day and find carvings on a rock showing that five Dutch ships had called two months earlier and lost more than two hundred men to dysentery. The English then suffer the same: officers and crew die, and a captain is killed by musket fire during a funeral.
- **Why it matters:** It shows the human cost of the voyage and that the Dutch are already ahead in the race.
