# Port Authority Bus Terminal

Midtown Manhattan bus station on Eighth Avenue, the city's main gateway for intercity buses. In Aoibheann Sweeney's novel it is where Miranda, up from Maine, first sets foot in New York.

## Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking — Aoibheann Sweeney (2007)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/among-other-things-ive-taken-up-smoking-9781101202227)

> The rain was pouring down in sheets when we arrived at the Port Authority bus terminal. A line of open umbrellas collected at the taxi stand outside, and people rushed from the brightly lit entrance out into the dark, bent over, holding newspapers above their heads.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After her father sees her off from Yvesport, Miranda rides the bus to New York and steps out into a downpour "in my old rain jacket, still stiff with salt, my father's duffel bag on my shoulder". She queues for a taxi that "smelled like cigarettes inside", and it carries her off the avenue to the brownstone she knows only from a photograph of her father with Arthur Mitchell.
- **Why it matters:** It is the hinge between the two halves of the book, the island and the city. The terminal itself is only a threshold, but its glare and crowds are Miranda's first measure of how far she has come from Crab Island.
