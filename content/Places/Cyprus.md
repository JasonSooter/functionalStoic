# Cyprus

Island country in the eastern Mediterranean. In _Brave New World_ it is the site of a failed experiment in an all-Alpha society.

## Brave New World — Aldous Huxley (1932)

Source: [[Brave New World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL64365W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvawxxyctc4yqe67xpax6p)

> The Controllers had the island of Cyprus cleared of all its existing inhabitants and re-colonized with a specially prepared batch of twenty-two thousand Alphas.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Mustapha Mond tells the Savage of an experiment in A.F. 473, when the Controllers cleared Cyprus of its inhabitants and resettled it with twenty-two thousand Alphas. Within six years there was civil war, nineteen thousand were dead, and the survivors asked the Controllers to resume government.
- **Why it matters:** It is Mond's evidence that a caste-less society is unstable, part of the argument for the whole World State. The island is an example, not a setting.
