## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g0f0e5g478vzsjf99gw4g1)

> SADIQA AND I rarely sat on the rounded cream sofa in our new home in Providence.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In late August 2013 the newlyweds wait in their living room for the radiologist's call and learn Sadiqa has invasive breast cancer.
- **Why it matters:** The diagnosis opens the book's closing link between cancer and racism.
