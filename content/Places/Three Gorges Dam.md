# Three Gorges Dam

Hydroelectric dam on the Yangtze at Sandouping, Hubei, [[China]] (the pin is the ship locks). It appears in Dan Wang's _Breakneck_ as an illustration of what big dams cost, with up to 1.5 million people resettled.

## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL42416011W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dcnp4dwd2yj2m4b826xtvr)

> The world’s biggest dam is the Three Gorges Dam, not far from Chongqing. Building it has demanded the resettlement of up to 1.5 million people.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Cited in a passage on what big dams cost: building it required resettling up to 1.5 million people.
- **Why it matters:** A short illustration of the human cost of building big; minor.
