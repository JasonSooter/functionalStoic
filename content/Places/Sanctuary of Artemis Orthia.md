# Sanctuary of Artemis Orthia

Sanctuary of Artemis on the bank of the Eurotas at [[Sparta]], known for the ritual flogging of Spartan youths. In _Gates of Fire_ it is the site of the beating in which the boy Tripod dies.

## Gates of Fire — Steven Pressfield (1998)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/133742/gates-of-fire-by-steven-pressfield/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m896hy99ycjm161a047aszw6)

> The beatings take place alongside the Temple of Artemis Orthia in a narrow alley called the Runway. The site is beneath plane trees, a shaded and quite pleasant space in less grisly circumstances.

- **Role:** setting, turning point
- **In the book:** Xeones describes the ritual. Boys grip an iron bar between two plane trees while a priestess of Artemis holds an ancient wooden image to receive the blood. The boy Teriander, called Tripod, will not let go and is beaten to death. His death prompts Dienekes's first lesson to Alexandros on fear and on what lies beyond it.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's first close look at Spartan endurance and its cost. The lesson it sets up is the theme the novel carries to Thermopylae.
