> On Friday, at Prime Minister Modi’s suggestion, I visited the Statue of Unity in Gujarat.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** On Friday, at Modi's suggestion, Gates visits the statue honoring Sardar Patel. He notes it is the tallest statue in the world and draws over 60,000 tourists a day at peak season, and is impressed by the tourism economy built around it.
- **Why it matters:** The most vividly described site in the post, used to illustrate local development; a waypoint rather than a turning point.
