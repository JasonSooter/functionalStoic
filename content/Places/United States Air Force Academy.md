## Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World — David Epstein (2019)

Source: [[Range]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550188/range-by-david-epstein/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjspnz4h2gspfa4knc50wc)

> In return for full scholarships, cadets at the Air Force Academy commit to serve as military officers for a minimum of eight years after graduation.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Economists used randomised assignment of over ten thousand cadets to Calculus sections with identical syllabus and exams. The study showed professors who boosted immediate exam scores harmed students' later performance.
- **Why it matters:** Only a setting with randomised sections and standardised grading could demonstrate that short-term and long-term learning diverge.
