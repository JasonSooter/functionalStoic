## The True Story Behind The Revenant, as Told in 1939 — TIME (2016)
Source: [Original](https://time.com/4171001/revenant-1939-true-story/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy6xsw0pcya4v6amh3srhe)

> Without a gun, he began to drag himself to the nearest post, Fort Kiowa, 100 miles away.

- **Role:** journey waypoint / turning point
- **In the article:** Glass crawls toward Fort Kiowa after being abandoned. He later returns after the nine-month trek, but his rage is spent and nothing happens.
- **Why it matters:** Both goal and anticlimactic ending of the journey.
