## The Soul of an Octopus — Sy Montgomery (2015)
Source: [[The Soul of an Octopus]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL27182716M/The_Soul_of_an_Octopus) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmt43e0penqq73fd4qam8g)

> I met Jennifer and Roland at the Octopus Symposium and Workshop at the Seattle Aquarium, to which both Bill and I had traveled to attend.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** The narrator and Bill travel to Seattle for an octopus symposium in a meeting room on an upper floor. Sixty-five octopus lovers attend, and the narrator meets researchers Jennifer Mather and Roland Anderson. Earlier passages also use the aquarium as a source of octopus anecdotes (Emily Dickinson, Lucretia McEvil).
- **Why it matters:** The symposium is where the narrator enters the community of octopus researchers and hears of the Pacific day octopus expedition. Elsewhere it would not change the main thread, but the Seattle Aquarium is where Roland's play discovery was made.
