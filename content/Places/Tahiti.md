# Tahiti

Largest island of French Polynesia in the South Pacific. In The Simple Path to Wealth (JL Collins) it is where the young Collins meets people who live boldly on their own terms.

## The Simple Path to Wealth — JL Collins (2016)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20916711W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fzchc9s73xqpqegmaz6qy4)

> Tahiti is a collection of islands in the South Paci c, each seemingly more stunning than the last. On one of these we stayed for a while in a hut built over the crystal clear water.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In the chapter 'Tales from the South Pacific', Collins and his future wife quit their jobs and travel to Tahiti, staying in an over-water hut. The hotel's co-owner Muk tells how he and friends bought a pineapple plantation sight unseen, went broke, and were asked by the bank in Papeete to finish a half-built hotel; on the beach they meet a couple who sail the South Seas while co-owning a business in Los Angeles.
- **Why it matters:** The stories Collins hears here are his parable of using money to buy freedom, the book's closing message.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The book does not name the island; the pin is Tahiti itself.
