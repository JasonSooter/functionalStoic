## Debate vs. dialogue — Associated Press (2025)

Source: [Original](https://tulsaworld.com/article_d94fa19d-4df5-5898-82f0-0890a4572ecb.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2z9kj8dzdzm3bcbthg3cz)

> A year later and 800 miles away, two teams of high school students convene at the University of North Carolina for the National High School Ethics Bowl finals.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The finals of the National High School Ethics Bowl are held here, with teams discussing a case on cloning a pet, presenting and asking each other questions rather than attacking. A photo caption places a semifinal round in Chapel Hill, N.C.
- **Why it matters:** It is the venue that contrasts with the debate tournament scene; the argument of the piece does not depend on the town itself.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfqxqqw4659bn43dt9sgkw)

> “the State University of North Carolina at Raleigh, or the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill?”

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Arriving a day late for a conference on gravity without the address, Feynman asks the airport taxi dispatcher for "the University of North Carolina" and is asked whether he means Raleigh or Chapel Hill. He describes the absent-minded gravity physicists who would have arrived the day before, muttering "G-mu-nu", and the dispatcher sends him to Chapel Hill.
- **Why it matters:** A short comic anecdote in which the choice between two campuses is the whole joke; Chapel Hill itself is not described.
