# Bernal Heights

Hilly neighborhood in southern [[San Francisco]]. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne goes to an after-hours party there that feels like "a chaotic and somewhat sexy utopia."

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m772wztwzetfa10q2q0ykk0r)

> After the show is over I go to the band’s rehearsal/living space in the Bernal Heights neighborhood where the Extra Action folks and their pals are having a party with live music

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After the CELLspace event Byrne follows the Extra Action Marching Band to their rehearsal and living space. A band called Loop!Station plays cello through electronics, one room has a genuine San Francisco light show of overlapping films and oil-and-water projections, and the band regroups for another set. He feels he has "entered a chaotic and somewhat sexy utopia."
- **Why it matters:** The party is the high point of his question of why this kind of tolerated eccentricity flourishes in San Francisco.
