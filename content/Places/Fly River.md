## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy8vxv9zw2cjj2v279pm17)

> They were working from a small village in the delta of the Fly River on the southern coast.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** From a village in the Fly River delta, Attenborough is hoisted by a pulley-and-kitbag rig up a tree next to the huge display tree. At eye level, he watches male Greater Birds of Paradise display, then see a female arrive and be mated.
- **Why it matters:** This is the fulfilment of a boyhood ambition from Wallace's book and a high point of the chapter: watching the species Wallace described, from the canopy.
