## Billionaires: They’re Just Like Us — Financial Samurai (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.financialsamurai.com/billionaires-are-just-like-us/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hj1frvjhm43nzzy04ncbtx)

> The grand finale is a concert at one of the piers. This year, Dreamforce hosted The Foo Fighters and The Killers (one of my favorite bands) at Pier 70. It was a zoo!

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The Dreamforce conference ends with a concert at Pier 70. The narrator describes a maze of paths and security checkpoints and a VIP benefits area, and on Platform 4, Lower Deck, he and his date end up near the Uber CEO.
- **Why it matters:** The pier is where the article's point plays out: up close, the very rich are standing in the same crowd, which is what the title means.
