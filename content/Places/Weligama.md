## In Sri Lanka, ‘the Ocean Is For Everyone’ — Zinara Rathnayake (2025)

Source: [Original](https://reasonstobecheerful.world/sri-lanka-seasisters-surfing/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2ymhzv9x3s47h7ct6cv6h)

> Amzhii’s hometown, Weligama, 148 kilometers south of the Sri Lankan capital Colombo.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Weligama is Amzhii's hometown and the base of SeaSisters' free swim and surf sessions. On International Women's Day Himaya and Udani teach junior school girls to swim there, and Udani takes girls to the ocean for their first time on a surfboard.
- **Why it matters:** It is the stage for the story of women reclaiming the ocean in a surf-tourism country; the sessions are tied to this coastal town.
