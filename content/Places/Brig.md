# Brig

Railway town in the upper Rhône valley, canton of Valais, [[Switzerland]]. It is where Bill Bryson spends his first Swiss night in _Neither Here Nor There_, marking his sour turn away from Italy.

## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74125W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bejrae9ygvrwqdpftwb0vb)

> Brig was a bit of a strange place.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Opening of chapter 17. He takes a room at the Hotel Victoria overlooking the station and goes out for food. Brig was once a staging post between Zürich and Milan but now "looked as if it didn't quite know what to do with itself", with shops selling refrigerators and televisions; then he realises shops everywhere do that, and that the real trouble is he is no longer in Italy.
- **Why it matters:** The town marks the border mood shift: the first pang of missing Italy, and the start of his sour Swiss chapter.
