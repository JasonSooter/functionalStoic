# Laoag

Capital of Ilocos Norte in the far north of Luzon, the Marcos home province. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne bases himself there and wanders into its Discolandia neighbourhood.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76rhyanw7yfb1qhzbz8m5s6)

> I spy on my local map a neighborhood on the outskirts of Laoag, the regional capital where I am staying. The neighborhood is named Discolandia, which sounds like it might be appropriate for my project, so I aim myself in that direction.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Staying in Laoag, Byrne picks out a neighbourhood called Discolandia on his map, hoping it suits his disco-flavoured Imelda project. Past the bus depot he finds a zone of clubs, quiet by day; inside one, an older woman leads him past bunk beds to a girl in a red dress who asks, "You like girls?", and he realises what the clubs are.
- **Why it matters:** It is a comic and sobering moment of naive tourism in the Marcos heartland, and the base for his trips to Batac and Vigan.
