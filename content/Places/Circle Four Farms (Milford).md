# Circle Four Farms (Milford)

Smithfield hog-farming operation near Milford, Utah. In The Intercept activists film inside it and carry out two piglets, setting off an FBI hunt.

## The FBI’s Hunt for Two Missing Piglets Reveals the Federal Cover-Up of Barbaric Factory Farms — Glenn Greenwald (2017)

Source: [Original](https://theintercept.com/2017/10/05/factory-farms-fbi-missing-piglets-animal-rights-glenn-greenwald/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7dkc7ra32p32ygac7gj57)

> Rather than leave the two piglets at Circle Four Farm to wait for an imminent and painful death, the DxE activists decided to rescue them.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** DxE activists entered the Smithfield-owned factory farm over the summer and filmed gestation crates and rotting piglets. They found two ailing piglets and carried them out to a sanctuary. The farm breeds and slaughters over a million pigs a year.
- **Why it matters:** Origin site of the rescue that triggers the FBI hunt.
