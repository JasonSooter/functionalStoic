## Why Does Portland Have so Many Strip Clubs? — Priceonomics (2015)

Source: [Original](http://priceonomics.com/why-does-portland-have-so-many-strip-clubs/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g8w3q8mwn88r9ghm7j24s2)

> Throughout the 1970s, Star Theatre, another Portland establishment, became known for featuring erotic films

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Through the 1970s the theatre showed erotic films and put strippers on stage. The city sued owner John Tidyman for obscenity in 1976; the case went to the Oregon Supreme Court, which cleared him under the state constitution's free-expression clause.
- **Why it matters:** The precedent from this venue is why Portland's clubs could multiply.
