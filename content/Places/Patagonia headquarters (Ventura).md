## Patagonia’s Philosopher-King – The New Yorker — Nick Paumgarten (2016)

Source: [Original](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2016/09/19/patagonias-philosopher-king) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy7vqmtf0pv1gej7trzr8v)

> The campus is at the west end of town, less than a mile from a couple of famous surf breaks. It has grown from the tin shed into a small village of about a dozen buildings.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The narrator visits the headquarters that grew from Chouinard's 1966 tin-shed forge behind an abandoned slaughterhouse into a dozen-building campus with on-site day care, where Chouinard practices 'management by absence' and sits under the jacarandas. He also dines at the Chouinards' oceanfront house a few miles north of town.
- **Why it matters:** Ventura is the physical embodiment of the company culture the article examines, from blacksmith origins to B Corp idealism.
