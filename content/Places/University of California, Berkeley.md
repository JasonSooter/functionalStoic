## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m772x4w743qem0a9dx3jhdcq)

> I do a talk about the computer presentation program PowerPoint at the University of California in Berkeley for an audience of IT legends and academics.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Byrne has made self-running PowerPoint "films" with dissolving arrows and soundtracks, and presents them to an audience that includes some of the program's creators. He is terrified, and argues that a slide talk is a kind of ritual theater rather than a neutral tool.
- **Why it matters:** It is the stage for his media-critical point about software's supposed neutrality, one of the book's detours from cycling into how tools shape thought.
