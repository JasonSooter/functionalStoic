# Fresno

Fresno is the largest city in California's Central Valley. In _Abundance_ it is where Ezra Klein tours the partly built California high-speed rail line.

## Abundance — Ezra Klein & Derek Thompson (2025)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL44034521W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m77p1vav4e5kzq18p7w16s)

> In October 2023, one of us—okay, it was Ezra—went to Fresno, California, and toured the miles of rail infrastructure that the California High-Speed Rail Authority has built.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In October 2023 Ezra walks the built track near Fresno with its engineers and stands on a stretch of the 99 freeway moved to clear the train's path; nearby, acquiring a Derrel's Mini Storage took four requests for possession and two and a half years. The project has shrunk from a San Francisco–Los Angeles line to a Merced–Bakersfield segment it still can't fully fund. Chapter 3 explains that federal stimulus rules steered first construction to the poorer, more polluted Central Valley.
- **Why it matters:** It gives the book's main example of failed building a physical location, and shows that land acquisition and politics, not construction, are the bottleneck.
