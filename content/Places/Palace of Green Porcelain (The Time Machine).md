# Palace of Green Porcelain (The Time Machine)

Ruined museum of the far future in The Time Machine, "some latter-day South Kensington". Pinned as a best guess near Coombe, south-west London, on the route towards Wimbledon.

## The Time Machine — H. G. Wells (1895)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/55966/the-time-machine) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy9v185wg8rbayf7msqzhe)

> Clearly we stood among the ruins of some latter-day South Kensington!

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The Time Traveller carries Weena there as a refuge from the Morlocks. Inside he finds fossil skeletons, mineral, natural-history, machinery, library and chemistry galleries, a box of matches, camphor, and dummy dynamite. He decides to leave at sunset and sleep outside under fire.
- **Why it matters:** The ruined museum gives him the matches and camphor that drive the Morlock fight, and it is the story's meditation on the waste of human learning.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The text sets it on a turfy down south-west of the Thames valley, sighted from the direction of Combe and Banstead, with Wandsworth and Battersea visible to the north-east, and calls it "some latter-day South Kensington". Pinned as a best guess at Coombe, Kingston upon Thames. Alternative: the real South Kensington museums.
