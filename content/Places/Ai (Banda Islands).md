# Ai

Small nutmeg island in the western Banda Islands, Indonesia, between Neira and Run. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ it trades with the English and twice fights off, then falls to, Dutch invasions in 1615–16.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8efcvb5fswafbeymn3wcwhc)

> It was a fatal mistake, for in the early hours the Bandanese crept out of their fort and launched a savage counter-attack.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In 1609 William Keeling is "busy buying nutmeg at Ai Island, a day’s sailing from Neira" when its chief warns him of the plot against the Dutch. Its shore is "so steepe that it seemeth nature meant to reserve this iland particularly to herselfe", with one dangerous anchorage. In 1615 Governor-General Reynst invades; after a first day's success the islanders counter-attack at night and the Dutch lose "thirty-six soldiers, with two hundred wounded". In 1616 Admiral Lam returns: Samuel Castleton's English ships first block the channel between Ai and [[Run (island)|Run]], then withdraw, and the islanders, who had raised "the flag of St George" for Richard Hunt, are conquered. Lam builds Fort Revenge on the shore; "it stands to this day", its "rusting cannon still points towards Run".
- **Why it matters:** Ai's fall leaves Run as the last free Banda island, which is why Courthope's stand there matters so much.
