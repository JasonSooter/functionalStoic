# Mona Island

Uninhabited island in the Mona Passage between Puerto Rico and Hispaniola. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ it is where Lancaster's crew are stranded when their ship sails off without them.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m82efk02e492kwwz0wzervjt)

> The ship limped towards the outpost island of Mona and, relieved to have reached land, all but five of the crew rowed ashore.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** At the end of Lancaster's first voyage the leaking Edward Bonaventure limps to Mona and most of the crew row ashore; at midnight the ship's carpenter cuts the moorings and sails away. Almost a month later a French ship sees their bonfire and takes them home.
- **Why it matters:** It is the last disaster of the 1591 voyage, whose losses made London's merchants hesitate before trying again.
