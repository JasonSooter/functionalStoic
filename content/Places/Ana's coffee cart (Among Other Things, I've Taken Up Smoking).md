# Ana's coffee cart (Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking)

Fictional sidewalk coffee cart outside a hospital near the [[West Village]], Manhattan. In Aoibheann Sweeney's novel it is where Miranda first falls for a woman, and where Ana hands her the cigarette of the title.

## Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking — Aoibheann Sweeney (2007)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/among-other-things-ive-taken-up-smoking-9781101202227)

> I was looking up the street, thinking I ought to turn around, when I spied an aluminum cart near the hospital entrance, its little awning sticking out over the sidewalk, with COFFEE written down the side of it in big enthusiastic letters.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On her first walk from the institute Miranda finds the cart, "like a boat pulled up at the dock", buys coffee and a chocolate donut, and feels the city settle inside her "like a flock of birds". On later visits the woman who runs it, from Washington Heights by way of the Dominican Republic, teases her about Maine; one afternoon, packing up her van, she offers Miranda a cigarette and gives her name, Ana. Later Miranda leads Nate there, several blocks out of their way.
- **Why it matters:** The cart is where Miranda's desire for women first surfaces, and where the smoking in the title begins. Hospital workers in scrubs queue there; the place is ordinary, which is what makes it hers.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The cart and Ana are invented; the hospital is never named.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Best guess. Clues: from the institute she walks to Eighth Avenue, across "a bigger avenue" lined with shops and restaurants, to "a tall white building, gigantic as a factory" that turns out to be a hospital; the cart stands at its entrance, a few blocks from the institute, and staff in green scrubs buy from it. No source names it. Chosen: the former St. Vincent's Hospital at Seventh Avenue and West 12th Street, the big hospital of the Village until 2010. Alternative: a hospital in Chelsea.
