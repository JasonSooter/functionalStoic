# Place de la Concorde

Paris's largest square, at the foot of the Champs-Élysées. Tom Hanks's 'A Junket in the City of Light' ends with Rory crossing it, finally free to see the city.

## Uncommon Type — Tom Hanks (2017)

Source: [[Uncommon Type]] · [Original](https://penguin.co.uk/books/1114786/uncommon-type/9781786091338.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5v3qpv3sxghx8dz0pfxa7)

> Beyond the gardens was a big traffic circle called Place de la Concorde—millions of cars and Vespa scooters, lanes and lanes of them going both ways and around and around a monument needle in the center. A huge Ferris wheel has been there since 1999.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** With the tour canceled, Rory walks out alone, crossing the river, passing Notre Dame and the Louvre pyramid, then the Tuileries where crews are dismantling the stage for the canceled event. At the Place de la Concorde he buys a ticket and rides the nearly empty Ferris wheel several times, seeing the whole city.
- **Why it matters:** The ride closes the story: Rory, unrecognized and no longer a promoted commodity, finally sees Paris for himself and takes it in 'for free'.
