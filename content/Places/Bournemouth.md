# Bournemouth

Seaside town on the south coast of [[England]]. _My Family and Other Animals_ opens there, on a wet August afternoon when the Durrells decide to leave for Corfu.

## My Family and Other Animals — Gerald Durrell (1956)
Source: [[My Family and Other Animals]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/289004/my-family-and-other-animals-by-gerald-durrell/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8fa96m810y3708w8pjx0ehc)

> Along the Bournemouth sea-front the beach huts turned blank wooden faces towards a greeny-grey, froth-chained sea that leaped eagerly at the cement bulwark of the shore.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The prologue, "The Migration", opens in the family's Bournemouth house in a cold, drizzly August. Every member is ill in some way (catarrh, earache, acne, a cold) except Larry, who complains about the "bloody climate", reads George's letter praising Corfu and talks Mother into going. They sell the house she has just bought and leave England "like a flock of migrating swallows".
- **Why it matters:** Bournemouth is the foil: its gloom sets up the sunlight of Corfu. It appears only in the opening scene, but it is the reason the family leaves.
