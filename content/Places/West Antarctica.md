## Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir (2021)
Source: [[Project Hail Mary]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/611060/project-hail-mary-by-andy-weir/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jx9m88w3dyrr0j63xnqjt8)

> We anchored off the coast of western Antarctica in the dead of night. The coastline sat in the extreme distance, visible only by moonlight.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Stratt's carrier and escort ships anchor offshore while Leclerc orders 241 nuclear weapons, buried along a fissure, detonated to cleave the shelf and release trapped methane. Leclerc explains Antarctica was once a jungle and weeps after the blast; the Amundsen-Scott Station is noted as 1,500 km away.
- **Why it matters:** The plan to warm Earth with methane is a major moral and plot turning point, and the continent itself is the target.
