# Aldan

Gold-mining town in the Sakha Republic (Yakutia), south of Yakutsk. In _Last of the Breed_ it is the district seat where the KGB colonel sends Kyra to run the manhunt and where the official Wulff takes furs from the exiles.

## Last of the Breed — Louis L'Amour (1986)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL51048W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6xdsfx6bh7xjvxzcrr8xhqh)

> Coolly he said, "I am sending her to Aldan."

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Colonel Zamatev tells his secretary he is sending Kyra Lebedev to Aldan, "a focal point," to make sure the search does not slacken in the cold; she is to fly there and take a car. In Aldan Kyra meets Wulff, "the man in charge here," a fat, wary official who wants her to stay in the town and not look around. The Lithuanian exile Baronas later tells Joe Mack that Wulff, "miles from here, in Aldan," keeps silent about the exiles as long as they deliver furs.
- **Why it matters:** Aldan is the pursuers' forward base and the seat of the corrupt arrangement that keeps the exiles' village hidden, so the chase and the refuge both hinge on it.
