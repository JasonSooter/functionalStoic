# Roosevelt High School (Seattle)

Public high school in north Seattle. In _The Boys in the Boat_ Joe Rantz spends his senior year there, and the crew coach spots him on the gym's high bar.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m869xmy932kzvm7gms92p6sj)

> In the end he boarded up the house in Sequim, told Joyce he’d be back at the end of the school year, took the ferry to Seattle, moved in with Fred and Thelma, and started attending Roosevelt.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In 1931 Joe's brother Fred, a chemistry teacher at Roosevelt, invites him to Seattle for his senior year. Joe makes the honour roll, sings in the glee club and stars on the gymnastics team. One spring day in 1932 a tall man in a fedora watches him doing giants on the high bar and leaves a card with Fred: Alvin M. Ulbrickson, head coach of crew. Joe graduates with honours that summer.
- **Why it matters:** Without the year at Roosevelt and that card, Joe would not have found his way to the university or to rowing.
