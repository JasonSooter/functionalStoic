## This new French train is a revolution... Here's why! — Simply Railway (2025)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iYvf1Dny-6Y) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crdm6k4gbkt8gqc2sez0xv)

> Here in Dijon, our train will reverse direction once again! Beautiful view of the Cathedral of Saint Benignus of Dijon.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the video:** At the second major stop the train reverses again and the overhead power changes from AC to DC; the presenter recalls the station's former international and night trains.
- **Why it matters:** A technical and historical waypoint on the route.
