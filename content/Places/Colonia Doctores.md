# Colonia Doctores

Neighbourhood in central [[Mexico City]]. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it is where Frida Kahlo finds her runaway sister Matilde, years after helping her elope.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)
Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m72jsafwayy5vtkwycnyf7xg)

> In the back of a patio, in the fourth room off a long corridor, I found her. The room was full of light and birds.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Frida had helped her older sister Matilde run off with her boyfriend, and the family lost track of her. A friend tells Frida that a married woman who looks like her, named Matilde Kahlo, lives in the Doctores section; Frida finds her there bathing with a hose, living with Paco Hernández. Frida tells her father and visits several times, but their mother refuses to see Matilde for a long time.
- **Why it matters:** A small family episode in Frida's own words, showing her loyalty and the rift in the household. The neighbourhood itself is only the place where the search ends.
