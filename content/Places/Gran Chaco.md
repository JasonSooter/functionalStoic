# Gran Chaco

Hot, dry lowland plain of western Paraguay and its neighbours; in _Life on Air_ the setting of the Zoo Quest armadillo hunt.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx3asbk0nqb0811kcw8bse)

> After several days' travel, we saw far away on the western horizon what I thought must be George Giles' estancia.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** They ride west on horseback toward the Pilcomayo with an ox wagon, hunting armadillos in thorn bush and dried swamp. At a remote estancia they meet George Giles, an Englishman who has forgotten everything but one rude phrase. Attenborough is stabbed by a palm thorn here and never finds the giant armadillo.
- **Why it matters:** The Chaco is the expedition's hardest country and gives the chapter its armadillo hunt and Giles anecdote.
