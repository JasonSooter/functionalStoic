## Into the heart of terror: behind Isis lines — Jürgen Todenhöfer (2016)

Source: [Original](http://www.theguardian.com/world/2016/apr/04/behind-isis-lines-jurgen-todenhofer-journalist-syria-iraq-my-journey-into-the-heart-of-terror) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjrjm0pa1bdf1nf7sx85z6)

> We drive by a circular plaza we recognise from media reports. It’s surrounded by an iron fence. This is where the heads of decapitated enemies are impaled and put on display.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** Arriving in Raqqa after dark, the authors drive past a circular plaza known from media reports, fenced with iron, where heads of enemies are displayed. Frederic is forbidden to photograph it.
- **Why it matters:** A brief but vivid image of the regime's violence. The text does not name the square; identification as Raqqa's Naim roundabout comes from outside the text.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Inferred. The report only says “We drive by a circular plaza we recognise from media reports”, fenced with iron, where severed heads were put on display, soon after arriving in Raqqa. Press reports from the time place these displays at Naim (Al-Naim) Square, the roundabout in central Raqqa, so the pin uses its point. Alternative: another Raqqa roundabout such as the Clock Tower square.
