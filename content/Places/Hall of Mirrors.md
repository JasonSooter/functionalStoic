# Hall of Mirrors

Mirrored gallery of the Palace of Versailles near Paris, built for Louis XIV. In _Germany: Memories of a Nation_ it is where the German Empire was proclaimed in 1871 and the 1919 treaty was imposed.

## Germany: Memories of a Nation — Neil MacGregor (2014)

Source: [[Germany - Memories of a Nation]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20201162W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m76jxqtexqxm4d1qb1tdsj)

> It was no coincidence that this ringing declaration was made not in Berlin, but in that same Hall of Mirrors at Versailles, before the Franco-Prussian War was even formally over.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 21, 'Bismarck the blacksmith', opens with the hall's 1680s celebration of Louis XIV's triumph over the Holy Roman Emperor. On 18 January 1871, before the Franco-Prussian War was over, Wilhelm I accepted the crown of the new German Empire there. In 1919 the road led back to the same hall, where a peace treaty was imposed on Germany.
- **Why it matters:** Chosen deliberately to reverse two centuries of French dominance, it became the stage for both the founding and the humiliation of Bismarck's empire.
