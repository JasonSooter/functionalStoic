## 25 Documentaries Everybody Should Watch Pt. 2 — Martijn Schirp (2016)
Source: [Original](https://highexistence.com/25-documentaries-everybody-watch-part-ii/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jkz6y33x8sj41sqemfdjvd)

> His little place sits in a subway station with just ten seats, yet has gotten a 3 star Michelin review and is sold out a year in advance.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** In the Jiro Dreams of Sushi entry, a sushi master in Tokyo has refined his sushi daily for over sixty years. His ten-seat restaurant in a subway station holds three Michelin stars and is booked a year ahead.
- **Why it matters:** The restaurant is unnamed in the text; it is the physical home of the dedication the entry praises.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Restaurant is not named; Tokyo and subway station are given.
