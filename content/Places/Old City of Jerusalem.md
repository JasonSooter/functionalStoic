## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7wdmyee186fvpyjcv6jdt)

> He liked walking in the Palestinian neighborhoods, around the souks where as a blind storyteller he was treated with particular reverence.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Borges walks the Old City alleys and trinket shops, drinking coffee and listening to stories. A later afternoon in a souk on Al-Zahra Street has him compare One Thousand and One Nights to a cathedral.
- **Why it matters:** The fragments make the city a place of storytelling and ancient stone.
