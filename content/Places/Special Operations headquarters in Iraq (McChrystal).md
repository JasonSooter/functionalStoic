## Why Every Organization Needs a Chief of Staff Like This One — Stan McChrystal (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/productivity-hacks-why-every-organization-needs-chief-stan-mcchrystal?trk=eml-mktg-inf-m-prodhacks-1008-p6) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gm1569kj3j0f5rey8ats76)

> I wiped the dust off my uniform as I entered our Special Operations headquarters in Iraq.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** McChrystal walks between buildings of the headquarters in a sudden haboob sandstorm; the runway that hugs the compound is silent, so his helicopter trip to Baghdad is impossible. His aide, Chris Fussell, has already rescheduled the day around the storm.
- **Why it matters:** The scene frames the whole argument for a strong chief of staff.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The text says only 'our Special Operations headquarters in Iraq', with a runway beside the compound and Baghdad a helicopter flight away. McChrystal's task-force headquarters is commonly reported to have been at Balad; this could not be confirmed in a reference work here. Chosen: Balad Air Base. Alternative: another base outside Baghdad.
