Source: [Original](https://xkcd.com/3154/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5d20sjm3kx933sj6c2d578e)

> When Galileo dropped two weights from the Leaning Tower of Pisa, they put him in the history books.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The comic's caption contrasts Galileo's experiment from the tower with the narrator doing the same thing today, who is detained by security for injuring several tourists.
- **Why it matters:** The tower is the gag's whole premise; there is no more to the text than this.
