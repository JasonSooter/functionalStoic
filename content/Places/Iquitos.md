# Iquitos

City on the Amazon in northeastern Peru, reachable only by river or air. In Greenlights it is the gateway to the jungle trip on which Matthew McConaughey sheds his new fame.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m700j6h6vwbgnqw0mdjdbp65)

> Then, I took a bus, boat, and plane ride to get to the largest city in the world you can’t get to by car, Iquitos, the “Peruvian capital of the Amazon.”

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Chasing a dream of floating down the Amazon, he flies to Lima and Cuzco, hikes to Machu Picchu, then travels by bus, boat and plane to Iquitos. On the twelfth night, camped in the jungle, he throws off every token of his identity and vomits until he is empty; next morning he finds a trail covered in thousands of butterflies and finally reaches the Amazon, where his guide greets him with "Sois luz, Mateo."
- **Why it matters:** The trip is his first escape from fame and the template for later journeys to Africa. The river and jungle are the point: the dream is specifically the Amazon, so another setting would lose the book's recurring "wet dream" motif.
