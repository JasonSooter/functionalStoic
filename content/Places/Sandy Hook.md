# Sandy Hook

Barrier spit at the mouth of New York Harbor in New Jersey. Henry Hudson anchors behind it in 1609 in _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_, and in _Wedding of the Waters_ De Witt Clinton pours Lake Erie water into the Atlantic here.

## Wedding of the Waters - The Erie Canal and the Making of a Great Nation — Peter L. Bernstein (2005)
Source: [[Wedding of the Waters - The Erie Canal and the Making of a Great Nation]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL3294009W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kve0ajzaqbhxpg6hf86sq6)

> and then, in calm seas and brilliant sunshine, sailed on out to Sandy Hook, at the very southern end of New York harbor.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On 4 November 1825 the steamboat flotilla reaches Sandy Hook; Clinton moves to the warship Washington, fills bottles for Lafayette, pours the keg of Lake Erie water into the Atlantic and gives his speech, and Dr Mitchill empties thirteen bottles of water from the world's rivers.
- **Why it matters:** It is the ceremony that gives the book its title and the climax of the whole story.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)
Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8efcgzp67ascfs3fnnkwren)

> At last the light strengthened and Harbour Hill on Long Island hove into view followed, a few hours later, by the gleaming flats of Sandy Hook.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** In early September 1609 the Half Moon anchors in "a very good harbour" here, and local people come out to trade. Hudson sends John Coleman with four men "through the Narrows"; as they admire the "very sweet smells" from the shore, arrows pierce Coleman's throat and kill him. He is buried at what the book calls Coleman's Point, close to Sandy Hook.
- **Why it matters:** It is Hudson's arrival at the mouth of the river that will carry his name, and the first bloodshed of the voyage into New York.
