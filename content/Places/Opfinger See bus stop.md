## The Opfinger See bus stop now has a wooden shelter — VAG Freiburg (2025)
Source: [Original](https://blog.vag-freiburg.de/die-bushaltestelle-opfinger-see-hat-jetzt-einen-holz-unterstand/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crc3629prwzr3b45mardb1)

> The "Opfinger See" stop was originally a temporary solution during the coronavirus pandemic. By now it is a permanent part of line 33, which runs regularly.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Carpentry trainees built a shelter of regional timber, with a bench and a green roof, for the stop by the lake, which began as a temporary stop during the pandemic and is now a permanent part of line 33.
- **Why it matters:** The stop itself is the subject: a small piece of rural public-transport infrastructure made permanent.
