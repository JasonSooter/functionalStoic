# Mount Yasur

Active volcano on Tanna, Vanuatu; in _Life on Air_ the sacred mountain of the John Frum cult (the book's 'Yahuwey').

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1njxrm5340g4w3dkss9j)

> But Yahuwey had an atmosphere I have not encountered on any other. It was eerie.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bob Paul drives the party through coconut plantations to the volcano in the heart of John Frum country. Red-painted sticks and a gateless gate stand on the ash plain, a peeling red cross marks the crater lip, and an explosion sends the party sprinting downhill. A wayside shrine to John Frum stands nearby.
- **Why it matters:** The cultists treat the volcano as sacred (Nambas says men live inside it and will bring cargo), so the place carries the cult's symbolism; Attenborough finds it uniquely eerie.
