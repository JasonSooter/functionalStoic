# Orange Grove

Suburb in north-eastern Johannesburg. In Born a Crime it is where seven-year-old Trevor Noah burns down a white family's house.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6yq4agsp88ck319q6hdccc3)

> He lived in a neighborhood called Orange Grove in a white family’s garage, which he’d turned into a cottage-type thing with a hot plate and a bed.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 6 ("Loopholes"). His mother's boyfriend Abel can live in a white suburb only as a tenant in servants' quarters, so he rents the family's garage. On a visit, Trevor plays with the white family's son, burning his name into wood with a magnifying glass and then with matches. The fire spreads and burns down the house. Abel is thrown out and moves in with Trevor and his mother in Eden Park. "After the fire I was notorious," and an uncle starts calling him "Terror".
- **Why it matters:** the fire brings Abel into the household, which sets up the family's later story. It also fixes Trevor's reputation as the family's troublemaker.
