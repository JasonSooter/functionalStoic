# Nishapur

City in Razavi Khorasan, northeast Iran, home of Omar Khayyam's tomb. A rest stop in Dervla Murphy's _Full Tilt_.

## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6ksda3x4024w2kqzxk5k1)

> We only did fifty-five miles today as this is Omar Khayyám’s town and I’ve stopped here to pay homage.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** She stops to see the poet's town, tours his tombs with eager English students, finds the new tomb grotesque and the old one dignified, and stays with a poor family who dance in the evening.
- **Why it matters:** A rest stop and cultural high point.
