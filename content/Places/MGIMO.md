# MGIMO

Moscow State Institute of International Relations, the elite Soviet diplomatic university in [[Moscow]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is where the young Oleg Gordievsky studies and is recruited by the KGB.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7p6zaw4a6eh2y9rwr5hdh9g)

> The Institute of International Relations was the Soviet Union’s most elite university, described by Henry Kissinger as “the Russian Harvard.”

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** At seventeen Gordievsky enrols at the institute, studies Swedish, reads censored foreign papers and secretly listens to the BBC and Voice of America. He records a naive speech about freedom in the language lab and is told to destroy it. He befriends the Czech Standa Kaplan and is recruited through a KGB office inside the institute.
- **Why it matters:** It is where he becomes a KGB officer, and where the friendship with Kaplan begins: Kaplan later returns as MI6's test of whether Gordievsky can be approached.
