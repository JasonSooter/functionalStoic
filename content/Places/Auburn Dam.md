# Auburn Dam

Abandoned dam site on the North Fork American River near Auburn, [[California]], the never-finished project that explains why California's tallest bridge stands so high.

## California’s Tallest Bridge Has Nothing Underneath — Wesley Crump (2025)
Source: [Original](https://practical.engineering/blog/2025/8/19/californias-tallest-bridge-has-nothing-underneath) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dckp1z2g5rhn5gr6s950cq)

> One of the first steps in the process was rerouting the American River.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** A tall concrete arch dam was authorised here in 1968. Crews built a cofferdam and diversion tunnel and grouted the foundation, but earthquake concerns, the Teton Dam collapse, cost and opposition halted it, and the river was returned to its channel in 2007.
- **Why it matters:** Without the dam the bridge would never have been built so high; its abandonment is the story's turning point.
