# Mountain House, California

Exurban planned community in San Joaquin County, [[California]], south-west of Stockton. In _Happy City_ it is home to Randy Strausser, whose long commute and lonely street embody the dispersed city.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m83dcf7m08z5931z1c3jer9t)

> Randy and his wife, Julie, bought a California-style ranch house in Mountain House, a partially finished exurban development south of Stockton, in 2007, when the market was tanking.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Randy and Julie bought at half what their neighbours paid, but Randy is not happy. He leaves before dawn for an office in the Bay Area and gets home after dark; Montgomery rides along at dusk as he merges from Route 101 to Interstate 680. About five thousand people live in the half-finished development, with virtually no jobs and few services, and Randy, who has been burgled, says nobody looks out for each other.
- **Why it matters:** Mountain House is the book's human portrait of how long-distance living erodes the social ties that happiness research ranks highest.
