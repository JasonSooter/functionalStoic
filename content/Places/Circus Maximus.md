## Uncommon Type — Tom Hanks (2017)

Source: [[Uncommon Type]] · [Original](https://penguin.co.uk/books/1114786/uncommon-type/9781786091338.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5v4p2d4eg7f6ea6rpb6g5)

> On the last night the teaser was shown outside at the Circo Máximo—where the chariot races were held in ancient times. To Rory it looked like just a big field.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** At the Rome stop of the junket, the teaser is projected on a huge temporary screen after a soccer trophy presentation, before about 21,000 people. Nothing happens when Rory waves, but fans rush the barricades when Willa appears, and carabinieri fight them as she is rushed away in an armored car.
- **Why it matters:** The scene shows Rory's place as an interchangeable costar beside the star and foreshadows his 'Rory No One' status.
