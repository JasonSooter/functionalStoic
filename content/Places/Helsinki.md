## How the Finnish survive without small talk — Laura Studarus (2018)
Source: [Original](https://www.bbc.com/travel/story/20181016-how-the-finnish-survive-without-small-talk) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7bwxbmn3n1m6bs1btv7dn)

> I met my now best friend Hanna a few years ago during my first visit to Helsinki, on a coffee date set up out of desperation.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author met her friend Hanna on a coffee date during her first visit to Helsinki. The drink turned into a four-hour dinner of deep conversation about politics, religion, sex and life. A year later she flew back to be a bridesmaid at Hanna's wedding.
- **Why it matters:** Opening anecdote that frames the contrast between Finnish reserve and sudden deep connection; Helsinki is also home to a student and a university professor quoted later.
