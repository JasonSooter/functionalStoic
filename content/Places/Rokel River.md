## Peace Corps: The Great Adventure — Peace Corps (2002)
Source: [[Peace Corps - The Great Adventure]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL16050643W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crb4dnxx658b5vp0565e8n)

> During the dry season, I spent the evenings fishing Sierra Leone's Rokel River with my host-country friends, the Conteh brothers-Moses, Bokarie, and Sanpha.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 'Fishing in Sierra Leone', Phil Bob Hellmich fishes the Rokel for Nile perch on dry-season evenings with the Conteh brothers, loses lures on its rocky bottom, and teaches the Contehs to make their own. On his last fishing trip he sees a farmer land a perch on a Conteh lure.
- **Why it matters:** The river is where the lure-making project is tested and succeeds; the story's arc runs from lost imported lures to local self-sufficiency.
