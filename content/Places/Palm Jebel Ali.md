## Dubai is Building a $15BN NEW PALM — The B1M (2026)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ZL0z3TUz87Y) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5c3p7fjqpsasxkeakrnxypd)

> This is the Palm Jebali, the world's largest palm-shaped island.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The B1M describes an island planned with 2,000 luxury villas, 80 hotels, a sea village, a yacht club and a large mosque, which would more than double Dubai's coastline. It explains how sand from the Gulf seabed was sprayed into place by "rainbowing" and then compacted.
- **Why it matters:** The island is the subject of the short. ⚠️ The transcript spells the name "Palm Jebali"; the note uses the standard name.
