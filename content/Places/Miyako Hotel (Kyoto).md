# Miyako Hotel (Kyoto)

Hotel in eastern Kyoto, today the Westin Miyako Kyoto. In _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_ Feynman and Abraham Pais share a Japanese-style room there during a physics conference.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfqtaqz4bmdk1b5xyr4wq6)

> We stayed at the Hotel Miyako, which had both American-style and Japanese-style rooms, and Pais shared a Japanese-style room with me.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On the Kyoto leg of the conference "We stayed at the Hotel Miyako", which had American-style and Japanese-style rooms, and Pais shares Feynman's Japanese-style room. The young woman bringing breakfast greets Pais, naked and dripping from the bath, with complete composure, and Pais remarks on how uncivilised Americans are.
- **Why it matters:** A short scene about Japanese ease with nudity; the hotel is only the stage.
