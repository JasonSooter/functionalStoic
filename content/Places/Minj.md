# Minj

Highland town in the Wahgi Valley, Papua New Guinea; in _Life on Air_ Attenborough watches a great sing-sing of plume-wearing dancers there.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx30zv4z13x4352cxw3c75)

> Minj , a village on the other side of the valley from Nondugl, was due to receive a ceremonial visit from people who lived up in the mountains to the south.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Visitors descend from the southern mountains over several hours, dancing and chanting, to trade pigs and pearl shells at a sing-sing. Hundreds of men in red clay and fat wear paradise-bird plumes, which Attenborough counts as the feathers of around twenty thousand birds.
- **Why it matters:** A vivid set-piece that provides the evidence of how intensely the birds are hunted.
