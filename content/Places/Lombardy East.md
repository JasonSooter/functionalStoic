# Lombardy East

Middle-class suburb on the north-eastern edge of Johannesburg, next to [[Alexandra (Johannesburg)|Alexandra]]. In Born a Crime a police raid on a party Trevor Noah is DJ'ing there wrecks his computer and ends the crew's DJ business.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zbjkgrv03nwqen4m59hj4d)

> One night I was DJ’ing a party, not in Alex but right outside Alex in Lombardy East, a nicer, middle-class black neighborhood.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 16 ("The Cheese Boys"). Police called about the noise come in riot gear with machine guns. While Trevor waits for Windows 95 to shut down, a nervous cop shoots the computer, hitting the monitor, and the police fire tear gas into the crowd. The explosion fries the hard drive and his whole music library with it.
- **Why it matters:** losing the music ends the DJ and CD business that kept the crew going, and pushes them further into hustling in the hood. Noah uses it to show that this is simply how the police deal with parties in black neighbourhoods, even a "nicer" one.
