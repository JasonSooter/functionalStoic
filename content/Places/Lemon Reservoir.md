# Lemon Reservoir

Reservoir on the Florida River in La Plata County, Colorado, north-east of Durango. It turns up in The Comfort Crisis (Mr. Money Mustache): remote reservoir near Durango where the author paddleboards, finding calm in difficulty.

> One day, my co-builder and I decided to take the afternoon off and head to the a wild, remote reservoir for some paddleboarding.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The two builders paddleboard out into a headwind with no phones, reach a sheltered part of the lake, explore a steep wildflower meadow, then drift back lying on their boards over two hundred feet of cold water. He calls it one of the best and most relaxed moments of his life.
- **Why it matters:** It is the emotional high point of the essay's practical example, though the photo caption is the only place the reservoir is named; the name Lemon Reservoir is from a caption.
