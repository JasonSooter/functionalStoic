# East Pintler Peak

Summit in the Anaconda-Pintler Wilderness, Montana (the book spells it Pintlar). Eric climbs it alone in the rain and breaks through the clouds at sunset.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8berytf87k9pg5p2k3hbsfk)

> East Pintlar Peak stood just south of us, and beyond the towering mountain I could see patches of blue sky.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On July 11, after fleeing a storm over a pass to Johnson Lake, Eric eyes East Pintlar Peak and the blue sky beyond it, to Tim's despair ("Oh God no, not another one, Eric!"). They drop to frozen Oreamos Lake at the base of the ridge between the East and West Pintlar peaks, and Eric climbs on alone in the rain, determined to get above the clouds. The clouds rupture into a golden sunset, his anger is "cleansed", and he shoots more than 70 photographs; Tim has cocoa ready and sings "Silent Night".
- **Why it matters:** It is the emotional peak of the Anaconda-Pintlar chapter and shows Eric's stubbornness, which later strains the brothers.
