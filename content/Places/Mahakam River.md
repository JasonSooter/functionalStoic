Major river of East Kalimantan, Indonesian Borneo; in _Life on Air_ the Zoo Quest team travels up it to a Dyak longhouse.

## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2kdcp0m7bgc1hjykgz8x)

> They lived in a single longhouse, a huge construction built on stilts.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Travelling up-river in the launch Kruwing from Samarinda, the team reaches a Bahau Dyak longhouse and stays there. Attenborough describes its veranda, communal space and family rooms, collects animals such as hanging parakeets, films a wild orangutan, receives a message in a cleft stick, and buys an orphan orangutan, Charlie.
- **Why it matters:** The key Borneo location, with the fullest description of a place and its way of life in the chapter.
