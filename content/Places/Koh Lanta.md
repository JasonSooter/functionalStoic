> The conversation is part of a session for a digital nomad camp on Koh Lanta, a Thai island district

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The article opens in a bamboo-bedecked lounge by the ocean, where 17 digital nomads spend 10 days of workshops and talks at a DNX camp run by Meurer and Hargarten.
- **Why it matters:** The camp is the scene through which the piece discusses nomad community; other destinations (Bangkok, Salvador) are only mentioned.
