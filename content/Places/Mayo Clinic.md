## Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World — David Epstein (2019)

Source: [[Range]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550188/range-by-david-epstein/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jkztfwg3nz49b36chb212k)

> Her pediatrician sent the family to the Mayo Clinic.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Jill is sent to the Mayo Clinic at age four and returns every summer. Doctors there find elevated creatine kinase but never name her condition, and she eventually writes Emery-Dreifuss on her own chart.
- **Why it matters:** The clinic stands for specialists who could not diagnose her; she solved the problem herself from outside. Minor as a place, but the repeated annual visits are the story's backbone.
