## Hiking Tennessee’s Mount LeConte — Bob Doucette (2015)

Source: [Original](https://proactiveoutside.wordpress.com/2015/12/08/hiking-tennessees-mount-leconte/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1m4sd38rgzjwxs8yv1cf)

> First off, Alum Cave is not really a cave. Instead, it’s a prominent chunk of overhanging rock that builds a sort of shelter at the base.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** They rest at the bluff, which is where many day hikers turn around. A sign says the summit is still 2.7 miles away. The author paces the group and Jen vows not to stop before the top.
- **Why it matters:** Major waypoint and the trail's namesake; it marks the commitment point of the hike.
