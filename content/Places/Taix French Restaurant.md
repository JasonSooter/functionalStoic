> Echo Park is a trendy hood. It’s hipster and heavily Hispanic.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** William Johnson, chairman of the American Freedom Party, holds a lunch for members here, planning for a Trump victory with five young alt-right men. The author notes the oddness of the venue: Echo Park is trendy, hipster and heavily Hispanic, with Latino kitchen staff.
- **Why it matters:** Opening scene of the piece; the mismatch of venue and politics sets the tone.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The highlighted line names only Echo Park; the lunch is at Taix, so the pin is Echo Park's point.
