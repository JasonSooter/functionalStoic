## \[RIDGELINE] Do You Want to Run a Kissa? — Craig Mod (2024)

> The reason for the short deadline? They’ve been renting the space for nearly half a century, and they have to “revert” it to “zero” in order to return it to the building owner by September.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Mod reports that the kissa closed on July 20 and has until the second week of August to find a successor before the space must be returned bare; its 1970s interior is by Matsukei Shinpei, and he asks readers who want it to reply.
