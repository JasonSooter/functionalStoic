# Santa Elena, Costa Rica

Small town in the Monteverde district of Costa Rica, the base for visiting the cloud forest. Retire by 40's family plans their Monteverde stay there and later finds it a touristy 'eco Disneyland'.

## Our 2 Week Costa Rica Vacation — retirebyforty (2015)
Source: [Original](https://retireby40.org/our-2-week-costa-rica-vacation/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gm1kms127amernkbt4eqzt)

> Santa Elena was even smaller than La Fortuna. The main street is paved, but all the side streets are dirt/gravel roads.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The family stays four nights at a B&B and finds the town small, with a paved main street and dirt side streets, packed with tour companies, restaurants and hostels. On their host's tip they walk through local streets to a strangler fig with a hollow trunk; the author climbs about 25 feet of it and values that, unlike everything else, it was free.
- **Why it matters:** The town's character drives the author's verdict that Monteverde is too touristy to revisit.
