# Do Lectures (Fforest)

Fforest farm near Cardigan in West Wales, home of the Do Lectures. In a David Hieatt blog post the event's remoteness is the point.

## david hieatt — David Hieatt (2015)

Source: [Original](https://davidhieatt.typepad.com/doonethingwell/2015/05/your-weakness-could-be-your-strength.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fzcdqpbfq69rm61qm5fhv5)

> It is hard to get to. It takes place on the far western edge of Wales.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Hieatt weighs the event's weaknesses: it is remote, the wifi is poor, people camp for three days and nights and there are only 150 places. Because it is hard to reach, speakers stay for the whole event, phones go down, and by the third night people are friends around the camp-fire.
- **Why it matters:** The venue's remoteness is the post's case for turning a weakness into a strength.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The post names only 'the far western edge of Wales'; the Do Lectures are held at Fforest, near Cardigan.
