# The paved park (Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking)

Unnamed waterfront park at the southern tip of Manhattan, most likely [[The Battery (Manhattan)|the Battery]]. In Aoibheann Sweeney's novel it is where Miranda, looking out at the bay, realises her father loved this island more than their own.

## Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking — Aoibheann Sweeney (2007)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/among-other-things-ive-taken-up-smoking-9781101202227)

> I came through to a paved park where the sky was lit unmistakably with the ocean.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The afternoon after her first night with Nate, Miranda walks south across Houston Street, through Canal Street and Chinatown, until she reaches a park where "the ground was paved all the way to where the land dropped off, with no shore, as if the whole island was made of cement". The sea smell "almost hurt, it was so close and familiar", and she pictures the bay at home. Turning to face the towers, she decides that "beyond a doubt my father had loved this island more than our own". She walks home up the West Side and across Christopher Street.
- **Why it matters:** It is the novel's point of reckoning between Maine and Manhattan: only at the city's edge, facing the sea she grew up on, can Miranda see that her father's real life lay here. An inland setting would lose the comparison that drives the scene.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The park is real but never named; the walk past Houston Street, Canal Street and the World Trade Center is given street by street.
- **Where is it?:** Inferred. Clues: she walks "south through the Village and across Houston Street", through "Canal Street and Chinatown", until "Manhattan reared up again, gloomy and municipal"; at the park "a crowd gathered by the water to wait for a boat", and ahead of a passing ferry is "the Statue of Liberty, the water like a carpet at her feet", with "bridges and tankers gray in the distance". No source names it. Chosen: the Battery, the park at Manhattan's southern tip where the Statue of Liberty ferries leave. Alternative: the Battery Park City esplanade just to the north.
