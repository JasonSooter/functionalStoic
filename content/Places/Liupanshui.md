## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL42416011W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dcnfz6vdkz9k406dyq0mrt)

> Financial inquisitors found something in Liupanshui, the westernmost city in Guizhou, home to the world’s highest bridge.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Party secretary Li Zaiyong backed tourism projects including fake European squares, a ski lift and snow machines; none succeeded, the city ran up some $21 billion of debt, and Li was sentenced to death with a reprieve.
- **Why it matters:** A cautionary case of how the political system rewards vanity construction.
