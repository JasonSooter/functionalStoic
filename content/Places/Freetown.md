## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1sjbjet4qg5f7cpc4r1p)

> Stepping out into the heat and humidity of a West African afternoon was like entering a heated sauna. The hedge beside the ramshackle airport was bright with the scarlet trumpets of hibiscus.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Arriving in Freetown, Attenborough sees sunbirds, a chameleon and sensitive mimosa in a roadside hedge. He calls it a revelation of tropical nature 'from which I have never recovered'.
- **Why it matters:** The moment of first contact with tropical nature that shapes his later career.
