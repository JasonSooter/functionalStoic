## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)
Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbmaxr9empkwg8dpzrrtmb)

> America has no shared-space examples as pure as Monderman’s, but one of the first attempts can be found on Espanola Way in Miami Beach—just two blocks from the street that was unnecessarily widened a few pages ago.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Two blocks west of Espanola Way's widened section, a key intersection was redesigned without curbs and paved building face to building face (Espanola Plaza, around 2000). Earlier Speck cited the street's sidewalks being halved to widen the roadway.
- **Why it matters:** Both a negative example (sidewalks cut for wider lanes) and a positive one (America's early shared space), from Speck's own South Beach neighborhood.
