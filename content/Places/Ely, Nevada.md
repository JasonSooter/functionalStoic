# Ely, Nevada

Small high-desert town in White Pine County, eastern Nevada. In The Comfort Crisis (Michael Easter) it is the start of an elk hunt in the White Pine Mountains, Easter's first taste of chosen discomfort.

## The Comfort Crisis — Michael Easter (2021)

Source: [[The Comfort Crisis]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/634446/the-comfort-crisis-by-michael-easter/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fycyj8xtnrkjbs6ee9pqxg)

> I wound up in Ely, Nevada, a town whose elevation number is bigger than its population.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In fall 2017 Easter drives from Las Vegas across the Great Basin to Ely to meet Donnie Vincent, then heads up a rough dirt road toward the 10,000-foot peaks of the White Pine Mountains. They camp in a freezing meadow and spend days climbing ridges to glass for elk, ending 20 yards from a bull Donnie decides not to shoot.
- **Why it matters:** The Nevada trip is Easter's first real immersion in hardship and presence, and it sets up the Alaska trek; set somewhere tamer, the lesson about discomfort would not land.
