## Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World — David Epstein (2019)
Source: [[Range]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550188/range-by-david-epstein/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvtn578kq9jea759jpk6rm)

> Weather reports on January 27, 1986, predicted unusually cool Florida weather for launch. After the conference call, NASA and Thiokol gave the okay to proceed.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The night before launch, 34 engineers in three locations argued over O-ring data on an emergency call; Thiokol reversed its no-launch stance after an offline caucus. Challenger exploded 73 seconds after launch on January 28, 1986.
- **Why it matters:** The central case study of the chapter: NASA's quantitative culture failed to drop its familiar tools. The exact call locations are not named beyond a Thiokol boss in Utah.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The text gives "Florida" and the launch; Challenger flew from Kennedy Space Center's Launch Complex 39B.
