## North Woods — Daniel Mason (2023)
Source: [[North Woods]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL34028309W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvcd0q29cecny2yr7vw8d2)

> For nearly a decade, Professor Trumbull had taught a literature course at the state prison in Concord, part of an education and rehabilitation initiative.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Professor Trumbull teaches a literature course at the state prison in Concord, and the Women's Benevolence League pen-pal programme writes to his students. Lillian writes first to Henry Jones and then to 'Harlan Kane', who claims to be her son Robert; she is told not to visit. Later the prison library appears in Morris Lakeman's research.
- **Why it matters:** The prison is the offstage source of Kane's deception, which ends with his body found by the oak behind the house.
