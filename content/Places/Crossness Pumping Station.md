## How Did The World Get So Ugly? — The Cultural Tutor (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tWYxrowovts) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crf0bbpghp84pn5sv0weyq)

> It’s an old sewage facility

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The narrator shows an ornate building that looks like a palace or a church, then reveals it is a 19th-century pumping station from Bazalgette's sewer system, next to the plain modern station that replaced it; later he names it as the model offices and warehouses could follow.
- **Why it matters:** The key exhibit for the argument that even utilitarian buildings were once built with ornamental pride.
