## This Formerly Homeless Harvard Grad Is Using Her Story To Change How We Help The Homeless — Gila Lyons (2018)
Source: [Original](https://www.good.is/articles/homeless-to-harvard) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jkz0esdr9easymd1scjn68)

> Chung survived on the streets for six years before benefiting from Covenant House’s services, but when she was 18, she walked by the Hollywood chapter and pushed through the front doors.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** After six years on the streets, Connie Chung walked into the Hollywood chapter of Covenant House at 18 and stayed four months. She describes a safe bed, regular meals and staff who believed in her, and credits it with saving her life and giving her confidence to apply to UC Berkeley.
- **Why it matters:** It is the turning point of her story, the place where stability began. The text does not give a street address.
