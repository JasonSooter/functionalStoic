## Peace Corps: The Great Adventure — Peace Corps (2002)
Source: [[Peace Corps - The Great Adventure]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL16050643W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crc4bbwbnwj91gcvf68qtj)

> There are four-wheel-drive cars and pickup trucks here in Lastoursville, a small town on the train line about two degrees south of the equator.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bonnie Black, a community health Volunteer, writes 'The Fridge Factor' from her post in Lastoursville. She walks about a mile to the market every day, buying fresh food, chatting and mock-tangoing with the vegetable sellers.
- **Why it matters:** The whole essay is about daily life without a refrigerator in this equatorial town; its market is the heart of it.
