# British Embassy in Moscow

The British embassy in [[Moscow]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is the base of the small MI6 station that runs Gordievsky's escape.

|---|---|---|---|

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7xy5zjjw0kpqa8d3849qnr0)

> In the safe-speech room of the British embassy in Moscow, the personnel of the MI6 station gathered to rehearse an amateur dramatic performance.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In the embassy's safe-speech room the MI6 staff rehearse "an amateur dramatic performance" for the KGB's bugs: Rachel Gee's bad back, a story meant to be overheard. The ambassador's reception covers the team's preparations, and on escape day the embassy is where the final arrangements fall into place.
- **Why it matters:** The embassy is both shelter and stage: every word outside the safe room is assumed to be overheard, so the escape has to be acted as well as planned.
