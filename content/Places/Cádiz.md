# Cádiz

Cádiz is an ancient port city on the Atlantic coast of Andalusia, south-western Spain. In _Candide_ it is where the fugitives from Lisbon join a fleet sailing to fight the Jesuits of Paraguay.

## Candide — Voltaire (1759)
Source: [[Candide]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL100672W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m759ftdgcdfb3hs7h9nkem)

> Candide, Cunegonde, and the old woman, having passed through Lucena, Chillas, and Lebrixa, arrived at length at Cadiz. A fleet was there getting ready, and troops assembling

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After crossing the Sierra Morena, Candide, Cunegonde and the old woman reach Cadiz, where a fleet is being readied against the Jesuit Fathers of Paraguay. Candide shows the general his Bulgarian drill, is made captain of a company of foot, and sets sail with the two women, two valets and the Grand Inquisitor's two Andalusian horses. Cacambo, the valet he brings from Cadiz, later leads him into Paraguay.
- **Why it matters:** The port where the Old World half of the journey ends and the New World begins. Candide sails in the hope that the new world is the one where all is well.
