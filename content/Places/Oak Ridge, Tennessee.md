# Oak Ridge, Tennessee

City in eastern Tennessee built for the Manhattan Project's uranium plants. In _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_ Feynman is sent there to stop the plant from risking an accidental chain reaction.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfq7q5k7txttzrwjxayx3t)

> At any rate, I arrived at Oak Ridge. The first thing I did was have them take me to the plant, and I said nothing, I just looked at everything.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Sent from Los Alamos after Segrè's visit, Feynman tours the plant without saying anything, then explains the danger of neutrons in water and persuades the colonel to let him tell the engineers what the work is for. On a later visit he bluffs his way through blueprints by asking about a symbol that turns out to be a valve. He also opens safes in the plant's offices.
- **Why it matters:** The visit shows how army secrecy put the plant at risk and how explaining the purpose changed the work; it is one of the book's arguments against compartmentalisation.
