Source: [[Caution Has the Right of Way - Our SC Match-Day Service]] · [Original](https://blog.vag-freiburg.de/sc-verkehr/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bez50yqywc6dp893rd3qff)

> Later she drives past the airport to the Europa-Park Stadion stop, where the fans are waiting.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** On SC home-game days VAG runs trams at three-minute intervals to the stadium and back after the final whistle. Driver and instructor Corinne Albrecht, who has done "the stadium trips ever since the new stadium opened", describes the concentration it takes: crowds, signals, points and commands, with staff posted at all stops to keep people from straying between trams.
- **Why it matters:** The stadium stop is the destination that gives the shift its pressure; the article is about moving a crowd safely to one place.
