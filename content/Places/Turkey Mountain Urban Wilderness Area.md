## From the mayor’s office, a tone-deaf remark concerning development on Turkey Mountain — Bob Doucette (2015)
Source: [Original](https://proactiveoutside.wordpress.com/2015/11/13/from-the-mayors-office-a-tone-deaf-remark-concerning-development-on-turkey-mountain/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hk4xtx6frna3jxj73b704n)

> Turkey Mountain delivers no direct revenue to the city. But indirectly, it generates millions of dollars.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The essay answers the mayor's office floating a restaurant on top of Turkey Mountain, a wild area inside city limits used by hikers, runners and cyclists. Doucette recalls Simon Properties' outlet-mall plan at U.S. 75 and 61st Street on its western edge, which thousands of trail users opposed until Simon looked elsewhere, and he contrasts the mountain with the developed riverside at 18th Street and Riverside Drive.
- **Why it matters:** The mountain is the subject: the argument is that its value lies in staying wild.
