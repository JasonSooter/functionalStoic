> I’m writing this from a hotel in Budapest, looking out at a Danube that has burst her banks, reaching the highest water levels for a decade.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Russell writes from a Budapest hotel on the morning she is to speak at PszichoFest ahead of her book's Hungarian launch. The Danube has burst its banks, and she reflects on the city's history, food scene and political climate.
- **Why it matters:** The city's flooded, unsettled state frames her question of whether 'living Danishly' travels. It is the setting of the whole post.
