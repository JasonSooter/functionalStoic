## Every Metro System Should be this Beautiful — Not Just Bikes (2026)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WJCCDYbK0Gc) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5c3pj4p1w5wq27nq5hkqdtk)

> In contrast, Hötorget station, apologies

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The host says Hötorget leans into the 1950s subway-tile look, recreating the station as built in 1952 with the original typeface and signage. The red bins were redesigned from photographs in the 1997 renovation, and the neon ceiling lights are not original.
- **Why it matters:** It is the video's example of heritage restoration rather than new art.
