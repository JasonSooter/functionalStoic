# Urum al-Kubra

Village about twenty kilometres west of Aleppo, Syria. In _The Beekeeper of Aleppo_ it is the first stop on Nuri and Afra's flight to the Turkish border.

> We were heading to Urum al-Kubra, about twenty kilometres west of Aleppo.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Leaving the divided city past a billboard of Bashar al-Assad, the smuggler drives Nuri and Afra to Urum al-Kubra, where he waits for a mother and child. A man on a bicycle warns them of a sniper on a water tank, and they take another route.
- **Why it matters:** A short waypoint on the escape; a stranger's warning saves them.
