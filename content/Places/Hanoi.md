> I am currently in Hanoi. Nothing keeps me more humble than being in Asia.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author writes from Hanoi, where strangers in elevators comment on her weight, singleness, lack of a degree and tanned skin. She ends with her neighbor in the elevator lecturing her about weight as she collects delivery food.
- **Why it matters:** Hanoi supplies the social pressure that triggers her reflection on success; the argument would need another example elsewhere in Asia but the specific city matters little.
