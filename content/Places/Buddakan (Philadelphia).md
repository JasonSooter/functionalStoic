# Buddakan (Philadelphia)

Asian-fusion restaurant on Chestnut Street in Philadelphia's Old City. In How to Be an Antiracist (Ibram X. Kendi) a dinner there starts a conversation about uplift suasion.

## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g0et08yeaw1bt5shxjryap)

> We dined near the window at Buddakan, an Asian fusion restaurant in Old City, Philadelphia.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In autumn 2011 Kendi and Sadiqa watch a drunk White man fondle the restaurant's Buddha statue; her remark 'At least he's not Black' leads them to their first shared critique of uplift suasion.
- **Why it matters:** The dinner opens his argument against the idea that good Black behaviour defeats racism.
