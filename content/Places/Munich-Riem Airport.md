# Munich-Riem Airport

Munich's airport until 1992, east of the city centre. A Zapier piece on silence cites a study of schoolchildren near it before and after the airport moved.

## The Power of Silence: Why You Need Less Noise for Work and Your Health — Belle Cooper (2017)

Source: [Original](https://zapier.com/blog/silence-health-productivity/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5t818a9j951ms5zygn53r)

> looked at children who went to school near the Munich airport before and after the airport was relocated.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** A study compared children at schools near the old Munich airport and the new one. Reading comprehension and long-term memory worsened while the airport was near and improved after it moved away, while schools near the new site declined. Blood pressure and stress hormone effects persisted.
- **Why it matters:** Central case study showing noise harms cognition.
