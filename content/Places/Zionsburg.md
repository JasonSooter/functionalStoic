# Zionsburg

Country estate in Vught, North Brabant, in the south of the [[Netherlands]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where the kidnapped seven-year-old wakes up and is presented to his father's family as the heir.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zmpy593d4vv1gfw52sr5g)

> “Welcome to my castle!” he roared. “Welcome to Zionsburg!” His name was Ewald Marggraff, the brother of the woman who had kidnapped me.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After the abduction from [[Palenberg]], the boy wakes in a whitewashed room with Romanesque windows, is dressed in new clothes and brought out to a gathering of his father's family and allies under the beeches, where the eccentric Ewald Marggraff greets him. He learns that the Marggraff estate was chosen because it lay "less than an hour and a half from the border". When Mimousse approaches, he tells her she is not his mother.
- **Why it matters:** The estate is where the kidnapping becomes a fait accompli and the boy is formally claimed as the Münninghoff heir, the role that gives the memoir its title.
