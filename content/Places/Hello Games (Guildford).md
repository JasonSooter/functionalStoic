# Hello Games (Guildford)

Video-game studio in Guildford, Surrey, south-west of London. In Raffi Khatchadourian's New Yorker story it is where No Man's Sky is built.

## The Galaxy-Sized Video Game — Raffi Khatchadourian (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2015/05/18/world-without-end-raffi-khatchadourian) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g0ece9sdaqvttb6nv0q71m)

> The universe is being built in an old two-story building, in the town of Guildford, half an hour by train from London.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The story opens and largely unfolds in the studio's two-storey building: coders downstairs, a lounge with a demo TV upstairs, and morning stand-ups; a Christmas Eve flood once destroyed its equipment.
- **Why it matters:** The small studio is the contrast to the galaxy-sized game it is making.
