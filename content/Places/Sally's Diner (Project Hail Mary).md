## Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir (2021)
Source: [[Project Hail Mary]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/611060/project-hail-mary-by-andy-weir/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvs8ajydv82rpjnwkfn325)

> I sipped my coffee, popped the last fragment of toast into my mouth, and signaled the waitress for my check.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In the first flashback Grace eats his usual eggs-and-bacon breakfast here, reads Irina Petrova's email about the infrared line, and is reminded of the life he left behind in astronomy. The diner is where the Petrova line first reaches him.
- **Why it matters:** Establishes Grace's ordinary San Francisco routine before the crisis; minor but it is the setting of the first flashback.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The diner is invented. Grace remarks on "the good people of San Francisco" while sitting there, and his home and lab are in San Francisco (Stratt: "bring it directly here to San Francisco"). Pinned at the San Francisco city point; no address is given.
