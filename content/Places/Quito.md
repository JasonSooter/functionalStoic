# Quito

Quito is the capital of Ecuador, high in the Andes near the equator. In _Moby-Dick_ it is the mint of the gold doubloon Ahab nails to the mainmast.

## Moby-Dick; or, The Whale — Herman Melville (1851)
Source: [[Exploration of Moby Dick]] · [[Moby Dick]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL102749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m77e16rrda0jbkcwg2nb7w)

> On its round border it bore the letters, REPUBLICA DEL ECUADOR: QUITO.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In Chapter 99 the gold doubloon Ahab nailed to the mainmast as the prize for sighting Moby Dick bears 'REPUBLICA DEL ECUADOR: QUITO', and Ishmael notes it came from a country beneath the equator, cast midway up the Andes. Ahab, Starbuck, Stubb, Flask, the Manxman, Queequeg, Fedallah and Pip in turn study its three peaks with a flame, a tower and a crowing cock, and Ahab reads all three as himself.
- **Why it matters:** The place is never visited; its equatorial, Andean origin gives the coin its imagery. The coin is the book's test of how each man reads the world.
