# Frankfurt (Main) Hauptbahnhof

Frankfurt's main railway station. In _On the Hippie Trail_ it is where Rick Steves finally meets his travel partner Gene at Track 13 to start their 1978 overland trip to Kathmandu.

## On the Hippie Trail — Rick Steves (2025)

Source: [[On the Hippie Trail]] · [Original](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/rick-steves/on-the-hippie-trail/9781641717458/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kje8n5etpscxbhsw760jc6)

> Tail between my legs, I returned to Frankfurt’s train station to our backup meeting place—lucky Track 13. And that’s where Gene was waiting for me.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Arriving from Helsinki, Rick waits an hour at Frankfurt airport for Gene without finding him, then goes to the station's backup meeting point, Track 13, where Gene is waiting. From here they take the train east towards Yugoslavia and Bulgaria.
- **Why it matters:** The start of the overland journey and of the partnership that frames the book.
