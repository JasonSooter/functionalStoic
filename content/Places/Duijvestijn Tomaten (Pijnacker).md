## This Tiny Country Feeds the World — Magazine (2017)

Source: [Original](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/magazine/2017/09/holland-agriculture-sustainable-farming/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7gaqpqg9s8hwyf9pjxpns)

> At the Duijvestijns’ 36-acre greenhouse complex near the old city of Delft, visitors stroll among ranks of deep green tomato vines, 20 feet tall.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** A self-contained, resource-independent tomato farm using geothermal heat, rainwater, basalt-fiber rooting and predatory mites, held up as a model of sustainable farming.
- **Why it matters:** The greenhouse is the article's model of a closed-loop, high-yield farm.
