# Legoland Billund

## The Year of Living Danishly — Helen Russell (2015)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20000553W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f1vf6j88a06059znktvtty)

> This is the highlight of the Jutland calendar: the closing of Legoland for winter.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In October, with Lego Man away, Russell goes to the park's end-of-season party alone in the rain. Pregnant and unable to ride, she holds coats; sparklers are lit and the fireworks only half work, and a neighbour warns her about the winter ahead. She calls Legoland the sole attraction in her corner of Denmark.
- **Why it matters:** It marks the start of the dark Jutland winter in the book's year.
