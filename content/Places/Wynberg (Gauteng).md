# Wynberg

Industrial suburb of Johannesburg, just west of [[Alexandra (Johannesburg)|Alexandra]]. In Born a Crime the family's car workshop, Mighty Mechanics, moves here, and for close to a year they live inside it.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zbjsp108nqnwbsmh78z78n)

> They moved the workshop from Yeoville to the industrial area of Wynberg, just west of Alex, and Mighty Mechanics became the new family business.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 18 ("My Mother's Life"). Trevor's mother uses her savings to help Abel buy Mighty Mechanics, the garage near Trevor's father's house in [[Yeoville]] where she first met him, and they move it to Wynberg. The business turns out to carry heavy debts. The family ends up living in the warehouse: concrete floors stained with oil, a drywall office by the roller door, a kitchenette and a janitor's sink to wash in. Trevor sleeps in customers' cars, works in the garage instead of doing homework, and for weeks they eat mopane worms.
- **Why it matters:** this is the low point of the family's money, and it comes from the mother tying her fortunes to Abel. The business's failure feeds his drinking and the violence that follows.
