# Camps Bay

Seaside suburb of [[Cape Town]] on the Atlantic coast. In Born a Crime it is where Trevor Noah, at twenty-four, finds his estranged father again.

## At a glance

| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6z18n05eengbq1qfqjfp5pq)

> He gave me his address in Cape Town, in a neighborhood called Camps Bay, and a few months later I went down to visit.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 8 ("Robert"). His father left Johannesburg for Cape Town when Trevor was thirteen, and they lost touch. At twenty-four Trevor writes to him through the Swiss embassy, gets a reply and an address, and goes down to visit. Walking down a street of "older, semiretired white people", he searches the face of every old white man until he finds his father. His father cooks the same Sunday meal as in Yeoville and shows him a scrapbook of every newspaper mention of Trevor's career.
- **Why it matters:** the reunion closes the father thread of the memoir. The scrapbook shows that his father had been following him all along.
