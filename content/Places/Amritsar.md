## It's All About the Bike — Robert Penn (2010)

Source: [[It's All About the Bike]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21030895W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bs7gd9857f7d9ax4w3hd16)

> I was slipstreaming a tractor on the Grand Trunk Road near Amritsar.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 1. Recalling his ride round the world, Penn hits a pothole on the Grand Trunk Road and bends the top and down tubes of his steel bike; a local mechanic in Amritsar bashes the frame straight for 100 rupees and a packet of cigarettes.
- **Why it matters:** It is his evidence for choosing a steel frame: it can be fixed anywhere. Set elsewhere the point would stand, but the remote road makes it vivid.
