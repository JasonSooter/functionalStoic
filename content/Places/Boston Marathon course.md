## The biggest threat facing middle-age men isn’t smoking or obesity. It’s loneliness. — BostonGlobe.com (2017)
Source: [Original](https://www.bostonglobe.com/magazine/2017/03/09/the-biggest-threat-facing-middle-age-men-isn-smoking-obesity-loneliness/k6saC9FnnHQCUbf5mJ8okL/story.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5rczwc47heceyhnwtjpff)

> We signed up to run last April’s Boston Marathon together, and even though he lives in Chicago, we were in regular contact about our training, his trip to Boston, etc., and our relationship became stronger than ever, even though our best and deepest conversation occurred during the four-plus hours it took us to get from Hopkinton to Boston, side by side.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** The author and his college friend Matt ran the Boston Marathon together. Their best conversation happened side by side on the run from Hopkinton to Boston, illustrating that men bond through a shared activity.
- **Why it matters:** The marathon route is where the key bonding occurs and illustrates the article's thesis.
