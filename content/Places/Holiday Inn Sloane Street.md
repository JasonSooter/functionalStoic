# Holiday Inn Sloane Street

Hotel on Sloane Street in Chelsea, [[London]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is where Gordievsky first meets his MI6 handlers in London, in 1982.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7rsbmhtb0edgkx2jp8eyhdw)

> The Holiday Inn on Sloane Street had a good claim to be London’s most boring hotel. Its sole distinction was to host the annual Slimmer of the Year competition.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** At 3 p.m. Gordievsky walks into the lobby and spots Geoffrey Guscott, sitting beside Veronica Price, who has worked on his case for five years but never seen him except in photographs. Without eye contact the two handlers walk out the back of the hotel, and he follows them to a waiting car that takes him to the safe flat.
- **Why it matters:** It is the moment the case moves from years of silence to the London operation. The hotel was chosen precisely because Russian spies were unlikely to be there.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real hotel. ⚠️ The pin marks Sloane Street, not the hotel building.
