# Morocco

Morocco is a country in north-west Africa. In Voltaire's _Candide_ it is the backdrop of the old woman's tale of captivity and civil war.

## Candide — Voltaire (1759)

Source: [[Candide]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL100672W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75a5kv6npccmx7a46306f)

> Morocco swam in blood when we arrived.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** In the old woman's story, corsairs carry her and her mother to Morocco while fifty sons of the Emperor Muley-Ismael fight fifty civil wars. Rival factions fight over the captives and tear her mother apart; she survives under a heap of the dead.
- **Why it matters:** An embedded story within the larger tale. It is one of the misfortunes the old woman uses to show Cunegonde that others have suffered worse.
