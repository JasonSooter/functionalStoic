# Waterford Flight

Series of five locks at Waterford, New York, where the modern Erie Canal leaves the Hudson. The Erie Canal article calls it one of the steepest lock flights in the world.

> The Waterford Flight series of locks (comprising Locks E2 through E6) is one of the steepest in the world, lifting boats 169 feet (52 m) in less than 2 miles (3.2 km).

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article describes the Waterford Flight as the steepest series of locks in the United States, where the modern Erie Canal begins at the Hudson and Mohawk confluence, lifting boats 169 feet in under two miles.
- **Why it matters:** A notable lock feature at the canal's eastern start.
