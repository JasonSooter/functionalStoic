## The Best-Designed Town in the Netherlands (and therefore, the world) — Not Just Bikes

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r-TuGAHR78w) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5cre5bw21a8f4fb7h3z4y1j)

> The train station here is called Castellum

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the video:** The station of Houten's second phase is styled as a Roman fort with a moat and bridges; the narrator later criticises the cramped area around it and the car access to its parking.
- **Why it matters:** A concrete example of the second phase's showier architecture and of its design flaws; minor.
