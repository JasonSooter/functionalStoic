## Outdoors in Arkansas: Hiking Devil’s Den State Park — Cary Aspinwall (2015)
Source: [Original](https://proactiveoutside.wordpress.com/2015/12/01/outdoors-in-arkansas-hiking-devils-den-state-park/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hk5nf9gwcn6m67sxhr7d5a)

> After talking to the park staff, we decided to start out with the beautiful Devil’s Den trail right by the visitor’s center (it’s a 1.5 mile loop, but it has some steep elevation changes).

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The writer and her friend Courtney pick the park, south of Fayetteville, for their annual destination hike. They walk the Devil's Den loop past closed bat caves and waterfalls, then a ranger steers them to the nearly six-mile Fossil Flats loop along Lee Creek, where they search a dry creek bed for fossils.
- **Why it matters:** The park is the article's subject: well-marked trails and helpful rangers, the opposite of their trip a year earlier.
