# Juchipila

Town in the Juchipila canyon of southern Zacatecas. In The Underdogs it is hailed as the cradle of the revolution and seen at the end in ruins.

## The Underdogs: A Novel of the Mexican Revolution — Mariano Azuela (1915)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1209983W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8zbj9pdm48kswz0atzgyb)

> Juchipila rose in the distance, white, bathed in sunlight, shining in the midst of a thick forest at the foot of a proud, lofty mountain, pleated like a turban.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The army enters Juchipila during the Sacred Heart fiesta and finds it ruined, hungry and cold to them. Seen from the canyon, it moves Valderrama to kiss the ground and call it the cradle of the 1910 revolution; the novel ends in an ambush in the canyon above it, with Demetrio still aiming his rifle.
- **Why it matters:** The town where the revolt began is now where it is seen to have failed, and the welcome has gone cold.
