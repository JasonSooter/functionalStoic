# Warrensburg, Missouri

College town in west-central Missouri. In Quiet (Susan Cain) it is where young Dale Carnegie becomes a campus speaking champion.

## Quiet — Susan Cain (2012)
Source: [[Quiet]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/55983/quiet-by-cain-susan/9780141970172) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g0favxj72hrnnf8cxchp35)

> His family moves to a farm three miles outside of Warrensburg, Missouri, so he can attend college there without paying room and board.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Carnegie's family moves near Warrensburg so he can attend the college; he loses speaking contests, then becomes a champion and campus hero before leaving to sell.
- **Why it matters:** Part of Cain's account of how the Extrovert Ideal took hold.
