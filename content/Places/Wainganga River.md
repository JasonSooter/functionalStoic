# Wainganga River

River of central India flowing south through Seoni district. In _The Jungle Book_ it is Shere Khan's hunting ground, and its ravine is where Mowgli has the buffalo herds trample the tiger.

## The Jungle Book — Rudyard Kipling (1894)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19870W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne2rb2f896gxkax6aq8zdq)

> Shere Khan was the tiger who lived near the Waingunga River, twenty miles away.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Shere Khan's home range is by the Waingunga, twenty miles from the wolves' cave. In 'Tiger! Tiger!' Gray Brother reports the full-fed tiger lying up in the big dry ravine of the Waingunga where it opens on the plain; Mowgli circles the buffalo herd to the head of the ravine and stampedes the bulls down it while Akela's herd blocks the foot, and Shere Khan is trampled to death.
- **Why it matters:** The ravine is the trap that settles the book's central feud; its sheer walls are what make Mowgli's plan work.
