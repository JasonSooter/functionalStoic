## Not the End of the World — Hannah Ritchie (2024)

Source: [[Not the End of the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL37518722W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edgt8qkcqcpyh067w7b8vf)

> As I write this, it’s creating havoc for farmers across Sri Lanka.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Ritchie uses Sri Lanka's 2021 ban on fertiliser imports, an attempt to go organic overnight, as a real-world experiment. Food production plummeted, vegetable prices rose more than five-fold, and the government was trying to reverse the policy.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's concrete case against a blanket move to organic farming, and a cautionary example. The place carries the argument as evidence, so another country would change the facts.
