# Sri Lanka

Island country in South Asia. It appears in the reading as Hannah Ritchie's example of an overnight switch to organic farming, and as the place where Emmanuel Carrère lived through the 2004 tsunami in David Brooks's _How to Know a Person_.

## Not the End of the World — Hannah Ritchie (2024)
Source: [[Not the End of the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL37518722W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edgt8qkcqcpyh067w7b8vf)

> As I write this, it’s creating havoc for farmers across Sri Lanka.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Ritchie uses Sri Lanka's 2021 ban on fertiliser imports, an attempt to go organic overnight, as a real-world experiment. Food production plummeted, vegetable prices rose more than five-fold, and the government was trying to reverse the policy.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's concrete case against a blanket move to organic farming, and a cautionary example. The place carries the argument as evidence, so another country would change the facts.

## How to Know a Person — David Brooks (2023)
Source: [[How to Know a Person]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/652822/how-to-know-a-person-by-david-brooks/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8g97em0rqabyn859xhtrt6e)

> On December 26, 2004, the French writer Emmanuel Carrère; his girlfriend, Hélène; and their respective sons were vacationing at a cliff-top hotel in Sri Lanka.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 5 retells Carrère's memoir. The couple had talked about separating the night before; that morning a four-year-old, Juliette, is on the beach with her grandfather when the wave hits, and she is killed. Carrère searches the nearby hospital for her body, and the two families eat together in the days after. Watching Hélène care for the grieving family, he sees her anew, and they stay together.
- **Why it matters:** Brooks uses it to show how a person can suddenly be seen differently, and how disaster reveals character. The tsunami is the event itself, so the story is bound to this coast; the hotel, beach and hospital are not named.
- **Real vs. fictional:** real (Carrère's memoir _Lives Other Than My Own_). ⚠️ Neither Brooks nor the reviews checked name the hotel or the town, so the section sits on the country note.
