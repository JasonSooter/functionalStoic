## How Mentors and Scholarships Help Community College Students Succeed — Holly Korbey (2016)

Source: [Original](https://ww2.kqed.org/mindshift/2016/06/28/how-mentors-and-scholarships-help-community-college-students-succeed/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jx5rk142qvs4j4bw7h8qsv)

> We knew that one-third of students were graduating from the Knox County school system and not pursuing any kind of post-secondary education directly following high school,

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** The Tennessee Promise last-dollar scholarship began in Knoxville, as KnoxAchieves, launched in 2009 by the mayor and local businessmen to bring a skilled workforce to the area. They found a third of Knox County graduates skipped post-secondary education, so they paired private-funded tuition with volunteer mentors.
- **Why it matters:** It is the origin point of the program the article is about, which later spread statewide and inspired a national proposal.
