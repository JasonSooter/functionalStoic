## This Legendary Night Train is FINALLY bookable ! — Simply Railway (2026)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hhGnBrXlmbQ) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5c3pbr2pm56t0p822s08geb)

> In the past (and in my previous video), this was done in Kapıkule, but it’s now done in Edirne.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the video:** Border control for the crossing into Bulgaria now happens at Edirne rather than Kapıkule. The narrator says the station is being completely rebuilt for the coming high-speed line; passengers go through passport control and the train waits almost three hours.
- **Why it matters:** It is the night's main interruption and the reason the timetable is so long.
