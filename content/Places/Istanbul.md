## Candide — Voltaire (1759)

Source: [[Candide]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL100672W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m764zjk0e0tsm4z5rmqbwf)

> The faithful Cacambo had already prevailed upon the Turkish skipper, who was to conduct the Sultan Achmet to Constantinople, to receive Candide and Martin on his ship.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Cacambo has told Candide that Cunegonde is at Constantinople, and they sail there with the deposed Sultan Achmet. At the Bosphorus Candide ransoms Cacambo and boards a galley to search the Propontis, where two badly rowing slaves turn out to be Pangloss and Cunegonde's brother; he buys them free. From the farm, Cacambo later carries vegetables to sell in the city, and news comes of viziers strangled there.
- **Why it matters:** The last city of the journey, where the scattered cast is gathered. The capital's politics then become the distant backdrop to the farm.
