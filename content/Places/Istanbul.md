## Attending MicroConf Europe 2025 — Herman (Bear Blog) (2025)

Source: [Original](https://herman.bearblog.dev/microconf-europe/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce3scjxsx7v543csxq7ve3)

> I arrived a week early to explore the city and see the sights.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author arrives a week early for MicroConf Europe to explore: the old city, mosques seen from the Bosporus, and the tourist-area scams he warns about. The conference hotel's hammam, steam room and Marmara-facing terrace are where the shop talk happens.
- **Why it matters:** The city is the setting of the whole post.

## Candide — Voltaire (1759)

Source: [[Candide]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL100672W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m764zjk0e0tsm4z5rmqbwf)

> The faithful Cacambo had already prevailed upon the Turkish skipper, who was to conduct the Sultan Achmet to Constantinople, to receive Candide and Martin on his ship.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Cacambo has told Candide that Cunegonde is at Constantinople, and they sail there with the deposed Sultan Achmet. At the Bosphorus Candide ransoms Cacambo and boards a galley to search the Propontis, where two badly rowing slaves turn out to be Pangloss and Cunegonde's brother; he buys them free. From the farm, Cacambo later carries vegetables to sell in the city, and news comes of viziers strangled there.
- **Why it matters:** The last city of the journey, where the scattered cast is gathered. The capital's politics then become the distant backdrop to the farm.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7641y1ewtq266kwgvd8dgg5)

> Ride a bike in Istanbul? Are you nuts?

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Byrne answers his own question: the traffic is chaotic and the hills steep, but the streets are so congested that by bike he beats cars around the centre, keeping to the shore roads. He loves the city's setting across three landmasses and its cosmopolitan feel, and regrets that old wooden houses are being replaced by bland concrete blocks. The chapter moves between the [[Pera Palace Hotel]], the [[Sakıp Sabancı Museum|Sabancı mansion]], [[Sulukule]] and [[Topkapı Palace]], around a music festival that is cancelled and revived.
- **Why it matters:** Istanbul is where he makes his point that cycling is held back less by terrain than by status: almost no one else is on a bike.
