## Westerners think that Buddhism is about peace and non-violence. So how come Buddhist monks are in arms against Islam? — Michael Jerryson

Source: [Original](https://aeon.co/essays/buddhism-can-be-as-violent-as-any-other-religion) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7degfxyh2248gczqaxr7p)

> On 11 June 1963, the Vietnamese Buddhist monk Thich Quang Duc self-immolated outside the Cambodian embassy in the city of Saigon in Vietnam.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Journalists were notified in advance and Malcolm Browne photographed the self-immolation, which became an enduring image of Buddhist protest. The author argues it was a protest against the pro-Catholic Diem administration, not the war, i.e. a defence of Buddhism.
- **Why it matters:** Used to rebut the Western reading of the event as pacifism.
