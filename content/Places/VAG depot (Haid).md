# VAG depot (Haid)

The Freiburger Verkehrs AG's depot and workshops in the Haid industrial area on Freiburg's western edge. Two VAG blog posts go inside it: one to the workshop halls where the city's buses and trams are serviced, the other to the bus-school stop and tram hall where children learn to ride safely.

## From servicing to repairs: The tasks of the VAG workshop are diverse — VAG Freiburg (2026)
Source: [Original](https://blog.vag-freiburg.de/von-wartungen-bis-reparaturen-so-vielfaeltig-sind-die-aufgaben-der-vag-werkstatt/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6rxkg0x9xqdtpqa2thfnbm4)

> Work like this is routine at VAG: in the two large workshop halls at the depot on the Haid, many buses and trams are repaired, serviced and maintained at the same time every day.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** A look round the workshops with Michael Zausch, head of workshops and vehicle technology. Every day four to five buses and one to two trams come in with faults; accident repairs can take weeks because e-bus spare parts are slow to arrive. Every vehicle gets a full-day service every three to four months, buses an annual main inspection, and each tram a four-to-eight-week strip-down every eight years. The electric buses, about a third of the fleet as of early 2025, are where the staff say they are "doing pioneering work".
- **Why it matters:** The depot is the whole setting: the piece is about the unseen work there that keeps the network running. ⚠️ The article says only "the depot on the Haid"; the pin is VAG's site at Besançonallee 99, which OpenStreetMap places in St. Georgen Nord, by the Haid.

## Safety Training for Children: First to Bus School, Then onto the School Bus — VAG Freiburg (2025)
Source: [[Safety Training for Children - First to Bus School, Then Onto the School Bus]] · [Original](https://blog.vag-freiburg.de/sicherheitstraining-fuer-kinder-erst-zur-busschule-dann-in-den-schulbus/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7efjd8kexae1ggr8tm3mzdx)

> The spots for the roughly two-hour traffic-safety training on the VAG operating grounds are in high demand, with dates often booked up half a year in advance (registration see below).

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Former bus driver Matthias Wölke runs VAG's bus school, about 50 two-hour sessions a year for kindergarten and primary-school children, around 20,000 so far. At the depot's dedicated bus-school stop he drives over a row of cones to show why children must stay behind the white line, and slams on the brakes so school bags pile up at the front of the 18-metre bus. In the tram hall he brakes a tram to show its much longer stopping distance and the braking sand between the rails.
- **Why it matters:** The depot is where the lesson happens: a real stop, a real bus and a real tram hall make the forces vivid in a way a classroom couldn't. The programme matters because around 60 percent of Freiburg's children ride buses and trams to school, often from first grade. ⚠️ The article says only "the VAG operating grounds" and "the VAG depot"; it is placed here as VAG's depot site, and its publication year is inferred from the photo dates (October 2025).
