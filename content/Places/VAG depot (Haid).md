# VAG depot (Haid)

The Freiburger Verkehrs AG's depot and workshops in the Haid industrial area on Freiburg's western edge. A VAG blog post goes inside its two workshop halls, where the city's buses and trams are repaired and serviced.

## From servicing to repairs: The tasks of the VAG workshop are diverse — VAG Freiburg (2026)
Source: [Original](https://blog.vag-freiburg.de/von-wartungen-bis-reparaturen-so-vielfaeltig-sind-die-aufgaben-der-vag-werkstatt/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6rxkg0x9xqdtpqa2thfnbm4)

> Work like this is routine at VAG: in the two large workshop halls at the depot on the Haid, many buses and trams are repaired, serviced and maintained at the same time every day.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** A look round the workshops with Michael Zausch, head of workshops and vehicle technology. Every day four to five buses and one to two trams come in with faults; accident repairs can take weeks because e-bus spare parts are slow to arrive. Every vehicle gets a full-day service every three to four months, buses an annual main inspection, and each tram a four-to-eight-week strip-down every eight years. The electric buses, about a third of the fleet as of early 2025, are where the staff say they are "doing pioneering work".
- **Why it matters:** The depot is the whole setting: the piece is about the unseen work there that keeps the network running. ⚠️ The article says only "the depot on the Haid"; the pin is VAG's site at Besançonallee 99, which OpenStreetMap places in St. Georgen Nord, by the Haid.
