# Navarino Bay

Navarino Bay is the bay at Pylos in the south-west Peloponnese, Greece, where the Battle of Navarino was fought in 1827. _Sapiens_ uses the battle to show armies protecting investors.

## At a glance
| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind | Yuval Noah Harari | turning point | Where a British-led fleet sank the Ottoman navy in 1827, freeing Greece and protecting London bondholders. |

## Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind — Yuval Noah Harari (2014)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17075811W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m77355ve3enc2vjfbkvdny)

> The bondholders’ interest was the national interest, so the British organised an international fleet that, in 1827, sank the main Ottoman flotilla in the Battle of Navarino.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Greek rebellion bonds were traded in London while the Turks were winning. To protect the bondholders, Britain organised an international fleet that sank the Ottoman flotilla at Navarino in 1827; Greece became free but mortgaged to British creditors for decades.
- **Why it matters:** It shows war and credit fused; the text gives the place itself no description.
