## Rural America Is Building Its Own Internet Because No One Else Will — Vice (2017)

Source: [Original](https://motherboard.vice.com/en_us/article/paax9n/rural-america-is-building-its-own-internet-because-no-one-else-will) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7fwbttft83cdzr34v5hxb)

> Garrett County is located at the most western reach of Maryland's panhandle. It sits just below the Mason-Dixon line, smack dab in the Appalachian Mountains.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The mountainous county, with under 60 percent broadband in 2011, uses unused TV white space, 5GHz wireless and antennas on barns, silos and trees to reach residents; over 150 new homes and businesses connected in a year.
- **Why it matters:** One of the three counties profiled.
