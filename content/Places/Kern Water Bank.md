## Meet the California Couple Who Uses More Water Than Every Home in Los Angeles Combined — Josh Harkinson (2016)
Source: [Original](http://www.motherjones.com/environment/2016/04/lynda-stewart-resnick-california-water) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jx9d273gsmz6fc1eyfsb8n)

> In exchange, they got ownership of the Kern Water Bank, a naturally occurring underground reservoir that lies beneath 32 square miles of Kern County, which sits toward the southern end of the Central Valley.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** In a 1995 deal after the Delta smelt listing, the Resnicks traded junior 'paper water' rights for the bank and gained a majority vote on its board. They used it to store and resell water at a premium, and a court later found the transfer lacked environmental review.
- **Why it matters:** The mechanism behind the couple's water power; the legal fight is over public ownership of this site.
