# Lubyanka Building

Former insurance palace on Lubyanka Square in central [[Moscow]], headquarters of the KGB. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is "the Center", where Gordievsky starts his career forging identities for illegals.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7p6zeracdpnfzt0vq822kpk)

> At its heart stood the sinister Lubyanka, a neo-Baroque palace originally built for the All-Russia Insurance Company, whose basement housed the KGB torture cells.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On August 20, 1963, Gordievsky reports for work at headquarters and is put in the German section of Directorate S, filling in forms and forging identities for illegals. He feels like a "galley slave". Here he meets KGB legends, among them Molody ("Gordon Lonsdale") and Fisher (Rudolf Abel).
- **Why it matters:** It establishes the institution he will betray and the gap between its myth and its dreary routine.
