# Nikola (Landshut)

Landshut's oldest city-extension district, between the old town and the main station in [[Landshut]], Bavaria. In the BBSR's 15-minute-city report it is the dense, well-served case-study quarter of a mid-sized city.

## Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde — Björn Schwarze et al. (BBSR) (2025)
Source: [[Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde]] · [Original](https://www.bbsr.bund.de/BBSR/DE/veroeffentlichungen/bbsr-online/2025/bbsr-online-027-2025-dl.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7kmj1fmz9yhgp1bnset2rym)

> Der Stadtteil Nikola als ältestes Stadterweiterungsgebiet ist zentral gelegen zwischen der Altstadt und dem Hauptbahnhof.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** About 11,500 people live on roughly 140 ha, at 8,200 per km² by far the densest part of Landshut, and car ownership is about 17 % below the city average. The accessibility model rates it a 5-minute city with very high fulfilment.
- **Why it matters:** Paired with Wolfgang, it shows how much proximity within one mid-sized city depends on density and age of the quarter.
