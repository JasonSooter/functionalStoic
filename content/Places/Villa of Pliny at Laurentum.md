## Pliny the Younger on Happy and Honorable Seclusion — calnewport.com (2023)
Source: [Original](https://www.calnewport.com/blog/2023/02/12/pliny-the-younger-on-happy-and-honorable-seclusion/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8vwfb54ssrq70h4knhx71)

> It turns out that Pliny maintained a rambling villa on the sea, southwest of Rome.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Pliny's letter praises Laurentum as a place free of gossip and distraction where he reads, writes and exercises. The article describes his seaside villa, with a separate Retreat reached by a covered arcade and shaped like a Greek cross so he could follow the light while writing. Pliny calls the sea and shore his private Helicon.
- **Why it matters:** The villa is the concrete place where Pliny's seclusion for focused work happened.
