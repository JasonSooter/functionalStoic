# Hado (Jeju)

Coastal village in Gujwa-eup on the north-east coast of Jeju Island, South Korea. It is the home village of Young-sook and Mi-ja in _The Island of Sea Women_.

## The Island of Sea Women — Lisa See (2019)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19865296W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvcgc9bxqy41rn7a08wxc1)

> I’d grown up in the Gul-dong section of Hado, while Mi-ja lived in the Sut-dong section, and the two of us always met in this spot.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Hado is Young-sook's village in 1938, with stone-walled olles, a central square and village tree, and seven bulteoks for seven diving collectives. Here Young-sook and Mi-ja join her mother's collective, and in the underwater canyon off the shore Yu-ri is crippled after Young-sook's greed; the Hado haenyeo join the protest march to Pyeongdae. Young-sook flees back here after the Bukchon massacre and lives on in her beachside compound, where in 2008 she reads Mi-ja's letters and agrees to teach her great-granddaughters to dive.
- **Why it matters:** Hado is the novel's home ground and its frame; the village's rules, stigma and sea shape the friendship from first dive to last.
