## The Soul of an Octopus — Sy Montgomery (2015)
Source: [[The Soul of an Octopus]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL27182716M/The_Soul_of_an_Octopus) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g0enh2mkpcb6z73zq0mywe)

> Jackie pulls up to the airport’s rst FedEx bay, and Dave goes inside to inquire after our cargo.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In January the narrator rides with aquarists Dave and Jackie to the FedEx bay to collect a giant Pacific octopus shipped from British Columbia; Jackie recalls earlier customs fiascos with other animals at Logan.
- **Why it matters:** The pickup brings Karma, the next octopus in the story, to Boston.
