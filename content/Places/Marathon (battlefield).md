## Financial Independence is Not a Marathon — MaggieBanks (2016)
Source: [Original](https://northernexpenditure.com/financial-independence-not-marathon-sprint/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvp3adf5rxdpq0rgb8tdh9)

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The author retells the origin of the marathon: after the Battle of Marathon the messenger Pheidippides ran to Athens to announce victory and died. She uses this to argue the marathon is a poor metaphor for financial independence.
- **Why it matters:** Origin point of the metaphor under attack; engaged as history, not as a visited place.
