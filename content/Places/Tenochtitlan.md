# Tenochtitlan

Tenochtitlan was the island capital of the Aztec Empire, on the site of today's Mexico City. In _Sapiens_ it is where Cortés captured Montezuma and conquered the empire.

## Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind — Yuval Noah Harari (2014)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17075811W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m76b93vfwhxm41yw2yef4f)

> He then marched towards the Aztec capital, the great metropolis of Tenochtitlan.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Cortés was led to Montezuma and, mid-interview, had him seized and held in the palace, so the centralised empire obeyed the Spaniards. The Aztec elite later revolted and drove them out, but Cortés returned with tens of thousands of local allies and took the city. Earlier, the money chapter opens on the same invasion, with the Aztecs puzzled by the Spaniards' craving for gold.
- **Why it matters:** It is the centre of the paralysed empire in Harari's account of how a few hundred Spaniards toppled it.
