## Stranger in a Strange Land — Robert A. Heinlein (1961)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL59688W)

> a few minutes later the driver left them on the roof of the New Mayflower.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After the meeting at the Executive Palace, a bus takes the party to the hotel roof and they hole up in a guarded suite. Over a lunch brought from the Poconos, Jubal, the Champion officers and Caxton discuss grokking, Mike's wealth, the Larkin rights and the missing Dr. Agnew; Douglas's signed papers arrive and they check out at once.
- **Why it matters:** A long private scene in which the deal over Mike's estate is sealed.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The 'New Mayflower' is a future hotel in the novel. No highlight: Reader could not anchor any span in this book.
