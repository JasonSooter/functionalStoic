# World Logistics Center (Moreno Valley)

Planned 2,610-acre warehouse complex on the east edge of Moreno Valley, Riverside County, California. A Wired piece uses the fight over it to show how the Inland Empire became a land of mega-warehouses.

## How the 'Orange Empire' Became the Land of MegaWarehouses — Emma Foehringer Merchant (2017)
Source: [Original](https://www.wired.com/2017/05/orange-empire-became-land-megawarehouses/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7bgqqmhh179by7v1ygjbk)

> The World Logistics Center, planned by a company called Highland Fairview, would be the largest such facility in the country, covering 2,610 acres—the size of 700 football fields.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The proposed 2,610-acre warehouse complex is the central subject: the city rezoned a dirt patch beside resident Tom Thornsley's house, approved the project 3-2 in August 2015, and opponents are fighting it in court and at a special council election over the pollution and truck traffic it would bring.
- **Why it matters:** The fight over this one project is the article's way into the warehouse boom and its costs for neighbours.
