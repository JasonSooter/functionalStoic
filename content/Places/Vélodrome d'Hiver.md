# Vélodrome d'Hiver

Former indoor velodrome in the 15th arrondissement of [[Paris]], demolished in 1959. It appears in Robert Penn's _It's All About the Bike_ in an aside about Hemingway editing proofs at the six-day races.

## It's All About the Bike — Robert Penn (2010)
Source: [[It's All About the Bike - In Pursuit of Happiness on Two Wheels]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21030895W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce6gjxp5eywjjs2gpps91v)

> He attended many six-day races in Paris in the 1920s, and edited the proof of

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Penn notes that Hemingway went to many six-day races in Paris in the 1920s and edited the proofs of A Farewell to Arms in a box at the Vélodrome d'Hiver in 1929, later writing that the races were better than any story he had written about them.
- **Why it matters:** A brief literary aside; minor in the book.
