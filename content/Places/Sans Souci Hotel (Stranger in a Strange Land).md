## Stranger in a Strange Land — Robert A. Heinlein (1961)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL59688W)

> Sans Souci Hotel, Gem of the Gulf, whose management is in no way responsible for this hunted

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After the Temple burns, the Nest hides in four top-floor suites of a big beach hotel that Mike owns through dummies; Jubal arrives and has his last long talk with Mike. Mike then walks out onto the grass in front of the 'Sans Souci Hotel, Gem of the Gulf', faces the mob, and is stoned, shot and beaten to death; Jubal later orders the spot bought as a shrine.
- **Why it matters:** The climax of the novel happens here.
- **Real vs. fictional:** No highlight: Reader could not anchor any span in this book.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Clues: a newscast says Mike has come out of 'a hotel room here in beautiful St. Petersburg, the City that Has Everything'; the hotel is the 'Sans Souci Hotel, Gem of the Gulf'; ads mention land reclaimed from the Gulf. The text does not say which St. Petersburg, but the Gulf, palms and 'subtropical resort city in the southern part of the same nation' point to St. Petersburg, Florida. The hotel has no real counterpart; pinned at the city centre.
