## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjr8m860qcbf98eswt5j3q)

> On the night of the murder, Zuaiter took two buses across Rome from the house of his girlfriend, an Australian artist, Janet Venn-Brown, and made his way towards his own apartment.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The text follows Wael Zuaiter across Rome on the night he was killed, from his girlfriend's house by two buses to his own apartment, carrying a notebook, pencils and volume two of One Thousand and One Nights. At the entrance to his staircase a gunman steps out of the dark and shoots him thirteen times; the thirteenth bullet lodges in the book in his pocket. Earlier the text mentions his habit of reading poetry at the Arab Bar on Via del Vantaggio.
- **Why it matters:** The street-level murder scene is the source of the shot-through book that recurs in Rami's story and in Emily Jacir's art. The text does not name the street of the apartment, so the place is the city and its apartment staircase.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The building and street of the apartment are not named in this section.
