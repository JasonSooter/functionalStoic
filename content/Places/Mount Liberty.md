# Mount Liberty

4,000-foot peak in the [[White Mountains (New Hampshire)|White Mountains]] of New Hampshire. Two Frugalwoods posts tell of a summer hike up it.

## Updates On The Lives Of Some Frugal Weirdos — Mrs. Frugalwoods (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.frugalwoods.com/2015/07/31/updates-on-the-lives-of-some-frugal-weirdos/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gan5z0qy8cmabv2d4xcvw6)

> July turned into hike-o-rama month as we scaled not one, but two, 4,000-footers in the White Mountains of New Hampshire.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The couple hike Mt. Liberty on a Saturday, about seven hours on the trail with lunch at the summit, while the author is 23 weeks pregnant; earlier in July they climbed Mt. Pierce and Mt. Eisenhower.
- **Why it matters:** The hike is the main event of the month's update and its example of cheap, healthy fun.

## Less Makeup, More Confidence: My Frugal Beauty Manifesto — Mrs. Frugalwoods (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.frugalwoods.com/2015/07/27/less-makeup-more-confidence-my-frugal-beauty-manifesto/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gan7s2bxbf6errc099tdra)

> On Saturday we scaled another 4,000-footer in the White Mountains of New Hampshire–Mt. Liberty.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** The author, five and a half months pregnant, mentions hiking Mt. Liberty that Saturday as part of a beauty routine built on health rather than makeup.
- **Why it matters:** A brief backdrop to the argument.
