## Uncommon Type — Tom Hanks (2017)
Source: [[Uncommon Type]] · [Original](https://penguin.co.uk/books/1114786/uncommon-type/9781786091338.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5wa1pyg4ey325ny4sp202)

> The show was going to be at Crowne Lanes in Fountain Valley—a long drive, so we stopped at an In-N-Out burger before hitting the freeway.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The friends drive Steve here for the ESPN show Alley Nation. Before taping he vomits in the parking lot among the TV trucks, then bowls eleven strikes in front of the crowd and pros. On the twelfth roll the arena goes silent, then roars, and he finishes his perfect game.
- **Why it matters:** The climax of the story: Steve gets his perfect game on TV and renounces bowling, leaving his shoes and ball on the foul line.
