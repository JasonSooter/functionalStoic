## Bonjour Tristesse — Françoise Sagan (1954)

Source: [[Bonjour Tristesse]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL584333W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crcmgeke23p7vbcybbgyjg)

> Elsa was holding forth about the various festivities taking place along the Riviera, but my father was not listening to her.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The summer is spent at a rented white villa on a Mediterranean promontory, with outings to Cannes and to Saint-Tropez nightclubs; Elsa returns full of the glamorous life she has been leading along the Riviera.
- **Why it matters:** The sunlit coast is the novel's whole world. The villa is never located, so the region is as specific as the text allows.
