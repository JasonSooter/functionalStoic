# Bordeaux

Bordeaux is a city on the Garonne in south-west France. In Voltaire's _Candide_ it is a brief stop where Candide lands from Surinam on his way to Paris.

## Candide — Voltaire (1759)

Source: [[Candide]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL100672W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75ja1fdq0vf291th3q153)

> Candide stayed in Bordeaux no longer than was necessary for the selling of a few of the pebbles of El Dorado, and for hiring a good chaise to hold two passengers;

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Candide and Martin land here after the voyage from Surinam. Candide sells some El Dorado gems, hires a chaise for Paris, and leaves his last sheep to the Academy of Sciences, which sets a prize on why its wool is red.
- **Why it matters:** A brief stop, used for a jab at academic prize questions. It is Candide's way into France.
