## Magic Cards and Knowledge Bottles — Steven Johnson (2025)
Source: [Original](https://adjacentpossible.substack.com/p/magic-cards-and-knowledge-bottles) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqymyq2aphfavq71cadrpz)

> in late July of 2022, one of the very first I had in Mountain View after joining Labs

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** In late July 2022 the author, newly at Google Labs, talked with Josh Woodward in Mountain View about Kevin Kelly's 'intelligence as a service'. The talk gave rise to the 'knowledge bottles' metaphor that led to Featured Notebooks in NotebookLM.
- **Why it matters:** Setting for the conversation that originated the product idea; passing in the essay's argument.
