## LA’s ‘No-Car’ Olympics Plan Explained — The B1M (2026)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TT1WO8zWk7o) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bretxb33b4qffqfbt9r52w)

> What they intend to do is build a temporary deck on the floor of the bowl, propped up on supports.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** A 1990 upgrade put seating where the running track used to be, so for 2028 the organisers will build a raised temporary deck for athletics; the Coliseum co-hosts the Opening Ceremony.
- **Why it matters:** The historic Olympic stadium is the plan's test of reusing old venues rather than building new ones.
