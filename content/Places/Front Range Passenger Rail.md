# Front Range Passenger Rail

Proposed passenger rail line along Colorado's Front Range, linking Fort Collins, Denver and Pueblo. Congressman Joe Neguse's newsletter celebrates federal funding for it.

## Front Range Rail Funding & Special Guests In Our District! — Congressman Joe Neguse (2024)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f0p5pg8nk1mjy9mmk0rkmf)

> From Fort Collins to Denver to Pueblo, Coloradans' dream of connecting our Front Range communities through rail is one step closer—and I for one am truly excited about what is in store.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the newsletter:** The newsletter announces \$66.4 million in federal funding toward Front Range Passenger Rail, announced with the governor and a senator. Neguse writes that the dream of joining Front Range communities by rail is one step closer.
- **Why it matters:** It is the lead story of the issue and the subject of the argument for regional rail. The pin is approximate: the line is a corridor, shown here at Denver Union Station.

## Listening to Coloradans 💭 — Congressman Joe Neguse (2024)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmqs9m5a2q88p4fvh4fwkx)

> This project would connect cities from Fort Collins to Pueblo, with intermediate stops in Boulder, Longmont, and Loveland — serving as a transportation "spine" along the Front Range.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the newsletter:** One section is devoted to a letter asking appropriators to fund the passenger rail project. The route is described as running from Fort Collins to Pueblo with stops in Boulder, Longmont and Loveland. The author argues it would connect communities and help local economies.
- **Why it matters:** The proposed line is the subject of a whole section and ties the district to the wider Front Range. It is a planned route, not an existing place.
