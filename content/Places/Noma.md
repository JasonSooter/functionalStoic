# Noma

Restaurant founded in 2004 in a former warehouse in Christianshavn, [[Copenhagen]], birthplace of New Nordic cuisine; in The Year of Living Danishly it anchors Helen Russell's look at Danish food.

## The Year of Living Danishly — Helen Russell (2015)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20000553W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f1x8egsy00qz5p32j3p8tq)

> It all started in 2004 when chefs Rene Redzepi and Claus Meyer turned a former warehouse in Christianshavn into a restaurant

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Chef Bo Basten explains how Noma and the New Nordic Kitchen Manifesto changed Danish cooking. Russell then interviews Ben Reade of the Nordic Food Lab, which works from a houseboat moored opposite Noma in the harbour; the Lab's foraging ideas later inspire Lego Man's mussel hunt.
- **Why it matters:** It is the subject that frames the September chapter's food theme. The pin is the original Christianshavn site the text describes.
