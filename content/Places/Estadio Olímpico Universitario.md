## Olympic Medalist Dick Fosbury and the Power of Being Unconventional — James Clear (2015)
Source: [Original](https://jamesclear.com/dick-fosbury) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ga14pnm6ppm5cv93hzt7kj)

> Dick Fosbury took a moment to meditate as 80,000 people looked down at him from their seats in Mexico City's Olympic stadium.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The essay opens in the Olympic stadium in 1968, where Fosbury meditates before his jump, clears 2.24 m with his backwards technique, sets an Olympic record and changes the high jump for good.
- **Why it matters:** It is the scene where the Fosbury Flop is shown to the world, the essay's emblem of being unconventional. The text says only 'Mexico City's Olympic stadium'.
