# Manzanita Bay

Cove on the west side of Bainbridge Island, Puget Sound, Washington. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is where Roger Morris learned to row as a boy.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m869xqmh59bpqa5zhn6zt7qc)

> As a boy Roger had idled away his summers rowing lazily about in Manzanita Bay, a lovely blue cove lying in the lee of the Olympic Mountains.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Roger Morris's family had a rustic cabin on the western side of Bainbridge Island, and he spent his summers rowing around the bay. At twelve, with a toothache, he rowed from here some fifteen miles home to Fremont, through Agate Passage, across Puget Sound and through the [[Ballard Locks]].
- **Why it matters:** It explains why Roger is the only freshman with any rowing experience, though the book says his easy, freewheeling style worked against the racing stroke.
