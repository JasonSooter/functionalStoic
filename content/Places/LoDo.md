## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)
Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbp89dmgvgms7jmkewpc50)

> So we went to Denver, and what we found there was not Denver, but Denver’s Lower Downtown, LoDo.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In 1993 Speck and Duany visited Denver on the buzz and found only a few almost-perfect blocks of Lower Downtown around the Wynkoop brewery and the empty Union Station. Those few blocks drew investment and a renaissance, and Denver's population grew 28 percent since 1990.
- **Why it matters:** It supplies the 'lesson of LoDo': start small with something as good as you can make it. The chapter's argument rests on this place.
