## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)
Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6m25tskgf8vt6pbtf5v24)

> A total of 135 members of the family plus twenty-two servants live in the compound.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** With no hotel in Mukur, she asks for the chief's house and is led through a blossoming orchard to his compound. She sleeps in the maidens' quarters, eats alone, meets the chief's sons, and is accidentally made drunk on a fierce spirit.
- **Why it matters:** Her first sight of Afghan women and the purdah system, and a vivid 'Arabian Nights' picture of a tribal household that shapes her views on women's freedom.
