# Seattle Central Library

Glass-and-steel main library of the Seattle Public Library, downtown. On William Kamkwamba's book tour in _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ it makes Seattle his favourite stop.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8ebdkgnvzd178mdbs2mmzc2)

> Seattle was perhaps my favorite place, mostly because of the spectacular public library downtown where Bryan and I gave our presentation. The library was like a massive spaceship made of glass, shimmering and glistening in the sun.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the afterword:** On the twelve-city tour for the book, William and Bryan Mealer present at the downtown library. "It appeared more like a museum, and I thought that if a city builds a public library as beautiful as this, then it’s the city for me." He also tastes clam chowder for the first time.
- **Why it matters:** A minor stop, but a pointed one: the memoir turns on a tiny village library, and here is its opposite.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The book doesn't name the building; the downtown library it describes is the Central Library.
