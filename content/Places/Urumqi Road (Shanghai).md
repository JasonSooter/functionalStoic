## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e30ce8jbmfjpm36wnngtxc)

> One day in November, people held a vigil on Urumqi Road, the center of Shanghai’s bar district where many foreigners live.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** A vigil for the Urumqi fire victims turns after midnight into a protest chanting against the Communist Party and Xi. The next day the author walks twenty minutes from home, meets Owen, sees police clear the street and put up high barricades. Later a sticker of the road sign appears in a Chiang Mai bookstore.
- **Why it matters:** It is the one place in the chapter where public political defiance happens and the witnessed climax of the white paper protests; it recurs as a symbol in Chapter 6.
