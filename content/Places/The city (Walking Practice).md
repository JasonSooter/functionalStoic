# The city (Walking Practice)

The unnamed South Korean city of subways, motels and apartment blocks where the alien narrator of _Walking Practice_ hunts for human prey, pinned at [[Seoul]] as a best guess.

## Walking Practice — Dolki Min (2022)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL28021938W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6x3ezaxxgjqta4bz7f29jhp)

> The stairs that I must tackle in order to transfer trains stare back at me murderously. There is no elevator or escalator to rescue me.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The narrator lives in a spaceship hidden in a forest on the edge of the city and travels in by subway for each "appointment" arranged in chat rooms: up sixteen floors of an old blue-green apartment block (building 210), to a motel above an alley, to an apartment 1.6 km uphill from a subway exit, and to a park where a gang ambushes and beats them. The last meeting, at the public toilets at the head of a forest trail near home, breaks the rule that hookups must be at least 3 km away and ends in the narrator's escape through a small window into the forest.
- **Why it matters:** Walking on human legs through a city built for humans (the nearly 140 steps of a transfer station, broken escalators, rush-hour crowds) is the novel's central ordeal; the generic, unnamed city makes the narrator's alienation the point rather than the place.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Fictional and unnamed; the copyright page says places are products of the author's imagination.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The text names no city, district, station or river. Its clues are only national: prices in won ("thirty thousand won"), gochujang, a "love hotel", a dense subway network with transfer stations, and numbered apartment blocks. Reviews and an interview with the author name no setting; the novel was first published in South Korea in 2022, and the author was interviewed in Mapo-gu, Seoul. Seoul is pinned as the likeliest big Korean city with such a subway; Busan or another Korean metropolis would fit the clues equally well.
