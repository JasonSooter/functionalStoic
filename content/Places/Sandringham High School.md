# Sandringham High School

Public "Model C" high school in the Sandringham suburb of [[Johannesburg]]. In Born a Crime it is Trevor Noah's high school, which he calls a microcosm of post-apartheid South Africa.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6z18smxbh0yagzqmgapdth5)

> After finishing primary school at H. A. Jack, I started grade eight at Sandringham High School.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 11 ("Outsider") onward. Noah describes Sandringham as a Model C school, part government and part private, with a thousand kids, tennis courts, sports fields and a pool. Its racial mix is "more like a spectrum" than the blocks of H. A. Jack. Because of his long walk from Highlands North he is late and in detention every day. He then sprints to the tuck shop and runs lunch orders for other kids for money, which lets him move between every group. The crush on Zaheera and Teddy's shoplifting interrogation in the principal's office also happen here.
- **Why it matters:** the school is where Trevor learns to be an outsider who belongs everywhere and nowhere, the role he plays for the rest of the book. Bongani, his later partner in Alexandra, is also a Sandringham classmate.
