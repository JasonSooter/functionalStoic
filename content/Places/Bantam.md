# Bantam

Former port city and sultanate on the north-west coast of Java, Indonesia, now the ruins of Old Banten. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ it is where the first Dutch fleet turns violent and where the English set up their first factory in the East.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m82efma0n0j5xtan9tbt76jq)

> The Javanese port of Bantam was the headquarters of the English in the East Indies.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Houtman arrives expecting cheap spices, finds high prices and a disputed throne, and the Dutch decide "to do all possible harm to the town", bombarding it. Lancaster later makes Bantam the English headquarters, buys pepper and spice, loses Captain John Middleton to fever there and leaves eight men and three factors with orders to send a pinnace east to the Bandas; most of them die, and Henry Middleton finds the survivors in a desperate state at the end of 1604.
- **Later chapters:** The factor Edmund Scott's account follows: arsonists tunnel under the English warehouse, and Scott tortures a suspect, "burned under the nayles of his thumbes"; on Queen Elizabeth's coronation day the fourteen Englishmen raise "our banner of Sainct George upon the top of our house" and march, so the townspeople can tell them from the Dutch. In 1612 John Jourdain finds the survivors of Sir Henry Middleton's fleet "like ghostes", 140 men dead including the general; among them is Nathaniel Courthope, who later quarrels with the factors and ships diamonds here from [[Sukadana]].
- **Why it matters:** It is the base from which every early English push towards the nutmeg islands starts, and its deadliness is part of the price of the trade.
