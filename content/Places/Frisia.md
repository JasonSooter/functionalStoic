# Frisia

Coastal region along the North Sea from the Netherlands into Germany. In _Beowulf_ it is where Hygelac dies on his raid and where the Finnsburh fight sung at Heorot took place.

## Beowulf — Seamus Heaney (1999)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1810107W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne63se813jstr1xge6vcfa)

> in Friesland: the people’s friend and lord,

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The poem recalls Hygelac's raid on Friesland, where he is killed and Beowulf escapes by swimming home with thirty suits of armour; the messenger predicts that Franks and Frisians will now attack the Geats. Earlier, Hrothgar's poet sings at Heorot of the fight at the Frisian king Finn's hall, the winter truce and the renewed feud in which Finn is killed.
- **Why it matters:** Hygelac's death in Friesland is the root of Geatish insecurity and of Beowulf's kingship, and the Finn song is an echo of feuds within a hall.
