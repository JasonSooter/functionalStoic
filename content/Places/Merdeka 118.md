## Why the Jeddah Tower Won’t be as Tall as You Think — The B1M (2026)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vVfEs9wrW98) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bremm7mvg5rf9vxm0wwgtq)

> Thanks to that spire, Merdeka tops out at a neat 678.9-metres, blasting past the Shanghai Tower.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** Completed in 2024 as the world's second-tallest building, its spire supplies a large vanity height and recalls the gesture of the declaration of independence in 1957, even though the Shanghai Tower has a higher occupied floor.
- **Why it matters:** The clearest example in the video of vanity height as national symbol.
