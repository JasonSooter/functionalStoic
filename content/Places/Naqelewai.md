## Peace Corps: The Great Adventure — Peace Corps (2002)

Source: [[Peace Corps - The Great Adventure]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crcfzz1csaedjv5qrp6pn5)

> On Fiji, books were scarce in our village, Naqelewai. All material possessions were scarce because of the village's remote location.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Donna Gessell spends two years in the remote village, a long bus and Land Rover journey from town, reading in a place without a reading culture. When her house catches fire, neighbours save her books first, and she leaves behind a primary-school library.
- **Why it matters:** The village's remoteness and oral culture frame the essay's question of reading versus talk.
