## The Crazy Engineering of Venice — Primal Space (2024)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=77omYd0JOeA) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f1xzrb2m5v1bfky0p1qs03)

> there needed to be an easier way to reach Rialto

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the video:** The video tells how the Doge offered a prize for a bridge to reach Rialto: a pontoon bridge, then a wooden one that burned and collapsed, then a stone bridge on more than 12,000 piles that still stands as the city's main artery. The text does not name it as the Rialto Bridge.
- **Why it matters:** It is the video's showcase of pile engineering.
