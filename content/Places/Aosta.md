## It's All About the Bike — Robert Penn (2010)
Source: [[It's All About the Bike - In Pursuit of Happiness on Two Wheels]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21030895W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce5kkx1x5k3945vm28812s)

> I sat down on a bench in a square beneath the Roman walls of the town and set to work.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After a rim fails on a mountain road, Penn finds Aosta's bike shop shut for the weekend, so he builds a wheel himself on a bench in a square beneath the Roman walls, using notes from Penang, while townsfolk gather for the passeggiata. The third attempt holds, and the next day he rides up the valley over the Grand Saint Bernard to Martigny.
- **Why it matters:** It turns the wheel-building chapter from craft observed into craft performed.
