# Monida Pass

Pass on the Continental Divide on the Montana–Idaho border, crossed by Interstate 15. The Ryback brothers find a sheep-shearing ghost town there.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bes80yz9h77444b9j98889)

> We stopped for lunch at a small spring and then ventured into the baked and naked hills around Monida Pass. There was a ghost town at the pass which still came annually to life during the sheep-shearing season.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Short of food after Tim's injury, Eric leaves the formal Divide route for old mining roads and plains, then plots a compass course east to Monida Pass. In the baked hills around the pass they find a ghost town that comes to life each year at sheep-shearing: a labyrinth of gates, walls and corrals, deserted but in good condition, with sheep everywhere in the dry pastures. They cross the Divide at Coal Mine Creek and camp beyond the pass.
- **Why it matters:** A dreamlike scene on the dry stretch between the Bitterroots and Yellowstone, and a foretaste of the empty Great Divide Basin ahead.
