# Nova Gorica

Slovenian border town facing Gorizia, Italy. Dervla Murphy's entry point into Yugoslavia in _Full Tilt_ (1963).

## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6kexq3m9d169stexmramb)

> The formidable female took one quick look at my intricately laden bicycle, my knapsack with its protruding loaf of bread and my scruffy self. Then she burst into good-humoured laughter – of which one would not have believed her capable – slapped me on the back and waved me towards the frontier.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** She reaches the divided frontier town at night and is shuttled between police and customs. A female customs officer, though Dervla has forgotten the pistol in her pocket, laughs and waves her through. In Nova Gorica she is taken in by the typist Romana and her room-mate Arita.
- **Why it matters:** Her entry into Yugoslavia and the first instance of the spontaneous hospitality that recurs through the book.
