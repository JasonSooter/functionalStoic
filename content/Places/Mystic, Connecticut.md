# Mystic, Connecticut

Seaport village on the Connecticut coast. In Aoibheann Sweeney's novel it is where Miranda's boyfriend Nate takes her for his sister's wedding, and where she walks out.

## Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking — Aoibheann Sweeney (2007)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/among-other-things-ive-taken-up-smoking-9781101202227)

> Nate was waiting for me on the platform at Mystic, wearing a bright red sweater, his shoulders hunched against the seaside chill.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Nate meets Miranda at Mystic station and drives her through "the fussy town" and the salt marshes to his parents' house for his sister Liz's wedding. At the yacht club she strips roses from centrepieces and sits through a rehearsal dinner of toasts and sing-alongs, feeling herself shrink. On the wedding day, told to cover her red dress with a borrowed shawl, she changes back into jeans, leaves Nate a note ("I'm sorry I wore the wrong dress") and runs through the flooded driveway to catch the train.
- **Why it matters:** It is the novel's turning point: Miranda rejects the life Nate's family offers and goes back to the city, and to Ana.
