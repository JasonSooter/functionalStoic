# St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador

Capital of Newfoundland and Labrador, on a sheltered harbour at the island's eastern tip. In _Cod_ it is where the 1992 cod moratorium is announced.

## Cod: A Biography of the Fish That Changed the World — Mark Kurlansky (1997)
Source: [[Cod]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/1032753/cod) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kkseb4952jcx7ws588pqe6)

> St. John’s, the oldest city in North America, was built on a deepwater harbor sheltered by high majestic cliffs.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Its waterfront once held European fleets; now tourists are "screeched in" at its bars by kissing a codfish. In July 1992 fisheries minister John Crosbie announces the cod moratorium at a hotel there, and Petty Harbour's Sam Lee pounds on the door when fishermen are refused entry.
- **Why it matters:** The capital of the collapse.
