## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy7q2m3x6p7s0g2retrqrh)

> Ned Kelly, Dickie Bird, Hugh Maynard and I were the only guests.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The crew are stranded in Port Stanley's only hotel over Christmas 1980. They have lunch with Iain Stewart and his wife and attend a party at Government House where Governor Rex Hunt plays eccentric billiard games.
- **Why it matters:** The text flags the Stewarts' hospitality as memorable 'in view of what was to come' (the 1982 war), giving the place retrospective weight.
