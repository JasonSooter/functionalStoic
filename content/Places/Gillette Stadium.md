## This Is The Story About Robert Kraft's Casino Holdings That Rupert Murdoch's Paper Never Ran — Bart Hubbuch (2017)

Source: [Original](https://deadspin.com/this-is-the-story-about-robert-krafts-casino-holdings-t-1794594130) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7cpepzfpbpf2vt87w77t3)

> Kraft summoned me to his office inside Gillette Stadium, where he promised me scoops about his team and the league because he was such good friends with “Rupert.”

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** In 2010, at the start of Patriots training camp, the author is called into Kraft's office inside Gillette Stadium. Kraft promises him scoops by invoking his friendship with Rupert Murdoch, which makes clear how close the two men are. The scoops never materialize, and the scene later frames the author's suspicion that Kraft got the casino story killed.
- **Why it matters:** The one concrete scene in the piece where the Kraft-Murdoch relationship is shown in person.
