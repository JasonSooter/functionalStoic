# St Nicholas Croatian Church (Millvale)

Croatian Catholic church in Millvale, a river town just outside [[Pittsburgh]], painted inside with Maxo Vanka's murals. In David Byrne's _Bicycle Diaries_ he visits on his birthday and calls Vanka "the Diego Rivera of Pittsburgh".

## At a glance
| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bicycle Diaries | David Byrne | subject | Maxo Vanka's murals of immigrant steelworkers, war and Death as an oligarch, dark with coal dust. |

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7641g4ewnytakzztabbb8km)

> The church in this little town is Croatian, and the murals, by Maxo Vanka, are spectacular. The Diego Rivera of Pittsburgh, I would say.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** With John Chernoff, Byrne drives up to Millvale, a former mining town with boarded-up shops and one French bakery, where he buys a birthday cake. Inside the church the murals show immigrants with a smoking steel foundry, anti-war scenes, and an oligarch as Death reading stock reports while black servants bring him dinner; coal dust has darkened them.
- **Why it matters:** The murals give the Pittsburgh section its strongest image of the steel era's human cost, "bold and brave stuff" for a parish church.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The text says only "the church in this little town is Croatian"; Vanka's Millvale murals are in St. Nicholas Croatian Catholic Church.
