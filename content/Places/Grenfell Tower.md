# Grenfell Tower

Residential tower block in North Kensington, [[London]], destroyed by fire in 2017. It is the subject of a B1M video reconstructing the fire, the cladding decisions and the inquiry that followed.

## The Grenfell Tower Fire: London’s High-Rise Scandal — The B1M (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=URX_5B1EaIE) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqvwvpzt4rjpfv69c3a4k1)

> It's the early hours of June 14th, 2017, and this West London neighbourhood is about to suffer one of the most shocking human tragedies of recent times.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The 24-storey council tower block in North Kensington caught fire after midnight on 14 June 2017, starting in a fourth-floor kitchen (Flat 16). The fire climbed the new aluminium composite cladding, engulfing the building; 72 of 297 occupants died. The video reconstructs the night, the cladding and insulation decisions, the inquiry's 1,700-page report, and the unfinished remediation across the UK; it also visits the shrouded tower eight years on, with the government confirming in February 2025 it will be carefully dismantled.
- **Why it matters:** The site is the entire subject: the worst UK residential fire in decades, now a memorial site under protective sheeting, with a contested decision about demolition vs. preservation.
