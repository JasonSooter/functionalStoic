## #savings: Can Social Media Nudge Teens into Smarter Money Choices? — Chris Berdik (2016)

Source: [Original](http://ww2.kqed.org/mindshift/2016/03/16/savings-can-social-media-nudge-teens-into-smarter-money-choices/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7tfnqf6ryzn91kfkj42st)

> In the fall of 2008, Ted Gonder was studying economics at the University of Chicago as the world economy melted down around him.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Gonder studied economics at the University of Chicago during the 2008 collapse while a nearby inner-city neighborhood, a bank desert with very high foreclosure rates, was among the hardest hit. The contrast led him and classmates to start the financial education program that became Moneythink.
- **Why it matters:** It is the origin story of the nonprofit the article profiles.
