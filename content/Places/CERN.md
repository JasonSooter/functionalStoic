# CERN

European laboratory for particle physics on the French–Swiss border near Geneva. In _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_ Feynman gives a talk there straight after the Nobel ceremony, in the suit from the King's Dinner.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfr2jn7rbjvh66yhq16tma)

> Instead of coming home immediately, I went to CERN, the European center for nuclear research in Switzerland, to give a talk.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** "Instead of coming home immediately, I went to CERN" after the Nobel ceremony. He gives his talk in the suit he wore to the King's Dinner and jokes that he likes it; the physicists boo and Weisskopf tears off his coat, and the formality of Sweden is undone.
- **Why it matters:** A short coda showing his relief at returning to informal physics culture; minor as a place.
