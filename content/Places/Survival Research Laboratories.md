# Survival Research Laboratories

Mark Pauline's machine-performance group, based in a warehouse in the Bay Area. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne tours its terrifying machines while asking about San Francisco's dark side.

## At a glance

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m772wwvkgmwxpdpywv6pk7tq)

> I visit Mark Pauline at the warehouse base of his art performance organization Survival Research Laboratories.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** From outside the place looks like an ordinary low-industrial building with machinery under wraps. Pauline leads Byrne from machine to machine: one shoots balls of molten copper hundreds of feet, another a flame eighty-some feet long. Byrne calls them "glorious and frightening."
- **Why it matters:** It opens the section "The Dark Heart of Peace and Love," his clearest example of the danger and forbidden fruit beneath the Bay Area's utopian surface.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The text gives no address, only "an ordinary urban low-industrial building." SRL later moved to Petaluma, so the site of Byrne's visit is uncertain; not pinned.
