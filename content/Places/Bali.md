# Bali

Indonesian island east of Java known for its Hindu temple arts; in _Life on Air_ Attenborough films its music and dance, returning eleven years later.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2j26rg15sqwewk1egppd)

> The gamelan plays an essential part in temple rituals and accompanies thrilling dances and theatrical portrayals of episodes from Hindu legends, so there was plenty of visual excitement for our camera to record.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** After the ferry from Banjuwangi, Attenborough and Lagus spend two weeks filming and recording Balinese music, dance and temple ceremony. He explains how the Hindu courts fled east from Islamic Java and gave Bali its arts, and he risks including it in an animal-questing series.
- **Why it matters:** An interlude of about two weeks given a page of description; not central to the animal story.

## The Global Yogi: Long-Term Travel through Five Continents — Chris Guillebeau (2015)

Source: [Original](http://chrisguillebeau.com/the-global-yogi/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6k641wayfmm91m2x0ef1f)

> While in Bali, I got a break-up email from my boyfriend.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** She went to Bali to attend the Bali Spirit Festival as Media after a week in southern Thailand. There she got a break-up email, which launched her long trip around Southeast Asia.
- **Why it matters:** The turning point that set off the six-month pilgrimage.
