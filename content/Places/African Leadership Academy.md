# African Leadership Academy

Pan-African secondary school in Honeydew, on the edge of Johannesburg. In _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ William Kamkwamba wins a place in its first class.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8ebdghjp644695ge0fq0g87)

> The school is located outside Johannesburg on a beautiful campus with giant trees; wide, green soccer pitches; and lots of peacocks, which are even louder and more disturbing in the morning than the noisy chickens at home.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In the epilogue William is one of "only 106" accepted out of 1,700 applicants from fifty-three countries, a school meant "to train Africa’s next generation of leaders". He takes ten courses, with entrepreneurship his favourite, and rooms with a Kenyan, Githiora Thuku. In the afterword he is "back in Johannesburg" filling out the application that takes him to [[Dartmouth College]].
- **Why it matters:** It turns a self-taught tinkerer into a student among peers from across Africa, the step between Malawi and an American university.
