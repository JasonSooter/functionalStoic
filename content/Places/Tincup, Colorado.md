## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs2nakbj3ne9b6n6vxtesy)

> Tincup would be my haven — if my frozen legs would still carry me.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Driven off the Divide by hail and sleet, Eric reaches the handful of buildings at Tincup. The elderly storekeeper lets him sleep on the floor of the only store and fills the night with anecdotes about the town.
- **Why it matters:** A chapter-long refuge ("A Refuge in Tincup") that restores him for the next stretch.
