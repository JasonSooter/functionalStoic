# Dimitrovgrad (Serbia)

Serbian town on the Nišava river at the Bulgarian border. In _Full Tilt_ Dervla Murphy crosses an unguarded Iron Curtain nearby.

## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6khedy1j4ja726ten1531)

> In December, the Bulgarian Embassy in London had issued me with a visa valid for only four days. Now this genial policeman, who spoke fluent English, took one look at the card, said that it was ridiculous, and issued me with a new visa entitling me to stay in Bulgaria as long as I wished! After which we sat by the stove and amiably discussed our two countries over glasses of brandy.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** The supposed first-class road is a flooded goat-track and the Nisava bridge is swept away, so she crosses on a railway bridge. At the unmanned Iron Curtain she walks through a gap in a half-hearted fence and finds a policeman dozing with a cat, who reissues her four-day visa for as long as she wishes.
- **Why it matters:** Comic deflation of the Iron Curtain and the start of her positive view of Bulgaria.
