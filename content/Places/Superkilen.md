# Superkilen

Public park in [[Nørrebro]], Copenhagen. Jack Harries calls it his favourite example of a city designed for play and for bringing people together.

## Where The City Meets The Wild — Jack Harries (2026)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5QLzefF2kZQ) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m78pdawg2zfmrp735hra8zw5)

> a public park and civic experiment

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** On a detour to Copenhagen before heading further north, Harries names Superkilen his favourite example of play designed into the city: "a public park and civic experiment designed to bring people together." Its objects come from around the world: a Moroccan fountain, swings from Syria, neon signs from Russia and a Japanese slide shaped like an octopus.
- **Why it matters:** It is the video's one concrete example. Harries takes it as a reminder that design "isn't just aesthetic" but can foster inclusivity and belonging; without it, the Copenhagen segment would rest on general impressions of trees and swimmable water.
