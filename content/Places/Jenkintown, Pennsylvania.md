## A Strong Town Pays for Its Sidewalks — Randy Garbin (2016)
Source: [Original](https://www.strongtowns.org/journal/2016/7/7/a-strong-towns-pays-for-its-sidewalks) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jx6w5f9nat5jfxe84bzbfh)

> Jenkintown, Pennsylvania actively promotes itself as a walkable community. Indeed, this borough that barely encompasses a square mile has sidewalks lining both sides of all of its streets.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author's home borough requires property owners to pay for sidewalk and curb repair. After a mass inspection he faces $3,000 to $5,000 estimates for four blocks of sidewalk and 40 feet of curb under threat of daily fines or jail, while the borough borrows $2.4 million for a 66-space parking lot.
- **Why it matters:** It is the case study for the argument that walkable towns should fund sidewalks as public goods.
