# Old Bailey

England's Central Criminal Court, in the [[City of London]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is where Michael Bettaney is tried, mostly in secret, and sentenced.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7vbqhh8968dbbem161gsrbp)

> The trial of Michael Bettaney opened at the Old Bailey on April 11, 1984, under the tightest possible security, with court windows masked, a large police presence, and a scrambler-telephone link to MI5 headquarters should consultation be necessary during proceedings.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Most of the trial takes place in camera. After five days of testimony Lord Lane, the Lord Chief Justice, sentences Bettaney to twenty-three years, calling him "puerile" and "dangerous". The book then follows the press reaction and the expulsion of the KGB _rezident_ Arkadi Guk.
- **Why it matters:** The trial ends the Koba affair in public, and Guk's expulsion clears Gordievsky's path to the top of the London _rezidentura_.
