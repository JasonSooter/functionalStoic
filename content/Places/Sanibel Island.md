## Sometimes You Just Need to Have Fun and Spend Money — Derek (2016)

Source: [Original](http://lifeandmyfinances.com/2016/01/have-fun-and-spend-money/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6jm4s4jjdx3fvwhb1bczv)

> Just last week, we were on the shelly beaches of Sanibel and Fort Meyers, hand in hand and talking about whatever came to our minds. We kayaked in the mangroves of Tropical Bay and saw dolphins playing throughout our entire vacation stay.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** After saving for six months following their July wedding, the author and his wife spend a couple thousand dollars on a January vacation to Sanibel Island. They walk the shelly beaches, kayak in the mangroves and watch dolphins throughout the stay.
- **Why it matters:** It is the concrete trip the author uses to illustrate that earned, affordable spending on memories is worthwhile for an extreme saver.
