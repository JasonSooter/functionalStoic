## Schools Must Resist Destructive Anti-racist Demands — John McWhorter (2021)
Source: [Original](https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/archive/2021/01/when-antiracist-manifestos-become-antiracist-wrecking-balls/617841/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8y7yddqne9gxna7rehx86)

> at Northwestern asks that the university recommit to the demands made by Black students who took over the bursar’s office in 1968, with the implication that these were all but ignored by a callously racist institution.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Today's activists want the university to recommit to the demands of Black students who took over the bursar's office in 1968. McWhorter argues most of those demands were met, and that the unmet demand to uproot racism in Evanston was beyond a university's purview.
- **Why it matters:** The 1968 takeover gives the essay its historical comparison with today's demands.
