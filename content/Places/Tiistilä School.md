# Tiistilä School

Public comprehensive school in Espoo, Finland. In _The Nordic Theory of Everything_ Anu Partanen revisits the school she attended to describe Finnish public education.

## The Nordic Theory of Everything — Anu Partanen (2016)

Source: [[The Nordic Theory of Everything]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20028831W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kjfpaxcfd8as18gbb6mqyh)

> I spent three years of my life at the Tiistilä School in the town of Espoo, from grades seven through nine.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Partanen bikes back to the school she attended from grades seven to nine and finds its low red-brick buildings unchanged. She uses it to describe Finnish school life: free hot meals, pupil-welfare teams, in-school tutoring and a student body that is now about a third immigrant-background.
- **Why it matters:** It grounds the chapter's case for Finnish public schools in her own schooling.
