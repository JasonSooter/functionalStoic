# Scheveningen prison

The prison complex in Scheveningen, [[The Hague]], whose wartime wing held Resistance fighters as the "Oranjehotel". In _The Son and Heir_ Frans is jailed here twice and broken by daily exercise beside SS leader Hanns Rauter.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85n9wpbmvjh0abdfewpcg8y)

> He was being held in the same wing of the Scheveningen prison where countless Resistance fighters had waited for death.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Deported from Belgium, Frans is handed to Dutch police at Roosendaal on 4 September 1946 and taken to Scheveningen. Sweerts, visiting as "Arie van der Molen", has him moved next to Hanns Rauter, leader of the Dutch SS, and their daily hour in the tiny walled yard turns into interrogations; Frans gives in, agrees to go to South America and is driven out on 26 November. Arrested again at [[Ridderborn Castle]], he is back in Scheveningen in March 1947, questioned by the Politieke Recherche Afdeling on the Lange Voorhout, and released on 30 January 1948 after ten months.
- **Why it matters:** The prison is where the Old Boss's plan to remove his son is forced through, and the place that turns Frans from a war returnee into a man under suspicion in his own country.
