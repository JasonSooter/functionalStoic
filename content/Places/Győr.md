## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7wxr4m2srjs6yftwhrg8f)

> At the age of fourteen, Yitzak was assigned to work as a runner for the rabbi in Gyor, smuggling gold down to the market.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Smadar's grandfather Yitzak ran through the alleys, market and red-light district of Gyor, scalped cinema tickets, and was caught by the Gestapo in the cinema. The seedcake wrapped in an advertisement for the film he watched is the detail that fascinated Smadar.
- **Why it matters:** Setting of the family's Holocaust story told to Smadar for a school project.
