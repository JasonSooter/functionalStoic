## I MISS LORINA BULWER — Shaun Usher (2024)

Source: [Original](https://news.lettersofnote.com/p/i-miss-lorina-bulwer) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbgje42hs64fnmm4enxj5n)

> I MISS LORINA BULWER LIVED AT BECCLES SUFFOLK WAS EDUCATED THERE UNTIL ABOUT SEVENTEEN YEARS OF AGE THEN CAME TO GT YARMOUTH NORFOLK TO LIVE

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** Bulwer says she lived and was schooled at Beccles until about seventeen, then moved to Great Yarmouth. The letter returns to Beccles repeatedly: the Belgian school, Mrs Dashwood teaching her piano, her father the Beccles grocer.
- **Why it matters:** Her childhood setting as she recounts it, and the source of much of the family and social detail in the letter. The article itself says only that she was born in Suffolk.
