# South Australian Museum

Natural history museum on North Terrace in [[Adelaide]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ it holds Reg Sprigg's Ediacaran fossils, some of the oldest animals known, in a quiet upstairs case.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W)

> can be seen in a glass case in an upstairs room of the stout and lovely South Australian Museum in Adelaide

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Some of Sprigg's original specimens from the [[Ediacara Hills]], with others of the fifteen hundred since found in the Flinders Range, are shown in an upstairs room of the museum. They are faint, small and mostly disc-shaped with vague trailing ribbons, and they don't attract much attention.
- **Why it matters:** A small coda to the Ediacara story: a revolutionary find ends up as an overlooked display case.
