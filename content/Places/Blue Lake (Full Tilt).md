# Blue Lake (Full Tilt)

Lake above Naran that Dervla Murphy calls the 'Blue Lake'; most likely Lake Saiful Muluk in the [[Kagan Valley]]. A scenic excursion in _Full Tilt_.

## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6nb2355jeshe7ha6q5z1v)

> This lake seems to be a volcanic crater, a mile wide and long, and it reflects all the snow-covered peaks that surround it on three sides.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** She climbs with the Karachi family to the Blue Lake, walks around the shore, and is photographed by forestry students. She later sleeps with another Afghan caravan.
- **Why it matters:** A scenic excursion in her descent.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real place, named in the book by a name that is not on modern maps.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The book calls it only 'the Blue Lake'. Clues: it is a climb from Naran, 'a mile wide and long', looks like 'a volcanic crater', and 'reflects all the snow-covered peaks that surround it on three sides'. Lake Saiful Muluk, the lake in the mountains above Naran, fits, so the pin is there (inferred). Alternative: Lulusar Lake, higher up the valley towards the Babusar.
