# Marin Headlands

Hilly peninsula at the north end of the Golden Gate Bridge, in [[Marin County]], California. In _Bicycle Diaries_ it is David Byrne's one wilderness ride from San Francisco.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m772x13619myjmeektdg296q)

> There are bike trails all over the headlands and around western Marin, much of which has been left as national preserve.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After tacos at the Taqueria Cancun, Byrne puts his bike on a Muni bus across the Golden Gate Bridge to Sausalito and Marin. The trails swoop over fog-swept barren hills to hidden coves; one can spot hawks, vultures, mountain lions and seals, and from inside the hills even the bridge is hidden. The brisk mist reminds him of the Scottish highlands.
- **Why it matters:** It gives the city chapter its only stretch of open country, and leads him to reflect that bare, beautiful landscapes like Scotland's were once forested.
