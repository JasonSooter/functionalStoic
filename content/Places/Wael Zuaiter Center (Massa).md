# Wael Zuaiter Center (Massa)

Cultural centre named for Wael Zuaiter in Massa, Tuscany. A turning point in Colum McCann's _Apeirogon_: Rami finds the shuttered Zuaiter Center in a cobbled lane in Massa, is given the key, and touches the bullet in the book.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjr6x4hejnz373h6sy5wbp)

> He was on holiday in Tuscany with Nurit when he saw the sign. He was taken aback. The Wael Zuaiter Center. The arrow pointed down a narrow laneway in the town of Massa.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Rami, on holiday in Tuscany with Nurit, comes across a sign for the Wael Zuaiter Center in the town of Massa. A woman in house slippers hands him the key to the locked old shopfront after asking where he is from, and he answers 'From Hungary' rather than Israel. Inside he sees tables with glass boxes, shelves and posters, and later touches the bullet that went through Zuaiter's copy of One Thousand and One Nights.
- **Why it matters:** This is where the book's thread of Zuaiter and the shot-through book reaches Rami in person, and the place where he withholds his nationality. The lookup of a real memorial centre in a small Tuscan town ties the Mossad assassination story to the main characters.
