## Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir (2021)
Source: [[Project Hail Mary]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/611060/project-hail-mary-by-andy-weir/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvsqhv9hjrvt71dn7pkb7d)

> “All rise,” said the bailiff, “the United States District Court for the Western District of Washington is now in session.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Stratt appears as sole defendant in Intellectual Property Alliance v. Project Hail Mary over copying all software and books for the ship's library. She moves to dismiss, waves a treaty immunity and a presidential pardon, and walks out under the guard of US Army soldiers.
- **Why it matters:** A brief comic scene showing Stratt's unchecked authority; the specific courthouse city is not given.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The bailiff names the court but not the city. The Western District of Washington sits in Seattle and Tacoma; pinned at the Seattle courthouse (700 Stewart Street) as the main seat. Alternative: the Tacoma courthouse.
