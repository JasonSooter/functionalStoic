# Suez Canal

Canal in Egypt linking the Mediterranean and the Red Sea. A turning point in Colum McCann's _Apeirogon_: Where Rami crosses by bridge during the 1973 war and takes up a dead enemy's rifle.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjpz7qsfmmkag06mz79kg6)

> Israel was crossing the Suez. The bridge was theirs. The dark was lit with bombsmoke.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In the Yom Kippur War Rami, a 23-year-old tank repairman, drives into the Negev, crosses the canal at night on a bridge, jumps out, picks up a Kalashnikov and fights the rest of the war with the enemy's gun. He kills at least one person.
- **Why it matters:** The crossing is the central combat scene of Rami's life and the source of what he never told Smadar; without it his later position loses some of its weight.
