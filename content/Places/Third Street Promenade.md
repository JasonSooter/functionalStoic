## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m700j8tnhtw03aq8t7gf0tfg)

> The day of the opening of A Time to Kill, I strolled to my favorite deli on the Third Street Promenade in Santa Monica, California, to get a tuna fish sandwich on toasted sourdough with extra pickles and ketchup on the side.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On the day A Time to Kill opens he strolls to his favourite deli on the Promenade, and only a handful of the hundreds of people there notice him. The film grosses fifteen million dollars that weekend; the following Monday he takes the same walk and 396 of the 400 people stare at him: "I was famous."
- **Why it matters:** The same ordinary walk, repeated, measures the change fame makes overnight; it opens the book's long reckoning with celebrity.
