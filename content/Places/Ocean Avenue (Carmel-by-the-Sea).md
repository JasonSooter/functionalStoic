# Ocean Avenue (Carmel-by-the-Sea)

## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)

Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbkpzgybxxmxdk4qp725aq)

> If you’ve been to the former artists’ colony of Carmel-by-the-Sea, California, you’ve probably enjoyed strolling its picturesque main street, Ocean Avenue.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Ocean Avenue is free of off-street parking because it is illegal; businesses pay in-lieu fees that fund shared city parking a few blocks away. That produced midblock courtyards and walkways and steady sidewalk activity.
- **Why it matters:** It is Speck's example of in-lieu parking fees producing a charming main street. Carmel's in-lieu fee is \$27,520 per space.
