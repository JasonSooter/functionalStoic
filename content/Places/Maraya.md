## Is The Line Really Dead? — The B1M (2025)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFVYgZMEOFg) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce3gg574a77cjhta13wn32)

> This is Maraya.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the video:** Presented as a likely inspiration for The Line's mirror finish: polished metal cladding spoiled in the sand, so a tempered, copper-backed mirror with over 90 percent reflection was developed. The video does not say where Maraya stands.
- **Why it matters:** It is the proof of concept for The Line's facade, and a cautionary one.
