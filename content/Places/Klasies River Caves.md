# Klasies River Caves

Coastal cave site in the Eastern Cape, [[South Africa]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ it is the often-cited but disputed debut of modern humans.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8333990wqk02bn36bmxk2w4)

> Many books place their debut at about 120,000 years ago in the form of remains found at the Klasies River Mouth in South Africa, but not everyone accepts that these were fully modern people.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Many books date the first modern humans to about 120,000 years ago, from remains found at Klasies River Mouth. Bryson notes that not everyone accepts these were fully modern people; Tattersall and Schwartz say the question still awaits clarification.
- **Why it matters:** Minor: a brief example of how uncertain the origin of our own species remains.
