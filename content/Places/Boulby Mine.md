# Boulby Mine

Deep potash and salt mine on the North Yorkshire coast, England. A Nautilus piece on dormant microbes visits it as a radiation-shielded refuge for ancient life.

## What Boredom Does to You — Manoush Zomorodi (2017)

Source: [Original](http://nautil.us/issue/53/monsters/what-boredom-does-to-you) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7dfpf8bysykc7y7j7f5pe)

> Around the time Davis was in Russia, Terence McGenity, a microbiologist at the University of Sussex, and his students went down to another deep salt mine, the Boulby mine, the deepest mine in the United Kingdom.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** McGenity and his students go down the deepest mine in the UK, which also houses a dark matter laboratory. He explains that salt blocks cosmic radiation, helping the halophile Hsal survive better when entombed in halite.
- **Why it matters:** The mine serves as an example of a deep, low-radiation environment that protects ancient microbes.
