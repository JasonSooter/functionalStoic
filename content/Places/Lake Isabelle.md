# Lake Isabelle

Glacial lake in the Indian Peaks Wilderness, Colorado. In The Ultimate Journey Eric finds it drained and littered with logging debris.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs2ctt6f5seqgshv5eqv16)

> The radiant Lake Isabelle looked like a bomb crater.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Expecting a glacier-fed blue lake, Eric finds a half-drained mudhole full of logging debris. For the first time he photographs ugliness, "as an epitaph to the slow destruction of the wilderness."
- **Why it matters:** The book's sharpest environmental protest; it changes how Eric uses his camera.
