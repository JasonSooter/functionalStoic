# Kunsthistorisches Museum

The great art museum on the Ringstrasse in [[Vienna (Literary)|Vienna]], Austria, whose endless halls give Bill Bryson museum fatigue in Neither Here Nor There.

## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74125W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bek50hdj1pec8ksnt78xcw)

> I decided to start at the top with the Kunsthistorische Museum. It was fabulous—vast, grand, full of great paintings.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 19. He praises the racks of cards in each room giving the paintings' histories in four languages. But the halls "just run on and on", and before a third of the way through he has museum fatigue and starts "involuntarily supplying captions to the pictures".
- **Why it matters:** It is the first of the Vienna chapter's museum set-pieces, mostly comic. It establishes the city's grandeur before the run of let-downs (the Albertina, the cafés, the Freud museum).
