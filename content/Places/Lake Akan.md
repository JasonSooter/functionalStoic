> The frozen water world of Lake Akan in northern Japan

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Each spring melting ice releases marimo algae balls. Winds create currents that carry them out of reach of whooper swans and clean them as they spin.
- **Why it matters:** A distinct habitat in which the marimos' survival depends on the lake's winds and currents.
