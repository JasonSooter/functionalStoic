## Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World — David Epstein (2019)
Source: [[Range]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550188/range-by-david-epstein/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjs3kava38j148n0pjcfs4)

> His house on a hill in Dunedin looks out over a gently rolling green farmscape.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 2 opens in Dunedin, the town at the base of a hilly peninsula on New Zealand's South Island. James Flynn lives there, and Epstein visits his house on a hill to interview him about the Flynn effect and the narrowness of higher education.
- **Why it matters:** The remote southern town is where a self-described outsider noticed a global pattern that psychometricians had accepted without question. The story would lose its sense of an outsider's view from far away if set elsewhere.
