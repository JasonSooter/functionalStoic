## Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir (2021)

> The chopper took me to Travis Air Force Base, about 60 miles north of the city.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** A Marine helicopter carries Grace here from the Bay Area. He is put on a waiting fighter jet at the tarmac and, sedated with a pill, takes off for an unknown destination.
- **Why it matters:** First leg of the whirlwind military relay across the Pacific; minor but named and dwelt on briefly.
