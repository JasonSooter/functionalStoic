# The Venetian Las Vegas

Resort hotel and casino on the Las Vegas Strip. In _Just for Fun_ its ballroom hosts Linus Torvalds's Comdex keynote.

## Just for Fun: The Story of an Accidental Revolutionary — Linus Torvalds and David Diamond (2001)
Source: [[Just for Fun - The Story of an Accidental Revolutionary]] · [Original](https://search.worldcat.org/oclc/45610395) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kks0z6vqxqwh834x796gja)

> He had attracted a standing-room crowd in the Venetian Hotel ballroom, which is about the size of seven average IKEA stores.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Bill Gates gives the Comdex keynote in the ballroom on Sunday, and Linus speaks there the next night. Strip traffic, lost badges and a vanished presentation computer make the build-up chaotic, his daughter cries during the speech, and he gets a standing ovation before he says a word.
- **Why it matters:** The biggest audience of his life, and a deliberately unglamorous keynote.
