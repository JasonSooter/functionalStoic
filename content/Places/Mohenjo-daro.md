# Mohenjo-daro

Mohenjo-daro is a ruined city of the Indus Valley civilisation in Sindh, Pakistan. In _Sapiens_ its excavation by the British is an example of empire producing knowledge.

## Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind — Yuval Noah Harari (2014)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17075811W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m7697kw85tmca9e2dy2nnj)

> Mohenjo-daro was one of the chief cities of the Indus Valley civilisation, which flourished in the third millennium BC and was destroyed around 1900 BC.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** No pre-British ruler of India paid the ruins any attention. A British archaeological survey noticed the site in 1922 and a British team excavated it, uncovering India's first great civilisation.
- **Why it matters:** A brief example of imperial curiosity producing knowledge of a conquered land's own past.
