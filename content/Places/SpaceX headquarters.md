## Elon Musk Series: Introduction — Tim Urban (2015)

Source: [Original](https://waitbutwhy.com/2015/05/elon-musk-the-worlds-raddest-man.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g16bmf15gktctqc0jqpyzt)

> The next day was the SpaceX factory, which might be even cooler, but the building contains advanced rocket technology

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Urban tours the SpaceX factory but may not photograph it because rocket technology counts as weapons technology; afterwards he talks with senior engineers.
- **Why it matters:** The visit is part of the reporting behind the series, though the text says little about the place itself.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The post says only 'the SpaceX factory'; it is the company's Hawthorne headquarters.
