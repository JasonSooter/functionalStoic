# Galería de Arte Mexicano

Inés Amor's pioneering commercial art gallery in [[Mexico City]]. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it hosts the January 1940 International Exhibition of Surrealism, where Frida shows alongside the Surrealists.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)
Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m73hnrt746153a8fehw0jtyf)

> When the “International Exhibition of Surrealism” opened on January 17 at Inès Amor’s Galería de Arte Mexicano in Mexico City, it was the season’s great cultural and social event.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 16 opens with the show, organized by Breton, César Moro and Wolfgang Paalen. Mexico City's elite crowd a lavish opening and then go on to dance at the El Patio cabaret. Reviewers remark that most of the Mexican participants, Frida among them, are not really Surrealists.
- **Why it matters:** It frames the book's discussion of whether Frida belongs to Surrealism at all.
