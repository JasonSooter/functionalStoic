- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The Foco Bike Mob newsletter announces its 3rd Friday night ride from The Sound Bar, which it has used as a base since 2019, and looks back on its Bike the Lights holiday ride, about sixty riders with a hot-cocoa stop at Lightning Mini Golf. ## Transfort Optimization Plan — Our City (2025)
Source: [Original](https://ourcity.fcgov.com/transfort-optimization-plan) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dr1c19knhh49mdsgjg67az)

> The City of Fort Collins has launched the Transfort Optimization Plan, aimed at working with the community to determine the most optimal way to deliver transit service to the community via our public transit system

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The City of Fort Collins launches the Transfort Optimization Plan to decide how to run its bus system within budget, with a survey open until July 14, 2025. Funding gaps are $13 million a year beyond the new sales tax. Scenario maps are shown.
- **Why it matters:** The article is about this city's transit network; it is a civic document, not a narrative.

## Fare-Free Continues and Ways to Stay Informed — Transfort
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f0rp9v1csgvn5n71bf83ye)

> Transfort has been fare-free for over four years, a decision initially made during the COVID-19 pandemic under Emergency Rules and Regulations 2020-03.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the newsletter:** The city's transit newsletter announces that Transfort will remain fare-free, a policy begun under 2020 pandemic emergency rules, and that MAX frequency is back to every 10 minutes at peak. The city is named only in the footer.
- **Why it matters:** It adds Fort Collins' fare-free buses to the picture of a city working on transport.
