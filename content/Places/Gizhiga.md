# Gizhiga

Village at the head of Gizhiga Bay on the Sea of Okhotsk, in Magadan Oblast, Russia. In _Last of the Breed_ Joe Mack is cornered and captured in the hills north of it.

## Last of the Breed — Louis L'Amour (1986)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL51048W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6ycpf4zeh9vfg67kd0gm7pa)

> His map showed him he was somewhere north of a village or town named Gizhiga. Although there were few roads in the area before him, those few would be patrolled.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Alekhin lands at Gizhiga only hours before the trapper Ostap spots Joe Mack and sells the sighting in exchange for his wife's release. Patrols close him in on "one of the few places around that had roads on three sides"; he caches his bow, arrows and pistol in the rocks and walks down into the open. Beaten in an old stable, he escapes when Ostap lifts the pin on the door, shoots up the camp and drives north in a stolen Volga, while Ostap and the soldiers use the road to Evensk.
- **Why it matters:** The only time the hunters hold him: the book's low point and reversal, after which he crosses the rest of the northeast unarmed and hurt.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The capture ridge is unnamed; the text places it north of Gizhiga, with Evensk reachable by road, so the pin is at the village.
