# Esalen Institute

Retreat centre at Big Sur on the California coast, known for its hot-spring baths. In _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_ Feynman sits in its baths and overhears a reflexology session.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfr3xwg264w5qng5kxmb42)

> At Esalen there are some large baths fed by hot springs situated on a ledge about thirty feet above the ocean.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** "At Esalen there are some large baths fed by hot springs situated on a ledge about thirty feet above the ocean." Sitting there, Feynman overhears a man claiming to practise reflexology massage on a woman, and blurts out a remark about the pituitary. Esalen leads into his discussion of the mystical and pseudo-scientific beliefs he investigated.
- **Why it matters:** It is one of the examples that set up the book's closing "cargo cult science" argument.
