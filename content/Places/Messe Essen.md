## The Invasion of the German Board Games — Jonathan Kay

Source: [Original](https://getpocket.com/explore/item/the-invasion-of-the-german-board-games) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8wj549tc3n8wwd202gtc7)

> At Germany’s world-leading Internationale Spieltage (“International Game Day”) fair in Essen—which now attracts an audience from all over the world numbering almost 200,000—bookish introverts are mobbed by groupies looking for selfies.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Game designers such as Phil Eklund are treated as celebrities at the Essen fair, in contrast to the corner-table obscurity he knew at US conventions.
- **Why it matters:** Shows the status designers hold in Germany.
