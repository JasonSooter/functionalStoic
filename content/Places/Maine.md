## How Heat Pumps Became America’s Hottest Home Energy System — Hannah Wallace (2025)

Source: [Original](https://reasonstobecheerful.world/how-heat-pumps-became-americas-hottest-home-energy-system/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebqy85j046mvk23cxw7yn5)

> Maine has traditionally been oil dependent, yet it has rolled out heat pumps faster than the national average.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Maine is the article's lead case: an oil-dependent state whose heat pump program tripled homes heating with electricity from 2014 to 2023. It hit its 100,000 goal early and its governor set a further target; its rural, cold, bipartisan context is dwelt on.
- **Why it matters:** Maine's cold, oil-reliant, rural character is what makes the heat pump success surprising and carries the article's argument.
