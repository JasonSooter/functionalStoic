## A new fossil discovery in Morocco will rewrite the history of human evolution — Ephrat Livni (2017)

Source: [Original](https://qz.com/1000324/fossils-of-homo-sapiens-found-in-morocco-push-back-the-origins-of-our-species-100000-years/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7dbqgh75wq2fegr430jgq)

> But fossils at Jebel Irhoud, a site in Morocco, show paleoanthropologists were mistaken about the date, location, and dispersal of our ancestors.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Fossils from at least five individuals at Jebel Irhoud were re-dated to about 300,000 years ago, pushing Homo sapiens origins back 100,000 years and out of an East African cradle. The site, discovered in the 1960s, has been excavated since 2004, and the fossils lay among gazelle bones and flint tools. The find shows early dispersal across Africa and ties the Maghreb to the rest of the continent.
- **Why it matters:** The whole article is about this site and what it changes in the story of human origins.
