# Alexandra

Township in [[Johannesburg]], beside Sandton. In Born a Crime it is where Trevor Noah spends three years after school with his friend Bongani, selling pirated CDs and DJing street parties.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6z18x0yjwzw2fsneahax0pr)

> Bongani lived in Alexandra. Where Soweto is a sprawling, government-planned ghetto, Alexandra is a tiny, dense pocket of a shantytown, left over from the pre-apartheid days.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapters 14–16. Bongani takes Trevor to Alexandra for a makeover before his matric dance. After school the two DJ street parties there, starting with a New Year's Eve party, and in "The Cheese Boys" Trevor spends his days in the township selling pirated CDs and running small loans. Noah explains its nickname, Gomorrah, "because it has the wildest parties and the worst crimes". He also explains its history: while Sophiatown and other black areas were razed, Alex "fought and held on", and wealthy suburbs like Sandton grew around it. He walks the reader in from the minibus rank and the KFC at its entrance, down the numbered avenues to the Jukskei River.
- **Why it matters:** Alexandra is where Trevor, a "cheese boy" with a suburban education, sees how the economy of the hood keeps people in place. It is the main setting of chapters 15 and 16.
