# Dartmouth

Harbour town on the River Dart in Devon. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ Henry Hudson puts in here instead of Amsterdam after his 1609 voyage, and England forbids him to leave.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8efcr7vmsh5b2ke6q9j9wh7)

> Instead, he dropped anchor in Dartmouth and sent notice to his Dutch employers informing them of his return.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Back from the Hudson River in late 1609, Hudson ignores his Dutch contract and asks for fifteen hundred florins to be sent to Dartmouth for a new voyage. The Dutch directors are "incensed", but the English government issues an Order in Council accusing him of a voyage "to the detriment of his own country" and forbidding him from leaving England. The Dutch consul Emanuel van Meteren fumes that the English are "inconstant, rash, vainglorious".
- **Why it matters:** Hudson's return to England means his next, fatal voyage is sailed for England, and the Anglo-Dutch rivalry over his discoveries begins here.
