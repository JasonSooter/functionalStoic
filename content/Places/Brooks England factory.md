# Brooks England factory

Leather-saddle factory in Smethwick, near Birmingham, West Midlands, [[England]]; Robert Penn tours it in It's All About the Bike and gets the saddle for his bike there.

## It's All About the Bike — Robert Penn (2010)
Source: [[It's All About the Bike - In Pursuit of Happiness on Two Wheels]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce6hbf9wttcqkdc2140bdn)

> The Brooks factory is down a side street in Smethwick, near the Birmingham Canal Navigations.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Penn tours the factory, on a Smethwick side street near the Birmingham Canal Navigations since 1950, with office manager Steven Green. He meets long-serving workers, watches saddles cut, moulded, riveted and chamfered by hand, and picks a Team Professional. The chapter places it in Birmingham's metalworking past, the heart of the world's densest cluster of bicycle makers.
- **Why it matters:** It is where the saddle chapter, and Penn's bike, get their saddle.
