# Changsha South station

Major high-speed station in Changsha, Hunan, where the Beijing–Guangzhou and Shanghai–Kunming lines meet. Simply Railway's overnight sleeper rolls through it late at night.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the video:** After crossing from Guangdong into Hunan, the train passes slowly through Changsha South at 22:50. Thibault notes that the station is a major crossroads of China's high-speed network, where the Beijing–Guangzhou and Shanghai–Kunming lines meet. Right after, he turns in, as the train runs in quiet mode from 22:00 to 06:00.
- **Why it matters:** It marks the last waypoint before the night, and the video uses it to show the network's scale. It is a minor stop in the story; any junction would have done.
