# Storm King Mountain (Colorado)

Mountain near Glenwood Springs, Colorado, site of the 1994 South Canyon fire. Range uses it as a second case of firefighters clinging to their tools.

## Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World — David Epstein (2019)

Source: [[Range]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550188/range-by-david-epstein/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvtryd8p2r35er5ba15src)

> In 1994, on Colorado’s Storm King Mountain, hotshots and smokejumpers faced a Mann Gulch situation when a fire jumped a canyon and erupted through a stand of gambel oak below them.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Fourteen firefighters lost the race with a wall of flame in the canyon; one victim still wore his pack and held a chainsaw 250 feet from safety, while survivor Quentin Rhoades recalled looking for a place to put down his saw.
- **Why it matters:** A second illustration, a generation after Mann Gulch, of the refusal to drop tools.
