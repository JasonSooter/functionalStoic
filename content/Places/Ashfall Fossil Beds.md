# Ashfall Fossil Beds

Fossil site in north-east [[Nebraska]], where Miocene animals lie under volcanic ash. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ Bryson visits with its discoverer, Mike Voorhies.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W)

> Today, the site of Voorhies’s discovery is called Ashfall Fossil Beds State Park

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 1971 Mike Voorhies finds a rhinoceros skull in a gully, which leads to a Miocene water-hole bed of animals buried under up to ten feet of volcanic ash, though Nebraska never had volcanoes. Bryson visits the park's visitor centre and lab, and Voorhies shows him the spot where he made the find.
- **Why it matters:** The puzzle of the ash opens the chapter on Yellowstone's supervolcano.
