## Don’t F\*ck With Our Eagles — Mark Hay (2016)

Source: [Original](http://www.good.is/posts/someone-or-something-is-killing-bald-eagles) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjr11q226710z3r4dfvsk9)

> Three of the birds discovered there are recovering, but five have died, and locals reported that others flying away seemed disoriented, leading some to suspect they could be dangerously ill.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Around Dagsboro, Delaware, about 30 miles from Federalsburg, folks found several more dead and injured eagles over a weekend. Three recovered, five died, and scientists were still testing the dead birds.
- **Why it matters:** The second die-off, close to the first, raises the question of whether the deaths are linked, though the text says there is no proof.
