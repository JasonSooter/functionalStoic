# Limón (Zacatecas)

Hamlet near Moyahua in the Juchipila canyon, Zacatecas. In The Underdogs it is Demetrio Macías's home, burned by federal troops in the opening chapter.

## The Underdogs: A Novel of the Mexican Revolution — Mariano Azuela (1915)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1209983W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8z3kk705yk80kt7pckasw)

> I was born in Limon, close by Moyahua, right in the heart of the Juchipila canyon.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The novel opens at night in Demetrio's hut at Limon, where federal soldiers arrive, are faced down by Demetrio, and later burn his house, seen blazing from the canyon by the river. In chapter XIII Demetrio tells Luis Cervantes he was born at Limon and that a quarrel with the cacique Don Monico drove him into the sierra.
- **Why it matters:** It is the home that is destroyed and the origin of Demetrio's rebellion, which the novel presents as a local quarrel rather than an ideological cause.
