# Montezuma Marsh

Wetland at the north end of Cayuga Lake, New York. The Erie Canal article tells of the malaria rumours that halted canal work there.

## Erie Canal — wikipedia.org (2001)
Source: [Original](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erie_Canal) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5sa4ne9dmk44t1e643pst)

> it was rumored that over 1,000 workers died of "swamp fever" (malaria), and construction was temporarily stopped.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** When construction reached the marsh at the outlet of Cayuga Lake, rumor held that over 1,000 workers died of malaria and work halted. The article notes recent research suggests the toll was far lower and no mass graves have been found, and crews later worked the swampland section when it froze in winter.
- **Why it matters:** A disease-ridden swamp that halted construction and shaped the work schedule.
