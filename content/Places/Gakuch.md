## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)
Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6n0wvnhsgnhz456cawv18)

> The food situation has improved here since I left a few days ago – apricots are ripe now and everyone can have their fill, gratis, including cattle.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** She rests at Gakuch amid ripe apricots, swims three times, and plans her Babusar crossing with advice from the Met. men. Next morning she leaves at 5.15 a.m. and later learns of Punial customs from an educated villager.
- **Why it matters:** Planning stop where she weighs the Babusar crossing and decides to hitch to Lahore on return later.
