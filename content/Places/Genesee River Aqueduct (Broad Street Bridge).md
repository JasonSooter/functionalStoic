# Genesee River Aqueduct (Broad Street Bridge)

Stone aqueduct that carried the Erie Canal over the Genesee River in Rochester, New York; Broad Street now runs over its successor. The Erie Canal article lists it among the route's engineering feats.

## Erie Canal — wikipedia.org (2001)

Source: [Original](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erie_Canal) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5sbmg2r0knf34pxhmg2mt)

> The canal crossed the river on a stone aqueduct, 802 feet (244 m) long and 17 feet (5.2 m) wide, supported by 11 arches.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** The canal crossed the Genesee River near Rochester on a stone aqueduct of 11 arches. The article later notes that a Second Genesee Aqueduct carried the Rochester Subway and now carries Broad Street.
- **Why it matters:** A major crossing point and engineering feat in Rochester.
