## How we planned our Norwegian team offsite as a remote startup — Daniel Doyon (2024)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fcp9jknn1gfw454g6vzhpa)

> But lo and behold, the team overwhelmingly voted for Juvet Landscape Hotel.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Readwise's sixth team offsite was held here in the fjords of Norway, in late April 2024. The team voted for it over a Portuguese resort despite 20+ hours of travel, and it became the model venue (20-30 beds, rentable whole, architecturally significant, communal meals, meeting space). Activities (founder presentation, hackathon, fjord excursions) were run from the hotel.
- **Why it matters:** The entire case-study of the article is set here, and it defines the venue criteria the company adopts. Set elsewhere (e.g. the Algarve option) the article would read as a coastal rather than mountain retreat.
