# Eden Park Police Station

Police station serving [[Eden Park (Ekurhuleni)|Eden Park]] on the East Rand. In Born a Crime the officers there refuse to take Trevor Noah's mother's charge against Abel after he hits her.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zbjvad97wkh73d0pmn0zgh)

> We walked to the Eden Park police station, over a kilometer away. My mom marched us in, and there were two cops on duty at the front desk.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 18 ("My Mother's Life"). After Abel hits her for the first time, Trevor's mother walks Trevor and the baby Andrew through the cold night to the station to lay a charge. The two officers talk down to her, ask what she did to make her husband angry, and refuse to write up a charge sheet. Abel then drives in, still drunk, and the station turns into "a boys' club". Afterwards the family stays with Trevor's grandmother in Soweto for a while.
- **Why it matters:** this is where Trevor, aged nine, learns that the police will not protect his mother: "They were men first, and police second." The pattern repeats each time Abel turns violent, up to the shooting.
