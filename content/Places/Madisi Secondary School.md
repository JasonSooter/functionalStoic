# Madisi Secondary School

Public boarding school in Madisi, off the M1 highway in Malawi's Dowa District. In _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ it is where William Kamkwamba goes back to school after five years out.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8ebd9gnwcp6rye5e6zh095q)

> The classrooms in Madisi had solid roofs that didn’t leak and smooth concrete floors that didn’t have holes.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Once the windmill has made the papers and the radio, his mentor Dr. Mchazime gets him a place here despite his age. William takes a pickup to [[Kasungu]], a minibus "down M1 highway to the small town of Madisi", and then walks "a kilometer with my suitcase bouncing behind on the gravel road" to the steel gates. After [[Kachokolo Secondary School]] he finds real desks and fluorescent lights, but the chemistry lab has expired chemicals and the dorms are crowded, two boys to a bed. Older classmates tease him until they see the newspaper article, and he finds refuge in the library. Mchazime takes him out of school from here to prepare for the trip to Arusha.
- **Why it matters:** It ends the dropout years that run through the whole book: the windmill gets him back into a classroom. It is also the bridge to [[Ngurdoto Mountain Lodge|TED in Arusha]].
