# Latche

François Mitterrand's country house near Soustons in the Landes, southwest France. Scene of his last ortolan dinner in _Apeirogon_.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7wgp6z8mn02dagjqca0jr)

> The ortolans were crated and driven in a darkened van to Mitterrand’s country house in Latche where he had spent his childhood summers.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Mitterrand eats a final ortolan dinner at Latche, hooded under a napkin, and dies days later. The story echoes the ortolans Tarek frees.
- **Why it matters:** A full fragment is devoted to this scene, setting up the ortolan motif.
