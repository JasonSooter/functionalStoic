## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7xfnfn69zgkdeayh0xqxq)

> One night in an abandoned warehouse in El-Arish, on the north coast of Egypt, the Commander of Rami’s unit sat down in the middle of a pile of grain.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** During Rami's first war, the Commander counts out grains after losing eight of eleven tanks. Rami steps outside into streaks of bomblight.
- **Why it matters:** Source of Rami's war memories.
