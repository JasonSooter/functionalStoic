## Why Teaching Kids To Cook Is the Key To A Better Future — Valentina Valentini (2016)

Source: [Original](https://www.good.is/articles/edible-schoolyards-new-orleans-lice-waters-chez-panisse-foundation) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jx5j0tbwxzcvx92sdm5e8a)

> at Martin Luther King Jr. Middle School in Berkeley 20 years ago, Waters has expanded her efforts countrywide to change the way kids.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Site of the first Edible Schoolyard, established about 20 years earlier by Alice Waters. A 2010 internal study there found students broadened their taste for fruits and vegetables.
- **Why it matters:** Origin point of the movement the article follows.
