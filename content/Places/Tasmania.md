# Tasmania

Tasmania is an island state of Australia south of the mainland. _Sapiens_ uses it first as a world sealed off for 12,000 years and then as the scene of the destruction of its native people.

## Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind — Yuval Noah Harari (2014)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17075811W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m758gr1cfrbgmc892bw9ng)

> Consider Tasmania, a medium-sized island south of Australia.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Cut off from Australia around 10,000 BC by rising seas, Tasmania held a few thousand hunter-gatherers who had no outside contact for 12,000 years. Later the book returns to it: within a century of Cook's arrival settlers drove the Tasmanians off the best land and hunted them, the survivors were confined by missionaries, and anthropologists took their remains; Truganini's skeleton was buried only in 1976.
- **Why it matters:** It serves twice: as the purest example of an isolated human world, and as the starkest case of science and empire working together against a native people.
