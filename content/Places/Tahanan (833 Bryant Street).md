# Tahanan (833 Bryant Street)

Tahanan is a 145-unit supportive housing building for the chronically homeless at 833 Bryant Street in San Francisco's SoMa neighborhood, completed in 2021. _Abundance_ opens its chapter on government with it as a model of fast, cheap affordable housing.

## Abundance — Ezra Klein & Derek Thompson (2025)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL44034521W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m77jg3bm43prv8pf1295vb)

> Tahanan was built on the former site of a parking lot and temporary bail bond office.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 3 opens inside Tahanan, with its murals, water damage, social workers and dogs. Built on a former parking lot and bail bond office, it was fast-tracked by Scott Wiener's 2017 legislation and paid for by a $65 million Schwab grant, which let it avoid the rules that come with public money. It went up in three years for less than $400,000 per unit, against Bay Area norms of six years and $600,000 to $700,000.
- **Why it matters:** It is the chapter's proof that affordable housing can be built quickly and cheaply when it escapes public-money requirements. It is one of the few places in the book the authors describe from the inside.
