# Warmoesstraat

One of the oldest streets in Amsterdam, near Dam Square. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ the first Dutch spice expedition is planned at Reynier Pauw's house there.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m82eft1n9c3zf71jedya39y9)

> For more than three years the inhabitants of Amsterdam’s Warmoestraat, a genteel neighbourhood close to the city’s main square, had watched an unusual amount of activity at the house of Reynier Pauw.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** For more than three years the neighbours watch comings and goings at Reynier Pauw's house; there the merchants study Petrus Plancius's maps, hear Linschoten's account of the Indies after his return in 1592, and resolve to send the first Dutch expedition east.
- **Why it matters:** It is where the meticulously planned Dutch venture, which the book contrasts with English improvisation, begins.
