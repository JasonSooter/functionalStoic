# Gorges de la Nesque

Limestone gorge of the Nesque between Monieux and Méthamis, Vaucluse, [[Provence]], France. It shows up as the route and scenery of a cycling post that climbs through it towards Monieux and [[Sault]].

## Mistletoe, Lavender — Mostly Bikes x Me (2026)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crb5s8zpe0p1e33w0jahp2)

> Rode up through the Gorges de la Nesque with a plan to grab lunch in the tiny commune of Monieux.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** The rider climbs up the gorge planning lunch in Monieux, finds the café shut, continues to Sault, and returns along the south side of the gorge through evergreens, lavender and mistletoe.
- **Why it matters:** The gorge is the ride's route and scenery and shapes the lunch detour; the same author's other Sault rides use it too. A short post, so a light but recurring waypoint.
