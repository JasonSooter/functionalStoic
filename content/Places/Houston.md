# Houston

Houston is the largest city in Texas, on the Gulf Coast plain. In Klein and Thompson's _Abundance_ it is the key example of a city that makes building easy, and in McConaughey's _Greenlights_ it is the family home where he earns his father's regard and later mourns him.

## Abundance — Ezra Klein & Derek Thompson (2025)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL44034521W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m77y1ft9nfjxmt718q91xk)

> Houston has no zoning rules at all, though it does have some land use regulations.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Chapter 3 uses Houston to answer critics who say easing zoning won't help. Its metro area issued almost 70,000 housing permits in 2023, against about 7,500 for San Francisco. Houston has the lowest homelessness rate of any major US city, and its median home costs a bit over $300,000.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's key comparison for the claim that making building easy brings housing costs down.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zp3xqmby6594e9nkbzv0sk)

> I drove home to Houston that night. We had an Irish wake two days later where hundreds of friends gathered and told stories about him, just the way he’d instructed us to when he talked about his passing.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Back home in Texas at nineteen, he and his father stop at a neon-lit pool hall in a strip mall on the southwest side of Houston; when the bouncer stops his father over the bill, he knocks the man down. "That night was my rite of passage": from then on he can go to the bar with his father and brother Mike. Later, five days into shooting Dazed and Confused in Austin, his mother calls to say his father has died, and he drives home to Houston for the Irish wake.
- **Why it matters:** Both of his rites of passage with his father happen here; he calls the loss his "most seminal rite of passage into manhood" and carves "less impressed, more involved" into a tree.
