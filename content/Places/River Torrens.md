# River Torrens

River running through central [[Adelaide]] to the sea, lined by a linear park path. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne follows it by bike to an empty beach.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76rj7ef0052879mav469673)

> I continue west and ride my bike to the beach by following a bike path along the Torrens River that runs through the center of Adelaide.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Byrne rides west along the Torrens path through eucalyptus groves full of magpies and pelicans until the gums thin out and the river reaches the sea. On a hot Sunday afternoon he counts only six people on the beach, which elsewhere would be packed.
- **Why it matters:** The emptiness of a city beach shows how new and unencroached the settled country still feels to him.
