## Hiking Tennessee’s Mount LeConte — Bob Doucette (2015)

Source: [Original](https://proactiveoutside.wordpress.com/2015/12/08/hiking-tennessees-mount-leconte/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1j1e92t0njk1jb5mffq9)

> That 11-miler is also the steepest, and just so happens to be the route we picked – the Alum Cave Trail.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The author and his sister-in-law Jen hike the 11-mile round-trip trail from the trailhead along Alum Cave Creek, past Arch Rock and Alum Cave Bluff, up to LeConte. The route is described step by step, including grades, ice and cables, and a getting-there guide.
- **Why it matters:** The whole article is built around this route; the text details its character and difficulty.
