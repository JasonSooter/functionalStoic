# Uchur River

Large river of southeastern Yakutia, a right tributary of the Aldan, here pinned where the Gonam flows into it. In _Last of the Breed_ Joe Mack follows the frozen Gonam down to the Uchur and crosses it on the ice at night, half frozen.

## Last of the Breed — Louis L'Amour (1986)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL51048W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6xr36r62asj1cvqs8v1ar0c)

> Near the base of that mountain the Gonam flowed into the Uchur.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** While living with the exiles, Joe Mack plans to "follow down the Gonam River to where it met the Uchur, cross that river and head across country to the Maya." After three days hidden at the Ningam, he runs along the Gonam, reaches the Uchur after midnight, follows a well-worn road along the bank past ice-fishermen's shelters, and crosses the ice tapping ahead with a stick. Collapsing on the far bank, he is pulled into a shack by a harsh-voiced woman who feeds and shelters him for a night.
- **Why it matters:** The crossing is the moment he leaves the exiles' country behind and heads east alone; the frozen-river ordeal and the stranger's help are among the book's starkest survival scenes. ⚠️ The book does not say where on the Uchur he crosses beyond "near the base of that mountain" at the Gonam mouth; the pin is that confluence.
