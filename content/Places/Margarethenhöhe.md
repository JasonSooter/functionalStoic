Garden-city district on the south-western edge of [[Essen]]. In the BBSR's 15-minute-city report it stands out less for proximity than for the strength of its neighbourhood.

## Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde — Björn Schwarze et al. (BBSR) (2025)
Source: [[Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde]] · [Original](https://www.bbsr.bund.de/BBSR/DE/veroeffentlichungen/bbsr-online/2025/bbsr-online-027-2025-dl.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7kmj6nn0mkcawzmvbryb2hj)

> Besonders auffallend ist dies in Essen-Margarethenhöhe, wo auch die lokale Verbundenheit außerordentlich stark ausgeprägt ist.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The report cites it as the garden-city type among its quarters (6.1 minutes, 96 % fulfilment, index 78.5). Local shops are limited, but residents reach them easily by Stadtbahn, the one quarter where public transport matters for supply trips. It has the most local volunteering and attachment, and once more the best neighbourly feeling, while Essen's inner-city Rüttenscheid is rated below average on that count.
- **Why it matters:** It shows a quarter whose social life is strong even where its 15-minute score is middling, a contrast the report draws within one city.
