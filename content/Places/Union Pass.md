# Union Pass

Pass on the Continental Divide in western Wyoming, between the Gros Ventre and Wind River ranges. The Ryback brothers cross it at night on a forced march that strains them both.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bese086j8br1wjay6e0cr2)

> It was dark by the time we reached Lake of the Woods and Union Pass, where our path became a moonlit logging road.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Eric pushes for a marathon: hike until two in the morning and reach the Green River Lakes by ten. It is dark when they reach Lake of the Woods and Union Pass, where the path becomes a moonlit logging road and the only water is stagnant. Tim falls asleep on his feet and Eric is curt with him; Eric admits later that he would only realize "the devastating effects that this march would have."
- **Why it matters:** It is the turning point of the "Bad Times" chapter, where the brothers' partnership begins to fray.
