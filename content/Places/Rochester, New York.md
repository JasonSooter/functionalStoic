# Rochester, New York

City on the Genesee River in western New York. In _Wedding of the Waters_ the canal makes it a boomtown of mills.

## Wedding of the Waters - The Erie Canal and the Making of a Great Nation — Peter L. Bernstein (2005)
Source: [[Wedding of the Waters - The Erie Canal and the Making of a Great Nation]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL3294009W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kve54wxm1ghka5dbvzww9p)

> The parade reached Rochester on the rainy morning of the twenty-seventh.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Geddes's 1808 survey passes just east of the town, and the canal crosses the Genesee there on its largest aqueduct. The Wedding of the Waters parade reaches Rochester on a rainy morning, hailed by the boat Young Lion of the West with its local animals, and the book later lists its paper mill, carriage factory and Hydraulic Building as signs of the Flour City's growth.
- **Why it matters:** Rochester is one of the cities the canal created out of a crossing point.
