## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)

Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbmsqmf6z9ahf00mb2kmps)

> In New York City, they have built a stunning “cycle track” along Sands Street in Brooklyn.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The cycle track cost \$13 million per mile, nearly ten times a signal-protected path and a hundred times a standard separated path; Speck asks whether building one mile is worth losing a hundred of separated path.
- **Why it matters:** Used as the cautionary example against greedy bicycle infrastructure.
