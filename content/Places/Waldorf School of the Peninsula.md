# Waldorf School of the Peninsula

Private Waldorf school in Los Altos, Silicon Valley, California. In _The Anxious Generation_ it is the device-free school that many tech executives choose for their own children.

## The Anxious Generation — Jonathan Haidt (2024)
Source: [[The Anxious Generation]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL37575384W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kk2gzkd6cqf5cqw4h5a129)

> such as the Waldorf School of the Peninsula, where all digital devices—phones, laptops, tablets—are prohibited.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Haidt cites the school as one where all digital devices are banned and where many tech executives send their children, and contrasts it with public schools that hand every pupil a device. He concludes that Waldorf is probably right.
- **Why it matters:** A pointed example in the argument against screens in schools.
