# Longview, Texas

Oil-boom city in the Piney Woods of East Texas. In Greenlights it is where Matthew McConaughey grows up from 1979, builds a thirteen-storey tree house and finishes high school.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zp3tzrr6n365afv46ad2kq)

> The thirteenth floor was over one hundred feet above the ground. From there I could see all the way to downtown Longview, fifteen miles away.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In the summer of 1979 the family moves here for the oil-boom money, and he and his father live alone in a double-wide trailer on the outskirts of town. He explores the Piney Woods barefoot with a BB gun, cuts through the fence of a lumberyard at night, and over six weeks builds a thirteen-storey tree house around a white pine. Later the family moves to a house on the other side of town, where his high-school years play out, including trading his truck for a candy-red 300ZX at the local Nissan dealership and then trading it back.
- **Why it matters:** "Where Uvalde taught me to deal, Longview taught me to dream." The tree house summer is the first big "greenlight" of the book.
