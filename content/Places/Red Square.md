# Red Square

Moscow's central square, beside the Kremlin. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is the scene of Yuri Andropov's funeral, where Margaret Thatcher performs a part partly scripted by Gordievsky.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7vbqfm3p22cy0x1b8fh86f9)

> Thousands of people filled Red Square for the funeral of Yuri Andropov on February 14, 1984.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Thatcher attends the funeral in mourning dress, a hot-water bottle under her coat against the cold. While other Western leaders chatter and even snigger when the coffin is dropped, she stays "suitably solemn", then spends forty minutes with Andropov's successor at the Kremlin reception. Her approach draws on advice that came through Gordievsky.
- **Why it matters:** It shows Gordievsky's intelligence turning into diplomacy: the book presents the funeral as a staged opening to the Soviet leadership, a move toward easing Cold War tension.
