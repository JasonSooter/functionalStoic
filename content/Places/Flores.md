# Flores

Flores is an island in the Lesser Sunda chain of eastern Indonesia. _Sapiens_ uses it as the home of Homo floresiensis, the dwarf humans shaped by island isolation.

## Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind — Yuval Noah Harari (2014)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17075811W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m74xgqsk07wz75dfa763n1)

> On another Indonesian island – the small island of Flores – archaic humans underwent a process of dwarfing.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Archaic humans reached Flores when sea levels were low and were trapped when the seas rose. On a resource-poor island the big ones died first, and over generations they became dwarves, Homo floresiensis, at most a metre tall and 25 kg, who still made stone tools and hunted dwarf elephants. The last of them vanished about 12,000 years ago.
- **Why it matters:** A vivid case of isolation producing a distinct human species, part of the argument that Sapiens once had many siblings.
