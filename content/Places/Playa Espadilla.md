# Playa Espadilla

Long beach at Manuel Antonio on Costa Rica's Pacific coast, just outside the national park. Retire by 40's family spends their beach days there.

## Our 2 Week Costa Rica Vacation — retirebyforty (2015)

Source: [Original](https://retireby40.org/our-2-week-costa-rica-vacation/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gm1p8avempwt8qp7bxwkbz)

> Playa Espadilla was right behind our hotel and we hung out here the rest of the time.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Playa Espadilla is right behind their hotel, and the family spends the rest of their time there: their four-year-old climbs trees, collects shells, chases crabs and plays in the surf; after 5 pm the tourists leave and they have the beach almost to themselves, with lightning shows in the evening.
- **Why it matters:** The author calls staying by this beach the best part of the trip, set against cold Oregon beaches back home.
