# Nenagh railway station

The railway station of Nenagh, a market town in County Tipperary near Lough Derg. In _The Country Girls Trilogy_ it is where Kate's father brings her home from Dublin and where she escapes by the morning train.

## The Country Girls Trilogy — Edna O'Brien (1986)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL69252W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75qhfbk4hahsh09hzzxxk)

> “I want to go to Nenagh to catch the eleven o’clock train,” I said, and wondered if Jack had told him the whole story.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** In The Lonely Girl Kate's drunken father takes her home from Dublin by train, singing in the bar car with Tim Healy, who is 'on his way to Nenagh'. Later Jack Holland arranges for Tom Duggan, the one-handed Ferret, to drive her there in a battered Ford; he proposes on the way, and she waits an hour in the Ladies' Waiting Room before boarding.
- **Why it matters:** Nenagh is the gateway between the home place and Dublin, and Kate's dawn escape from it is one of the novel's turning points. Its role is functional rather than scenic.
