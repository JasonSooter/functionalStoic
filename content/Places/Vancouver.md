## Why Successful People Spend 10 Hours A Week Just Thinking — Brian Scudamore (2016)
Source: [Original](https://medium.com/@BrianScudamore/why-successful-people-spend-10-hours-a-week-just-thinking-79a5b16eb404) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jkzfjq9s4vzm0q7n0xtdz1)

> I often spend the day wandering around Vancouver.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author spends his Monday thinking day away from the office, wandering Vancouver. He moves between coffee shops, forest, bike rides, the beach and park benches whenever he feels stuck.
- **Why it matters:** The city is where his method plays out, but nothing specific to Vancouver is argued; any walkable city would do.
