# Women's Museum (Aarhus)

Denmark's women's history museum (now the Gender Museum) in Aarhus. In The Year of Living Danishly, Russell visits to learn the history of women's rights.

## The Year of Living Danishly — Helen Russell (2015)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20000553W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f0rdnb70mhbsazw7q1j1yy)

> Aarhus, Denmark’s second city, is home to one of the world’s few women’s history museums.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Russell drives to Aarhus to visit one of the world's few women's history museums, where a guide gives her an oral history of women's rights in Denmark; she also looks at the city's shops.
- **Why it matters:** It is the main setting of the June chapter's research.
