## Retiring Retirement — Linda Marsa (2016)

Source: [Original](http://nautil.us/issue/36/aging/retiring-retirement) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvq31trheae900254kaxwv)

> In 2007, the managers of its 2,500-employee powertrain plant in Bavaria were wringing their hands over their aging staff, which was projected to rise in average age from 39 to 47 over the next decade.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Managers trial a 'pensioners' line' with ergonomic chairs, magnifiers and custom shoes for \$50,000; productivity of older workers rose 7 percent within a year.
- **Why it matters:** Main case study that older workers can stay productive; the place is the setting of the example.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The article says only "its 2,500-employee powertrain plant in Bavaria"; reporting on the 2007 experiment places it at BMW's Dingolfing plant (Equipment World, "BMW revives an aging workforce").
