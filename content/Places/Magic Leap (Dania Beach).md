## Inside Magic Leap, The Secretive $4.5 Billion Startup Changing Computing Forever — David M. Ewalt (2016)
Source: [Original](https://www.forbes.com/sites/davidewalt/2016/11/02/inside-magic-leap-the-secretive-4-5-billion-startup-changing-computing-forever/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy9jcqke6fh9km0edgz81n)

> The hottest ticket in tech is an invitation to a banal South Florida business park, indistinguishable on the outside from countless other office buildings that dot America's suburban landscape.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The article opens inside the ordinary-looking South Florida business park where mixed-reality illusions (floating TV, robots, monsters) are demoed. Later it names the town as Dania Beach, a town of 30,000 south of Fort Lauderdale, where billions in venture money flowed to a secretive startup.
- **Why it matters:** The contrast between a banal suburban exterior and the secret technology inside frames the piece; the offices are being vacated for the new campus.
