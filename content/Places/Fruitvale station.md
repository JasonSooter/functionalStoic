> Responding to reports of a fight at the Fruitvale Station early in the morning of New Year’s Day 2009, Pirone saw Oscar Grant walking between train cars and ordered him to wait with others who had been corralled for questioning.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Site of Oscar Grant’s killing by BART officers; newly learned facts show the officer’s testimony was a lie, which the author ties to white disbelief.
