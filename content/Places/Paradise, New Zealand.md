# Paradise, New Zealand

Paradise is a rural locality near Glenorchy at the head of Lake Wakatipu in Otago, New Zealand. It is pinned for _The Hobbit_ because Peter Jackson's Hobbit films shot Beorn's house there; it is not a place in the book.

## The Hobbit (Middle-Earth Universe) — J. R. R. Tolkien (1937)

Source: [[The Hobbit]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL27482W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m77d4v7s1bche9hyej67xq)

> He lives in an oak-wood and has a great wooden house; and as a man he keeps cattle and horses which are nearly as marvellous as himself.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In the book, after the eagles leave them at the Carrock, Gandalf leads the company to Beorn, a skin-changer who lives in a great wooden house in an oak-wood with talking animals and great bees. Gandalf introduces the dwarves two by two; Beorn feeds and shelters them and advises their route to Mirkwood.
- **Why it matters:** Beorn's hall is the last refuge before Mirkwood, and Beorn returns at the Battle of Five Armies.
- **Real vs. fictional:** This is where Peter Jackson's Hobbit films were shot; it is not a place in the book.
- **Where is it?:** Wikipedia (The Hobbit film series, filming locations) lists Paradise, Otago, for Beorn's house (citing Stuff, 10 November 2011), with Ohuto Station near Ohakune for its surroundings.
