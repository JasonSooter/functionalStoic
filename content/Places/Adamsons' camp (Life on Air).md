## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1rxbn1t5aktmgfnty2se)

> Geoff and I flew to Nairobi . There we chartered a small plane to take us up to the Adamson's camp in the wild country in the far north of Kenya.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Attenborough and Geoff Mulligan stay in the camp of Joy and George Adamson, with a thorn-hedge boma, tents and a riverbank. He wakes on a sand bank to find Elsa the lioness's jaw above his face, then films her and her cubs while the Adamsons quarrel. Later sections trace the violent fates of the lions and the Adamsons.
- **Why it matters:** The main stop on the way to Madagascar and the setting of the Born Free encounter; the text dwells on the camp's tension beneath its idyll.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Best guess. The book says only that the camp lay 'in the wild country in the far north of Kenya', with a thorn-hedge boma and tents by a river, and that Elsa the lioness and her cubs lived there. Wikipedia's articles on Elsa and on Meru National Park say Elsa was released and is buried in what is now Meru National Park, one of the two areas where the Adamsons raised her, so the pin is Meru National Park. Alternatives: the Isiolo area further north, or Kora National Park (George Adamson's later camp, which the book does not name).
