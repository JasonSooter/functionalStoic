# Limburg an der Lahn

Cathedral town on the Lahn in [[Hesse]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is the hometown of Frans's SS comrade Karl Reinhard, where Frans begins an affair with Karl's wife and becomes godfather to her daughter.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zmw8qa5172my3764x17w8)

> A week later, he was standing beside the parents at her christening ceremony in the beautiful cathedral of Limburg an der Lahn, without Mimousse.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Karl Reinhard comes home to his "sleepy hometown" after the war and marries his high-school sweetheart Claudia. From 1952 Frans visits under the guise of "comrade service", and when Andrea is born on 5 March 1953 he insists on being godfather. The affair continues until the Lingen operation is shut down and German justice drives Frans away; in 1961 Karl's workshop burns and the marriage sours. Years later, driving past on the Autobahn, Frans tells his son the story.
- **Why it matters:** The town holds the secret at the centre of Part 3, "The Guardian Angel": a hidden daughter whose fate pulls Frans back to Germany decades later.
