# Place de la Bastille

Square in eastern central [[Paris]], France, around the July Column. In _Happy City_ it is where Charles Montgomery's exhilarating, nerve-racking Vélib' ride through Paris traffic ends.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m84c7kgx8shds1yqrj4kv4tz)

> I barreled toward Bastille and the monument to the Revolution of 1830.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Chapter 9. After leaving Eric Britton, Montgomery rides a shared Vélib' bike down Rue Monge, across the Seine and around the Bastille roundabout at sunset, under the column topped by Dumont's gilded figure of Freedom holding a broken chain. The ride, through cars, buses and scooters, feels thrilling and free.
- **Why it matters:** The ride shows the limits of Paris's bike share: exhilarating for the bold, but too frightening for most people, which leads the chapter to argue that streets must feel safe before most people will cycle.
