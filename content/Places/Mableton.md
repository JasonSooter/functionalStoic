# Mableton

Suburban community in Cobb County, west of [[Atlanta]], Georgia, USA. In _Happy City_ it is Charles Montgomery's case of a cul-de-sac suburb trying to give itself a town centre, and finding the law in the way.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m84c8ewsxpqnvs0a8g3dhczx)

> She was standing in the middle of the parking lot in front of the neocolonial facade of the U.S. post office in Mableton, insisting that this spot, right here, was the center of town.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 12, "Retrofitting Sprawl". Montgomery stands with resident Robin Meyer and architects Galina Tachieva and Scott Ball in the post office parking lot, looking across a swale and the five lanes of Floyd Road toward the 1843 Mable House. Mableton has cul-de-sacs, strip malls and no downtown, and walking to the arts centre is "a death march". They imagine a square, shops, a slower Floyd Road and seniors' housing; "It was also illegal."
- **Why it matters:** Mableton turns the chapter's argument into a single afternoon: what would make the suburb walkable, safe and welcoming to children and seniors is forbidden by Cobb County zoning and road standards. Earlier in the book it is also the car-dependent foil to [[Midtown Atlanta]].
