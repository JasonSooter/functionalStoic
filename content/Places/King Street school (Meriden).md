# King Street school (Meriden)

Elementary school on King Street in South Meriden, Connecticut, unnamed in the text. In Tomie dePaola's memoir it is where he starts kindergarten in 1939, and walks out on the first day.

## 26 Fairmount Avenue — Tomie dePaola (1999)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/351865/26-fairmount-avenue-by-tomie-depaola/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8d25str8gqddk6ypkdpsje6)

> I walked up the long front stairs where the boy with the umbrella had floated down during the hurricane.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** During the 1938 hurricane a boy with an umbrella is blown down the school's front steps. In fall 1939 Tomie walks to King Street with his mother to start kindergarten. On his first day he walks home alone, because kindergarten won't teach him to read. His parents are called and send him back. Miss Burke, the principal, is introduced.
- **Why it matters:** It is the memoir's school chapter and a turning point for Tomie, who wants to learn to read straight away. The school matters only as the place where this happens.
- **Real vs. fictional:** ⚠️ The text names only King Street, not the school, so the pin is on the street.
