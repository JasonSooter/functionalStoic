# Tracy, California

Commuter town in San Joaquin County, [[California]], on the far side of the Diablo Range from the Bay Area. In _Happy City_ it is where Kim Holbrook's two-hour super-commute breaks down.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m83qhkbfq3rr186w8vffztv6)

> Hot tears were streaming down her face when she heard the sound of a police siren and pulled over on Eleventh Avenue in Tracy.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Kim Holbrook, Randy Strausser's daughter, lives in a modest ranch house in Tracy and commutes two hours each way to a job at the Hewlett Foundation in Menlo Park, fifty miles west over the Diablo Range. When day care calls to say her toddler is feverish, she speeds back and is pulled over on Eleventh Avenue. That night she and her husband vow to "unstretch" their lives.
- **Why it matters:** It turns the cost of distance in the dispersed city into one family's crisis, and marks the generation that starts to choose proximity over space.
