# Haarlem

City in North Holland, west of Amsterdam. Not Just Bikes uses its historic centre to show Dutch klinker (brick) paving: reused weathered bricks on the main square and smooth bricks on a busy cycle street.

## Why Streets in the Netherlands are Made of Bricks — Not Just Bikes (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq1kV6V_jvI) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8cqw1ncnzy9cnbvbc721bwy)

> This is the historic square in Haarlem. And you can see that there are these nice old and weathered klinkers.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** Haarlem's historic square is the showcase. Its weathered klinkers look original but aren't: in the 1960s the area was paved with asphalt and full of cars, and the bricks now there were brought in from other parts of the city. A street in the centre of Haarlem shows the smoother, more expensive klinkers used on busy cycle routes where asphalt would look wrong.
- **Why it matters:** The square is the video's proof that klinkers can be lifted, kept and relaid, which is the whole case for bricks over asphalt. The video doesn't name the square (⚠️ probably the Grote Markt), so the pin is on the city centre.
