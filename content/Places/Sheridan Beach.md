# Sheridan Beach

Lakeshore neighbourhood at the north end of [[Lake Washington]], in Lake Forest Park near Seattle. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is the finish line of the Pacific Coast Regatta, where Joe's freshmen win in 1934 and the _Husky Clipper_ beats California in 1936.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m878t9rktec9b76ae823jrt8)

> the train would run north along the western shore of Lake Washington, paralleling the racecourse all the way from Sand Point to the finish line at Sheridan Beach, then returning to the starting line before the next race.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Each April the Pacific Coast Regatta finishes here at a barge marked with a black arrow, with an observation train running along the west shore from Sand Point and hundreds of boats anchored off the beach. On April 18, 1936, the student ferry _Chippewa_, a navy cruiser and nearly four hundred other vessels head north to the finish. Moch keeps his crew at a low stroke and lets Cal's challenges go unanswered, and a long roar goes up from the tens of thousands along Sheridan Beach as the _Husky Clipper_ crosses three lengths ahead of the _California Clipper_. About a mile down the lake, Joe's father, his half-siblings and Joyce follow the race from Fred Rantz's dock.
- **Why it matters:** This race convinces Seattle, and Ulbrickson, that Joe's boat is the one for Berlin; it is the crew's first big public proof as a varsity eight.
