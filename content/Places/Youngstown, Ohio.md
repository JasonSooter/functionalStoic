## A World Without Work — Derek Thompson (2015)
Source: [Original](https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/archive/2015/07/world-without-work/395294/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7ebzmsdgpeksk4z8pq5s5)

> The end of work is still just a futuristic concept for most of the United States, but it is something like a moment in history for Youngstown, Ohio, one its residents can cite with precision: September 19, 1977.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The author travels to Youngstown as a national metaphor for the decline of labor. On September 19, 1977 Youngstown Sheet and Tube announced the closure of its Campbell Works mill; the city lost 50,000 jobs in five years and suffered a psychological and cultural breakdown. He returns to it throughout, interviewing residents who live in a post-wage precariat, and ends by leaving Youngstown after talking with Howard Jesko.
- **Why it matters:** Anchors the essay: both the cautionary case and a model of contingent resourcefulness.
