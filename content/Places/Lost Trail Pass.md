# Lost Trail Pass

Pass on the Montana–Idaho border in the Bitterroot Mountains. The Ryback brothers camp near it on their first day out of Sula, and Eric recalls its Lewis and Clark history.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bes1w23w2hr305r22vxse4)

> The pass was also an historic site, a place where Lewis and Clark had stopped because they had lost the trail they were following to the Pacific Coast.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** The brothers camp near Lost Trail Pass on the first day out of Sula. Eric explains that it is a historic site where Lewis and Clark stopped because they had lost the trail they were following to the Pacific, and recounts their retreat toward Lemhi Pass. The stretch follows his account of the Big Hole and the Nez Perce.
- **Why it matters:** A minor waypoint, but one where the book reads the Divide as a route of American history as well as wilderness.
