## How to Do Nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)

Source: [[How to Do Nothing — Reader's Companion]] · [[How to Do Nothing]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20078135W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebv8jkzyh1mg3vraavtf1c)

> I went to a wedding in Palm Springs that was being held at the Ace Hotel.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** She finds the hotel a generic aesthetic simulacrum, sits by the pool among influencers, and is fixated by the San Jacinto Mountains she cannot reach without a car.
- **Why it matters:** It supplies the contrast of placeless surfaces versus the attention-demanding land beyond; the hotel is minor.
