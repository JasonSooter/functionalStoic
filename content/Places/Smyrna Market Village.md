# Smyrna Market Village

New town centre built in 2003 along Spring Street in Smyrna, a suburb north-west of [[Atlanta]], Georgia. In _Happy City_ it is the cautionary tale of a retrofit that looks like a village but doesn't work like one.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m84ph0v1qvz6bqarb1b5j4mr)

> I wandered Smyrna’s new Market Village with the mayor, Max Bacon, in 2010.

- **Role:** case study
- **In the book:** Chapter 12, section "The Aesthetic Trap". Montgomery walks Spring Street with Mayor Max Bacon and architect Mike Sizemore: faux-antebellum facades, a fountain, a gazebo and a Romanesque city hall, which Sizemore happily compares to Disney's Main Street U.S.A. The village was a commercial hit when it opened in 2003, but residents refused buildings that would hide the civic facades from five-lane Atlanta Road.
- **Why it matters:** Smyrna chose "a design that felt like a village but did not actually perform like one", the book's lesson that the system matters more than the package.
