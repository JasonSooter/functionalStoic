# Williamsburg Bridge

Suspension bridge over the East River between Manhattan's Lower East Side and Williamsburg, Brooklyn, in [[New York City]]. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne crosses it during a blackout and concludes that "electrical power is political."

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m772xcv6nwhvvc1by124ydes)

> I assume that the party is still on, so as it gets dark I bike over the Williamsburg Bridge.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Riding to his assistant's good-bye party in Greenpoint, Byrne finds the bridges full of cyclists because subway and bus service is still intermittent. From the bridge he sees that the Village and SoHo have power while parts of the East Village and most of the Lower East Side are still dark, and uptown sparkles; halfway across, where the lamps are fed from Brooklyn, the light comes on.
- **Why it matters:** It is the punchline of the blackout section: who gets power back first shows how the city ranks its neighborhoods.
