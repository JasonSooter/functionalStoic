# University of Texas at Austin

Public university in central Austin, Texas. In Greenlights it is where Matthew McConaughey goes to college instead of SMU and drops law school plans for film school.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zp3z9rs0s9kgyj9r1sq6m4)

> Out of respect for my dad, I attended the University of Texas at Austin but never told him why.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** His brother tells him their father is close to bankruptcy now that the oil boom has dried up, and that SMU would cost far more than public UT, so he switches to UT and claims he just likes Longhorns better than Mustangs. At his fraternity he picks up a copy of The Greatest Salesman in the World and, after a friend at NYU has suggested film school, decides to leave the law track for film, then calls his father to tell him.
- **Why it matters:** The college years in Austin are where the book's career story starts; without the switch to film school there is no Dazed and Confused.
