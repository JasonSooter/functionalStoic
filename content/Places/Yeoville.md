# Yeoville

Inner-city neighbourhood of Johannesburg, around Rockey Street. In Born a Crime it is where Trevor Noah's Swiss father lives after apartheid, and where Trevor spends his Sundays with him.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6z18kekx4p34abvp47b5pn6)

> After apartheid fell, my father moved from Hillbrow to Yeoville, a formerly quiet, residential neighborhood that had transformed into this vibrant melting pot of black and white and every other hue.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 8 ("Robert"). Noah describes Yeoville as a melting pot of immigrants from Nigeria, Ghana and elsewhere, with Rockey Street as its strip of vendors, restaurants and bars. His father lives two blocks over on Yeo Street, beside a park where kids of all races play. Every Sunday his father cooks him the same meal, _Rösti_ with a bottle of Sprite.
- **Why it matters:** after years of secret visits under apartheid, Yeoville is the first place Trevor can openly be with his father, and its mixed street life stands for the new South Africa. His father's later move to Cape Town ends these Sundays and opens the long estrangement.
