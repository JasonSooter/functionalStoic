# Berliner Straße (Köln-Mülheim)

Main road through Mülheim, on the right bank of the Rhine in [[Cologne]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is the street of the "Meinhofburg" squat where Frans finds and buys out his secret daughter Andrea in 1976.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)

Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zn31avxm1ec1cf42ejr5z)

> The taxi took the two former SS sergeants to a dilapidated building in Mülheim, halfway down Berlinerstrasse.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Karl waits in a bar farther up the road while Frans knocks at the "Meinhofburg", a squat named after the Red Army Faction's Ulrike Meinhof. The squatters demand money for Andrea's months of keep, raising their price, and Frans pays. He walks out with Andrea and her daughter Daniela, everything they own in two duffel bags, and puts them up in a guesthouse around the corner from his own.
- **Why it matters:** The scene is one of the book's sharpest ironies: a former SS sergeant buying his daughter out of a commune of West German radicals, and the moment his "guardian angel" role becomes real.
