# Spilve Airport

Riga's interwar airport on the Daugava's left bank, north of the city centre. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where an overheard conversation made the Münninghoff fortune.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85b007ssvw6dnb5w2y21k2w)

> One day while he was waiting for his flight to Berlin to board in the first-class lounge at Spilve Airport in Riga, two men sat down at the table next to him.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Waiting for a Berlin flight, the author's grandfather hears two men discuss, in Latvian, Ulmanis's campaign debts and their plan to expose them before the election; they take him for a German, not knowing he speaks Latvian. He lets the plane leave without him, finds Ulmanis in Riga and offers to repay the debts as an interest-free loan, on condition that he is never named as the backer.
- **Why it matters:** The scene explains how a Dutch outsider became one of the most powerful men in Latvia, and it introduces the double-dealing the author sees running through his grandfather's life.
