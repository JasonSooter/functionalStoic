# Sparta

City-state of ancient Greece in the Eurotas valley of Laconia, in the southern Peloponnese. It is the home of the warriors whose training and departure fill the first part of _Gates of Fire_.

## Gates of Fire — Steven Pressfield (1998)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/133742/gates-of-fire-by-steven-pressfield/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m896j3ysddfcy9hg0s2r6r47)

> I stood in the crowd before the Hellenion with Alexandros and the other boys of the training platoons, the women and children, the whole city drawn up beneath the acacias and cypresses, singing the hymn to Castor

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The text calls the city Lakedaemon. Dienekes teaches the boy Alexandros about fear on the slope below the statue of Phobos, beneath the temple of Athena. The two of them sit in the myrtle grove called the Twins, which Alexandros later returns to when he defies his mother and follows the army. Alexandros runs the Big Ring past Amyklai and across the slopes of Taygetos to beat his asthma. The whole city sees Leonidas's regiments march out along the Going-Away Street to Antirhion. Field training takes place at "the Oaks" in the Otona valley, which the text does not place more exactly.
- **Why it matters:** The novel is Xeones's portrait of Spartan training and character, seen by an outsider, and this is where it is formed. The march-out shows the city sending its men to war.
