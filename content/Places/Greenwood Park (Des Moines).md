# Greenwood Park (Des Moines)

Park on the west side of Des Moines with the Ashworth Pool. In _The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid_ it is the summer world of swimming and Reed's ice cream.

## The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid — Bill Bryson (2006)
Source: [[The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4620956W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5khtkba1dm564dh3q7cek38)

> Driving home from Ashworth Pool through Greenwood Park, under a flying canopy of green leaves, nicely basted in chlorine and knowing that you would shortly be plunging your face into three gooey scoops of Reed’s ice cream

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Bryson recalls driving home from Ashworth Swimming Pool through Greenwood Park under the trees, smelling of chlorine and looking forward to three scoops at Reed's ice-cream parlour nearby.
- **Why it matters:** A nostalgic aside that captures the book's idea of 1950s pleasure.
