# Eagle Pass–Piedras Negras International Bridge

Bridge over the Rio Grande between Eagle Pass, Texas, and Piedras Negras, Coahuila. In Like Water for Chocolate Chencha paces it rehearsing a lie for Mama Elena.

## Like Water for Chocolate — Laura Esquivel (1989)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL953162W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8z2jpcdc421bccgwfszkr)

> Chencha crossed the bridge between Eagle Pass and Piedras Negras for the hundredth time, without even realizing it, as she tried to think of the best way to break the news to Mama Elena.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After Tita refuses to return to the ranch, Chencha walks back and forth over the international bridge between Eagle Pass and Piedras Negras, rehearsing what to tell Mama Elena; the guards of both countries let her pass because they have known her since childhood. Tita has been staying across the river in Eagle Pass, in Dr. John Brown's house and laboratory.
- **Why it matters:** The only explicit real geography in the range: it fixes the ranch at the Texas-Coahuila border and shows the two-country world the family lives in.
