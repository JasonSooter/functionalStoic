## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)

Source: [[Neither Here Nor There]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74125W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce7z48f1rfw11c72b4025a)

> It was awful, but then near the Hofburg Palace I passed into the charmed circle of the Ringstrasse, and it was like the sun breaking out from behind clouds. Everything was lovely and golden.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Walking in from the station along an ugly stretch of Mariahilferstrasse, Bryson doubts Vienna's reputation; then near the Hofburg he passes into the "charmed circle" of the Ringstrasse and everything turns golden.
- **Why it matters:** It is the hinge of the Vienna chapter.
