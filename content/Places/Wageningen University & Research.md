## This Tiny Country Feeds the World — Magazine (2017)
Source: [Original](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/magazine/2017/09/holland-agriculture-sustainable-farming/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7g0qagfkc9qtxygyb2dcp)

> Widely regarded as the world’s top agricultural research institution, WUR is the nodal point of Food Valley, an expansive cluster of agricultural technology start-ups and experimental farms.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The brain trust behind Dutch agriculture, compared to Silicon Valley. The piece covers its plant sciences director, its international student body (Uganda, Nepal, Indonesia), the World Soil Museum and its projects in more than 140 countries.
- **Why it matters:** Wageningen is presented as the engine of Dutch agricultural efficiency.
