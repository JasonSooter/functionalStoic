## It's All About the Bike — Robert Penn (2010)
Source: [[It's All About the Bike - In Pursuit of Happiness on Two Wheels]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21030895W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce5md2rkvtmafary1rjmx9)

> I had a set of notes I’d written two years earlier, in Penang, Malaysia.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Two years before Aosta, Penn's rear wheel failed in Penang, and Abar, an aged Cantonese-Malayan mechanic, taught him wheel-building through pidgin, signs and drawings. They ate satay and mangosteens under the stucco arch of the shop; the notes Penn wrote there later save him in Aosta.
- **Why it matters:** The source of the knowledge the Aosta scene puts to work.
