> Mackinac Island is particularly special because there are no cars allowed on the island! Everyone gets around by bikes or horse draw carriages.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The family spends a day on the island by bike, rides the loop road, eats downtown and ends at the library. The writer says it is a peaceful retreat despite the fudge shops and tourist spending.
- **Why it matters:** The whole post is about the island; its car-free character shapes the day.
