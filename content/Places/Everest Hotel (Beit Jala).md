## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7w6brx15sjbm4nct2smw5)

> The original meetings of Combatants for Peace took place amid the pine trees of the Everest Hotel in Beit Jala, in Area B, just across the hill from the bird-ringing station.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Eleven Israelis and Palestinians meet in its hilltop restaurant, first wary with phone batteries removed, then dining and finding common ground. Bassam insists on paying, saying this is his home, and they settle on the name Combatants for Peace.
- **Why it matters:** The founding of Combatants for Peace happens here, and Bassam and Elik meet.
