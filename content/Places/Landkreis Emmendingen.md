# Landkreis Emmendingen

Rural district just north of Freiburg in [[Baden-Württemberg]], Germany. The state's 2024 public-transport report singles it out for the biggest improvement in night-time service.

## ÖPNV-Report 2024 — Ministerium für Verkehr Baden-Württemberg (2025)
Source: [Original](https://vm.baden-wuerttemberg.de/fileadmin/redaktion/m-mvi/intern/Dateien/PDF/250527_OEPNV-Report_2024_barrierefrei.pdf) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7h2c3k6vphb456tzmkfv2bh)

> Besonders hervorzuheben ist der Landkreis Emmendingen. Hier ging die Angebotsreduktion von 82 auf 69 Prozent zurück.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The report measures how much bus and rail service falls away at night compared with the day. Emmendingen gets its own example section with a chart comparing its 2020 and 2024 daily service profiles; flexible on-demand services account for the extra night departures. It also appears as the dense-rural district whose night service is above the state average.
- **Why it matters:** It is the report's worked example of what on-demand services can do for rural night-time transport.
