# Dayton

City in south-western [[Ohio]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ Thomas Midgley discovers there, at General Motors Research, that tetraethyl lead stops engine knock.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m80txtensfsmqj0kmx8d84sj)

> In 1921, while working for the General Motors Research Corporation in Dayton, Ohio, he investigated a compound called tetraethyl lead (also known, confusingly, as lead tetraethyl), and discovered that it significantly reduced the juddering condition known as engine knock.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 1921, working for the General Motors Research Corporation in Dayton, Thomas Midgley investigates tetraethyl lead and finds it significantly reduces engine knock. In 1923 General Motors, Du Pont and Standard Oil of New Jersey form the Ethyl Gasoline Corporation to make as much of it as the world will buy.
- **Why it matters:** Leaded gasoline, the contamination that later complicates Clair Patterson's dating of the Earth, starts here. The book names the city and company, not the building.
