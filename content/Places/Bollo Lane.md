# Bollo Lane

Street in Chiswick, west London. Brompton moved its factory here in 1993, once the bike had started winning awards and the company had paid off its overdraft.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8c38vpmtnhpjhj1ww9jcb7x)

> The bike started winning design awards and getting recognition in the press, and had to move to bigger premises in Chiswick, still in west London.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** By 1993, five years after Julian Vereker's investment, Brompton had £100,000 in cash and had repaid its overdraft. The book's note on the factories places the next step at "a small factory at Bollo Lane, Chiswick", and a caption shows the team soon after the move. Andrew and his brazers worked here until the 1998 move to Kew Bridge in [[Brentford]].
- **Why it matters:** A short chapter in the company's history, it marks the moment the bike became a business that could pay its own way. Little of the book's action happens here.
