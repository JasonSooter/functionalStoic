# Cobh

Harbour town in County Cork, Ireland, called Queenstown in the 19th century. In _Around the World in Eighty Days_ Fogg lands there to catch the mail route to Liverpool.

## Around the World in Eighty Days — Jules Verne (1872)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1100007W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne42nkzns5dwhptm9xfkvn)

> Queenstown is the Irish port at which the transatlantic steamers stop to put off the mails. These mails are carried to Dublin by express trains always held in readiness to start; from Dublin they are sent on to Liverpool by the most rapid boats, and thus gain twelve hours on the Atlantic steamers.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On 20 December the burnt-down Henrietta sights the Irish coast and Fastnet Light, and Fogg decides to land at Queenstown to catch the mail route (train to Dublin, then a fast steamer to Liverpool). The Henrietta enters Queenstown harbour at one in the morning at high tide, and Fogg leaves Captain Speedy on the hulk.
- **Why it matters:** Gives Fogg the twelve hours he needed after the Atlantic crossing nearly failed.
