# Challenger Deep

Challenger Deep is the deepest point of the Mariana Trench in the western Pacific. It turns up in What if you drained the oceans? (xkcd's What If?): It is the fixed location of the thought experiment's drain, chosen as the deepest spot; the rest of the piece is global.

## What if you drained the oceans? — xkcd's What If? (2024)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jpy55EgMQgY) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbjbj4t7rrwkq2yxa5hsq3)

> appeared at the bottom of Challenger Deep, the deepest spot in the ocean?

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The listener's question puts a 20-metre circular portal at the bottom of Challenger Deep and asks how fast the oceans would drain; the answer is hundreds of thousands of years, with a water drop of much less than a centimetre per day.
- **Why it matters:** It is the fixed location of the thought experiment's drain, chosen as the deepest spot; the rest of the piece is global.
