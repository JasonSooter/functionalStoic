## Rent vs. Buy: If You Have to Ask, You Should Probably Rent — Mr. Money Mustache (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.mrmoneymustache.com/2015/07/27/rent-vs-buy/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ganqmvnkqzgj5kbxzqc0cy)

> Brand new 900 square foot luxury apartment with 9 foot ceilings and two walls of glass overlooking the city’s hottest “Byward Market” district

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** In Case Study Two the author finds a new 900-square-foot apartment with walls of glass overlooking the ByWard Market for \$1,950 a month, and praises Ottawa's riverfront, forests and parks.
- **Why it matters:** It is one of the two worked examples behind the post's argument for renting.
