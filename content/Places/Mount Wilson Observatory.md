# Mount Wilson Observatory

Observatory on Mount Wilson in the San Gabriel Mountains above [[Los Angeles]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ it is where Hubble shows the universe is expanding.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W)

> In 1919, now aged thirty, he moved to California and took up a position at the Mount Wilson Observatory near Los Angeles.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Hubble takes a post here in 1919, aged thirty, and swiftly becomes, in the book's words, the most outstanding astronomer of the twentieth century. Using Mount Wilson's new hundred-inch Hooker telescope he takes up the spectra work Slipher began in Arizona and works out that all galaxies outside our local cluster are moving away from us.
- **Why it matters:** The expanding universe, and so the Big Bang story the chapter tells, comes out of this telescope.
