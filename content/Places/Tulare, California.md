## ‘Poverty is often looked at in isolation, but it is an American problem’ — the Guardian (2016)

> USA. Tulare, California. 2014. Birds. Tulare has a population of 59,278 and 21.4% live below the poverty level.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Tulare is the site of Black's image of birds on telegraph wires, which the author compares to postwar Japanese photographers. Poverty statistics for the town are given in the caption.
- **Why it matters:** A second concrete example of Black's California starting point; fairly minor beyond the image.
