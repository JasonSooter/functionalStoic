# Joubert Park

Park and surrounding neighbourhood in central Johannesburg. In Born a Crime it is where the mixed-race toddler Trevor Noah cannot be seen walking with either parent.

## At a glance

| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6yq41mcgkbrw58xnwhhakca)

> My mom and I used to go to Joubert Park all the time. It’s the Central Park of Johannesburg—beautiful gardens, a zoo, a giant chessboard with human-sized pieces that people would play.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 2. After Trevor's birth his mother lives in a flat in Joubert Park, and the park is where they go for outings. When his father once comes along, walking at a distance, the toddler runs after him shouting "Daddy! Daddy! Daddy!", and Robert panics and runs away. Because a light-skinned child could not be seen with a black mother either, she brings a coloured neighbour, Queen, who walks beside Trevor as if she were his mother while his mother follows like a maid.
- **Why it matters:** the park is where the book stages apartheid's absurdity in public. A family could not walk together, and the child read it all as a game.
