# Von Lomani

A small estate in Ilguciems, across the Daugava from central [[Riga]], pinned at the neighbourhood. It was the Münninghoff family seat and the hub of Riga's 1930s high society in Alexander Münninghoff's _The Son and Heir_.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85azyensvdd99y82cez5kbx)

> Von Lomani was the center of Riga’s high society in the 1930s.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** By the 1930s the author's grandfather, the Old Boss, has traded his house on Reimerstrasse in central Riga for Von Lomani, a small estate in Ilguciems within walking distance of most of his factories. He builds a massive villa with a swimming pool, a tennis court and stables for his three horses; the dining room seats twenty-four. The estate hosts legendary parties for the teenage jeunesse dorée of Baltic German families, Frans and his sister among them. When war comes, the residents of Von Lomani have already fled.
- **Why it matters:** Von Lomani is the family's lost world: the wealth and Baltic German glamour that shaped Frans's sense of who he was, and that the Dutch boarding schools and the flight from Latvia took from him.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real estate as the book tells it; ⚠️ its exact site in Ilguciems could not be verified, so the pin is the neighbourhood.
