## How I Learned to Cycle Like a Dutchman – The New Yorker — Dan Kois (2019)
Source: [Original](https://www.newyorker.com/culture/personal-history/how-i-learned-to-cycle-like-a-dutchman) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7cn89dp3cs22k5stcq77g)

> But in the old city center, with its narrow brick streets, steep canal bridges, and crowds of pedestrians, we kept causing accidents.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The old center's narrow brick streets and bridges defeat the family's tentative riding: the girls send a student into an alley and Lyra collides with an elderly Dutchman outside their apartment. The piece also explains that planned car roads through the center were abandoned, so crossing Delft by car is effectively impossible.
- **Why it matters:** Delft's old centre is the testing ground for the family's year of learning to ride like the Dutch.
