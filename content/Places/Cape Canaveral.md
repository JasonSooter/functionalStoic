# Cape Canaveral

Cape on Florida's Atlantic coast, home of the US launch sites. In _The Martian_ the Iris supply probe fails here on launch.

## The Martian — Andy Weir (2011)
Source: [[The Martian]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17091839W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7v9870qb0emrvxrxpy2ww)

> Thousands had gathered at Cape Canaveral, the largest crowd ever to watch an unmanned launch. They listened to the timer controller’s voice as it echoed across the grandstands.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Iris lifts off from the Cape with thousands watching from grandstands, with launch control in Florida talking to Houston. A shimmy from loosened cargo wrecks the vehicle and it is lost over the Atlantic, with debris reported by a US destroyer.
- **Why it matters:** Real, famous launch site where the first rescue attempt plays out and fails publicly.
