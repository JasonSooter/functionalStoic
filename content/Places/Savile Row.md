# Savile Row

Street in Mayfair, London. Phileas Fogg's house at No. 7 Saville Row is where _Around the World in Eighty Days_ starts and where the missing day is discovered.

## Around the World in Eighty Days — Jules Verne (1872)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1100007W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne38f86h0et2grxkn4c6a1)

> Mr. Phileas Fogg lived, in 1872, at No. 7, Saville Row, Burlington Gardens, the house in which Sheridan died in 1814.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 1872 Phileas Fogg lives at No. 7 Saville Row, keeping a clockwork household; in Chapters I and II he dismisses his servant over shaving water two degrees too cool and hires Passepartout, and after the wager he returns to order the bags packed for Dover. On 22 December he comes home believing himself ruined, Aouda proposes, and Passepartout's errand to the clergyman reveals the day they gained.
- **Why it matters:** The house is the fixed point of order the journey leaves and returns to, and the setting of the book's emotional resolution.
