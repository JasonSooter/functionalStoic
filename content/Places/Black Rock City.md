# Black Rock City

Temporary city of the Burning Man festival in Nevada's Black Rock Desert ([[Nevada]]). A TechCrunch piece argues it is a microcosm of Silicon Valley.

## At a glance

| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elon Musk Is Right, Burning Man Is Silicon Valley | Sarah Buhr | setting | First-person tour of the playa city, from the lavish camps on the edge of town to the Temple. |
| Burning Man Survival Guide 2022 | Burning Man Project | subject | The 2022 guide describes the temporary city and its decommodified rules. |

## Elon Musk Is Right, Burning Man Is Silicon Valley — Sarah Buhr (2014)
Source: [Original](http://techcrunch.com/2014/09/04/elon-musk-is-right-burning-man-is-silicon-valley/?ncid=rss&cps=gravity) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gby5gdm1kkect84y9tv792)

> It’s nighttime when my driving buddy Ben Parr and I arrive in Black Rock City.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author arrives at night and tours the city's lettered streets and clock-face avenues, visits a lavish camp of walled glamping tents on the edge of town, and reads mourners' messages at the Temple beyond the Man.
- **Why it matters:** The city is the whole subject of the argument that Burning Man mirrors Silicon Valley.

## Burning Man Survival Guide 2022 — Burning Man Project (2022)
Source: [Original](https://survival.burningman.org/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy73rkjgz7jr847ng4cz81)

> Black Rock City is not the place to increase your online following or generate media attention for your ideas, your business, or yourself.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The guide describes Black Rock City as a temporary city and a decommodified community, returning in 2022 after a two-year hiatus. It lists changes: a solar-powered Man, no sales at Center Camp Cafe, no delivered housing.
- **Why it matters:** The whole guide is about this temporary city. The text never names Nevada; location is from outside knowledge.
