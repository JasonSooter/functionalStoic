# Empire State Building

Skyscraper in Midtown [[Manhattan]], New York City, opened in 1931. In _The Boys in the Boat_ Joe Rantz has an epiphany on its observation deck before sailing for Berlin.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m878ttttck9yvpwgbr80wj3s)

> For Joe, the moment of epiphany came on the eighty-sixth floor of the new Empire State Building.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Touring New York in July 1936, Joe, who has never been higher than a tree, looks out from the eighty-sixth floor over the murmuring city toward the Brooklyn Bridge and the Statue of Liberty. He realises that the crew now represents not just the University of Washington but the country and its ideas of liberty, trust and fair play.
- **Why it matters:** It marks the crew's shift from a college boat to Team USA on the eve of the voyage to Hitler's Germany.
