# Lynford Hall

Victorian country house near Mundford in [[Norfolk]], England. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where young Frans Münninghoff spends Easter 1939 with the Kennedys.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85b0bgd45tf62vtvfxa0mcp)

> Sir James would take his guest for a week-long holiday at his enormous country house Lynford Hall in Norfolk.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The Old Boss's friend Sir James Calder hosts Frans for a week over Easter 1939, with Joseph Kennedy, the US ambassador, and some of his children. Frans is too shy to speak to Kathleen, and when John explains everything that is wrong with Hitler and calls the occupation of Czechoslovakia an injustice, Frans, unable to argue in English, stays silent but boils inside. He leaves knowing he will never belong to that upper class.
- **Why it matters:** A brush with the Anglo-American establishment on the eve of war shows how firmly Frans has already chosen Germany.
