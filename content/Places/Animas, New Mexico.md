## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs2ymac6dphq0eavjjg3k8)

> For its part of the reclamation, Animas offered a general store, a gas station and a few homes.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After waking to find the desert flooded around his camp, Eric wades toward Animas. In its turn-of-the-century general store three old men by the stove are wary until he says he has walked from Canada; they dry his boots and he tells them the whole journey.
- **Why it matters:** The last human stop of the trek, where the journey is told back to strangers.
