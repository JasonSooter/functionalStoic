# Theresienstadt

Theresienstadt is a former garrison town in Bohemia that the Nazis turned into a ghetto and concentration camp. It turns up in Four Thousand Weeks (Oliver Burkeman): It is the grim consequence that explains the family's anxiety about timing and departure.

## Four Thousand Weeks — Oliver Burkeman (2021)
Source: [[Four Thousand Weeks]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL22355777W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fcqehapx5rbgnrw1j6k3vy)

> never made it out, and later died in the concentration camp at Theresienstadt.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Burkeman's great-great-grandmother failed to leave Germany and later died in the concentration camp at Theresienstadt. This is part of the family history behind his relatives' obsessive planning.
- **Why it matters:** It is the grim consequence that explains the family's anxiety about timing and departure. It is mentioned briefly but carries real weight.
