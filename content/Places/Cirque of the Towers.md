# Cirque of the Towers

Ring of granite peaks around Lonesome Lake in the southern Wind River Range, Wyoming. The brothers' last camp among the big peaks before the desert.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs2529vx05cdry4b3ywyaa)

> We spent a dry night within a magnificent ring of 11,000-foot pinnacles called Cirque of the Towers.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Eric slows his pace after Tim falls on the descent. They camp at Lonesome Lake in a boulder alcove inside the ring of pinnacles, and next morning cross Jackass Pass, the last big pass before the Great Divide Basin.
- **Why it matters:** It closes the Wind River section and marks Eric's first real concession to his brother's pace.
