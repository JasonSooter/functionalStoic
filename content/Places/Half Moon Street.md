# Half Moon Street

Half Moon Street is a Mayfair street running north from Piccadilly in the West End of London. In _The Importance of Being Earnest_ Algernon's flat there is the setting of the whole first act.

## The Importance of Being Earnest — Oscar Wilde (1895)
Source: [[The Importance of Being Earnest]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL27776452W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m768vtrz856x5jg5sfrj0e)

> Morning-room in Algernon’s flat in Half-Moon Street. The room is luxuriously and artistically furnished.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Act One takes place in the morning-room of Algernon's flat. Algernon uses the inscribed cigarette case to force Jack to admit he is Ernest in town and Jack in the country, and explains his own invalid friend Bunbury. Lady Bracknell and Gwendolen call; Jack proposes, Lady Bracknell questions him from her notebook and refuses him over the hand-bag, and Algernon secretly writes Jack's country address on his shirt-cuff.
- **Why it matters:** The London half of the double life, where both deceptions are set up. A fashionable Mayfair bachelor's address fits Algernon exactly.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Algernon's flat is fictional; Half Moon Street is a real Mayfair street.
