# Maputo

Capital of [[Mozambique]]. In Hans Rosling's memoir it is where he and his wife arrive in 1979 to be assigned a post, and where they return in 2011 to take tea with Janet Mondlane.

## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling (2019)
Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20831115W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqzdf688ft3pts3cagv8h8)

> It was afternoon when our plane landed in Maputo’s modest airport.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** They arrived 23 October 1979, ate ice cream with coordinator Ninni Uhrus, and were interviewed at the Ministry of Health, which assigned them to Nacala rather than Beira.
- **Why it matters:** Arrival point fulfilling the promise to Mondlane.

## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling
Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20831115W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e32k42nnxa24tq9aem1fvp)

> Her home was in a beautiful spot on the top of a hill in the center of the city. You could see the harbor entrance from her windows.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On their last day in 2011, Rosling and Agneta take tea with Janet Mondlane, widow of FRELIMO's first leader, and recall a 1968 supper in Sweden. She asks about changes since they worked there and discusses the difficulty of being head of state in a poor country.
- **Why it matters:** Closes the return visit with a reflective conversation on development and corruption.
