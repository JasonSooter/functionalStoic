# Kolding

City in southern Jutland, Denmark. In The Year of Living Danishly, Russell visits its language centre to ask why Danish is hard.

> I seek out Søs Nissen, deputy head of Jutland’s Kolding language centre.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Russell seeks out Søs Nissen, deputy head of Kolding's language centre, to ask why Danish is so difficult and why Danes love learning other languages.
- **Why it matters:** It is the setting for the book's language chapter. Minor as a place.
