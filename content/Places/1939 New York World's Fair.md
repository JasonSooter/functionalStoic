## Uncommon Type — Tom Hanks (2017)
Source: [[Uncommon Type]] · [Original](https://penguin.co.uk/books/1114786/uncommon-type/9781786091338.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5w40k7cn8ape055p6mkc7)

> The IRT discharged fairgoers at the Bowling Green Gate, where Bert paid seventy-five cents for admission and bought a guidebook for a dime.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 'The Past Is Important to Us' Bert takes the IRT out to the Fair, tours the Trylon and Perisphere and the Futurama, eats pie at Borden's with Carmen and Virginia, and overstays his deadline on a bench by the Lagoon of Nations as the fountains light up.
- **Why it matters:** The Fair is the story's main stage, the place where Bert repeatedly relives a single day and finds Carmen.
