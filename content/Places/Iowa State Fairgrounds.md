# Iowa State Fairgrounds

Fairgrounds on the east side of Des Moines that host the Iowa State Fair each August. In _The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid_ the fair is the year's great event, and the strippers' tent its forbidden prize.

## The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid — Bill Bryson (2006)

Source: [[The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4620956W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5khtt4vys78d233m01shxhh)

> THE ONE AMUSEMENT OF THE YEAR that everyone did get genuinely excited about was the Iowa State Fair, which was held at enormous fairgrounds way out on the eastern edge of town late every August.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bryson describes the exhibition halls, the Butter Cow, the Varied Industries Building, the hog pavilions and the midway. Over several years he and his friends try and fail to get into the strippers' tent, until Jed Mattes bribes the ticket seller.
- **Why it matters:** The site of a running adolescent obsession and its comic payoff, and a point of civic pride.
