> Recognizing this problem, the city government commissioned the architect Christian Sottile to study what happened to just one thoroughfare, East Broad Street, when it became a speedway.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** After a 1969 one-way conversion, East Broad Street lost almost two-thirds of its active addresses per Christian Sottile's study. When the city returned it to two-way, active addresses rose 50 percent.
- **Why it matters:** Quantified before-and-after evidence of the effects of one-way streets, within an otherwise walkable Oglethorpe grid.
