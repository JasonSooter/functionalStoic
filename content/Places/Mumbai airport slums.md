## What Seeing Real Poverty Showed Me About Pretending to Be Poor — Pretend to Be Poor (2015)

Source: [Original](http://www.pretendtobepoor.com/india3/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hj1f69hbs8264bhej7fje7)

> I didn’t visit the streets of Calcutta, but I saw one of the world’s largest slums, the massive encampment spanning the outskirts of the Mumbai airport grounds

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** Arriving in India, the author sees from the plane window the huge encampment around the Mumbai airport, a sea of blue tarps she says houses about a million people. The sight stays with her through the trip.
- **Why it matters:** It is the image that anchors her reflection: real poverty makes her own chosen frugality look like play, and pushes her to give more.
