# Battle of the Wabash site (Fort Recovery)

Site of St. Clair's defeat in 1791, now Fort Recovery, Ohio. Phil Klay cites the disaster as the moment Congress accepted a standing army.

## The Citizen-Soldier Moral Risk and the Modern Military — Phil Klay (2016)

Source: [Original](https://www.brookings.edu/research/essays/2016/the-citizen-soldier/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvq15znp6y8nygec2wftns)

> His poorly trained, undisciplined men engaged an equal-sized force at the Battle of the Wabash in November 1791, also known by the considerably more evocative title, the Battle of a Thousand Slain.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** St. Clair's militia-heavy force is destroyed by the Western Confederacy, the worst US military disaster; Congress then concedes a standing army.
- **Why it matters:** Second historical example in the argument about citizen-soldiers versus professionals; place is named but little described.
