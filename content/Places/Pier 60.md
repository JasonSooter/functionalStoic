# Pier 60

Hudson River pier in Chelsea, [[Manhattan]], now part of Chelsea Piers. In _The Boys in the Boat_ the U.S. Olympic team sails from here for Germany on the SS _Manhattan_ in July 1936.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m878twknz02mv10qzd2vah4d)

> drove it through the heart of New York City to Pier 60 on the Hudson River

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On July 13 the shell is trucked through the city under police escort to the pier, where the boys struggle for an hour before getting it up a baggage chute onto the boat deck. On sailing day more than ten thousand people crowd the pier as the _Manhattan_ backs into the Hudson and steams past the Battery, Ellis Island and the Statue of Liberty. The crossing that follows, tourist class against first class, ends with Eleanor Holm thrown off the team for drinking.
- **Why it matters:** It is the crew's departure for Berlin and the start of their first look at the world beyond America.
