## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling (2019)
Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20831115W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebtfs55d9dqa6ab5jf390b)

> Already by the beginning of September, people were dying in the streets of Monrovia, Liberia’s capital city, something that had not happened in modern times except during wartime or because of a natural disaster.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Monrovia is the feared epicentre of exponential ebola growth, with full treatment facilities and slums; Rosling later works there and maps cases and contacts across the city. By December new cases fall below ten per day and 600 beds sit empty.
- **Why it matters:** The whole chapter turns on a capital with large slums and an international airport, which Rosling says made ebola a global threat.

## Impulsive Rich Kid, Impulsive Poor Kid — Priceonomics (2015)
Source: [Original](http://priceonomics.com/impulsive-rich-kid-impulsive-poor-kid/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g8vkgdntj00wexnwnaadah)

> Subjects, recruited from the slums of Monrovia.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Chris Blattman's study recruited young men from Monrovia's slums for an eight-week therapy programme, some also given $200. Crime fell 20–50% soon after, and the therapy-plus-cash group kept the gains a year later.
- **Why it matters:** A second, independent site that shows the effect holds outside Chicago.
