## 2 Weeks in Costa Rica — retirebyforty (2015)
Source: [Original](https://retireby40.org/2-weeks-in-costa-rica/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gm08sw9bmyk09w575qy1dv)

> Arenal Volcano National Park– Volcano, hot springs, hiking, and wildlife.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** The pre-trip post lists Arenal as the first of three stops: four nights at the Casa Luna Hotel at a low-season rate, a $110 shared shuttle from the airport hotel, and plans for hot springs, a spa visit and hikes.
- **Why it matters:** It anchors the first leg of the itinerary and its budget; the post is written before departure, so this is only the plan.

## Our 2 Week Costa Rica Vacation — retirebyforty (2015)
Source: [Original](https://retireby40.org/our-2-week-costa-rica-vacation/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gm1efcp3gt9w7c3mjq04m8)

> We got off at the Observatory Lodge, enjoyed the great view, and hike around the park for a few hours.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** Rather than walk the long way from the bus stop, the family takes a $35 guided tour, gets off at the Observatory Lodge for the view and hikes for a few hours, seeing a toucan, a coati and a margay, crossing two suspension bridges and ending at a free hot spring with a mud facial.
- **Why it matters:** The main outing of the La Fortuna leg; the author notes the lava stopped flowing in 2010, so the volcano is less dramatic than its fame.
