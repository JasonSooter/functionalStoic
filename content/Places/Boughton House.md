# Boughton House

Stately home near Kettering in [[Northamptonshire]], England, and for twelve years the site of the Greenbelt festival. It is the subject of a newsletter's festival diary and farewell to the site.

## #092 Colanders are back — Diagram Club (2026)

Source: [Original](https://diagramclub.substack.com/p/092-colanders-are-back) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5b456a9fn368q31npzv537f)

> Greenbelt has, for 12 years, happened in the beautiful grounds of Boughton House, a stately home in Northamptonshire. This is the last full festival there, which is sad.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Dave Walker's newsletter gives a ten-point diary of the August bank-holiday festival in the grounds: bands (including the Proclaimers), talks, poetry, rain and mud, food stalls, and a golf-cart ride.
- **Why it matters:** The grounds are the issue's main subject, and the farewell to the site gives it its wistful note.

## #006 Hopeful news about cycling — Dave Walker (2024)

Source: [Original](https://diagramclub.substack.com/p/006-hopeful-news-about-cycling) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f1x4rn9g9r59z99w9zv0s7)

> This festival has been, for as long as I can remember, a highlight of my year, boosting morale, inspiring my work, and it’s the place I see lots of friends and people from my past who I don’t see for the other 361 days of the year.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** Walker is heading to Greenbelt that day to speak on a panel and recalls 24 years of attending, with music, talks, a beer tent and one muddy year.
- **Why it matters:** It shows what the festival means to him.
