## 30 Days, 9 Cities, 1 Question: Where Did American Prosperity Go? — Kyla Scanlon (2025)

Source: [[30 Days, 9 Cities, 1 Question - Where Did American Prosperity Go]] · [Original](https://kyla.substack.com/p/30-days-9-cities-1-question-where) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce5cnsx3fg5xwpc3q7kjda)

> Baltimore was the next stop, three days of conversations about whether the United States has the collective will to survive the AI era.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** A gloomier stop where people keep saying "bubble". She interviews a New York Times labor reporter about college-educated workers turning to unions.
- **Why it matters:** One stop in the essay's survey.

## America's Peculiar Bail System — Priceonomics (2015)

Source: [Original](http://priceonomics.com/americas-peculiar-bail-system/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g0ejxnqdmbkatdq14r9qy7)

> At the apex of Baltimore’s Freddie Gray protests, 18-year-old Allen Bullock grabbed an orange street cone, leapt atop an unmarked police car, and smashed out its windows.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** During the 2015 protests after Freddie Gray's death, Allen Bullock smashes a police car's windows, turns himself in and has bail set at \$500,000; his case opens the article.
- **Why it matters:** The Baltimore case gives the article's argument about bail a human face.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m764175hmhv804vzqvzgh4zc)

> I am on a train passing through Baltimore, where I grew up. I can see vacant lots, charred remains of burned buildings surrounded by rubbish, billboards advertising churches, and other billboards for DNA testing of children’s paternity. Johns Hopkins Hospital looms out of the squalor.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Looking for America's "ruins in progress", Byrne finds them in his own hometown: from the train he sees Johns Hopkins Hospital as an isolated island cut off from downtown. Earlier in the chapter he remembers taking the bus downtown as a teenager to ride the escalators at Hutzler's and Hecht's, before malls and white flight emptied it.
- **Why it matters:** Baltimore makes the book's argument about hollowed-out American cities personal: it is the decline he grew up inside.
