# Inland Empire

Inland region east of Los Angeles around Riverside and San Bernardino, California. A Wired piece traces its change from the 'Orange Empire' of citrus groves to a hub of warehouses.

## How the 'Orange Empire' Became the Land of MegaWarehouses — Emma Foehringer Merchant (2017)
Source: [Original](https://www.wired.com/2017/05/orange-empire-became-land-megawarehouses/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7bhmw62npxs05hm86ywc8)

> Just a few decades ago, California’s Inland Empire billed itself as “the Orange Empire” for the citrus orchards that fueled its primary industry.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** The region has shifted from citrus groves to a billion square feet of warehouses serving the LA/Long Beach ports. Its basin geography traps pollution from LA and Orange County, making it one of the unhealthiest places in the US.
- **Why it matters:** The region's shift from citrus to logistics is the backdrop that makes the warehouse fight matter.
