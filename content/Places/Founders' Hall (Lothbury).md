# Founders' Hall

The Founders' Company hall on Lothbury in the City of London, where in 1599 merchants voted to found what became the East India Company, as told in _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m82efzv0z66fzn1rn9pa8pj1)

> a loud cheer was heard coming from the half-timbered Founders Hall in London’s Lothbury Street.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On 24 September 1599 about eighty merchants and sea captains meet in the half-timbered hall, vote unanimously to ask Queen Elizabeth I to approve a voyage to the East Indies and appoint directors; the Privy Council then holds them up, but they press on.
- **Why it matters:** It is the founding moment of the East India Company in Milton's account.
