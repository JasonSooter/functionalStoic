# City Hall (The Caves of Steel)

The towering City Hall of a future, enclosed [[New York City]] in Isaac Asimov's The Caves of Steel, home of the Police Department where Baley works. Pinned at today's New York City Hall.

## The Caves of Steel — Isaac Asimov (1954)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL46401W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hj3ektck037fpycg04yt2t)

> The Police Department was in the upper levels of City Hall, and City Hall reached high. From the Commissioner’s window, the neighboring towers fell short and the tops were visible.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Commissioner Enderby briefs Baley on the Spacer murder from his office high in City Hall, looking out west toward Spacetown. Later R. Sammy's body is found at headquarters, and in the end Baley accuses Enderby there of the murder.
- **Why it matters:** It is the seat of the City's authority and the place where the case starts and ends.
- **Real vs. fictional:** A future City Hall of New York; the text names the building but gives no street.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Clues: the Police Department is 'in the upper levels of City Hall', the City is New York, and Spacetown lies to the west. No reference found places it more exactly; pinned at the present New York City Hall in Lower Manhattan. Alternative: anywhere in a future Manhattan.
