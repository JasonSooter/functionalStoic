# Alliance Française (Paris)

French language and culture institute on Boulevard Raspail in [[Paris]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is where Britain opens secret talks to free Gordievsky's family.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7xy5wdgvyy87wfg8z1r780c)

> A month after Gordievsky’s escape, the scientific counselor at the Soviet embassy in Paris was surprised to be invited to tea at the Alliance Française by a British diplomat he only vaguely knew.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** A month after the escape, the scientific counselor of the Soviet embassy in Paris is invited to tea at the Alliance Française by a British diplomat. He is told calmly that a senior KGB officer is "alive and well and living in the UK under close protection," and "would like his family back." So begins Operation HETMAN, the campaign to reunite the family.
- **Why it matters:** It opens the long effort to get Leila and the girls out, which shapes the book's ending. A neutral cultural venue in a third country keeps the approach deniable.
