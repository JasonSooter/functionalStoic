## ‘Snow Farming’ Is Making Spring Skiing More Sustainable — Jennifer Cole (2025)

Source: [Original](https://reasonstobecheerful.world/snow-farming-ski-areas/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebpb5w8m2nns50fry7a2cv)

> Snow fences go up at Banff Sunshine with the first snowfall in mid-October.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Banff Sunshine spans 3,300 acres across three mountains and has no nearby water source, so its terrain relies solely on farmed snow. Five-foot fences catch snow, then the snow is spread into open terrain. Even at 7,200 feet it is seeing cyclical snowfall.
- **Why it matters:** It shows snow farming as the only option rather than a choice, and it carries the climate-risk point.
