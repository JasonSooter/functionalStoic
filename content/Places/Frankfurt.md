# Frankfurt

## The Girl Who Drew Butterflies — Joyce Sidman (2018)

Source: [[The Girl Who Drew Butterflies]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19731743W)

> Her father, Matthäus Merian the Elder, ran a thriving Frankfurt publishing shop, staffed by Maria’s older half brothers and sisters from an earlier marriage.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Maria Sibylla Merian is born in Frankfurt in 1647 into a family of printers and engravers; in her father's shop she watches maps and pictures of the New World being engraved. After his death her mother marries the flower painter Jacob Marrel, in whose garden and studio Maria learns to grind pigments and paint, and at thirteen she starts raising silkworms to watch them change.
- **Why it matters:** Frankfurt is where both her art and her lifelong study of metamorphosis begin.
