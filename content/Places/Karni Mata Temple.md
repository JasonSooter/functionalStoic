# Karni Mata Temple

Hindu temple at Deshnoke near Bikaner, Rajasthan, famous for its rats; in _Life on Air_ Attenborough films barefoot among the rats.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy93hk4kyqy6b54k3zye3p)

> Not far from the Indian city of Bikaner, in Raj asthan, there is a temple where rats are worshipped.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Attenborough, who dislikes rats, must film to-camera pieces barefoot in a temple where rats swarm the floor and are fed grain. He finds Huw Cordey smearing bananas up his stool's legs to lure the rats.
- **Why it matters:** Vivid comic scene for the opportunists programme; text gives only 'near Bikaner' (the Karni Mata temple is not named).
