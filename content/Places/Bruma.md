# Bruma

Suburb in the east of Johannesburg, built around Bruma Lake. In the tenth-anniversary epilogue of Born a Crime, the Audi dealership there is where Trevor Noah unexpectedly meets his childhood friend Teddy again.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zbjy0yqsx53gqwf1jsz2w6)

> I knew that the only people who could tell me for sure were the mechanics at the Audi dealership, so the next day I drove over to the dealership in Bruma Lake.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** "Epilogue for the 10th Anniversary Edition". As an adult Trevor buys an Audi at auction, and his friend Ryan thinks it has been in a crash. Trevor takes it to the dealership in Bruma Lake, the manager fetches "one of our top technicians", and out walks Teddy, the friend he left to be caught in the shoplifting episode years before. Trevor hugs him and apologises. Teddy says nothing much came of it and his family was moving anyway, and the car turns out to be fine.
- **Why it matters:** it closes the guilt left over from the mall chase at [[Balfour Park Shopping Mall]], one of the book's loose ends. Its weight comes from the coincidence, not from Bruma itself.
