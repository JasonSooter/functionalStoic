# Hadar

Fossil site in the Afar region of [[Ethiopia]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ it is where Lucy, the most famous hominid skeleton, is found in 1974.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W)

> The most famous hominid remains in the world are those of a 3.18-million-year-old australopithecine found at Hadar in Ethiopia in 1974 by a team led by Donald Johanson.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The 3.18-million-year-old australopithecine known as Lucy was found here in 1974 by a team led by Donald Johanson. Bryson dwells on how small and incomplete her skeleton is, and on the disputes over how she walked and whether she is an ancestor of ours at all.
- **Why it matters:** Lucy is his test case for how far popular claims about human ancestry outrun the thin evidence.
