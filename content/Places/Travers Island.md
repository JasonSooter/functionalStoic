# Travers Island

New York Athletic Club's country grounds on Long Island Sound at New Rochelle, New York. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is the Olympic crew's base for the last weeks before sailing to Germany.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m878tsbq5pyrb1yarwzq6y40)

> Travers Island sat on Long Island Sound, just south of New Rochelle.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After the trials the crew lives at the club's thirty manicured acres with its clubhouse, dining room and oyster bar, while Pocock sands and varnishes the _Husky Clipper_. Afternoons are spent on the club's Huckleberry Island a mile offshore, swimming and lying on the granite until Ulbrickson bans it. Under a tree here Moch reads a sealed letter from his father telling him the family is Jewish.
- **Why it matters:** It is the staging ground for Berlin, and the scene of the private revelation that gives Moch a personal stake in going to Nazi Germany.
