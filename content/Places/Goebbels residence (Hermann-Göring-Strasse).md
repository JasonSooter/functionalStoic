# Goebbels residence (Hermann-Göring-Strasse)

Joseph Goebbels's official house on what is now Ebertstraße, a block south of the Brandenburg Gate in [[Berlin]]. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is where Goebbels courts the German film world, including Leni Riefenstahl.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m86m5qpyrfgebjy3sfyhgyfa)

> The previous June, Hitler had awarded Goebbels a sumptuous personal residence on the recently renamed Hermann-Göring-Strasse, just a block south of the Brandenburg Gate.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 1934 Goebbels expands the hundred-year-old former palace of the marshals of the Prussian court, adding a second story, a private cinema, greenhouses and formal gardens, while Magda Goebbels fills it with tapestries and paintings taken from German museums. Actors, producers and directors whose careers now depend on him flock to the house at number 20, and young starlets are of particular interest to him. Among the guests is Leni Riefenstahl.
- **Why it matters:** The house shows the regime's control of German film taking shape, the machinery that will stage the 1936 Olympics as propaganda. It is the German counterpoint to the Depression-era Seattle chapters.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real; the street is today's Ebertstraße and the pin is approximate.
