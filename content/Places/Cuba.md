## Bookish Diversions: Do Audiobooks Count? — Joel J Miller (2024)
Source: [Original](https://www.millersbookreview.com/p/bookish-diversions-do-audiobooks-count) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fcpdd69z51e39r57dqtaj3)

> It’s still a vital part of cigar manufacturing in Cuba.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article traces the practice of el lector, cigar-factory readers paid by Cuban torcedores from the mid-nineteenth century, which spread to other places such as Tampa Bay. Today Lara Reyes still reads aloud to La Corona cigar rollers in Cuba.
- **Why it matters:** Cuba is where the lector tradition is rooted and still lives, tying together the article's themes of listening versus reading; minor in that the article is about audiobooks, not the country.
