## Notes on China — Dwarkesh Patel (2024)

Source: [Original](https://www.dwarkeshpatel.com/p/notes-on-china) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5edfp1xry582yzh45bfsz38)

> Even in Emeishan, a city of just half a million people (considered a quaint countryside town by Chinese standards), we found a Buddhist temple of comical scale

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author visits a vast, nearly empty Buddhist temple in Emeishan where each gate opens onto a larger, more ornate building, five or six times over. A monk asked how it was funded answers 'We've got a lot of supporters, dude' and changes the subject.
- **Why it matters:** It is the author's concrete illustration of China's scale and of opaque funding; the 'Scale' section would lose its most vivid anecdote elsewhere.
