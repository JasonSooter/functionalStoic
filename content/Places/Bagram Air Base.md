## Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World — David Epstein (2019)
Source: [[Range]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550188/range-by-david-epstein/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvttajx790keb7gzbn8nz8)

> As Captain Tony Lesmes described it, his team at Bagram Air Base in northeast Afghanistan only went to work when someone got really unlucky.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Captain Lesmes's pararescue team waited on alert here; in autumn 2009 a 9-line came in for a roadside bomb on a convoy half an hour away by helicopter. Lesmes decided to stay at the operations center to leave room in the helicopters, and all wounded survived.
- **Why it matters:** The base is the setting of the chapter's example of deliberately dropping a sacred tool (togetherness).
