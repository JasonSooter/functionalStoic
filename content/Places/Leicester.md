## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx20wrqvjkvqv3kakmf42b)

> We lived in Leicester where my father was Principal of the University College., a recently founded institution supported by local philanthropists, which under his care and leadership was to grow and become the city's University.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Describing his lifelong animal keeping, Attenborough recalls the family house beside the former municipal Lunatic Asylum that housed the college, with a greenhouse where he kept his captures and tropical fish. He bred guppies and gouramis through his schooldays.
- **Why it matters:** The childhood home where his animal-keeping began, a brief flashback rather than a dwelt-on setting.
