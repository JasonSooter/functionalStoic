# Angel Guardian Orphanage

Former orphanage on Chicago's North Side, closed in 1974. In a Basecamp story it is where the florist Roy Sheffield grew up and first worked with flowers.

## Special Arrangements — Basecamp (2015)

Source: [Original](https://thedistance.com/ashland-addison-florist-co/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g16pe7kqqn6yr7e5k3t5vy)

> He grew up at Angel Guardian Orphanage, an institution on Chicago’s north side that operated for more than 100 years before closing in 1974.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Sheffield grows up at the orphanage and is put to work in its greenhouse; at 18 he leaves and starts hauling flowers from growers to wholesalers.
- **Why it matters:** The greenhouse work starts the career that becomes the business.
