> McMorris was born and raised in rural farmland in the province of Saskatchewan.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** Mark McMorris grows up on farmland in Saskatchewan, far from any mountain. With little access to snow, he and his brother build their skills through wakeboarding, skateboarding, surfing and trampoline jumping.
- **Why it matters:** The flat province is the constraint in the story; elsewhere his path would have been ordinary.
