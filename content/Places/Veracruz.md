# Veracruz

Veracruz is a port city on the Gulf coast of Mexico, near where Cortés landed in 1519. _Sapiens_ uses the landing as the Aztecs' first encounter with an unknown people.

## At a glance
| Source | Author | Role | In one line |

## Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind — Yuval Noah Harari (2014)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17075811W)

> When Cortés and his men landed on the sunny beaches of today’s Vera Cruz, it was the first time the Aztecs encountered a completely unknown people.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In 1519 Cortés landed on the beaches of today's Vera Cruz with about 550 men, claiming to be a peaceful emissary of the king of Spain. The Aztecs, who believed they knew the whole world, did not know what to make of the strangers and hesitated while he marched inland with local allies. Within a century of the landing, the book says, the native population of the Americas had fallen by about 90 per cent.
- **Why it matters:** It marks first contact in the book's account of the Aztec collapse, told as an alien invasion.
