# Bern

Swiss federal city on a loop of the Aare; see [[Switzerland]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ Einstein works at its patent office while writing his 1905 papers.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m80txjht260339bdps91w5rv)

> In between these events, in 1902, Einstein took a job with the Swiss patent office, where he stayed for the next seven years.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Einstein takes a job with the Swiss patent office in 1902 and stays seven years. As a technical examiner third class with no university post, he writes the 1905 papers, special relativity among them, and Bryson pictures him conceiving spacetime while staring out of a patent office window in the capital of Switzerland. When the papers draw little notice he goes back to his job.
- **Why it matters:** The patent office is the unlikely birthplace of relativity. ⚠️ The text names the office and the capital, not the building, so the pin is the city.
