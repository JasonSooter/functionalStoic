# Vandenberg Space Force Base

Launch base on the California coast (Vandenberg Air Force Base until 2021). In Range Gravity Probe B's launch is scrubbed there.

## Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World — David Epstein (2019)

Source: [[Range]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550188/range-by-david-epstein/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvtyx95sy7ms4d327pch61)

> Plus, the probe had been moved to Vandenberg Air Force Base in California for launch, and a delay would increase the chance of GP-B sitting there when an earthquake struck.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Gravity Probe B waited here before launch while program manager Rex Geveden decided to scrub the launch and pull a suspect electronics box. An earthquake damaged the launch vehicle a month later, and the probe launched in April 2004.
- **Why it matters:** Setting for Geveden's decision, a counterexample to NASA's Challenger-era conformity. Secondary place.
