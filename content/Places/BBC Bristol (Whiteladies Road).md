## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx3npg8dvqhgs71qgmm708)

> The one spectacular and lasting success, however, was West Region, based in Bristol.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Attenborough explains how BBC regional policy led to the Natural History Unit in Bristol under Desmond Hawkins, contrasted with Manchester, Birmingham and Scotland.
- **Why it matters:** Explains the institutional home of BBC natural history; a place as institution rather than scene.
