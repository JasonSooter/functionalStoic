# Taipei International Cycle Show

Asia's big annual bicycle trade fair in Taipei, [[Taiwan]]. In _The Brompton_ it is where the young Will Butler-Adams publicly confronted Brompton's Japanese distributor, a fight that ended up opening the Asian market.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8cdhhwzxyz5611d77dn6mme)

> That was how I ended up arguing at the Taipei International Cycle Show with a man who collected samurai swords.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In his first years at Brompton, Will travels to sign up distributors. At the Taipei show he tells Toshi Mizutani, head of the Japan Bicycle Association, that Brompton will find another distributor unless he stops selling Eurotai's look-alike Neobikes beside real Bromptons and cuts the trading-house chain that made the bikes so expensive in Japan. London hears he is a "loose cannon", but the supply chain is shortened, and Mizutani later takes Will to buy swords.
- **Why it matters:** The book credits the row with fixing Brompton's position in Japan and the wider Asian market, and as an early sign of Will's style.
