# Washington Heights

Neighbourhood at the northern tip of [[Manhattan]], below the George Washington Bridge. In Aoibheann Sweeney's novel it is Ana's home, where Miranda has a dress made and the two first make love.

## Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking — Aoibheann Sweeney (2007)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/among-other-things-ive-taken-up-smoking-9781101202227)

> The dressmaker’s shop was on a busy street full of small shops in Ana’s neighborhood, above a grocery store and advertised by the word TAILOR in pink neon lighting; WEDDINGS & COMMUNIONS was painted in neat letters in the next window.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Ana drives Miranda up the West Side Highway, the Hudson on one side and the George Washington Bridge ahead, to Coco's tailor shop above a grocery, where the fussy dressmaker settles on a backless red silk dress chosen by Ana. Nearby, at Ana's family apartment among the brownstones, boys on a stoop heckle them; inside, under a picture of the Virgin Mary, the two kiss and begin to make love on the couch.
- **Why it matters:** It is where Miranda's affair with Ana becomes real, in Ana's own world rather than at the coffee cart or the institute.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The novel never names the neighbourhood the drive leads to, but Ana tells Miranda earlier that she is from Washington Heights, and the drive north toward the George Washington Bridge fits.
