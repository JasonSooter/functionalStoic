# Kagan Valley

Valley of the Kunhar river in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. Dervla Murphy follows its river down from the Babusar in _Full Tilt_.

> The Kagan Valley is so beautiful that one has to stop every hundred yards to sit and look.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** She follows the infant Kagan River from its glacial lake, past glaciers, rock falls, nomads' herds, eagles and wild flowers, down to Naran and beyond, watching it widen.
- **Why it matters:** A valley of great beauty whose river she follows from source to the plains.
