# La Casa Permanent Supportive Housing

Seven-storey supportive-housing building for formerly homeless men in Columbia Heights, [[Washington DC|Washington, D.C.]]. A LifeEdited post holds it up as smart design for people at the margins.

## Living Well in the Margins — David Friedlander (2015)

Source: [Original](https://lifeedited.com/living-well-in-the-margins/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g8v2ry89jevrp1xtgt2p0k)

> La Casa Permanent Supportive Housing in Washington DC is a great example of such a beast.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The post profiles the building, designed by LEO A DALY and Studio Twenty Seven Architecture for the DC Department of Human Services: furnished efficiency apartments for 40 chronically homeless men, a lobby, a second-floor community room and terrace, and a green roof. It won a 2015 AIA Housing Award.
- **Why it matters:** The building is the whole subject of the post, its example of dignified small-space living for people with the least. The text gives no address; the pin uses the city's published address.
