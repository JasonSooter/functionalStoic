# Mayflower Hotel

Grand hotel on Connecticut Avenue in [[Washington DC|Washington, D.C.]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ its bar is where Aldrich Ames waits for a Soviet contact before walking to the embassy to sell secrets.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7vbqmv94xq0zejvvttvz9fw)

> Chuvakhin agreed to meet Ames for drinks at 4 p.m. on April 16, at the bar of the Mayflower Hotel, not far from the Soviet embassy on Sixteenth Street in Washington, DC.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In April 1985 the CIA officer Ames, nervous, waits at the Mayflower bar and drinks a vodka martini, then two more. When the Soviet diplomat Sergei Chuvakhin still hasn't come after an hour, Ames decides to "improvise" and walks up Connecticut Avenue to the Soviet embassy with the package he meant for him.
- **Why it matters:** The decision taken at this bar sets off the betrayal that will eventually expose Gordievsky. Had Chuvakhin turned up, the package might have gone by a slower route.
