> At Urbana ’70, Ma and Dad found themselves leaving the civilizing and conserving and racist church they realized they’d been part of.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** At InterVarsity's Urbana '70 conference, Kendi's parents heard Tom Skinner's keynote and Soul Liberation, and attended Black caucuses all week. They left their old church and were converted to Black liberation theology.
- **Why it matters:** The author says his own journey begins here, so the conference shapes the book's definitions and the family's creed; the arena choice is incidental but the event is foundational.
