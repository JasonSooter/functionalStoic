# Måsøyvær (The Place of Tides)

Anna's family sea estate in the [[Vega Archipelago]], Norway, a little west of [[Vega (Norway)|Vega]]. In James Rebanks's memoir it is the island her family is named after, abandoned after the war, and the duck station she set out to bring back to life.

## The Place of Tides — James Rebanks (2024)

Source: [[The Place of Tides]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/315745/the-place-of-tides-by-rebanks-james/9780241426937) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m81td4vcptbg8zet4zrwk6ba)

> Anna looked towards the south and pointed across the sea, a little to the west of Vega. ‘Måsøyvær,’ she said.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** On the hill of Fjærøy, Anna tells Rebanks she has run that island for only twelve years: "Måsøyvær before that … I am from Måsøyvær." Over rainy days she tells its history: her great-great-grandfather Harald bought the sea estate in 1852 and his children built it up to 1,500 nests; in the war German soldiers netted the tame eiders, and by 1945 Måsøy made only 400 nests; her parents left in 1946, two years before she was born. Her father kept going out to Måsøy, but escaped farm mink "would turn up on an island and kill every last eider", and the duck station "was slowly falling into disrepair". After seeing a stuffed eider in the new museum on Vega, Anna resolves to bring the ducks back, and the government has her released from her nursing-home job "to work on the abandoned duck station of Måsøyvær".
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's buried question: why Anna now keeps ducks on someone else's island rather than her own. Its decline and rebirth carry the story of the whole outer-island way of life.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Best guess. The text puts Måsøyvær "a little to the west of Vega", south of Fjærøy as seen from its hill, at one corner of "a giant triangle of ocean, a few miles long on each side" with Vega and Fjærøy. No mapped island near Vega carries the name (Nominatim finds only Måsøya islands in Brønnøy, Rødøy and further north), so it may be a pen name. Pinned at the Vega Archipelago's representative point. Alternative: one of the outer duck islands west of Vega, such as Lånan, but no source names it.
