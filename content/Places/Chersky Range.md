# Chersky Range

Mountain system in north-eastern Siberia, cut by the canyons of the Indigirka and Kolyma rivers. In _Last of the Breed_ its deep canyons are the refuge Joe Mack makes for while the army sweeps the country south of the Kolyma.

## Last of the Breed — Louis L'Amour (1986)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL51048W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6y2dje2gpwy5nxq3tm2acc0)

> One of the things Baronas had told him was of the great canyons in the Chersky region through which the Indigirka and Kolyma rivers flowed, canyons said to be more than six thousand feet deep.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** With the Kolyma watched, Joe Mack asks himself, "Suppose he fled to those canyons? Lost himself in one of them until the chase was over?" Slipping past a camp of soldiers, he works west and then north, meaning to "find the Chersky Mountains somewhere ahead and lose himself in one of the canyons of which he had heard," while Rukovsky sweeps the forest south of the river with a thousand men and later moves his base to Oymyakon. He reaches a canyon at least a mile deep that he takes for the Indigirka's.
- **Why it matters:** The range gives the middle of the book its strategy: instead of outrunning the army, Joe Mack goes to ground in country so deep and broken that searchers cannot cover it.
