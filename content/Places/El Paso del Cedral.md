# El Paso del Cedral

Reef dive site off the west coast of Cozumel, Mexico. In The Soul of an Octopus (Sy Montgomery) it is where she sees her first wild octopus.

## The Soul of an Octopus — Sy Montgomery (2015)

Source: [[The Soul of an Octopus]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL27182716M/The_Soul_of_an_Octopus) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fzcrtnkg144gtrtdg41d80)

> “We are diving at a place called El Paso del Cedral,” our charismatic, barrel- chested divemaster, Francisco Marrufo, explains

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On a drift dive, divemaster Francisco leads her to a hole beside a large brain coral where an octopus changes colour and peers out; she weeps with joy back on the boat.
- **Why it matters:** Her longing to meet octopuses in their own world is fulfilled here.
