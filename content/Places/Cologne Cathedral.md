# Cologne Cathedral

Gothic cathedral beside the main station in [[Cologne]], which Bill Bryson found ruined by the barren plaza around it and a rail video names as the landmark at the end of its Brussels ride.

## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74125W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5b43k9w3ap34vee6n4n64jd)

> If you can imagine Salisbury Cathedral in the middle of a shopping-center parking lot, you may get the picture.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Chapter 7. "Cologne is a dismal place, which rather pleased me," Bryson writes: it is comforting that the Germans "could make a hash of a city as well as anyone else". You come out of the station and the cathedral stands at the top of an escalator, in "a vast, windswept, elevated concrete plaza that is heart-numbingly barren and forlorn". He goes in, and walks on to the terrace over the Rhine. Later the station's porn cinema puts him off staying, and he leaves for Amsterdam.
- **Why it matters:** It's his evidence that postwar planning could wreck even a famous city, and the counterweight to the small cathedral at [[Aachen Cathedral|Aachen]] that moved him the day before.

## I Rode Germany's Most Insane High-Speed Train — Simply Railway (2026)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jPEgRQaYDQc) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5brexwewzbbm47r43c2cdz9)

> More than 600 years of construction! It survived the bombing of World War II — and it’s right outside the station.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the video:** The ride from Brussels-Midi via [[Liège-Guillemins station]] and Aachen ends at Cologne, where the narrator recommends using a layover to see the landmark outside the station. The text doesn't name it, but six centuries of construction, wartime survival and its place beside the station identify it as the cathedral.
- **Why it matters:** The destination's reward rather than a subject; a quick stop, unlike Bryson's long look.
