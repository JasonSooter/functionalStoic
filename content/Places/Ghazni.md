## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)
Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6m31zpjpzresp1ysbvvn5)

> I was pushing myself to allow time to explore this city which I guessed would be congenial because of historical associations and no Americanisation; in fact it’s a good second to Herat.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** She reaches Ghazni early, but while strolling below the old fortifications she is seized by soldiers and locked in a filthy cell for fifty minutes. An apologetic officer then lends her an army car to see the poet's tomb on a hill at sunset.
- **Why it matters:** Ghazni is a comic scene of military suspicion followed by a calm sunset above orchards, and the last town before the road blocks that put her on a bus to Kabul.
