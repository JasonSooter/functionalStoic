## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2fxdz0d7yb19ngs48j3k)

> Surabaya is the second biggest city in Java.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Surabaya is the team's base. They meet Daan and Peggy Hubrecht at a Chinese restaurant party, stay in their house, hire a sugar-factory jeep (whose half-shafts have to be bought back from the thieves' market), and keep their animal collection in the Hubrechts' garden and Surabaya Zoo between trips.
- **Why it matters:** A recurring base and the source of the hospitality that makes the expedition possible.
