# Columbia Trail

Rail trail of about 15 miles through Hunterdon and Morris counties, New Jersey, starting at High Bridge. A cycling blog rides it before a night at an observatory.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The author rides the unpaved, shaded former railroad trail, climbing gradually for the first miles from High Bridge, and finds the Morris County section more uneven, with horse manure to dodge.
