# New England Aquarium

Public aquarium on Central Wharf on Boston's waterfront, Massachusetts. It turns up in The Soul of an Octopus (Sy Montgomery): Boston aquarium where the narrator first meets and befriends octopuses Athena and Octavia.

## The Soul of an Octopus — Sy Montgomery (2015)

Source: [[The Soul of an Octopus]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL27182716M/The_Soul_of_an_Octopus) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmt2satccy5tk4cy5nxt6y)

> But I quit the blessed sunlight for the moist, dim sanctuary of the New England Aquarium. I had a date with a giant Paci c octopus.

- **Role:** setting

- **In the book:** The narrator goes to the aquarium to meet Athena, a giant Pacific octopus, and returns repeatedly through the chapter, touching her in the Cold Marine Gallery and meeting Octavia later. The text describes the Cold Marine tank rooms, the spiral ramp around the Giant Ocean Tank and the Freshwater Gallery's electric eel exhibit. Staff Scott, Bill and Wilson are based here.

- **Why it matters:** It is the main setting of the opening chapters, where the narrator's friendship with octopuses begins. Set anywhere else, the cast of keepers and the Athena and Octavia encounters would not exist.

- **Later in the book:** In chapters 3 and 4 the aquarium stays the main stage: the new octopus Kali lives in a drilled pickle barrel in the sump behind the scenes, where staff and volunteers visit her on 'Wonderful Wednesdays', and the narrator arrives before opening to watch Octavia guard her eggs in her exhibit tank.

- **Later still:** As the Giant Ocean Tank is rebuilt, Kali is moved from her barrel into tank C1 in the Cold Marine gallery behind the scenes; overnight she escapes and is found on the floor in the morning, and dies despite efforts to revive her.
