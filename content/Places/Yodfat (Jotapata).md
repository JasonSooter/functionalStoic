# Yodfat (Jotapata)

Village in the Lower Galilee, Israel, near the ancient Jewish town of Jotapata. In an Aeon essay on Masada it is where Josephus survived a suicide pact, a story he may have reused.

## Have archaeologists proven the ancient tale of mass suicide in the Judaean desert or twisted science for political end? — Eric H Cline
Source: [Original](https://aeon.co/essays/decoding-the-ancient-tale-of-mass-suicide-in-the-judaean-desert) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5rjzm7gx4cmjz4078xvwg)

> Several years earlier, in 67 CE, during the initial rebellion against Rome, Josephus had been a Jewish general fighting the Romans at a site called Jotapata.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** At Jotapata Josephus and 40 others took refuge in a cave and agreed to kill each other rather than surrender, leaving only Josephus and one other alive. Cline suggests Josephus transplanted this experience into his Masada account.
- **Why it matters:** Offers an argued source for the suspect Masada story.
