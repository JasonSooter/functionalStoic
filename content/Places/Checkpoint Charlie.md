## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling (2019)
Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20831115W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqz74q1r30mas4hpxxh9qd)

> I crossed the border at Checkpoint Charlie and spent the whole day walking around East Berlin.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On the way home from his 1966 hitchhiking trip, aged eighteen, Rosling crossed into East Berlin and walked around it all day, five years after the Wall went up.
- **Why it matters:** He calls the visit an effective vaccination against extreme left-wing views.
