# Magadan

Port town on the Sea of Okhotsk in Russia's Far East. In _Last of the Breed_ it is where Kyra Lebedev hunts for word of Joe Mack through the black market, and the town he weighs and rejects as a winter hideout.

## Last of the Breed — Louis L'Amour (1986)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL51048W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6y2dcwtywbk6kn8zd3rkezg)

> Magadan was just such a place of empty spaces, structures hurriedly thrown up on somebody's order or some bureaucratic whim.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Working for Colonel Zamatev, Kyra comes to Magadan in winter, keeps off Lenin Square and walks back streets to her cousin Katerina's and then to Vanya, a writer on Siberia who sympathises with the American; she learns that Shepilov is in town recruiting trappers to track him. Later Ostap, Lev, Kraslov and Botev meet by a fire in the unfinished framework of a building ("One never knew in Magadan") and talk of selling the American to Zamatev, and Kyra slips out through a coal room and an alley to Stegman's helicopter. Joe Mack himself considers wintering in Magadan and decides it is too dangerous.
- **Why it matters:** Magadan is the hunters' base in the far north-east, the town of informers ("If anyone breathes in Magadan, he hears it in Yakutsk!"). Its black market shows how the chase runs on bribes and tip-offs as much as on soldiers.
