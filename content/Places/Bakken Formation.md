## 5 things I learned about managing my money from covering the oil bust — Emily Guerin (2016)
Source: [Original](https://www.hcn.org/articles/5-things-i-learned-about-managing-my-money-from-covering-the-oil-bust) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy74gd4t0kt046pf0cgg4p)

> A lot of people in the Bakken did not start out as oilfield workers.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article is built on the author's reporting in the Bakken oil patch during the 2014-15 price collapse. She describes the boom-era optimism, truck drivers on golden handcuffs, and workers who reinvented themselves when the bust hit. Each of the five lessons comes from people she met there.
- **Why it matters:** The oil boom and bust in North Dakota is the entire premise of the piece and shapes every lesson.
