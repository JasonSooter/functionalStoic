# I-95 bridge (Philadelphia)

This is the stretch of Interstate 95 in Northeast Philadelphia that collapsed after a tanker fire on June 11, 2023. _Abundance_ uses its quick rebuilding as proof that government can build fast.

## Abundance — Ezra Klein & Derek Thompson (2025)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL44034521W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m7811a10t1e2v5426xbcdm)

> The truck ignited underneath the I-95 bridge in Philadelphia, killing the driver and melting the steel beams undergirding it.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On June 11, 2023, a tanker truck carrying gasoline flipped and burned under the I-95 bridge in Philadelphia, and the bridge collapsed. Governor Josh Shapiro signed an emergency declaration that let the state skip its usual design, review and bidding steps; transportation secretary Mike Carroll describes the risks. The road reopened in twelve days instead of the months first forecast.
- **Why it matters:** It is chapter 3's showcase of a government that can make choices and build quickly, the counterexample to California's high-speed rail.
