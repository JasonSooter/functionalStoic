## Why the Jeddah Tower Won’t be as Tall as You Think — The B1M (2026)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vVfEs9wrW98) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5breqw4f7027kssbm0me3vt)

> Shortly after ground broke on the Chrysler Building, Ohrstrom came up with a plan for   a shorter, 47 storey building a few miles away at 40 Wall street.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** George Ohrstrom's tower was raised in height to beat the [[Chrysler Building]], only to be overtaken when the Chrysler's spire went up.
- **Why it matters:** The other half of the 1929 rivalry; without it the Chrysler story has no opponent.
