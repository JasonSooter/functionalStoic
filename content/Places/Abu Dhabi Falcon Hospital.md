## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7xbmcpdztdyhbzfy3j3x2)

> One of the most intricate operations at the Falcon Hospital is the repair of broken feathers.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The offspring of Hassan's peregrines are photographed for the hospital's catalogue; the fragment details the feather repair room and new flight patterns captured on camera. The sheikh calls the birds his Birds of Sorrow.
- **Why it matters:** The distant end of the falcons' journey from Jerusalem.
