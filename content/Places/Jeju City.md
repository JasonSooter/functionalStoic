# Jeju City

The capital of Jeju Province on the island's north coast. In _The Island of Sea Women_ it is the port, Mi-ja's married home, and the scene of the 1 March 1947 shooting.

## The Island of Sea Women — Lisa See (2019)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19865296W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvcmh9kxswtk790fzdpk5r)

> We came around a corner and into a large square. Police stood on the broad steps leading to the entrance of the station, with their bayonets thrust threateningly before them.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Mi-ja's stories of her childhood in Jeju City fascinate Young-sook; later the friends wait on its wharf for the Busan ferry and meet Lee Sang-mun there. On 1 March 1947 twenty thousand marchers reach the police station square, police fire into the crowd and six die; Young-sook and Mi-ja pull a baby from beneath her dead mother, and Sang-mun slaps Mi-ja. Mi-ja's quiet Japanese-style house in the city shows her isolation.
- **Why it matters:** The city is the island's gateway and the seat of power; the 1947 shooting opens the 'Fear' section and exposes Sang-mun.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The 1 March 1947 shooting near Gwandeokjeong is a real event.
