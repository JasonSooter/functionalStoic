## Why Cities Feel so Lonely Now. — Type Ashton (2025)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JDSAkaep9c0) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crcs63j0xs39eams2y8tq4)

> Just take a look at this phenomenon called the Delmare Divide. In St. Louis, Missouri, Delmare Boulevard cuts in city from east to west.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The narrator (the transcript spells it 'Delmare') presents the Delmar Divide as an example of segregation in American cities: the street separates wealthier neighbourhoods to the south from poorer ones to the north, with [[Forest Park (St Louis)]] on one side and [[Sherman Park (St Louis)]] on the other.
- **Why it matters:** The street is the evidence for the argument that access to good parks tracks wealth.
