## Frugal Man Buys $52,000 Car – Why?? — Mr. Money Mustache (2023)
Source: [Original](https://www.mrmoneymustache.com/2023/04/27/why-buy-model-y/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmq8ye9bj7pca5zjtr3vz5)

> A recent life change (becoming a co-owner of a fixer-upper vacation rental compound in beautiful Salida Colorado) has reignited the travel fire in my heart

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** The author has just become co-owner of a fixer-upper vacation rental compound in Salida, shown in a sunrise photo as the new 'Friends Mountain Resort'. It reignites his love of travel and 'Carpentourism', and a transmission failure on a mountain pass on the way home from his project there is the nudge to buy the car.
- **Why it matters:** Salida is the occasion for the car purchase and the author's recent life change, but the text gives little about the town itself; elsewhere the argument would be unchanged.

## The Comfort Crisis — Mr. Money Mustache (2023)
Source: [Original](https://www.mrmoneymustache.com/2023/07/23/the-comfort-crisis/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmqaambj3hv6r677hdmfhb)

> fixing up a mini-resort property in Salida, and starting construction on a small cabin in Durango

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** On the solo road trip the author limps into Salida after a rainstorm, a traffic fine and a cracked windshield, to unpack and work on the mini-resort property. It is the first stop of the trip before he heads three hours deeper into the mountains.
- **Why it matters:** It is the starting construction site and first stop of the trip whose hardships illustrate the book's lessons.
