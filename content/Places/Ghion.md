## Peace Corps: The Great Adventure — Peace Corps (1997)

Source: [[Peace Corps - The Great Adventure]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL16050643W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e33m9twvpvsda26wzx1ra7)

> And thanks to months of language training, I arrived in the highland village of Ghion armed with a great deal of Amharic, Ethiopia's national language

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** A woman volunteer describes teaching English in the highland village of Ghion in Ethiopia. She learns to spin cotton from a neighbour, is judged a ferenji by everyone, and one night on the bus into Ghion stabs a pompous Addis lawyer with her umbrella for taking a farmer's seat; the police jail the lawyer rather than her, and she is hailed as the Peace Corps volunteer from Ghion.
- **Why it matters:** The village is the place where she finally belongs, the setting of her closing anecdote about being a foreigner yet at home.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The text does not name the real place; the pin is a best guess.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ best-guess. Clues in the text: "the highland village of Ghion"; "Africa is mighty cold at 8,000 feet above sea level"; buses made "a second run back to the capital before nightfall" from a village halfway to Ghion; "lawyer from Addis Ababa"; teaches at a school, speaks Amharic. Reference works: GeoNames search 'Ghion' (ET) returns only Waliso (336372, 8°32′03″N 37°57′54″E, populated place, Oromiya), so Ghion is an alternate name; en Wikipedia Waliso: 114 km southwest of Addis Ababa, elevation 2,063 m (about 6,770 ft) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waliso). The 8,000 ft claim is somewhat higher than Waliso's elevation. Chosen: Waliso (Ghion), Southwest Shewa Zone, Oromia, Ethiopia — Single GeoNames alternate-name match plus bus-to-capital and highland clues; elevation is slightly off, so best-guess. Alternatives: A different Ghion (the Gion/Giyon name is shared by the Blue Nile and by Addis Ababa's Ghion Hotel, which is a city landmark, not a village) cannot be excluded.
