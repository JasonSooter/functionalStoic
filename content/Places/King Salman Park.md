# King Salman Park

Giant urban park under construction on the site of Riyadh's old airport, in the centre of [[Riyadh]]. A B1M video presents it as one of the world's largest parks, meant to cool and green the city.

## This Will Be The World’s Biggest Park — The B1M (2026)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VpuWi9a3MrE) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m73hnv8af7sqzcsmrebvshtx)

> King Salman Park is more   than five times larger than Central Park.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The park is built on a former airport in the middle of Riyadh, at over 16 square kilometres. Earthworks move some 54 million cubic metres of earth to cut artificial wadis up to 30 m deep, road tunnels take main arteries underground, and on-site nurseries grow most of its million trees. It is laid out as a ring with hubs and "green fingers", and the video says 75% of the capital will live within 30 minutes of it.
- **Why it matters:** The whole video is about this site: its scale, its promise to cool the city's heat island, and Riyadh's ambition to become more livable by 2030.
