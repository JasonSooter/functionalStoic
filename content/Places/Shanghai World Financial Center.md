## The Skyscraper That Divided China — The B1M (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8eKzeUbiy7E) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqy3dw2bkp2aeqwwb2dxqk)

> This is the Shanghai World Financial Center. Almost the world's tallest

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** American architect William Pedersen (spelled Pettison/Pedison in the transcript) of KPF designed it for Japanese developer Minoru Mori with a circular void symbolising the heavens. Plans were approved in 1993, work stopped after the 1997 crisis, and it was raised 32-40 m after 2001. Chinese public outrage that the circle resembled a Japanese flag forced a redesign in 2005 to a trapezoid, and it finished in 2008.
- **Why it matters:** It shows the symbolic power of tall buildings and the China skyscraper boom. It briefly held the roof-height record and is nicknamed the bottle opener.
