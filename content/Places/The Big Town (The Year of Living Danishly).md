# The Big Town (The Year of Living Danishly)

The Big Town is Helen Russell's name for the market town near her Jutland village, best guessed as [[Vejle]]. It appears in her memoir as the public stage for the year's Danish rituals.

## The Year of Living Danishly — Helen Russell (2015)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20000553W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f1vgtpd58qmcze9hmcb73r)

> Lego Man and I are just finishing off a quiet dinner in The Big Town when an almighty roar starts up from the street.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In the last two chapters the Big Town is the stage for J-Day (a foam truck, a Tom Cruise lookalike and julebryg), Santa's arrival and the tree lighting in the main square, a julefrokost in the old town hall, Russell's meeting with her accountant, and New Year's Eve fireworks on the high street.
- **Why it matters:** It is where the book's year of Danish rituals plays out in public.
- **Real vs. fictional:** "The Big Town" is Russell's nickname; the book never names it.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Best guess. The text gives a market town a short drive from the village, with a main square, a canal, an old town hall, red-brick flats and a high street. No reference work names it. If the village is Brejning, the obvious large town is Vejle; Russell's acknowledgements thank "Team Vejle", and Scan Magazine says she later moved to Vejle. The main alternative is Børkop, the small town next to Brejning.
