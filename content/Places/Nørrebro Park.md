# Nørrebro Park

Inner-city park in [[Nørrebro]], Copenhagen, Denmark. In _Happy City_ it is Charles Montgomery's example of a public space designed so that everyone, including the neighbourhood's drinkers, has a place in it.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m84c82s0jd7jaefae75ramxm)

> I realized this in Copenhagen when planners walked me through the recently renovated Nørrebro Park, an inner-city green space.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 10. Planners walk Montgomery through the renovated park. Beside the lawn and the playground is a fenced hut for the drinkers who spend their days there: planner Henrik Lyng found them by going to the park with a case of beer, and they asked for a place to hang out undisturbed and a toilet.
- **Why it matters:** The park shows fairness at the scale of a single green space: it works, the chapter concludes, because it acknowledges through design that everyone has a right to be there.
