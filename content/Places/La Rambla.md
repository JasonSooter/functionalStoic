## Why Cities and Walking Go Hand in Hand — Sandy James (2016)

Source: [Original](https://www.strongtowns.org/journal/2016/8/22/why-cities-and-walking-go-hand-in-hand) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy87z0vxq6d1depjcb9pm1)

> In Barcelona, I met a reporter for Radio Spain while walking through the neighborhoods surrounding La Rambla, a tree-lined pedestrian mall full of families, public art and artists.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** She walks the neighbourhoods around La Rambla with a radio reporter, discussing how walking aids health and sociability and how people make public spaces feel complete.
- **Why it matters:** Illustrates the author's claim that pedestrian places are social; a moderately developed example.
