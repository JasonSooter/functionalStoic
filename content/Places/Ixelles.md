# Ixelles

French-speaking municipality in the south of [[Brussels]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where Frans hides after the war and falls for Mimousse.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85n9se5hj1pcb9pe2yz279f)

> On December 1, 1946, he found himself standing on Mimousse’s doorstep in Ixelles.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Cousin Kurt Strandmann, exploiting the post-war chaos, gets papers for an apartment in the French-speaking district of Ixelles; Frans, smuggled into Belgium from [[Oss]], joins him there in late 1945. He meets the divorced Mimousse, and it is love at first sight; after his release from [[Scheveningen prison]] he turns up on her Ixelles doorstep on 1 December 1946, days before he is meant to leave for South America. In February 1947 police raid her Brussels apartment and find black-market pharmaceuticals.
- **Why it matters:** Ixelles is where Frans's post-war life slips out of his father's control: the love affair begun here wrecks the Old Boss's South America plan.
