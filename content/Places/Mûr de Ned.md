> The Mûr de Ned is a short cobbled hill in Lewisham SE13 7UA.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** TV cycling commentator Ned Boulting runs a timed 'hill climb' up the hill for the second year; entrants can run, walk or ride, on bikes supplied by XO bikes. Short of entrants on the day, the author joins in, his first bike race in years, and rides it in 11.16 seconds. Entrants include Nigel the health and safety guy and Gilbert the dog.
- **Why it matters:** The hill is the whole event: a quirky community race on one short cobbled street, which leaves the author wondering whether to compete on a bike again.
