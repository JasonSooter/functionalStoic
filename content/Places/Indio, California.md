# Indio, California

Desert city in the Coachella Valley on Interstate 10. In Greenlights it is where Matthew McConaughey mistakes the Sunset Drive exit for Sunset Boulevard on his drive to Hollywood.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zp464p370w9fa6wgy7k7sf)

> My adrenaline pumping for arrival, I’d been driving for just over twenty hours straight when I got to Indio, California.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On the I-10 drive west from Austin he sees an exit for "Sunset Dr." at Indio at 8 p.m., decides it must be the legendary Sunset Boulevard, and starts the Doors' L.A. Woman he had saved for his arrival. Sunset Drive turns out to be about 162 miles from Sunset Boulevard, and he plays the song twenty-two times before reaching Malibu.
- **Why it matters:** A comic waypoint on the road to Hollywood; it shows his eagerness rather than changing the story.
