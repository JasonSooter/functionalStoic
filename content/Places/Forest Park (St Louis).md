## Why Cities Feel so Lonely Now. — Type Ashton (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JDSAkaep9c0) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crctp95dhgbaqggf3phcb3)

> South of Delmare in the wealthier neighborhood is Forest Park, home to the St. Louis Zoo, a history museum, a golf course, and an ice skating rink.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** Named as the well-equipped park south of [[Delmar Boulevard]]. The narrator argues that nearness, not mere availability, matters, so being allowed to visit Forest Park does not make up for the poor parks north of the divide.
- **Why it matters:** It is the well-resourced half of the comparison.
