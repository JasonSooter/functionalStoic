# Whole Foods Market (East Houston Street)

Grocery store filling most of a block of East Houston Street between Orchard and Ludlow on the [[Lower East Side]], Manhattan. In _Happy City_ its long smoked-glass facade is where volunteers on the author's psychological walking tours felt least happy.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m841xcazpq6j6qx4jp76aegs)

> On East Houston Street in Lower Manhattan, the small-lot urban fabric between Orchard and Ludlow was replaced in 2006 by a Whole Foods grocery store that presents a nearly unbroken swath of smoked glass for much of an entire city block.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** During experiments run from the BMW Guggenheim Lab, volunteers on the author's psychological tours of the neighbourhood reported feeling markedly less happy on the sidewalk outside this facade than almost anywhere else on the route. They felt much better a block east on Houston, along a grittier stretch of small shops and restaurants.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's concrete evidence that long, dead facades do not just speed people up but bring them down emotionally.
