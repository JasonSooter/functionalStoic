# Milner Pass

Continental Divide pass on Trail Ridge Road in Rocky Mountain National Park, Colorado. Where Tim Ryback's part of the trek ends.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs29ed36pz0xxfpj24e94r)

> The next day we rose at dawn and, without breakfast, fought our way through the wooded valleys in order to arrive at Milner Pass before noon, when we had scheduled our rendezvous with the family.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After telling Eric the night before that he can't go on, Tim waits with him beside the Continental Divide sign for the family. Tim leaves the trail here; after four days of rest Eric is dropped back at the pass, alone.
- **Why it matters:** It splits the book: from here Eric walks solo, and the book's tone shifts from the brothers to solitude.
