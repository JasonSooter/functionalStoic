## Is This New York's Most Cursed Skyscraper? — The B1M (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=01KX_JXHH2M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqy42z5df482ra24kpgmmh)

> 432 Park Avenue is one of the world’s most iconic skyscrapers stretching high above the New York skyline.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The video tells the story of Rafael Viñoly's 425.5 m, square-footprint residential tower: its slender 1:15 structure, the tuned mass dampers added against wind sway, the 2015 launch and 90 percent sell-out. It then follows the condo board's construction-defect lawsuits against developers Harry Macklowe and CIM Group ($125m, later a second suit seeking more than $160m) over noise, elevators, flooding and a cracking facade.
- **Why it matters:** It is the article's whole subject: a tallest-in-class luxury tower whose engineering and litigation illustrate the risks of pencil-tower supertalls on Billionaires' Row.
