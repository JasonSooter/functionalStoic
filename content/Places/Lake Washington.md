# Lake Washington

Large lake on Seattle's eastern edge, Washington. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is the Washington crew's training water, the scene of the 1932 race against California that Coach Ulbrickson cannot forget, and of the freshmen's 1934 win over Cal.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m869y9xph2gjyvwwhh2bjeax)

> Biting winds blew in from the southwest, kicking up legions of whitecaps on Lake Washington.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 1932 more than a hundred thousand people lined the shores to watch Washington race California, and a strong wind swamped the Washington varsity boat in the worst defeat in the school's history, which still haunts Ulbrickson. In the autumn of 1933 the freshmen move the training barge _Old Nero_ out of the Cut onto the lake, and as the weather turns, cold, rain and whitecaps wear the 175 hopefuls down. Earlier, Hiram Conibear ran along its shores shouting at his crews through a megaphone. On April 13, 1934, nearly eighty thousand people watch from a student ferry, an observation train along the west shore and a semicircle of boats off Sheridan Beach as Joe's freshmen race two miles from Sand Point and finish four and a half lengths ahead of California, almost twenty seconds under the course record. In the autumn of 1934 rough water keeps the crews ashore day after day. In 1936 Joe's father builds a new house on a lot near the north end of the lake, next door to his brother Fred; standing on Fred's dock in the rain, Joe turns over Pocock's advice about trusting others. On April 18, 1936, the family and Joyce follow the Cal race from that dock as the _Husky Clipper_ wins at [[Sheridan Beach]]. On the morning of the Olympic final, Harry's family and Joyce gather around an old Philco in the nearly finished house to hear the NBC broadcast, and Joe comes home to the house by the lake that September. In 1946, 1956, 1976 and a last time in 1986 the surviving crew takes the _Husky Clipper_ out onto the lake again, "still pulling together as one".
- **Why it matters:** The lake is the crew's daily proving ground; its rough autumn water weeds out all but the toughest boys, the 1934 regatta is the freshmen's first big victory at home, and the house on its shore becomes the home Joe had lost.
