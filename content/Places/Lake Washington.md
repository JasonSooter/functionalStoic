# Lake Washington

Large lake on Seattle's eastern edge, Washington. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is the Washington crew's training water, and the scene of the 1932 race against California that Coach Ulbrickson cannot forget.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m869y9xph2gjyvwwhh2bjeax)

> Biting winds blew in from the southwest, kicking up legions of whitecaps on Lake Washington.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 1932 more than a hundred thousand people lined the shores to watch Washington race California, and a strong wind swamped the Washington varsity boat in the worst defeat in the school's history, which still haunts Ulbrickson. In the autumn of 1933 the freshmen move the training barge _Old Nero_ out of the Cut onto the lake, and as the weather turns, cold, rain and whitecaps wear the 175 hopefuls down. Earlier, Hiram Conibear ran along its shores shouting at his crews through a megaphone.
- **Why it matters:** The lake is the crew's daily proving ground; its rough autumn water is what weeds out all but the toughest boys.
