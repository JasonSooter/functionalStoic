# Anak Krakatau

Volcanic island in the Sunda Strait between Java and Sumatra; in _Life on Air_ Attenborough tries to film its eruption.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx3dywsq54qb2vf4d33bqk)

> A small group of islets, lying in the strait between J ava and Sumatra, were pulverised in a series of titanic explosions.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The team sails from Labuan at night to film the erupting Anak Krakatau. They land on the black ash beach, but repeated rain cycles prevent filming and they spend a miserable night. At dawn Attenborough delivers the eruption story to camera with figures chalked on the boat.
- **Why it matters:** First freelance assignment shot; the eruption story and failed pyrotechnics are a central episode.
