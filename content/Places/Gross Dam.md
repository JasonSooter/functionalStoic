# Gross Dam

Denver Water gravity dam and reservoir in the foothills west of Boulder, [[Colorado]], USA. It is the subject of a B1M video on the plan to raise it and the court injunction that stalled the work.

## Is Colorado’s Massive Dam Expansion in Trouble? — The B1M (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5uh6qLtPpV8) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce3m4h5wwkk3hc0a9kxh6a)

> Back in the 1950s, a large gravity dam was built here to serve the Denver metropolitan area.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The video follows the plan to raise the 340-foot dam by 131 feet with roller-compacted concrete, nearly tripling the reservoir for Denver Water. A court injunction halted work in April 2025, permission to fill the enlarged reservoir has not been given, and environmental groups and residents contest it.
- **Why it matters:** The dam is the subject; its stalled state is the story.
