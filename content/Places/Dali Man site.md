## How China is rewriting the book on human origins — Nature (2016)
Source: [Original](http://www.nature.com/news/how-china-is-rewriting-the-book-on-human-origins-1.20231) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jx74bty5wssejqqamphbh6)

> One nearly complete skull unearthed at Dali in Shaanxi province and dated to 250,000 years ago, has a bigger braincase, a shorter face and a lower cheekbone than most

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The Dali skull is argued by Chinese palaeontologists to be more advanced than European/African H. heidelbergensis and is later floated as possibly Denisovan.
- **Why it matters:** One of the key transitional fossils.
