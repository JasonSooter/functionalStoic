## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy7zeebyfywbbd9yqpj8da)

> Kelly knew j ust the place. The Kali Gandaki valley in Nepal.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The opening prologue of The Living Planet is shot travelling up this valley to show how plants and animals change with altitude. It ends at a small Sherpa hamlet where Attenborough talks of human adaptation as Sherpa women drag a plough.
- **Why it matters:** Provides the series prologue and a key early scene, though the valley itself is described mainly through the planned sequence.
