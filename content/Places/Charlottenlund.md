# Charlottenlund

Residential suburb on the coast north of [[Copenhagen]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ the MI6 station secretary's flat there is the first safe flat where Gordievsky meets his handlers.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7p6zqqafz634386m29yp80k)

> The secretary of the Copenhagen MI6 station lived in a flat in the residential suburb of Charlottenlund.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bromhead arranges to meet Gordievsky at 7 p.m. in the doorway of a butcher's shop near the flat, three weeks after the SAS hotel meeting. On a cold, snowy night he takes a roundabout route to the flat, pours whisky, promises that Britain will keep the case very tightly held, and hands him over to Philip Hawkins, who is waiting there.
- **Why it matters:** It is the first meeting in a safe flat and the handover from recruiter to case officer, where the courtship becomes an operation.
