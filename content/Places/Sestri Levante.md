# Sestri Levante

Seaside town on the Ligurian coast of Italy, east of Genoa. In Greenlights it is where a friend totals his rented Ducati and the shop owner drives down with a new one.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zp4b6hraq2majx9pgkn2ct)

> About eleven days into our ride, we were approaching the seaside town of Sestri Levante, Italy, when Rory laid the Ducati down doing 120 getting off the autobahn exit.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Rory survives with cuts, bruises and a night in hospital, but the new Ducati Monster is totaled. He phones Johan, who asks only whether he is okay and sends a truck to the field "just off exit 74 into Sestri Levante". The next afternoon Johan himself is driving it, and he unloads another brand-new Ducati: "Keep riding."
- **Why it matters:** The crash is where Johan's trust is tested and proved; without it the Rosenheim story would be just a rental.
