# Mammoth Hot Springs

Hot-spring terraces and the park headquarters in the north of [[Yellowstone National Park]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ Bryson starts and ends his day with the park geologist Paul Doss here.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8158kzd00csfz1yvaxc8377)

> when we met at the park headquarters at Mammoth Hot Springs early on a lovely morning in June.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bryson meets Paul Doss, the park geologist, at the park headquarters at Mammoth Hot Springs early one June morning. From here they drive south through the park toward Old Faithful, with Doss pointing out sulphurous springs where "you can see rocks as they are being born", and they return here at the end of the day.
- **Why it matters:** It is the base for the book's only first-hand tour of the supervolcano; the day's itinerary hangs on it, though the springs themselves get little attention.
