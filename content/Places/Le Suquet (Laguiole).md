# Le Suquet

Sébastien Bras's restaurant on the hill above Laguiole, in the Aubrac of southern [[France]]. In _How to Know a Person_ it is where a chef gave back three Michelin stars to get his creativity back.

## How to Know a Person — David Brooks (2023)
Source: [[How to Know a Person]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/652822/how-to-know-a-person-by-david-brooks/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8gxr8b2qsqhv41dndvavzgq)

> Sébastien Bras is the owner of Le Suquet, a restaurant in Laguiole, France, that earned three Michelin stars, the world’s highest culinary distinction, for eighteen consecutive years. Then one year he asked the Michelin folks to stop coming to his restaurant and never come back again.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In the "Life Tasks" chapter, Brooks uses Bras as an example of people breaking out of the career-consolidation stage. The text says Bras realised that his wish to please the Michelin system "had imposed tremendous pressure, crushing his creativity", so he asked the inspectors to stay away.
- **Why it matters:** A short illustration, but a concrete one: the restaurant is where outside approval stopped being the goal. Any other top restaurant could have served, so the place matters less than the decision made there.
