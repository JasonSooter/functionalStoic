## Water Worlds — PBS (2022)
Source: [Original](https://www.pbs.org/video/water-worlds-jqbzx5/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2zke2zyp7pw23h1p9c710)

> This is the largest inland water world on Earth, the Pantanal.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Water lettuce, water hyacinth and Ludwigia race for surface space until the giant water lily, with about 40 leaves over 6 feet across, dominates. The team spends over a year filming this battle.
- **Why it matters:** The central story of the episode; the segment is the longest sustained sequence and the closing behind-the-scenes section returns to it.
