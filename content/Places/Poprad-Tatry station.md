> Welcome to Poprad-Tatry! This is where you need to get off if you want to connect with the Tatra Electric Railway

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the video:** After a night train from Berlin and four hours on the Tatran from Bratislava, the presenter changes here to the metre-gauge Tatra Electric Railway, the third and last train of the trip.
- **Why it matters:** The hinge of the journey, from crossing countries to climbing into the mountains.
