## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs2x4t6hgd3pk2cc42r9qw)

> Not having anticipated the difficulty of the terrain, my progress toward Reeds Peak ran three hours overtime, and I didn't arrive at a good point of attack for the summit until early afternoon.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After his final food pickup at Beaverhead Ranger Station, Eric crosses the Black Range, follows a buck through the woods and climbs Reeds Peak. From an abandoned fire tower he looks west toward the Gila Cliff Dwellings and south to flatter, bleaker land.
- **Why it matters:** The last mountain: from here the trek is all downhill to the border.
