# Marcos Mausoleum (Batac)

Mausoleum in Batac, Ilocos Norte, where Ferdinand Marcos's embalmed body lay on display. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne visits it and finds it eerily theatrical.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76rhzryksyxw56zrd9xfms5)

> I continue by bus to Batac, a small town where Marcos lies in state (supposedly it is his real body) in a refrigerated glass casket that sinks into the floor when no one is around.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Byrne takes the bus to Batac, where Marcos lies in a refrigerated glass casket said to sink into the floor when no one is around. The air-conditioned chamber has piped-in liturgical Mozart, staffs topped with Masonic-looking symbols and an eerie blue light; photos are banned, the body looks like a waxwork, and rumour says the real one lies deeper below.
- **Why it matters:** It is the most uncanny scene of the Manila chapter and the physical end point of the Marcos myth Byrne is tracing.
