# Wrigley Field

Ballpark of the Chicago Cubs on Chicago's North Side. In _The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid_ the young Bryson sits in its clubhouse handing baseballs to Ernie Banks.

## The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid — Bill Bryson (2006)

Source: [[The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4620956W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5khv2tk80km8cf4tfc8b46k)

> Once on a hot July afternoon I sat in a nearly airless clubhouse under the left-field grandstand at Wrigley Field beside Ernie Banks, the Cubs’ great shortstop, as he autographed boxes of new white baseballs

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On baseball trips with his sportswriter father, the young Bryson sits in the clubhouse under the left-field stand beside Ernie Banks, passing him new balls to autograph; Banks thanks him each time.
- **Why it matters:** It shows the easy access a sportswriter's son had in the 1950s, a world the memoir misses.
