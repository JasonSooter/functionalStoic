# Fleet Street

Historic street in the City of London, long the home of British newspapers. In _Brave New World_ it holds Propaganda House, Helmholtz Watson's workplace.

## Brave New World — Aldous Huxley (1932)
Source: [[Brave New World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL64365W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvae7prjphranvbqatyms4)

> The various Bureaux of Propaganda and the College of Emotional Engineering were housed in a single sixty-story building in Fleet Street.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bernard flies south toward the river and lands on the roof of Propaganda House in Fleet Street to fetch Helmholtz Watson. The text describes the single sixty-storey building that houses the papers, the Bureaux of Propaganda and the College of Emotional Engineering, where Helmholtz lectures, before the two men leave to talk in Bernard's rooms.
- **Why it matters:** Putting the World State's media machine on Fleet Street keeps the old newspaper street's function and turns it into propaganda. It introduces Helmholtz, Bernard's only real friend.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Fleet Street is real; Propaganda House is fictional.
