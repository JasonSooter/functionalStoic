# Lough Derg (Shannon)

The largest lake on the River Shannon, bordered by Clare, Galway and Tipperary. In _The Country Girls Trilogy_ it is the 'great Shannon lake' where Caithleen's mother drowns.

## The Country Girls Trilogy — Edna O'Brien (1986)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL69252W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75paf3px42tddevfg5x00)

> they had dragged the great Shannon lake but they hadn’t found them

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Mama sets out with Tom O'Brien in his boat ('That bloody boat was rotten,' says Hickey) and both are lost; the guards search the river and drag the lake, but the bodies are never found. Caithleen realises she will have no grave to put flowers on. The lake recurs in Mr. Gentleman's Lough Derg trout and in Aunt Molly's house on the Shannon island, and Kate later recalls her mother rowing across at night to a ball.
- **Why it matters:** The drowning is the trilogy's founding loss, and Kate's search for love is shaped by it to the end. That it happens on the water the whole district lives beside makes it feel both ordinary and fated.
