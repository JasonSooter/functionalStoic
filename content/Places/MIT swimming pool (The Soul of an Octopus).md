## The Soul of an Octopus — Sy Montgomery (2015)

Source: [[The Soul of an Octopus]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL27182716M/The_Soul_of_an_Octopus) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fzckvrfzqd32t6ty8z6xts)

> Here in the MIT swimming pool, I am practically delirious, reveling in my amphibious transformation.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** At Scott's suggestion Montgomery takes an intensive scuba course in the pool. Water floods her regulator, her ears hurt badly, and she fails the first half of the course, which drives her to try again elsewhere.
- **Why it matters:** Her failed start makes the later dives with octopuses in the wild hard-won.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The text names only 'the MIT swimming pool'. MIT's main pool is in the Zesiger Sports and Fitness Center, so the pin is there; the older Alumni Pool is the alternative.
