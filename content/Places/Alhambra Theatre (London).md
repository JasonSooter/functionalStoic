# Alhambra Theatre (London)

Music hall on the east side of Leicester Square, London, demolished in 1936. Three Men in a Boat ends with the three friends turning up there in filthy boating flannels.

## Three Men in a Boat — Jerome K. Jerome (1889)

Source: [[Exploration of Three Men in a Boat]] · [[Three Men in a Boat]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1793164W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gkzh33yg60zb7agvrz517v)

> We attracted a good deal of attention at the Alhambra.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After abandoning the boat at Pangbourne and taking the train to Paddington, the three go to the Alhambra in their dirty boating flannels and attract a good deal of attention; they are mistaken for performers and then go on to dine at a restaurant.
- **Why it matters:** The comic final scene: the escape from the river ends in a London music hall, which gives the book its last laugh.
