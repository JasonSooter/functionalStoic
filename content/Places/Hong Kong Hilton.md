## The Rise and Fall of the Hotel Mini-Bar — Priceonomics (2015)

Source: [Original](http://priceonomics.com/the-rise-and-fall-of-the-hotel-mini-bar/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fzbsrc28hdf9tr686na168)

> the Hong Kong Hilton became the first hotel to include a “liquor-stocked” mini-bar in each of its 840 rooms

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** In 1974 the hotel put a liquor-stocked mini-bar in each of its 840 rooms; in-room drink sales jumped 500% and overall revenue rose 5%, and Hilton spread the idea worldwide.
- **Why it matters:** It is where the article's story of the mini-bar begins; the boom it started is what the rest of the piece follows into decline.
