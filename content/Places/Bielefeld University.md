# Bielefeld University

University founded in the late 1960s in [[Bielefeld]], North Rhine-Westphalia, where Niklas Luhmann became a professor in 1968, as told in Sönke Ahrens's How to Take Smart Notes.

## How to Take Smart Notes — Sönke Ahrens (2017)

Source: [[How to Take Smart Notes]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL18635700W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5brdws4e8b73zr54hefe7vq)

> Shortly after, in 1968, he was chosen to become professor of sociology at the University of Bielefeld – a position he would hold for the rest of his life.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In the introduction, Ahrens tells how Luhmann, a civil servant without sociology credentials, is invited to a chair at the newly founded university, completes his doctorate and habilitation from his slip-box in under a year, and takes up the professorship in 1968.
- **Why it matters:** This is where the slip-box stops being a private habit and becomes a career: the book's origin story of the method turns on the Bielefeld appointment. Any university would do for the argument, but this is the real one.
