# Mashhad

Shrine city in northeast Iran. In _Full Tilt_ (as 'Meshed') Dervla Murphy collects mail but is barred from the shrine quarter.

## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6ktee29vftb6szr2tx09t)

> A car was kindly laid on to take me round the city yet it was most frustrating just to glimpse the out-of-bounds beauties of the mosques and shrine and museum and library.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** She detours to collect mail at the British Council, cannot enter the shrine quarter, has Roz hammered at a cycle shop, and rides back at night scared by men in a car.
- **Why it matters:** Last Persian city and a symbol of forbidden holy sights.
