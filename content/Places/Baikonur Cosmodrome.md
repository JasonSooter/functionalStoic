## Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir (2021)

Source: [[Project Hail Mary]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/611060/project-hail-mary-by-andy-weir/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jx9rtgpv76f81j83gf0tqd)

> The Russians had their hands full erecting dozens of temporary shelters a few miles from Baikonur Cosmodrome.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In a flashback nine days before launch, Grace works as an administrator in a portable mobile home a few miles from Baikonur, looking out over the flat Kazakhstani steppe. An explosion destroys the nearby research center, five kilometers away, shaking his trailer; DuBois, Shapiro and fourteen staff are killed by what is evidently an Astrophage accident. Stratt locks down the base, the leaders are moved into Cold War-era bunkers, and Grace is told to find a replacement science specialist. Grace arrives to find the whole Hail Mary team seated and silent. Stratt explains the research-center explosion (a milligram of Astrophage supplied instead of a nanogram, vaporizing the building and DuBois and Shapiro), that the launch cannot be delayed, and that Grace is the only coma-resistant candidate. All of Baikonur is on lockdown to keep the deaths secret. At five p.m. Grace tells Stratt no, with a Russian guard, Private Meknikov, in the corner. Stratt calls him a coward, has Meknikov seize him, and reveals the plan to hold him in a cell, sedate him into the Soyuz, and use the French DGSE amnesia drug so he wakes at Tau Ceti remembering nothing. On launch day Grace sits in a dorm-like cell with a steel door and barred windows, waiting for guards and a doctor to sedate him. Stratt visits, lectures him on how history was always about food, predicts famine, wars and plague if Astrophage wins, and tells him why she did it.
- **Why it matters:** It is the launch site of the Hail Mary and the setting for the disaster that kills the original science crew and eventually puts Grace on the mission. The same passage also supplies the steppe backdrop.
