## \[RODEN] Burnout? a TV Show, FM Radio, a Big Walk, and More — Craig Mod (2024)

Source: [Original](https://craigmod.com/roden/092/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbgxzkpfqfvc58z2m80vp8)

> I made it from Kyoto to Tokyo, walking at a near Edo-era pace, finishing the walk in seventeen days with one day of rest.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Straight after the TV shoot Mod went to Kyoto and began the Tōkaidō walk, with first days of 43 and 44 km. He finished Kyoto to Tokyo in seventeen days, writing every night for a pop-up newsletter.
- **Why it matters:** The walk is the counterweight to the TV shoot: hard, anonymous, physical work. He sets it against media fame as the real work.
