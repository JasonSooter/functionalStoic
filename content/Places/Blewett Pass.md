# Blewett Pass

Mountain pass in the Cascades of central [[Washington (state)|Washington]], between Cle Elum and Wenatchee. In _The Boys in the Boat_ Joe Rantz's old Franklin crawls over it as he leaves Seattle in 1935 to look for work at the Grand Coulee.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m86yeqw5w4jpvwstd14s8a5d)

> Joe’s old Franklin labored and coughed and wheezed, crawling up the long, steep ascent to Blewett Pass, high in the Cascade Mountains.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Having said goodbye to Joyce for the summer, Joe drives east over the pass, down through dry ponderosa forest to the apple and cherry orchards of Wenatchee, across the Columbia and over the wheat fields of the plateau into the scablands, thinking about money, crew and Berlin as he goes.
- **Why it matters:** The crossing of the Cascades marks Joe's move from the university world back into manual labor; it is a waypoint rather than a scene, but it opens the Grand Coulee chapter.
