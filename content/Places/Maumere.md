Town on the north coast of Flores, Indonesia; in _Life on Air_ the Zoo Quest team is stranded there on the way to Komodo.

## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2pe4b0rb9ph55mv3mt8g)

> As its wheels touched Maumere's grassy strip, water spurted from the turf on either side.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** The team lands on a waterlogged grass strip, and the pilot warns them that planes will stop landing. A Chinese trader tells them the road to west Flores is broken, and they find only one prau in the harbour. Its captain, a gun-runner, takes them to Komodo.
- **Why it matters:** A marooning point: it sets up the sea voyage and the hazards that follow.
