# Sukadana

Port on the south-west coast of Borneo, Indonesia. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ Nathaniel Courthope runs the English diamond factory here from 1614.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8efd36frsqrn1k7hkd9h009)

> Sukadana was already home to one of the Company’s more flamboyant factors, a professional sailor called Sophony Cozucke.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In spring 1614 Courthope is sent here, where it was rumoured "the best diamonds in the world [are] to be procured". With "Sophony the Russe" he turns the factory into "a going concern, buying gemstones on the cheap and exporting them to Bantam", but they are so short of money they must "refuse 1,000 carats of diamonds". When Courthope leaves in 1616, he goes to command the voyage to Run.
- **Why it matters:** It is Courthope's apprenticeship as a factor, just before the mission that makes him the book's hero.
