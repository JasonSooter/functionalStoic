## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjqkxf3ayr35p1z66v2ffr)

> He walked by the Main river. The height of the skyline surprised him.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Arriving in Germany, Rami is anxious in the hotel and then sleeps well. He walks by the Main, meets a journalist on the Goethestrasse, and addresses a crowd of two hundred.
- **Why it matters:** The first German stop, where Rami's dread of the country is replaced by surprise at its brightness; it frames the tour and the interviews that follow.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Inferred. The book never names the city on the German tour's first stop, but its clues fit Frankfurt am Main: Rami “walked by the Main river”, is surprised by “the height of the skyline”, and there is “a law firm in Innenstadt” and “a restaurant on the Goethestrasse”. No reference work names the city. Alternatives: another city on the Main such as Offenbach or Würzburg, which fit the skyline clue far worse.
