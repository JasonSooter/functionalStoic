# Bauhaus Dessau

The Bauhaus school building in Dessau, Saxony-Anhalt, home of the school from 1925 to 1932. In _Germany: Memories of a Nation_ it marks the Bauhaus's politically troubled middle years.

## Germany: Memories of a Nation — Neil MacGregor (2014)
Source: [[Germany - Memories of a Nation]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20201162W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m76dbkyv4ce7ns0yxy05j8)

> But the real crisis came when the Nazis won control of Dessau and forced the school to move yet again, this time to Berlin, where in 1933 it closed, condemned by the Nazis as a centre of cultural Bolshevism.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Chapter 20 follows the school to Dessau in 1925, where the politics grew sharper: Gropius resigned in 1928, the Communist Hannes Meyer was fired in 1930 and Mies van der Rohe took over. When the Nazis won control of the town, the school was forced to move again, to Berlin, where it closed in 1933. The text places Dessau 'in Saxony'.
- **Why it matters:** A waypoint in the story of how the Bauhaus was squeezed out by politics; East Germany later claimed Meyer's Dessau tradition.
