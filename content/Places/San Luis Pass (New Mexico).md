# San Luis Pass

Pass between the Animas and San Luis mountains near the Mexican border in New Mexico's Bootheel. The end of The Ultimate Journey.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs2zwp7abg7qxc27cxvvte)

> Then, the second night, I camped at the base of the San Luis Pass, near a water hole which abounded in desert plants.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Eric camps at the base of the pass, then on October 23 walks the last miles to the boundary fence, where his father and Ranger Lou Armijo wait. The book ends with him speaking to his absent brother: "We did it, Tim."
- **Why it matters:** The goal of the whole trek; the border fence itself is not named in the text, and the pass is the last named place.
