## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)

Source: [[Neither Here Nor There]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74125W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce7vs0068djhp6082m3kpz)

> Finding nothing to detain me there, I went straight to the Union Bank of Switzerland offices on the Rue du Rhône to claim my refund on my Visa travelers’ checks. I was directed to a small room in the basement, where international transactions were dealt with.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** In Geneva Bryson goes straight to the Union Bank of Switzerland on the Rue du Rhône to claim a refund for his stolen travellers' cheques, and is sent to a small basement room for international transactions, where it takes most of the afternoon.
- **Why it matters:** The end of the stolen-cheques thread; minor.
