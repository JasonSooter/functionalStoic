# Rantz farm (Silberhorn Road)

The Rantz family's stump farm on Silberhorn Road outside [[Sequim]], Washington. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is where Joe's father and stepmother drive away in 1929 and leave him behind at fifteen.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m869y0m1bhhfmp3zk14wjbre)

> Over the next few weeks, things continued to unravel at the Rantz house on Silberhorn Road.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Harry builds the farmhouse among the tree stumps outside town, a work forever in progress, with Joe's help: water diverted from an irrigation canal off the [[Dungeness River]], a water-powered sawmill and a fireplace of river rocks. After the 1929 crash, wild dogs left behind by departing families chase the cows and minks raid the chickens. In November, on a rainy night, Harry and Thula pack the younger children into the car and drive away; Joe watches the taillights vanish and is left alone in the cold house.
- **Why it matters:** This is the book's central wound: Joe's second abandonment, after which he learns to rely only on himself, the trust he will have to relearn in the boat.
