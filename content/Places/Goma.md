## Your Phone Was Made By Slaves: A Primer on the Secret Economy — Kevin Bales (2016)

Source: [Original](http://blog.longreads.com/2016/03/08/your-phone-was-made-by-slaves-a-primer-on-the-secret-economy/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7tctmrd1rhj0wner268zv)

> A few days before flying to Walikale we arrived at the border city of Goma, a good place to start if you want to understand what is happening in Eastern Congo.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** Goma is the aid-dependent border city and UN hub for the Kivus, filled with NGO offices and SUVs. Bales describes the looming Nyiragongo volcano, the 2002 lava flow that destroyed part of the city, and the refugee camps built on the lava fields after the 1994 Rwandan genocide.
- **Why it matters:** The city carries the region's history of volcano, genocide and war that explains the later slavery in the mines.
