# Soviet Embassy in Washington

The Soviet Union's embassy on Sixteenth Street in [[Washington DC|Washington, D.C.]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is where Aldrich Ames offers his services to the KGB and is accepted.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7vbqq7f6ra25z6tzzh83gm3)

> On Wednesday, May 15, Ames reappeared, as bidden, at the Soviet embassy, having informed the CIA and FBI he was following up his earlier efforts to cultivate the military specialist.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In April 1985 Ames hands the receptionist a parcel for the KGB _rezident_, naming himself as a CIA counterintelligence branch chief and asking $50,000. On May 15 he returns; Chuvakhin introduces him to the KGB officer Cherkashin, who leads him to a small basement room and, without a word, hands him a note accepting the offer. The book argues that at some point in these first contacts Ames told the Russians about a senior Soviet agent run by the British.
- **Why it matters:** This is where the threat to Gordievsky begins: the book calls what followed one of the biggest manhunts in KGB history. ⚠️ The pin is the period embassy building at 1125 16th Street NW; the book gives only the street.
