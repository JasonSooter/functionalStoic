# Pedion tou Areos

Large public park in central Athens, Greece. In _The Beekeeper of Aleppo_ Nuri and Afra camp there for weeks among refugees, drug dealers and predators.

## The Beekeeper of Aleppo: A Moving Testament to the Human Spirit — Christy Lefteri (2019)
Source: [[The Beekeeper of Aleppo - A Moving Testament to the Human Spirit]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20112913W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kgrfjze8fmrt9x384kb28d)

> In the morning there was prayer and later Pedion tou Areos was like a playground.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** An aid worker leaves Nuri and Afra on blankets between two palm trees opposite a statue in the park, where they live for some two weeks among tents and campfires. Nuri befriends the musician Nadim and the Somali woman Angeliki, follows Nadim at night to a clearing where men pay for the boys, and after Nadim's death is drawn into a killing in the woods. Nadim tells him that the park is named for the god of war.
- **Why it matters:** The park is where Nuri crosses a moral line, and from which he resolves to find a smuggler to England.
