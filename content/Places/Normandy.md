## The Daily Stoic — Ryan Holiday and Stephen Hanselman (2016)

Source: [[The Daily Stoic]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20046443W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f0qehk8h5jmzqnq1ytbsmb)

> When General Dwight D. Eisenhower wrote to his wife on the eve of the invasion of Normandy, he told her, “Everything we could think of has been done, the troops are fit everybody is doing his best. The answer is in the lap of the gods.”

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In the November 2 entry, Eisenhower writes to his wife on the eve of the Normandy invasion that everything has been done and the answer is 'in the lap of the gods'. The text adds that he drafted a second letter in case the invasion failed, illustrating Epictetus's idea of wishing for what will be.
- **Why it matters:** The invasion is the high-stakes test that makes the Stoic point vivid. The pin is the region's point, not the landing beaches (approximate).
