> OpenAI began one evening last summer in a private room at Silicon Valley's Rosewood Hotel---an upscale, urban, ranch-style hotel that sits, literally, at the center of the venture capital world along Sand Hill Road in Menlo Park, California.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The article says OpenAI began at a dinner in a private room at the Rosewood Hotel. Elon Musk dined with Ilya Sutskever, and Sam Altman had brokered the meeting with several AI researchers and Greg Brockman.
- **Why it matters:** Origin point of the lab in the narrative; the article stresses its position at the centre of the venture capital world on Sand Hill Road.
