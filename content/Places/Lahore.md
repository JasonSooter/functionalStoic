## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6nbxyepa5bk0r99500kzm)

> my first impression was of a depressing dump of a city, all stinks and stenches and not to be compared with either Peshawar or Pindi.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** She arrives by third-class train in intolerable heat and stays in Farhat's rooms in the Lady Willingdon Hospital beside the red-light district. She collects mail, notes the London 6,372 miles signpost, and watches Farhat perform a Caesarean. Farhat pronounces her fit to cycle again with strict conditions.
- **Why it matters:** Staging point for the final crossing into India; recovery from illness.
