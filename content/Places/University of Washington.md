## Science says lasting relationships come down to 2 basic traits — Emily Esfahani Smith (2015)
Source: [Original](http://www.businessinsider.com/lasting-relationships-rely-on-traits-2015-11) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy7b0nxkfkhxqv6bwwa9ye)

> In a follow-up study in 1990, he designed a lab on the University of Washington campus to look like a beautiful bed and breakfast retreat.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Gottman built a lab on the University of Washington campus dressed as a bed and breakfast and invited 130 newlywed couples to spend the day there cooking, cleaning, eating and chatting. Observing them there led to his discovery of 'bids' for connection and whether partners turn toward or away.
- **Why it matters:** The designed setting is the venue of the study's key finding; the place is incidental to the argument but is a concrete scene. The original 1986 Love Lab at the same university is mentioned only in passing.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m869xfa87dsd92rw034ahpnt)

> On the campus of the University of Washington, perched on a bluff overlooking the lake, slanting rays of sunlight began to warm the shoulders of students lounging on a wide quadrangle of grass in front of the university’s massive new stone library, eating their lunches, poring over books, chatting idly.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On the October day in 1933 when crew sign-ups open, students lounge on the quadrangle before the new library while Joe Rantz, in his worn clothes, crosses the campus with Roger Morris, past Frosh Pond and down to the [[ASUW Shell House]]. Joe works his way through as an engineering student, and the book returns to the campus as the place he could lose if he fails to make the crew.
- **Why it matters:** The prosperous campus is the world Joe is trying to hold on to; the contrast with his poverty gives the crew tryouts their stakes.
