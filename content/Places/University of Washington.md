## Science says lasting relationships come down to 2 basic traits — Emily Esfahani Smith (2015)
Source: [Original](http://www.businessinsider.com/lasting-relationships-rely-on-traits-2015-11) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy7b0nxkfkhxqv6bwwa9ye)

> In a follow-up study in 1990, he designed a lab on the University of Washington campus to look like a beautiful bed and breakfast retreat.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Gottman built a lab on the University of Washington campus dressed as a bed and breakfast and invited 130 newlywed couples to spend the day there cooking, cleaning, eating and chatting. Observing them there led to his discovery of 'bids' for connection and whether partners turn toward or away.
- **Why it matters:** The designed setting is the venue of the study's key finding; the place is incidental to the argument but is a concrete scene. The original 1986 Love Lab at the same university is mentioned only in passing.
