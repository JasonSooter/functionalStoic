## It Takes A Village: When the Barber Also Pushes Literacy — Jennifer Guerra (2016)
Source: [Original](https://ww2.kqed.org/mindshift/2016/10/13/it-takes-a-village-when-the-barber-also-pushes-literacy/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy9n2wk9k8yg1fvjrthzep)

> At the Fuller Cut, kids get a $2 discount if they read a book aloud to their barber.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Kids at the Fuller Cut get $2 off a haircut for reading a book aloud to their barber, who quizzes them on comprehension. Seven-year-old Jozef reads from a stack of books in the waiting room while waiting for a mohawk. More than a hundred kids have read to the barbers in the past year.
- **Why it matters:** The whole story is set in and about this shop and how it works as a community literacy space in a working-class town.
