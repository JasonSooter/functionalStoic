# Pearl Island (Illinois)

Mississippi River island near Hannibal, Missouri, also called Glasscock Island. It is named as the model for Jackson's Island in Tom Sawyer.

## The Adventures of Tom Sawyer, Complete — Mark Twain (1876)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL53919W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7hc8q0wcqjw4eneg4qr0v)

> Three miles below St. Petersburg, at a point where the Mississippi River was a trifle over a mile wide, there was a long, narrow, wooded island, with a shallow bar at the head of it, and this offered well as a rendezvous.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Tom, Joe Harper and Huck row a stolen raft to the uninhabited island three miles below town and live as pirates: camping, fishing, swimming off the sandbar and riding out a violent thunderstorm, while the village believes them drowned.
- **Why it matters:** The island is the book's idyll of freedom, the place where the boys act out their romances, grow homesick and where Tom hatches the funeral-return plan.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Jackson's Island is fictional. English Wikipedia identifies Pearl (Glasscock) Island in the Mississippi near Hannibal as its model ⚠️ (the claim is flagged there as needing a citation).
