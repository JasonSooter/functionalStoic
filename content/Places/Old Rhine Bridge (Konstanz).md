Source: [[The Rhine River Explained in Under 3 Minutes]] · [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IpFTi7-T-TI) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce8j8mhwf186jj1hmcrxxe)

> in the city of Constance marks the 0 km datum

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the video:** Following the river from the Alps to the North Sea, the video says the Old Rhine Bridge in Konstanz marks kilometre 0 of the Rhine-kilometre scale, before the river cuts through limestone to the Rhine Falls.
- **Why it matters:** The datum point of the river's official length; a waypoint.
