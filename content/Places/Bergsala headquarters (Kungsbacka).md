# Bergsala headquarters (Kungsbacka)

Head office of Nintendo's Nordic distributor, south of Gothenburg in Kungsbacka, Sweden. An IGN feature tells how its founder won the Nintendo rights.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Bergsten's office is in a building at 'Marios Gata 21' south of Gothenburg, paid for by Nintendo money. Kids visit to see a Mario warp pipe outside, and the lobby holds a Nintendo museum or shrine. The author interviews Bergsten here.
- **Why it matters:** Interview setting and symbol of the empire built on the lie.
