# African Bible College Christian Academy

Private mission school in Lilongwe, Malawi's capital. In _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ William Kamkwamba studies here after TED, learning online from teachers in America.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8ebdebr4k546kwpkr2ts4tg)

> I was finally accepted at African Bible College Christian Academy (ABCCA) in Lilongwe, which was run by Presbyterian missionaries.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Other private schools turn him away for his age before ABCCA accepts him. His class of twelve follows "a distance-learning curriculum from America" with teachers in Colorado; he is the only local high-school student, since tuition is "five thousand dollars per year, which most Malawians can’t afford". Ashamed of his English, he becomes "a little depressed" until his tutor Blessings Chikakula, from a poor village near [[Dowa]], encourages him. He lodges with Gerry Douglas, where city power cuts leave him "sitting in the dark in my success".
- **Why it matters:** It is the first rung of the education the windmill bought him, and it prepares him for the [[African Leadership Academy]].
