# Coram's Fields

Park and children's playground in [[Bloomsbury]], London. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ Gordievsky leaves a KGB money drop there while MI5 watches.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7vbqr7xs8wqqpy7berhr5cp)

> Gordievsky was instructed to deposit the money brick, inside a plastic bag, on a verge between a path and a high fence on the north side of Coram’s Fields, a park in Bloomsbury.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On Saturday, May 18, 1985, Gordievsky takes his daughters to play in the park and at 7:45 p.m. drops the hollow brick holding cash for the illegal agent DARIO (Operation GROUND). The only people nearby are an MI5 surveillance expert with a camera in her pram and John Deverell, head of K Section, posing as a cyclist. A man scoops up the bag, is photographed, and vanishes into the Tube at King's Cross.
- **Why it matters:** Being trusted with the drop suggests to Gordievsky that Moscow still trusts him, days before he flies back to a trap. The family outing as cover makes the scene.
