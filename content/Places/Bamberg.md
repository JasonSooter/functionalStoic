# Bamberg

Old cathedral town in Upper Franconia, northern [[Bavaria]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where the wounded Frans lies in a chaotic military hospital and Wera finds him again.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85n9k1fpbz2z7rkg6b34fhq)

> Before long, my father was wounded in the leg and taken to a hospital in Bamberg.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After his storm-troop unit's suicide missions, Frans is wounded in the leg and sent to Bamberg; in 1943 Wera gets the telegram "Wounded, at hospital in Bamberg" and goes at once. She finds the hospital "in a state of total anarchy": Frans hails her with a sarcastic "Heil Hitlerchen" in front of everyone, and the ward, nurses and doctors burst out laughing beneath a portrait of Hitler. Declared fit after a few months, he goes straight to Posen.
- **Why it matters:** The hospital is the hinge between the front and the author's own conception in [[Poznań|Posen]], which he reads as the act of "a dead man walking"; the scene also shows the mockery the war has left in Frans.
