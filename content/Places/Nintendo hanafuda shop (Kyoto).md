## Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World — David Epstein (2019)

Source: [[Range]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550188/range-by-david-epstein/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jkzvwx5raetdgemw54m1g9)

> So it was in the fall of 1889 that a young man opened a tiny wooden shop in Kyoto and hung a sign in the window: “Nintendo.”

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 9 opens with the founding of Nintendo as a tiny hanafuda card shop in Kyoto. Later Yokoi, who did not want to leave Kyoto, is hired to repair card-making machines there, and the company is described as a small Kyoto operation that big-city graduates avoided.
- **Why it matters:** The card-making company is the base from which Yokoi's lateral thinking grows; he says he was lucky to join a card firm rather than an established electronics maker.
