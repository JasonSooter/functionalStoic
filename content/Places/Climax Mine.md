# Climax Mine

Molybdenum mine at Fremont Pass near Leadville, Colorado. Eric Ryback finds a mountain there cut in half.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs2j9p5cx76tysdfn7hhf8)

> At the base of its barren slopes, small buildings came into view, along with the sign "Climax Molybdenum Mine."

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Seeing a mountain that looks "cut in half as if it were a hard-boiled egg", Eric leaves the Divide to look, finds the mine, and sets it beside the Anaconda smelter and Lake Isabelle, though he grants the Climax people some credit.
- **Why it matters:** Part of the book's running catalogue of industrial damage along the Divide.
