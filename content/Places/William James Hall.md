## Quiet — Susan Cain (2012)

Source: [[Quiet]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/55983/quiet-by-cain-susan/9780141970172) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g172vs1agwj25j0pw7cpr1)

> Kagan ushers me inside his office in Harvard’s William James Hall, surveying me unblinkingly as I sit down: not unkind, but definitely discerning.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Cain interviews Kagan in his office; he rejects her premise and corrects her single-cause view of temperament, nature and nurture.
- **Why it matters:** The interview anchors her chapter on whether temperament is destiny.
