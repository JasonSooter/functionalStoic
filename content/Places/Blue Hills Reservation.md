## The Comfort Crisis — Michael Easter (2021)

Source: [[The Comfort Crisis]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/634446/the-comfort-crisis-by-michael-easter/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fzd9k2mhf38sqryxzf3sa6)

> The next day I met her at Blue Hills Reservation, a 7,000-acre state park south of Boston.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The day after the arboretum, Easter and Hopman hike a rocky trail to a summit with a view of the Atlantic, an example of 'country nature' worth about five hours a month.
- **Why it matters:** The step up from city park to wilder nature in the book's argument.
