## The shadow of the windmill cult — Helen Russell (2025)
Source: [Original](https://mshelenrussell.substack.com/p/the-shadow-of-the-windmill-cult) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5eddzcebpss34gm365rehzb)

> with one of Tvind’s most legendary achievements being its windmill, built between 1975 and 1978.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The article traces Tvind, the alternative school movement founded in Jutland by Mogens Amdi Petersen. Its windmill, Tvindkraft, was then the world's largest modern windmill and a milestone in renewable energy before the movement was exposed for control and fraud.
- **Why it matters:** The windmill is the piece's emblem: the idealistic achievement that frames the cult-like shadow of the title. The text gives no precise town.
