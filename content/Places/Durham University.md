# Durham University

University in Durham, north-east England. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ it is where Arthur Holmes is, for years, the entire geology department.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m80txx72z18bxd8y5arp2q9r)

> At Durham University, Holmes was for many years the entire geology department.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Arthur Holmes, pioneer of radiometric dating, is for many years the entire geology department at Durham. He has to borrow or patch together equipment and waits a year for an adding machine.
- **Why it matters:** It shows how poorly funded the effort to date the Earth was. A short portrait; the campus itself isn't described.
