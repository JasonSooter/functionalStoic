## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bek1f9fxqwq1p0zesqad3h)

> Along Getreidegasse, the site of Mozart’s birthplace

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Chapter 19. Every shop has a hanging filigree sign over its door, "including, God help us, the local McDonald's", with the golden-arches M worked into the ironwork, as if burgers had been sold there since the Middle Ages. "I sank to my knees and beat my poor old head on the cobbled pavement."
- **Why it matters:** A comic set-piece on the souvenir-shop Salzburg that Bryson finds in place of the city he remembered. It sits with the [[Mozartplatz]] as the two scenes that sour him on the old town.
