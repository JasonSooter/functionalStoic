# Manhattan Municipal Building

Civic skyscraper at 1 Centre Street in Lower [[Manhattan]], home of the city's marriage bureau for decades. In Aoibheann Sweeney's novel it is the backdrop of the wedding photo of Miranda's parents.

## Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking — Aoibheann Sweeney (2007)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/among-other-things-ive-taken-up-smoking-9781101202227)

> He had a picture in his bedroom of the day they got married, standing in front of the Municipal Building in New York.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Thinking about whether her father ever loved her mother, Miranda describes the photograph in his bedroom of their wedding day in front of the Municipal Building: a businessman entering the frame behind them, her father "like a paper doll in his tuxedo", her mother holding his stiff arm in a winter coat over her pale dress. She later sees what the coat may have hidden.
- **Why it matters:** A remembered image, not a scene she visits, but it carries the novel's buried family history; any city hall would do for the wedding, yet this one ties her parents' story to the New York she is now discovering.
