## I’m in Wyoming to celebrate the next nuclear breakthrough – Bill Gates — Bill Gates (2023)

Source: [Original](https://www.gatesnotes.com/Wyoming-TerraPower) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbgkwqpqz2v4k35ae7svqz)

> Today I’m in the town of Kemmerer, Wyoming, to celebrate the latest step in a project that’s been more than 15 years in the making: designing and building a next-generation nuclear power plant.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Gates is in Kemmerer to mark the next step toward the Natrium reactor plant designed by TerraPower. The town's coal plant, operating for over 50 years, is due to shut; the new plant is meant to employ 200-250 people, absorb the roughly 110 coal workers, and bring up to 1,600 construction jobs.
- **Why it matters:** The site was chosen partly because of the coal-plant transition, so the town's economy is central to the argument. The article names the town but not the plant site's exact location.

## We just broke ground on America’s first next-gen nuclear facility – Bill Gates — Bill Gates (2024)

Source: [Original](https://www.gatesnotes.com/Wyoming-TerraPower-groundbreaking) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbhayvs92v99958rjmz0wt)

> Kemmerer, Wyoming will soon be home to the most advanced nuclear facility in the world.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Gates writes from Kemmerer just after the groundbreaking for TerraPower's first Natrium plant. He describes the restored downtown (an old opera house now a mercantile and bakery), new businesses, and coal plant workers who will see their future job site across the highway. Kemmerer and neighbouring Diamondville are credited with embracing the project.
- **Why it matters:** The whole article is about this town as the site of the plant and its economic revival; elsewhere the story would be a different project.
