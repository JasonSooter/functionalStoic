## Stranger in a Strange Land — Robert A. Heinlein (1961)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL59688W)

> Mike's Temple is a big place, almost a labyrinth.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Ben Caxton visits the Temple: a labyrinth with a public auditorium for outer services, smaller rooms for the inner circles and the private Nest above, with bowls of free money by the street door. He flees down a faulty bounce tube; later the Temple is bombed and burned, the Nest escapes, and Mike is arrested and breaks out of jail.
- **Why it matters:** It is the home of Mike's church and the setting of most of the last part of the book.
- **Real vs. fictional:** No highlight: Reader could not anchor any span in this book.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Clues: 'a lush subtropical resort city in the southern part of the same nation'; palm trees and the ocean beyond them; Jubal flies down from the snowy Poconos 'sweltering in winter clothes'; a city university; gambling 'against the law here'. After the fire the Nest hides in a beach hotel 'here in beautiful St. Petersburg'. No reference work places the Temple. Chosen: St. Petersburg, Florida (same city as the refuge); alternatives: Miami or another Florida resort city.
