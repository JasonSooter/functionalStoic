# Verrazzano-Narrows Bridge

Suspension bridge between Brooklyn and Staten Island in [[New York City]]. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne rides across it, car-free, at the end of the Five Boro Bike Tour.

## At a glance
| Source | Author | Role | In one line |

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m772x5pq0x3dp6b0t3c6wsyn)

> The organizers have closed part of the FDR Drive, the BQE, the Belt Parkway, and the Verrazano Bridge—so we participants get the thrill of riding in the middle of a highway, and of not having to stop at red lights.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On the 42-mile Five Boro Bike Tour nobody races; residents cheer from their yards, there is a mandatory banana stop near the Brooklyn end of the bridge, and the long route through the Brooklyn and Queens waterfront passes small family-run factories. The tour crosses the Verrazano to Staten Island.
- **Why it matters:** The car-free bridge and highways give the New York chapter its picture of a shared, civic city and of the old small-scale industrial New York that still survives.
