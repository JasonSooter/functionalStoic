## Beyond Data: Building Empathy in Adults Through Student Shadow Days — Katrina Schwartz (2016)

Source: [Original](https://ww2.kqed.org/mindshift/2016/03/22/beyond-data-building-empathy-in-adults-with-student-shadow-days/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7v2v5jytgvsfqxtnz3g7d)

> That’s what Principal Lena Van Haren has done at Everett Middle School in San Francisco's Mission District.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Principal Lena Van Haren makes student shadowing a regular part of Everett's culture, using it to dig into survey data such as low sense of belonging among Asian and Pacific Islander students. Educators fit shadowing into parts of their schedules and use it to focus instructional goals.
- **Why it matters:** It serves as the contrasting example of shadowing made routine rather than a one-off challenge.
