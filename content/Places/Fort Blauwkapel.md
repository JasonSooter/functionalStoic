# Fort Blauwkapel

Old fortress on the north-eastern edge of Utrecht, in the [[Netherlands]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it houses Special Counterintelligence, the base of Sweerts, the agent the Old Boss uses to get rid of his son.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85n9ydq43skj1tt0rydzdad)

> He became the head of Special Counterintelligence, which was housed in Blauwkapel, an old fortress, in Utrecht.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Sweerts, once interrogated and hired by MI6 as a Netherlands specialist, heads Special Counterintelligence from the Blauwkapel fortress and becomes the Old Boss's fixer. When Frans leaves [[Scheveningen prison]] in a Special Counterintelligence car, Sweerts takes him to his Utrecht apartment, feeds him jenever, whisky and rest, then sets out the plan: Argentina, by way of Paraguay.
- **Why it matters:** Minor as a setting, but it shows how close the Old Boss's private plans ran to the state's own intelligence service.
