# Ballerup

Suburb at the end of a rail line west of [[Copenhagen]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ a bugged safe flat there is where Gordievsky meets MI6 every month.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7p6zs5t06t0scvre2h5515e)

> So began a series of monthly meetings, in a one-bedroom flat on the third floor of a nondescript apartment block in Ballerup.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Hawkins picks Ballerup because it is far from the Soviet embassy and easy to reach. Danish and British intelligence share the rent, and PET technicians posing as electricians hide microphones in the lights and sockets. Gordievsky and Hawkins talk for hours over tea; he later works out that the flat is bugged. One winter evening a young PET officer spots a Soviet embassy car in a side street, follows footprints to the flat and reports it, and the report has to be quietly buried; from then on Gordievsky comes by underground.
- **Why it matters:** It is the working heart of the case, where Gordievsky turns from hesitant contact into a full source. The near miss shows how much its secrecy depended on a few people inside PET.
