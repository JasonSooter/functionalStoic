# Lakewood Towne Center

Town centre built on the site of the Villa Italia mall in Lakewood, southwest of [[Denver]], Colorado, USA. In _Happy City_ it is Charles Montgomery's example of a dying mall repaired into a real downtown.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m84c8gqzcjntfb7qj4k5tkcp)

> One of the most striking retrofits is growing on the former site of a vast mall surrounded by parking on 104 acres in Lakewood, southwest of Denver.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Chapter 12. Villa Italia lost its customers to bigger malls on the periphery. Instead of a big-box power centre, the city and a developer cut the superblock into twenty-three smaller blocks tied into the surrounding street network, wrapped parking structures in small shops, and anchored the site with a town green and a central plaza. More than fifteen hundred people live there in town houses, lofts and apartments above stores.
- **Why it matters:** Montgomery holds it up as genuine sprawl repair, unlike faux main streets, because its public space is truly public.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real. The book calls it Lakewood Towne Center; the site is now generally known as Belmar.
