## Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World — David Epstein (2019)
Source: [[Range]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550188/range-by-david-epstein/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjsnb7c0t9k0vpd2k3qrbf)

> by thirty-one was so adept that he was chosen as the soloist to play a concerto by none other than Vivaldi accompanied by an orchestra for a crowd in Chicago’s Grant Park.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** At thirty-one Jack Cecchini, who had taught himself guitar in Chicago clubs, soloed in a Vivaldi concerto in Grant Park. The Chicago Tribune critic's review the next day praised him as one of the few who master the classical guitar.
- **Why it matters:** It marks the proof of Cecchini's late, self-taught route to classical world-class status; it is a minor setting rather than a place examined at length.
