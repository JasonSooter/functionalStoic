# Brunssummerheide

Heath and forest nature reserve in South Limburg, close to the German border in the [[Netherlands|Netherlands]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is the last stretch of Wera's escape with her children into Germany.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zmkjqpz3sz98vef74ea6k)

> When I woke up, we were driving through a dark, shadowy forest at dusk, the Brunssummerheide nature reserve in Limburg, as I later learned. I could just make out the contours of a giant monastery in the distance.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Having lost custody of her son, Wera flees The Hague on the escape date she and Guus planned, 12 April 1951, with Alexander and the baby Tatiana in Titty's DKW. The boy sleeps through the drive and wakes in the forest at dusk, then the car stops at a roadblock; a guard with a Limburg accent checks their papers and lets them through to [[Palenberg]].
- **Why it matters:** The crossing turns a custody ruling into flight: once past the forest and the border post, mother and son are out of the Dutch family's reach, at least for a few months.
