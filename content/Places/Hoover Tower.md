# Hoover Tower

Tower of the Hoover Institution library at [[Stanford University]], Palo Alto. In Stranger in a Strange Land (Robert A. Heinlein) Mike tries to read the whole 'Hoover Library'.

## Stranger in a Strange Land — Robert A. Heinlein (1961)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL59688W)

> They stopped in Palo Alto long enough for Mike to try (and fail to) swallow all the Hoover Library in mammoth gulps.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On their travels Mike and Jill stop in Palo Alto so Mike can read the entire Hoover Library. The scanners cannot spin fast enough and he cannot turn pages fast enough, so he gives up after hours of contemplation; they move on to San Francisco.
- **Why it matters:** A small episode in Mike's search to understand humans, leading to the San Francisco chapters.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real place. No highlight: Reader could not anchor any span in this book.
