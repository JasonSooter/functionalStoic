# Lime Grove Studios

Former film studios in Shepherd's Bush, west London, used by BBC Television; in _Life on Air_ they are the base for Attenborough's Talks programmes and Zoo Quest.

## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1mfnkyhen7g0pt2jvdv8)

> The BBC had acquired some old film studios that stood in Lime Grove, a shabby suburban street close to Shepherd's Bush in west London.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The BBC fits out old film studios with more advanced cameras (electronic viewfinders, lens turret). Animal, Vegetable, Mineral? is staged here, including the Huxley/Glyn Daniel snail-egg row. Later a reconciliation dinner is held in a garage-like room next to the Lime Grove canteen, and Attenborough first meets Charles Lagus in a pub at the top of Lime Grove.
- **Why it matters:** Where Talks programmes graduate to better technology and where several set-piece anecdotes occur; recurring base for the rest of the Zoo Quest story.
