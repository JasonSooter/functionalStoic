## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)

Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbk7ntkjsrk6yvd6ddm0vd)

> What happened over the next decade was as shocking in reality as it was predictable in theory. Old Pasadena staged a brilliant revival, while Westwood Village entered a steady economic decline that continues to this day.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Westwood Village cut on-street parking prices in half and kept a replacement-parking requirement, so cruisers burned 426 vehicle hours a day and redevelopment was blocked despite 1,250 empty spaces at peak. The district declined while Old Pasadena thrived.
- **Why it matters:** It is the failure half of the comparison. Speck calls its leaders boneheaded and uses it to show how underpriced curb parking and rigid minimums damage a district.
