## The Price of Civilization — Jim Wright (2015)
Source: [Original](https://www.stonekettle.com/2015/11/the-price-of-civilization.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hk5188c2wsd2e894cjch5g)

> And we reduced Iraq to lawless ruin.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Wright, who says he served there, argues the US won the war in Iraq but had no plan to govern or rebuild, so the country fell apart and the US walked away: another step in the chaos that breeds terrorism.
- **Why it matters:** Iraq is the second link in the essay's chain from abandoned wars to the Paris attacks.
