## Jackie Chan’s Plan to Keep Kicking Forever — Alex Pappademas (2017)

Source: [Original](https://www.gq.com/story/jackie-chans-gq-profile-2017) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7gqpc53r25bcyjqphpbkk)

> So today Jackie's driven 30 miles from Beijing to the Panlong Valley to visit his International Stunt Training Base and talk to some foreign visitors about the movie.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The writer visits Chan's sprawling complex, which has his logo on the aluminum facade, an equestrian statue made of old camera equipment, a trompe l'oeil canyon painting, and a glass booth holding an ancient tree. Chan gives tours and gifts and is interviewed in a lounge above the main workout room.
- **Why it matters:** Main scene of the profile; the complex embodies Chan's personality and wealth.
