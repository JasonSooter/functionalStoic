# The downhill course under Ben Nevis

The downhill course under Ben Nevis is the Scottish World Cup mountain-bike track, pinned inferred at the Nevis Range on [[Aonach Mòr]]. It appears in Robert Penn's _It's All About the Bike_ as the place where he was sent to write a feature and fell three times.
