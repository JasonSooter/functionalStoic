## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2zjf6kgrm7xtzandazj6)

> The Sixth Floor of the Television Centre in White City was the heartland of BBC administrators and thus, very properly, rich with perks.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Attenborough's workplace as Controller of BBC2 from March 1965: the sixth-floor office, desk choice, the studios below where jazz bands rehearsed, the tiny Presentation Studios used for colour tests, and the circular corridor with its control-room galleries.
- **Why it matters:** The base of the whole chapter: where BBC2's policy was devised and colour television prepared.
