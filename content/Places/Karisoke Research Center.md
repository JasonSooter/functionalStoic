# Karisoke Research Center

Dian Fossey's mountain gorilla research camp in the Virunga Mountains, Rwanda; in _Life on Air_ Attenborough visits Fossey there shortly before her death.

## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy7m5p19rrzj4qrpx89kvt)

> Karisoke, Dian's camp, proved to be a group of half a dozen green-painted corrugated iron huts connected by paths edged by low rails.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Dian Fossey, ill with pneumonia and grieving Digit's killing, receives the crew in her hut and tells of her anti-poacher actions. Ian Redmond then guides them on the forest slopes to habituated gorillas, where Attenborough has his close encounter with a female and youngsters.
- **Why it matters:** A major set piece and emotional centre of the range, combining gorilla conservation and the encounter.
