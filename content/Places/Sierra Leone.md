## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1vga7mdmcb8n5w4se1ar)

> We walked through the forest for an hour or so and came at last to a great overhanging rock face.

- **Role:** journey waypoint / goal
- **In the book:** After local legend of a one-eyed devil, a guide leads them to a rock face with mud nests. Charles and David film from a hide the next morning, and the guide later nets one of the birds for the Zoo.
- **Why it matters:** The objective of the whole quest; achieving it sets up the first Zoo Quest series.
