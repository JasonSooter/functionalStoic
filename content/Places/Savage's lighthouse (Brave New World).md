# Savage's lighthouse (Brave New World)

The disused air-lighthouse on the hill between Puttenham and Elstead in Surrey where John the Savage ends _Brave New World_, pinned at Puttenham.

## Brave New World — Aldous Huxley (1932)
Source: [[Brave New World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL64365W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvat4end5fpjkxbygkeq68)

> The Savage had chosen as his hermitage the old lighthouse which stood on the crest of the hill between Puttenham and Elstead.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** John retreats to the old lighthouse on the crest of the hill between Puttenham and Elstead to purify himself with prayer, fasting and self-flogging, looking north to the chalk ridge of the Hog's Back and Guildford's skyscrapers. Reporters, a hidden feely cameraman and then swarms of helicopter sightseers invade; Lenina comes, he whips her, an orgy follows, and the last scene shows his feet hanging in the archway.
- **Why it matters:** It is the setting of the closing chapter and of John's last attempt at solitude; its heath reminds him of Malpais, closing the book's circle.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The lighthouse is fictional; Puttenham, Elstead, the Hog's Back and Guildford are real.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The text places it precisely: "the crest of the hill between Puttenham and Elstead", with the Hog's Back to the north, a quarter of an hour's flight from the Charing-T Tower. Pinned at the Puttenham village point; the hill lies just south-west of it.
