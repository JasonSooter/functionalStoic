## Rick Steves' Iran (2009) — Rick Steves' Europe (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CYoa9hI3CXg) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqx7tkdacm0y44tp6b20j3)

> After a few hours, we reach Esfahan.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the video:** After a few hours south on the main highway the crew reaches Esfahan, a city of a million and a half known for blue-tiled domes and bridges. Most of the film's middle section is set here, including the palace, the square, the bazaar and the riverbank.
- **Why it matters:** Steves treats it as the showcase of Persian splendour and the place to understand Iran's religious and cultural complexity.
