## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjq3ts7c6ehzenkv0f6zme)

> we lived what seemed to be a perfect, sheltered life in Jerusalem in our secure house in the Rehavia neighborhood at the time.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In Rami's spoken testimony, Rehavia is where his family lived before 4 September 1997, a life he calls a bubble detached from the outside world. Nurit taught at the Hebrew University and he did graphic design for any wing that paid.
- **Why it matters:** Rehavia stands for the sheltered Israeli elite life that Smadar's death bursts; it is named once but frames the before-and-after of the story.
