# Church of Saint Spyridon

Church in the old town of [[Corfu Town]] that holds the relics of Saint Spyridon, the island's patron. In _My Family and Other Animals_ the family is swept into the crowd filing past his silver coffin.

## My Family and Other Animals — Gerald Durrell (1956)
Source: [[My Family and Other Animals]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/289004/my-family-and-other-animals-by-gerald-durrell/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8fa9exeg0dpaz5277n333jj)

> Saint Spiridion was the patron saint of the island. His mummified body was enshrined in a silver coffin in the church, and once a year he was carried in procession round the town.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On a trip into town to buy furniture for the new villa, Spiro's car runs into the crowd for the saint's day. Mother, Margo and Gerry are pushed into the main square and then up the steps into the dark church, where the faithful file past the saint standing upright in his silver coffin and kiss his slippered feet. Mother tells Gerry to kiss the air above them; Margo kisses the feet and goes down with influenza the next day, for three weeks.
- **Why it matters:** A comic set piece that shows how present the saint is in island life (Yani also calls on him), and it puts Margo in bed just as the household fills with guests. It is a minor episode in the book's larger story.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The text says only "the church"; the saint's relics are kept in the Church of Saint Spyridon in Corfu's old town.
