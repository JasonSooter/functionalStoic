Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbkzyav841npdkfbmt1kx2)

> Currently, one such project, in Brownsville, Brooklyn, hangs in the balance. It would replace surface parking lots with housing, shops, schools, and gardens, but it is being held up by parking minimums

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** A New York City Housing Authority 'tower in the park' project in Brownsville would infill surface parking lots but is held up by the Authority's parking minimums, even beside two subway stops.
- **Why it matters:** It shows that even the 'Green Metropolis' is hobbled by parking requirements, especially in publicly assisted housing.
