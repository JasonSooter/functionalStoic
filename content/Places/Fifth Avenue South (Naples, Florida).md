## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)

Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbp0228qd6nfzhkqmpwarr)

> In 1993, I was working with Andres Duany on the resuscitation of 5th Avenue South, the main street of Naples, Florida.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In 1993 Speck and Duany worked on reviving the main street of Naples, Florida. They found shops with tiny awnings that could not shade the sidewalks, because the building code required any larger awning to be sprinkled for fire suppression, and they got rid of that rule first.
- **Why it matters:** A personal, concrete case of fixing a code by deleting an old rule. It is a short anecdote, so the place is more illustration than setting.
