# Sophiatown

Suburb in western Johannesburg, a black freehold area bulldozed under apartheid and rebuilt as the white suburb Triomf. In Born a Crime it is where Trevor Noah's grandparents married and were driven out.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6yq461y7qe8d5kr2xkwkdp5)

> They met and married in Sophiatown, but one year later the army came in and drove them out.

- **Role:** turning point (family backstory)
- **In the book:** Chapter 5 ("The Second Girl"). Trevor's grandparents meet and marry in Sophiatown. A year later the government seizes their home and bulldozes the area to build "a fancy, new white suburb, _Triomf_. Triumph." They are forcibly moved, with tens of thousands of others, to the Meadowlands in Soweto, and soon divorce. That sets off his mother's unwanted childhood.
- **Why it matters:** the forced removal is the family's origin under apartheid. It explains why they are in Soweto at all, and why his mother is sent away to Transkei.
