# Chequers

Country house of the British prime minister in Buckinghamshire; in _Life on Air_ Attenborough plays tennis there with Anthony Eden.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2v6h5he141jhty9t3z3m)

> As we drew up in front of Chequers, Philip de Zulueta came out to greet me.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Attenborough is invited for tennis with Eden. His case is unpacked by a footman who removes his tennis clothes to iron them, so he plays in Philip de Zulueta's oversized borrowed kit, and then dines with the Prime Minister, where the Amboise/Arbois wine mistake is made.
- **Why it matters:** A full comic scene set at the Prime Minister's country house.
