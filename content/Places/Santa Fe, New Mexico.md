# Santa Fe, New Mexico

Capital of New Mexico. In _Brave New World_ it is the civilised base for the Reservation, where Bernard phones London and wins permission to bring John and Linda back.

## Brave New World — Aldous Huxley (1932)

Source: [[Brave New World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL64365W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvam5snn2hh795kv6dwpc5)

> At ten thirty-four they landed on the roof of the Santa Fé Post Office; at ten thirty-seven Bernard had got through to the World Controller’s Office in Whitehall;

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Bernard and Lenina land at Santa Fé after a six-and-a-half-hour rocket flight and sleep at an excellent hotel before visiting the Warden's office. Later Bernard flies back from Malpais, lands on the roof of the Santa Fé Post Office at 10.34, phones the World Controller's Office in Whitehall, collects a permit from the Warden and is back at Malpais by half past two.
- **Why it matters:** The minute-by-minute call from the Post Office roof is the hinge that moves John and Linda from the Reservation to London and sets the second half in motion.
