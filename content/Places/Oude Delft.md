# Oude Delft

Delft's oldest canal, in the historic centre of the Dutch city. In a New Yorker essay it is where an American family first meets Dutch street design on their new bikes.

## How I Learned to Cycle Like a Dutchman – The New Yorker — Dan Kois (2019)

Source: [Original](https://www.newyorker.com/culture/personal-history/how-i-learned-to-cycle-like-a-dutchman) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7cmmgd7681fcv1sffz3s7)

> To our left was the seven-hundred-year-old Oude Delft canal, its still surface covered with lily pads and floating trash. There was no protective guardrail or fence, not even a curb—just an unnerving, cliff-like drop.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** On their first ride out, past the leaning Oude Kerk, the family rides along the unfenced canal edge. Kois imagines plunging in, Thelma & Louise style, and a car passes inches from Lyra, who shouts that she doesn't like it.
- **Why it matters:** The canal edge is the family's first, frightening taste of Dutch street design.
