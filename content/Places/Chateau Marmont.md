# Chateau Marmont

Hotel on Sunset Boulevard in Hollywood, famous as a celebrity hideout. In Greenlights it is Matthew McConaughey's home during a stretch of excess around 2000.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m700jbm1n1b9cd94zhxekkbe)

> Ms. Hud and I made our new address the legendary Chateau Marmont in the heart of Tinseltown.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Taking a big cheque to star in The Wedding Planner, he makes the hotel his address, buys leather pants and a Triumph Thunderbird motorcycle and writes the Chateau a running tab of 120 grand. He lives there through a period of excess, and it is his base before the Reign of Fire shoot and his journey to Mali.
- **Why it matters:** The legendary rock-and-roll hotel suits the indulgent chapter it frames; the book uses it as the high point of the excess he later sets out to shed.
