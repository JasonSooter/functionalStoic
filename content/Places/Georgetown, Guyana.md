# Georgetown, Guyana

Capital of Guyana at the mouth of the Demerara River; the base of the 1955 Zoo Quest expedition to British Guiana in _Life on Air_.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1wr331f6wgdv7rspqa1h)

> The town stands on the coast close to the mouth of the Demerara River. There was little here that was authentically South American.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** The team arrives for the second Zoo Quest, by way of Amsterdam, Azores and Surinam, and sets up a mini-zoo. Attenborough describes the sugar-cane plantations, Dutch-built canals and the population of British, African and Indian descent.
- **Why it matters:** Base for the 1955 Guiana expedition; the text then dwells on the coastal creeks nearby.
