# Harrison, Arkansas

Small city in the Ozarks of northern Arkansas. In How to Be an Antiracist (Ibram X. Kendi) a billboard there claims antiracism is anti-White.

## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fzcz01cm267b627h2kwvq1)

> On October 15, 2013, workers unveiled a twelve-by-twenty-four-foot sign near a major roadway in Harrison, Arkansas, known in those parts as Klan territory.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Kendi describes the 2013 billboard reading 'Anti-racist is a code word for anti-White', put up in an area known as Klan territory, and rebuts the slogan.
- **Why it matters:** The billboard is the foil for his definition of antiracism.
