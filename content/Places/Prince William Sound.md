# Prince William Sound

Sound on the Gulf of Alaska coast, [[Alaska]]. Bryson uses its glaciers and the 1964 earthquake to show the power of ice, and Walt Morey sets all of _Gentle Ben_ around its fishing towns and bear islands.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W)

> In 1964, at Prince William Sound in Alaska, one of the largest glacial fields in North America was hit by the strongest earthquake ever recorded on the continent. It measured 9.2 on the Richter scale.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In 1964 the strongest earthquake ever recorded on the continent, magnitude 9.2, struck one of North America's largest glacial fields here. The land rose by as much as twenty feet along the fault and water sloshed out of pools in Texas, yet the glaciers just soaked it up and kept moving.
- **Why it matters:** Bryson's measure of the power of ice: nothing humans could do would stop an advancing ice sheet.

## Gentle Ben — Walt Morey (1965)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9347710M/Gentle_Ben) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8dcf4jv10maewhd3y4d8etm)

> His Viking is the mail boat, you know. Twice a month he makes that five-hundred-mile swing around the Sound, delivering groceries, medicine, freight of all kinds, and mail.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** The book never names the Sound in full, but it is plainly this one: the Andersens seine for salmon among "eleven hundred seiners in the Sound", buy bread for Ben in Chenega, Latouche and Valdez, end the season off Bligh Island, and see the St. Elias Range across the water. In winter Karl takes over the mail-boat run around the Sound, and the _Far North_ is wrecked on that run. Ben is put ashore on [[Hinchinbrook Island (Alaska)|Hinchinbrook]], "one of the largest brown-bear islands in the Sound".
- **Why it matters:** The Sound's mix of fishing towns, canneries, fish traps and wild bear islands is what lets the story move a tame bear from a town shed to the wild and back into the family's life. Its home port, [[Orca City (Gentle Ben)|Orca City]], is fictional.
