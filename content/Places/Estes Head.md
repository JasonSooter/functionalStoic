# Estes Head

Headland and deep-water cargo pier at the south end of [[Eastport]], Maine. In Aoibheann Sweeney's novel it is where the supertanker is loading on the night Miranda goes aboard with a sailor.

## At a glance

## Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking — Aoibheann Sweeney (2007)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/among-other-things-ive-taken-up-smoking-9781101202227)

> Soon I was learning to play pool, and they were drinking more beers, and one of the men, the one with the hat, was telling me about the supertanker they were on, which was being loaded at Estes Head.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** At [[McMann's (Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking)|McMann's]] two sailors tell Miranda their supertanker is loading at Estes Head before a twenty-nine-day crossing to China. Susie drives them past the security check down to the empty, yellow-lit loading docks, where "the iron flanks of the supertanker rose up above her into the fog". On board, Miranda sleeps with one of the sailors, Greg.
- **Why it matters:** It is the climax of Miranda's one reckless day in Part 1 and the end of her island innocence. The working port, with ships bound for the far side of the world, is the opposite of her father's book-lined island; a quieter harbour would lose that.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Estes Head is real. The novel calls the town Yvesport but names this real Eastport pier, which is the main clue that Yvesport is modelled on [[Eastport]].
