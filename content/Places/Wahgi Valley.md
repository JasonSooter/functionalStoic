## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2zhpy8s5xnt2jbk5wbjp)

> The Wahgi Valley, in which Nondugl lies, was completely unknown to the outside world until 1933

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Attenborough recounts how the Leahy brothers found the densely populated valley in 1933-37 and met people who had never seen metal. He explains that plumes serve as currency and that the end of tribal warfare has let hunters strip the wild birds from the valley.
- **Why it matters:** The history and ecology of the valley supply the chapter's key argument about the birds.
