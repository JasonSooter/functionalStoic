# Eton College

Boarding school in Eton, Berkshire, beside the Thames. In _Brave New World_ its Upper School is where John and Bernard see how the World State educates; in _The Boys in the Boat_ its boathouse is where the shell builder George Pocock grew up.

## Brave New World — Aldous Huxley (1932)
Source: [[Brave New World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL64365W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvaq6cjzwej5xsgkwe6fek)

> At Eton they alighted on the roof of Upper School. On the opposite side of School Yard, the fifty-two stories of Lupton’s Tower gleamed white in the sunshine.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** John and Bernard land on the roof of Upper School, opposite the fifty-two storeys of Lupton's Tower, and are shown round by the Provost and the Head Mistress. They see a Malthusian Drill, a film of the Penitentes of Acoma that makes the boys laugh, the Hypnopaedic Control Room and a library of reference books only, while the Provost mentions the Slough Crematorium and death conditioning.
- **Why it matters:** The visit shows conditioning applied to the elite, and the boys' laughter at the penitents pains John. A real school keeps its buildings but loses its purpose.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Eton, Upper School and Lupton's Tower are real; the fifty-two-storey tower is not.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m869y7t3gx3cmb7ha0fgtsk7)

> And it was in Eton’s ancient boathouse, just across the river from the looming eminence of Windsor Castle, that George had grown up.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** George Pocock's father Aaron builds and maintains Eton's racing shells, and George grows up in the boathouse across the Thames from Windsor Castle. At fifteen he is apprenticed to his father for six years, and he also learns to row, adapting the short, powerful stroke of the Thames watermen to racing shells.
- **Why it matters:** The boathouse is where the craft and the rowing style that Pocock later brings to Washington's crew were formed.
