# Eton College

Boarding school in Eton, Berkshire, beside the Thames. In _Brave New World_ its Upper School is where John and Bernard see how the World State educates.

## Brave New World — Aldous Huxley (1932)

Source: [[Brave New World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL64365W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvaq6cjzwej5xsgkwe6fek)

> At Eton they alighted on the roof of Upper School. On the opposite side of School Yard, the fifty-two stories of Lupton’s Tower gleamed white in the sunshine.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** John and Bernard land on the roof of Upper School, opposite the fifty-two storeys of Lupton's Tower, and are shown round by the Provost and the Head Mistress. They see a Malthusian Drill, a film of the Penitentes of Acoma that makes the boys laugh, the Hypnopaedic Control Room and a library of reference books only, while the Provost mentions the Slough Crematorium and death conditioning.
- **Why it matters:** The visit shows conditioning applied to the elite, and the boys' laughter at the penitents pains John. A real school keeps its buildings but loses its purpose.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Eton, Upper School and Lupton's Tower are real; the fifty-two-storey tower is not.
