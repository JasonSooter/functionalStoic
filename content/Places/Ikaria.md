# Ikaria

Greek island in the Aegean ([[Greece]]), known for its long-lived people. Mr. Money Mustache calls it the original island of his frugal, social way of life.

## Get Rich With: Your Own Urban Tribe — Mr. Money Mustache (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.mrmoneymustache.com/2015/08/19/urban-tribe/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gbgjrss9a3hcg6h1gtm800)

> There’s a Greek island called Ikaria that pops up regularly in health news because its people enjoy some of the longest, healthiest lives on Earth.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The author presents the island as the original model of the life he recommends: plenty of sleep, outdoor work, a lot of socialising, olive oil and wine. He jokingly calls it the original island of the Mustachians.
- **Why it matters:** It is the benchmark the post measures modern towns against.
