# Far Rockaway

Neighbourhood on the Rockaway Peninsula in Queens, New York City. Richard Feynman's home town, where his boyhood home laboratory starts the tinkering of _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfpv94z8wjgkm57350vdhn)

> I was born in 1918 in a small town called Far Rockaway, right on the outskirts of New York, near the sea.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The book opens with Feynman's birth in Far Rockaway in 1918, and he lives there until he goes to MIT at seventeen. In the family house he builds a lab from a wooden packing box, with a lamp bank, a storage battery and a Ford coil, repairs radios and wires the house; once he shakes burning material out of the window onto the street. Years later he meets an old friend on the beach at Far Rockaway, which leads to his summer job in a plating company.
- **Why it matters:** The town is where his tinkering and puzzle-solving habits begin; he later says Princeton's cyclotron room was the first lab that reminded him of his lab at home.
