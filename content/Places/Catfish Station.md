# Catfish Station

Blues bar on Sixth Street in Austin, Texas. In Greenlights it is where the college-age Matthew McConaughey talks his way into a job as the only white waiter.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6zp413wqa5k53sgktwr965k)

> When my college buddies and I would hit Sixth Street for a night out, they’d go to the popular bars littered with sorority girl possibilities and I’d go to Catfish Station, an all-black, sweaty bar that sold catfish, beer, and blues.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** As a UT student he drinks by the beer cooler while Kyle Turner plays sax, then asks the manager, Laron, for a job. Laron sends him to the owner, Homer, at the bigger club on Sixth Street the bar is moving into, who first has him mop. The waitress Tammy trains him, and he waits tables there Thursday to Saturday for two years.
- **Why it matters:** McConaughey uses the bar to make his point about finding the common values beneath differences between people and cultures.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real bar; the pin is a representative point on Sixth Street, as the book gives no address.
