## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6ky2d3twf828x3grj451x)

> I sat on the shady side of the enormous courtyard for almost an hour, enjoying the mosaics and the gold of the brickwork glowing against the blue sky.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The author, free to enter this mosque without a camera, sits for an hour in its enormous courtyard among the mosaics and martins. She judges it so beautiful that it makes up for the frustration of being barred at Meshed.
- **Why it matters:** A rare uninterrupted moment of peace and aesthetic payoff after the Persian bureaucratic frustrations; it sets Herat as the high point against which later Afghan towns are measured.
