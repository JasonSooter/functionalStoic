## Patagonia’s Philosopher-King – The New Yorker — Nick Paumgarten (2016)
Source: [Original](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2016/09/19/patagonias-philosopher-king) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy83s00z746jtvqj3xz5k1)

> He and his peers, colonizing the infamous Camp Four, called themselves the Valley Cong.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** After army service Chouinard returned to Yosemite where, with Robbins, Frost and Pratt, he made the first ascent of the North American Wall on El Capitan. Camp Four was the climbers' base where he later met Malinda.
- **Why it matters:** Yosemite is the crucible of his climbing fame and clean-climbing ethic that later became Patagonia's philosophy.
