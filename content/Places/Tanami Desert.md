# Tanami Desert

A remote desert in Australia's [[Northern Territory]]. In _A Short History of Nearly Everything_ Eugene Shoemaker dies there in a car crash.

## A Short History of Nearly Everything — Bill Bryson (2003)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74128W)

> On a dirt track in the Tanami Desert—normally one of the emptiest places on Earth—they came over a slight rise just as another vehicle was approaching. Shoemaker was killed instantly, his wife injured.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On the third anniversary of the Shoemaker-Levy impact, Eugene Shoemaker and his wife are searching the Australian outback for impact sites, as they do every year. On a dirt track in the Tanami Desert they come over a rise as another vehicle approaches; Shoemaker is killed instantly.
- **Why it matters:** It ends the story of the man who founded impact geology. The exact crash site isn't given.
