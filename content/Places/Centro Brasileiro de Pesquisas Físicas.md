# Centro Brasileiro de Pesquisas Físicas

Physics research centre in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. In _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_ Cesar Lattes invites Feynman there, and it is his base for his long stays in Brazil.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfqbygw1est905djytvqj2)

> Why don’t you come to Brazil? I’ll get a position for you at the Center for Physical Research.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Cesar Lattes offers Feynman a position at "the Center for Physical Research", and Feynman flies to Rio, where Brazilian TV films Lattes welcoming him. They agree he will lecture in the mornings so he can go to the beach in the afternoons, and he decides to give his lectures in Portuguese. Later the Center's people also connect him to Carnaval.
- **Why it matters:** It is the institutional reason for the whole Brazil section; the book calls it the Center for Physical Research rather than by its Portuguese name.
