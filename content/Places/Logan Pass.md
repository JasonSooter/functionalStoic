# Logan Pass

Pass on the Continental Divide in Glacier National Park, Montana, crossed by the Going-to-the-Sun Road. In Ryback's memoir it is where a ranger turns the brothers back from grizzly country.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8berh429k1pbzasnts6jvnw)

> We managed a foothold at Logan Pass this time and were prepared to continue along the Cut Bank River route through the high mountains when we encountered a ranger who squelched our plan.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After the Garden Wall retreat the brothers get a foothold at Logan Pass and plan to continue along the Cut Bank River route through the high mountains. A ranger stops them: the Cut Bank area is closed because of grizzly sightings. Eric, used to black bears but not grizzlies, does not argue and turns back.
- **Why it matters:** It is the second blocked route in Glacier and introduces the grizzly as the one danger Eric will not challenge.
