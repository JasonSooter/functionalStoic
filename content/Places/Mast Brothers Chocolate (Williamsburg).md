## How the Mast Brothers fooled the world into paying $10 a bar for crappy hipster chocolate — Deena Shanker (2015)
Source: [Original](https://qz.com/571151/the-mast-brothers-fooled-the-world-into-buying-crappy-hipster-chocolate-for-10-a-bar) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1z7jv6ccw9nm14kf63sp)

> Now, as a tour guide at the Brooklyn retail location told Quartz, the Williamsburg spot alone pulled in $28,000 in chocolate sales in just one December weekend.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The brothers' first Brooklyn factory and shop, where a Crankandstein Cocoa Mill was photographed in March 2009. Tours there ban photographs and notes, and a guide offers the 'trial and error' origin story that the article disputes. It is also the location of the $28,000 December weekend of sales.
- **Why it matters:** The site is where the Mast myth is staged and where the article tests it. The Williamsburg factory and shop are central to the case, though no street address is given.
