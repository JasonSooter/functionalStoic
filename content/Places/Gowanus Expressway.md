# Gowanus Expressway

Elevated expressway in [[Sunset Park]], Brooklyn, New York City, USA. It is Dan Wang's test case in Breakneck for whether Robert Moses's destruction of a neighbourhood was fatal.

## Breakneck — Dan Wang (2025)
Source: [[Breakneck]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL42416011W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebtd2edyjdz4afyc4x2pbf)

> Moses had barely taken a glance at the neighborhood before he thrust an elevated expressway above its busiest commercial street.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In Caro's The Power Broker, Sunset Park is an irredeemable slum whose heart is torn out when Moses's expressway drives away Finnish eateries and Norwegian shops. Wang observes the expressway still stands while the neighborhood, now Spanish-, Cantonese- and Mandarin-speaking, has endured.
- **Why it matters:** It is the test case for whether Moses's destruction was fatal; Wang uses the neighborhood's survival to argue critics overstated the damage.
