## Colorado is becoming a leader in constructing wildlife crossings — and there's much more to come — Stephanie Butzer (2024)
Source: [Original](https://www.denver7.com/news/local-news/colorado-is-becoming-a-leader-in-constructing-wildlife-crossings-and-theres-much-more-to-come) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebs4mgxssm5te9m61hpxh5)

> The project encompassed two overpasses — the first two in the state — five underpasses, more than 60 wildlife escape ramps, 29 wildlife guards and nine pedestrian walk-throughs over 10 or so miles between Green Mountain Reservoir and Kremmling.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Completed in 2016, it is called a gold star; collisions fell about 90% and over 112,000 mule deer movements were recorded over five years.
- **Why it matters:** It is the proof-of-concept case the article uses to show that crossings work.
