# Hotel Adlon

Luxury hotel on Pariser Platz at the head of [[Unter den Linden]], by the [[Brandenburg Gate]] in [[Berlin]]. In _The Boys in the Boat_ the Washington crew dines there in their sweaters among Olympic officials and SS officers in August 1936.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m87k3nnbpyz855d9k023hxns)

> When the athletic director from Stanford, Jack Rice, invited them to dinner at the posh Adlon hotel, they jumped at the chance.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In early August 1936, before the regatta, the boys walk through a police cordon into the Adlon's ornate lobby in everyday trousers and their crew sweaters. In the high-ceilinged dining room they sit wide-eyed at a candlelit table, watching international Olympic officials, well-heeled Americans and Brits, elegant German women in evening gowns, and SS officers sitting apart at their own tables, laughing and drinking French wine.
- **Why it matters:** The dinner drops the working-class crew into the glittering surface of Nazi Berlin, where the SS mixes easily with the world's elite.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The 1936 building was ruined in 1945; today's Hotel Adlon Kempinski was built on the same site in 1997.
