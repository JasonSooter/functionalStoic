## Python: The Documentary | An origin story — CultRepo (formerly Honeypot)
Source: [Original](https://youtube.com/watch/?v=GfH4QL4VqJ0) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dckv4znrbffsjnsbcap45d)

> November 1994, Gaithersburg, Maryland, in this windowless government office building at NIST.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the video:** The first Python workshop, about two days with some 20 people, was held in a windowless NIST office building. Speakers in the film call it the beginning of the Python community, and it led to Guido van Rossum's move to CNRI in the US.
- **Why it matters:** Marks the shift from a one-person project to a community.
