> I was living in Brooklyn, New York with two roommates in Crown Heights–a neighborhood that no one would describe as “safe” in 2006.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author lived in Crown Heights on foodstamps and an AmeriCorps stipend, in a tiny, dingy apartment shared with college friends, mice and cockroaches. She includes photos of the street and block where she lived.
- **Why it matters:** It anchors the broke starting point from which the couple's frugality began.
