# Unit 19 (Brentford)

Overflow unit behind the Golden Mile of the A4 Great West Road in [[Brentford]], west London. In _The Brompton_ it is the split-off site whose isolated staff showed Will Butler-Adams that the company had outgrown Brentford.

## The Brompton: Engineering for Change — Will Butler-Adams (2022)
Source: [[The Brompton - Engineering for Change]] · [Original](https://profilebooks.com/work/the-brompton/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8cdhfwaxcwde68nehxdwhph)

> We had been reduced to splitting between two sites, with the dispatching, spare parts and some customer services consigned to ‘Unit 19’, a space tucked away behind the ‘Golden Mile’ of the A4 highway.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In chapter 8, as the Brentford factory runs out of room, dispatch, spare parts and some customer services move to Unit 19, "ten minutes away by bike". The staff sent there feel marginalised and ignored, and Will's good intentions to visit them often never work out.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's case study of how a split site breeds uncertainty and resentment, and one of the pressures behind the 2015–16 move to a single factory in [[Greenford]].
