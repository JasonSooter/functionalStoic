## Quiet — Susan Cain (2012)
Source: [[Quiet]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/55983/quiet-by-cain-susan/9780141970172) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g8wm2ddzv9f57y9pybt240)

> Walker Creek Ranch sits on 1,741 acres of unspoiled Northern California wilderness.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Cain attends a weekend meeting of highly sensitive people at the ranch's Buckeye Lodge, hears Elaine Aron speak and finds an unusually quiet, deep-talking community. She leaves valuing a balance between sensitive and bold temperaments.
- **Why it matters:** The ranch is the setting for the chapter's second half, a place where the usual social rules are reversed.
