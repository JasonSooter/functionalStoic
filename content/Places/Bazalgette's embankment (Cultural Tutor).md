# Bazalgette's embankment (Cultural Tutor)

Stretch of the Thames embankment in London built by Joseph Bazalgette, most likely the Victoria Embankment, where the Cultural Tutor video argues that even Victorian utilities were made beautiful.

## How Did The World Get So Ugly? — The Cultural Tutor | Sheehan Quirke (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tWYxrowovts) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e333j0wsw203n7gctwayma)

> constructed this embankment that I'm standing on

- **Role:** setting
- **In the video:** Standing on the Thames embankment with Parliament in view, the presenter explains that Joseph Bazalgette built London's sewer network and this embankment, completed in 1870, to replace a stinking swamp. He points to the memorial to Bazalgette that nobody looks at and to the winning design for the lamps along the new promenade, using them to argue that Victorians decorated even utilities.
- **Why it matters:** The embankment is the video's physical setting for the thesis that ordinary infrastructure can be beautiful; the lampposts and the Bazalgette memorial are both on it, with Crossness Pumping Station as the follow-up site.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The text does not name the real place; the pin is a inference from the text.
