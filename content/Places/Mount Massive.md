# Mount Massive

Second-highest peak in Colorado, in the Sawatch Range near Leadville. Eric nearly gives up on it, then gets the view of his trek.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bs2ksk2f0vrfbv1a0ke9ks)

> But the view was not really what drove me upward; Massive was a landmark, and I had to leave my footprints upon its summit.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The endless slope and closing clouds defeat him; he swears off all mountains and makes camp. One last try just before dark: the clouds split at sunset and he sees "all of Colorado", the Divide twisting away like a serpent.
- **Why it matters:** A small parable of the whole trek: near-surrender, then the reward for one more try.
