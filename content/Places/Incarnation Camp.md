# Incarnation Camp

Summer camp near Ivoryton in Essex, Connecticut, founded in 1886. In _How to Know a Person_, David Brooks met his lifelong friend Pete Marks there, and his chapter on Pete's depression starts at the camp.

## How to Know a Person — David Brooks (2023)
Source: [[How to Know a Person]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/652822/how-to-know-a-person-by-david-brooks/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8gkd7aec93jmkagxryyf3g2)

> We met as kids at Incarnation Camp in Connecticut, were campers and counselors together for a decade, and remained close for life.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Chapter 10 opens Brooks's account of his friend Pete's depression by going back to camp, where Pete was "handsome, strong, athletic, and kind", once skipping around the dining hall in a fit of silliness. Pete's wife Jen was also at camp with them. Years later, at the wedding of Brooks's son, Pete's sons coax their mother onto the dance floor, "since this was what we did at camp".
- **Why it matters:** The camp is the shared past against which Brooks measures how far Pete fell and how hard it was to know what he was going through. The dining hall and ball field are never located, so the camp stands for the friendship rather than for any scene's geography.
