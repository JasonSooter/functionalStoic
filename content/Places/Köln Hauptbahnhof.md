# Köln Hauptbahnhof

Cologne's main railway station, beside the cathedral in [[Cologne]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where Frans meets his old SS comrade Karl in 1976 and takes on the role of guardian angel to his secret daughter.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zn1dgn6dxervw3znfjcq0)

> When he saw Karl Reinhard in the main hall of the central train station in Cologne, he hardly recognized him.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Karl's telegram reads "Problems with Andrea. Cologne central station, 2pm OK? Karl." Frans packs and goes. In the main hall he barely recognises the once-cheerful ski instructor; over beers in a bar near the station Karl tells him that Andrea and her small daughter are living in a hippie squat, and Frans answers: "Don’t blame yourself. I’ll handle everything. Take me to Andrea."
- **Why it matters:** The meeting pulls Frans back into the German life he had to abandon and begins the last act of his story, the years of supporting Andrea that cost him his family's goodwill.
