# Bangudae Petroglyphs

Prehistoric rock carvings near Ulsan, South Korea. Not the End of the World cites them as perhaps the first evidence of whaling.

## Not the End of the World — Hannah Ritchie (2024)

Source: [[Not the End of the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL37518722W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f0qx12v4a74k84xqa1e9sh)

> In the early 2000s, researchers were exploring Bangudae, a site in South Korea, which dates back to 6000 BC.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In the history of whaling, Ritchie cites researchers exploring Bangudae in the early 2000s, a site dating to about 6000 BC, where rock carvings show whales beside boats of men with harpoons.
- **Why it matters:** It anchors the deep history of humans hunting whales.
