# Mount Vernon Public Library

Carnegie library in Mount Vernon, Westchester County, [[New York State]], just north of New York City. In _Great Short Books_ it is the library of Kenneth C. Davis's childhood, where his life as a reader began.

## Great Short Books — Kenneth C. Davis (2022)
Source: [[Great Short Books]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8f01y5n3pqc55qhxwgqrz3b)

> But as a child, my world was shaped by trips to the majestic, temple-like Mount Vernon Public Library, an Andrew Carnegie jewel, just outside New York City.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In his introduction, Davis says his mother took him to the library weekly, until he could go by himself, visits as significant to him as church on Sunday. He remembers the day he graduated from the street-level Children's Room to the Adult Room, up a grand staircase "into a reverential world of books", and the weekly Bookmobile that brought the library to those who couldn't get there.
- **Why it matters:** It is the origin story of the "Common Reader" voice of the whole book. In his entry on _Charlotte's Web_, Davis notes that E. B. White was also born in Mount Vernon, his hometown.
