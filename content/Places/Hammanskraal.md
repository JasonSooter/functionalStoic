# Hammanskraal

Town north of Pretoria in [[South Africa]], a former relocation settlement. In Born a Crime Trevor Noah's friend Tom gets him on stage at a talent show there as "Spliff Star".

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6z18v76n6q1zyqb7ywzjbt2)

> I went with him once to Hammanskraal, a settlement that was like a homeland, but not really.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Chapter 14 ("… Part III: The Dance"). Noah explains that Hammanskraal, "the kraal of Hamman", was once a white man's farm. It is one of the empty places where the government dumped deported black people, unlike the proper homelands such as Venda, Gazankulu and Transkei. Tom takes him to a talent show in a community hall there. The host introduces him as "Spliff Star", a rapper all the way from America, and Trevor has to perform for an audience that does not speak English.
- **Why it matters:** the comic set piece rests on the history lesson: forced removals left whole communities in places with nothing, and a fake American star is a big event there.
