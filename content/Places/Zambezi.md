## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2m35nayze3rdyww1d74k)

> a journey down the Zambezi from source to mouth.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Attenborough proposes and makes a BBC series following the Zambezi from source to mouth, combining wildlife, archaeology (Great Zimbabwe), anthropology (Barotseland), Livingstone history and the Kariba Dam. Elephants, hippo, bee-eaters and pelicans line the river.
- **Why it matters:** The organising journey of the chapter; each later waypoint (Limalunga, Sesheke, Victoria Falls, Shupanga) hangs on it. The author admits the films gave no real insight.
