# DC USA

DC USA is a big-box shopping complex in Columbia Heights, Washington, D.C.. It turns up in Walkable City (Jeff Speck): It is Speck's cautionary tale of overbuilt required parking in a walkable, transit-served location, which finally led DC to eliminate parking minimums near Metro stations.

## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)

Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbk42pbvsv98bk9pg07b4z)

> Whenever I feel like complaining about our own Washington parking struggle, I remind myself of the story of DC USA.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** DC USA is a $145 million retail complex at a Metro stop in Columbia Heights. The city halved its parking requirement yet a $40 million, 1,000-car garage was built, and it sits mostly empty (never over 47 percent) while the shops thrive.
- **Why it matters:** It is Speck's cautionary tale of overbuilt required parking in a walkable, transit-served location, which finally led DC to eliminate parking minimums near Metro stations.
