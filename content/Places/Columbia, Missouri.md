## How Zapier Went From Zero to 600,000+ Users in Just Three Years — Groove (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.groovehq.com/blog/zapier-interview-with-wade-foster) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy732x93ta6e01yw5yz9ws)

> We wanted to make a go of it and turn it into a business, but we were in Columbia, Missouri.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Zapier's founders built the first prototype at a Startup Weekend in Columbia, then kept day jobs and worked nights and weekends there, because the city had little funding and businesses were bootstrapped. The article frames it as the opposite of Silicon Valley.
- **Why it matters:** Origin city and the article's example of a startup outside a tech hub.
