# Zomba

University city and former colonial capital in southern Malawi. In _The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind_ it is where William Kamkwamba spends his first night in a hotel, rehearsing for his trip abroad.

## The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind — William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer (2009)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314792/the-boy-who-harnessed-the-wind-by-william-kamkwamba-and-bryan-mealer/9780593866894) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8ebday5h531pcac4mf27pkp)

> I took a minibus six hours to Zomba, and we visited the Hotel Masongola, where many tourists stay.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Before the TED conference, Dr. Mchazime takes William out of [[Madisi Secondary School]] for a weekend "crash course on international travel". At the Hotel Masongola the manager shows him a room, how to fill out guest cards and how to order in the restaurant. The Masongola is too expensive, so he sleeps at Peter's Lodge: "It was my first night in a hotel, and the first time I’d ever slept on a real mattress." Mchazime had earlier driven five hours from Zomba to see the windmill.
- **Why it matters:** A small scene, but it marks the first step from village life toward the wider world that the rest of the book opens up.
