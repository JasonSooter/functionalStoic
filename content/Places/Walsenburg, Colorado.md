## If You Let Them Build It, They Are Much More Likely to Come — David Friedlander (2015)

Source: [Original](https://lifeedited.com/if-you-let-them-build-it-they-are-much-more-likely-to-come/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx20xr10b9xghn746fvxcv)

> Walsenburg is a former coal mining town of 3K that has seen better days. The tiny house subdivision is meant as a way of providing affordable housing and revitalizing the economy.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Walsenburg's city council removed its 600 sq ft minimum so Sprout Tiny Homes can build a 32-unit tiny-house subdivision with foundations, grid hookup, a community center and outdoor movie theater. The town is a former coal town of about 3,000, and a large cannabis growing facility is going up on its outskirts, so the homes may house its workers.
- **Why it matters:** The article's whole argument rests on this small town as a possible precedent for legal tiny-house housing.
