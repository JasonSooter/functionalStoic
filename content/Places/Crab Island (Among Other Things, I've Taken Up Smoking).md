# Crab Island (Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking)

Fictional island off the Down East coast of Maine, pinned here at Treat Island off [[Eastport]] as a best guess. It is where the narrator of Aoibheann Sweeney's novel grows up alone with her father after her mother drowns.

## Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking — Aoibheann Sweeney (2007)

Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/among-other-things-ive-taken-up-smoking-9781101202227) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7654mrpagkyms2n5pt03yhx)

> I grew up on Crab Island, about a mile or so across the water from a small town called Yvesport, which, more often than not, was hidden from us by a thick Down East fog.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Miranda's father, a classicist translating Ovid, lives on the island in a house without phone or power lines, and her mother walks into the fog and is found drowned three days later. As a girl Miranda tries to swim the channel toward the red channel marker and is fished out by Mr. Blackwell. When the channel freezes before Christmas, she and her father are cut off from the mainland for months and walk out on the ice to the marker.
- **Why it matters:** The island's isolation is the shape of Miranda's childhood: a closed world of fog, myth and her father's silence that she has to leave for New York in the rest of the novel.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Crab Island is invented.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Best guess. The text gives a Down East setting, a crossing of about a mile to Yvesport (inferred as [[Eastport]]), a view of "the harbor … around the point to Estes Head Pier and Deep Cove", winter sea ice and a Coast Guard icebreaker. No source names a model; reviews say only "an island in Maine". Treat Island, a small island in Eastport about a mile south of Estes Head, matches the distance and view. Alternatives: Dudley Island or another islet in Cobscook Bay.
