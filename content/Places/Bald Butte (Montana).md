# Bald Butte (Montana)

Peak near the Continental Divide northwest of Helena, Montana, beside Roundtop Mountain. Under it the Ryback brothers find an old prospector's sluice boxes and Tim pans for gold.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8bert427e17xg999vvzz13t)

> The next morning we headed south toward Greenhorn Mountain, leaving our dreams of gold at the base of Bald Butte.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The brothers camp between Roundtop Mountain and Bald Butte, beside a stream with three rotting sluice boxes and a prospector's rusted tools. Tim rebuilds the sluice, pans with a cooking pot and shrieks "Gold!", but the fleck Eric examines is a translucent amber-like stone. Next morning they head south toward Greenhorn Mountain, "leaving our dreams of gold at the base of Bald Butte."
- **Why it matters:** A comic interlude that gives the chapter its title ("Gold!") and ties the trek to Montana's mining past.
