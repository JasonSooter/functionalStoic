- **Role:** journey waypoint

## Race the wind north. — Mostly Bikes x Me (2026)

Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bs6qeqqjs5cvgd5q6w3and)

> I didn't hit the wall of wind until Nyons where I stopped briefly to acquire a sack of the village's famous olives.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** Riding north to set up a tailwind for the return, the author meets the full force of the wind at Nyons, stops for a sack of olives, then turns south through the valley below Montauleiu and onto forest gravel.
- **Why it matters:** The ride's turning point; the town's olives make it the natural stop, as in the earlier post.
