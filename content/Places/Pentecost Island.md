## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx3jhvvph32adyq87ae485)

> On the southern tip of the island of Pentecost in the northern part of Vanuatu, the people indulged in land-diving.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** On a steep hillside outside a village, a 100-foot scaffold tower is built with diving boards and vine lianas. Attenborough films men and boys diving head first, tethered by the ankles, the first time it had been filmed.
- **Why it matters:** The subject of the chapter's most spectacular sequence; the village is unnamed.
