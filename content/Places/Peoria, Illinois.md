# Peoria, Illinois

Peoria is a city on the Illinois River in central Illinois. In _Abundance_ it is where wartime scientists found how to mass-produce penicillin.

## At a glance

## Abundance — Ezra Klein & Derek Thompson (2025)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL44034521W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75933xjnc755drtm3rj6e)

> With OSRD’s encouragement, scientists in Peoria, Illinois, discovered that adding “corn steep”—water soaked with corn—could increase penicillin production tenfold.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In chapter 5, penicillin production stalls because larger batches get spoiled. With the Office of Scientific Research and Development's encouragement, scientists in Peoria discover that adding corn steep raises yields tenfold. Along with new mold strains, this made mass production possible during World War II.
- **Why it matters:** It shows a deployment bottleneck solved by government-backed work, the book's model for turning discoveries into abundance.
