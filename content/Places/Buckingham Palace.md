## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy841msfwh925b675weyc1)

> The lift descended, clanged to a halt and the Queen stepped out wearing the mushroom outfit and looking like thunder.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** For the 1988 message, Attenborough picks a small green-papered room, then argues with the Queen over her outfit, ending with the joke about re-papering the room.
- **Why it matters:** A small but vivid episode; the place is the stage for the anecdote.
