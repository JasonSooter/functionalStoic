# Café Hawelka

Coffee house off the Graben in central [[Vienna|Vienna]], Austria; in Neither Here Nor There it is the one friendly café Bryson finds there.

## Neither Here Nor There — Bill Bryson (1991)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL74125W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bek7p4wtnt3v9kmkems5y7)

> The one friendly café I found was the Hawelka, around the corner from my hotel.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 19. It is "musty, disheveled, and so dark that I had to feel my way to a table", with newspapers on racks. An old waiter brings coffee unasked and, realising Bryson is American, piles copies of USA Today in front of him; Bryson's pleas for real newspapers go unheard.
- **Why it matters:** The warm exception to his complaint about Viennese cafés (compare [[Café Central]]), played as a gentle comic vignette.
