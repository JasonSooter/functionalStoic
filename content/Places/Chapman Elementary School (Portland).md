> Our public school, Chapman elementary, had an okay rating.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** While the family lived downtown, Chapman was their zoned school; its district covers downtown and NW Portland and had grown more crowded and diverse. Its middling rating is what made them consider private school.
- **Why it matters:** It is the starting point of the debate the article works through.
