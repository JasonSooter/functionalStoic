# Dormston

Dormston is a small village in Worcestershire, England, where Tolkien's aunt Jane Neave lived in a Tudor farmhouse called Bag End. It is pinned for _The Hobbit_ as the source of the name of Bilbo's home.

## The Hobbit (Middle-Earth Universe) — J. R. R. Tolkien (1937)
Source: [[The Hobbit]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL27482W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m773vdkctz33tzgbrwhjnx)

> He shuddered; and very quickly he was plain Mr. Baggins of Bag-End, Under-Hill, again.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bag-End, Under-Hill, is Bilbo's comfortable hobbit-hole, where Gandalf and thirteen dwarves descend for the unexpected party in the first chapter. Listening to the dwarves sing, Bilbo briefly longs for adventure, then shudders back into plain Mr. Baggins of Bag-End. When he finally comes home, he finds his belongings being sold at auction.
- **Why it matters:** Bag End is where the story starts and ends, the comfort Bilbo leaves and returns to; the real farmhouse lent it only its name.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The farmhouse called Bag End was a real house in Dormston; only the name is documented as borrowed, not the house's layout.
- **Where is it?:** Wikipedia (Bag End; The Shire) states that "Bag End" was the real name of the Tudor home of Tolkien's aunt Jane Neave in the village of Dormston, Worcestershire, citing the Worcestershire Historic Environment Record (2013) and Andrew Morton's Tolkien's Bag End (2009). The pin is the village, not the farmhouse itself.
