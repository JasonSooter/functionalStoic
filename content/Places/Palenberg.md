# Palenberg

Former mining village on the Dutch border, now part of Übach-Palenberg in [[North Rhine-Westphalia]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where Wera hides with her son at her mother's house in 1951, until he is kidnapped back to the Netherlands.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85zmn4589c0nvvcwn8qmrft)

> “To Palenberg,” Wera replied. “My mother lives there.” It was the first I’d heard of our destination.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Wera's mother Nadia takes them into her little house on Aachenerstrasse, on the edge of a forest. Alexander turns seven there, fights tin-soldier battles with Hans from across the street, and meets Hans's father, a former notary maimed in the war, a sight that shows him "the true face of the war". Wera works as an interpreter at the American airbase in Aachen. One Sunday, while the boy is at a match in "Palenberg's shabby little stadium", his grandfather visits to announce that Wera has lost the case; soon after, Dirk Sopers and Louise Marggraff lure him into the woods with an ice cream and a model ship and drive him off, chloroformed, in a red Volkswagen.
- **Why it matters:** Palenberg is the boy's brief taste of an ordinary childhood with his mother's side of the family, and its loss through the kidnapping decides the rest of his upbringing.
