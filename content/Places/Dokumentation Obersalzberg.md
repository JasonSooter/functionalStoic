> The Obersalzberg Documentation Center examines this period in detail.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** At the Berchtesgaden stop the video turns to the Obersalzberg, the Nazi-era site of Hitler's headquarters and bunkers; the documentation centre examines that period.
- **Why it matters:** It is the historical counterweight to the scenic stops; the video doesn't present Berchtesgaden as scenery only.
