## Death in Venice — Thomas Mann (1912)

Source: [[Death in Venice]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5c4pzc9fx9xwzs5bn02gkpc)

> An autumnal, off-season mood seemed to lie upon the once so colorful and animated, now all but deserted pleasure haunt, its sand no longer kept clean.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Aschenbach arrives by steamer at the Piazzetta, admiring the Palace, the Bridge of Sighs and the columns, and is rowed by an unlicensed gondolier across the lagoon to the Lido against his wishes. He stays at the Hôtel des Bains, where he first sees Tadzio in the lobby, and on the hotel's beach his mornings under a rented cabana become a ritual of watching the boy. In the final chapter, on a nearly deserted beach, he watches Tadzio walk out along a sandbar and turn to look back, and dies in his chair.
- **Why it matters:** The Lido is the novella's main stage and the foil to the infected city across the water; the lost-luggage reversal, the beach routine and the sandbar image all depend on it. Specific Venice places with their own pins are listed under Sub-places.
- **Not pinned separately:** the Hôtel des Bains and the Lido beach (no standard-source coordinate: the English and Italian Wikipedia pages give none), St Mark's Basilica (folded into [[Piazza San Marco]]) and the Piazzetta.
