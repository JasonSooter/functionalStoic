## How to Build an Opportunity Fund — Jim Wang (2015)

Source: [Original](https://wallethacks.com/build-opportunity-fund/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hj2bw4xfd8f45p20fjj2gb)

> Originally, we weren’t going to the concert. We were going to a nearby pub because we like U2 but not enough to want to spend a few hundred dollars to see them.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** On a European vacation, friends who were meant to use U2 tickets missed their flight. The author dips into his opportunity fund to buy the tickets and goes to the Croke Park concert, which he calls incredible in person.
- **Why it matters:** It is the article's worked example of what an opportunity fund is for: money set aside to say yes to an unplanned chance. The venue itself is incidental; any concert would make the point.
