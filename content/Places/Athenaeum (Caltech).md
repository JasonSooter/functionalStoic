# Athenaeum (Caltech)

Faculty club of the California Institute of Technology in Pasadena. In _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_ its basement holds the one-man show of Feynman's drawings.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfqqgq8qfbag317myfqz45)

> The exhibition was held in the basement of the Athenaeum, the Caltech faculty club.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** A coordinator of Caltech's new art studios organises a one-man show of Feynman's drawings, signed "Ofey", borrowing works back from their buyers. "The exhibition was held in the basement of the Athenaeum, the Caltech faculty club." He hangs a portrait titled "Madame Curie Observing the Radiations from Radium", and a visitor asks how he got Madame Curie to pose.
- **Why it matters:** The show caps the chapter on how Feynman learned to draw; the club itself is only the venue.
