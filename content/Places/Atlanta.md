# Atlanta

Capital of the US state of Georgia. In _Happy City_ it is the case study of how decades of highway building, ringed by the Perimeter beltway, fed sprawl and congestion instead of curing them.

## Happy City — Charles Montgomery (2013)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21093281W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m83qhx3jf0ke9k4qcsbt97kz)

> Atlanta was encircled by a new beltway—known as the Perimeter—in 1969, and highway expansion programs continued for more than thirty years.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Montgomery uses Atlanta to show induced traffic and planners' "presentism": each new lane pulled in new drivers and scattered development, filling up within five or six years. He ties the suburban spread partly to white flight, and notes the Perimeter, up to twelve lanes in places, still grinds to a halt at peak hours.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's clearest case that building road capacity to cure congestion entrenches both sprawl and congestion.
