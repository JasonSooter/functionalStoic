## An Illustrated Celebration of Trailblazing Women in Science — Maria Popova (2016)

Source: [Original](https://www.brainpickings.org/2016/07/28/women-in-science-rachel-ignotofsky/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jx89bmwqkwxdkqst5tk2g5)

> That was the attitude in the 1930s, anyway; when Barbara McClintock wore slacks at the University of Missouri, it was considered scandalous.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Quoting Ignotofsky, the piece recounts Barbara McClintock being seen as bold and unladylike at the University of Missouri. The faculty excluded her from meetings, offered little support, and would fire her if she married, so she left to find a better job.
- **Why it matters:** The university stands for institutional sexism in science and is the place that prompted McClintock's career-defining departure.
