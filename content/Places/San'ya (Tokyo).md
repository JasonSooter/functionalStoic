## The chilling stories behind Japan’s ‘evaporating people’ — Maureen Callahan (2016)

Source: [Original](https://nypost.com/2016/12/10/the-chilling-stories-behind-japans-evaporating-people/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5sf5pwz4bk0hx8cd07694)

> The city of Sanya, as Mauger writes, isn’t located on any map. Technically, it doesn’t even exist. It’s a slum within Tokyo, one whose name has been erased by authorities.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Sanya is described as an officially erased slum within Tokyo where the 'evaporated' people live in tiny squalid hotel rooms, with work run by the yakuza or off-the-books employers. Norihiro takes his usual train line in the other direction toward Sanya after losing his job, and Yuichi also disappears there after leaving his sick mother in a cheap hotel.
- **Why it matters:** It is the destination and refuge of the vanished people and the article's central place.
