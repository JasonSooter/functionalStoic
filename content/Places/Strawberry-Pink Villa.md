# Strawberry-Pink Villa

The Durrells' first house on [[Corfu]], at Perama on the coast south of [[Corfu Town]]. It is the setting of the first part of _My Family and Other Animals_, where Gerry begins studying the island's wildlife.

## My Family and Other Animals — Gerald Durrell (1956)
Source: [[My Family and Other Animals]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/289004/my-family-and-other-animals-by-gerald-durrell/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8fa9brsp3rvbgnypf2za234)

> Halfway up the slope, guarded by a group of tall, slim cypress trees, nestled a small strawberry-pink villa, like some exotic fruit lying in the greenery.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After Mother rejects every bathroomless villa, Spiro drives the family along a dusty road lined with prickly pears to a hilltop and points to the villa on a slope above the sea. They feel they have "come home". In its tiny geometric garden Gerry watches colour-changing crab spiders, a lacewing laying eggs on stilts and an earwig's nest. From here he roams the olive groves with Roger, meeting Agathi, the shepherd Yani and the Rose-Beetle Man, from whom he buys the tortoise Achilles. George gives him lessons in the dining-room and at a small crescent-shaped bay nearby, reached by a goat track along low cliffs.
- **Why it matters:** This is where the book's double plot starts: the family's comic household and Gerry's apprenticeship as a naturalist. The garden and the olive groves around it are the first stage of his education.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The book names no village. Durrell guides place the real villa at Perama, on a hill above the coast near the end of the airport runway (Sunvil's Durrell guide; the Durrelliana "Corfu landscapes" page: "on a hill-top in Perama"). ⚠️ The pin is Perama village, not the house itself.
