> After a twenty-minute drive where they refused to answer any of my questions or even speak to me at all, they parked and showed me into a generic-looking business-park building.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** FBI agents take Grace to a generic building where Stratt has outfitted a biology lab, soon converted into an argon-filled quarantine with a hazmat airlock and observation room. Grace examines the ArcLight sample, discovers the Astrophage moves by emitting light, names it, and kills one cell with a nanosyringe to find it is mostly water. Grace, guarded by army soldiers at the doors, builds a lightproof closet from hardware-store lumber and works out the Astrophage life-cycle in the lab, sleeping there and taping over windows for midnight infrared experiments. He discovers the Venus-seeking behaviour and accidentally breeds a fourth cell. Stratt, who has just allotted him three cells, flies him out from here the same day.
- **Why it matters:** The main Earth setting of the book's early flashbacks: Astrophage is first studied and named here, and Stratt's control over Grace begins here.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The lab is unnamed. Clues: a "twenty-minute drive" from Grace's home, a "generic-looking business-park building", and Stratt's order to "bring it directly here to San Francisco". Pinned at the San Francisco city point; the real site could be anywhere in the Bay Area.
