# Storm Lake (Montana)

Mountain lake in Deer Lodge County, Montana, at the edge of the Anaconda-Pintler Wilderness. It is the Ryback brothers' first camp back in the high wilderness after days of road walking.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8berxbgpxfq8mp6p6dr8cgf)

> it was Storm Lake, which we reached on the afternoon of July 9, that provided our first glimpse of the Anaconda-Pintlar Wilderness.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After the road walking around Butte and Anaconda, the brothers reach Storm Lake on the afternoon of July 9, their first glimpse of the Anaconda-Pintlar Wilderness. With nothing to do they feel miserable and improvise fishing; Tim tries to spear the cutthroat trout and gives up ("They're armor plated"). Next day they climb Storm Lake Pass.
- **Why it matters:** It marks the return to the mountains after the smelter, opening the Anaconda-Pintlar section that the book counts among its finest.
