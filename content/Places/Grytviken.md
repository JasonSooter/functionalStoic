## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy7sv1nhnxzrh0m9h7qmc2)

> This had once been a Norwegian whaling station.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The abandoned station is described: the whale butchery imagined, then derelict half-sunk catcher ships, a saw, a cinema projection room and rats among stores. The company simply sailed away when factory ships arrived.
- **Why it matters:** A strongly dwelt-on scene of industrial abandonment against the peaks.
