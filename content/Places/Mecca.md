## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjqh9e5139ksgpha755afz)

> He entered Masjid-al-Haraam, circled the Kabaa seven times in the pale light. I am here, he thought.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Bassam travels alone by bus on the pilgrimage, which Salwa insisted on; he has memorised Mecca's geography since boyhood. He circles the Kaaba, casts stones, visits the cave at Hira and sees Uhud, and notes checkpoints on the way. It is placed beside the account of Abir's funeral procession.
- **Why it matters:** A rare place of spiritual release for Bassam, set against the checkpoints and permits that otherwise bound his movement.
