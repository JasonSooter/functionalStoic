## Stranger in a Strange Land — Robert A. Heinlein (1961)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL59688W)

> landed on the flat of the Executive Palace shortly before ten

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Jubal's party, with Mike, Jill and Caxton, lands on the Palace roof; Jubal refuses a private pre-meeting and goes straight to the crowded conference room to negotiate with Secretary General Douglas.
- **Why it matters:** The conference here settles Mike's legal status, a turning point of the plot.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The Palace is Heinlein's invention. No highlight: Reader could not anchor any span in this book.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Clues: on the flight Mike sees Washington and groks its age; the party lands on the Palace roof shortly after. No reference places the building. Pinned at Washington, D.C.
- **Later in the scene:** In the Palace's great conference hall, with the President, Senator Boone, the Papal Nuncio and Assemblyman Kung present, Jubal manages the protocol so that Mike and the Secretary General sit down together, has Mike speak in Martian and English, and asks Douglas to become Mike's attorney-in-fact; Douglas agrees in green ink, and Jubal then defuses Kung's challenge over the Larkin Decision.
