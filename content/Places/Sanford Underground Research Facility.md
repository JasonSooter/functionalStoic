## Why America is Hollowing Out a Mountain — The B1M (2025)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8d1ugv8vQ4w) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqxzwgv0x934ahjt39agra)

> And it’s here that the far detector is being built: at level 4,850.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** Formerly the longest continuously operated and deepest gold mine in America (closed 2002), it is being hollowed out at level 4,850 (about 1,475 m deep) for three huge caverns. Work from 2012 replaced the steel sets down the 1,500 m shaft, and by 2024 800,000 tonnes of rock had been excavated for the DUNE far detector.
- **Why it matters:** It is the main site of the video: the far end of the experiment and the biggest caverns ever built at that depth.
