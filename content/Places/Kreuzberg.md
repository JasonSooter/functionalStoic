# Kreuzberg

District of [[Berlin]] that lay against the Wall in the West. David Byrne remembers it in _Bicycle Diaries_ as the city's punk, heroin and black-leather underworld of the Wall years.

## At a glance

| Source | Author | Role | In one line |

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7641t4f2p9dhm8xazb4g4f5)

> Kreuzberg, the Lower East Side of West Berlin, was one of the most decadent low-life places I’ve ever seen.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Byrne recalls black leather, heroin and late-night punk clubs, "a sort of government-sponsored bohemian world" just out of reach but visible to East Berliners over the Wall.
- **Why it matters:** Kreuzberg is his memory of divided Berlin, set against the post-Wall luxury that has since moved into Mitte and Friedrichstrasse.
