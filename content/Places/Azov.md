# Azov

Azov is a town on the lower Don near the Sea of Azov in southern Russia, long fought over by the Ottomans and Russians. In _Candide_ the old woman survives its siege, where she loses a buttock.

## Candide — Voltaire (1759)
Source: [[Candide]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL100672W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75axbrs4ws8v1s9cja8az)

> At length I became the property of an Aga of the Janissaries, who was soon ordered away to the defence of Azof, then besieged by the Russians.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** In the old woman's story, her master, an Aga of the Janissaries, takes his seraglio to defend Azof and lodges the women in a small fort on the Palus Méotides. The Russians burn the town, and the starving garrison cut a buttock from each woman to eat. The Russians then arrive in flat-bottomed boats and kill every Janissary, and a French surgeon cures the women.
- **Why it matters:** The most grotesque scene of the old woman's tale, and the reason she rides 'only on one buttock'. It is a story within the story.
