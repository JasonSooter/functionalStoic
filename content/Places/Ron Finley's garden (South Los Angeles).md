## In the Culture Wars, Gardening Is This Man’s Weapon of Choice — GOOD (2016)

Source: [Original](http://www.good.is/posts/gardening-inspires-communities) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjs09er1enyvyw68w1wvsh)

> Ron Finley planted organic vegetables on the grass next to the sidewalk in front of his South Los Angeles home and a revolution was started.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Finley writes that after driving 45 minutes for pesticide-free produce he planted a simple garden on a curbside dirt strip that had been an eyesore in his neighbourhood, open to anyone. The author note places it in front of his South Los Angeles home, and he says it is now more about the people than the food.
- **Why it matters:** The garden is the concrete site of his argument that growing food can rebuild community; the essay itself never names the city, only the author note does.
