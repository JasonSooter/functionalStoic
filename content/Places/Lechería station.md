# Lechería station

Small railway station at Lechería, on the northern outskirts of [[Mexico City]]. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it is where Leon Trotsky's train arrives in January 1937, met in secret by Rivera, Frida and police.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)
Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m73hn4zdhre5w6mbbw69e0x0)

> In the predawn darkness, at a nearby restaurant, Rivera (temporarily released from the hospital for the occasion) and other members of the Mexican Trotskyite group joined forces with various government officials and police to form a welcoming party.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** In Chapter 13 the Trotskys' train from Tampico arrives at this small station on January 11, 1937. Rivera, out of hospital for the occasion, gathers with Trotskyites, officials and police in the dark, while decoy welcomers wait at the Riveras' San Angel house and at the main railroad station. Photographers catch Trotsky, Natalia and Frida stepping off the train before he is driven by side streets to Coyoacán.
- **Why it matters:** It is the staged, secretive start of Trotsky's Mexican exile, which opens the Trotsky chapter of Frida's life.
