## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx338jfqz426k1vvajxr2p)

> Hagen, today, is a large and thriving town. Then, in 1957, it was no more than half a dozen corrugated-iron buildings.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** The team flies to the small government station to seek permission from the District Commissioner to enter the Jimi Valley. The DC sets conditions and sends a radio message to Griffin, and the permission leads to their trek.
- **Why it matters:** A bureaucratic gateway visited briefly; its significance is as the seat of authority.
