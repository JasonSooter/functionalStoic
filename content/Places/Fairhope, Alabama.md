# Fairhope, Alabama

Small town on Mobile Bay, Alabama. In Matthew Teague's Esquire essay it is where his wife Nicole is dying of cancer and his friend Dane moves in to help.

## The Friend: Love Is Not a Big Enough Word — Matthew Teague (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.esquire.com/lifestyle/a34905/matthew-teague-wife-cancer-essay/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g0e8tqnvn8tjgd2vmz405s)

> we were living in a small town called Fairhope, Alabama

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Most of the essay happens in the couple's house in Fairhope: the bedroom with the IV pump, the bathroom floor where Teague breaks down, and the porch where his friend Dane sits up at night after moving in to help.
- **Why it matters:** The house is the stage for the friendship the essay is about.
