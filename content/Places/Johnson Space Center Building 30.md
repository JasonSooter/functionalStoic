# Johnson Space Center Building 30

## Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir (2021)
Source: [[Project Hail Mary]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/611060/project-hail-mary-by-andy-weir/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jx9qfsdevwkfmagrqfkd2g)

> I left the NBL and walked to Building 30. They would have sent a car if I’d asked, but I didn’t want one. It was only a ten-minute walk.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Grace teaches Astrophage biology (the Krebs cycle) to astronauts DuBois and Shapiro in a small conference room. The scene is mostly comic: DuBois announces his sexual relationship with Shapiro.
- **Why it matters:** Minor but real setting; the text gives no description beyond a conference room, security and a bathroom down the hall.
