# Union Canal (Scotland)

Contour canal from Falkirk to Edinburgh in [[Scotland]], which appears in Wesley Crump's article on the Falkirk Wheel as the upper canal, about 115 feet above the Forth and Clyde Canal.

## The Hidden Engineering Behind the Falkirk Wheel — Wesley Crump (2025)

Source: [[The Hidden Engineering Behind the Falkirk Wheel]] · [Original](https://practical.engineering/blog/2025/11/17/the-hidden-engineering-behind-the-falkirk-wheel) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crdbcfxk2mnnqs9vkyhz7z)

> The Union Canal connected the cities of Falkirk and Edinburgh, opening up a route for coal and other minerals from the mines and quarries around Lanarkshire to the capital.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** Introduced as the second canal, which sits 'about 115 feet or 38 meters' above the Forth and Clyde; the height difference required the original 11-lock staircase and later the [[Falkirk Wheel]].
- **Why it matters:** The level gap between the two canals is the engineering problem the story turns on.
