# Ithaca, New York

City at the south end of Cayuga Lake, home of Cornell University. Frederic Ives learned printing there before inventing halftone.

> Ives got his start as a printer’s apprentice in Ithaca, New York. After two years of learning the ins and outs of the printing process, he began managing the photographic laboratory at nearby Cornell University.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Frederic Ives learns printing as an apprentice in Ithaca, then runs Cornell's photographic lab and experiments there through the 1870s. He wakes in Ithaca in 1881 with the halftone process fully worked out.
- **Why it matters:** It is the setting of the case study the article uses to illustrate the creative process, though the article does not dwell on the place itself.
