# Museum of Modern Art

Modern art museum on West 53rd Street in Midtown [[Manhattan]]. In Aoibheann Sweeney's novel Miranda walks through a Mondrian show there and feels for the first time that she could change everything.

## Among Other Things, I've Taken Up Smoking — Aoibheann Sweeney (2007)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/among-other-things-ive-taken-up-smoking-9781101202227)

> after a while I took the subway up to the Museum of Modern Art, just for the sake of going somewhere. It was in midtown, near the row of swanky department stores on Fifth Avenue.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Urged by Walter to visit museums, Miranda takes the subway up one Sunday "just for the sake of going somewhere" and works through a Mondrian exhibition, from the still lifes and _Flowering Trees_ to the grid canvases. The rooms of black lines and primary colours give her a sense that she could change everything, and set her thinking about her father's habit of keeping things while letting her mother go.
- **Why it matters:** It is a quiet turning point in Miranda's education in the city: art she meets on her own terms rather than through her father's classics. Afterwards she walks back down through Forty-second Street to the institute.
