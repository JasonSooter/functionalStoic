# Sher Qila

Village in Punial, Ghizer valley, Pakistan, seat of the Raja of Punial. The emotional high point of the Gilgit stay in _Full Tilt_.

## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)
Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6n2tgbz9h9hrp9ghr2jm0)

> This is quite the most fantastic set-up I’ve ever met.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Led by a horseman to the Raja of Punial's new bungalow across a suspension bridge, she stays several days. She tours the farmland, visits two mosques, throws in the ball at a Sher Quila v Gulapur polo match, reads the Raja's letters, and is offered a farm.
- **Why it matters:** The emotional high point of the Gilgit stay: an idyllic, near-paradise community whose Raja she greatly admires, which she contrasts with Swat.
