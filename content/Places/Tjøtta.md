# Tjøtta

Island village in Helgeland, Nordland, Norway. In Nordic Tales it is home to Jack of Sjöholm, whose fishing crew sails north and is wrecked.

## Nordic Tales — Chronicle Books (2017)

Source: [Original](https://chroniclebooks.com/products/nordic-tales) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fydasvtdgbqvcqh7639beq)

> Now there was once a boat’s crew from Thjöttö in Helgeland, which had put to sea, and worked its way right up to the East Lofotens.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 'Jack of Sjöholm and the Gan-Finn', a crew from Thjöttö fishes in vain off the East Lofotens, and Jack urges them north to Finmark, where a storm wrecks the boat. After his escape Jack is set down near his boathouse at Sjöholm, builds the first of his new boats there and makes his pact with the Draug.
- **Why it matters:** Jack's home island is where the story starts and ends; its boat-building and fishing life drive the plot.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The book spells it Thjöttö; Sjöholm is Jack's farm there.
