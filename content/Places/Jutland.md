## The post I didn't want to write… — Helen Russell

Source: [Original](https://mshelenrussell.substack.com/p/the-post-i-didnt-want-to-write) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f0q0trhjmgg2wkbnt35gak)

> I had all three of my children in Jutland - each one born into the Danish healthcare system that, to this day, leaves me in awe.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Russell writes that she had all three of her children in Jutland and that its endless skies and wide, flat farmland surprised her. It is the home she is now leaving for the UK.
- **Why it matters:** Jutland, not Copenhagen, is the Denmark Russell knew; its rural character is part of what she says she will miss. The pin is the peninsula's representative point (approximate).
