## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx3gvhqks1daw3m54d7zn6)

> We stayed in the little village of Lomaloma.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** A thatched-house village by a lagoon that Attenborough remembers as a storybook Polynesian paradise. The crew lives there, fishing and drinking kava, and goes to a shallow inland lake for a priest-led fish-catching ceremony in which all-night swimming stirs gas from the mud and brings the fish to the surface.
- **Why it matters:** A little-known custom is filmed here, and the lake episode is the chapter's most distinctive ritual.
