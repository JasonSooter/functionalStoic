## Real Change Starts from the Ground Up — Barack Obama (2016)

Source: [Original](https://medium.com/the-white-house/real-change-starts-from-the-ground-up-9a1e74f585b3) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvr7jp8h15wvbbr8fyvybt)

> My journey to the White House began as a community organizer in a poor neighborhood on the South Side of Chicago, working with people to help improve their lives.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Obama opens by placing the start of his career in a poor South Side neighborhood, where he worked as a community organizer. The experience taught him that change comes from ordinary people organizing locally.
- **Why it matters:** Origin point of the essay's thesis, though the neighborhood itself is not described in detail.
