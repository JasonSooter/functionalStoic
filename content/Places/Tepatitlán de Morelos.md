# Tepatitlán de Morelos

Town in the Los Altos highlands of Jalisco. In The Underdogs Demetrio's band passes through it on the road toward Guadalajara.

## The Underdogs: A Novel of the Mexican Revolution — Mariano Azuela (1915)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1209983W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k8z8b8j8bc9zrc2d90767h)

> "Well, mate, we're almost to Guadalajara," Venancio said, glancing over the smiling row of houses in Tepatitlan nestling against the hillside.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** The troop rides in after leaving before dawn, turns schools into barracks, and Demetrio lodges in the chapel of an abandoned church. The soldiers loot, and a widower complains of his corn being taken; Camilla gets Demetrio to sign an order, which Blondie later turns into a beating.
- **Why it matters:** Shows the army preying on civilians as it marches toward the sierra, and Tepatitlan later becomes Demetrio's example of the complaining peon.
