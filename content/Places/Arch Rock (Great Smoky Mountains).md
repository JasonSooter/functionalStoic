# Arch Rock (Great Smoky Mountains)

Stone arch on the Alum Cave Trail in the Great Smoky Mountains, Tennessee, where Bob Doucette's Mount LeConte hike turns steep.

## Hiking Tennessee’s Mount LeConte — Bob Doucette (2015)

Source: [Original](https://proactiveoutside.wordpress.com/2015/12/08/hiking-tennessees-mount-leconte/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1k6nzscbnkvq7rsbaqzw)

> The trail steepened as we approached a feature called Arch Rock, which has a cleverly placed stone staircase that goes through the arch and begins the first steep pitch of the hike.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** Arch Rock is where the climb gets serious: a stone staircase passes through the arch. The author says this is where hikers realize the hike is harder than the 4,000 ft trailhead suggests.
- **Why it matters:** A dwelt-on waypoint with its own photo caption and route description, but a short stop.
