## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)

Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbk5w8xdbfcfab04x2e6mw)

> As if we weren’t convinced, Shoup has a final morality lesson to teach, and that is the story of the two Southern California shopping districts of Old Pasadena and Westwood Village.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Old Pasadena installed 690 new meters, allowed in-lieu fees and spent meter revenue locally. It revived and tripled sales-tax receipts in six years. Residents of Westwood now drive to Old Pasadena to shop.
- **Why it matters:** Old Pasadena is the success half of Shoup's parking comparison and the origin of the parking benefit district idea. The deal that returned meter revenue to the district made the policy politically workable.
