## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling
Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20831115W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e32c6x6chcgj7k8fq2shdj)

> I hurried through the slushy snow, clutching a bundle of documents, on my way to the Karolinska Institute, where I was to be interviewed for the position of Chair in International Health.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In 1996 Rosling uses his twenty minutes to teach eight professors his bubble graph rather than argue his qualifications. He does not get the chair but is offered a six-year senior lectureship and later a professorship.
- **Why it matters:** Career turning point: the bubble graph, not research, makes him a professor.
