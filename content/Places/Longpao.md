## Capturing the Plight of Rural China’s ‘Left Behind’ Children — Joseph Bien-Kahn (2016)

Source: [Original](http://www.good.is/posts/china-left-behind-children) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjrb0xqvj1nhtz4p4eje46)

> A few months later, they were all in Longpao, a rural village near Nanjing, where they made the 18-minute “Left Behind,” released earlier this month.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The filmmakers and Xinyi Hua went to Longpao, a rural village near Nanjing, to shoot the short documentary and run the mentorship program with a small school. They were the first foreigners many children had seen and were welcomed into villagers' homes.
- **Why it matters:** The village is the case study for the left-behind children issue, where grandparents raise children while parents work in cities.
