# Huize Katwijk

A former Catholic boarding school on the Raamweg in [[The Hague]]. In _The Son and Heir_ it is the elite school where Frans Münninghoff withdraws, fails and turns toward Nazism.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85b0544rhcrnjbvx9b45gr4)

> Huize Katwijk had the air of an English boarding school, with manicured fields for sports, but students were still required to spend nearly three hours a day in Mass.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Frans moves here from Oss in summer 1933, among boys from wealthy Catholic families with names like Van Hövell tot Westerflier. He is often absent and depressed and hangs a portrait of Tsar Nicholas II above his bed. In early summer 1937 the director, Father Albers, writes that Frans cannot repeat his second year a third time and is "overpowered by tempting ideas". The twins later attend the school too, and a half-Jewish classmate from here hides at the family villa in [[Voorburg]] during the war.
- **Why it matters:** The school is where the Old Boss's plan to make his son a Dutch gentleman fails; the elite he was meant to join instead confirms Frans's sense that he does not belong.
