# Crozet Islands

The Crozet Islands are a remote sub-Antarctic archipelago in the southern Indian Ocean, part of the French Southern and Antarctic Lands. In _Moby-Dick_ the Pequod meets the whaler Goney off the 'Crozetts' and later crosses meadows of brit nearby.

## Moby-Dick — Herman Melville (1851)

Source: [[Exploration of Moby Dick]] · [[Moby Dick]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL102749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m771wx6kwczw8x12fgqcst)

> South-eastward from the Cape, off the distant Crozetts, a good cruising ground for Right Whalemen, a sail loomed ahead, the Goney (Albatross) by name.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In Chapter 52 the Pequod meets the Goney, a Nantucket whaler long absent and bleached like a walrus skeleton. Ahab hails to ask if she has seen the White Whale, but her captain drops his trumpet overboard and the ships part, and Ahab orders the helm kept on round the world. In Chapter 58, steering north-eastward from the Crozetts, the Pequod passes through vast yellow meadows of brit with Right Whales feeding like mowers, which leads Ishmael to the 'insular Tahiti' in the soul.
- **Why it matters:** Setting of the first of the ship-meetings about Moby Dick, a failed exchange that sets the pattern of the gams to come. Its remoteness underlines how far the Pequod is from home.
