## The tiger is in the library — Shaun Usher (2024)
Source: [[The Tiger Is in the Library]] · [Original](https://news.lettersofnote.com/p/the-tiger-is-in-the-library) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbgp1qa38xekke49jkrecg)

> I was sitting in the porch of the Pwlldu Inn on a cold, sunny afternoon, eating an unnaturally large sandwich and sipping at a quart mug

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Dylan Thomas, writing in December 1934, describes sitting on the inn's porch with a sandwich and a quart mug when a huge, fat rat stamps up and stares at him. He throws it cheese and decides it must be the Father of all Rats.
- **Why it matters:** It is the sole physical setting of the letter's anecdote. The text itself gives no region; the identification with Pwlldu on the Gower coast near Swansea (Thomas's home area) is outside knowledge and should be verified. A different pub would leave the comic story intact.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real place per outside knowledge; the text only names the inn and gives no county or country.
