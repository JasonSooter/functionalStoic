> Columbus Circle, built on the southwest corner of Central Park, New York

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** The article opens its history with Columbus Circle, built at the south-west corner of Central Park in 1905 and widely seen as the first large traffic circle, an alternative to the four-way intersection; the big rotaries it inspired gave circles a reputation for danger.
- **Why it matters:** It anchors the origin story of American distrust of circles, though the place itself gets only a sentence.
