# Eden Park

Coloured suburb on the East Rand in Ekurhuleni, south-east of Johannesburg. In Born a Crime it is where Trevor Noah grows up after his mother buys a house there to get away from Soweto.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6yq3vmtr14ttweerg3pn55g)

> Eden Park was a colored neighborhood adjacent to several black townships on the East Rand. Half-colored and half-black, she figured, like us.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** in Chapter 5 Trevor's mother moves them to Eden Park, away from Soweto's gossip and her family. It is flat and nearly treeless, with "the same dusty red-clay dirt and grass as Soweto". Its streets are named after cars (Jaguar, Ferrari, Honda), and their tiny house is "at the bend in the road right off Toyota Street". In Chapter 1 it is home base for the church circuit. The broken Volkswagen sits in its driveway, and on many evenings they drive home through riots, because Eden Park sits beside Thokoza and Katlehong, sites of the Inkatha–ANC clashes. After the Orange Grove fire, Abel moves in here (Chapter 6), and the family's cats and the dog Fufi live here (Chapter 7).
- **Why it matters:** the suburb's in-between status matches the family's: a mixed-race household in a coloured neighbourhood where they never quite fit in. Its closeness to the East Rand violence is part of the backdrop to the early chapters.
