# Alert Bay

Kwakwakaʼwakw village on Cormorant Island, British Columbia; in _Life on Air_ the BBC films a potlatch in its ceremonial big house.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy7azp1pc658mw9mqxpqbw)

> The strength of Alert Bay's respect for tribal custom is immediately plain to the visitor.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The crew film Arthur Dick's potlatch in the huge windowless ceremonial hall amid rival film-team tension and council disputes. Their transformer blows and plunges the hall into darkness, nearly causing a fight. Two hours later the dances resume and filming is completed.
- **Why it matters:** A dramatic episode combining the town's history of suppressed potlatches with a near-disaster for the production.
