## The Soul of an Old Machine — scott-rosenberg (2016)

Source: [Original](https://shift.newco.co/the-soul-of-an-old-machine-20a297b1fb8c) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy9bcjf8ztgs8710taerne)

> Greenville, South Carolina was a textile manufacturing hub till the industry moved overseas.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Greenville is a former textile town that became a centre of advanced automated manufacturing, home to the largest BMW plant in the world. It kept its investment and tax base but has below-average per capita income and high poverty because the jobs demand skills its blue-collar population lacks.
- **Why it matters:** Used as a case study for the future of manufacturing: higher productivity with less human labor.
