## The Comfort Crisis — Mr. Money Mustache (2023)

Source: [Original](https://www.mrmoneymustache.com/2023/07/23/the-comfort-crisis/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmqbj40k20jsfqsnhn7kfd)

> I drove three hours deeper into the mountains and up the steep dirt road to arrive at my second friend’s piece of land – a plot of forest in the mountains just outside of Durango.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** This is where the author spends a week building a cabin with a friend, with no indoor plumbing, limited water, heat, tree pollen, and sleeping in his car. The hardship leaves him feeling more alive. It ends with a thank-you dinner at a Mexican restaurant.
- **Why it matters:** It is the main setting of the voluntary-hardship experience that the essay turns into its lesson; the exact plot is unnamed, so the coordinate is for Durango.
