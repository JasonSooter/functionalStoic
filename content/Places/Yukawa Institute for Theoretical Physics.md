# Yukawa Institute for Theoretical Physics

Theoretical physics institute of Kyoto University, Japan. In _Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!_ Feynman works there for some months and gives up on learning Japanese.

## "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!": Adventures of a Curious Character — Richard P. Feynman (1985)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL514629W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kfqwkj20nnkr4a0h4bghgf)

> After visiting a number of universities I spent some months at the Yukawa Institute in Kyoto. I really enjoyed working there. Everything was so nice: You’d come to work, take your shoes off, and someone would come and serve you tea in the morning when you felt like it.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** "After visiting a number of universities I spent some months at the Yukawa Institute in Kyoto." He enjoys taking his shoes off and being served tea, and takes Japanese lessons every day. Asking the office how to say "solve the Dirac Equation", he finds the verb must change with the level of politeness, and decides to stop learning Japanese.
- **Why it matters:** The institute is his working base in Japan and the setting of the chapter's punchline about language and status.
