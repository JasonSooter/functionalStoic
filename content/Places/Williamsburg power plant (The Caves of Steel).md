## The Caves of Steel — Isaac Asimov (1954)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL46401W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hj3h68an4ff789ksg9mb7a)

> The alpha-sprayer was taken from the Williamsburg power plant. You and I passed through the Williamsburg power plant yesterday. We were seen, and the fact will come out.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Shaking off followers, Baley leads Daneel through the humming plant with its red-lined danger zones and its hot ash piped out under the ocean. The alpha-sprayer that kills R. Sammy turns out to have come from the same plant, which frames Baley, and he later shows the Commissioner took it from there.
- **Why it matters:** The plant ties the chase to the murder weapon and gives the frame-up its logic.
- **Real vs. fictional:** A fictional plant in a real New York neighbourhood name.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Clues: named only as 'the Williamsburg power plant' inside New York City; there is also a near riot 'in Williamsburg'. No street is given; pinned at the Williamsburg neighbourhood of Brooklyn.
