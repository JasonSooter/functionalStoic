## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6mx549twwq2wezdbptse7)

> Murree is Pakistan’s only hill-station and the road was pretty busy all day as rich families in sleek cars sped up from the plains to their mountain residences. During summer the population of Murree rises from 15,000 to 50,000 so it’s not exactly my sort of place …

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** She climbs from 1,300 to 7,040 feet, walking the last sixteen miles, and reaches the PWD rest-house exhausted at night. The cool pine woods and familiar flowers give relief from the plains heat, though she shares her room with a small snake.
- **Why it matters:** A cool hill-station interlude and the start of her detour into Azad Kashmir.
