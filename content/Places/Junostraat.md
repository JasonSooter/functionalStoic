# Junostraat

Street in the Binckhorst industrial area of [[The Hague]]. In _The Son and Heir_ Frans and Mimousse live on a leaky houseboat in the harbour of the family's Poortensdijk oil refinery here.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85na07yhdwgk7t98jazdyj9)

> which was anchored in the harbor of the Poortensdijk oil refinery on Junostraat in The Hague.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After his release, Frans finds shelter with Mimousse on the _Juno_, an old, leaky houseboat in the refinery harbour, because the Old Boss will not have her at Briva Latvija. He takes an administrative job at Poortensdijk, his father's trading company, while his uncle Wim runs the refinery; from the boat he has a clear view of the factory he will inherit a few years later.
- **Why it matters:** The houseboat sums up the "armed peace" between father and son: Frans is kept on the family payroll, within sight of his inheritance, but outside the house.
