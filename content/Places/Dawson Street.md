# Dawson Street

Street in central Dublin running from Nassau Street to St Stephen's Green. In _The Country Girls Trilogy_ Kate meets Eugene again by chance outside its bookshop.

## The Country Girls Trilogy — Edna O'Brien (1986)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL69252W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m769mwa7788v34jdna6x05)

> As usual I went to the bookshop at the bottom of Dawson Street where I had a free read every week.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Kate reads free each Wednesday at the bookshop at the bottom of Dawson Street. Coming down its stone steps she meets Eugene by chance, shelters from the rain with him, and goes to a tea shop across the road. This leads to their dinner and the Bull Wall walk.
- **Why it matters:** The chance meeting turns a brush at a reception into the central love affair of The Lonely Girl. A bookshop suits Kate, who reads everywhere.
