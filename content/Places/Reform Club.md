# Reform Club

Gentlemen's club at 104 Pall Mall, London. Fogg makes his £20,000 wager at its whist table in _Around the World in Eighty Days_ and wins it there with seconds to spare.

## Around the World in Eighty Days — Jules Verne (1872)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1100007W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ne39nj5txx8d51mbbyn6cf)

> reached the Reform Club, an imposing edifice in Pall Mall, which could not have cost less than three millions.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In Chapter III Fogg takes his meals and plays whist at the Reform Club, and the talk about the Bank of England robbery leads to his £20,000 bet that he can go round the world in eighty days, reporting back on 21 December at 8.45 pm. On the deadline evening crowds fill Pall Mall around the club while his opponents play a rubber in the great saloon, and Fogg walks in at the last moment.
- **Why it matters:** The wager that drives the whole plot is made and settled here.
