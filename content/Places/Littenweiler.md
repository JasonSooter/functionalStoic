# Littenweiler

Eastern district of [[Freiburg]], Germany, in the Dreisam valley. In the Wikipedia history of Freiburg's trams it shows how the network first grew to absorb a new suburb and then shrank in the 1960s.

> In 1925, a further extension went into operation in the suburb of Littenweiler, which had been incorporated in 1914.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** The extension, delayed eleven years by the First World War, opened in 1925; the article says the promised tram was one of the reasons the community gave up its independence. In 1962 the track was cut back to a turning loop at Laßbergstraße, so Littenweiler lost its tram, part of a contraction that shrank the network by almost 30 percent. Line 1 still ends at Laßbergstraße.
- **Why it matters:** It is the article's clearest case of the network's growth and retreat in one place.
