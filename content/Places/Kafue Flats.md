## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2ptnzptxn67e0c8qa7vg)

> Herds of them, tens of thousands strong, lived on the flooded plains around the Kafue River, a tributary of the Zambezi that joins it from the north.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The team watches and films red lechwe antelope, with splayed hooves, grazing in swamps and bounding away in great splashing leaps. Their herds migrate with the seasonal flood.
- **Why it matters:** The wildlife highlight of the trip, and the lechwe's seasonal movement is paralleled with that of the Barotse people.
