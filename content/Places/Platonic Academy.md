## A Primer on Plato: His Life, Works, and Philosophy — Brett and Kate McKay (2016)

Source: [Original](https://www.artofmanliness.com/2016/07/10/a-primer-on-plato-his-life-works-and-philosophy/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jx6z5qd0xy9kywexb8gync)

> After returning to Greece, Plato founded one of civilization’s earliest institutions of higher learning: the Academy. Built outside the walls of Athens in the mid-380s BC, the Academy was an exclusive organization where philosophers, scientists, and thinkers of all kinds pondered and argued about the nature of the universe and moral truth.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Plato founds the Academy after his travels; it is an informal gathering place for dialectic rather than a formal school. He writes the Republic there, teaches for the rest of his life, and Aristotle joins at 18.
- **Why it matters:** The institutional home of Plato's mature work and a model for later higher learning.
