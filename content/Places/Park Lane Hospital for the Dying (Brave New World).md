## Brave New World — Aldous Huxley (1932)

Source: [[Brave New World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL64365W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvarh3sgp048x70ts1tgvn)

> The Park Lane Hospital for the Dying was a sixty-story tower of primrose tiles.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** John visits Linda in Ward 81 on the seventeenth floor, where she dies amid television and synthetic music while death-conditioned twin children swarm the ward. In the vestibule he then tries to stop the Delta staff taking their soma ration and throws the tablets out of a window; the riot draws in Helmholtz and Bernard before the police quell it.
- **Why it matters:** Linda's death and the failed 'freedom' revolt are the turning point that leads to the arrest and the final debate with Mustapha Mond.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The hospital is fictional; Park Lane is real.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The text names "The Park Lane Hospital for the Dying" and the address "Three Park Lane" but no other landmark. Pinned at the Park Lane street point.
