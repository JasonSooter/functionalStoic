## Why Is Everyone Waiting Hours for Frozen Yogurt? Why Am I? — Amy X. Wang (2026)

Source: [[Why Is Everyone Waiting Hours for Frozen Yogurt Why Am I]] · [Original](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/16/magazine/frozen-yogurt-lines-myka.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5crc1cq9a2qqsankw49cwq5)

> On one of the hottest Saturdays of the year, under a noontime sun, I counted 74 people waiting in front of me at Myka, a noncommitally Mediterranean yogurt shop on Seventh Avenue in the West Village that had set up a maze of blue velvet ropes outside its entrance.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The writer queues for about an hour behind 74 people outside Myka, interviews others in line, unpicks the viral hype and the chain's expansion, buys a \$14 yogurt and eats it on a stranger's stoop down the block.
- **Why it matters:** The shop and its line are the piece's set scene; the sidewalk queue carries the argument about modern 'third places'.
