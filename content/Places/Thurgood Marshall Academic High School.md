## Beyond Data: Building Empathy in Adults Through Student Shadow Days — Katrina Schwartz (2016)

Source: [Original](https://ww2.kqed.org/mindshift/2016/03/22/beyond-data-building-empathy-in-adults-with-student-shadow-days/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j7v22h35q83pamv7prn9yc)

> Marthaa Torres, principal of Thurgood Marshall Academic High School in the Bayview neighborhood, had done one shadow day before and found it enormously helpful to create a more well-rounded picture of students.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** Principal Marthaa Torres shadows 10th-grader Anderson Tejada through a full school day, rushing between classes and ending tired and starving. She observes that he is not always challenged, and sees how teachers sometimes do and sometimes don't engage him. The experience prompts her to rethink group work and to consider peer classroom observation.
- **Why it matters:** The school is the main scene where the shadow-day empathy experiment plays out.
