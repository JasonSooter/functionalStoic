## The $40BN Plan to Save The World’s Fastest Sinking City — The B1M (2025)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B7PHRVQgTKM) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqxkhe3zy1r2qny2rt3gba)

> Jakarta is the largest city in Southeast Asia, but it’s sinking at a rate of up to 15 centimetres a year

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The video explains why Jakarta, a megacity of more than 11M people on a low-lying coastal plain at the mouth of 13 rivers, is sinking up to 15 cm a year, mainly from groundwater extraction. It traces the Dutch founding of Batavia, post-1945 population growth and informal riverbank settlement, then the government's two responses: a sea wall and a new capital.
- **Why it matters:** The city is the article's whole subject: a real-world case of a capital literally sinking and being partly abandoned.

## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx2e1fppwh6bm16t8spd2y)

> When we landed in Jakarta, our letter did us no good whatsoever.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** On arrival the team's filming gear is impounded because their letter is only a request. Visits to the Ministry of the Interior and Natural Resources bring a ban on going to Aru and on exporting a Komodo dragon, and they learn that more permits keep being required. Jakarta is also where the collection is flown to London at the end.
- **Why it matters:** The bureaucratic obstacle course here forces the change of plan; the city itself is only sketched.
