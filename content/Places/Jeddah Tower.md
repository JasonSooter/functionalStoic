## Why the Jeddah Tower Won’t be as Tall as You Think — The B1M (2026)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vVfEs9wrW98) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5breh6aaa6p2pyr75yeh3ze)

> work for the Jeddah Tower began in 2008,   a year before the neighbouring Burj Khalifa opened for business.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** First planned at 1,600 m and then cut to about a kilometre, the tower is built around a Y-shaped core and switches to steel above level 167; its spire could exceed 300 m, a very large "vanity height".
- **Why it matters:** It is the framing question of the video: how tall a building "really" is when much of its height is unusable spire.
