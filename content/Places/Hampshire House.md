# Hampshire House

Hotel and apartment tower on Central Park South in [[Manhattan]]. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it is the building Dorothy Hale jumped from in 1938, the subject of Frida's _Suicide of Dorothy Hale_.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)
Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m73vwcvw5rqjgp3wq2p2rasw)

> At about 6:00 A.M., Dorothy Hale had jumped out of the window of her top-story suite in the Hampshire House.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Clare Boothe Luce, who commissioned the painting, recalls how Hale gave a last cocktail party and was found dead the next morning after jumping from her suite on October 21, 1938, wearing a black velvet dress and a corsage of yellow roses from Isamu Noguchi. Frida's painting shows the fall in three stages: a tiny figure at the building's high window, a falling body among clouds, and the body on the ground.
- **Why it matters:** The book reads the gory picture, painted during Frida's separation from Diego, as a commission she turned into a statement of her own despair. The building's window is the starting point of the image.
