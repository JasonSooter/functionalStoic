# Gadsden County, Florida

Rural county west of Tallahassee, Florida, with a large Black population. In How to Be an Antiracist (Ibram X. Kendi) it is his evidence of racist vote spoilage in 2000.

## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fzcxn4h1gg43vmqr4w4yt9)

> Gadsden County, next to Tallahassee, had Florida’s highest percentage of Black voters and the highest spoilage rate.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Kendi cites the county's high share of Black voters and its high rate of spoiled ballots in the 2000 election, arguing that only racist policy explains the gap.
- **Why it matters:** A concrete case in his argument that the election was shaped by racist policy.
