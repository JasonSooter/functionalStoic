# Sa'ir

Palestinian town near Hebron in the southern West Bank. A setting in Colum McCann's _Apeirogon_: Bassam's childhood home in the hills near Sa'ir and Hebron; flag, stones, grenades, arrest at seventeen.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjqjy2rn7e1h8xmqn4hnce)

> I lived in a cave but not a cave as you might think of it—we had shelves full of books, carpets on the walls, it was cool in summer, warm in winter, always alive with voices and good cooking, we were happy there, we had what we wanted.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Bassam says his parents were both born near Sa'ir, a village close to Hebron, and that his father raised goats in the hills. He grew up in a furnished cave, raised the Palestinian flag in the school playground as a boy and threw stones at soldiers, and with friends found discarded grenades in a cave before his arrest at seventeen.
- **Why it matters:** The hill home near Hebron is where Bassam's resistance and later prison years begin; it grounds him in rural Palestinian life, unlike the town settings elsewhere.
