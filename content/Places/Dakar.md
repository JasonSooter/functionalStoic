## Could a grain older than the wheel be the future of food? – Bill Gates — Bill Gates (2024)
Source: [[Could a Grain Older Than the Wheel Be the Future of Food]] · [Original](https://www.gatesnotes.com/Fonio) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fcp23fzangwyk8c388zsa9)

> recently partnered with a Senegalese company called CAA to build a commercial processing facility right in Dakar.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Gates describes a commercial fonio processing facility built in Dakar by Terra Ingredients and the Senegalese company CAA. He visited it on his trip and saw it help local farmers earn more by removing the hand-hulling bottleneck. It is also where chef Pierre Thiam's lost-crops conference is to be held.
- **Why it matters:** Dakar is the one place the author visits and describes at first hand; the facility is the article's evidence that fonio can scale commercially. The argument would be the same elsewhere in Senegal, but the visit is specific to Dakar.
