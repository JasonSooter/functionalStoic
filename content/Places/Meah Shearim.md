## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjqmfve5wha6grxvwtbmxx)

> He bundled her in the car to a clockmaker’s house, an elderly Armenian woman who lived in the Meah Shearim district.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Smadar's waterlogged watch, her late grandfather Peled's Timex, sends Rami to a clockmaker's home full of working clocks. Smadar notices the clocks in the back rooms are an hour off, and Rami reasons they keep Armenian time, perhaps so that what happened there has not yet happened here.
- **Why it matters:** A small tender scene of father and daughter whose clocks image the book's themes of time, exile and memory.
