# P'tit Train du Nord

Rail trail of about 200 km in the Laurentians, Quebec, from Saint-Jérôme to Mont-Laurier. A cycling blog rides it over three days.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** The post introduces the former railway as a 200-km rail trail between Mont-Laurier and Saint-Jérôme. A shuttle takes the riders to the northern end, and they ride lakeside scenery on a paved, sometimes bumpy path to Nominingue.

## P’tit Train du Nord day 3: A 9-mile(!) hill — alliumstozinnias (2024)

Source: [Original](https://exploringbybike.com/2024/09/24/ptit-train-du-nord-day-3-a-9-mile-hill/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5f1vsg4m5dr8krq66f4exaa)

> But right after Mont-Tremblant came the climb. It kept going. And going. I thought it might be 12 km long.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Day 3 retraces the trail from Mont-Tremblant to Saint-Jérôme, with an unexpected 15-km (9-mile) climb right after Mont-Tremblant, then flat and gentle downhill as asphalt turns to crushed stone.
- **Why it matters:** It completes the series and gives the trail's one real climb.
