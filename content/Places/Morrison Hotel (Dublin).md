# Morrison Hotel (Dublin)

Hotel on Ormond Quay, on the north bank of the Liffey in Dublin. In Greenlights it is where Matthew McConaughey has the dream that sends him to Africa.

## Greenlights — Matthew McConaughey (2020)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL21911019W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m700jd7q3ygc3h75tkvgnakh)

> I was getting some much-needed sleep one night in the Morrison Hotel on the north side of the Liffey River in Dublin, Ireland, when…

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Three days after principal photography on Reign of Fire wraps, he is asleep in the Morrison Hotel when he has another vivid dream: floating down the Amazon, then surrounded by African tribesmen. He reads it as a sign to go to Africa; a few nights later, listening to Ali Farka Touré over an atlas, he decides to go to the bluesman's home town, Niafunké.
- **Why it matters:** The hotel is only where the dream happens, but the dream starts the book's Mali journey; any hotel would serve, though the book names it.
