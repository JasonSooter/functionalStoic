## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx34rajsjqnc1dvz754nb8)

> The path ahead broadened and opened out into the red-earthed open arena I had seen from the forest above. At least a thousand people were there awaiting us.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Tabibuga is a Government station in a red-earth clearing, run by patrol officer Barry Griffin, who lives in a one-room house on the ridge. Attenborough and Charles Lagus stay there six days, trade with the Milma and Maraka for animals, and learn that the station was founded to end a war between those two groups.
- **Why it matters:** Journey destination and base for the Jimi expedition; the book gives a detailed picture of life at a remote 1950s outpost.
