# San Miguel Regla

Village in Huasca de Ocampo, Hidalgo, northeast of Mexico City. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ Trotsky retreats to a hacienda farm nearby in July 1937, and Frida's visit there seems to end their affair.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)
Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m73hn975xfcvjstkyry0pa0g)

> On July 7, Trotsky left the Coyoacán house and went to stay at a farm that was part of a large hacienda near San Miguel Regla, approximately eighty miles northeast of Mexico City.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** In Chapter 13 Trotsky leaves the Casa Azul for the hacienda farm to ease the strain of the affair. On July 11 Frida drives out with Federico Marín; they lunch, drink, and tour old mines, the farmhouse and a basalt canyon before leaving at dusk. Ella Wolfe believes Frida ended the affair on this visit; Trotsky writes her a nine-page plea, and she says she is "very tired of the old man".
- **Why it matters:** It is where the Frida–Trotsky affair apparently ends and Trotsky turns back to Natalia.
