## Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World — David Epstein (2019)

Source: [[Range]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550188/range-by-david-epstein/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjsd9sa49egx3pfrv0t6x3)

> Individual farmers in isolated areas of what is now Uzbekistan had long survived by cultivating small gardens for food, and cotton for everything else.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In 1931 Alexander Luria travelled to remote villages in what is now Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan, then being forced into collective farming. He questioned villagers in teahouses and pastures and compared remote herders and farmers with collective farmers and students.
- **Why it matters:** The villages are the natural experiment that shows how modern work changes how people think; the argument depends on villages that had not yet been touched by restructuring serving as a control group.
