# Gare de l'Est

Mainline railway station in Paris, the terminus for trains to eastern France. VAG Freiburg notes that the summer Deutschlandticket deal for Grand Est stretches on selected TER routes all the way to this station.

## To France with the Deutschlandticket: Here's How — VAG Freiburg (2026)

Source: [Original](https://blog.vag-freiburg.de/mit-dem-deutschlandticket-nach-frankreich-so-gehts/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m64yyk0krhfg61dvch1ndyr8)

> Paris is not actually part of the Grand Est region, but the Gare de l’Est station lies at the edge of the area where TER Fluo connections are valid.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** The station is the furthest point the free summer ticket reaches: "on selected routes you can even travel all the way to Paris", but only by TER, never TGV. Trains between Paris Gare de l'Est and Châlons-en-Champagne, and between Paris Gare de l'Est, Troyes, Chaumont and Mulhouse, need a free seat reservation.
- **Why it matters:** It turns a regional offer into a free ride from Freiburg to Paris, the article's most striking destination.
