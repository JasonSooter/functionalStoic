## Walkable City — Jeff Speck (2012)

Source: [[Walkable City]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL36883695M/Walkable_City) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbks72q1zdhrpgc03w7zre)

> This continues to be the situation in Davenport, Iowa, where the big local lenders have long been unwilling to support the construction of housing on the very streets surrounding their offices

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Speck's planning team worked in downtown Davenport: banks would not finance downtown housing, so outside investors came in. Later he describes repricing its on-street parking to zero to fix a ghost-town effect, and the mayor decapitating a meter with the Jaws of Life.
- **Why it matters:** A midsized-city client example used twice, for housing finance and for the point that parking pricing works both ways.
