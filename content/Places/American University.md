# American University

University in northwest Washington, D.C. In How to Be an Antiracist (Ibram X. Kendi) it is where he founds the Antiracist Research and Policy Center.

## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g0f1ykce2t557f95a0590m)

> IN THE SUMMER of 2017, I moved to American University in the nation’s capital to found and direct the Antiracist Research and Policy Center.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Kendi moves to American University in 2017 to found the Center; that September a man posts Confederate-flag flyers with cotton balls outside his classroom.
- **Why it matters:** The new Center embodies his turn from ideas to antiracist policy, and the flyers show the resistance it meets.

## That Time I Went To Grad School For Free\* — Mrs. Frugalwoods (2015)

Source: [Original](https://www.frugalwoods.com/2015/08/26/that-time-i-went-to-grad-school-for-free/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gbgg6zgy219q59zvpmd365)

> I was accepted into my program of choice: the Master’s of Public Administration with a concentration in Nonprofit Management at American University.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** After her husband's job moved them from Cambridge to Washington in 2010, the author took a full-time job at the university and studied for an MPA in the evenings and at weekends, on campus every day of the week. Her employment earned the tuition remission that made the degree nearly free.
- **Why it matters:** The university is both employer and school, the whole mechanism of the 'free' degree.
