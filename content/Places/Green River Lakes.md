# Green River Lakes

Pair of lakes at the head of the Green River at the north end of Wyoming's Wind River Range and Bridger Wilderness. The Ryback brothers reach them at the end of a 48-hour forced march.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8besfbwf881a1dwz26t1bm9)

> We spent the remainder of the morning and the early afternoon charging over stretches of high scrub and through groves of quivering aspen toward the Green River Lakes.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After the night march over Union Pass, the brothers charge through high scrub and aspen, cross the Roaring Fork and reach the wooded shores of the larger of the two Green River Lakes. They had planned to rest there after the 48-hour marathon, but Eric, excited, pushes on along the lakeside to get into the mountains before nightfall.
- **Why it matters:** It is the goal of the forced march and the gateway to the Wind Rivers; Eric's refusal to rest deepens the rift with Tim.
