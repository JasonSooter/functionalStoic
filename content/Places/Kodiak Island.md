## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy9215tyfghf8jfzfphvqd)

> we went to Kodiak Island off the south coast of Alaska, where the grizzly bears are reputed to be the largest in the world.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On the open gravel flats around the estuaries, Attenborough walks behind Jeff Turner, who knows the bears individually. Speaking to camera with his back to a female bear, he hears her plunge into the stream and rise with a salmon.
- **Why it matters:** Source of the grizzly two-shot; dwelt on for danger and perfect timing.
