## How to Start a Successful Business Anywhere — Vero (2014)
Source: [Original](http://blog.getvero.com/start-a-business-anywhere/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5g0eehj5dv3ydcnaejtrmnn)

> After just six months we decided to move back home to Sydney, Australia.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** After founding Vero in San Francisco, the founders move back to Sydney after six months to bootstrap with the help of friends and family, and use the move to argue a company can succeed from anywhere.
- **Why it matters:** Sydney is the worked example of the post's argument.
