## [RODEN] Burnout? a TV Show, FM Radio, a Big Walk, and More — Craig Mod (2024)
Source: [Original](https://craigmod.com/roden/092/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fbgvby0qp4c0kpbwq3tb33)

> In the end, I said yes to the show as a gift to Morioka.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** Mod films a two-day TV show in Morioka with the celebrity Tamori, walking around town with him. Locals react with huge excitement and thank Mod afterward. He says he agreed to the show as a gift to the city and its shops.
- **Why it matters:** Morioka is what the whole TV episode is about, and the town is where Mod has neighbours and shops he loves. Set elsewhere the story of Tamori's reception would not exist.
