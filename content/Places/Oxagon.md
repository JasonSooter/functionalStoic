## Is The Line Really Dead? — The B1M (2025)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFVYgZMEOFg) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ce3frfqbc8346hd1bcgf05)

> It all begins here at The Oxagon, another of NEOM’s megaprojects

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the video:** The video says it all begins at Oxagon, where a desalination plant would supply seawater to a chain of concrete factories along The Line. Work began only in mid-2025, none of it exists yet, and there are unconfirmed reports the plant has been scrapped.
- **Why it matters:** If Oxagon's supply never arrives, the video argues, The Line cannot be built.
