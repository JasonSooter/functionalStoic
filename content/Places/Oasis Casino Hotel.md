# Oasis Casino Hotel

Former casino hotel on the outskirts of Jericho, in the West Bank. A subject in Colum McCann's _Apeirogon_: Short-lived Palestinian Authority casino near Jericho, closed to ordinary Palestinians and later shot up in the Intifada.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)
Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjqvx5a5e12dqx65n8vw9y)

> The Oasis Casino Hotel resort in Jericho was, for a short period, at the turn of the twenty-first century, one of the most successful gambling houses in the world.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The text describes the Palestinian Authority casino off Highway 1: its gaming tables, who was admitted and who was not, its windowless interior, and its end in the Second Intifada. Bassam's night drive passes it.
- **Why it matters:** A strange emblem of the occupation's contradictions: a casino closed to Palestinians who are not staff, destroyed by the Intifada. Its description is among the text's longest on any single building.
