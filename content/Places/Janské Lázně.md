# Janské Lázně

Spa town in the Krkonoše mountains of the [[Czech Republic]], the German-speaking Johannisbad of the interwar Sudetenland. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where Jimmy recovers from polio and breaks with his father.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85b06jz28sddx7353mjs3rc)

> Two weeks later, Omi and Jimmy moved into a suite at the Johannisbad spa.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After Jimmy catches polio, the family learns that the Roosevelt warm-water method is used in Johannisbad, and Omi, Jimmy and his twin Xeno move in with a Dutch tutor. The Sudeten-German salons talk of little but annexation to Hitler's Germany; after ten months the treatment works. Jimmy joins the gymnastics club Frisch-Fromm-Fröhlich-Frei, which resembles the Hitler Youth, and when an orchestra on a café terrace plays the Nazi Party anthem, he stays standing against his father's order and declares he will no longer be ordered around.
- **Why it matters:** The Sudeten spa shows a second son pulled toward Germany, and the terrace scene ends Jimmy's obedience to the Old Boss.
