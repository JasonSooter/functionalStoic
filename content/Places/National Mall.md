## Do We Want to Live in China’s World? — ChinaFile (2017)

Source: [Original](https://www.chinafile.com/viewpoint/do-we-want-live-chinas-world) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k5rhv08tes2j032xnrx7d3)

> Each weekday morning, I cross D.C.’s National Mall and pass a sign on Constitution Avenue bearing an epigram by the U.S. architect Daniel Burnham

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The essay opens and closes with the author's morning walk across the Mall past a sign quoting Daniel Burnham's 'Make No Little Plans.' The sign prompts him to contrast 20th-century American ambition with China's present big plans.
- **Why it matters:** The sign on the Mall is the essay's frame: it opens and closes the argument about American versus Chinese ambition.
