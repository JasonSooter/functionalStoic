# La Chacarita Cemetery

Large cemetery in the Chacarita district of [[Buenos Aires]]. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne visits Carlos Gardel's plaque-covered tomb in its walled city of the dead.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76e96dt6fw1h5znc0e2csvw)

> In another necropolis—la Chacarita—there is the grave of Carlos Gardel, the famous tango artist who died in a plane crash.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Right after Recoleta, Byrne describes this second necropolis, where tango singer Carlos Gardel's tomb is covered in commemorative plaques. He sees long avenues of tombs in art deco, Greco-Roman, gothic and modern styles, a slightly reduced copy of the city outside its high walls, with a few men sweeping and a few visitors bringing flowers.
- **Why it matters:** It pairs with Recoleta as the popular counterpart, and introduces the Gardel myth that Byrne later says he has had enough of.
