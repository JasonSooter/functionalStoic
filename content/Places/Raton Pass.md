# Raton Pass

Mountain pass on the Colorado–New Mexico line, crossed by the Santa Fe railway. In Captains Courageous (Rudyard Kipling) the Cheynes' special climbs it on the race east.

## At a glance

| Source | Author | Role | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|

## Captains Courageous — Rudyard Kipling (1897)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20173W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gaptn3tm4yp919zqxxqs06)

> up and up to the Raton Tunnel on the State line, whence they dropped rocking into La Junta

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** On the record run from San Diego to Boston, crews swing the private car up from Albuquerque past Glorieta and Springer to the Raton Tunnel on the state line, then drop to La Junta and on to Dodge City.
- **Why it matters:** The climax of the railway chapter's set piece of speed.
