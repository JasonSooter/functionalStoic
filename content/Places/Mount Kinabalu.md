## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx3jaj184ts2d8ybjmr2ja)

> The mountain stands thirty miles inland from the north-west coast of Borneo, not far from the Malaysian city of Kota Kinabalu.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Attenborough climbs it for its geology, sleeping in a hut and reaching the summit plateau before dawn. He describes the Donkey's Ears, ice-scored granite and the sunrise, concluding he has no doubts about leaving the desk.
- **Why it matters:** The emotional climax of the chapter: the place that confirms his career change.
