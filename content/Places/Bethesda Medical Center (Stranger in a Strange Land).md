# Bethesda Medical Center (Stranger in a Strange Land)

Medical center in Bethesda, Maryland, just northwest of Washington, D.C. It turns up in Stranger in a Strange Land (Robert A. Heinlein): guarded hospital suite where the Man from Mars is held and meets Jill Boardman.

## Stranger in a Strange Land — Robert A. Heinlein (1961)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL59688W)

> was installed in a suite in Bethesda Medical Center, transferred gently into a hydraulic bed,

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Valentine Michael Smith is installed in guarded suite K-12 of Bethesda Medical Center after the Champion lands. Nearly all of the early chapters (Nelson, Thaddeus, Frame, Nurse Jill Boardman's visit and the water ritual) unfold in its rooms, corridors and rooftop landing flat. Caxton later fears Smith may already have been removed from 'Bethesda Center'.
- **Why it matters:** The hospital is the book's first Earth setting and the place of Smith's quarantine-like captivity and first meeting with Jill; set elsewhere the Washington-area geography (Potomac, Alexandria taxi flight) would change, but the story depends more on its function as a secure ward than on the real site.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The hospital suite and its guards are Heinlein's invention; the pin is the town of Bethesda, Maryland, where the novel places the medical center. No highlight: Reader could not anchor one in this book.
