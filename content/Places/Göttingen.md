## Poverty What Does It Mean To Be Poor? — DER SPIEGEL (2016)

Source: [Original](https://www.spiegel.de/international/tomorrow/a-1093371.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jvr4m42bcxc2vhpv5w7d6z)

> They live in a white house near the university town of Göttingen. Rabbits hop around in the backyard, a wooden horse stands on the patio and buzzing can be heard from a beehive.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The Ehlers family's pleasant house looks middle-class, yet the mortgage is unaffordable with a fourth child and they fall through a gap in the welfare system, ineligible for aid.
- **Why it matters:** Illustrates hidden poverty behind a comfortable appearance.
