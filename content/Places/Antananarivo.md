## Life on Air [revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx1t9gdykkep8rh6st3f6h)

> We acquired a Land Rover; we recruited an expert Malagasy helper from the Scientific Institute in Tananarive, which also ran the city's zoo; and we travelled around the island for the next three months.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** The capital looks Asiatic from the air, with flooded rice fields and two-storeyed tiled houses. The team does permit-chasing in French and has dinner with an eccentric White Russian woman and her crystals, then returns at the end for export permits and a gift of lemurs from the Scientific Institute.
- **Why it matters:** The base for the whole Madagascar trip, bookending it; its character is mostly sketched through the dinner anecdote.
