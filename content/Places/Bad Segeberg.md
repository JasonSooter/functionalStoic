# Bad Segeberg

Small spa town in [[Schleswig-Holstein]], between Hamburg, Lübeck, Neumünster and Kiel. The BBSR's 15-minute-city report uses it as a small-town case study whose core works but whose best addresses do not.

## Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde — Björn Schwarze et al. (BBSR) (2025)
Source: [[Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde]] · [Original](https://www.bbsr.bund.de/BBSR/DE/veroeffentlichungen/bbsr-online/2025/bbsr-online-027-2025-dl.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7kmj4vfpe9fj1aff33jatv3)

> Besonders niedrige Erreichbarkeitsqualitäten im Sinne der 15-Minuten-Stadt weisen die gehobenen Wohnlagen am Ihlsee im Norden der Stadt auf.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The town of the Karl-May-Spiele has about 17,700 inhabitants and 610 cars per 1,000 people. The study area covers the centre, Kurhausstraße up to the clinic, the clinic grounds and the housing along Ziegelstraße and Theodor-Storm-Straße. The centre measures as a 5-minute zone, but the upscale homes at the Ihlsee in the north have particularly poor proximity, which the authors read as wealthy, highly motorised households choosing attractive locations over nearness. Workshop participants credit tourism for the quality and variety of its restaurants.
- **Why it matters:** It brings the argument down to small-town scale and adds a social point: in places like this, poor proximity can be a choice of the well-off rather than a deficit.
