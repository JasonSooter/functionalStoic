## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjq2rcp352a5w4nbm8hm21)

> Smadar went to Paris Square in Jerusalem with Nurit to join the protesting women. Nine years old, she had no proper black shoes and wore, instead, a pair of ballet slippers which she darkened with polish.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Smadar stands at the intersection with Women in Black holding a sign reading End the Occupation, unflinching when settlers throw popcorn at her feet.
- **Why it matters:** A brief scene that shows Smadar as a young protester beside her mother.
