# Lincoln, Montana

Small town in Lewis and Clark County, Montana, near the Continental Divide. It is the Ryback brothers' first food drop and the warmest welcome of the Montana stretch.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8berq19v8khh5trmjk9p1ta)

> as we proceeded along the road to Lincoln, Tim was cheerily defining civilization as "a milkshake, a telephone and a peanut butter-and-jelly sandwich."

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After days in the Sun River valleys, the sight of a road down Alice Creek releases Tim from his silent suffering, and on the road to Lincoln he defines civilization as "a milkshake, a telephone and a peanut butter-and-jelly sandwich." At Lincoln they collect their first food drop, mail home gear they no longer need, and stay in the home of the postmaster, Bob Cook, who points them to the mountain wildflowers. They leave via Stemple Pass.
- **Why it matters:** It is the first return to civilization and the measure the book later uses for every other town (Sula's welcome is compared to it).
