## The Surprising Success of Gondola Transit Systems — Wendover Productions (2025)
Source: [Original](https://youtube.com/watch?v=a5126u88E7E&si=1gWTN5lb3hW199bC)

> The city is spread across steeprugged terrain within a canyon whereelevation ranges from 9,000 ft, or 2,700m, to 12,000 ft, or 3,600 m.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** La Paz is presented as a humble capital whose steep canyon geography led to a gondola network (Melopherico) of 10 lines that carried some 520 million passengers in its first decade. The text says it cut commutes and needs no operating subsidy.
- **Why it matters:** The canyon terrain is the reason gondolas make sense; the case study anchors the whole video.
