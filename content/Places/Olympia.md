# Olympia

Sanctuary of Zeus and site of the ancient Olympic Games, in Elis in the western Peloponnese. In _Gates of Fire_ Dienekes sits above its stadium at night and tells the story of his brother and of Arete.

## Gates of Fire — Steven Pressfield (1998)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/133742/gates-of-fire-by-steven-pressfield/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m896j197cd8dxmbm3snpx1j7)

> He selected a sheltered site beneath the pines at the crest of the slope overlooking the stadium, and there he settled.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On the ephors' orders, Dienekes marches from Pellana to Olympia with Xeones, arriving on the second day. He shows Xeones the stele of his brother Iatrokles, then settles beneath the pines above the stadium. There he tells how Iatrokles saved him at Achilleion and how Arete, Iatrokles's wife, came into his life.
- **Why it matters:** It is a quiet interlude that gives Dienekes his history. Placing it at the games' sanctuary ties his story to Greek honour and memory rather than to war.
