## The Black Art of Escape — Casey Gerald (2019)

Source: [Original](https://nymag.com/intelligencer/2019/08/the-black-art-of-escape.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7gmrqejy7n8cmnbnb33j1)

> The 20-odd Negroes who arrived by force at Point Comfort that August may not have been enslaved in the way my grandmother’s grandfather was

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** The 1619 landing, whose 400th anniversary the essay commemorates, is the starting point of the argument about centuries of captivity and the need for flight.
- **Why it matters:** Point Comfort marks the 1619 landing that frames the whole essay.
