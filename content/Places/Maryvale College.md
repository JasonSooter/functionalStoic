# Maryvale College

Private Catholic school in Johannesburg's northern suburbs. In Born a Crime it is Trevor Noah's school from preschool through grade six.

## At a glance
| Source | Author | Role | In one line |

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6yq434rkf8ja0g6vdqz3vzt)

> she managed to get me into Maryvale College, an expensive private Catholic school.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** in Chapter 4 Maryvale is a racially mixed school run by nuns, where nobody asks Trevor what he is: "Maryvale was an oasis that kept me from the truth." In Chapter 6 he is always in trouble with the nuns. Before Mass he sneaks behind the altar and eats the whole bag of Eucharist and drinks the grape juice. His mother takes his side against the principal, and at the end of grade six he leaves Catholic school. Chapter 1 also mentions the "Maryvale College sports day" when he and his mother outrun the minibus men.
- **Why it matters:** the school's shelter makes the contrast at H. A. Jack Primary, where he must choose a race, so sharp. It is also where his clashes with authority over religion play out.
- **Real vs. fictional:** real school. The pin is its pre-primary and primary campus in the suburb of Maryvale; the book names no site.
