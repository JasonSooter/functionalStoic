# Charleston, South Carolina

Historic port city on the South Carolina coast. In How to Be an Antiracist (Ibram X. Kendi) it is the scene of Denmark Vesey's planned slave revolt.

## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fzd4edpwthbypzvna4z9sh)

> A fifty-one-year-old free Black carpenter had to first teach away these racist ideas in 1818 as he began recruiting thousands of enslaved Blacks to join his slave revolt around Charleston, South Carolina.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Kendi tells how Denmark Vesey recruited enslaved people around Charleston for a revolt planned for July 1822; a house slave betrayed it, Vesey was hanged, and the betrayer was freed and pensioned.
- **Why it matters:** Kendi uses the betrayal as an early example of a Black person using limited power in service of racist policy.

## Going on a Cruise Part 3 – Save on Board and on Transportation — Justin (Root of Good) (2015)

Source: [Original](http://rootofgood.com/cruise-saving-tips-on-board-transportation/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hj1zs6g93p68jqb1jh23mv)

> For example, I can book a 5 night cruise from Charleston, South Carolina that’s a 4.5 hour drive from home instead of sailing from Miami that is 11.5 hours drive time from home.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** Arguing for a departure port close to home to cut travel costs, the author uses Charleston as his example: a 4.5-hour drive against Miami's 11.5, reachable by noon for a 4 pm sailing.
- **Why it matters:** The city is a practical example in a budgeting tip; any nearby port would make the same point.

## November 2015 Financial Update — Justin (Root of Good) (2015)
Source: [Original](http://rootofgood.com/november-2015-financial-update/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hk5tj9gseg3p40b1999593)

> We’ll be sailing out of Charleston, South Carolina to the Bahamas for six days and five nights.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** The family books a six-day January cruise from Charleston to two islands in the Bahamas, six people in two cabins, from a port they can drive to on the morning of sailing. The booking is most of the month's spending.
- **Why it matters:** A practical departure port; the cruise matters more than the city.
