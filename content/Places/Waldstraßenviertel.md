# Waldstraßenviertel

Gründerzeit quarter north-west of central [[Leipzig]]. In the BBSR's 15-minute-city report it is one of the two best case-study quarters: almost everything is within a five-minute walk or ride.

## Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde — Björn Schwarze et al. (BBSR) (2025)
Source: [[Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde]] · [Original](https://www.bbsr.bund.de/BBSR/DE/veroeffentlichungen/bbsr-online/2025/bbsr-online-027-2025-dl.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7kmhy6pj4qp5fxmyvjecs7f)

> Das Waldstraßenviertel im Nordwesten der Innenstadt gilt als größtes erhalten gebliebenes Gründerzeitviertel in Deutschland.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** One of the report's fifteen case-study quarters. With 11,000 residents it is denser than Leipzig as a whole (2,800 against 2,100 people per km²) and has only 295 cars per 1,000 people. The accessibility model rates it a 4.9-minute city with 98.3 % of everyday destinations reachable and an index of 91.1. In the resident survey it and Essen-Rüttenscheid stand out as the most positively rated quarters, with high shares of 15-minute trips even for leisure.
- **Why it matters:** It is the report's proof that a dense, mixed, old inner-city quarter already works as a 15-minute city in Germany; a suburban quarter would not make the point.
