# Semyonovskoye sanatorium

KGB sanatorium on the Lopasnaya River south of Moscow, [[Russia]]. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is the comfortable prison where Gordievsky resolves to flee.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7xy62yvshtxrcsntvyfd8tx)

> Semyonovskoye sanatorium was, in reality, an extremely comfortable prison, where the KGB could keep a close eye on Gordievsky, and wait for him to drop his guard.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Sent away under KGB suspicion, Gordievsky finds himself in a spacious room overlooking the Lopasnaya River in one of Russia's most luxurious official resorts, with a minder for a roommate. The book calls it "an extremely comfortable prison" where the KGB waits for him to drop his guard. Running daily and studying the border, he decides: "There's no alternative. If I don't get out I'm going to die."
- **Why it matters:** It is where he makes the decision that drives the rest of the book. The resort's luxury sharpens the sense of being watched.
