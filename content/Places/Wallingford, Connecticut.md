# Wallingford, Connecticut

Town just south of Meriden in central Connecticut. In Tomie dePaola's memoir it is home to his Irish grandparents, Tom and Nana, and their grocery store, which the family visits every Sunday.

## 26 Fairmount Avenue — Tomie dePaola (1999)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/351865/26-fairmount-avenue-by-tomie-depaola/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8d25v5e1r6vfgr4xmj0vbhf)

> The Irish relatives came to visit the most because they lived in Wallingford, which wasn’t too far from Meriden.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Every Sunday the family drives down to Wallingford. Tomie spends the visits with Nana Upstairs, and Tom and Nana's grocery store is across the street from their house. The family spends moving day in January 1940 here before driving up to the new house. On New Year's Eve the parents go dancing at the Wallingford Elks Club.
- **Why it matters:** Wallingford is the family's second home in the book and the source of its warmest scenes. It is minor as geography: what matters is the relatives.
