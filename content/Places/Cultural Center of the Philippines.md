# Cultural Center of the Philippines

Arts complex on reclaimed land on Manila Bay, built under Imelda Marcos. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne rides through its landfill and archives while researching his Imelda song cycle.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76rhsx9abtasjj4x4wtsn09)

> We drive by Imelda’s Cultural Center on the way to the hotel—a giant Lincoln Center–type edifice that Mrs. Marcos had built on landfill.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Byrne passes Imelda's Cultural Center on his way in from the airport and notes she wanted to put the Philippines on the world cultural map and nurture local talent. Later he rides back to the landfill, where the Film Center now hosts a Korean drag show and is rumoured to be cursed because part of it collapsed during rushed construction and bodies may still be in the concrete.
- **Why it matters:** It is the most visible monument to Imelda's ambitions, the subject of the song cycle Byrne is researching in Manila, and the rumours around it show how her projects turned into local legend.
