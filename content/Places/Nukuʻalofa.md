## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx3cw0yx2aazw96h8hvnbt)

> When we moored alongside the quay at Nuku'alofa, the island's capital, Jim Spillius was there to meet us.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Arrival port in Tonga, where Ve'ehala of the Palace Records meets them. They spend three weeks planning the taumafa kava in his office near the Palace and filming local life, including the royal tombs, bark-cloth making and coastal blow-holes.
- **Why it matters:** Base for the Tongan section.
