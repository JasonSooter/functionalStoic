## A hilly bicycle ride to a French bakery in New Jersey — alliumstozinnias (2024)

> We always leave from the Hopewell, N.J., store and head into the Sourland Mountain.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** Nine women meet at 10:30 a.m. for the shop's women-only Friday ride, the day after Thanksgiving, and set off from the Hopewell store into the hills.
- **Why it matters:** The starting point and social hub of the ride; the outing exists because of the shop's regular group.
