# Stasi Museum

Museum in the former headquarters of East Germany's secret police in Lichtenberg, [[Berlin]]. David Byrne rides into its courtyard in _Bicycle Diaries_ and tours the spy gadgets and Erich Mielke's preserved offices.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7641vpwzkdrmrnyv8vzfrn3)

> The Stasi Museum is a massive compound that encloses a whole city block. I ride my bike into the inner courtyard and lock it up.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** At the end of his Karl-Marx-Allee ride Byrne locks his bike in the inner courtyard, where comings and goings were hidden from the street. He looks at cameras hidden in logs, coat buttons and a birdhouse, then at the preserved offices and small apartment of Stasi chief Erich Mielke. He is told the complex is for sale for one euro, on condition that it becomes a proper museum.
- **Why it matters:** The visit is the core of his reflection on surveillance and fear in the East, and on what Berlin chooses to preserve.
