# Taybad

Border town in Razavi Khorasan near the Afghan frontier. Dervla Murphy's last night in Iran in _Full Tilt_ (as 'Tieabad').

## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6kve5cby3rvsamkct630k)

> Actually I shouldn’t be here – on arrival the proprietor told me that no women are allowed outside the Women’s Quarter. I meekly went off to same but it consisted of a tiny room with six beds and just enough space to walk between them. One bed was mine; the other five were occupied by women who possessed a minimum of two infants apiece – all being fed and changed at the time of my appearance. Both window and door were tightly sealed and the stink was appalling

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Roz's wheel falls off after the Meshed repair, an Afghan tanker driver rescues them, and she repairs the wheel herself. At the hotel she argues her way out of the Women's Quarter and sits in the moonlit courtyard before leaving Persia.
- **Why it matters:** Her last night in Persia.
