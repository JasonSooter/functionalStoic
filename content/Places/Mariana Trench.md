# Mariana Trench

The deepest oceanic trench, in the western Pacific east of the Mariana Islands. Its 11 km depth is the whole premise of xkcd's What If? question about dropping a bowling ball into it.

## What if you dropped a bowling ball in the Mariana Trench? — xkcd's What If? (2026)
Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FYoHYq8VRt0) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bre539x2yzsqyddscqtn5m)

> The Mariana Trench is 11 km deep, so the ball will take about 2 hours and 20 minutes to reach the bottom.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** A heavy bowling ball sinks at about 1.3 m/s and needs roughly two hours twenty to fall the trench's 11 km; lighter balls float, drift for years and may end up near California and in the Great Pacific Garbage Patch.
- **Why it matters:** The trench's depth is the whole premise; nowhere else gives the question its scale.
