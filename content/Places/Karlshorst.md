# Karlshorst

Locality in the east of [[Berlin]], once the KGB's enclave in East Berlin. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ the young Gordievsky lodges there the night the Berlin Wall goes up.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7p6zcd2z11jzbah1486vw8r)

> Gordievsky arrived in East Berlin on August 12, 1961, and traveled to a student hostel inside the KGB enclave in the suburb of Karlshorst.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Sent to East Berlin as a translator for six months, Gordievsky moves into a student hostel in the KGB enclave at Karlshorst the day before the border closes. He wakes to bulldozers building the Wall and watches East Germans trying to flee. He also makes his first KGB contact, with a former German informant.
- **Why it matters:** The Wall is his first shock at what the regime is capable of, and the book traces his later disillusionment back to it.
