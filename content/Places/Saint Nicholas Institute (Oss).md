# Saint Nicholas Institute (Oss)

A Catholic monastery boarding school run by the Brothers of Tilburg in [[Oss]], North Brabant, pinned at the town. In _The Son and Heir_ it is where Frans Münninghoff's estrangement from the Netherlands begins.

## The Son and Heir: A Memoir — Alexander Münninghoff (2020)
Source: [Original](https://www.letterenfonds.nl/en/books/heir-and-successor) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m85b03g82fn0m5nse1a00bx3)

> my father found himself in the early summer of 1932, at the age of eleven, in the care of the Brothers of Tilburg at the Saint Nicholas Institute in Oss.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The Old Boss wants his children educated as Dutch, so Frans goes from the luxury of Riga to a monastery school of black habits, hard benches, little sport and Mass three times a day. He spends his free time writing home, about six letters a week, and takes his first communion in Oss before Christmas 1932.
- **Why it matters:** The author traces his father's lasting refusal to feel Dutch to this first exile; set anywhere gentler, the story of a boy drifting toward Germany would lose its starting point.
