## The Hidden Engineering of Landfills — Practical Engineering (2024)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HRx_dZawN44) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2zwy8fm1nz1wv6vsf4p32)

> This is the Puente Hills Landfill outside of Los Angeles, California. The first truckload of trash was dumped here in 1957, and the trucks just kept coming.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** Opened in 1957, it took about four million tons of trash a year at its peak and closed in 2013. It has a gas-to-energy facility running since 1987 powering around 70,000 homes, and plans to become a park with trails, biking, slides and gardens.
- **Why it matters:** It frames the video as an example of how landfill engineering changed, from a hole in the ground to a highly engineered one, and how closed landfills can be reused.
