## how to do nothing — Jenny Odell (2019)
Source: [Original](https://medium.com/@the_jennitaur/how-to-do-nothing-57e100f59bbb) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7f4ybbpy9fer9w2r6cph4)

> Not far from the rose garden is the Chapel of the Chimes, a columbarium designed by Julia Morgan, another labyrinthine space whose many, many rooms contains hundreds of containers of ashes.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the article:** Odell presents the columbarium as another architecture of nothing: a maze of rooms, some underground, with few windows, where annotated urns invite reflection on a whole life. She likes a map with no 'you are here' marking.
- **Why it matters:** A second example of enclosed contemplative space.
