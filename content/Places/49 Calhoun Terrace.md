# 49 Calhoun Terrace

Address on Telegraph Hill, [[San Francisco]]. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it is where Diego Rivera settles with Irene Bohus after leaving Mexico in 1940, and where Frida stays with him before entering hospital.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)

Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76e9fb6fqw0sch9e9v04mjm)

> Soon he was installed with Irene Bohus in a studio apartment at 49 Calhoun Street on Telegraph Hill.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** After the attack on Trotsky, Rivera leaves Mexico to paint his Treasure Island mural and installs himself here with Irene Bohus, who was to appear in the mural. She leaves his employ before her portrait is finished, reportedly because her mother objected to the arrangement. When Frida flies up in September 1940 she spends a few days with Diego in his apartment before being admitted to [[Saint Luke's Hospital (San Francisco)|Saint Luke's Hospital]].
- **Why it matters:** It is a backdrop rather than a scene: the flat marks Rivera's exile and his new attachments before the couple's reconciliation and remarriage in San Francisco.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Herrera writes "49 Calhoun Street"; the street on Telegraph Hill is Calhoun Terrace, and the pin is No. 49 there.
