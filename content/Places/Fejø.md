# Fejø

Small island in southern Denmark known for its orchards. In _The Little Book of Hygge_ Meik Wiking and his friends go there each year to pick apples and plums.

## The Little Book of Hygge: The Danish Way to Live Well — Meik Wiking (2016)

Source: [[The Little Book of Hygge - The Danish Way to Live Well]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19352003W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jrfyb6s0thyapnk9g9x4cy6p)

> About once a year, my friends and I go to Fejø – a small island in the southern part of Denmark known for its apples.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** About once a year Wiking and his friends go to Fejø to pick fruit among rows of apple and plum trees; late in summer the Opal plums and Filippa apples are ripe, and the haul goes into jam-making or cider plans.
- **Why it matters:** A small personal example of hygge outdoors and with friends.
