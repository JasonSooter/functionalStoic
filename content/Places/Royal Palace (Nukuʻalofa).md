## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx3ec0y24a9cw37pt0tr4d)

> The Palace overlooked Tonga's lagoon. It was a two-storeyed white-painted gabled building made of wooden clapboard with a red corrugated iron roof that had been put up in the late nineteenth century by New Zealand builders.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The Palace is the social centre of the island with a band playing in the corridor. The nearby open lawn, the mala'e, hosts the sacred taumafa kava, with nobles in a hundred-yard circle under Norfolk Island pines while Queen Salote watches from her pavilion and balcony.
- **Why it matters:** The site of the sacred ceremony the whole trip was organised around and the book's most detailed Tongan setting.
