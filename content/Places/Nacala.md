## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling (2019)

Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20831115W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqzehq6xkfsjebv2bqrygg)

> Nacala has one of the best deep-water harbors on the east coast of Africa.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** From 1979 Rosling was district doctor for a town of about 85,000 and a rural district of 300,000, served by one fifty-bed hospital. Episodes include the bicycle ride that made the town laugh, the tetanus vaccination programme, the beach day with Niheriwa and the wrongly set leg.
- **Why it matters:** Where he learned practical lessons (change only what must be changed; the two-by-two table) that shaped his later work.

## How I Learned to Understand the World — Hans Rosling (2019)

Source: [[How I Learned to Understand the World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20831115W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5ebtm7qwhpmdh17pcav40m9)

> Apparently, the Nacala pharmacist was running a private black market in contraceptive pills.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Agneta tells how a poor couple revealed that the pharmacist was selling free pills; Hans investigated and the man was jailed. Earlier, Janet tells him that what he says about Nacala fits her view of Mozambique's progress.
- **Why it matters:** Recurring emblem of his early doctor years and his anger at obstruction of public health.
