## The Price of Civilization — Jim Wright (2015)
Source: [Original](https://www.stonekettle.com/2015/11/the-price-of-civilization.html) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hk506bkxs6z0g6vfzans1s)

> Thirty years ago we denounced the Soviets for destroying Afghanistan and leaving nothing but ruins, a destroyed civilization, chaos.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** Writing after the Paris attacks, Wright traces modern terrorism to Afghanistan: the US funded the Mujahedeen against the Soviets and then left rather than rebuild, and those fighters became the Taliban and Al Qaida.
- **Why it matters:** It is the first link in the essay's chain from abandoned chaos to terror.
