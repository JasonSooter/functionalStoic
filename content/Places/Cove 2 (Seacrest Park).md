# Cove 2 (Seacrest Park)

Dive site at Seacrest Park in West Seattle, on Elliott Bay. In The Soul of an Octopus (Sy Montgomery) it is 'Olive's Den', where a wild octopus brooded her eggs.

## The Soul of an Octopus — Sy Montgomery (2015)

Source: [[The Soul of an Octopus]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL27182716M/The_Soul_of_an_Octopus) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fydfk0r0f0rj62nw8s1k38)

> “It was sad,” Becken said. “Some divers didn’t want to look. But since her life, since her death, this place has always been known as Olive’s Den.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Told by diver Guy Becken at an octopus symposium in Seattle: in 2002 a wild giant Pacific octopus, Olive, laid eggs under old pilings at the Cove 2 dive site, about a mile across the water from the Seattle Aquarium. Divers watched her guard and hatch them until she died, and the spot has been called Olive's Den since.
- **Why it matters:** A wild counterpart to Octavia's brooding in the aquarium, showing that the behaviour Montgomery watches in captivity happens in the sea too.
