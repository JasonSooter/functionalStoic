# Feather island (The Place of Tides)

A tiny outer island of the [[Vega Archipelago]], Norway, where Anna keeps her eider ducks. It is the title place and destination of James Rebanks's memoir, which in the part read so far doesn't give its map name.

## The Place of Tides — James Rebanks (2024)

Source: [[The Place of Tides]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/315745/the-place-of-tides-by-rebanks-james/9780241426937) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7h2cp3hkfcqxetp3jvfccam)

> Beneath a little hill in the middle of the island, tucked away from the worst Atlantic gales, was a white clapboard house and some scattered red barns. This seemed to be the final outpost before the ocean.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On his first visit Rebanks is taken out by fishing boat past tide-marker poles to an island of a few acres, where he meets Anna on a seaweed-covered rock plateau and sees her tame eiders nesting in old hen huts, barns and cow byres. The helmsman says his people always called it "the place of tides"; the guide translates its name as "feather island". The second journey, with Anna, Ingrid and Henrik, heads back out to it for the season.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's title and destination; Anna's work, and Rebanks's search for a better way to live, happen here.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Best guess. In the part scanned the island is unnamed apart from "the place of tides" and "feather island", reached by boat from [[Vega (Norway)|Vega]] towards the open sea. A review in _Resurgence_ calls it Fjærøy ("feather island"), which doesn't match a mapped island; it may be a pen name. Pinned at the archipelago's centre rather than at a specific island. Main alternative: Lånan, the archipelago's largest working eiderdown station (Hakai Magazine), but no source ties Anna to it.
