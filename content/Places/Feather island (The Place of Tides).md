# Feather island (The Place of Tides)

Fjærøy, a tiny outer island of the [[Vega Archipelago]], Norway, where Anna keeps eider ducks on an island owned by someone else. It is the title place and setting of James Rebanks's memoir, which names it but whose name matches no mapped island.

## The Place of Tides — James Rebanks (2024)

Source: [[The Place of Tides]] · [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/315745/the-place-of-tides-by-rebanks-james/9780241426937) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7h2cp3hkfcqxetp3jvfccam)

> Beneath a little hill in the middle of the island, tucked away from the worst Atlantic gales, was a white clapboard house and some scattered red barns. This seemed to be the final outpost before the ocean.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** On his first visit Rebanks is taken out by fishing boat past tide-marker poles to an island of a few acres, where he meets Anna on a seaweed-covered rock plateau and sees her tame eiders nesting in old hen huts, barns and cow byres. The helmsman says his people always called it "the place of tides"; the guide translates its name as "feather island". On the second journey, with Anna, Ingrid and Henrik, the boat passes the tidy neighbouring station of Flatholm ("This side is Flatholm … beyond is Fjærøy") and the book names it: "The main island in the sea estate of Fjærøyvær was called, simply, Fjærøy". Anna has run it for twelve years; it belongs to "an old man on Vega called Terje". The household airs the huts, dries seaweed on Crow Island, a tidal islet at the northern edge of the estate, and builds nests across the islets; Rebanks and Anna rebuild the stone nests on Ship Island, the rock in front of the house, and row to South Island, the biggest island of the _vær_. Anna's job includes keeping visitors away.
- **Why it matters:** It is the book's title and setting; Anna's work, and Rebanks's search for a better way to live, happen here.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Best guess. The book names the island Fjærøy, the main island of the sea estate Fjærøyvær, reached by boat from [[Vega (Norway)|Vega]] past Flatholm; from its hill [[Måsøyvær (The Place of Tides)|Måsøyvær]] lies to the south, a little west of Vega. A review in _Resurgence_ also calls it Fjærøy ("feather island"). No mapped island near Vega carries the name (Nominatim finds none in Nordland), so it may be a pen name. Pinned at the archipelago's centre rather than at a specific island. Main alternative: Lånan, the archipelago's largest working eiderdown station (Hakai Magazine), but no source ties Anna to it.
