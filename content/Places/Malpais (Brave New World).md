# Malpais (Brave New World)

The mesa-top pueblo of Malpais on the New Mexican Savage Reservation, where John the Savage grows up in _Brave New World_. Pinned as a best guess at El Malpais in western New Mexico.

## Brave New World — Aldous Huxley (1932)
Source: [[Brave New World]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL64365W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvajj3bj6epwnqweqbs8xp)

> The mesa was like a ship becalmed in a strait of lion-coloured dust.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After the Warden's briefing on the fenced Reservation, a guard flies Bernard and Lenina over a string of pueblos to the valley of Malpais. They climb to the mesa-top pueblo, watch a boy whipped in the square, and meet John, born there to Linda. In flashback John recalls Linda's squalid house on the outskirts, Popé, learning to read from Shakespeare, and being stoned away from the initiation at the Antelope Kiva to sit alone on the mesa edge.
- **Why it matters:** Malpais is the counter-world to civilisation; John's childhood here gives him the values that collide with the World State. Without it there is no Savage.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Malpais is fictional; the Reservation is set in real New Mexico.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ The guard flies them "over Taos and Tesuque; over Nambe and Picuris and Pojoaque, over Sia and Cochiti, over Laguna and Acoma and the Enchanted Mesa" before landing "in the valley of Malpais", and the pueblo sits on a mesa "like a ship becalmed in a strait of lion-coloured dust". Malpais is Spanish for badlands; the El Malpais lava country lies just west of Acoma, the last pueblo named, so the pin is El Malpais National Monument. One web search found no reference work naming the model. Alternative: Acoma Pueblo itself, the best-known mesa-top pueblo.
