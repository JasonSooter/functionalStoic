# Tidore

Volcanic clove island beside Ternate in the North Moluccas, Indonesia. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ Magellan's surviving ships load spices there in 1521, and the Dutch later storm its Portuguese fort.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m82efanrhzwdc6p3dw4whfjd)

> sighting the clove-covered cone of Tidore’s volcano in the first week of November 1521.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** After Magellan's death, his surviving ships sight Tidore's clove-covered volcano in November 1521; Pigafetta records the island's weights and measures, and the Victoria leaves laden with cloves, nutmeg, cinnamon and mace while the leaking Trinidad stays behind. Drake intends to anchor there but is drawn to Ternate instead, and later Henry Middleton watches the Dutch storm Tidore's Portuguese fort.
- **Why it matters:** It is the first Spanish landfall in the spiceries, sparking the Spanish–Portuguese dispute over the islands, and later a marker of the Dutch takeover.
