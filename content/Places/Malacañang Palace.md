# Malacañang Palace

Official residence of the Philippine president on the Pasig River in [[Manila]]. In _Bicycle Diaries_ David Byrne tours it while researching Imelda Marcos.

## Bicycle Diaries — David Byrne (2009)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL14993423W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m76rhwhsaqaawc7ej6snqv5r)

> I get a tour of the Malacañang Palace—the Manila White House.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** A guard lets Byrne park his bike behind a service building and dry off before the tour. Inside he sees the chair where Marcos signed the 1972 declaration of martial law, and walls of photos celebrating People Power, which ousted the Marcoses in 1986.
- **Why it matters:** The palace is the clearest stage for the Manila chapter's theme of mythmaking: the official story of the Marcos years, told in the house where they ruled.
