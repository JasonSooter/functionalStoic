# 32 Smith Square

Building on Smith Square in Westminster, [[London]], long the Conservative Party's headquarters. In _The Spy and the Traitor_ it is where MI5's agent Rosemary Spencer works, the source Gordievsky uses to impress the KGB.

## The Spy and the Traitor — Ben Macintyre (2018)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19749410W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7rsbr7a08pe2rgvg92j0x65)

> The striking, tall, dark-haired figure of Rosemary Spencer was a familiar sight in Conservative Central Office, the nerve center of the Tory Party based at 32 Smith Square, in the heart of Westminster.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Spencer works in the international section of the research department and is secretly an MI5 agent. Gordievsky cultivates her over lunches, and the Tory gossip she passes, padded out with material supplied by MI6, helps build his standing inside the KGB.
- **Why it matters:** Minor as a place, but it marks the double game at its subtlest: a staged "contact" at the heart of British politics, used to make the spy look valuable to Moscow.
