# Grand Canyon

Deep canyon of the Colorado River in northern Arizona. In The Comfort Crisis (Michael Easter) it is the scene of Marcus Elliott's failed rim-to-rim-to-rim run.

## The Comfort Crisis — Michael Easter (2021)

Source: [[The Comfort Crisis]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/634446/the-comfort-crisis-by-michael-easter/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fyd3xk26chgybdary0ws34)

> Elliott’s most recent was a rim-to-rim-to-rim run of the Grand Canyon.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** Elliott recounts his latest misogi, a run of about 46 miles and 22,000 feet of elevation change, which he failed after his knees gave out on the descent from the South Rim; he turned at the North Rim and caught the last shuttle back to the South Rim.
- **Why it matters:** It illustrates the book's claim that the possibility of failure is the point of a misogi.
