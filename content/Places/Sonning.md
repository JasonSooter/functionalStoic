## Three Men in a Boat — Jerome K. Jerome (1889)

Source: [[Exploration of Three Men in a Boat]] · [[Three Men in a Boat]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1793164W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gkyja5qyzkgk376488xncm)

> We got out at Sonning, and went for a walk round the village. It is the most fairy-like little nook on the whole river.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The friends get out at Sonning and walk round the village of rose-smothered cottages; the narrator recommends the Bull behind the church before they decide it is too late to pass Reading and turn back for the night.
- **Why it matters:** A gentle pastoral interlude; the village's prettiness is the point.
