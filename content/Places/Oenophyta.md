# Oenophyta

Town in Boeotia, north of Athens, today's Oinofyta. In _Gates of Fire_ it is the battlefield where the helot squire Rooster saves Alexandros's father from Theban cavalry.

## Gates of Fire — Steven Pressfield (1998)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/133742/gates-of-fire-by-steven-pressfield/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m89gte12pp2q6gv9gvww7pnz)

> This occurred at Oenophyta against the Thebans, one year after Antirhion.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Xeo tells this as a flashback. The Spartan regiments clash with a Theban left stacked twenty deep. Olympieus is crippled by a spear-butt through the foot and left alone on the open "gleaning ground" behind the line. Rooster, fifteen and unarmed, runs in, drives off two Theban cavalrymen, kills the third with the man's own helmet, and takes a horse to drag his master's litter off the field.
- **Why it matters:** Rooster's heroism here is what binds Olympieus and Alexandros's family to him. Xeo says it "came powerfully to affect" Dienekes, Alexandros and Arete when the Three Hundred are chosen. The battle itself is lightly sketched, and any open field would have served.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Oenophyta is a real place, and the historical battle there (457 BC) was fought by Athens against the Boeotians. The novel's Spartan–Theban fight there is invented. ⚠️
