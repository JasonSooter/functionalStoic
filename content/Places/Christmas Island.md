## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy8pc865xfcw86nty6z7w9)

> The one the crabs inhabit lies a couple of hundred miles south of Java.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** To open The Trials of Life, the team reaches Christmas Island by an Australian Air Force cargo plane during a pilots' strike. At night the red crabs pour down the cliff to the beach, with the sea washing round Attenborough as he speaks.
- **Why it matters:** The opening scene of the series and one of its most vivid.
