> We also partnered with local brands who target families (for instance a local theatre in Memphis offered free tickets for their family series for the kids that did enough chores through our app) and did a bit of cross-promotion which helped with growth.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** After launch, most users turned out to be American, mostly from southern states, so the Slovenian founders searched for accelerators in that region and joined Start Co in Memphis. It gave seed capital and connections, and they made local partnerships such as a Memphis theatre offering free tickets for kids who did chores.
- **Why it matters:** Moving to Memphis to be near customers is the turning point and the subject of the title.
