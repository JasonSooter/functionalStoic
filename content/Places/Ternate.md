# Ternate

Volcanic clove island in the North Moluccas, Indonesia. In _Nathaniel's Nutmeg_ it is where Drake makes England's first spice deal and where Henry Middleton later rescues its king from pirates.

## Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Giles Milton (1999)

Source: [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg — Reader's Companion]] · [[Nathaniel's Nutmeg]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1970050W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m82ef9g2z1ng873bc36cmj11)

> Drake had intended to drop anchor at the volcanic island of Tidore but as he edged his ship through the treacherous shallows a canoe drew alongside carrying a viceroy from the neighbouring island of Ternate.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** A viceroy from Ternate persuades Drake away from Portuguese-leaning Tidore; the King of Ternate comes out to Drake's ship with ceremony and cannon fire and lets the English buy whatever spices they need, and Drake's overloaded ship has to jettison cannon and three tons of cloves to get off a shoal. Years later Henry Middleton rescues the king's boat from pursuing pirates and gets him to sign a treaty with King James, though the king has a secret agreement with the Dutch.
- **Why it matters:** It is England's first real contact with a spice-producing ruler, and the later scene shows how far the Dutch already dominate the Moluccas.
