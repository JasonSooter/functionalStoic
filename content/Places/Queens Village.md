## How to Be an Antiracist — Ibram X. Kendi (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42715690M/How_to_Be_an_Antiracist) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmsc7cekgxcjvrqp01f3ss)

> Several public elementary schools resided within walking distance of my house in Queens Village.

- **Role:** backdrop
- **In the book:** Kendi's childhood home is in Queens Village; his parents commute far for private schooling. He mentions other Queens locations and says he is 'from Queens, New York'.
- **Why it matters:** The home neighborhood grounds the family's middle-class choices; minor as a named place.
