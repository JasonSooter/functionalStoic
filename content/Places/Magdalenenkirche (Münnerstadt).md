# Magdalenenkirche (Münnerstadt)

Parish church of St Mary Magdalene in Münnerstadt, Lower Franconia, north of Würzburg. In _Germany: Memories of a Nation_ it is the original home of Riemenschneider's Evangelists.

## Germany: Memories of a Nation — Neil MacGregor (2014)

Source: [[Germany - Memories of a Nation]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20201162W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5m75v2dh5xjsttvmg80tz5f)

> were part of a great carved altarpiece, made for the Magdalenenkirche in Münnerstadt, near Würzburg, and completed in 1492.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Chapter 12, 'Sculpting the spirit', explains that Riemenschneider's Four Evangelists, now in Berlin's Bode Museum, were part of a tall altarpiece completed in 1492 for this church. They sat just above the altar, face to face with the priest and a few yards from the kneeling faithful. The altarpiece survived the Reformation but was later repainted, dismantled and sold off piecemeal.
- **Why it matters:** It is the original setting that explains how the chapter's carved figures were meant to be seen; MacGregor reads the altarpiece as an emblem of the Holy Roman Empire's sacred framework.
