## The Serviceberry — Robin Wall Kimmerer (2024)

Source: [[The Serviceberry]] · [Original](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Serviceberry/Robin-Wall-Kimmerer/9781668072257) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy9pmga4aw13ec1zqnw07w)

> Tahawus is the Algonquin name for Mount Marcy, the highest peak in the Adirondacks.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 'An Offering' the author recalls childhood summers canoe camping in the Adirondacks, where her father poured the first coffee on the ground each morning saying 'Here's to the gods of Tahawus.' Names of sites such as Forked Lake, South Pond and Brandy Brook Flow are invoked, and the author reflects that naming turns wilderness into homeland.
- **Why it matters:** The emotional origin of the author's practice of reciprocity and the book's closing ceremony.
