## In the Netherlands, Anyone Can Turn a Slice of Sidewalk Into a Garden — Klaus Sieg (2025)
Source: [Original](https://reasonstobecheerful.world/netherlands-tegelwippen-flowerbeds/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5e2z1nneyfvvdwevh1sb8nt)

> Unadorned houses and gray sidewalk slabs line the street of the Katendrechtse Lagendijk neighborhood.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** On a Saturday, neighbours led by Boris Stein and Raymond Landegent lift paving slabs along a 100-metre stretch and replace them with flower beds. The street is described as dreary, with a few puny trees, hot in summer and often flooded in heavy rain.
- **Why it matters:** It is the main scene of the piece and shows the Dutch tile-lifting (tegelwippen) practice at work in an immigrant neighbourhood.
