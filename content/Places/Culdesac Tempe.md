## The Arizona Experiment! — Mr. Money Mustache (2023)

Source: [Original](https://www.mrmoneymustache.com/2023/11/02/moving-to-culdesac/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmra5vv3msfx6jhqgrv4kx)

> Meet as many new people as possible, and answer the burning question that we all have: what kind of people choose to move to a car-free neighborhood in the center of a super-car-based metropolis?

- **Role:** subject
- **In the article:** MMM announces he will spend four winter months in a two-bedroom apartment at Culdesac Tempe, a car-free neighborhood. He lists plans: meetups in its plazas, the on-site gym, bikes and e-bikes, a free handyman service, and storing his car offsite.
- **Why it matters:** The whole post is about trying a deliberate life change in this place; it matters as a car-free experiment inside a car-dependent metro and as a model he hopes will be copied.
