# Summit Lake (Yellowstone)

Backcountry lake in the west of Yellowstone National Park, about nine miles from Old Faithful. Ryback holds up its elk as untouched paradise just over the hill from the crowds.

## The Ultimate Journey - Canada to Mexico Down the Continental Divide — Eric Ryback (1973)
Source: [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m8besaz13tfye5hbkpxtrxgf)

> An elk emerged from the wooded depths and advanced to the water near our campsite at Summit Lake.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Camped at Summit Lake, the brothers watch an elk come out of the woods to the water, then two elk wading into the misty lake and splashing each other. Eric calls it virgin land where man has never brought fear, and notes the irony that it is "just over the hill and some nine miles from Old Faithful" and its masses of tourists. Two hours later they stand on the Firehole River looking at an expressway.
- **Why it matters:** It is the hinge of the Yellowstone chapter ("Man vs. Yellowstone"): the wild park set against the commercial one.
