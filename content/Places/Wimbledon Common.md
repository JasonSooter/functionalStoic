## The Time Machine — H. G. Wells (1895)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/55966/the-time-machine) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jy9w4gv3t8gtdajdtf22aj)

> As the hush of evening crept over the world and we proceeded over the hill crest towards Wimbledon, Weena grew tired and wanted to return to the house of grey stone.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** They walk over the hill crest towards Wimbledon at dusk, wade a small river, pass a headless faun statue, and sleep on an open hill before a thick wood. Later the Time Traveller flees through the forest, sets it afire accidentally, loses Weena, and crosses a burning hillock where Morlocks blunder in the glare.
- **Why it matters:** The night walk and forest fire are the story's emotional climax, where Weena is lost.
