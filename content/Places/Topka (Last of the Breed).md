# Topka (Last of the Breed)

A way-station village and collective in the Khabarovsk taiga in _Last of the Breed_, where Joe Mack leaves a KGB man's car and later holds the manager at gunpoint; pinned at [[Nelkan]] as a best guess.

## Last of the Breed — Louis L'Amour (1986)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL51048W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6y2dbemb48v38f368ftvba5)

> Alekhin, being driven in another black Volga, came to Topka late on the same afternoon. Peter Petrovich was awaiting him at the office of the collective.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After killing a KGB man who shot at him, Joe Mack drives the body nearly thirty miles to "a mere cluster of houses and sheds," a way station with no power line, and leaves the car in the shadow of a shed. Alekhin, arriving with the collective's manager Peter Petrovich, cuts open the dead man's coat, finds an arrowhead and knows the American is the killer. When Peter Petrovich drives back, Joe Mack is waiting in his house: they eat, talk about books and why he will not surrender, and he leaves with food, cartridges and a map, the tied-up manager behind him.
- **Why it matters:** The arrowhead tells the hunters for certain where the American is and how he fights, and the map he takes steers his next leg north to the Maya River.
- **Real vs. fictional:** No village of this name is recorded in the region (GeoNames lists Topkas only in Irkutsk, Buryatia and elsewhere); probably invented.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Clues in the text: the road "went nowhere except to swing in one great circle or to drive back to Nel'kan"; the dead man had been "driving to the coast, heading for Aldoma"; Joe Mack had crossed a road and power line "no more than sixty miles from the sea" after the Maymakan River; Alekhin flies on to Kurun-Uryakh on the Maya. The pin is at Nelkan on the Maya, the road's one named end; the alternative is a point on the Nelkan–Ayan road nearer the coast.
