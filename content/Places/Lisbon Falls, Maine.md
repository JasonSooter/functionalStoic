## HOW I MADE NEARLY $1,000 ON CRAIGSLIST GIGS THIS SUMMER — Sara McGrath (2015)
Source: [Original](http://networthyblog.com/posts/how-i-made-nearly-1000-on-craigslist-gigs-this-summer) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hj1gtn9hyzab99edz3ndy1)

> So every year they come from all over the US to gather in Lisbon Falls, Maine and celebrate the soda.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** The author, who lives in Maine, takes a Craigslist gig as a brand ambassador for Moxie, the state's divisive soda. At the yearly gathering in Lisbon Falls she hands out swag, runs a photobooth and interviews fans.
- **Why it matters:** It is the most vivid of her summer gigs and shows the kind of odd, local work that added up to nearly $1,000.
