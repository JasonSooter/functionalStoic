# Royal Festival Hall

Concert hall on London's South Bank; in _Life on Air_ the scene of a lighting mishap at Stravinsky's farewell concert.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx3182zd5d0c1jrr87hva5)

> The farewell tour of one of the great composers of the century was clearly an event of real importance and I determined that BBC2 viewers should be there to share the occasion. I was disappointed to discover that the promoters were strongly opposed to the idea.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Attenborough sits in the hall for Stravinsky's concert. TV lights are accidentally switched on during Craft's austere first half after electricians mistook the rehearsal noises for a break. Stravinsky's Firebird in the second half is filmed.
- **Why it matters:** The hall is the site of a funny disaster and a historic recording, though the building itself is not described.
