# Vladivostok

Russian port on the Sea of Japan. In _The Island of Sea Women_ Young-sook and Mi-ja dive there on 'leaving-home water-work'.

## The Island of Sea Women — Lisa See (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19865296W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvcq6c8xzw9aq8qaeptscx)

> We lived in a boardinghouse for Korean haenyeo tucked in an alley down by the docks.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In 1944 the girls live in a boardinghouse for Korean haenyeo by the docks and dive in freezing water off the coast; on their Sunday off they wander the boulevards and a park and whisper about husbands. On a later contract they sleep in a sooty dormitory, discover they are both pregnant, and give birth in June 1945, diving with newborns cradled on deck.
- **Why it matters:** Away from Jeju and their husbands, the friends are closest here; it is where both become mothers.
