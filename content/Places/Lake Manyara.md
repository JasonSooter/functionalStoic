# Lake Manyara

Rift Valley lake and national park in northern Tanzania; in _Life on Air_ Attenborough goes there to film Iain Douglas-Hamilton's elephants.

## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx391q8bwpnwj7c8y8t04n)

> A few days later, feeling unhealthily pallid and very unprepared, I walked into the bar at Manyara safari lodge, on the edge of the Rift Valley in Tanzania.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Attenborough arrives to film Iain Douglas-Hamilton's elephants. At the bar a woman asks 'Weren't you once David Attenborough?' as a prank arranged by Alan Root.
- **Why it matters:** It opens the 'Exotic Interludes' chapter; this part of the book gives only Attenborough's arrival at the lodge.
