# Gan-Finn's Gamme (Nordic Tales)

A Sámi turf hut on the Finnmark coast in Nordic Tales, where Jack of Sjöholm is sheltered by the Gan-Finn and saved by Seimke. The hut is unnamed; the pin is a rough guess in Finnmark, Norway.

## Nordic Tales — Chronicle Books (2017)
Source: [Original](https://chroniclebooks.com/products/nordic-tales) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fydbyxfd9ner6hky89erh1)

> Now as he stood there, speering and spying about him, he saw, far away, the smoke of a Finn Gamme,

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Jack, sole survivor of the wreck in Finmark, sees the smoke of the Gan-Finn's hut and is taken in by the old sorcerer and the girl Seimke. The Gan-Finn hides Jack's way home with fog and flies; Seimke helps him escape, and at the end Jack comes back for her.
- **Why it matters:** The hut is the story's magical centre, where Jack's fate and his boat-building gift are decided.
- **Real vs. fictional:** The hut and the Gan-Finn are folk-tale inventions; Finmark (Finnmark) is real.
- **Where is it?:** ⚠️ Clues: the crew is wrecked 'up to Finmark' after sailing north from the East Lofotens; the hut lies beneath a cliff on the shore, with reindeer on the fjeld. No reference names a site. Pinned at the representative point of Finnmark; the hut could be anywhere on that coast.
