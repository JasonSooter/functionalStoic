# Enewetak Atoll

Atoll in the Marshall Islands where the United States tested nuclear weapons. In _The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid_ the first hydrogen bomb explodes there in 1952, when Bryson is ten months old.

## The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid — Bill Bryson (2006)

Source: [[The Life and Times of the Thunderbolt Kid]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4620956W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5khv96tmqmhwvq1329gxhsy)

> AT 7:15 IN THE MORNING local time on November 1, 1952, the United States exploded the first hydrogen bomb in the Eniwetok (or Enewetak or many other variants) atoll in the Marshall Islands of the South Pacific

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The bomb chapter opens with the first hydrogen-bomb test at Enewetak on 1 November 1952, then describes the 1954 Bikini blast, whose fallout fell on the islanders of Rongelap and on the Japanese fishing boat Lucky Dragon. Bryson places himself in Des Moines, ten months old.
- **Why it matters:** The test sites frame the chapter's mix of Cold War fear and comedy.
