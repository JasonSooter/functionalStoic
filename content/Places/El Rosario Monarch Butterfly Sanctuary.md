## How to Bug a Bug — Jessica Leigh Hester (2018)

Source: [Original](https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/how-to-tag-a-butterfly) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5k7gw3mfrqjbmbg68dv24qj)

> In the reserves of El Rosario and Sierra Chincua, in Michoacán, they blanket the branches of

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the article:** Monarchs east of the Rockies migrate to these mountain forests, where upwards of a billion arrive yearly and cover sacred fir branches. Local farmers collect tags from them for Monarch Watch, but recovery rates are low (about 14,000 of 1.4 million).
- **Why it matters:** Destination of the migration the tagging aims to map.
