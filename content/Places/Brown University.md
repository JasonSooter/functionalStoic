## Another Brooklyn — Jacqueline Woodson (2016)
Source: [[Another Brooklyn]] · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5dqys5h1wzfbmgztz5y9gnv)

> When I stepped off the bus in Providence, Rhode Island, I was alone.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** August leaves Brooklyn by bus at sixteen/seventeen for college in Providence, studies death and dying, and takes her first lover; later 'my first year at Brown' she sees Angela dancing on television. Her decades of wandering follow (Wisconsin, Bali, Korea, San Francisco).
- **Why it matters:** The endpoint of the escape from Bushwick the AP classes were meant to buy; the place she chooses 'no past'.
- **Real vs. fictional:** Real; the text names the city and, once, 'Brown'.
