# Dupont Circle

Neighbourhood and traffic circle in north-west Washington, D.C. A turning point in Colum McCann's _Apeirogon_: Bassam at a Dupont Circle reception, where a woman lifts a fallen Band-Aid from his collar.

## Apeirogon — Colum McCann (2020)

Source: [[Apeirogon]] · [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/207433/apeirogon-a-novel-by-colum-mccann/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5jjra957svhkteg9a0gqtsn)

> At a reception in Dupont Circle he was surrounded. Handshakes. Business cards.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** After his AIPAC speech, Bassam is surrounded at a reception in Dupont Circle and a small blond American woman reaches for his collar. She lifts away the Band-Aid that had fallen onto his shirt and folds it in a white napkin, a small round circle of blood. Later, on a road lined with white fences and rosebushes, he cannot shrug off the thought of the woman who lifted the Band-Aid.
- **Why it matters:** An intimate, unsettling moment of American hospitality and surveillance of his body that stays with him; the scene sits between the stage address and the Senator's office. Little is said about the Circle itself beyond the reception.
