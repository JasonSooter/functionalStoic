# University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

Flagship public university in Urbana and Champaign, Illinois. It turns up in The Simple Path to Wealth (JL Collins): his 1968-72 college, used to contrast cheap tuition then with today's student debt.

## The Simple Path to Wealth — JL Collins (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20916711W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5fmt8fv6km84v4wpvy33hx0)

> When I was in college at the University of Illinois from 1968-72, the total annual cost was $1,200.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Collins recalls attending in 1968-72, when a year cost $1,200 and he paid for it with summer work felling diseased elm trees. He lived in one room of a dilapidated house on rice and ketchup, and notes the house was later torn down for fancy dorms.
- **Why it matters:** It anchors his contrast between cheap college then and debt-funded college now. Set elsewhere the cost comparison would change, but the argument would stand.
