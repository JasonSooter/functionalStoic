- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The host gives Kungsträdgården, at the end of the blue line, the award for the most unhinged artwork: candy-cane stripes, gargoyle heads, bare natural rock, and stone pieces said to come from a 17th-century royal palace. Its exit leads onto a car-free street with a bike path.
- **Why it matters:** It is one of the most fully described stations in the video and the clearest case of the system's art going all the way.
