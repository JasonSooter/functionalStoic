# King David School (Linksfield)

Jewish school in the Linksfield suburb of [[Johannesburg]]. In Born a Crime it is where Trevor Noah's crew performs "Go Hitler!" at a cultural day.

## Born a Crime — Trevor Noah (2016)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17824318W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6z18zw7sbhaky9vyf44mked)

> She sent us the information with the time and place and the name of the school: the King David School. A Jewish school.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Chapter 15 ("Go Hitler!"). Trevor and Bongani's DJ group, the Black and White Boys, are booked for a cultural day in a Linksfield school. Their best dancer is a friend whose mother named him Hitler, a name Noah explains is common in South Africa. On stage in the assembly hall, in front of hundreds of Jewish kids in yarmulkes, Trevor chants "Go Hit-ler!" A teacher yanks the plug and calls them disgusting, and Trevor at first thinks she objects to the dance move.
- **Why it matters:** the scene is the book's sharpest joke about how history is taught: Trevor's schooling never made the name mean to him what it means to the school.
