# Armanaz

Town in Idlib Governorate, Syria, near the Turkish border and the Orontes (Asi) River. In _The Beekeeper of Aleppo_ Nuri and Afra cross the river into Turkey from there.

## The Beekeeper of Aleppo: A Moving Testament to the Human Spirit — Christy Lefteri (2019)

Source: [[The Beekeeper of Aleppo - A Moving Testament to the Human Spirit]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20112913W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kgr8dkx9mx4x3yka7tpw9q)

> Between Armanaz and the border is the Asi River. It separates Turkey from Syria and I knew that we would need to get across.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** The smuggler leaves the pickup in woods near Armanaz, and Nuri and Afra walk to the bank of the Asi River, where thirty or forty people are waiting to cross into Turkey. A crying girl is hauled across in a large saucepan. Nuri looks back at the path home before they cross.
- **Why it matters:** The river is the border and Nuri's last chance to turn back; after it they are refugees.
