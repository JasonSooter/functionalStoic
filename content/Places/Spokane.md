# Spokane

City on the Spokane River in eastern Washington. In _The Boys in the Boat_ it is Joe Rantz's birthplace, the house on Nora Avenue where his mother dies.

## The Boys in the Boat — Daniel James Brown (2013)
Source: [Original](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/310727/the-boys-in-the-boat-by-daniel-james-brown/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m869xwxkh3yqvk958pw4915v)

> Harry and Nellie and Fred Rantz moved into one of those houses, at 1023 East Nora Avenue, and Joe was born there in March of 1914.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** The Rantzes come to the booming, still rough-and-tumble city in 1906; Harry opens a car repair and manufacturing shop and the family settles north of the river, where Joe is born in 1914. His earliest memories are of his mother's death, and of his brother Fred in the upstairs room on Nora Avenue explaining why he can't go east with him. Harry later returns from Canada, builds a new house in Spokane and marries Thula; that house burns to the ground, with only Nellie's piano saved, and the family leaves for the Idaho mines.
- **Why it matters:** Joe's mother's death here begins the losses that leave him feeling he has no one but himself, the theme the whole first part of the book builds on.
