## Life on Air — David Attenborough (2002)
Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hy8qr2x33exayek6p3psw6)

> He and his wife Hedwige lived in the middle of a rain forest in Africa's Ivory Coast observing chimpanzees.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** With Christophe Boesch, the team runs through the forest at 4 a.m. to find sixty chimps, then spends a week following them and films a cooperative colobus monkey hunt.
- **Why it matters:** A central, long scene: the hunt ends the programme and reveals team-work origins. The park is not named in the text.
