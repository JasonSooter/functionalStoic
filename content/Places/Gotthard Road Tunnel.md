## Switzerland’s \$2BN Tunnel U-Turn — The B1M (2026)

Source: [Original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bzTntRHmpa8) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5bs72760jqygedmhkbb062t)

> Fortunately, that's unlikely to happen because the A2 is part of one of the most impressive tunnel networks in the world and not just for cars.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the video:** The tunnel has carried traffic since 1980, replacing the winding Gotthard Pass, and must close for renovation. A second tube is being bored from both ends; one machine got stuck in fractured rock in June 2025, adding about 20 million francs, and half the spoil is to be shipped to Lake Lucerne.
- **Why it matters:** The tunnel and its second tube are the subject of the whole video; the A2 corridor is the argument.
