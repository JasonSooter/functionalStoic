## Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde — Björn Schwarze et al. (BBSR) (2025)
Source: [[Die Stadt Der Viertelstunde]] · [Original](https://www.bbsr.bund.de/BBSR/DE/veroeffentlichungen/bbsr-online/2025/bbsr-online-027-2025-dl.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7kmj923f89g8a3gqdf0px4b)

> Dagegen werden in Essen-Rüttenscheid Gastronomie, Sport- und Kulturevents sowie Angebote für aktiven Sport ausgesprochen positiv bewertet; dennoch wird in der Freizeit der ÖV relativ häufig genutzt.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** The model rates it a 4.3-minute city with 98 % fulfilment and an index of 94.2, the best of the case studies. With Leipzig's Waldstraßenviertel it is the quarter residents rate most positively and one where many trips, even for leisure, stay within 15 minutes. Yet residents still use public transport fairly often in their free time, and neighbourly ties are weaker than in Essen's garden-city Margarethenhöhe.
- **Why it matters:** It is the report's best-case German inner-city quarter, and also a reminder that a rich local offer does not by itself keep every trip local.
