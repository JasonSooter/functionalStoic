# 18 East 48th Street

Address in Midtown [[Manhattan]] of photographer Nickolas Muray's shop and home. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it is the place Frida recalls in her 1939 farewell letter to Muray, where their affair began.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)

Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m73hntjcnpwn2jjz0cysr5qe)

> Nevertheless you have to understand that no matter what happens to us in life, you will always be, for myself, the same Nick I met one morning in New York in 18 E. 48th St.

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** In Chapter 17 Frida writes to Muray on June 13, 1939, recalling breakfast at the Barbizon-Plaza and going on to his shop to take photographs. She asks him to send back a little cushion from the couch downstairs, to move her photograph from the fireplace, and not to take his new wife to Coney Island; the letter ends when she learns he has already married.
- **Why it matters:** It is where the Muray affair began, and the letter shows Frida mourning it and erasing herself from his home.
