> After dinner we took a walk through the West Village. It was a warm summer night.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the article:** A recently divorced woman tells of Bryon driving up from DC after nine months of Zoom happy hours and calls. They eat outside at an Italian restaurant as restaurants reopen, then walk through the West Village, where he holds her hand, gets her flowers and walks on the outside of the sidewalk.
- **Why it matters:** The walk is the first in-person courtship scene after a pandemic romance; it is a backdrop for the feeling of being courted rather than a place analysed in detail.
