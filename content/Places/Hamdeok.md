# Hamdeok

Coastal village and beach in Jocheon-eup, three kilometres west of Bukchon on Jeju. In _The Island of Sea Women_ it is Mi-ja's home on the base and the police post where trust between the friends breaks.

## The Island of Sea Women — Lisa See (2019)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19865296W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kvcp4rwbrh8p0fkxfjc59m)

> Instead, I watched in horror as some policemen came out on the roof, ran to a mounted machine gun, and, without warning, began to fire.

- **Role:** turning point
- **In the book:** Sang-mun's job for the Americans gives him and Mi-ja a house on the base at Hamdeok, three kilometres from Bukchon, and the friends meet halfway. After two policemen shoot at leaflet-sellers in Bukchon, villagers drag them to the Hamdeok station to complain; police fire a machine gun at them and Young-sook sees Sang-mun in the window. Later refugees are held in the school playground there.
- **Why it matters:** Hamdeok puts Mi-ja on the police side of the line and breaks Young-sook's trust in her.
