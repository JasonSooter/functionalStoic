- **Role:** turning point
- **In the article:** The first New Orleans school garden, built as a healing space for children after Hurricane Katrina; later students from Green Charter help Michelle Obama plant and harvest her garden.
- **Why it matters:** Origin of Edible Schoolyard New Orleans and tied to post-Katrina recovery.
