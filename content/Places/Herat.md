## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6kx5bttv1ma63wksym70d)

> This is a city of absolute enchantment in the literal sense of the word. It loosens all the bonds binding the traveller to his own age and sets him free to live in a past that is vital and crude but never ugly. Herat is as old as history and as moving as a great epic poem

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** She arrives and describes the hotel staff, men's dress, the bazaar, camel traffic, and the mosque with its mosaics.
- **Why it matters:** One of the book's most admiring portraits of a city.

## On the Hippie Trail — Rick Steves (2025)

Source: [[On the Hippie Trail]] · [Original](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/rick-steves/on-the-hippie-trail/9781641717458/) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5kjegh1bf94d42qvzgs0kpv)

> Hotel Mowafaq, the fanciest hotel in downtown Herat (\$5), was just what we needed: centrally located, showers, swimming pool, clean restaurant, and free of con men and hustlers.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** After the Iran–Afghanistan border, they settle into the Hotel Mowafaq, "the fanciest hotel in downtown Herat (\$5)". Herat becomes a high point: bargaining in the bazaar for clothes and a mink pelt, Rick's first hashish, a wedding, a torchlit horse-buggy ride and bicycles around town.
- **Why it matters:** Rick says this is where the trip really begins.
