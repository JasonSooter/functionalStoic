## Full Tilt — Dervla Murphy (1965)

Source: [[Full Tilt - Ireland to India With a Bicycle]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL9584029M) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5j6kx5bttv1ma63wksym70d)

> This is a city of absolute enchantment in the literal sense of the word. It loosens all the bonds binding the traveller to his own age and sets him free to live in a past that is vital and crude but never ugly. Herat is as old as history and as moving as a great epic poem

- **Role:** subject
- **In the book:** She arrives and describes the hotel staff, men's dress, the bazaar, camel traffic, and the mosque with its mosaics.
- **Why it matters:** One of the book's most admiring portraits of a city.
