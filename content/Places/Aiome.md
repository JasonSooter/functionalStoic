## Life on Air \[revised & updated 2nd edition] — David Attenborough (2002)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24547776M/Life_on_air) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5hx374dh42pktzwmwj2fcr6)

> We marched into Aiome the following day at noon. The Patrol Officer gave us cold beer, chairs to sit on, and showers. In that order.

- **Role:** journey waypoint
- **In the book:** Aiome is the Government station on the Ramu side where the charter plane is to pick them up. They wait on the Patrol Officer's veranda until Tabibuga men arrive with Barry and the abandoned loads, then fly back to the Wahgi.
- **Why it matters:** End point of the Jimi trek and the place where the charter is met.
