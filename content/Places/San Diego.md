# San Diego

City in southern [[California]]. In Captains Courageous (Rudyard Kipling) Harvey's grieving parents are there when the telegram comes.

## Captains Courageous — Rudyard Kipling (1897)

Source: [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20173W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m5gapgw6ygna8m64t44qdjx4)

> He had taken the wife to his raw new palace in San Diego, where she and her people occupied a wing of great price

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** Cheyne has taken his wife to his new palace in San Diego, where he runs his railroads from a verandah room while she declines. The telegram announcing Harvey's survival arrives there, and their private car leaves at dawn for the record dash east.
- **Why it matters:** It sets up the cross-country race that reunites the family.
