# Galerie Pierre Colle

Paris art gallery (Renou et Colle) on the rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré, [[Paris]]. In Hayden Herrera's _Frida_ it hosts the 1939 "Mexique" show, Frida's first exhibition in Europe.

## Frida — Hayden Herrera (1983)

Source: [[Frida - A Biography of Frida Kahlo]] · [Original](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4114749W) · [Readwise highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m73hnq162e403b68yj0dk36x)

> Finally there was a gallery called “Pierre Colle” which accepted the damn exhibition.

- **Role:** setting
- **In the book:** In Chapter 15 Marcel Duchamp gets Frida's paintings out of customs and secures this gallery. Colle's associate at first thinks only two works are not too shocking, but "Mexique" opens on March 10 with seventeen of her paintings amid Breton's Mexican objects and photographs. The opening draws praise from Kandinsky, Picasso and Miró; sales are poor, but the Louvre buys _The Frame_.
- **Why it matters:** It is the culmination of the Paris trip, her first European exhibition and her first museum acquisition.
