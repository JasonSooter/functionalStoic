## Theme within a theme: Dreiländereck

Freiburg sits a short ride from both France and Switzerland. The goal is at least one coffee in each.

## The rules, briefly

- 8 rides, to at least 7 different places (one repeat allowed)
- At least 2 miles (3.2 km) round trip each time
- No more than 2 rides a week, and one coffee stop per day
- Any approved autumn drink counts: coffee, tea, hot chocolate, cider
- A "coffee shop without walls" counts too: a bench, a summit, a bridge

Full rules on [Chasing Mailboxes](https://chasingmailboxes.com/2026/10/03/coffeeneuring-challenge-2026-and-were-16-basic-in-the-best-way/).
