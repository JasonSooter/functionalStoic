## From Marina Cooley's "42 Things I Know to Be True at 42"

_Added 2026-09-26 by Claude (`source/ai/claude`), from two Readwise tasks. Both items checked in the full post in Reader._

Source: Marina Cooley, [42 Things I Know to Be True at 42](https://readwise.io/open/955962375), _Professor Off Duty_ (Substack). The two bullets above under Content come from the same list (#32 and the small-talk item) · [[Bookshelf/Highlights & Notes/Articles/42 Things I Know to Be True at 42|highlights]].

### A hobby is insurance against burnout (#32)

The burnout line above is Cooley's item **#32**; it links to her fuller post, [The best insurance policy against burnout is a hobby](https://professoroffduty.substack.com/p/the-best-insurance-policy-against). The argument there: a hobby gives **full absorption**, the kind of attention work rarely allows, and _"your hobby might be the most stable thing you have."_

### Share a special hobby with each kid (#38)

> [!quote] Marina Cooley, #38
> **Find a special hobby to share with each kid.** So much of parenting is presented as a drag. Sharing hobbies with kids is delightful. ([View Highlight](https://readwise.io/open/955962654))

Also in [[Concepts/Parenting|Parenting]]. ⚠️ Her link on this item goes to "Workhorses Only, Edition 25", which is about a pared-down wardrobe; nothing on kids' hobbies was found there, so the link looks mismatched in the original.
